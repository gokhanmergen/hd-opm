  75. Co-author of LMHR study protocol says the design could not detect plaque progression

    Dec 10, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 17 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

    Louis paraphrases Dr. Spencer Nadolsky, who helped write the LMHR (keto hypercholesterolemia) study protocol, saying it enrolled only extremely healthy people with no diabetes, prediabetes, atherosclerosis, smoking, overweight or high hs-CRP and perfect blood pressure. The study's absence of progression should therefore not be read as proof of safety.
