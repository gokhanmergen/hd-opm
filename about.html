About

This site is an archive of posts from the Facebook group Heart Disease – Optimal Prevention & Management, where members discuss the latest scientific research on preventing, managing, and potentially reversing cardiovascular disease through evidence-based interventions.

The group focuses on proven and emerging therapies: optimizing blood markers, and optimal treatment using lifestyle changes, medications, supplements and off-label drugs that may extend lifespan. This archive collects the group admins' posts in one place, so they can be searched, browsed by topic and read long after they scroll off Facebook.

Key principles

  • Our goal is to learn and work together to address cardiovascular disease scientifically.
  • We believe peer-reviewed research and clinical trials should guide our knowledge.
  • We are open to proven pharmaceuticals like statins, ezetimibe and PCSK9 inhibitors, as well as lifestyle changes.
  • We help address risk factors like obesity, high blood pressure, diabetes and high cholesterol.
  • We embrace supplements and drugs shown to slow down atherosclerosis or reverse metabolic disorders.
  • Our ultimate goal is maximizing your disease-free years and lifespan.
  • While we hold diverse opinions, we share a commitment to rigorous analysis.
  • No conspiracies. Claims are defended with facts.
