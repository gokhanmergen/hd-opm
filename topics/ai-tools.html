ACC guidance backs AI plaque analysis on CT angiography to guide and track treatment

Jun 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 7 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Shared summary of new American College of Cardiology guidance on AI quantitative coronary plaque analysis (QCPA, e.g. Cleerly, HeartFlow). Unlike CAC, which only measures calcified plaque, QCPA characterizes all plaque types, including soft plaque more likely to rupture, and can help follow treatment response.

Apple Watch hypertension alerts: 69% of alerts correct, 79% of non-alerts correct
