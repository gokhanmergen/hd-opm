A zero CAC score does not rule out blockages: 54% of 23,759 symptomatic patients had CAC 0

Louis challenges the myth that a CAC of zero means no risk. In the Western Denmark Heart Registry of 23,759 symptomatic patients undergoing CT angiography, 54% had CAC zero, and half of people with obstructive blockages have zero CAC.
