Magnesium L-threonate for 6 weeks improved cognitive age ~7.5 years (RCT, n=100)

Jun 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 29 · 💬 22 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 100 healthy adults aged 18-45 who were unhappy with their sleep. Magnesium L-threonate improved overall cognition, cognitive age, working memory and reaction time, and some subjective but not objective sleep measures. (Louis's post says six months, but the trial and article describe six weeks.)

'Grounding' during sleep is unnatural because primates evolved sleeping off the ground

Mar 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 36 Myths, misinformation & controversies

Louis argues that sleeping grounded (connected to the earth) is not natural, because primates slept in trees for tens of millions of years and wood is an insulator, so a raised bed mirrors natural primate behavior. This is an evolutionary argument and cites no study.

Magnesium plus potassium improved insomnia and sleep hormones in type 2 diabetes

100 mg vitamin B6 reduced depression symptoms in a new randomized trial; B12 failed

Aug 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes a new RCT reports that 100 mg vitamin B6 significantly reduced depression symptoms while B12 did not. The same authors found in 2022 that B6 reduces anxiety. He notes depression and anxiety are CVD risk factors and warns of nerve toxicity with prolonged high doses.

Satchin Panda: wait 2 hours after waking to eat; finish dinner 3 hours before sleep

Mar 20, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 8 Fasting & meal timing

Louis summarizes meal-timing advice from circadian researcher Satchin Panda for glucose control. Delay breakfast at least two hours after waking because the pancreas regulates insulin poorly then, and eat the last large meal at least 3 hours before sleep since melatonin begins rising about 3 hours before bedtime and inhibits insulin secretion. This is expert advice from a podcast clip rather than a cited trial.

Magnesium glycinate and rapid depression recovery: a 2006 case series, not a trial

Louis comments on a shared claim that magnesium glycinate (125-300 mg with each meal and at bedtime) produced rapid recovery from major depression.
