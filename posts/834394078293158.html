Ezetimibe linked to lower Alzheimer's risk in database analysis and lab experiments

Aug 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 3 Brain health & dementia

UAMS researchers found ezetimibe prevented tangled aggregate proteins from clumping in lab-grown neurons, and a clinical database of nearly 950,000 older adults showed lower Alzheimer's and related dementia incidence among ezetimibe users. Findings are preliminary.

Ezetimibe use linked to 86% lower Alzheimer's and dementia risk in database analysis

Oct 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 38 · 💬 27 Brain health & dementia

A 2024 Aging Biology paper analysed clinical databases and found that people taking the LDL-lowering drug ezetimibe had a much lower risk of Alzheimer's disease and related dementias. Brain tissue from patients also showed more of two proteins, HK1 and 14-3-3 gamma. This is an observational association, so it does not prove the drug prevents dementia.

Statin- and ezetimibe-like gene variants linked to 76-82% lower dementia risk per 40 mg/dL

Aug 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis highlights a Mendelian randomization and meta-analysis of about 1 million people showing lifelong lower non-HDL cholesterol via statin-like (HMGCR) and ezetimibe-like (NPC1L1) variants was associated with 76% and 82% lower dementia per ~40 mg/dL drop. CETP variants showed a 70% reduction.

Only lipophilic statins like atorvastatin enter the brain; PCSK9 drugs and ezetimibe don't

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 10 Brain health & dementia

Louis explains the blood-brain barrier and cholesterol: the brain makes its own cholesterol separately from blood, so non-statin LDL drugs and hydrophilic statins like rosuvastatin have little brain effect. Lipophilic statins do enter the brain, with promising observational data against Alzheimer's, and a 20,000-person atorvastatin trial is under way. Two prior trials showed statins do not worsen existing Alzheimer's, and midlife high LDL is a leading modifiable dementia risk (2024 Lancet Commission, about 7% of US cases per Louis).
