Jack LaLanne's longevity credited to exercise, plant-heavy diet, fish and two meals a day

Louis Nervegna · Aug 21, 2024 · ♥ 22 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevityMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

The post credits the long, healthy lives of Jack LaLanne (96) and his wife (98) to lifestyle rather than chance. It describes exercise, 10+ fruits and vegetables daily, two meals a day, and fish as the main animal protein. This is anecdotal.

  • LaLanne lived to 96; his wife is 98
  • At least 10 fruits and vegetables per day
  • Two meals per day; fish main animal protein

Do you think it’s just a coincidence that Jack Lalanne lived to 96 in relatively good health? And now his wife is 98 and can do this?

No way.

This was largely lifestyle.

In addition to all the exercise, they both ate a plant heavy diet, consuming at least 10 fruits and vegetables per day, but usually a lot more. They only ate 2 meals per day, and their main source of animal protein was fish.
