Veritasium on PFAS: DuPont concealed Teflon chemical toxicity; avoid exposure

Gokhan Can · May 15, 2025 · ♥ 8 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post
Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)
AI summary & key facts

Summary of a Veritasium video on PFOA (C8) and PFAS contamination, DuPont's concealed knowledge, and the persistence of these chemicals in nearly all human blood. Gokhan suggests learning how to avoid PFAS and donating blood regularly.

  • PFAS persist in the body and are detectable in nearly all human blood
  • Gokhan's suggestions: avoid exposure; donate blood if possible

PFAS accumulates in human body and stays there forever. Solution? 1) Learn how to avoid it 2) Donate blood regularly if you can.

Veritasium's video, "How One Company Secretly Poisoned The Planet," chronicles the history of Teflon and the associated "forever chemical" PFOA (C8), exposing how its primary manufacturer, DuPont, knew about its toxicity and persistence for decades but concealed this information while discharging it into the environment. This corporate cover-up, eventually brought to light by lawyer Rob Bilott, led to widespread global contamination of water systems and the presence of PFOA and other PFAS in nearly all human blood, linked to serious health issues like various cancers and thyroid disease. The video explores the science behind PFAS, the scale of contamination, the inadequate shift to similarly problematic alternatives like GenX, and discusses potential solutions ranging from individual water filtration to systemic regulatory changes and technological innovations to combat this pervasive chemical pollution.
