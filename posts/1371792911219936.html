Longevity clinic promotion for multi-system biological age testing

Louis Nervegna · Aug 17, 2026 · ♥ 7 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Share about a Singapore longevity clinic that combines genetic, epigenetic, metabolic, inflammatory and microbiome measures. Mostly a service promotion with no research data.

This is the future of preventative medicine.

The aging process itself is the biggest risk factor for most diseases. Until we learn to track it properly and intervene early where possible, we are flying blind.

Chi LongevityLongevity Services | The Maier Method | Virtual & In-ClinicHealthy longevity programmes delivered through the Maier Method. In-clinic and virtual options spanning health assessments and biological age insights, available worldwide.chilongevity.com
Shared from Longevity.Technology · original post

As biological-age testing becomes increasingly common across the longevity sector, Chi Longevity's approach extends beyond biological-age measurements alone by focusing on how those data points connect.

The Singapore clinic's proprietary framework, The Maier Method, brings together genetic, epigenetic, metabolic, inflammatory and microbiome measures, then examines how these biological systems interact to build a more integrated understanding of an individual's health.

Rather than returning a single biological-age result, the framework considers relationships across multiple systems to help inform more personalised interventions.

The framework was developed by Prof. Andrea Maier, Co-Founder of Chi Longevity and a recognised leader in precision geromedicine, reflecting a systems-based approach to understanding healthy longevity.

SCOT-HEART: CT angiography cut 5-year coronary death or MI risk 41% in chest pain patients

Jan 03, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 18 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summarises SCOT-HEART (4,146 patients with stable chest pain, standard care vs standard care plus coronary CTA). CTA guidance lowered coronary death or nonfatal MI to 2.3% from 3.9%, mainly by directing aspirin and statin use to patients with plaque. Total procedures were similar by year 5; results apply to chest-pain patients only.

Same CT plaque, lower MI risk in women than men in a 10,003-person Danish cohort

Aug 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A Danish population study of 10,003 asymptomatic adults over 40 found subclinical coronary plaque less frequent in women at every age, for example 6% vs 20% at age 40-44. After 6 years, obstructive disease carried 4.1-fold higher MI risk in women versus 12-fold in men, though the sex interaction was not statistically significant.
