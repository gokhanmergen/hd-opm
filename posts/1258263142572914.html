Largest cocoa extract trial: may prevent high BP, but only if BP is already healthy

AI summary & key facts

An Examine.com breakdown of the largest cocoa clinical trial finds cocoa extract may prevent hypertension in people with healthy blood pressure but not those who already have high BP.

  • Benefit only in participants with healthy baseline BP

Research cited

  • Cocoa extract and blood pressure trial · RCT
Shared from Examine.com · original post

In by far the largest ever clinical trial on cocoa, researchers found that supplementing with cocoa extract may prevent high blood pressure, but only if you have healthy blood pressure to begin with.

Read our full breakdown on this study:

Examine.comDaily cocoa for maintaining healthy blood pressure during aging - Study SummaryIn this randomized controlled trial in 8,905 older adults (average age of 71), supplementing with cocoa extract reduced the risk of developing high blood pressure, but only in participants with a normal systolic blood pressure (<120 mmHg) at baseline.examine.com
