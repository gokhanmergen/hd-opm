Shared from BoxLife Magazine · original post

Cancer diagnoses in people under 50 are rising globally, with six obesity-related cancers—thyroid, breast, colorectal, kidney, endometrial and leukemia—showing increased rates in over three-quarters of countries studied.

Rising obesity, now affecting one in eight adults worldwide, fuels chronic inflammation and hormonal imbalances that promote tumor growth.

Younger generations face greater risk as excess weight accumulates earlier in life, challenging assumptions that screenings alone explain the surge.

Experts emphasize prevention through healthy weight maintenance, regular exercise and whole-food diets to curb this alarming trend.
