Group rules reminder: back factual claims with evidence, label opinions

Louis Nervegna · Apr 29, 2024 · ♥ 24 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Group news & community
AI summary & key facts

Admin reminder that this is a science-based group: factual statements on controversial topics should be backed by studies such as PubMed-indexed papers, opinions should be labeled as opinions, and repetitive spamming of opinions should be avoided.

  • Support controversial factual claims with PubMed-indexed studies
  • Label opinions clearly; avoid repeating them as spam

Reminder of the group rules:

This is a science-based group.

If you make a factual statement (as opposed expressing an opinion), the science should be reasonably accurate.

If there is any major doubt— or if it’s a controversial subject— then you need to support your factual statement by scientific evidence. For example, scientific studies indexed on pubmed.

The admins don’t have time to fact check every comment or post, so this is your responsibility when you are writing something.

You are, of course, allowed to express your opinion, as long as you clearly label it as such.

Please don’t phrase your opinion as a statement of fact. This is when it becomes a problem.

We are here primarily to discuss science and not opinions. So please avoid repeating the same opinion over and over again on posts to the point of creating spam or a distraction for other members.

Thanks for participating in the group.
