High triglycerides are causal in CVD via ApoB particles and as a marker of liver fat

  • Triglycerides raise ApoB-tagged particles
  • Marker of excess liver fat and insulin resistance

High triglycerides are causal in CVD.

They are a major contributor to high levels of ApoB. These ApoB tagged particles are directly atherogenic.

High triglycerides are also an indirect marker of excess liver fat and insulin resistance which also causes CVD.
