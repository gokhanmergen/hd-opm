FDA allows a qualified health claim that yogurt may reduce type 2 diabetes risk

Louis Nervegna · Jul 05, 2024 · ♥ 4 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

In March 2024 the FDA permitted a qualified health claim that regular yogurt intake may reduce type 2 diabetes risk. The agency judged the evidence credible but limited, and the claim should state the 2 cups (3 servings) per week threshold.

  • Qualified health claim announced March 2024
  • At least 2 cups (3 servings) of yogurt per week
  • FDA: some credible evidence, but limited

In March 2024, the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) announced a qualified health claim that eating yogurt regularly may reduce the risk of type 2 diabetes (T2D).

Yogurt manufacturers in the USA are now allowed to state this claim on their product.

The FDA concluded that the claim is supported by some credible evidence, but that the evidence is limited. The claim states that eating at least 2 cups (3 servings) of yogurt per week may reduce the risk of T2D, and this consumption criteria should be included in the claim.
