Your brain physically ages slower if you eat this way, and researchers have the MRI scans to prove it. 🧠🥦

Researchers tracked 1,647 middle-aged and older adults from the Framingham Heart Study Offspring cohort for an average of 12 years, giving them repeated MRI brain scans and dietary questionnaires. People who closely followed the MIND diet, a hybrid of the Mediterranean and DASH eating plans that emphasises leafy greens, berries, poultry, and fish while limiting fried foods and sweets, lost significantly less grey matter over time. Every three-point increase in MIND diet score was linked to 20% less age-related grey matter decline, the equivalent of 2.5 years of slower structural brain aging.

This is one of the first long-term longitudinal studies to show the MIND diet protects the physical structure of the brain, not just cognitive test scores.

News Source: Journal of Neurology Neurosurgery & Psychiatry (BMJ Group), "Adherence to the MIND diet and longitudinal brain structural changes over a decade: evidence from the Framingham heart study offspring cohort" (March 2026)
