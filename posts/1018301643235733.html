Bill Harris: omega-6/omega-3 ratio does not predict heart disease or diabetes

Louis Nervegna · May 01, 2025 · ♥ 5 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)How to read research
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a clip where fatty acid scientist Bill Harris explains the omega-6 to omega-3 ratio does not predict cardiovascular disease or diabetes risk. He notes Harris co-authored two very large pooled biomarker studies (~70,000 and ~40,000 people) showing higher blood omega-6 linked to lower heart disease and diabetes.

  • Two pooled analyses of ~70,000 and ~40,000 people used blood biomarkers, not questionnaires
  • Higher blood omega-6 linked to lower CVD and diabetes

In this very recent interview clip, Bill Harris explains why the ratio of omega 6 to omega 3 does not predict risk of cardiovascular disease or diabetes.

Harris is a well known fatty acid scientist who has published numerous papers on the health benefits of omega 3's. He is also a co-author on two influential papers showing that higher blood levels of omega 6 are associated with lower rates of both heart disease and diabetes. These studies were very large (70,000 and 40,000 people) and were based on direct measurements of omega 6 in the blood, not food questionnaires. See comments for links to both studies.
