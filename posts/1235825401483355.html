DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed ~40 mm3 of soft plaque in one year

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains the DISCO-CT randomized trial, where a DASH diet plus statins produced the largest soft (non-calcified) plaque regression of any dietary trial on CT angiography. Adjusted to one year it was -39.5 mm3, versus an average at-risk person growing about 5 mm3 per year, so roughly 8 years of progression reversed. He notes the DASH diet was about 30% fat, mostly unsaturated, and adds caveats about interpretation.

MIND diet: 20% less grey matter loss per 3-point score rise in Framingham MRI study

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

In 1,647 Framingham Offspring participants followed about 12 years with repeated MRI scans, closer adherence to the MIND diet (Mediterranean-DASH hybrid) was linked to slower grey matter loss. Each 3-point rise in MIND score matched 20% less age-related grey matter decline, equal to about 2.5 years of slower brain aging. This is an observational association.

Nearly half of dementia cases may be preventable; Willett recommends the MIND diet

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a BBC Science Focus article noting the 2024 Lancet Commission estimated 45% of dementia cases are attributable to modifiable risk factors. Walter Willett says diet is most important and recommends the MIND diet to put dementia off for some years.
