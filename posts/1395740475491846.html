Honest scientists use words like 'may' and 'could': uncertainty is part of science

Louis Nervegna · Sep 15, 2026 · ♥ 1 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
How to read researchMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

A short shared video clip notes that credible scientists such as Walter Willett rarely sound 100% certain, and that hedged language reflects testing and revising. It promotes a podcast episode and carries little concrete content.

Science vs Ideology

Quoted:

Ever notice how honest scientists never sound 100% certain? That’s not weakness. It’s part of the process!

Science is built on curiosity, testing, revising, and being straightforward about what we don’t know just yet.

That’s why experts like Dr. Walter Willett, MD, DrPH, one of the most cited nutrition researchers in the world, uses words like
“may,” “could,” and “likely.”
