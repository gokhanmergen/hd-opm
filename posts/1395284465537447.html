After the Novartis pelacarsen Lp(a) trial failed, doubts arise about Lp(a) as a cause

Louis Nervegna · Sep 14, 2026 · ♥ 8 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post

Louis shares a New York Times article on why the Novartis Lp(a)-lowering drug failed to prevent heart attacks. The article raises the possibility that Lp(a) is a marker rather than a cause of heart disease, though many researchers are not ready to abandon the hypothesis.

  • Novartis Lp(a) outcome trial did not show benefit
  • Possibility raised: Lp(a) may be a marker, not a cause
  • Many researchers still support the Lp(a) hypothesis

But the failure of the Novartis study raised an uncomfortable possibility: Maybe Lp(a) is not a cause of heart disease, but instead is a marker of heart disease risk, like white hair is a mark of aging.

If Lp(a) is a marker, though, what is it marking? Is there another cause of heart disease that no one has thought of?

That is “the nagging question we are all pondering,” said Dr. David Maron, president of the American Society for Preventive Cardiology.

There may not be an easy answer, either. “Maybe we were fooled into thinking this would be simple,” Dr. Alexander said.

Still, many researchers are not ready to throw out the Lp(a) hypothesis, saying that there might be other explanations for the failure and that they need to take a close look at the study data. Pelacarsen (Lp(a)HORIZON) and ziltivekimab (ZEUS) cut their targets but not heart events

Nov 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Lp(a)

Shared summary of two NEJM trials. Pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) sharply but event rates were 12.3% vs 12.1% on placebo, and ziltivekimab cut CRP and IL-6 but events were 17.6% vs 17.7%. The post notes prior biomarker failures (torcetrapib, dalcetrapib), and that both trials enrolled people with existing disease and so do not prove Lp(a) or inflammation are not causal.

Tsimikas: Lp(a) risk can be managed with LDL under 65 and tight risk-factor control

Louis quotes Sam Tsimikas, who calls Lp(a)HORIZON a success because it suggests Lp(a) risk can be reduced by keeping LDL-C at or below 65, blood pressure at 120 or lower, controlling other risk factors and taking proven medicines. Cleveland Clinic: pelacarsen did not cut CV events in Lp(a)HORIZON (12.3% vs 12.1%)

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Lp(a)

Louis shares the Cleveland Clinic press release on Lp(a)HORIZON, a 797-site randomized trial in 8,322 patients with prior CVD and Lp(a) of at least 70 mg/dL. The primary endpoint occurred in 12.3% on pelacarsen versus 12.1% on placebo after a median 57 months. Senior author Steven Nissen said a single neutral trial does not prove lack of benefit from lowering Lp(a).
