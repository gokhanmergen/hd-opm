Butter and cream may differ from cheese and yogurt in how saturated fat affects health

Gokhan Can · Feb 08, 2026 · ♥ 17 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Meat, eggs & dairy
AI summary & key facts

Admin opinion that free saturated fat in butter and cream behaves differently from protein-bound saturated fat in cheese and yogurt: avoid butter and cream, while whole-fat cheese and yogurt look more benign if they do not raise cholesterol much. No sources are cited.

  • Butter and cream: avoid as much as possible
  • Whole-fat cheese and yogurt: appear more benign if LDL does not rise

Free saturated fat as those found in butter and cream have uniquely different biological effects from protein bound saturated fats found in cheese and yogurt.

Butter & cream -> avoid as much as possible.

Whole-fat cheese & yogurt -> appear much more benign, perhaps even beneficial if they don't raise your cholesterol significantly.

Full-fat yogurt lowered ApoB 4 mg/dL while butter raised it 10 mg/dL in a crossover trial

Jan 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 12 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that non-fermented dairy saturated fat strongly raises LDL and ApoB while fermentation attenuates this. In a randomized crossover trial, full-fat yogurt reduced ApoB while low-fat yogurt with butter increased it, likely due to milk fat globule membrane.

Full-fat yogurt lowered ApoB while yogurt with butter raised it in a crossover trial

Apr 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 24 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis summarizes a randomized crossover study that matched saturated fat in full-fat yogurt versus low-fat yogurt mixed with butter for 4 weeks each. The butter version raised ApoB while full-fat yogurt lowered it, which the authors attribute to the milk fat globule membrane (MFGM) that stays intact in yogurt but is lost in butter. This may partly explain yogurt's favorable epidemiology.
