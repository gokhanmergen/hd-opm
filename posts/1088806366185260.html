Low-fat vegan diet improved blood sugar and liver fat without calorie restriction

Oct 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A human trial covered by Lifespan.io found that a low-fat plant-based diet improved glucose control, reduced liver fat and lowered skin AGEs even without calorie restriction. The poster adds that Mediterranean, keto and low-fat vegan diets all reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity, and argues the common mechanism is loss of visceral fat.

Pancreatic fat loss can restore insulin secretion, per Roy Taylor's randomized trial

The poster argues that the pancreatic failure behind type 2 diabetes, including loss of first-phase insulin secretion, can be reversed by losing fat in the pancreas. He cites Professor Roy Taylor's randomized trial in humans, and the shared text explains how loss of first-phase insulin secretion causes the post-meal glucose spike.

Pancreatic fat drives beta-cell dysfunction and can reverse with weight loss in diabetes

Aug 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a post describing pancreatic fat as a key cause of beta-cell dysfunction in type 2 diabetes, citing Roy Taylor's work at Newcastle. Fat clears from the liver within weeks and from the pancreas over months of dietary change, with beta-cell function partly or fully returning.

In a small randomized trial, around half of participants achieved remission within 3 months and stopped medication, maintained over a year. Average weight loss was about 5.9 kg (13 lb).

Low-carb and whole-food high-carb diets cleared liver and visceral fat equally in an RCT

Louis explains a randomized trial comparing three isocaloric diets over one year. All three cleared liver fat, and a whole-food higher-carb diet with intact whole grains worked as well as low carb. He draws the practical point that whole grains and fruit are fine for liver fat loss.
