Video on how blood pressure targets evolved: ideal SBP under 120, drug targets under 130

Shares Dr Stanfield's video on the history of blood pressure targets and why ideal systolic BP is now under 120 while drug therapy targets under 130. The admin adds that potassium supplementation took his SBP to 107, from ~140 five years earlier and mid-120s before potassium (anecdote).

  • Ideal SBP <120; drug therapy target <130
  • Admin anecdote: SBP 107 after potassium, mid-120s before, ~140 five years ago

This wonderful video explains how blood pressure targets changed over time. And why the most ideal is SBP today is less than 120, while drug based therapies target less than 130 (some people don't feel great at 120).

PS: I started supplementing with potassium recently and got a shockingly-low SBP reading of 107 in the last doctor's visit. I will honestly take it! Feels great vs the 140 average I was getting ~5 years back. Before potassium, I think I was in mid 120ies.
