Low omega-3 index (below 4%) linked to mortality risk like smoking, observational data

Louis Nervegna · Sep 05, 2025 · ♥ 12 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a FoundMyFitness post claiming that a low omega-3 index carries mortality risk like smoking and that about 2 g/day of EPA plus DHA raises the index from 4% to 8%. He cautions that the research is primarily observational.

  • Omega-3 index below 4% linked to mortality risk comparable to smoking
  • Nearly 90% of Americans fall short of EPA and DHA needs (per post)
  • About 2 g/day EPA+DHA raises omega-3 index from 4% to 8%, associated with a 5-year longer life expectancy

This research is primarily observational, but it is compelling.
