Peter Attia's 10 jerky sticks daily means over 5x average processed meat intake

Louis Nervegna · Oct 31, 2025 · ♥ 27 reactions · 19 comments · original Facebook post
Meat, eggs & dairyCancer
AI summary & key facts

Louis calculates that a longevity influencer eating up to 10 cured venison jerky sticks daily consumes more than 5x the average American's processed red meat and nitrosamines. He places this in the highest-risk tier in human studies.

  • Over 5x the average American's processed red meat intake
  • Cured meat is classified by the WHO as carcinogenic

He claims to consume 10 cured red meat jerky sticks per day.

He co-owns a company that sells them: ↪ 'Uncured' and 'nitrate-free' processed meats can carry as many or more nitrosamines

I calculated that he was consuming over 5x the amount of processed red meat as the average American.

This much cured meat translates into more than 5x the amount of carcinogenic nitrosamines than the typical American consumes in a day— which puts him very far into the highest risk tier in human studies on processed red meat and cancer.

And the average American already consumes way too much processed red meat.

If you’re going to eat red meat, don’t eat this carcinogenic garbage. Eat fresh unprocessed meat like steak, hamburger, pork chops, uncured venison, etc. Or better yet, choose seafood. Or poultry, as the next best choice of animal flesh.

Let’s do the math…

The WHO estimates that every 50g of cured meat eaten per day raises colon cancer risk by 18%:

who.intCarcinogenicity of red and processed meatwho.int

The venison jerky sticks that Peter Attia’s company sells are 28g. He eats 10 per day, so that’s 280g total.

So according to the WHO data, he is raising his risk for colon cancer by (280g/50g)*18% = 100.8%.

He is doubling his colon cancer risk just from the venison jerky sticks alone, that he eats every day.

That’s not even counting all the other processed meat that he eats during the day.
