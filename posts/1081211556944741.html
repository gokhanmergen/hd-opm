Opinion: replace carbs with nuts and eat eggs for breakfast

Gokhan Can · Aug 02, 2025 · ♥ 31 reactions · 47 comments · original Facebook post
Low-carb, keto & carnivoreNuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsMeat, eggs & dairy
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan describes his preferred diet of eggs, limited meat and vegetables, nuts and cheese, and claims fat burning supports mitochondrial respiration. It is personal opinion with no cited studies.

One of the most consequential (beneficial) dietary interventions anyone can make is to replace carbs in their diet with nuts. Carbs unfortunately tend to be mostly refined in western societies. You practically cannot eat carbs outside and avoid refined. Instead if one can forgo carbs as much as possible and instead focus on nuts, you basically get the best of both worlds.

Also, eggs by and large have been proven innocent. I consider any form of egg as an excellent breakfast. I believe keeping the fat burning on for as long as possible is supportive of mithochondrial respiration (vs feeding carbs which unfortunately can shuttle into glycolysis).

I find eggs (breakfast) + limited meat/veggies (lunch) + mostly nuts and some cheese (dinner) to be the diet where I feel my best. I think I have to cheat and eat carbs every 1-2 days. But aside from that, the more strictly I stick to this regimen, the better I feel metabolically.

PS: I control my LDL via low dose Rosuvastatin (2.5mg/day). And I lost something like 13% body weight on a GLP-1 drug. But I still find this diet highly beneficial. Image by Google Gemini.
