SCOT-HEART: CT angiography cut 5-year coronary death or MI risk 41% in chest pain patients

Jan 03, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 18 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summarises SCOT-HEART (4,146 patients with stable chest pain, standard care vs standard care plus coronary CTA). CTA guidance lowered coronary death or nonfatal MI to 2.3% from 3.9%, mainly by directing aspirin and statin use to patients with plaque. Total procedures were similar by year 5; results apply to chest-pain patients only.

JACC review: rapid plaque progression precedes rupture and LDL below 70 mg/dL halts it

Jan 02, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summarises a 2019 JACC review arguing plaque progression is a necessary, modifiable step between subclinical atherosclerosis and MI, and that preventive strategy should shift toward imaging and aggressively treating subclinical disease. It reports data from PROSPECT, PARADIGM, GLAGOV and PESA showing progression stops at LDL <70 mg/dL and regression needs lower.

Vitamin C has not reduced heart attacks or strokes and plaque data are mixed

Apr 12, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Admin summarises that vitamin C alone has been tested widely without reducing hard outcomes (MACE), and plaque-reversal data are mixed: supportive in animals, better/worse/no effect in human trials. He does not advise it as a heart-disease cure, though physiological doses appear safe; he takes 250 mg/day to avoid deficiency. Links an (largely AI-generated, author-vetted) summary document.

Statins cut deaths 31% in people 80+, showing absolute benefit depends on baseline risk

Apr 02, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 5 Statins

Quotes Dr Carvalho on a new analysis in people aged 80 or older: statin users had a 31% lower relative risk of dying and, as stated, a 17% lower absolute risk. The key principle is that absolute benefit depends on control-group risk, so it looks small in young people over the short term but grows with time, age or illness.

TARGET-D: tailored vitamin D dosing missed its main endpoint but cut heart attack 52%

Nov 10, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 25 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises TARGET-D (630 adults with acute coronary syndrome, ~4.2 years): doses adjusted to reach 40-80 ng/mL did not significantly reduce the composite of death, heart failure hospitalization or stroke, though heart attack risk was 52% lower. Authors call for validation to rule out data mining.

VESALIUS-CV: Repatha cut first heart attack or stroke 25% in high-risk primary prevention

Nov 08, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 3 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summarises VESALIUS-CV in 12,000+ high-risk patients without prior MI or stroke (median age 66, LDL-C >=90 mg/dL on therapy). Adding evolocumab cut 3-point MACE 25% over ~4.5 years, with LDL-C reduced about 55% to a median 45 mg/dL versus 109 mg/dL on placebo.
