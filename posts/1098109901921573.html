Doctor's path to halving ApoB and triglycerides with rosuvastatin plus ezetimibe

Gokhan Can · Aug 25, 2025 · ♥ 9 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsLp(a)LDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Summary of a video where a doctor documents his own bloodwork: diet and exercise first, then statin trials (simvastatin, atorvastatin, rosuvastatin) after finding high Lp(a). Low-dose rosuvastatin plus ezetimibe with weight loss was the most effective and tolerable combination.

  • Final regimen: low-dose rosuvastatin plus ezetimibe
  • ApoB and triglycerides cut roughly in half with continued weight loss

In the video "Doctor Reveals: My FULL Bloodwork on statins," Dr. Gil Carvalho transparently documents his personal four-year journey to optimize his cardiovascular health after a stressful family crisis led to his "worst bloods" ever, including high triglycerides, cholesterol, and ApoB.

He details a methodical process of first using lifestyle changes (diet and exercise) to lose weight and improve his numbers, and then, upon discovering a high genetic risk factor in his Lp(a), experimenting with various statins to further lower his ApoB.

Dr. Carvalho shares the specific results and side effects he experienced with simvastatin, atorvastatin, and rosuvastatin, ultimately finding the most effective and tolerable solution by combining a low dose of rosuvastatin with ezetimibe.

This final regimen, coupled with continued weight loss, successfully cut his ApoB and triglycerides in half and reduced his estimated lifetime risk of a heart attack or stroke by nearly two-thirds, from 26% down to 9.7%.

My thoughts: This is how you do bloodwork optimization. Kudos!
