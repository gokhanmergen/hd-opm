AHA: BMI is a screening tool, and small steps like moving more help if risk is higher

Louis Nervegna · Aug 28, 2025 · ♥ 5 reactions · 13 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatGuidelines, policy & public health
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares an AHA statement describing BMI as a screening tool for weight-related heart and stroke risk and recommending small steps like moving more and sitting less.

#bmiHD #obesityHD #weightlossHD

Shared from American Heart Association · original post

Body mass index, or BMI, is a screening tool to identify whether an adult is at a healthy weight or at risk for heart diseases or stroke. If based on your height and weight you are at moderate or high risk, losing weight and keeping it off can help. Start with small steps, like moving more and sitting less every day.
