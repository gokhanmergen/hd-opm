Keto beat Mediterranean and low-fat diets on liver fat and glycemia at equal weight loss

Aug 27, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Petersen et al. (Cell Metabolism, 2026) fed 42 adults with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver all meals in a controlled trial until ~10% weight loss. All diets improved muscle insulin sensitivity and body composition, but the very-low-carb ketogenic diet gave clearly larger improvements in liver metabolic function, intrahepatic fat and glycemic control.

Dietary fiber as a longevity intervention: 15-30% lower mortality at 25-29 g/day

Dec 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 12 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Long summary arguing fiber is a bioactive longevity tool. The WHO-commissioned Reynolds 2019 meta-analysis found 15-30% lower all-cause mortality at 25-29 g/day, with benefits continuing per extra 8 g; newer NHANES data suggest diminishing returns past ~30 g/day. It describes mechanisms including butyrate-driven epigenetic effects and gut barrier reinforcement against toxins such as microplastics.

Why elevated cholesterol harms: ApoB particles drive plaque and inflammatory aging

Nov 09, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Explains that cholesterol is essential, but too many ApoB-containing particles like LDL get trapped in artery walls and drive plaque, and high circulating cholesterol may push bone-marrow stem cells toward inflammatory myeloid lineages. Keeping ApoB/LDL low is described as a powerful lever for arterial health.

An advocacy-style essay arguing that glyphosate should be avoided. It cites the 2015 IARC classification as probably carcinogenic to humans, based on limited human evidence of non-Hodgkin lymphoma, sufficient animal evidence and strong mechanistic evidence. Not specific to heart disease.

Strategies for lowering TMAO: diet, supplements and repurposed drugs

A written review of how TMAO forms from choline and carnitine via gut bacteria and the liver, and how to lower it. Gokhan says TMAO is not his top concern but worth knowing; he mentions a small human berberine trial that lowered TMAO and plaque.
