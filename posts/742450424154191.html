COSMOS: 3 years of daily multivitamin slowed cognitive decline by 60% in older adults

AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes the COSMOS randomized trial, in which a basic RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver) taken for three years slowed cognitive decline in healthy adults over 65. Benefits were relatively more pronounced in participants with significant cardiovascular disease, a group already at higher risk of cognitive impairment.

  • Centrum Silver for 3 years, adults over 65
  • ~60% slowing of cognitive decline, about 1.8 years
  • Larger benefit in people with significant cardiovascular disease
  • Run at Brigham and Women's Hospital, Harvard and Wake Forest

Taking a basic RDA level multivitamin (Centrum Silver) for 3 years slowed cognitive decline in healthy older adults (>65yo) by 60%.

This was a large randomized controlled trial (COSMOS) run at Brigham and Women’s Hospital and Harvard / Wake Forest Medical Schools.

“The researchers estimated that three years of multivitamin supplementation roughly translated to a 60% slowing of cognitive decline (about 1.8 years). The benefits were relatively more pronounced in participants with significant cardiovascular disease, which is important because these individuals are already at increased risk for cognitive impairment and decline.”
