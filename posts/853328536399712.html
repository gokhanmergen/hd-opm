Under 10% of heart attacks are now fatal, but out-of-hospital cardiac arrest is ~90% fatal

Louis Nervegna · Aug 16, 2024 · ♥ 8 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Heart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

Modern treatment means less than 10% of heart attacks result in death, versus up to half of victims decades ago. However, a cardiac arrest or widowmaker heart attack outside a hospital still has about a 90% chance of death, and cardiac arrest (heart stops) is distinct from a heart attack.

  • Under 10% of heart attacks are now fatal
  • Decades ago heart attacks killed up to half of victims within days
  • ~90% death rate for cardiac arrest or widowmaker MI outside hospital
  • Most heart attacks do not cause cardiac arrest

Today, less than 10% of heart attacks result in death.

This reflects the huge advancements that have been made in treating heart attacks in the last several decades.

However, there is still about a 90% chance that a cardiac arrest or widowmaker heart attack will result in death, if it occurs outside of a hospital.

Note that a cardiac arrest is not the same as a heart attack.

A cardiac arrest is when your heart completely stops beating.

Most heart attacks do not result is cardiac arrest— but when they do, they are usually deadly.
