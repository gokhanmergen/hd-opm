Range restriction may explain why ApoB showed no link to plaque progression in Keto-CTA

Louis Nervegna · Apr 22, 2025 · ♥ 4 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyLDL, ApoB & lipid panelLow-carb, keto & carnivore
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares an illustration suggesting that the lack of association between ApoB and plaque progression in the LMHR Keto-CTA study may be an artifact: sampling a small number of extreme points makes a real association look absent. This is a statistical explanation, taken from an LMHR group post.

  • Keto-CTA found no ApoB-plaque progression association
  • Small sample from an extreme range can hide a real association

This is a possible explanation for why ApoB (LDL) was not associated with plaque progression in the LMHR study (Keto-CTA).

The red plot on the right is obtained by taking a small sampling of extreme points from the black plot on the left.

This sampling makes the association appear to vanish, but this is in fact just an artifact of the small sample size and the extreme location from which it is taken.
