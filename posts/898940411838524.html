The nocebo effect can produce real symptoms from believing a pill is harmful

Louis Nervegna · Oct 27, 2024 · ♥ 8 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post
Sleep, stress & social healthStatins
AI summary & key facts

Louis explains that the nocebo effect is when expecting harm from something one takes generates real physical symptoms from the mind alone. He notes mind-body interventions can also be used as treatments. No studies are cited.

The nocebo effect is extremely powerful.

This is when your mind creates physical symptoms in response to the thought that you are taking a something (e.g. a pill) that is somehow harming you. These physical symptoms are very real, but in the case of the nocebo effect, they are generated entirely by the mind. Not by the thing you are taking.

The nocebo effect is an example of a mind body intervention. These mind body interventions can be exploited as effective treatments as well.
