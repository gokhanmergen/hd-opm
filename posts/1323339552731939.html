Stem-cell heart patch (BioVAT) raised ejection fraction at 3 months in phase 1-2 trial

Louis Nervegna · Jun 20, 2026 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Heart rhythm & heart failure
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a NEJM interim report of a phase 1-2 trial in which stem-cell-derived engineered tissue was placed on the surface of failing human hearts. At 3 months it was associated with thicker target heart wall, higher ejection fraction and better quality-of-life scores, though every patient had at least one adverse event and longer follow-up is needed.

  • Phase 1-2 interim analysis of BioVAT-HF
  • Increased target heart-wall thickness, LVEF and KCCQ-OSS at 3 months
  • All patients had at least one adverse event
