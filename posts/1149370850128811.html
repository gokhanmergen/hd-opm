Green Mediterranean diet cut visceral fat about 14% in an 18-month randomized trial

Oct 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A shared summary of an 18-month RCT comparing a healthy diet, a Mediterranean diet and a Green Mediterranean diet enriched with green tea and Wolffia globosa (mankai duckweed). The Green Mediterranean diet reduced visceral fat by about 14%, the largest effect of the three arms. Visceral fat drives inflammation and metabolic dysfunction.

Low-fat vegan diet improved blood sugar and liver fat without calorie restriction

Oct 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A human trial covered by Lifespan.io found that a low-fat plant-based diet improved glucose control, reduced liver fat and lowered skin AGEs even without calorie restriction. The poster adds that Mediterranean, keto and low-fat vegan diets all reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity, and argues the common mechanism is loss of visceral fat.

Pancreatic fat drives beta-cell dysfunction and can reverse with weight loss in diabetes

Aug 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a post describing pancreatic fat as a key cause of beta-cell dysfunction in type 2 diabetes, citing Roy Taylor's work at Newcastle. Fat clears from the liver within weeks and from the pancreas over months of dietary change, with beta-cell function partly or fully returning.

High insulin tells the liver to make cholesterol, tying insulin resistance to high LDL

Aug 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that insulin signals the liver to produce cholesterol, one reason insulin-resistant people tend to have high LDL. Losing visceral and especially liver fat to correct insulin resistance should therefore bring LDL down naturally. Supported by a Mass General Brigham explainer.

Fasting reported to target visceral and liver fat first, improving insulin resistance

Jul 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 7 Fasting & meal timing

Louis shares a BoxLife Magazine interview with Dr. Alan Goldhamer, who says supervised extended fasting preferentially mobilizes visceral fat, which kept declining during refeeding. This is claimed by Louis to explain fasting's effect on insulin resistance; the source is a magazine interview, not a peer-reviewed paper.

Semaglutide-driven visceral fat loss slowed epigenetic aging clocks in a 32-week trial

Louis highlights a FoundMyFitness note that in a 32-week semaglutide trial in adults with excess visceral fat, multiple epigenetic aging clocks showed improvement. The proposed mechanism is less adipose-driven inflammatory and metabolic signaling.
