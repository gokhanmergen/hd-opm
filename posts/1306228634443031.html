GLP-1 drugs don’t steal your muscle. Weight loss does.
A 2026 meta-analysis in Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism compared lean mass changes between GLP-1 drugs and intensive lifestyle intervention in adults with overweight or obesity. The finding: the proportion of weight lost as lean mass is broadly comparable between the two approaches — roughly 20–26% regardless of whether you’re on semaglutide, tirzepatide, or dieting hard.
The concern about muscle loss on these drugs is real. But it’s a weight loss problem, not a drug problem.
The fix is the same either way: resistance training, adequate protein, and body composition monitoring.
