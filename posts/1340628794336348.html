Vitamin D is not a panacea: big trials missed primary endpoints

Louis Nervegna · Jul 11, 2026 · ♥ 13 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Louis says he takes vitamin D, but large trials failed primary endpoints, with some secondary benefits. The strongest human evidence is for autoimmune disease protection.

  • Big trials missed primary endpoints
  • Strongest evidence for autoimmune protection

I take Vitamin D, but it is definitely not the panacea that influencers make it out to be.

The big trials all failed to meet their primary endpoints— although there were some benefits seen in certain secondary endpoints, subgroups, post hoc analyses, etc.

Betting your life on vitamin D is a big mistake.

That being said, some of the strongest evidence in humans is for a protective effect against autoimmune diseases. This is the main reason that I take it. See these posts:

Vitamin K2 slows coronary calcium mainly in severe calcification; event benefit unproven

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 16 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Review of vitamin K2 (with or without vitamin D) and CAC. A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n=1,533) found a -17.3 Agatston units/year pooled difference. The largest RCT (389 men, 720 ug MK-7 + 1000 IU D vs placebo for 2 years) missed significance overall but slowed CAC at baseline CAC >=400; some CKD trials were null. Not yet a standard therapy.

TARGET-D: tailored vitamin D dosing missed its main endpoint but cut heart attack 52%

Nov 10, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 25 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises TARGET-D (630 adults with acute coronary syndrome, ~4.2 years): doses adjusted to reach 40-80 ng/mL did not significantly reduce the composite of death, heart failure hospitalization or stroke, though heart attack risk was 52% lower. Authors call for validation to rule out data mining.
