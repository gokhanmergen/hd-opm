TOGETHER metformin trial retracted; COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6 still support metformin

Louis Nervegna · Mar 08, 2026 · ♥ 5 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

The TOGETHER trial of metformin for early COVID-19 was retracted by its authors in October 2025 after an analysis error led to premature stopping. Louis notes that two other large randomized trials, COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6, independently showed metformin lowers long-COVID risk, and hopes for a corrected re-publication.

  • TOGETHER retracted October 2025 (Lancet Regional Health-Americas)
  • Error led to early stopping; primary endpoints were not met early after reanalysis
  • COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6 results not called into question

The TOGETHER trial, one of the multiple randomized trials that demonstrated a protective effect of metformin against long covid, was retracted from publication by its authors in October 2025.

It looks like there was an unintentional error in the data analysis that led to the trial being stopped early (successfully). After reanalysis, the trial should not have been stopped, as primary endpoints were not met early.

It is important to point out that two other large randomized trials, COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6, have both independently demonstrated that metformin lowers the risk of long covid by a substantial amount. These results have never been called into question.

This is a sad situation. There appears to have been a trend towards benefit in TOGETHER, but stopping early has prevented the authors from determining the true result. Hopefully, we will see a re-publication with updated analysis and findings that discuss this trial in the context of the others that have been published.

In the meantime, results from the successful COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6 trials were published here:
