Admin's experience adding ezetimibe, pitavastatin and Repatha after statin intolerance

Gokhan Can · Jan 08, 2026 · ♥ 31 reactions · 24 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsPCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs
AI summary & key facts

Personal story: atorvastatin felt bad at any dose; rosuvastatin was uneventful and dropped LDL from ~155 to ~75 mg/dL; ezetimibe added in 2025; the past week he switched to pitavastatin 1 mg plus Repatha and feels good. He cites speculated benefits (anti-inflammatory, PPAR-gamma) without sources; anecdotal.

  • Atorvastatin 20 mg (2020) felt bad at all doses
  • Rosuvastatin took LDL from ~155 to ~75 mg/dL, minor rise in blood sugar and fasting insulin
  • Switched rosuvastatin 5 mg to pitavastatin 1 mg and ezetimibe to Repatha

Everytime I add a new drug or supplement, I get both excited and slightly terrified -- will I tolerate it? Is it going to make me feel miserable? What if, what if...

I'm extremely pleased to report that combined LDL lowering with Ezetimibe and/or Repatha feels great.

My journey started with Atorvastatin 20mg in 2020. I tried every possible dose, and I just hated it. Made me feel spaced out and awkward. Made me feel off at any dose.

My cardiologist recommended Rosuvastatin after some time. And it was completely uneventful regarding side effects, nothing good, nothing bad, with the exception of minor increase in blood sugar and fasting insulin. Crashed my LDL from ~155 to ~75 mg/dL. I still was very sceptic and used it at 2.5mg daily dose most of the time. Upped it to 5mg for 1-2 yrs.

Come 2025, I was reading exciting things about Ezetimibe and "extreme" LDL lowering. Lowers inflammation, might lower insulin resistance, lower for longer is better, right? Not just that I was fascinated to imagine that better blood flow is ought to be good for practically anything. This stuff has to be anti-aging!

My cardiologist was very supportive (despite me being a non-classical case of high risk). He strongly encouraged me to target lower LDL values for longevity.

After adding Ezetimibe, the biggest suprise was positive sexual side effects. I'm not going get into too much, made a detailed scientific post about it earlier. Suffice it to say when your vascular function improves it improves everywhere. (Mild but noticeable.)

This past week I switched from

  • Rosuvastatin 5mg -> Pitavastatin 1mg.
  • Ezetimibe -> Repatha.

The early indications are that the switch feels great.

Pitavastatin is extremely anti inflammatory. It has the side effect of activating PPAR-gamma, which I believe is a life extension pathway. It's anti cancer (anti blood cancers in particular). It feels like low dose Rosuvastatin plus low dose Pioglitazone combined (both of these molecules showed plaque reversal individually. Combination is ought be the bomb, imo). I'm super excited about the switch. Also improves your blood sugar.

Going back to Repatha, I injected yesterday. And woke up feeling great. Very refreshed and energetic despite doing nothing special. It feels great! My worries have been unjustified so far. Let's see how it goes long term.

Just reporting about my progress. Because I like to share :) And you too might be in a similar situation. Wishing you the best of health.
