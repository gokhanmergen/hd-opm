Unhealthy plant-based diets raise CHD risk, healthy ones lower it, in Harvard cohorts

Louis Nervegna · Dec 26, 2024 · ♥ 13 reactions · 12 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsCarbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains
AI summary & key facts

Louis stresses that not all plant-based diets are healthy. In the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study, a plant-based index rich in healthy plant foods was linked to substantially lower CHD risk while one emphasizing sugary drinks, refined grains, potatoes and sweets was linked to higher risk, worse than the standard diet baseline.

  • Higher healthy plant-based diet index: substantially lower CHD risk
  • Unhealthy plant-based index: higher CHD risk
  • Unhealthy items: sugar-sweetened beverages, refined grains, potatoes/fries, sweets

Not all vegan and plant-based diets are healthy.

Unhealthy plant-based diets high in sugar-sweetened beverages, refined grains, potatoes/fries, sweets, are associated with increased risk of chronic diseases and mortality— worse than the standard American diet that includes animal foods (the baseline diet in the study).

The results on the slide come from the Harvard Nurses Study and Harvard Physicians Followup Study:

Satija et al, J Am Coll Cardiol 2017

Paper · J Am Coll Cardiol · 2017Healthful and Unhealthful Plant-Based Diets and the Risk of Coronary Heart Disease in U.S. Adults.Conclusion: Higher intake of a plant-based diet index rich in healthier plant foods is associated with substantially lower CHD risk, whereas a plant-based diet index that emphasizes less-healthy plant foods is associated with higher CHD risk.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
