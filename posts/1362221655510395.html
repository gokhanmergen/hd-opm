CDC NHANES: 54% of Americans have an Omega-3 Index below 4%

Louis Nervegna · Aug 06, 2026 · ♥ 1 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsCardiovascular disease: the big picture
AI summary & key facts

First nationally representative Omega-3 Index data from NHANES show 54% of Americans below 4% and nearly 98% below the optimal 8%. Louis suggests this may raise CVD risk.

  • 54% below 4%
  • ~98% below 8%

Over half of Americans are deficient in omega 3 to the degree that it might raise risk for CVD.

The results?
