Pooled blinded trials: statins cause only about 1 in 15 reported muscle aches

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 12 Statins

A pooled analysis of 19 blinded trials with 123,940 people found that over about four years, 27.1 of 100 on a statin reported muscle pain or weakness versus 26.6 of 100 on placebo. This implies that the vast majority of muscle complaints on statins are not caused by the drug, consistent with a nocebo effect.

CoQ10 shuttles electrons in the mitochondrial chain, relevant to statin users

May 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 13 Supplements & vitamins

A shared explanation that CoQ10 is the only mobile electron carrier between Complexes I/II and III of the mitochondrial chain, making it central to ATP production rather than just an antioxidant. Posted under statin and CoQ10 tags.

CoQ10 supplements do not reliably prevent statin muscle symptoms

Apr 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 6 Statins

Statins lower CoQ10 in blood and immune cells but barely in muscle, and the best-designed trials show no benefit of CoQ10 for statin muscle symptoms. A shared summary notes supplementation may not raise muscle CoQ10.

CoQ10 curbed Alzheimer's-like damage spread in mice; human CoQ10 trials disappointed

Nov 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 3 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a mouse study (Nature Aging) in which mitochondria-derived vesicles from Alzheimer's-model brains impaired memory in healthy mice and daily CoQ10 injections cut their release. Human CoQ10 trials in Alzheimer's were mostly disappointing, but it might still work as early prevention. Louis stresses mouse studies reveal mechanisms, not proof of benefit in humans.

CoQ10 improved tinnitus severity and sleep in presbycusis patients in a small 2025 RCT

Oct 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 5 Supplements & vitamins

A 2025 double-blind randomized trial in the Iranian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology tested coenzyme Q10 added to routine care in patients with tinnitus due to age-related hearing loss. It reported reduced tinnitus disability and improved sleep. It is not related to heart health.

CoQ10 trial in chronic kidney failure: 36% vs 90% needed dialysis

Oct 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 1 Kidney health

The post highlights an older randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of coenzyme Q10 in chronic renal failure in which far fewer CoQ10 patients needed dialysis than placebo patients. The poster stresses he is not claiming CoQ10 reverses kidney disease, only that the research exists. The linked paper is from 2000, so confirmation in larger recent trials matters.
