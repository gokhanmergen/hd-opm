Nattokinase and PAI-1: how it may work and why PAI-1 matters

Gokhan Can · Dec 03, 2023 · ♥ 14 reactions · 16 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBlood vessels, circulation & endothelium
AI summary & key facts

Notes that nattokinase appears to act by inhibiting PAI-1, a marker linking thrombosis, inflammation, metabolic disorder and aging. People who genetically make less PAI-1 live about 7 years longer, and PAI-1 inhibition helped fatty liver and dyslipidemia in preclinical work.

  • PAI-1 elevated predicts adverse outcomes in MI, stroke, cancer and diabetes
  • Genetically low PAI-1 associated with about 7 years longer life (2017)
  • PAI-1 inhibition activates FGF21 5-fold and reduces PCSK9 (2021)

Nattokinase <-> PAI-1

  • How Nattokinase does its magic (2017): Inhibition of PAI-1.
  • Elevated PAI-1 predicts adverse outcomes in many conditions - MI, stroke, cancer, diabetes, pregnancy complications. It is considered a promising biomarker linking thrombosis, inflammation, metabolic disorders, and aging (2010).
  • People who genetically doesn’t make too much PAI-1 live on average 7 yrs longer (2017).
  • Metabolic Syndrome is associated with an overproduction of PAI-1 (2006).
  • Inhibiting PAI-1 significantly helps with fatty liver and dyslipidemia. Activates FGF21 5-fold — a longevity molecule. And reduces PCKS9 (2021).
  • Giving natto bacteria to worms makes the worms live longer (2023).
  • Several drugs are in the clinical pipeline to inhibit PAI-1, but high dose nattokinase might be the safest option today.

Many more references and details in the blog post.
