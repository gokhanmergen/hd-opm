Cooking with extra virgin olive oil is fine: Lyon, PREDIMED and CORDIOPREV cut events

Louis Nervegna · Aug 12, 2024 · ♥ 22 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

The post says three long RCTs (Lyon Heart Study, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV) used extra virgin olive oil for nearly all cooking and all reduced cardiovascular events and deaths. The only caution is to stay below the oil's smoke point; extra light olive oil has smoke points up to 450F.

  • Three large RCTs: Lyon Heart Study, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV
  • Intervention groups cooked almost all food with EVOO
  • Extra light olive oil smoke point up to 450F

It is NOT true that it is unhealthy to cook with olive oil— even extra virgin olive oil.

There have been 3 large long-running randomized controlled trials testing olive oil verses heart disease, as part of a traditional Mediterranean diet:

The Lyon Heart Study,
PREDIMED,
CORDIOPREV.

In all 3 studies, the intervention groups cooked almost ALL of their food with extra virgin olive oil. And in all 3 studies, there were major reductions in cardiovascular events and deaths.

facebook.com/share/p/mzK2J6Q4ZguyQuAp

The only caution is that you should not exceed the smoke point of the oil.

If you need to cook at very high temperatures, you can buy extra light olive oils that have smoke points as high as 450F (which is high enough even for deep frying).
