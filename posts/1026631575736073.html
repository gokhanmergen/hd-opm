8-hour eating window for 3 months aided weight loss regardless of timing, early RCT data

A Medscape item reporting preliminary randomized trial results that restricting eating to 8 hours a day for 3 months improved weight loss in people with overweight or obesity. Benefit persisted for at least a year and did not depend on when the eating window fell.
