Shared from BoxLife Magazine · original post

This randomized controlled trial led by Dr. Ben Levine showed that a 2-year program of just five hours of exercise per week reversed two decades of age-related heart stiffness in sedentary adults over 50.

Participants combined aerobic intervals—specifically the Norwegian 4×4 method—with strength training.

After two years, their hearts were more elastic, larger, and functioned like those of 30-year-olds.

The study challenges the notion that it’s too late to start exercising in midlife.

It outlines steps to replicate the regimen, from interval sessions to resistance work.
