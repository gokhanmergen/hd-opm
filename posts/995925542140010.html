There are at least 6 strategies that have been proven to reverse plaque in human randomized controlled trials:

  1. Major weight loss (e.g. 30lbs)

  1. Traditional mediterranean diet (with nuts) / DASH diet

↪ DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed soft plaque by about 40 mm3 in a year

↪ CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet shrank carotid plaque and cut recurrent events about 25%

↪ CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet reduced atherosclerosis versus low-fat diet over 7 years

↪ PREDIMED: nuts regressed carotid plaque and a nut-rich Mediterranean diet cut stroke 50%

  1. Statins, PCSK9 inhibitors, ezetimibe, and other LDL-lowering drugs

a. ↪ Six randomized trials show statins reduce total plaque volume 5-17% from baseline

b. ↪ Plaque volume begins to regress once LDL-C falls below about 90 mg/dL across RCTs

c. ↪ Japan-ACS: pitavastatin and atorvastatin both regressed coronary plaque by about 17-18%

d. ↪ Statin plaque regression RCTs: SATURN, REVERSAL, ASTEROID, COSMOS, JAPAN-ACS

e. ↪ Intensive statin reversed atherosclerosis on IVUS in a 2004 Cleveland Clinic-led trial

f. ↪ Meta-analysis: lipid-lowering therapy regresses coronary plaque (Li 2021, SATURN)

g. ↪ Plaque regression: about half at LDL near 80, 90% of regressable plaques below 60

h. ↪ Statin studies on plaque regression, with a link to a longer blog post

i. ↪ Repatha for 18 months reduced non-calcified plaque on CT angiography in a 47-patient study

j. ↪ YELLOW-III: statin plus evolocumab thickened fibrous caps over 30% in 6 months

k. ↪ YELLOW-III reversed total atheroma volume 4.4% in 6 months at LDL of about 39 mg/dL

  1. Lowering arterial inflammation with the drug Colchicine

↪ Colchicine 0.5 mg cut plaque volume by over 1% in one year on CT angiography (Budoff)

  1. Exercise

↪ Exercise, including HIIT, can reduce coronary plaque burden and add to LDL lowering

  1. High dose EPA from fish oil

↪ EVAPORATE: high-dose EPA reversed plaque in patients with triglycerides above 135 mg/dL

The Esselstyn and Ornish diets also seem to work for some people. But the published studies for the Esselstyn diet are not RCT’s. Ornish did publish one small RCT in the late 90’s showing plaque regression. This regression was similar to what was seen in the large PREDIMED and CORDIOPREV trials using the traditional Mediterranean diet with nuts (plus statins), and less than the DISCO-CT trial using a DASH diet (plus statins).

↪ Ornish trial: lifestyle program cut cardiac events, but plant-based diet was not a cure

The Ornish trial used invasive xray angiography to measure plaque, which has serious accuracy issues, as described here:

↪ Ornish trial's 'plaque reversal' may be x-ray angiography measurement error

There have been a total of 6 randomized controlled trials in humans that have demonstrated that diet can reverse arterial plaque:

Lifestyle Heart Trial (1990) - Ornish diet
Stars Trial (1992) - AHA diet
Scrip Trial (1994) - AHA diet
PREDIMED (2013) - Mediterranean diet
CORDIOPREV (2021) - Mediterranean diet
DISCO (2021) - DASH diet

They were each successful to varying degrees. This video goes through each of them, one by one:

↪ Six randomized diet trials showed plaque reversal, from Ornish to CORDIOPREV and DISCO

There are some supplements that might regress plaque as well, but the evidence comes either from small human studies or animal studies. It is a gamble. If you go this route, focus on things that are safe and won’t conflict with proven treatments.

↪ Plaque-stopping supplements rest on small, low-quality studies; gotu kola most plausible

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet shrank carotid plaque and cut recurrent events about 25%

Mar 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 13 ↩ 27 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes CORDIOPREV, an RCT of 1002 coronary patients comparing a Mediterranean diet rich in extra virgin olive oil with a low-fat diet. The Mediterranean diet reduced carotid intima-media thickness at 5 and 7 years while the low-fat diet showed no change, and also lowered carotid plaque height. He also cites roughly 25% fewer recurrent major cardiovascular events over 7 years, with benefits seen despite statin treatment and mean LDL-C below 90.

Six randomized diet trials showed plaque reversal, from Ornish to CORDIOPREV and DISCO

Louis lists the only six human RCTs in which diet reversed plaque: Lifestyle Heart Trial (Ornish diet), STARS and SCRIP (AHA diet), PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV (Mediterranean) and DISCO (DASH-style). Successful diets emphasized produce, legumes, whole grains, nuts, olive oil, fish and some dairy. They limited junk food, refined grains, butter, fatty red meat and heavy alcohol. He links each paper.

Louis highlights a Budoff-led CCTA trial in which low-dose colchicine reduced percent atheroma volume by over 1% in a year, with lower CRP and a slowing of calcified plaque. He contrasts this with the Keto-CTA study, where PAV rose 0.8% on a keto diet. Budoff notes it was a small study but supports colchicine in chronic stable coronary disease.
