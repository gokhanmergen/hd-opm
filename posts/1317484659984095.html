CARDIAC BOOST: A common diabetes drug slashed heart failure risk by 8x in patients carrying a genetic variant linked to cardiomyopathy, according to a new study in Nature Medicine.

Researchers analyzed over 12,000 adults with type 2 diabetes and found that dapagliflozin dramatically reduced hospitalization for heart failure in those with inherited gene variants.

The findings could eventually lead cardiologists to use genetic screening to identify high-risk patients and begin treatment before symptoms ever appear.

HCM, athlete's heart and LVH look alike on imaging but differ greatly in risk

Apr 03, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis explains HCM, athlete's heart and left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH) can look alike on EKG or ultrasound. HCM is usually genetic and serious, potentially causing sudden cardiac arrest; athlete's heart follows long-term intense exercise and is usually benign; LVH is usually due to uncontrolled hypertension or aortic stenosis. A person can have both HCM and athlete's heart.
