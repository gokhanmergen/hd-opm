Shared from Linus Pauling Institute · original post

We’re mourning the loss of a friend of the LPI and a giant in biochemistry and aging research.

Dr. Bruce Ames passed away on Oct. 5 at the age of 95. His legacy includes the development of a widely used test for carcinogens in food and consumer products. Dr. Ames was a mentor to several researchers at the Linus Pauling Institute, and in 2001 he received the LPI’s first “Prize for Health Research”.

To read more about Dr. Ames’ incredible career, see this video we produced in 2021 at lpi.pub/BruceAmes
