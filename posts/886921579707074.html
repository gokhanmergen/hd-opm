Rat study: extra-virgin avocado oil improved memory in an Alzheimer's model

Louis Nervegna · Oct 09, 2024 · ♥ 11 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Brain health & dementia
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a rodent study in which extra-virgin avocado oil improved memory, hippocampal biochemistry and oxidative stress in a rat Alzheimer's model. He notes avocado oil is rarely studied and that rodent Alzheimer's models often fail to predict human results.

  • Rat model (D-galactose, ovariectomized Wistar rats)
  • Improved memory and reduced oxidative stress
  • Louis: rodent Alzheimer's models have a poor track record in humans

There are very few studies on avocado oil, even in animals. Ordinarily I wouldn’t post a rodent study, but there are so few that it is worth mentioning— since this oil has recently become so popular.

(As a caveat, rodent models of human Alzheimer’s have a long track record of failing to predict what actually happens in humans.)

Research cited

  • Extra-virgin avocado oil improves cognitive impairment in D-galactose-induced Alzheimer's disease model on ovariectomized Wistar rat · animal · Biologia · 2024
Shared from Samvel Aleksanyan · original post

Extra-virgin avocado oil improved memory, enhanced hippocampal biochemistry, and reduced oxidative stress in a rat Alzheimer's model. These findings suggest it may help prevent or alleviate Alzheimer's-related cognitive decline.
