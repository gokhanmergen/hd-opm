With the same CAC score, fitter people have much lower heart attack risk over 20-25 years

Louis Nervegna · Aug 23, 2024 · ♥ 26 reactions · 14 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyExercise & fitness
AI summary & key facts

Sharing a clip of sports cardiologist Andre La Gerche, the post says that for any degree of coronary calcification, higher fitness goes with lower heart attack risk. Two people with the same CAC score differ substantially in long-term risk depending on whether they exercise.

  • Same CAC score: exerciser has much lower 20-25 year heart attack risk
  • Fitter people have lower risk at any degree of calcification

Given two people with the same calcium (CAC) score, the one who exercises has a much lower risk of having a heart attack over the next 20 to 25 years.

The fitter you are for any degree of calcification, the lower your heart attack risk.

25 statin trials: about 40% fewer heart attacks and strokes per 75 mg/dL LDL drop

Mar 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 37 · 💬 35 Statins

Louis shares a graph of all 25 statin RCTs up to 2016 (excluding those under 6 months or under 50 events), showing about 40% lower MACE per 75 mg/dL LDL reduction, with larger effects in the 7 primary prevention trials. In his own comments he argues calcified plaque is the stable endpoint of soft plaque, and that statins and exercise help by preventing new soft plaque, regressing it and stabilizing the remainder through calcification.

About 10% of people with a zero calcium score still have significant soft plaque

Nov 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis notes that roughly 10% of patients with a CAC of zero have significant soft plaque, and shares a primer explaining why a zero score does not exclude heart disease. CAC detects only calcified plaque while CT coronary angiography shows both calcified and non-calcified plaque and narrowing. A zero score still means very low event risk over the following years, but low is not none.

MESA: CAC under 100, hsCRP under 2 and low visceral fat may protect those with high Lp(a)

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 6 Lp(a)

Louis summarizes MESA findings that three markers seem to protect people with high Lp(a): CAC score below 100, hsCRP below 2 mg/L and low visceral fat. He warns MESA is an observational cohort that shows associations, not causality.
