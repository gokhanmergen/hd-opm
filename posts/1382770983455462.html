Video accuses Diary of a CEO of knowingly spreading health misinformation

Shares a Gil Carvalho (Nutrition Made Simple) video criticising the podcast for guest claims such as curing cancer with keto, discouraging fibre and fear-mongering about glucose spikes. It says the producers attach research PDFs or footnotes that contradict guests yet still promote the claims.

  • Claims cited: keto curing cancer, discouraging fiber, fear of normal glucose spikes

I greatly enjoyed watching this episode of Nutrition Made Simple.

In his video, Dr. Gil Carvalho exposes the popular podcast Diary of a CEO for consistently broadcasting dangerous health and nutrition misinformation to its massive audience in pursuit of virality and profit. Carvalho highlights egregious claims made by guests—such as curing cancer with a keto diet, discouraging fiber intake, and fear-mongering about normal glucose spikes—while revealing that the podcast's creators are fully aware these claims are false. He shows how they quietly attach deeply researched PDFs or brief on-screen footnotes that directly contradict their guests with actual scientific evidence, yet continue to feature the sensationalized claims prominently to drive views.
