p-tau217 blood test could predict timing of Alzheimer's symptoms within 3-4 years

Louis Nervegna · Feb 25, 2026 · ♥ 3 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaRisk assessment & lab tests
AI summary & key facts

Shared post about a Washington University clock model using plasma p-tau217 to estimate when symptoms will appear: about 20 years after rising at age 60, about 11 years after rising at age 80.

  • Accuracy within 3-4 years
  • Rise at 60: symptoms about age 80
  • Rise at 80: symptoms in about 11 years

#ptau217HD #alzheimersHD #dementiaHD

Shared from Viral Pulse · original post

The One blood draw. One protein. A countdown to Alzheimer’s, accurate within 3-4 years.

Scientists at Washington University built a “clock model” using a protein called p-tau217 that mirrors the silent buildup of damage in the brain long before memory loss starts.

If levels rise at age 60, symptoms appear around age 80. If they rise at age 80, symptoms show in about 11 years.

No brain scans needed. No spinal taps. Just blood.
More than 7 million Americans live with Alzheimer’s. This test could change how we fight it.
