Diabetes researchers cite fat in muscle and liver as the main cause of insulin resistance

Louis Nervegna · Oct 19, 2025 · ♥ 23 reactions · 13 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Louis says leading diabetes researchers increasingly agree that fat build-up inside muscle and liver is what causes insulin resistance in most people. Food types can make accumulation easier or harder, but the main cause is overall calorie overconsumption, and very lean people with no ectopic fat rarely develop insulin resistance whatever they eat.

  • Consensus view among cutting-edge diabetes researchers: ectopic fat in muscle and liver causes most insulin resistance
  • Main cause of the fat build-up is overconsumption of calories
  • Very lean people without liver or muscle fat are very unlikely to develop insulin resistance regardless of diet

It looks like some influencers are finally starting to catch up with the latest diabetes research…

If you go to a major diabetes research center and ask what causes insulin resistance in most people, they are likely going to answer:

Fat build-up inside muscles and liver.

There is now a growing consensus on this answer amongst scientists doing cutting-edge research on diabetes.

The kinds of foods that you eat can make it easier or harder to accumulate fat inside muscles and liver, but the ultimate cause of insulin resistance is the fat build-up itself.

And the main cause of this fat build-up is the overconsumption of calories, in general.

If you are very lean and have no fat in your muscles and liver, you are very unlikely to develop insulin resistance regardless of what you are eating.
