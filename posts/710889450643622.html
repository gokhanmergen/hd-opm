Regression and stabilization of atherogenic plaques, 2023

drive.google.comGoogle Drive filedrive.google.com

Plaque regression and stabilization may be the most crucial component in treating atherosclerosis, the leading cause of death worldwide. Therapies and interventions that demonstrate this ability offer a truly anti-atherosclerotic effect. The prevailing paradigm, which focuses on reducing MACE, could be revised to measure earlier stages of events if atherosclerosis is being modified from the outset. The future of plaque-modifying therapies is promising, and further research is encouraged.

Coronary Atherosclerotic Plaque Regression: JACC State-of-the-Art Review, 2022

Paper · Journal of the American College of Cardiology · 2022Coronary Atherosclerotic Plaque Regressionjacc.org

Topics covered:

  • Mechanisms of Plaque Formation and Natural History
  • Plaque Regression Strategies

A large body of evidence suggests that statins induce plaque regression in a dose-dependent manner and proportionally to reductions in LDL-C. IVUS has been the main modality used to assess changes in plaque burden, although several studies have begun to use CCTA in the last few years. In response to statins, fibrous and calcified plaque volumes appear to increase, whereas noncalcified, fibrofatty, and necrotic core volumes decrease.

JACC review: rapid plaque progression precedes rupture and LDL below 70 mg/dL halts it

Jan 02, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summarises a 2019 JACC review arguing plaque progression is a necessary, modifiable step between subclinical atherosclerosis and MI, and that preventive strategy should shift toward imaging and aggressively treating subclinical disease. It reports data from PROSPECT, PARADIGM, GLAGOV and PESA showing progression stops at LDL <70 mg/dL and regression needs lower.

LDL is necessary but not always sufficient for atherosclerosis, among 25+ risk factors

Mar 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 2 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis summarizes a Joel Kahn video: there are at least 25 major heart disease risk factors, all of which should be addressed, but LDL is special because it appears necessary for plaque to form. It is not always sufficient, since some people with very high LDL never develop plaque. No other risk factor has been shown to be necessary, which is why cardiologists focus on LDL.

SCOT-HEART: CT angiography cut 5-year coronary death or MI risk 41% in chest pain patients

Summarises SCOT-HEART (4,146 patients with stable chest pain, standard care vs standard care plus coronary CTA). CTA guidance lowered coronary death or nonfatal MI to 2.3% from 3.9%, mainly by directing aspirin and statin use to patients with plaque. Total procedures were similar by year 5; results apply to chest-pain patients only.

Why a coronary calcium score can rise as plaque stabilizes on statins

Dec 03, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 28 · 💬 47 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Explains that the Agatston score is not true calcium volume: it multiplies area by a stepwise density factor (1-4), so slightly denser plaque can jump a category and raise the score. Statins turn soft lipid-rich plaque into stable calcified plaque, so a rising CAC on treatment can reflect stabilization rather than failure.

Statins also thicken the fibrous cap of plaques, shown by OCT studies

Apr 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 Statins

Louis explains that besides reversing plaque, statins thicken the fibrous cap, stabilizing plaque, as shown in OCT studies and a meta-analysis.

New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Aug 31, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 38 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified. Carvalho argues calcium stabilises plaque, so slowing calcification without reducing plaque could even be harmful, and no evidence shows K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, so he advises relying on proven therapies, diet and exercise.
