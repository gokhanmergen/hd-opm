Orthopedic surgeon on carnivore warns followers not to ignore high LDL

Louis Nervegna · Jun 09, 2024 · ♥ 6 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a video of a doctor who lost 43 lbs on a carnivore diet but urges others on keto or carnivore diets to keep monitoring LDL cholesterol, particularly with a family history of heart disease. He says the diet is physiologically extreme and that the long-term risks are unknown.

  • Doctor lost 43 lbs on the diet and plans to continue
  • Urges caution especially with a family history of heart disease
  • Says influencers downplaying risks are misleading people: 'we just don't know'

Doctor (orthopedic surgeon) on carnivore diet is begging other people on keto/carnivore diet not to ignore their LDL cholesterol, especially people with a family history of heart disease.

He lost 43lbs on the diet, and plans to stay on it. But he is concerned that influencers— some of them other doctors— are lying to people about the risks and unknowns of the diet. “We just don’t know,” he says. He warns that the diet is “physiologically extreme” and urges a responsible and cautious approach, like he is taking himself.
