Curcumin has the strongest natural-product evidence for lowering TNF-alpha

Louis Nervegna · Jun 24, 2026 · ♥ 17 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post
Inflammation & CRPSupplements & vitaminsAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis states curcumin has the strongest evidence of any natural product for lowering the inflammatory cytokine TNF-alpha, which he says plays a causal role in many chronic diseases and aging. The shared FoundMyFitness note stresses that phytosomal forms are far more bioavailable and that TNF-alpha inhibitors are observationally linked to lower Alzheimer's risk.

  • Standard curcumin is poorly absorbed; phytosomal delivery improves bioavailability
  • TNF-alpha inhibitors for conditions like rheumatoid arthritis are observationally associated with lower Alzheimer's risk
  • Curcumin also lowered liver fat in a small human RCT

Curcumin has the strongest evidence of any natural product for lowering TNF-alpha.

TNF-alpha is an inflammatory cytokine that plays a causal role in the pathogenesis of multiple chronic diseases, including the aging process itself.

As a side note, curcumin has shown very impressive results in decreasing liver fat in a small human randomized trial: ↪ Phytosomal curcumin 1000 mg/day for 8 weeks cut liver fat in an NAFLD randomized trial.
