Blood donation lowers risk for developing cancer, but does not seem to lower risk for heart attacks or strokes.

These results are from a randomized trial.

Blood donation had a surprisingly large effect on cancer in this trial. After 6 years, deaths from cancer were HALF as much in blood donors as in controls! Reduction in cancer rates were seen in the trial in as little as 6 months after starting blood donation.

The thought is that lowering blood levels of iron is what is responsible for the anti-cancer effect.

However, recent work in geroscience tells us that the health benefits of diluting blood probably extend far beyond just lowering iron levels.
