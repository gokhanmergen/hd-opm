GLP-1 drugs improved testosterone and sperm quality, probably via weight loss

Louis Nervegna · Jul 10, 2026 · ♥ 0 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)Hormones, women's & men's healthWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Menno Henselmans explains that studies show GLP-1 drugs improve testosterone and sperm measures, but the effect is likely due to fat loss, since fat cells convert testosterone to estrogen via aromatase.

  • Obesity lowers T and raises estrogen via aromatase
  • Effect likely due to weight loss, not the drug itself

#glp1HD #trtHD #testosteroneHD #glp1testosteroneHD

Shared from Menno Henselmans · original post

Multiple new studies have found that GLP-1 weight loss drugs like Ozempic improved testosterone levels and measures of sperm quality in addition to their now-well-established weight loss benefits.

I won't detail all of the studies, because I can tell you this is a real effect, but probably not of the drugs themselves. Being overweight often reduces T levels in men while raising estrogen, because fat cells produce aromatase, an enzyme that converts male sex hormones like T to female sex hormones like estrogen.

In some men, obesity can even result in 'gyno' (male breast formation) and clinically low T levels (hypogonadism).

A yet-to-be-published systematic review of 5 studies found that T improved in 2 studies on overweight men but not in 3 studies on healthy men. This strongly suggests the improvements in T and sperm are the result of going from overweight to a more healthy weight, not of the drugs themselves.

Practical implications:

  • Before you consider TRT, make sure you've optimized your lifestyle and body composition to a reasonable degree. If you're overweight, fat loss is often enough to restore normal testosterone production.
  • To my bodybuilding friends, there's no need to run to your supplier to get you some GLP1s to boost your gains.
