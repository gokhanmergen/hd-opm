Tricaprin supplement reversed atherosclerosis in two patients with TGCV

Gokhan Can · Jan 09, 2025 · ♥ 18 reactions · 22 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsPlaque, CAC & CT angiography
AI summary & key facts

Osaka University research in the European Heart Journal reported that tricaprin, a dietary supplement that promotes lipid breakdown in heart muscle cells, reversed atherosclerotic lesions and relieved chest pain in two diabetic patients with TGCV. This condition involves triglyceride deposits and resists standard treatment. Only two patients were reported.

  • Two diabetic patients with refractory chest pain
  • Tricaprin promotes triglyceride lipolysis in cells
  • Described as first documented atherosclerosis regression of this kind in TGCV

This research from Osaka University, published in European Heart Journal in January 2023, presents a significant breakthrough in treating triglyceride deposit cardiomyovasculopathy (TGCV), a specific type of coronary artery disease. Led by Ken-ichi Hirano, researchers discovered that tricaprin, a commercially available dietary supplement, dramatically reversed atherosclerotic lesions and relieved symptoms in patients with TGCV, a condition they had identified 15 years earlier. TGCV differs from traditional atherosclerosis as it involves triglyceride deposits in blood vessels due to defective cellular breakdown, making it resistant to standard treatments. The study focused on two patients with diabetes and refractory chest pain who showed remarkable improvement after taking tricaprin, which promotes lipid breakdown in heart muscle cells. This represents the first documented case of atherosclerosis regression through increased triglyceride lipolysis within cells, offering hope for patients who don't respond to conventional coronary artery disease treatments.
