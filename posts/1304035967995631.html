pTau217 blood test may give early warning of memory decline

Louis Nervegna · May 29, 2026 · ♥ 3 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaRisk assessment & lab tests
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares Joel Kahn's note on the AD Detect lab panel, whose new data predict memory decline, and reminds readers that heart disease risk factors overlap with dementia risk. The idea is an early warning while lifestyle changes still matter.

  • Blood test (pTau217-based AD Detect panel) predictive of memory decline

This is the pTau217 blood test that we’ve talked about before in the group.

People at higher risk for heart disease are also at higher risk for dementia. Many of the risk factors are the same. This new blood test can give you an early warning, while you still have time to make changes to lower your risk.

May 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a pre-specified BROADWAY substudy (1,535 patients) in which obicetrapib improved five Alzheimer's biomarkers, with the largest benefit in APOE4/4 homozygotes. Caveats are that these are biomarkers, not cognitive outcomes, in a subgroup needing replication.

Plasma p-tau217 predicts dementia: 3x per SD, 7x top vs bottom quartile in 2,766 women

Apr 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 6 Brain health & dementia

A cohort of 2,766 women followed up to 25 years found each SD rise in p-tau217 tripled dementia risk and the top quartile had 7 times the risk of the bottom, with stronger effects in women over 70 and APOE4/4 carriers.
