Fact-check AI-generated content before posting it in the group

Louis Nervegna · Jul 28, 2025 · ♥ 13 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
AI & digital toolsGroup news & community
AI summary & key facts

An admin reminder that tools like ChatGPT can hallucinate citations or misquote references, and that members should verify AI output before posting.

These new generative Al tools like ChatGTP can be very useful for researching health topics.

BUT they often make mistakes. In particular, they can make errors when citing references. They can hallucinate citations that do not exist, or hallucinate information or quotes from references that are wrong.

If you're going to post Al generated content in the group, please take some time to fact-check what the Al actually says, particularly with respect to references. Look up the actual references that it cites, before relying on what it says.
