CETP inhibitors might help prevent Alzheimer's and Lewy body dementia in APOE4 carriers

Louis Nervegna · May 24, 2026 · ♥ 1 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaPCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugsLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a breakdown of why a lipid drug class could affect the brain: APOE is the brain's main cholesterol carrier, and a 2024 Mendelian randomization found the CETP benefit strongest in APOE4 carriers. It cites genetic and biomarker evidence, but this is early and not proof of clinical benefit.

  • Lewy body dementia odds ratio 0.61 in APOE4+ vs 0.89 in non-carriers (interaction p=5.81e-4)
  • CETP VV variant linked to doubled odds of MMSE >25 in Ashkenazi centenarians
  • Cache County: CETP I405V slowed cognitive decline 0.6 points/year per valine, without effect on AD diagnosis
  • Synthetic HDL cleared amyloid from brain vessels in mice

More on why cholesterol drugs, specifically CETP inhibitors, might prevent or treat Alzheimer’s and Lewy Body Dementia (LBD), especially in ApoE4’s.

(Note that ApoE4 raises the risk for Lewy Body Dementia, as well as Alzheimer’s.)

Quoted:

Why would a cholesterol drug touch the brain? Because APOE is the main cholesterol-carrier protein in your brain. Block CETP, raise HDL particles AND the ApoE riding on them, and synthetic HDL clears amyloid from brain vessels (mouse data). And genetics predicted it: Mendelian randomization in 2024 showed the CETP benefit was MOST PRONOUNCED in APOE4+ carriers. LBD odds ratio 0.61 in APOE4+ vs 0.89 in non-carriers, interaction p = 5.81 x 10^-4. 2006 Barzilai centenarians: CETP VV variant doubled odds of MMSE > 25. 2015 Cache County: CETP I405V slowed cognitive decline by 0.6 points/year per valine. Note: Cache County did NOT link CETP I405V to Alzheimer's diagnosis — trajectory not disease prevention. Convergence across 20 years of genetics, MR, and now a Phase 3 biomarker readout.

The European Commission approved obicetrapib, a low-dose oral CETP inhibitor, on 21 September 2026 in two versions: obicetrapib 10 mg alone and combined with ezetimibe 10 mg. Phase 3 trials reported LDL-C reductions of about 40% alone and about 50% with ezetimibe versus placebo. The poster says the drug has shown promise for both cardiovascular disease and Alzheimer's.

In the phase 3 BROADWAY trial, obicetrapib 10 mg cut LDL-C by 29.9%. A biomarker substudy of 1,535 participants (367 APOE4 carriers) found reduced p-tau217, greatest in APOE4 carriers, shaping a planned Alzheimer's prevention trial.

Louis notes obicetrapib, a CETP inhibitor, may treat both CVD and Alzheimer's. A Phase 2 prevention trial will include 75% APOE4 carriers. BROADWAY showed slower p-tau217 rise over 12 months.

Statin- and ezetimibe-like gene variants linked to 76-82% lower dementia risk per 40 mg/dL

Aug 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis highlights a Mendelian randomization and meta-analysis of about 1 million people showing lifelong lower non-HDL cholesterol via statin-like (HMGCR) and ezetimibe-like (NPC1L1) variants was associated with 76% and 82% lower dementia per ~40 mg/dL drop. CETP variants showed a 70% reduction.
