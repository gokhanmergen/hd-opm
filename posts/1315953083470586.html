The admin announces a March 2026 Frontiers in Hematology paper with Dr Hasselbalch after five years of research. It analyses how cardiovascular drugs, supplements and lifestyle can be used in polycythemia vera and related blood disorders, and the links between cardiovascular disease, chronic inflammation and blood stem cell mutations.

  • Published in Frontiers in Hematology, March 2026

After five years of extensive research, I am proud to share that Dr. Hasselbalch and I have published our latest article in Frontiers in Hematology this March. Our paper provides a comprehensive analysis of how cardiovascular drugs, dietary supplements, and lifestyle interventions can be leveraged to treat Polycythemia Vera and related blood disorders. In this work, we take a deep dive into the complex, underlying connections between cardiovascular disease, chronic inflammation, and blood stem cell mutations.

Niacin breakdown products 2PY and 4PY linked to higher cardiovascular risk (Ferrell 2024)

Feb 19, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 6 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes Ferrell et al. 2024: untargeted metabolomics found that the niacin end-products 2PY and 4PY predict major adverse cardiac events, with 4PY promoting vascular inflammation. The link was supported by genetics (the ACMSD gene) in humans and mice, which raises questions about heavy niacin intake from food fortification.

Atherosclerotic plaques contain large clonal, mutated cell populations, like tumors

Jun 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 14 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A JCI Insight study used deep whole-exome sequencing of carotid plaques and non-atherosclerotic arteries and found somatic mutations in plaques but not in healthy arterial tissue. This suggests plaques grow by clonal expansion, paralleling clonal hematopoiesis (CHIP), which is causally linked to cardiovascular disease.

Clonal hematopoiesis (CHIP) mutations precede and raise atherosclerosis risk in PESA study

CNIC researchers report in Nature Medicine, using the PESA-CNIC-Santander cohort, that people with clonal hematopoiesis mutations at baseline were more likely to develop atherosclerosis later, supporting it as a cause rather than a consequence. A related European Heart Journal study is also noted.

2025 ACC statement: inflammation is actionable in CVD, with universal hsCRP screening

Oct 09, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 13 Inflammation & CRP

Summarises the 2025 ACC Scientific Statement (JACC) concluding the inflammation-ASCVD link is now clinically actionable. It recommends universal hsCRP screening in primary and secondary prevention alongside cholesterol, notes IL-6 and fibrinogen also predict risk, and says CT perivascular fat attenuation is promising but not routine. It contrasts this with a more cautious 2002 CDC/AHA workshop.
