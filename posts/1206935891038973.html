Coffee drinkers have lower head and neck cancer risk in pooled analysis of 9,548 cases

Louis Nervegna · Jan 22, 2026 · ♥ 17 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsCancer
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a pooled analysis from the INHANCE consortium finding coffee and tea intake linked to lower head and neck cancer, suggesting hot coffee does not raise throat cancer risk. He calls for RCTs of coffee extract.

  • 9,548 cases and 15,783 controls from 14 studies
  • Still best not to drink extremely hot

It’s stunning how many different diseases coffee seems to protect against in observational studies.

We’re at the point now where coffee extract needs to be tested as a drug in long-term randomized trials. That’s how promising it looks.

(Note that there has been some lingering concern that hot beverages might raise the risk for throat cancer by damaging the delicate tissue lining the throat. This study seems to show that coffee does not do this. Still, it is probably safest not to drink it while it is extremely hot.)
