Higher blood linoleic acid tied to lower inflammation in about 1,900 people

Jun 23, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 8 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A study of blood markers in nearly 1,900 people found that higher membrane omega-6 (linoleic acid, which comes only from food) was associated with lower inflammation and better metabolic markers, challenging the claim that seed oils are inflammatory.

Over 100 randomized trials show polyunsaturated seed-oil fats improve insulin sensitivity

Nov 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis states that over 100 human randomized trials in aggregate show polyunsaturated fats improve insulin sensitivity and blood sugar control. The shared FoundMyFitness post adds that these omega-6 fats can lower LDL and have not been shown to raise inflammation in trials, but repeatedly reheated oil forms harmful oxidation products.

Olive oil has the strongest trial evidence, but canola and soybean oil also have support

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.

DGLA, an omega-6 fat made from linoleic acid, may selectively kill senescent cells

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Cell study summary showing senescent cells keep the omega-6 fat DGLA unusually low because it triggers ferroptosis, making DGLA a possible natural senolytic. The body makes DGLA from GLA and linoleic acid, found in nuts, seeds and their oils, and Louis notes that unlike other senolytics it is made endogenously.

Willett: seed oils lower LDL and do not raise inflammation in dozens of randomized trials

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Walter Willett clip arguing the 'seed oils are toxic' claim does not hold up. Unless partially hydrogenated or heated to extremes, seed oils are good for you, lower LDL, and dozens of randomized studies show no increase in inflammation.

North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. The shared cardiologist's post describes North Karelia's 700 per 100,000 CVD death rate in 1972, with 24% of calories from saturated fat, and a 20% cholesterol drop with coronary deaths in working-age men falling 82%.
