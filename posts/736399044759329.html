Louis eats a cup of beans daily and reviews ready-to-heat sofrito-style bean packets

Louis says he aims for a cup of beans a day, in line with Blue Zone diets, and describes simple-ingredient pre-cooked bean packets (olive oil as only fat, about $2.75 each) he found at Walmart. It is a personal product tip with no cited research.

  • Goal: one cup of beans per day
  • Packets are exactly one cup, cooked sofrito style with olive oil
  • About $2.75 each

I try to eat a cup of beans per day— in line with how long lived people eat in the so-called Blue Zones. There may be no food healthier for your heart.

I saw these at Walmart and decided to try them. The ingredients are all very simple and healthy, and the only fat used is EVOO. Each packet is exactly one cup which makes it easy. All you have to do is warm them. They are also reasonably priced at about $2.75 each (worth it for the convenience and healthy ingredients like olive oil).

Each of them is cooked sofrito style, which is a Puerto Rican blend of spices like garlic, onions, tomatoes, bell pepper, cumin, cilantro, etc.
