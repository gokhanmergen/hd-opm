Mediterranean diet has the strongest evidence of any diet: 4 RCTs and decades of cohorts

Louis Nervegna · May 10, 2025 · ♥ 14 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post

Louis argues the traditional Mediterranean diet has unmatched evidence: four 4-7 year RCTs show lower heart attack, stroke and CV death (46% lower CV mortality overall), and Nurses' Health and Health Professionals cohorts plus a dozen more large cohorts confirm long-term benefit. He allows other diets might work better but says none has comparable evidence.

Four large randomized controlled trials running 4-7 years have proven that the traditional Mediterranean diet lowers risk of heart attack, stroke, and cardiovascular mortality.

In aggregate, the risk of dying from CVD is reduced by 46% in these trials.

  • Lyon Heart Study
  • Indo-Mediterranean Diet Heart Study
  • PREDIMED
  • CORDIOPREV

↪ Meta-analysis of 4 Mediterranean diet RCTs: MACE down 48%, CVD death down 46%

Prospective cohort studies tracking groups of people for up to 40 years of their lives have confirmed that this holds true in the long run.

The Nutrition Source - Harvard Chan SchoolDiet Review: Mediterranean Diet • The Nutrition SourceResearch supports the use of the Mediterranean diet as a healthy eating pattern for the prevention of cardiovascular diseases, increasing lifespan, and healthy aging.nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu

This includes the famous:

  • Harvard Nurses Study (HNS)
  • Harvard Physicians Followup Study (HPFS)

Over a dozen more large prospective cohort studies, tracking people for 10-30 years, have also confirmed this longer term.

These studies are at the very top of the evidence hierarchy in medicine. That is why this diet is now recommended by the vast majority of cardiologists.

↪ Check where a study sits on the evidence hierarchy before citing it

Are there other diets that might work better? Maybe… but all of that is mostly just guesses and opinions. No other diet has anywhere close to this level of scientific evidence supporting it.
