Omega-3 plus low-dose aspirin treated severe gum disease about as well as antibiotics

Shared from Healthy Harbor · original post

A one-year clinical study found that omega-3 combined with low-dose aspirin was nearly as effective as the antibiotics amoxicillin and metronidazole in treating severe gum disease (periodontitis). About 57.7% of patients receiving omega-3 and aspirin achieved the study’s clinical treatment goal, compared with about 58% of those receiving the antibiotics.

The findings suggest that targeting inflammation with omega-3 and low-dose aspirin could potentially provide an alternative for some patients while reducing reliance on antibiotics.
