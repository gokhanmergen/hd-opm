Refined olive oils have smoke points of 410-446 F, high enough for deep frying

Louis Nervegna · Dec 15, 2023 · ♥ 14 reactions · 15 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods
AI summary & key facts

Louis says it is a myth that olive oil cannot be used for high-heat cooking: some (light/refined) olive oils have smoke points of 220-230 C, above typical deep-frying temperatures of 350-375 F, and the flavor is almost fully removed so they suit baking. He names the product he uses in an image.

  • Smoke point 410-446 F (220-230 C) for some olive oils
  • Typical deep-frying temperatures 350-375 F
  • Light olive oil has almost no olive flavor

You can buy olive oils with a smoke point as high as 410-446 degrees Fahrenheit (220-230C).

It’s a myth that you can not cook at high temperatures with olive oil.

This smoke point is even high enough to deep fry with. (Typical deep frying temperatures are 350-375 Fahrenheit.)

Here is the olive oil that I use for high temperature cooking.

It is also great for baking or anything that requires a neutral taste, because the olive flavor is almost completely removed.
