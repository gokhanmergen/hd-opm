The basics of insulin resistance, explained in 5 minutes, by a Harvard diabetologist.

In most people, the biggest driver of insulin resistance is actually fat stored in the liver. Lose the fat in the liver (by any means), and the IR dramatically improves. When we diet, the first place that we lose weight is the liver. This is why weight loss is so effective at treating IR.

A simple biomarker for the amount of fat in your liver is the amount of visceral fat that you see when you look in the mirror. This is the fat in your abdomen that sits underneath your abdominal muscles, stored in and around your liver and other organs.
