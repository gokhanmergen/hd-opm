Keto-CTA figures suggest mean non-calcified plaque rise of 32.3 mm3, not the stated 18.8

Louis Nervegna · Apr 19, 2025 · ♥ 12 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyLow-carb, keto & carnivore
AI summary & key facts

Louis reports that people checking Keto-CTA's published figures reconstructed the scatter plots and found a mean non-calcified plaque volume increase of 32.3 mm3, versus the median 18.8 mm3 stated by the authors. NCPV was the trial's primary outcome. This is based on community reconstruction, not an official correction.

  • Authors' stated median NCPV increase: 18.8 mm3
  • Reconstructed mean from scatter plots: 32.3 mm3
  • NCPV was the primary outcome
  • Checked by multiple people per the post

In case you thought the LMHR soap opera could not possibly get any worse…

People have been carefully checking the figures in their published paper.

Now it turns out that the non-calcified plaque progression (NCPV) in their figures is much worse.

This was the primary outcome in their trial.

They said that the median was 18.8mm^3, which was already very bad.

A reconstruction of the scatter plots in their paper shows an average (mean) of 32.3mm^3.

Multiple people have checked this, and it seems to be true.
