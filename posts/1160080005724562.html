Admin's view of cancer as epigenetic and genetic misprogramming from aging

Gokhan Can · Nov 13, 2025 · ♥ 3 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
CancerAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Short opinion that cancer arises when aging cells lose their epigenetic and genetic identity and faster-proliferating mutant tissues outcompete healthy ones, making aging organs a mosaic. No sources.

Cancer is a cell/tissue whose programming has gone wrong. The misconfigurations tend to be epigenetic and genetic. Over time our cells & tissues age and they lose their identity (epigenetic and genetic program). This manifests itself as cancer as those evolved tissues that proliferate faster gain a competitive advantage over their healthy (wild type) counterparts. Every organ in aging human body is a mosaic (patchwork) of mutant (evolved) tissues epigenetically and genetically.
