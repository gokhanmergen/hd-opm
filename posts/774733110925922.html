High insulin tells the liver to make cholesterol, tying insulin resistance to high LDL

Aug 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that insulin signals the liver to produce cholesterol, one reason insulin-resistant people tend to have high LDL. Losing visceral and especially liver fat to correct insulin resistance should therefore bring LDL down naturally. Supported by a Mass General Brigham explainer.

Plant-based diet quality and dementia: whole plant foods protect, refined ones raise risk

Apr 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A new study shared via MedPage Today links whole grains, vegetables, vegetable oils, nuts and legumes to lower dementia risk and refined grains, juices, white potatoes and added sugars to higher risk. Middle-age and older adults who improved diet quality had an 11% reduced dementia diagnosis risk over 10 years.

Overfeeding saturated fat raised liver fat 55%, sugar 33%, unsaturated fat 15% in an RCT

Apr 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A randomized trial overfed three groups by 1000 calories for 3 weeks from butter/dairy, olive oil/nuts or added sugar. Liver fat rose 55%, 15% and 33% respectively, showing calories are not equal for the liver.
