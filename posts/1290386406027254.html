Tirzepatide cut major kidney events 23% vs dulaglutide in SURPASS-CVOT post-hoc analysis

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a summary of a post-hoc kidney analysis of SURPASS-CVOT in people with type 2 diabetes and heart disease. Tirzepatide gave greater kidney protection than dulaglutide, supporting the kidney-protective role of GLP-1-based therapies.

  • 23% lower major kidney event risk overall
  • 30% lower risk in low-to-moderate CKD; 21% lower in high-risk CKD
  • Reduced macroalbuminuria progression and slower eGFR decline

Shared from Dr. Karl Nadolsky · original post

GLP therapies have established themselves as kidney-protective in type 2 diabetes and high risk of kidney disease.

This new post-hoc analysis of SURPASS-CVOT’s kidney outcomes shows greater kidney protection with tirzepatide (#mounjaro) versus dulaglutide (Trulicity) in people with type 2 diabetes and heart disease.

  • 23% lower major kidney event risk overall
  • 30% lower risk in low-to-moderate CKD
  • 21% lower risk in high-risk CKD
  • Reduced macroalbuminuria progression
  • Slower eGFR decline with tirzepatide
