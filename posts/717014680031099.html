Starts a thread about favorite health foods and lists the author's picks: vegetable soup, roasted vegetables, keto pizza, low-carb chocolate, teas, fish every other day and salads. It is a personal menu rather than evidence.

  • Wild salmon or sardines every other day
  • 2-4 large cups of hibiscus, green, black or berry teas and decaf coffee

Mediterrenean + Low Carb?

Want to have a thread about your favorite health foods? Let me start.

  1. Vegetable soup. Season with salt, cumin and cayenne pepper. Optional: 1 tsp of lemon (per bowl) + shredded cheese.
  2. Roasted vegetables. Season with salt, olive oil and cayenne pepper.
  3. Keto pizza of any kind (eg homemade or blaze pizza). Ideally some veggies on top. Many low carb options exist for the crust.
  4. Low carb chocolate. Again a ton of options.
  5. 2-4 large cups of hibiscus + green tea, black tea, "berry powder tea", decaf coffee <-- all of them antioxidants.
  6. Fish every other day (Wild Salmon or Sardines).
  7. Salad of any kind. Ideally with some nuts & olive oil.
  8. Eggs of any kind plus some side/toppings of veggies, limited bacon.
  9. Nuts of any kind.

The more I can stick to this menu, the healthier I get.

What're your favorite health foods? Feel free to share.

Cheers.
