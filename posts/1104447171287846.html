Cocoa raised circulating stem cells 2.2-fold and endothelial function 47% in heart disease

Louis Nervegna · Sep 03, 2025 · ♥ 31 reactions · 25 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsBlood vessels, circulation & endotheliumBlood pressure, salt & potassium
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a UCSF crossover study in men with heart disease where high-flavonoid cocoa improved flow-mediated dilation and increased circulating angiogenic cells. He links this to the COSMOS cocoa-extract trial, in which cardiovascular death fell 27%.

  • Flow-mediated vasodilation improved 47% on high-flavanol cocoa vs low
  • CD34+/KDR+ circulating angiogenic cells increased 2.2-fold
  • Systolic BP fell 4.2 mm Hg; plasma nitrite rose 74 nM
  • COSMOS cocoa extract: 27% lower CVD death, 21,000 adults over 3.6 years (per post)

Drinking cocoa increases circulating stem cells and improves endothelial function in people with heart disease.

Link to the full study:

PaperScienceDirect articlesciencedirect.com

Quoted:

“Endothelium-dependent vasomotor function, as measured by flow-mediated vasodilation of the brachial artery, improved by 47% in the HiFI period [cocoa] compared with the LoFI period [no cocoa]. After HiFI, the number of CD34+/KDR+-CACs [stem cells], as measured by flow cytometry, increased 2.2-fold as compared with after LoFl.”

“The HiFI [cocoa] led to a decrease in systolic blood pressure (mean change over LoFI [no cocoa]: −4.2 ± 2.7 mm Hg), and increase in plasma nitrite level (mean change over LoFI: 74 ± 32 nM). Applying a mixed-effects linear regression model, the results demonstrated a significant increase in flow-mediated vasodilation and a decrease in systolic blood pressure with increasing levels of CD34+/KDR+-CACs [stem cells].”

———-

Note that a cocoa-flavonoid extract reduced CVD death by 27% in a placebo controlled RCT of 21,000 adults over 3.6 years. This was a very high quality trial run at Harvard Medical School and Brigham & Women’s Hospital in Boston (COSMOS). 🍫 Can dark chocolate really boost your stem cells?

According to a UCSF study in 60-year-old men with heart disease, just two cups of hot cocoa made with 80% high-flavonoid chocolate:

✅ Doubled circulating stem cells
✅ Boosted blood vessel resiliency
✅ Supported heart + metabolic health

The secret? Polyphenols called proanthocyanidins—powerful natural compounds that regenerate circulation and support healthy angiogenesis.
