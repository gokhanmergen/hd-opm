Review of vitamin K2 (with or without vitamin D) and CAC. A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n=1,533) found a -17.3 Agatston units/year pooled difference. Vitamin C 1-2 g/day raises urinary oxalate, a concern for calcium oxalate stone-formers

Aug 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 10 Supplements & vitamins

Louis warns that vitamin C supplementation increases oxalate production, with 1-2 g daily producing a detectable rise in urinary oxalate excretion. Quoting a Reviews in Urology paper, he notes it does not change urinary pH and might promote stone activity in calcium oxalate stone-formers.

Plaque starts in neck and leg arteries decades before the heart; calcium scores miss it

Sep 17, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post summarises a video describing an NEJM study of 16,000 asymptomatic people showing plaque accumulates first in the neck and leg arteries, during the 20s to 40s, before the heart. Heart scans such as coronary calcium may therefore miss early disease; carotid intima-media thickness ultrasound can detect peripheral plaque, and the ankle-brachial index offers a simple at-home check for leg artery blockage.
