Oils are not harmful on a plant-based diet: substitution analyses of olive oil and others

Quoted explainer addressing the claim that oils are harmful on a whole-food plant-based diet, using substitution analyses. Replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat (oils, nuts, seeds) lowered CHD risk, and olive oil substituted for vegetable oils gave similar outcomes; avocado data from the NHS/HPFS are also cited.

  • Replacing saturated with unsaturated fats, especially PUFA, and/or high-quality carbohydrates reduces CHD risk
  • Olive oil vs vegetable oils gave similar overall and CV outcomes

Addressing the claim that oil is unhealthy on a plant-based diet

Quoted:

“When I post about the health benefits of vegetable oils, such as canola oil and sunflower oil, I am often met with claims that oils are only beneficial compared to saturated fat-rich foods like butter and would actually be harmful if added to a whole food, plant-based diet, so let’s address that.

The crux of this argument can be addressed with the classic “compared to what?” question. First off, a substitution analysis, which is a type of study that attempts to evaluate the impact of substituting one food for a another, provided other dietary variables and lifestyle factors are kept similar, found that substituting olive oil for vegetable oils resulted in similar overall and cardiovascular health outcomes. Of course, the response to this is usually that any oil is harmful, not just vegetable oils. Well if that were the case, we should see oils confer harm when consumed in place of a whole food like an avocado, but we don’t see that either, and the oils also perform similarly to nuts and seeds. The response to this is usually that low fat foods like whole grains would be healthier options than both avocados and oils. 🥑🥜

Well, we have a substitution analysis of dietary fats and various sources of carbohydrates that helps address that concern. This analysis found that replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat, which was most often consumed in the form of vegetable oils, nuts and/or seeds, resulted in a greater reduction in risk of coronary heart disease than carbohydrates consumed from whole grains. The results were similar when evaluating the replacement of refined carb sources rather than saturated fat.

The data is clear as day that vegetable oils can be a part of a healthy diet and can actually be very beneficial, but if anyone doesn’t want to consume them, nuts and seeds can also be great sources of polyunsaturated fats, which can reduce cardiovascular risk. ❤️
