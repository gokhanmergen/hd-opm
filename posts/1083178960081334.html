US age-adjusted CVD death rate has fallen 75% since 1950

Louis Nervegna · Aug 05, 2025 · ♥ 14 reactions · 13 comments · original Facebook post
Cardiovascular disease: the big picture
AI summary & key facts

Louis notes that for people of the same age, the annual risk of dying from cardiovascular disease in the US is now about a quarter of what it was in 1950, citing Our World in Data.

  • 75% decline in age-standardized CVD death rate since 1950 (US)
  • Our World in Data credits surgery, drugs, prevention and emergency response

In the United States alone, the age-standardized death rate from cardiovascular disease has fallen by 75% since 1950.

This means that for people of the same age, the annual risk of dying from cardiovascular disease is now just 25% of what it was in 1950.
