Fatty liver in older adults mostly signals multimorbidity, not cirrhosis

Louis Nervegna · Jul 26, 2025 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

Louis notes that the real problem of liver fat is usually the cardiometabolic disease it drives, not liver damage. A shared Nature study found steatotic liver in 33.3% of older adults but only 2.4% had significant fibrosis.

  • Steatotic liver prevalence 33.3% in older adults
  • 2.4% had significant fibrosis

The real problem with accumulating fat in the liver is usually not damage to the liver itself— but rather a major increase in risk for other diseases.

Why? Because fat accumulation in the liver is the main cause of insulin resistance. See the previous posts on this by clicking the tags #liverfatHD and #roytaylorHD.
