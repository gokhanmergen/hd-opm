Plaque-stopping supplements rest on small, low-quality studies; gotu kola most plausible

AI summary & key facts

Louis comments on a video about 7 supplements said to stop artery plaque. He says the studies are small and low quality but promising, and personally finds gotu kola (Centella asiatica) the most plausible for CVD based on mechanism, explained in the comments.

  • All cited studies were small and lower quality
  • Louis's pick: gotu kola, for its plausible mechanism of action

These were all small lower-quality studies, but they look promising. We just don’t know.

In my opinion, the most promising for CVD mentioned in the video is gotu kola (centella asiatica). This has the most plausible mechanism of action, in my view. (See comments for explanation.)

At least six strategies have reversed plaque in randomized trials

Aug 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists six strategies shown to reverse plaque in human RCTs: major weight loss, Mediterranean/DASH diet, LDL-lowering drugs (statins, PCSK9 inhibitors, ezetimibe), colchicine, exercise and high-dose EPA fish oil. He would like to see a trial combining all of them.

Six strategies shown to regress plaque in human randomized trials

Feb 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 10 ↩ 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists strategies with RCT evidence for plaque regression: major weight loss, a Mediterranean or DASH diet, LDL-lowering drugs, colchicine, exercise and high-dose EPA. He notes six RCTs show diet can regress plaque and that Ornish's results resembled PREDIMED and CORDIOPREV, while Esselstyn's data are not from RCTs.

The 'iodine raises IQ 12 points' claim applies only to iodine-deficient populations

Apr 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Supplements & vitamins

A shared summary of two meta-analyses of iodine deficiency and IQ: Bleichrodt and Born (1994, 21 studies) found 13.5 IQ points and Qian et al. (2005, 37 studies, 12,291 Chinese children) found about 12.45 points, in deficient regions. Hashtag-only post.

Evidence that plaque can shrink: CENIT, PRECISE-IVUS, ZEUS, EVAPORATE and GLAGOV

Jun 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 72 · 💬 42 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Post by group member Daniel Chong summarising trials showing plaque change: supervised HIIT in CENIT, ezetimibe in PRECISE-IVUS, ezetimibe added to statin in ZEUS, icosapent ethyl in EVAPORATE and evolocumab in GLAGOV. It notes some research suggests a 1% reduction in plaque volume may equate to a 20% reduction in event risk.

Mitoquinone (MitoQ) may reverse early heart failure signs in type 2 diabetes

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 3 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A Medical Xpress news item reports that mitoquinone, an improved form of CoQ10, showed potential to reverse early signs of heart failure in people with type 2 diabetes, improving cardiac energy efficiency and relaxation without major side effects. Larger trials are planned to confirm the result.
