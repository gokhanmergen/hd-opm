  • FDA eligibility for the HoFH pathway

Quoted:

Plaque Reversal Takes Aim

Repair Biotechnologies received FDA eligibility under the Rare Disease Evidence Principles Pathway for its investigational mRNA therapy, REP-0003, targeting homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia. Rather than simply lowering LDL cholesterol, the therapy aims to remove intracellular cholesterol that drives plaque formation, opening the door to the possibility of reversing cardiovascular damage rather than just slowing it.

Centenarians' higher LDL reflects survivorship bias, not a benefit of high LDL

Oct 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 36 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that centenarians tend to have higher LDL simply because higher LDL is common in the general population; they reach 100 because of protective genes that let them survive despite high LDL. Low LDL is uncommon in Western countries, genetics is the main determinant of reaching 100, and low LDL may delay cardiovascular death but will not by itself get someone to 100.
