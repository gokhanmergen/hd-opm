Coconut oil raises LDL and ApoB, long-term outcomes unknown; MCT oil is a cleaner option

Oct 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes a Nutrition Made Simple video: there are few long-term studies of coconut oil and heart disease, so claims rely on short-term biomarker studies. Because it is 80-90% saturated fat it raises LDL and ApoB (though less than butter); its HDL rise is unlikely protective, and he suggests plain MCT oil if one wants MCT benefits.

Butter and cream may differ from cheese and yogurt in how saturated fat affects health

Admin opinion that free saturated fat in butter and cream behaves differently from protein-bound saturated fat in cheese and yogurt: avoid butter and cream, while whole-fat cheese and yogurt look more benign if they do not raise cholesterol much. No sources are cited.

Dr Gil Carvalho: evidence contradicts RFK Jr's claims that seed oils are harmful

Summary of a video arguing that seed oils are associated with a 15% reduction in heart disease events and 21% lower risk of heart disease death, and that replacing butter with seed or olive oil lowers mortality. It also says systematic reviews find seed oils neutral or anti-inflammatory.

North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. The shared cardiologist's post describes North Karelia's 700 per 100,000 CVD death rate in 1972, with 24% of calories from saturated fat, and a 20% cholesterol drop with coronary deaths in working-age men falling 82%.

Full-fat cheese raises LDL less than butter, possibly due to the cheese matrix

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis shares a summary suggesting full-fat cheese affects LDL less than butter or cream despite similar saturated fat. In a randomized trial full-fat cheese lowered LDL more than reduced-fat cheese supplemented with butter, and another study found cheese eaters excreted more fat in stool than butter eaters. Results are not perfectly consistent and benefits may level off around 50 g/day.
