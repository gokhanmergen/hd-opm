Vigorous exercise can cut visceral fat without calorie deficit or weight loss

Louis Nervegna · Jul 06, 2026 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatExercise & fitness
AI summary & key facts

Louis says vigorous exercise can reduce visceral fat without a calorie deficit. FoundMyFitness lists calorie deficit, HIIT, sleep and stress management.

  • Visceral fat linked to roughly double early-death risk
  • Linked to about 44% higher cancer risk

Vigorous exercise can reduce visceral fat, even in the absence of a calorie deficit, and even without a reduction in total body weight.

#visceralfatHD #exerciseHD

Shared from FoundMyFitness · original post

If you want to lose visceral fat, focus on the big 4:

  • Get into a calorie deficit
  • Do high-intensity interval training
  • Protect your sleep
  • Manage stress

Visceral fat is the deep inflammatory fat stored around your organs, and high levels are linked to roughly double the risk of dying early

It's also linked to ~44% higher cancer risk

The good news: it's often one of the first fat stores your body starts to lose when you create the right conditions

Why insulin resistance lowers HDL, and why improving insulin sensitivity beats chasing HDL

Mar 21, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 ↩ 10 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis lists six mechanisms by which insulin resistance lowers HDL: impaired HDL maturation (lower LCAT), reduced apoA-I production, faster HDL catabolism (hepatic lipase, CETP), altered HDL composition and function, higher triglycerides making small dense HDL, and inflammation and oxidative stress. He concludes the best way to raise HDL is improving insulin sensitivity (lose visceral fat, exercise, cut refined starch and sugar), since insulin resistance is causal in heart disease while large RCTs suggest HDL itself is probably not.

Visceral fat had the strongest inflammatory protein signature of any trait in VITAL/COSMOS

Nov 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a cross-sectional proteomics analysis of 896 adults from VITAL and COSMOS. Visceral fat measured by DXA was associated with 221 circulating proteins, more than blood pressure, HDL, triglycerides or insulin resistance. It cannot prove causation, but suggests visceral fat is a meaningful target for reducing chronic inflammation.
