Microplastics at everyday doses caused insulin resistance and liver damage in mice

A news item on a study linking dietary microplastics to early liver damage, gut disruption and glucose problems in mice. Gokhan's own view that microplastics are the new smoking is opinion; the study itself is animal data.

Video: immunoglobulin G buildup in fat tissue drives insulin resistance and aging

Feb 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Gokhan summarizes a Mike Lustgarten video on research showing IgG accumulates in fat tissue with age and obesity, impairs insulin signaling, promotes inflammation and fibrosis, and is reduced by calorie restriction. Blocking IgG recycling through the FcRn receptor in mice improved metabolic health and extended lifespan. He calls it potentially Nobel-worthy 'if it holds up'.
