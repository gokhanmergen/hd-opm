If you’re a typical American, eliminating snacking in between meals will cut your calories by ~25% and your sugar intake by 1/3.

The typical American gets a full meal’s worth of calories just from snacks. It amounts to a fourth meal every day. Most of it is empty calories with little nutritional value. (No wonder why we’re all overweight.)

Eliminating snacking in between meals might be the simplest way to lose weight and improve the quality of your diet. Think of the time in between meals as a mini fast.

This comes from a study of the diets of 23,000 adults in the USA.

Quoted:

“Snacks make up ~25% of 24-hour energy intake among US adults, contributing a meal’s worth of energy to overall intakes without conforming to central tenets of the Dietary Guidelines or the ADA diabetes treatment guidelines.”
