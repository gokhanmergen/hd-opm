Excess calories shut down autophagy, one reason calorie restriction extends lifespan

Louis Nervegna · Aug 18, 2025 · ♥ 11 reactions · 14 comments · original Facebook post
Fasting & meal timingAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis states that overeating shuts down autophagy (cellular self-recycling), a main reason calorie restriction extends lifespan in animals including mammals. A shared item describes autophagy as triggered by fasting or restriction.

  • Excess calories suppress autophagy

Eating too many calories shuts down autophagy.

This is one of the main reasons why restricting calories extends lifespan in most animal species tested, including mammals.

Shared from Unbox Factory · original post

Autophagy — from the Greek meaning “self-eating” — is the body’s natural process of breaking down and recycling damaged or unhealthy cells. Triggered during fasting, calorie restriction, or certain forms of stress, autophagy removes malfunctioning cell components, preventing diseases like cancer, Alzheimer’s, and other age-related conditions.

Scientists have linked autophagy to improved brain function, reduced inflammation, and enhanced longevity. Nobel Prize-winning research in 2016 brought global attention to this process, showing that fasting can activate powerful cellular clean-up systems. It’s like a built-in detox mechanism at the microscopic level.

While intermittent fasting is one way to promote autophagy, researchers caution that balance is key — excessive fasting can be harmful. Understanding and harnessing autophagy could open new doors to preventive medicine and healthier aging.
