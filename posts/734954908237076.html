Akkermansia probiotics may have a downside: gut levels linked to poor bone health

Louis Nervegna · Jan 04, 2024 · ♥ 4 reactions · 22 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis asks whether group members take Akkermansia probiotics, often advertised as a natural GLP-1 booster, and points to a Harvard Medical School report linking Akkermansia levels in the gut to poor bone health. No numbers are given in the post.

  • Akkermansia is marketed as a natural GLP-1 agonist
  • Harvard Medical School news: gut bacteria linked to bone density and strength
  • Higher Akkermansia associated with poorer bone health (per the post)

Is anyone taking Akkermansia probiotics?

I see online ads for this all the time.

They tout the beneficial effects, e.g. it is a natural GLP-1 agonist.

But it appears to have a dark side: Akkermansia levels in the gut are associated with poor bone health.

Bisphosphonates for bone loss: fracture reductions summarized

Oct 13, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Review of bisphosphonate evidence for osteoporosis. Alendronate cuts vertebral and non-vertebral fractures by about 50% in women with prior vertebral fractures; yearly IV zoledronic acid cuts vertebral fractures 70% and hip fractures 40%; a 2017 meta-analysis found overall fracture OR 0.62. Not a heart topic but relevant to aging.
