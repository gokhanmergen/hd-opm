Plaque reduction: statins, PCSK9 inhibitors and ezetimibe have the strongest evidence

May 18, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 32 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Ranks interventions for regressing or stabilizing atherosclerotic plaque. Statins, PCSK9 inhibitors and ezetimibe have the strongest trial evidence, with high-dose EPA and pioglitazone fairly good; colchicine, aged garlic, Centella, pine bark, nattokinase and plant-based diets are promising but need rigorous imaging RCTs, and evidence for exercise, L-arginine and vitamin K2 is limited.

Peter Libby on inflammation in atherosclerosis, CHIP, canakinumab and colchicine

Feb 17, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 9 Inflammation & CRP

Summarizes a talk by Dr. Peter Libby describing atherosclerosis as a disease of constant cellular conflict in plaques, with superficial erosion increasingly recognized in thrombosis. Bone marrow inflammatory signaling and clonal hematopoiesis drive white-cell accumulation, and canakinumab and colchicine have shown cardiovascular benefit.

2025 ACC statement: inflammation is actionable in CVD, with universal hsCRP screening

Oct 09, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 13 Inflammation & CRP

Summarises the 2025 ACC Scientific Statement (JACC) concluding the inflammation-ASCVD link is now clinically actionable. It recommends universal hsCRP screening in primary and secondary prevention alongside cholesterol, notes IL-6 and fibrinogen also predict risk, and says CT perivascular fat attenuation is promising but not routine. It contrasts this with a more cautious 2002 CDC/AHA workshop.

Clonal hematopoiesis (CHIP) mutations precede and raise atherosclerosis risk in PESA study

Sep 05, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 12 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

CNIC researchers report in Nature Medicine, using the PESA-CNIC-Santander cohort, that people with clonal hematopoiesis mutations at baseline were more likely to develop atherosclerosis later, supporting it as a cause rather than a consequence. A related European Heart Journal study is also noted.

Meta-analysis: statins, bempedoic acid, ezetimibe and omega-3 lower CRP independent of LDL

Feb 19, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 3 Inflammation & CRP

Highlights a Cardiovascular Research meta-analysis of RCTs showing that statins, bempedoic acid, ezetimibe and omega-3 fatty acids significantly reduce CRP, independent of how much they lower LDL-C. The author adds a list of other anti-inflammatory options to consider, such as colchicine and GLP-1 RAs.
