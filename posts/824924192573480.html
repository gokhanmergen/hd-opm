Relative risk vs Absolute risk, when it comes to statin trials.

——

Statins have been shown to reduce absolute risk of having a heart attack or stroke by anywhere from 1.1% to 4.7% in randomized controlled trials lasting about 5 years.

A common claim on social media is that this makes little practical difference.

Let’s examine the basic math behind this claim:

For the average American, the absolute risk of having a heart attack in their entire lifetime is only about 20%. (1 in 5 Americans will have a heart attack in their lifetime.)

A drop in absolute risk of 1.1% to 4.7% in only 5 years reduces this lifetime risk from 20% down to 15.3% to 18.9%. All in just 5 years.

That is huge. If the average American continues this for decades, they will likely drop their lifetime risk of a heart attack or stroke all the way down to 10%.

So instead of 1 in 5 Americans having a heart attack, it would be 1 in 10. That is fantastic. Millions of lives would be saved.
