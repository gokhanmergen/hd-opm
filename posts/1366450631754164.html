Higher stored EPA linked to 36% lower bleeding stroke risk in a 21-year Danish study

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Louis shares a 21-year Danish study of 54,546 adults using fat-tissue samples of EPA and DHA. People with the highest EPA had a 36% lower risk of bleeding stroke, DHA was neutral, and ALA was linked to 32% higher risk (needs confirmation). Pooled RCTs found no extra brain bleeding, except small risk with high-dose prescription EPA. The study is observational.

Does a CAC scan change outcomes? Two 2026 randomized trials say not for the average person

Sep 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 31 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis asks whether CAC scanning changes whether you have a heart attack; for some it is a wake-up call, but for the average person it does not seem to change outcomes. The shared text cites a Danish trial of 30,000 men (no reduction in death, MI or stroke at seven years, more serious bleeding) and the US CorCal trial of nearly 6,000 people (same event rates as a risk calculator).

2026 ACC statement: daily aspirin for primary prevention adds bleeding with little benefit

Jul 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

A quoted summary of the 2026 ACC antiplatelet statement says pooling 13 trials of 165,265 people, aspirin modestly reduced heart attacks and strokes but raised major bleeding and did not reduce all-cause death. Benefit faded in trials after 2000, likely because statins now supply the protection.

Aspirin halved colorectal cancer in Lynch syndrome in a 861-patient RCT

Apr 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

Louis shares a BBC article describing Burn's 2020 trial of 861 Lynch syndrome patients: 600 mg daily aspirin for at least two years roughly halved colorectal cancer risk over 10 years, with a follow-up trial of lower dose pending.

CHIP not tied to heart attack or stroke in a 9,434-person aspirin trial, but to bleeding

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 · 💬 1 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

Louis shares a JAMA Cardiology secondary analysis of a primary-prevention aspirin trial (9,434 participants) on clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential (CHIP), a common aging-related blood cell mutation. CHIP was not associated with more cardiovascular events but was associated with more clinically significant bleeding, and aspirin's effects did not differ by CHIP status. He also explains what CHIP is.

Natto: spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase in one food, with mortality and plaque data

Summarizes natto as a food rich in spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase. It cites a Frontiers clinical study where high-dose nattokinase slowed carotid plaque progression, an association with lower all-cause mortality, and mouse lifespan data for spermidine.
