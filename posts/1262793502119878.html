Iodine deficiency is rising in the UK, US and Australia as people drop iodized salt

Louis Nervegna · Apr 06, 2026 · ♥ 11 reactions · 19 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBlood pressure, salt & potassium
AI summary & key facts

Louis cites a New Scientist column warning that fashionable salts are causing iodine deficiency to return. He lists seafood, dairy, seaweed, skin-on potatoes and lima beans as sources and suggests a basic multivitamin with iodine as insurance.

  • Good sources: seafood, dairy, seaweed, potatoes with skin, lima beans

Be careful if you’ve stopped using iodized salt.

We need some iodine. It’s an essential nutrient.

Seafood and dairy are good sources. The best vegan source is seaweed. Potatoes (with skin) and lima beans are also good sources.

A basic RDA level multivitamin that includes iodine is a good insurance policy.

Kimchi meta-analysis: lower triglycerides, glucose and blood pressure across nine studies

Sep 21, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

A University of Connecticut meta-analysis of nine studies linked regular kimchi intake to better cardiometabolic markers. The blood pressure benefit is attributed to potassium and fermentation bacteria despite kimchi's high sodium, though most studies were done in Korea.

Potassium-enriched salt cut recurrent stroke 14% and stroke deaths 21% in stroke survivors

Feb 11, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 10 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

A cluster randomized trial in rural northern China (a subgroup analysis of a larger trial) gave stroke survivors a salt substitute containing 25% potassium chloride. It reduced recurrent stroke, hemorrhagic stroke and stroke-related death compared with regular salt, suggesting a cheap, widely usable strategy where sodium intake is high.
