Engineered regulatory T cells targeting oxidized LDL cut plaque 70% in mice

Nov 22, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 35 · 💬 7 Inflammation & CRP

Summarises a University of Pennsylvania preclinical study adapting CAR T therapy: Tregs engineered to recognise oxidized LDL dampened arterial inflammation and reduced plaque 70% in cholesterol-prone mice. Unlike cancer CAR-T, the cells suppress rather than destroy; it is a possible add-on for patients at risk despite cholesterol drugs.

ERSPC 23-year follow-up: PSA screening cut prostate cancer deaths 13% with overdiagnosis

Nov 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Cancer

Summarises the 23-year European Randomized Study of Screening for Prostate Cancer follow-up: 13% lower prostate cancer mortality (22 fewer deaths per 10,000 men), one death prevented per 456 men invited (628 at 16 years), with higher diagnosis rate (14% vs 12%) reflecting overdiagnosis. Authors call for risk-based screening.

Small Swedish study: colesevelam lowered blood PFAS up to 38%

Nov 11, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 12 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

Summarises a 10-person Swedish study of adults with high PFAS exposure: cholestyramine increased fecal PFAS excretion and a 12-week course of colesevelam reduced blood PFAS up to 38%. Authors say it was very small and observational, needs larger trials, and these drugs should not be used off-label.
