Obicetrapib lowers LDL and raises HDL on top of statins, but outcome benefit is unproven

Steve Kaplan's shared post reports a study of the CETP inhibitor obicetrapib in high-risk patients already on statins, with meaningful reductions in LDL and other atherogenic lipids and higher HDL. Louis notes earlier CETP inhibitors failed on CVD outcomes and that hard-event results remain to be seen.

It will be interesting to see if this translates into lower risk of hard outcomes, like heart attacks and deaths.

Shared from Steve Kaplan · original post

In a just-released study, researchers evaluated the drug Obicetrapib — a potent inhibitor of the cholesteryl ester transfer protein (CETP) — in patients at high cardiovascular risk who were already on statin therapy. They found that Obicetrapib produced meaningful reductions in LDL-cholesterol and other atherogenic lipids, while simultaneously raising HDL-cholesterol.

Obicetrapib gets first approval worldwide in the EU, lowering LDL up to 40-50%

Sep 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 5 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

The European Commission approved obicetrapib, a low-dose oral CETP inhibitor, on 21 September 2026 in two versions: obicetrapib 10 mg alone and combined with ezetimibe 10 mg. Phase 3 trials reported LDL-C reductions of about 40% alone and about 50% with ezetimibe versus placebo. The poster says the drug has shown promise for both cardiovascular disease and Alzheimer's.

Obicetrapib lowered Alzheimer's marker p-tau217, most in APOE4 carriers (BROADWAY)

Jul 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 6 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

In the phase 3 BROADWAY trial, obicetrapib 10 mg cut LDL-C by 29.9%. A biomarker substudy of 1,535 participants (367 APOE4 carriers) found reduced p-tau217, greatest in APOE4 carriers, shaping a planned Alzheimer's prevention trial.

CETP inhibitors might help prevent Alzheimer's and Lewy body dementia in APOE4 carriers

May 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a breakdown of why a lipid drug class could affect the brain: APOE is the brain's main cholesterol carrier, and a 2024 Mendelian randomization found the CETP benefit strongest in APOE4 carriers. It cites genetic and biomarker evidence, but this is early and not proof of clinical benefit.

CETP inhibitor obicetrapib slowed p-tau217 accumulation in the BROADWAY analysis

Sep 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis notes that HDL-raising drugs failed for heart disease but may have value for Alzheimer's. In a secondary analysis of the BROADWAY trial presented at AAIC, the CETP inhibitor obicetrapib slowed rise of the Alzheimer's biomarker p-tau217 versus placebo.

Obicetrapib may prevent both heart disease and Alzheimer's

Jul 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 5 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis notes obicetrapib, a CETP inhibitor, may treat both CVD and Alzheimer's. A Phase 2 prevention trial will include 75% APOE4 carriers. BROADWAY showed slower p-tau217 rise over 12 months.

Statin- and ezetimibe-like gene variants linked to 76-82% lower dementia risk per 40 mg/dL

Aug 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis highlights a Mendelian randomization and meta-analysis of about 1 million people showing lifelong lower non-HDL cholesterol via statin-like (HMGCR) and ezetimibe-like (NPC1L1) variants was associated with 76% and 82% lower dementia per ~40 mg/dL drop. CETP variants showed a 70% reduction.
