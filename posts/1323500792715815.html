Shared from BoxLife Magazine · original post

Joe Breen’s struggle with severe alcoholism took an unexpected turn when his doctor prescribed the GLP-1 weight-loss drug Wegovy in April 2023, initially intended to manage his weight rather than addiction.

As Breen lost significant weight, his alcohol cravings virtually vanished, a phenomenon echoed in thousands of anecdotal reports on Reddit and TikTok.

Early animal studies and large-scale patient analyses suggest semaglutide and similar GLP-1 agonists may dampen alcohol-seeking behavior by affecting brain reward pathways.

While not yet FDA-approved for addiction, these medications could revolutionize treatment for alcohol use disorder.

Breen’s 180-pound weight loss and newfound sobriety highlight their transformative potential.

Semaglutide cut inflammation 38% in SELECT; biggest drops tracked with fewer events

Aug 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 4 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

A new analysis of the SELECT trial asks why semaglutide reduced cardiovascular events. Inflammation markers fell 38% versus placebo, early and even in people who lost little weight, and those with the biggest drops had the fewest events. Modelling suggests part of the drug's benefit runs through lower inflammation, though this shows a link rather than proof of cause.
