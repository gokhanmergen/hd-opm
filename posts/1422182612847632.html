Low-fat vegan diet improved blood sugar and liver fat without calorie restriction

Louis Nervegna · Oct 15, 2026 · ♥ 9 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

A human trial covered by Lifespan.io found that a low-fat plant-based diet improved glucose control, reduced liver fat and lowered skin AGEs even without calorie restriction. The poster adds that Mediterranean, keto and low-fat vegan diets all reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity, and argues the common mechanism is loss of visceral fat.

  • Low-fat plant-based diet improved glycemic control, reduced liver fat and lowered skin AGEs without caloric restriction
  • Poster's view: any diet that reduces visceral fat is likely to give these benefits, faster or slower depending on the diet

Mediterranean and keto diets have both been shown to be effective at reducing liver fat and improving insulin sensitivity & glycemic control. So have low-fat vegan diets, as this new study once again demonstrates.

The bottom line is that any diet that reduces visceral fat is very likely to produce all of these effects. Visceral fat can be lost, even in the absence of calorie restriction. Depending on which diet you choose, the results may happen faster or slower, but ultimately all of these diets will produce good results in due time if you lose significant visceral fat. We now have plenty of human trials that have proven this.
