Long-term restrictive elimination diets can harm the gut microbiome

Louis Nervegna · Jul 14, 2026 · ♥ 0 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Gut microbiomeMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a Tim Spector clip arguing elimination diets can damage the microbiome, damage that may be hard to repair, so short-term benefits should be weighed against long-term risk.

  • Damage may be hard to repair (Louis's view)

A major problem with staying on restrictive elimination diets for the long term is that they can damage the microbiome.

This damage can be very difficult to repair.

When it comes to these restrictive diets, you have to balance short term benefits with long term risks.
