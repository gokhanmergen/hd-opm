Shared from Frank Lipman MD · original post

SGLT2 inhibitors were originally developed to help manage blood sugar in people with type 2 diabetes.

But over time, researchers began noticing something interesting: their benefits seemed to extend beyond glucose control.

Studies have shown protective effects on the heart and kidneys, while emerging research suggests these drugs may also influence pathways involved in aging, including inflammation, mitochondrial function, autophagy, AMPK and mTOR signaling.
