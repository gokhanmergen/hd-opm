Mayo Clinic recruiting for beetroot juice, sleep and blood pressure study

AI summary & key facts

A Mayo Clinic recruitment notice for healthy adults aged 55-80 for a beetroot juice study. It gives no findings.

  • Participants aged 55-80
  • 3 overnight stays at Mayo Clinic Hospital

#beetsHD #bloodpressureHD #mayoclinicHD #sleepHD #nitricoxideHD

Shared from Mayo Clinic · original post

Mayo Clinic is seeking healthy individuals between the ages 55-80 to participate in a research study. The purpose of this study is to find out if boosting nitric oxide (a molecule naturally made by your body), by consuming a beetroot juice supplement improves overall sleep and early morning blood pressure control in humans. Participation will include 3 overnight stays in the Clinical Research and Trials Unit, located at the Mayo Clinic Hospital, St. Mary’s campus. At each visit, you will be asked to complete a sleep study, answer questionnaires, provide blood and saliva samples, and undergo several vascular health tests, stress tests, and blood pressure measurements. You will be compensated for your time spent in this study.

For more information, please contact a member of the study team at (507) 422-0768 or bock.joshua@mayo.edu or visit Link to mayocl.in (mayocl.in)
