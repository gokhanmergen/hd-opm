Two-day NHANES diet recall cannot support conclusions about long-term disease risk

Louis Nervegna · Oct 08, 2025 · ♥ 5 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
How to read researchFasting & meal timing
AI summary & key facts

Louis criticizes the stream of papers that use NHANES dietary data based on just two days of recalled eating to draw conclusions about heart disease, cancer and death. Two days of diet cannot represent a lifetime of eating.

  • NHANES diet analyses rely on only 2 days of dietary recall

People keep publishing low-quality papers using dietary recall data from the NHANES database.

These studies use only 2 days of diet data! They are literary based on what people ate for only 2 days of their entire lives!

Needless to say, you cannot draw any kind of reliable conclusion about someone’s longterm risk for heart disease, cancer, death, etc. from how they happened to eat during only 2 days of their entire lives.
