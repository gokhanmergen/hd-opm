VO2max as a causal longevity metric is doubtful; Mendelian randomization shows no effect

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 6 ↩ 7 Exercise & fitness

Louis endorses Chris Masterjohn's article arguing that most studies link treadmill performance, not measured VO2max, to lifespan, and that treadmill tests are influenced by many other factors and by unhealthy volunteers. Louis adds that Mendelian randomization shows no forward causal effect of VO2max on longevity but evidence of causality in the reverse direction.

Aging causes VO2max to decline, not the reverse

Nov 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 8 ↩ 4 Exercise & fitness

Louis argues that aging drives VO2max decline regardless of activity habits, and that Mendelian randomization suggests the association comes mainly from reverse causality. He cites a longitudinal Circulation study showing the decline accelerates in each successive decade.

Mendelian randomization finds no causal effect of VO2-max on longevity or diabetes

Sep 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 24 Exercise & fitness

Louis argues that a 2024 two-sample Mendelian randomization study found no association between genetically predicted cardiorespiratory fitness and longevity or diabetes. He interprets the observational VO2-max/mortality link as reverse causality or confounding.
