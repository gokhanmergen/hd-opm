It’s also worth mentioning that this is iodized. Many people in the USA don’t get enough iodine anymore, in part because they have stopped using iodized salt. See this post: ↪ Iodine deficiency is rising in the UK, US and Australia as people drop iodized salt. Each 1/4 teaspoon contains 60mcg of iodine, which is 40% of the RDA.

————-

Warning:

If you have kidney problems, do NOT take potassium salts like this.

The kidneys regulate blood levels of potassium. Too much potassium can cause a fatal cardiac arrest in people with kidney problems.
