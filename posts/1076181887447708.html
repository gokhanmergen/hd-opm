Physically active people with diabetes have about 20% lower all-cause mortality

Louis Nervegna · Jul 26, 2025 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

A shared Medscape report: weekend warriors and regular exercisers with diabetes had 21% and 17% lower all-cause mortality, mostly via fewer heart-related deaths (33% and 19% lower).

  • Weekend warriors: 21% lower all-cause mortality
  • Regular exercisers: 17% lower
  • Cardiovascular mortality 33% and 19% lower

People with diabetes who exercise have about a 20% lower all-cause mortality than people with diabetes who do not.

#diabetesHD #exerciseHD

Shared from Medscape · original post

Adults with diabetes who are physically active on weekends or who exercise regularly have a 21% and 17% lower risk for all-cause mortality, respectively, than people who are inactive. These benefits are mainly due to fewer deaths from heart-related issues, with ‘weekend warriors’ and regularly active participants showing 33% and 19% lower risks, respectively. Even ‘Weekend Warriors’ With Diabetes Can Reduce Death Risk (Medscape)

PREDIMED: Mediterranean diet with olive oil or nuts cut major CV events by 30% and 28%

Aug 26, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 ↩ 7 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes the PREDIMED randomized trial of about 7,500 people testing the traditional Mediterranean diet for primary prevention. Groups supplemented with extra-virgin olive oil or nuts had a 30% and 28% lower rate of heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death after 5 years versus an AHA-style control diet. A shared Annals post adds that Mediterranean diet plus calorie reduction and exercise cut diabetes risk by 31% in a large trial.
