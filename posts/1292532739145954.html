Valter Longo’s company ProLon has introduced a plant-based protein powder supplement that minimizes insulin secretion and activation of mTOR and IGF1. They call it Longevity Protein (L-Protein), and advertise it as a way to get the muscle-building benefits of extra protein without the potential harm of spiking insulin and systemically overactivating mTOR and IGF1.

Each serving has 25g of complete protein with an amino acid profile designed to limit activation of insulin, mTOR, and IGF1.

Most protein powders and shakes cause very large increases in insulin, especially whey. In fact, whey protein causes considerably more insulin secretion than an equivalent number of calories from white bread or pure glucose:

↪ Whey protein raised insulin about twice as much as glucose or white bread per calorie

This particular protein powder, on the other hand, does NOT significantly raise insulin levels.

It is a blend of peas, black beans, and chickpeas. There are no grains and the amount of carbohydrate is low.
