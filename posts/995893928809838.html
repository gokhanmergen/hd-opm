AI summary & key facts

A Reuters report that the highest dose of an experimental Lilly drug lowered Lp(a), a genetic risk factor, by 94% in a mid-stage trial. Gokhan notes the key question is whether these drugs reduce cardiovascular events.

We should soon see how well these drugs work not only for lowering lp(a) but also cardiovascular events.
