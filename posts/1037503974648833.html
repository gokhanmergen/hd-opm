Dr. Montgomery's death shows plant-based diets are not heart attack proof

Louis Nervegna · May 31, 2025 · ♥ 22 reactions · 28 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsHeart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

Louis comments on the death at 59 of Dr. Baxter Montgomery, saying it resembles sudden cardiac arrest from a heart attack. His point is that whole-food plant-based no-oil diets reduce risk but are not a cure, and some adherents who refuse medication still see disease progress.

  • Died age 59, May 24, 2025
  • WFPBNO diets reduce risk but are not a magic cure

This has all the markings of a sudden cardiac arrest, caused by a heart attack.

He was only 59yo.

It’s a huge mistake to think that these WFPBNO diets magically make you heart attack proof.

They are healthy diets that reduce risk— but not a magic cures for heart disease.

If you join the various Esselstyn and Ornish groups on FB, you will see that many people still see their disease progress on these diets, despite good adherence— particularly the ones who refuse medication.
