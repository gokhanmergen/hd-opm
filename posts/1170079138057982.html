Metformin may work partly by reshaping the gut microbiome

  • Increases Akkermansia and Bacteroides

#metforminHD #microbiomeHD

Shared from Amazing Science Facts · original post

Multiple studies now show that the diabetes drug metformin partly works by reshaping the gut microbiome, especially increasing helpful species such as Akkermansia and Bacteroides that improve glucose control. Researchers are isolating and engineering bacterial strains whose metabolic products mimic some of metformin’s effects on blood sugar and insulin sensitivity. Early models in animals and organoid systems suggest that transplanting or boosting these microbes can lower glucose levels and improve metabolic health. None of these bacteria have yet replaced metformin in large human trials, and most are at the experimental or supplement concept stage. The big vision is a future where tailored “live biotherapeutics” help manage diabetes by turning the gut into a blood sugar control organ.

Statin muscle pain is common in some, severe damage is rare; statins may aid sarcopenia

Jan 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 15 Statins

Louis argues statins are not toxic to muscle in most people. Myalgia occurs in some, possibly with a genetic susceptibility, while rhabdomyolysis is very rare (about 1 in 100,000, or 1 in 10,000 conservatively). Switching statins (e.g., to pravastatin or pitavastatin) or using non-statin LDL drugs can help, and low-dose statins are being studied as a treatment for sarcopenia.
