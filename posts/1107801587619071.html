That fizzy drink might taste refreshing, but science shows it comes with a hidden cost. A University of Michigan study found that a single can of Coke can shorten your life by about 12 minutes, ranking it among the ultra-processed foods most harmful to health and longevity. Over time, these small losses add up, raising risks of obesity, diabetes, and heart disease.

But here’s the good news: the same research also identified foods that add minutes back. Nuts, seeds, and even simple fruits like bananas can boost life expectancy, while cutting processed meats in favor of vegetables can extend life by nearly an hour a day. The choice is clear — what you eat and drink today could directly shape how much time you have tomorrow.
