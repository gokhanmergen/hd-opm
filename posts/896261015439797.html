Bedtime statin and BP dosing has a rationale: overnight cholesterol synthesis, AM surge

Louis Nervegna · Oct 23, 2024 · ♥ 26 reactions · 14 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis passes along a tip that taking statins and blood pressure medication before sleep has a logic. Most cholesterol is produced during sleep and heart attacks cluster in the morning after waking, when a sudden blood pressure rise can trigger plaque rupture. No studies are cited.

  • Most cholesterol is produced during sleep (per Louis)
  • Most heart attacks occur in the morning shortly after waking
  • Night dosing aims to inhibit cholesterol production and keep morning blood pressure low

Just want to pass this tip along, in case you don’t know.

There is a certain logic to taking statins and blood pressure medications right before you go to sleep.

Most cholesterol is produced during sleep.

Also most heart attacks happen in the morning, shortly after waking. (The most common place to find someone dead from a heart attack is in the bathroom.)

The rapid and sudden rise in blood pressure right after waking can precipitate a plaque rupturing.

So taking cholesterol and blood pressure meds at night maximizes the effect on inhibiting cholesterol production and keeps your blood pressure low when you first wake up.
