Moderate statin plus ezetimibe matched high-intensity statins on MACE with lower LDL

Gokhan Can · Nov 24, 2024 · ♥ 15 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan shares a JAMA Cardiology paper finding that a moderate-intensity statin combined with ezetimibe gives similar major cardiovascular event outcomes to high-intensity statins in ASCVD, while lowering LDL further, with better tolerance and less new diabetes.

  • Similar MACE with moderate statin plus ezetimibe vs high-intensity statin
  • Greater LDL-C lowering
  • Better tolerance and lower incidence of diabetes

A moderate-intensity statin combined with ezetimibe achieves similar MACE outcomes compared to high-intensity statins, while providing superior LDL-c lowering, better tolerance, and a lower incidence of diabetes. https://ja.ma/4fz3eoS (t.co)

Limiting diabetes risk on LDL-lowering drugs: statin risk is modest, alternatives exist

Nov 26, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 17 Statins

Review explaining that statins modestly increase new-onset diabetes in a dose-dependent way, mostly in people with existing metabolic risk factors, and that cardiovascular benefit clearly outweighs this. It suggests statins mainly bring forward diagnosis in predisposed people, outlines mechanisms (beta-cell dysfunction, insulin resistance) and proposes ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors and pitavastatin as options that limit glycemic harm.

Summarizes a 2023 JAHA review of personalized LDL-C management, giving risk categories from low to very high and the LDL goals for each. High-risk patients should reduce LDL at least 50% to below 70 mg/dL, while very-high-risk patients should aim below 55 mg/dL, or below 40 after recurrent events on statins.

Mendelian randomization in 1 million people: statin-like genetics cut dementia risk 76-82%

Nov 06, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 17 Brain health & dementia

Summarises Dr Stanfield's video on a Mendelian randomization study of over a million people. Short RCTs show no cognitive harm or benefit and observational studies suggest protection; genetic variants mimicking lifelong statin and ezetimibe action were linked to 76-82% relative dementia risk reduction, implying long-term benefit missed by short trials. The video also notes treating hearing loss, vision loss and midlife depression.

Ezetimibe lowers LDL-C about 18-20% alone and a further 21-30% on top of a statin

Sep 24, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 19 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

A quick reference on ezetimibe: it blocks cholesterol absorption in the small intestine and is most useful added to a statin. It also modestly lowers apoB and triglycerides and slightly raises HDL.
