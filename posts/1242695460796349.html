Rhonda Patrick: COSMOS multivitamin trials showed about 2 years less brain aging

A shared FoundMyFitness summary of Rhonda Patrick's remarks on the Harvard COSMOS trials. It claims Centrum Silver in adults 65+ was associated with about 2 years less brain aging and roughly 5 years 'younger' episodic memory versus placebo. The shared text is a podcast summary, not the primary papers.

  • Centrum Silver daily in adults aged 65+
  • About 2 years less brain aging vs placebo (as stated in the shared post)
  • Episodic memory aging about 5 years 'younger' (as stated)

She is referring to the COSMOS studies at Harvard.

A run-of-the-mill multivitamin can measurably change how your brain ages

In three large randomized controlled trials of older adults (65+), daily Centrum Silver led to about 2 years less brain aging versus placebo

Even more striking, episodic memory aging (remembering experiences, people, and events) looked roughly 5 years “younger” in the multivitamin group

On top of that, it acts as basic insurance, filling micronutrient gaps that most diets simply don’t cover
