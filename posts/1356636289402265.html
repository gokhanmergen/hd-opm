Glutathione supplements mostly end up outside cells, where they don't belong

Louis Nervegna · Jul 30, 2026 · ♥ 10 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsCancer
AI summary & key facts

Louis explains glutathione is large and poorly enters cells, even liposomally. A Nature study shows some cancer cells can use extracellular glutathione as a cysteine source.

This is the problem with taking glutathione supplements. Glutathione belongs inside your cells, not outside.

It is a large molecule that doesn’t get inside cells very easily. When you take it as a supplement, it ends up outside the cell where it does not belong. Even when you take it liposomally, a lot still ends up outside the cell.

Research cited

  • Extracellular glutathione as nutrient source in cancer · other · Nature
Shared from Genuinely Healthy · original post

New research published in Nature found that some cancer cells can use extracellular glutathione (GSH) as an alternative nutrient source when cystine is limited. Instead of using glutathione only as an antioxidant, these tumor cells break it down with γ-glutamyltransferase (GGT) enzymes to release cysteine, an amino acid that supports metabolism, growth, and survival under stress.

The study showed that extracellular glutathione is abundant in the tumor microenvironment, and blocking its breakdown reduced cysteine availability and slowed tumor growth in experimental models, revealing a previously unrecognized metabolic pathway that helps tumors adapt to nutrient-poor conditions.
