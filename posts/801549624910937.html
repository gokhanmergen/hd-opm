A personal 'cocktail of metabolites' for longevity: AKG, arginine, beta-alanine, taurine

Gokhan Can · May 12, 2024 · ♥ 10 reactions · 16 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

The author describes the metabolite supplements he uses (AKG, L-arginine, beta-alanine, taurine, L-carnitine) with brief rationales, mostly from animal data, and links to his blog notes. It is a personal experiment rather than evidence of cardiovascular benefit.

  • AKG: inhibits platelets and mTOR; 8 years epigenetic younging in a human study (per the post); author takes 250-375 mg
  • L-arginine: author takes 700 mg, says it improves RBC rheology and blood flow
  • Beta-alanine and taurine extended lifespan in some animal models; author suspects he overdosed both

Cocktail of metabolites

Correctly chosen cocktail of metabolites might be key to epigenetic younging and longevity.

  • AKG might have been the first clue here, where it has very impressive pre-clinical studies, and very energizing when I take it (250mg-375mg). Inhibits platelets and mTor. Resulted in 8 years epigenetic younging in a human study. Alpha Ketoglutarate (AKG) (mylongevityjourney.blogspot.com)
  • L-Arginine: I think it's great! Improves RBC rheology. Opens up your veins, improves blood flow, has great mental effects. 700mg is more than sufficient for me. L Arginine (mylongevityjourney.blogspot.com)
  • Beta Alanine: A precursor for carnosine synthesis, with antioxidant properties, protects cells from oxidative damage, and reduces AGEs. Extended the lifespan of rotifers, fruit flies and laboratory mice. Provides energetic buffer for high intensity exercise. Beta Alanine (mylongevityjourney.blogspot.com)
  • Taurine: Extended the lifespan in mice, reduces cellular senescence, and protects against telomerase deficiency. I think I overdosed Beta Alanine and Taurine in the past. I may be very sensitive to these epigenetic/metabolic modifiers. Will revisit. Taurine (mylongevityjourney.blogspot.com)
  • L-Carnitine : Has potential mitochondrial benefits, aids cellular energy production, acts as an ROS scavenger, protects cellular membranes, prevents fatty acid accumulation, and eliminates toxic metabolites. Extends lifespan in C. elegans and decreases mortality in rats. L-Link to mylongevityjourney.blogspot.com (mylongevityjourney.blogspot.com)
  • Others: L-glycine never helped me, it was either no-op or didn't improve how I feel. L-glutamine in small doses is intriguing.

I think metabolites are super exciting for epigenetic programming to a younger phenotype, and many of them have strong scientific backing.
