Starting LDL control earlier gives larger lifelong benefit by delaying the event threshold

Louis Nervegna · Sep 28, 2026 · ♥ 16 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
LDL, ApoB & lipid panelHeart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

The poster explains a chart from a JACC: Advances paper showing that earlier LDL lowering produces a bigger lifelong benefit than starting late. Lowering LDL does not cure disease but delays crossing the risk threshold for heart attack or stroke, buying time.

  • Earlier LDL control gives greater lifelong benefit
  • Starting late still gives increasing benefit versus doing nothing, but smaller
  • LDL lowering delays reaching the threshold for heart attack or stroke

The earlier you start controlling LDL, the greater the lifelong benefit (blue-green line).

If you start late, you still get an increasing benefit as time goes by (yellow line) compared to doing nothing (red line). But the benefit is much smaller.

Lowering LDL is not a cure, by any means. It simply delays crossing the threshold at which a heart attack or stroke is most likely to occur (dashed line). The result is that this threshold is reached later in life, compared to when it would have been reached otherwise.

In principle, this buys you more time. That’s what lowering LDL really does.
