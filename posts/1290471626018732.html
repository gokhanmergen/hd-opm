Oat beta-glucan may help clear PFAS and lowers LDL about 5-7% via bile acid binding

Louis Nervegna · May 13, 2026 · ♥ 20 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

FoundMyFitness shares that beta-glucan from oats and mushrooms reduces bile acid reabsorption, which in animal studies increases fecal PFAS excretion and also underlies LDL lowering of about 5-7% at roughly 3 g/day of beta-glucan. PFAS clearance in humans is a proposed mechanism.

  • LDL down about 5-7% with oat beta-glucan
  • About 3 g/day beta-glucan
  • Bile acid pathway increases PFAS fecal excretion in animal studies

Oats help the body to clear “forever chemicals”.

The same mechanism by which this occurs (reducing bile acid reabsorbtion) is also the reason why oats modestly lower LDL cholesterol.

Shared from FoundMyFitness · original post

β-glucans, a soluble fiber in oats and mushrooms, may be one of the simplest tools we have for helping the body clear PFAS (“forever chemicals”)

The proposed mechanism is reduced bile acid reabsorption in the gut, which increases fecal excretion (a pathway shown to increase PFAS elimination in animal studies)

That same bile-acid pathway is one reason oat-derived β-glucans lower LDL cholesterol by ~5–7% in human studies, often using ~3 g/day of supplemental β-glucan powder

With forever chemicals showing up in water, cosmetics, and even some produce, oats or supplemental β-glucan may be a simple daily defense worth taking seriously
