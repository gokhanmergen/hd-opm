Low dietary fat intake may modestly reduce testosterone in men

Louis shares a meta-analysis summary showing lower fat intake modestly lowers testosterone and notes it might not be bad, since calorie restriction lowers testosterone too and optimal levels for longevity are unknown.

  • Low-fat diets modestly reduce serum testosterone

Low dietary fat intake may modestly reduce testosterone in men.

(Please note that this is not necessarily a bad thing, but it could be. Calorie restriction extends lifespan in most animal species tested, but also lowers testosterone. Females outlive males in most species of mammals, and there is evidence that androgen hormones play a role in why. The optimal testosterone levels for longevity in healthy humans are still unknown.)
