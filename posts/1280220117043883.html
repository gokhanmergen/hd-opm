Louis shares news of a documentary, narrated by Edward Norton, on fasting and calorie restriction and aging, noting GLP-1 drugs make the subject relevant.

This new documentary explores the possible health benefits of fasting and calorie restriction in humans, especially as it relates to potentially slowing down the aging process and preventing/treating age-related diseases like CVD and cancer.

The widespread adoption and success of GLP1 drugs has made this a very important subject in medicine. Calorie restriction is the primary mechanism by which these drugs work.

Valter Longo is recognized as a world expert on this topic, and has published some of the most influential academic papers in this area. The documentary is narrated by actor Edward Norton.

The documentary covers many topics that we have discussed extensively in the group, including mTOR, IGF1, Laron Syndrome, calorie restriction, growth hormone, testosterone, protein, nutrition, rapamycin, the traditional Mediterranean diet, blue zones, the Adventists Health Study, lifespan studies in mice, etc.
