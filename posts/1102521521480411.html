PREDIMED-Plus: energy-reduced Mediterranean diet plus exercise better prevented diabetes

Louis Nervegna · Aug 31, 2025 · ♥ 7 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsExercise & fitness
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a prespecified secondary analysis of the PREDIMED-Plus trial in adults with obesity and metabolic syndrome. An energy-reduced Mediterranean diet with physical activity was more effective than an unrestricted Mediterranean diet at lowering type 2 diabetes incidence, with modest weight loss.

  • Population: persons with obesity and metabolic syndrome at high risk of type 2 diabetes
  • Compared energy-reduced MedDiet + physical activity vs ad libitum MedDiet

Shared from Dr. Karl Nadolsky · original post

Reducing calories in a Mediterranean pattern + exercise is good.

This prespecified secondary outcome analysis of a famous multinational randomized clinical trial (PREDIMED - Prevención con Dieta Mediterránea) examined the comparison of an energy-reduced #Mediterranean Diet + Physical Activity versus an Ad Libitum (not restricted) Mediterranean Diet in the prevention of Type 2 diabetes.
The Mediterranean diet has been shown to reduce the risk for developing diabetes that adding caloric reduction and #exercise with just modest #WeightLoss was more effective than only an ad libitum MedDiet in reducing #diabetes incidence in persons with obesity and metabolic syndrome at high risk of type 2 diabetes.

Comparison of an Energy-Reduced Mediterranean Diet and Physical Activity Versus an Ad Libitum Mediterranean Diet in the Prevention of Type 2 Diabetes : A Secondary Analysis of a Randomized Controlled Trial. Ann Intern Med. 2025 Aug 26. doi: 10.7326/ANNALS-25-00388. Epub ahead of print. PMID: 40854218.

Low-carb diets lose their diabetes advantage by 12 months, trials and meta-analyses show

Nov 25, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 8 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Argues low-carb gives better remission and HbA1c at 6 months, but the edge fades. A 2021 BMJ meta-analysis (23 RCTs) found benefit at 6 months diminishing by 12; Silverii 2020 (37 RCTs, 3,301 patients) found HbA1c differences vanishing and reversing by 24 months; a 2-year calorie-matched RCT showed identical weight loss. Concludes the benefit comes from adherence, not metabolic magic.
