Most heart attacks occur in right-handed people, a lesson in correlation versus causation

Louis Nervegna · Jul 09, 2024 · ♥ 30 reactions · 16 comments · original Facebook post
How to read researchMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

The poster notes that 90% of heart attacks occur in right-handed people and asks whether training yourself to be left-handed would lower CVD risk, to illustrate how influencers misinterpret correlations. Most people are right-handed, so the statistic reflects base rates, not causation.

  • 90% of heart attacks occur in right-handed people (illustrative)
  • Correlation does not imply causation

90% of heart attacks occur in people who are right handed.

This is a fact.

Is it legitimate to use this fact as evidence that training yourself to be left handed will lower your risk of CVD?

If you understand what’s wrong with all of this, then you understand why most health influencers on social media misinterpret research to arrive at nonsensical conclusions about the causes of heart disease.
