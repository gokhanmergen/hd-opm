Avoiding seed oils does not lower CRP or other inflammatory markers

Louis Nervegna · Nov 22, 2023 · ♥ 4 reactions · 9 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Inflammation & CRPMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

Louis says human studies repeatedly show that reducing dietary omega-6 fails to lower inflammatory markers, so avoiding seed oils is not a route to lower CRP. He links a Nutrition Made Simple video reviewing the evidence on nine seed oils, heated seed oils and genetic variability.

  • Reducing omega-6 intake repeatedly fails to lower inflammatory markers in humans

You are not going to lower CRP and other inflammatory markers by avoiding seed oils. This simply does not work. It has been tested over and over again in human studies, and reducing dietary intake of omega 6 repeatedly FAILS to lower inflammatory markers. It would be wonderful if getting rid of inflammation were as simple as just avoiding seed oils, but the biology of inflammation in humans is far more complex than this.

Layne Norton rebuts seed-oil claims with human evidence on inflammation, LDL and oxidation

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A shared post argues that human trial evidence does not support the claim that seed oils are harmful. Systematic reviews of randomized trials find that raising linoleic acid does not consistently increase inflammatory markers, replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL and cuts coronary events, and trials show no significant increase in lipid peroxidation. It adds that LDL oxidation mostly occurs after retention in the artery wall, so LDL concentration still matters.
