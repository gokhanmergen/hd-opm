Vitamin K2 MK-4 improved insulin sensitivity in a small placebo-controlled trial

Louis Nervegna · Mar 17, 2026 · ♥ 23 reactions · 19 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

Louis explains he takes K2 for insulin sensitivity, not unproven calcification claims. In a Diabetes Care 2011 trial, 42 participants given 30 mg MK-4 for 4 weeks improved insulin sensitivity and disposition index, apparently via osteocalcin.

  • 42 participants, 30 mg MK-4, 4 weeks
  • Improved insulin sensitivity index and disposition index
  • Healthy young men; linked to carboxylated osteocalcin

The main reason that I take vitamin K2 is because it improves insulin sensitivity.

Not because of unproven claims about it reversing arterial calcification.

Reference:

Paper · Diabetes Care · 2011Vitamin K2 supplementation improves insulin sensitivity via osteocalcin metabolism: a placebo-controlled trial.pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

In this small randomized trial, 42 participants were given 30 mg of K2 MK-4 for 4 weeks.

K2 supplementation significantly improved:

  • Insulin sensitivity index— a measure of how easily insulin can move sugar out of the bloodstream and into cells.
  • Disposition index— a measure of how well the pancreas is keeping up with insulin resistance. It combines insulin sensitivity with insulin secretion.

Other small randomized trials have shown similar results, even at much lower doses. See these previous posts:

"To summarize, we have demonstrated for the tirst time that vitamin K2 supplementation for 4 weeks increased insulin sensitivity in healthy young men, which seems to be related to increased cOC rather than modulation of inflammation."

Vitamin K2 slows coronary calcium mainly in severe calcification; event benefit unproven

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 16 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Review of vitamin K2 (with or without vitamin D) and CAC. A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n=1,533) found a -17.3 Agatston units/year pooled difference. The largest RCT (389 men, 720 ug MK-7 + 1000 IU D vs placebo for 2 years) missed significance overall but slowed CAC at baseline CAC >=400; some CKD trials were null. Not yet a standard therapy.

K2 plus D3 slowed coronary calcium progression about 21% in a 2-year trial

Sep 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 14 Supplements & vitamins

In 398 adults with severe coronary calcification, two years of 720 mcg vitamin K2 (MK-7) plus 1,000 IU vitamin D3 daily slowed calcium-score progression versus placebo. However, total plaque volume rose similarly in both groups among the 143 assessed, so the trial does not show plaque reversal or fewer heart attacks.
