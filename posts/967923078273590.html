High-dose DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 2-year APOE4 trial, but reached the brain

Jun 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Brain health & dementia

Louis comments on a Medscape report of a trial of high-dose omega-3 in older adults at high dementia risk. It found no improvement in memory, cognition or hippocampal volume, although DHA reached the cerebrospinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. He notes the trial was short at two years, and APOE4 carriers with the highest DHA to arachidonic acid ratio declined less.

PREVENT-E4: 2 g/day DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 365 at-risk older adults

Louis reports the PREVENT-E4 randomized trial found no cognitive or hippocampal-volume difference between DHA and placebo over two years, though it showed 2 g DHA reached spinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. The trial was small and designed mainly to test brain penetration.

Single dose of donor stem cells raised walking endurance about 20% in frail older adults

Feb 25, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 9 Aging & longevity

Describes a trial of donor bone-marrow mesenchymal stem cells in 148 walking adults aged 70-85 with frailty (118 on varying doses, 30 on placebo). The cells lack immune-triggering surface proteins so no immunosuppressants were needed; at nine months the highest dose group walked about 60 m farther in a six-minute walk test than at baseline.
