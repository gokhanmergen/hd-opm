FLOWPROMOTE: combination therapy shrank soft plaque 28% vs 20% with LDL near 42 vs 70

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A shared post on FLOWPROMOTE (JAMA Cardiology, Oct 30, 2026): 104 statin-naive people with coronary narrowing were scanned with CT, then given atorvastatin 40 mg or rosuvastatin 40 mg plus ezetimibe 10 mg. After 18 months LDL fell to 42.5 versus 69.5 mg/dL, and soft plaque shrank 28.2% versus 20.0%. Louis shares it with hashtags only.

Lp(a) explained: an LDL particle with a tail, genetic, unaffected by standard drugs

Sep 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Lp(a)

Louis shares graphics explaining Lp(a). It is an LDL-like particle that is genetically set, affects about one in five people, and is not lowered by statins, ezetimibe, fibrates or fish oil. New drugs lower it by 80-99%, but benefit on outcomes was not yet shown.

No randomized trial shows ezetimibe alone lowers heart attack or stroke risk

Aug 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis warns against stopping a statin for ezetimibe monotherapy because no randomized trial has shown ezetimibe alone reduces events. It is usually prescribed in addition to statins.

Ez-PAVE: LDL target below 55 mg/dL cut major events 33% versus below 70 mg/dL

May 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Shared summary of the Ez-PAVE NEJM trial randomizing 3,048 patients with ASCVD to LDL target below 1.4 mmol/L (55 mg/dL) or below 1.8 mmol/L (70 mg/dL) for 3 years. The intensive target had fewer events with equivalent safety, supporting combination lipid-lowering earlier.

EZ-PAVE: LDL target <55 vs <70 cut major CV events by 33% in over 3,000 patients

Mar 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 15 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

A NEJM randomized trial of over 3,000 patients with established heart disease compared an LDL goal under 70 (statin) with under 55 (statin plus ezetimibe). The lower target had 6.6% vs 9.7% major events over 3 years with similar safety.
