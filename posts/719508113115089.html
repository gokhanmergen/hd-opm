One admin's story: high-risk blood disorder, LDL 70, hsCRP down from 1.2 to 0.2

Gokhan Can · Dec 05, 2023 · ♥ 22 reactions · 19 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan describes his personal regimen at 45 with a chronic blood disorder, tracking BMI, blood pressure, LDL, glucose and inflammation. His latest labs show LDL 70 and hsCRP 0.2, with a BMI of about 32.5 still being brought down and no calcification on heart CT.

  • Targets: BMI <30, systolic BP <120 mmHg, LDL <70 mg/dL, glucose <90 mg/dL
  • LDL 70, HDL 45, triglycerides 79, total cholesterol 131 mg/dL
  • hsCRP fell from about 1.2 to 0.2
  • High fasting insulin and HOMA-IR; no calcification on heart CT

Gokhan's Story

As a 45-year-old with a chronic blood disorder, I am at high risk for developing heart disease and blood clots. Together with my team of physicians and specialists, we have developed a customized regimen to proactively monitor and manage my health. Key markers we are tracking include keeping my BMI under 30, systolic blood pressure below 120mmHg, LDL below 70mg/dL, blood sugar under 90 mg/dL, and inflammation low. By closely following this regimen, I hope to reduce my risks and maintain my long-term wellbeing.

  • Latest LDL 70mg/dL, HDL 45mg/dL, Trigs 79 mg/dL, TC 131 mg/dL.
  • HS-CRP used to be around 1.2, the most recent measurement was 0.2.
  • Almost always good fasting blood sugar (averaging 90 or lower).
  • BMI is around 32.5, we're trying to bring it below 30. I dieted all my life, but it's been yo-yo every year, until this drug came along.
  • High fasting insulin and HOMA-IR in general.
  • No calcification seen in heart CT scan.
  • I get labs regularly, and track a range of blood markers, body weight, sleep, exercise.
