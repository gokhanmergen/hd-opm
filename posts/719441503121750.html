New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Aug 31, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 38 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified. Carvalho argues calcium stabilises plaque, so slowing calcification without reducing plaque could even be harmful, and no evidence shows K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, so he advises relying on proven therapies, diet and exercise.

Vitamin C has not reduced heart attacks or strokes and plaque data are mixed

Apr 12, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Admin summarises that vitamin C alone has been tested widely without reducing hard outcomes (MACE), and plaque-reversal data are mixed: supportive in animals, better/worse/no effect in human trials. He does not advise it as a heart-disease cure, though physiological doses appear safe; he takes 250 mg/day to avoid deficiency. Links an (largely AI-generated, author-vetted) summary document.

WOSCOPS 20-year follow-up: 5 years of pravastatin gave lasting mortality and event benefit

Apr 07, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Statins

Describes the 20-year follow-up of the West of Scotland Coronary Prevention Study: 5 years of pravastatin in middle-aged men with high cholesterol and no prior CHD produced a lasting legacy effect, with reduced all-cause, CV and coronary mortality and fewer heart failure and MI hospitalizations. No excess cancer or non-CV mortality was seen.

Shingles vaccine tied to slower biological aging and less inflammation in 3,800 elders

Feb 28, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 22 · 💬 11 Infections & vaccines

Reports a large national study of more than 3,800 Americans aged 70+, in which shingles-vaccinated people showed slower biological aging, lower chronic inflammation and slower aging-related changes in gene activity. This suggests the vaccine may calm 'inflammaging', the low-grade inflammation tied to heart disease, frailty and cognitive decline.
