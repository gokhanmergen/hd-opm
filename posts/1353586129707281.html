Yale: taurine loss triggers NLRP3 inflammasome; raising taurine eased inflammaging in mice

Nov 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Inflammation & CRP

Louis shares a summary of a Yale Cell study showing macrophages release up to 80% of their taurine after inflammatory triggers, which helps activate the NLRP3 inflammasome. Restoring taurine suppressed NLRP3 activation, and raising taurine in older mice improved grip strength, coordination, some glucose measures and inflammatory markers. It is preclinical research.

Taurine lowers age-related inflammation via the NLRP3 inflammasome in mouse macrophages

Oct 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A Cell paper from Yale reports that taurine levels increase with age in mice and humans, and boosting taurine further in aged mice increased healthspan and dampened inflammation by regulating the NLRP3 inflammasome in macrophages.

Mouse taurine doses of 500-1000 mg/kg scale to about 2.8-5.7 g for a 70 kg human

Oct 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 7 Aging & longevity

Louis explains that the 2023 Columbia taurine study used 500-1000 mg/kg in mice, and that allometric scaling for faster mouse metabolism converts this to about 2.8-5.7 g for a 70 kg person. He suggests that is the range to test in human trials, since mouse mg/kg doses cannot be applied directly to humans.

Taurine is not a reliable aging biomarker, says new multi-species study

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A shared summary of a large longitudinal study across humans (26-100 years), monkeys and mice finds taurine levels do not consistently fall with age, often rise (especially in women), and vary more between individuals than with age. The post notes that taurine, like NAD+ boosters and resveratrol, shows promise in animals but human evidence is limited.
