Meta-analysis: statins, bempedoic acid, ezetimibe and omega-3 lower CRP independent of LDL

Gokhan Can · Feb 19, 2024 · ♥ 11 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Inflammation & CRPPCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs
AI summary & key facts

Highlights a Cardiovascular Research meta-analysis of RCTs showing that statins, bempedoic acid, ezetimibe and omega-3 fatty acids significantly reduce CRP, independent of how much they lower LDL-C. The author adds a list of other anti-inflammatory options to consider, such as colchicine and GLP-1 RAs.

Ezetimibe beyond statins: fewer events, no mortality gain in trials, registry benefits

Nov 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Evidence digest on ezetimibe. A meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (123,785 patients) found add-on lipid lowering cut cardiovascular events 19% versus statins alone but did not improve all-cause mortality. Other data show moderate statin plus ezetimibe matches high-intensity statins, and registry studies hint at lower mortality, with strong LDL-target attainment.
