Shared claim: most people lack potassium, and low intake worsens sodium's effect on BP

Louis Nervegna · Nov 06, 2024 · ♥ 14 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Blood pressure, salt & potassium
AI summary & key facts

Louis reposts a FoundMyFitness note that over 90% of people are deficient in potassium and that low intake disrupts the sodium-potassium balance regulating blood pressure. He adds that the RDA is 5 g per day. Banana, pistachio and avocado are listed as sources.

  • More than 90% of people deficient in potassium (per shared post)
  • Low potassium makes excess sodium more harmful for blood pressure
  • Foods: bananas, pistachios, avocados

The RDA for potassium is 5g per day.

Potassium-enriched salt cut recurrent stroke 14% and stroke deaths 21% in stroke survivors

Feb 11, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 10 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

A cluster randomized trial in rural northern China (a subgroup analysis of a larger trial) gave stroke survivors a salt substitute containing 25% potassium chloride. It reduced recurrent stroke, hemorrhagic stroke and stroke-related death compared with regular salt, suggesting a cheap, widely usable strategy where sodium intake is high.
