Weight gain and liver fat feed insulin resistance in a self-reinforcing cycle

Louis Nervegna · Aug 25, 2025 · ♥ 5 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Louis describes a vicious cycle in which weight gain and liver fat raise insulin through insulin resistance, which in turn promotes more weight gain and liver fat, often ending in obesity, fatty liver, diabetes and heart disease. He says breaking it requires a diet that removes excess abdominal and liver fat.

  • Weight gain and liver fat induce insulin resistance and high insulin, which promotes further weight and liver fat gain

It’s a vicious positive feedback cycle. Weight gain and liver fat cause high insulin, via inducing insulin resistance— which in turn makes it easier to gain even more weight and accumulate even more liver fat. There is a snowball effect.

For many people, it ultimately culminates in obesity, fatty liver, diabetes, and heart disease.

The only way to truly break this viscious cycle is through a healthy diet that gets rid of any excess weight in the abdomen and clears the liver of ectopic fat: ↪ Liver fat is the biggest driver of insulin resistance in most people, per a Harvard expert
