Keto beat Mediterranean and low-fat diets on liver fat and glycemia at equal weight loss

Aug 27, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Petersen et al. (Cell Metabolism, 2026) fed 42 adults with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver all meals in a controlled trial until ~10% weight loss. All diets improved muscle insulin sensitivity and body composition, but the very-low-carb ketogenic diet gave clearly larger improvements in liver metabolic function, intrahepatic fat and glycemic control.

Flavanol-rich cocoa improved 6-minute walk distance by 42.6 m in peripheral artery disease

Sep 04, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 3 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A six-month double-blind pilot RCT in 44 older adults with PAD tested a daily flavanol-rich cocoa drink against placebo. The cocoa group walked farther 2.5 hours after consumption while placebo declined, with better calf muscle mitochondrial activity, capillary density and perfusion.

No drug is approved for sarcopenia; strength training and protein remain the foundation

Jul 13, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 8 Exercise & fitness

A review summary (Rolland et al., Metabolism, Dec 2023) of drug options for age-related muscle loss. No drug has regulatory approval for sarcopenia, few trials enrolled diagnosed patients, and non-drug measures remain first-line.

Tricaprin supplement reversed atherosclerosis in two patients with TGCV

Jan 09, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 22 Supplements & vitamins

Osaka University research in the European Heart Journal reported that tricaprin, a dietary supplement that promotes lipid breakdown in heart muscle cells, reversed atherosclerotic lesions and relieved chest pain in two diabetic patients with TGCV. This condition involves triglyceride deposits and resists standard treatment. Only two patients were reported.

Bisphosphonates for bone loss: fracture reductions summarized

Oct 13, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Review of bisphosphonate evidence for osteoporosis. Alendronate cuts vertebral and non-vertebral fractures by about 50% in women with prior vertebral fractures; yearly IV zoledronic acid cuts vertebral fractures 70% and hip fractures 40%; a 2017 meta-analysis found overall fracture OR 0.62. Not a heart topic but relevant to aging.

Gokhan argues creatinine and eGFR are imperfect kidney markers and ranks five tests worth tracking, especially for people on many supplements or drugs: cystatin C (very useful, independent of muscle mass), CBC/chemistry/lipids, urine albumin/creatinine ratio, urine protein/creatinine and urinalysis.
