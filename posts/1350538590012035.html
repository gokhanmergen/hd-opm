Shared from Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health · original post

Most adults can safely consume up to 400 mg of caffeine each day—the amount found in up to five eight-ounce cups of coffee—and doing so may have cardiovascular benefits, according to a recent American Heart Association statement published in Circulation. Harvard Chan School’s Frank Hu was vice chair and a co-author of the statement, which outlined the current body of knowledge on caffeine and heart health and where more research is needed.

“We have made tremendous progress in understanding the health effects of caffeine, but there is still a lot to learn,” said Hu.
