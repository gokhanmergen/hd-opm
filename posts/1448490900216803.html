Protein type matters more than restriction: whole-food plant protein beats processed meat

Louis Nervegna · Nov 12, 2026 · ♥ 18 reactions · 38 comments · original Facebook post
ProteinMeat, eggs & dairyAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues against the 'protein craze': there is no good evidence protein restriction slows human aging, but long-term data show whole-food plant protein beats animal protein, especially processed and red meat. Among animal sources, seafood, poultry and fermented dairy have the lowest mortality and processed meat is worst. Plant protein's lower isoleucine, valine and methionine may explain part of it.

  • No good human evidence that protein restriction slows aging
  • Whole-food plant protein beats animal protein for mortality
  • Lowest-mortality animal sources: seafood, poultry, fermented dairy
  • Processed meat is worst by far

As I have brought up many times before, there are good reasons to be deeply suspicious of the on-going “protein craze”.

While there is still no good evidence that protein restriction actually slows aging in humans, like it does in lower animals such as mice… there is very good evidence that the type of protein that you consume makes a big difference.

When it comes to long-term data on mortality in humans, plant protein from whole food consistently outperforms animal protein, especially processed and red meat. When it comes purely to animal protein, the sources associated with the lowest mortality are seafood, poultry, and fermented dairy. Processed meat is the worst, by far. Reference: ↪ Protein type matters more than amount: processed meat worst, plant protein best.

One of the possible explanatory reasons for these results is that plant protein is naturally lower in the amino acids isoleucine, valine, and methionine, which shorten lifespan in lower animals like mice.
