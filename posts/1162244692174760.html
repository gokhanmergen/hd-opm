CHIP (clonal hematopoiesis): a little-known risk factor for heart disease and stroke

Apr 08, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Risk assessment & lab tests

Explains clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential: blood cells carrying acquired mutations that raise the risk of blood cancers, coronary disease and stroke. It becomes more common with age, can be detected with a next-generation sequencing panel, and has no specific treatment yet. The post notes testosterone therapy and anything strongly stimulating bone marrow may help CHIP expand.

Ultra-processed foods tied to about 50% higher CVD death risk in a 45-meta-analysis review

Feb 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares a CNN report on a review of 45 meta-analyses covering almost 10 million people. Higher ultra-processed food intake was linked to over 70% of the 45 health outcomes assessed, with strong evidence of roughly 50% higher risk of cardiovascular death and common mental disorders. Associations are observational.

North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. The shared cardiologist's post describes North Karelia's 700 per 100,000 CVD death rate in 1972, with 24% of calories from saturated fat, and a 20% cholesterol drop with coronary deaths in working-age men falling 82%.
