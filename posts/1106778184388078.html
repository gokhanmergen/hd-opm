Veritasium video shared with view that conventional foods carry glyphosate residues

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan shares a Veritasium documentary and states his opinion that conventionally grown wheat, oats and legumes are the most contaminated. He notes human health data on glyphosate are sparse. The linked video's metadata describes it as being about farmers and seed laws.

Veritasium Illuminates Biotoxins in Food Supply

Please be aware that conventional foods in USA are doused in glyphosate and other pesticides. Wheat, oats, legumes etc tend to be highest contaminated. Fruits, vegetables, animal products, nuts tend to be safe(r). This stuff cannot be good for you. It's not very different from air pollution, smoking, and other toxic exposures as far as I'm concerned. Except the correlation between glyphosate exposure and human health is minimally studied --- studies are far and between, despite the extremely dubious nature of these chemicals (they're biotoxins to plants, fungi, bacteria etc).

Veritasium recently put out a documentary about its chemistry and biology (very interesting!!): Why Farmers Can't Legally Replant Their Own Seeds (Veritasium)

A must watch for health enthusiasts!

CDC & EPA should rather study environmental toxin <-> human health relationship, instead of re-litigating correlations which have been debunked many times.
