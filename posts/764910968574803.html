Alpha Ketoglutarate (AKG)

(AKG is one of the most impactful longevity supplements with some evidence of benefits against cardiovascular disease. I prefer the AKG formulation from Double Wood)

Alpha-ketoglutarate (AKG) is a molecule involved in cellular energy production and metabolism. Research suggests AKG may have anti-aging effects. A 2014 study showed AKG extends lifespan in worms by around 50% by inhibiting ATP synthase and the target of rapamycin (TOR) pathway. AKG seems to work by mildly suppressing mitochondrial function and ATP production, triggering pathways linked to longevity like AMPK, FoxO and autophagy. The mild suppression of mitochondrial function is believed to mimic effects of calorie restriction. More recent research has shown AKG can also extend healthspan and lifespan in aging mice. Current findings suggest AKG could become a promising anti-aging nutraceutical.
