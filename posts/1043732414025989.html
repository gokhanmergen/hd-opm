FOURIER stroke analysis: LDL below 40 mg/dL cut major events ~31% and recurrent stroke 27%

Nov 13, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 26 · 💬 2 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Summarises a Circulation analysis of 5,200+ patients with prior ischemic stroke from FOURIER and its extension: lower achieved LDL-C meant fewer events. Versus LDL >=70 mg/dL, below 40 mg/dL had ~31% fewer major CV events and 27% fewer recurrent strokes, and even <20 mg/dL showed no excess hemorrhagic stroke.

Cholesterol as a master regulator of blood stem cells, driving inflammatory hematopoiesis

Oct 19, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Inflammation & CRP

Review of preclinical evidence that high LDL pulls hematopoietic stem cells out of dormancy, causing proliferation, oxidative stress, telomere shortening, impaired repopulation and lasting epigenetic changes that program blood and immune cells toward chronic inflammation. This offers a molecular basis for residual inflammatory risk after cholesterol is treated.

KETO-CTA compared high-LDL to very-high-LDL keto dieters with no low-LDL control group

Apr 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 21 · 💬 20 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Gokhan argues media coverage misrepresented KETO-CTA: it compared keto dieters with LDL around 200 to those around 300, with no low-LDL control, and still showed substantial new plaque, progressing like high-risk conditions. He says he would not keto as a lean mass hyper-responder and controls his own LDL with low-dose rosuvastatin.

NLA consensus: apoB testing helps when LDL-C and risk are discordant

Oct 15, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 6 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Summary of a National Lipid Association expert consensus on apoB testing. ApoB counts all atherogenic particles and may predict risk better than LDL-C, especially after statin treatment when the two diverge, and testing is suggested for borderline-risk patients deciding on a statin and in other discordant cases.

Over a third of 'low-risk' adults had coronary plaque; low LDL and ApoB were protective

Aug 18, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A JACC Advances study of 1,033 asymptomatic adults aged 40-65 classified as low risk found 35.9% had coronary plaque and 25.3% early calcification, increasing with higher LDL, non-HDL cholesterol and apoB, even below guideline levels. Gokhan reads it as showing that keeping LDL under 70 mg/dL greatly reduces risk.

Why low LDL-C correlates with higher mortality: causation versus correlation

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 ↩ 2 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Points to a video on how ignoring the difference between correlation and causation can mislead, and argues that the association of low LDL-C with higher mortality is a marker of illnesses such as cancer or liver disease rather than a cause. Also links a paper from the author's cardiologist on causal roles of LDL-C, HDL, triglycerides and inflammation.
