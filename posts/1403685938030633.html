Paleo diet is a myth: our ancestors also ate wild grains, legumes and starchy tubers

Louis Nervegna · Sep 25, 2026 · ♥ 6 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsCarbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains
AI summary & key facts

A video interview with Harvard palaeoanthropologist Daniel Lieberman explains why the paleo diet is not what Stone Age ancestors ate. The poster highlights that they also ate wild grains, legumes and starchy tubers.

  • Paleolithic ancestors ate wild grains, legumes and starchy tubers
  • Paleo diet makes little evolutionary sense according to Lieberman

A Harvard paleo-anthropologist explains why the “paleo diet” is not what your paleolithic ancestors actually ate.

Spoiler alert: They were also eating wild grains, legumes, and starchy tubers.

Quoted:

We are clearly mismatched to ultra-processed foods, so should we eat like our Stone Age ancestors?

The Paleo Diet suggests that we should banish the foods of modern life from our plates, and instead focus on lean meats and fish, fresh fruit and veg, and a handful of nuts and seeds.

Palaeoanthropologist Daniel Lieberman explains why this diet doesn't make a lot of evolutionary sense, and combats some of the health claims used to encourage people to take it up.
