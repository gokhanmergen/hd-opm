Shared from Louis Nervegna · original post

Removing fat in the liver and pancreas eliminates type 2 diabetes.

It’s about as close as you can get to a cure.

Roy Taylor has proven this in a large well-done randomized controlled trial.

The trial accomplished this with a very low calorie diet that caused major loss of visceral fat over a period of 8 weeks.

The low calorie liquid diet that he used in this large trial cleared the fat out of the liver and pancreas— and as a result, insulin sensitivity and beta cell function returned to normal. The diabetes disappeared.

The average weight loss necessary to clear the liver and pancreas of fat in Taylor’s trials was 15kg or 33lbs.

After the 8 week weight loss period with the liquid diet was over, patients were transitioned to a normal calorie traditional mediterranean diet for maintenance.

Some patients in this trial experienced a full curative response. All clinical signs of their diabetes disappeared and their pancreas regenerated (as proven on imaging) with full restoration of beta cell function:
