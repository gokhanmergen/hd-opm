Louis: heart-disease death rates have been falling for decades

Louis Nervegna · Dec 20, 2023 · ♥ 6 reactions · 29 comments · original Facebook post

Louis states that heart disease rates have declined for decades and continue to, mainly from better risk-factor awareness and treatment, and that those who claim otherwise are misinformed. He links a CDC MMWR report; no numbers are given in the post.

  • Decline attributed to better awareness of risk factors and better treatments

Heart disease rates have been dropping for decades, and this trend has continued to the present day.

This is primarily the result of better awareness of risk factors and better treatments.

People claiming the opposite are either ignorant or are purposely misleading you.

High Lp(a) raises CVD death risk 16% per 50 mg/dL, independent of other risk factors

May 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 22 Lp(a)

Louis argues Lp(a) alone raises heart disease mortality even in people with no other risk factors. Citing a Current Opinion in Lipidology review, he notes each 50 mg/dL increase corresponds to a hazard ratio of 1.16 for CVD mortality, and works an example: Lp(a) of 250 mg/dL would imply roughly an 80% higher risk, taking a 20% lifetime risk to about 36%. The linear extrapolation is his own illustration.

North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. The shared cardiologist's post describes North Karelia's 700 per 100,000 CVD death rate in 1972, with 24% of calories from saturated fat, and a 20% cholesterol drop with coronary deaths in working-age men falling 82%.
