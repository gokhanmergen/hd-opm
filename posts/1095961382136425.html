In animal models (rats), surgically removing visceral fat significantly extends lifespan.

This is a fascinating study because it hints that a big chunk of the life extending benefit of calorie restriction in mammals may actually be coming simply from the reduction in visceral fat.

This fits well with hundreds of epidemiological studies in humans which have shown that BMI is the single biggest modifiable risk factor for chronic disease, not counting smoking and heaving drinking.

It also fits well with randomized controlled trials in humans whch have shown that reducing visceral fat reverses diabetes, fatty liver, insulin resistance, and metabolic syndrome (reference Roy Taylor).
