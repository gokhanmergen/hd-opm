I want to emphasize this again.

The research showing that omega 6 fats likely PROTECT against heart disease and diabetes is NOT based only on food frequency questionnaires or studies about cholesterol.

It is based primary on direct measurements of omega 6 in blood and tissue. They measure the levels of omega 6 in blood and tissue periodically for decades BEFORE people get sick. Then they record who gets heart attacks, strokes, and diabetes and who does not. This is called a prospective cohort study, and it is the highest quality evidence in medicine short of a long-term randomized controlled trial. It is impossible run a randomized controlled trial on this subject for decades, so prospective cohort studies are the only rigorous way to test this scientifically in humans over a long period of time.

The people who have the highest omega 6 levels in blood and tissue for the longest time, have the lowest rates of heart disease and diabetes. The people who exclude omega 6 from their diet and have the lowest blood and tissue levels, instead have the highest rates of heart attack, stroke, and diabetes.

↪ Higher omega-6 (linoleic acid) biomarker levels linked to 43% lower type 2 diabetes risk

↪ Higher omega-6 biomarker levels linked to 22% lower CVD death in 30-cohort pooled analysis

Omega 6 fats are essential. Your body cannot make them. If you have high blood and tissue levels for a long time, then the only possible cause of that is high dietary intake of omega 6.

If you are excluding omega 6 from your diet (e.g. by avoiding nuts and seeds) then you are going to have very low blood and tissue levels of omega 6 in the long term. As a result, there is a very good chance that you are causing serious harm to your health. You are probably raising your long-term chances of having a heart attack, stroke, diabetes, etc. by doing this.

Influencers telling you to avoid eating nuts and seeds because they are high in omega 6 are causing very serious harm.
