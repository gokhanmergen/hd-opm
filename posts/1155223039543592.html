Gut-microbe D-lactate raises blood sugar and liver fat; an oral 'trap' helped obese mice

Jul 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Gut microbiome

Canadian researchers found D-lactate made by gut bacteria signals the liver to produce excess glucose and fat in obese mice and humans. An oral polymer trap binding it in the gut improved glucose control, insulin resistance and liver inflammation in mice without changing diet or weight.

Fatty liver can scar the liver years before ALT rises; 10% weight loss resolves it in 90%

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Routine ALT testing can miss advanced fatty liver disease: a Hepatology study biopsied 51 patients with normal ALT and found 12 with bridging fibrosis and 6 with cirrhosis. The post notes selection bias in that sample but says fibrosis behind a normal ALT is well established. Ten percent body weight loss resolved fatty liver in 90 percent and regressed fibrosis in 45 percent, and Mediterranean diet, resistance training and insulin-resistance management reduce liver fat independent of weight.

Resistance training lowers liver fat and insulin resistance markers despite no weight loss

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis notes that resistance training reduces liver fat and insulin resistance markers even when body weight does not fall, citing a Gut 2011 study in non-alcoholic fatty liver disease. The shared post adds that a Mediterranean-style diet and 150 minutes of walking or biking a week help reverse fatty liver.

Fatty liver and liver stiffness reversed in one year on a Mediterranean diet

Oct 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A dietitian shares a single client's success story: complete reversal of fatty liver and liver scarring within a year on a high-polyphenol Mediterranean diet including beans and nuts. The poster stresses this is not just anecdote, since randomized trials show this type of diet reverses liver fat, and links earlier posts.

Choline deficiency can cause fat to accumulate in the liver

Sep 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A shared post explains that choline is needed for phosphatidylcholine, which packages and exports liver fat in lipoproteins. Severely inadequate intake can cause liver fat to accumulate in controlled feeding studies and is reversible with adequate choline. Good sources include eggs, liver, meat, fish and soybeans.

CBD and CBG reduced liver fat and improved blood sugar in obese mice after 4 weeks

Sep 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 8 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a mouse study in which CBD and CBG reduced liver fat and improved blood glucose, with CBG also boosting insulin sensitivity, via phosphocreatine buffering rather than cannabinoid receptors. He emphasizes it is just a mouse study, so human relevance is unproven.
