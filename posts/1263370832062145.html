Shared from BoxLife Magazine · original post

A Kansas State nutrition professor lost 27 pounds in 10 weeks by eating mainly Twinkies and Doritos while capping calories at 1,800 per day.

Shockingly, his LDL cholesterol dropped 20%, HDL rose 20%, and triglycerides fell 39% despite the junk-food diet.

The experiment underscores that calorie control drives weight loss and metabolic gains, no matter the food source.

Yet critics warn about missing nutrients and unknown long-term health risks without whole grains, fruits, and veggies.
