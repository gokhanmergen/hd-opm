Shared from David Perlmutter, M.D. · original post

When you think about GLP-1 medications, weight loss is probably the first thing that comes to mind.

What I actually find most interesting is what these medications are teaching us about the brain.

GLP-1s appear to influence pathways involved in metabolism, insulin signaling, inflammation, reward processing, and more. It's a reminder that the brain is deeply connected to the rest of the body. I think that's a humbling realization.

The more we learn, the more we discover just how interconnected our biology really is.
