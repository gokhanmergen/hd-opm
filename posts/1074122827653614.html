AHA now lists sleep among the 8 most important modifiable heart disease risk factors

Louis shares a Medscape report on a new AHA statement on multidimensional sleep health and cardiovascular health, urging clinicians to ask about more than sleep duration.

  • Sleep is one of the Life's Essential 8

The latest guidance from the AHA lists sleep as one of the 8 most important modifiable risk factors for heart disease.

(The other 7 were described in this post: ↪ Following AHA Life's Essential 8 for ~10 years linked to 66% lower coronary heart disease)
