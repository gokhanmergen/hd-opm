90% of people who suffer cardiac arrest die.

“Sudden cardiac death is the largest cause of natural death in the United States. It causes about 325,000 adult deaths in the nation each year. Sudden cardiac death is responsible for half of all heart disease deaths.”

(This 90% statistic applies to cardiac arrest that happens outside of a hospital, e.g. at home or at work.)

Note that a cardiac arrest is not the same as a heart attack.

A cardiac arrest is when your heart completely stops beating.

Most heart attacks do not result is cardiac arrest— but when they do, they are usually deadly.

New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified.
