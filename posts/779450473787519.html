Mar 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 19 Supplements & vitamins

Louis explains he takes K2 for insulin sensitivity, not unproven calcification claims. In a Diabetes Care 2011 trial, 42 participants given 30 mg MK-4 for 4 weeks improved insulin sensitivity and disposition index, apparently via osteocalcin.

Vitamin K2 trials show better glucose and HbA1c in type 2 diabetes, per Life Extension

Sep 09, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a Life Extension article summarizing RCTs of vitamin K and glucose metabolism. Three RCTs of MK-7 vitamin K2 (90-360 mcg/day for 3-6 months) lowered fasting glucose and HbA1c in type 2 diabetes versus placebo, and trials in healthy and prediabetic people improved insulin sensitivity. Louis takes it for insulin sensitivity despite failed arterial calcification trials. Source is a supplement-industry magazine.

Vitamin K improved fasting glucose and HOMA-IR and was linked to lower T2DM risk

Apr 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 6 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis suggests the main value of vitamin K may be improving insulin sensitivity and lowering risk of pre-diabetes and diabetes, citing a 2023 systematic review and meta-analysis. The quoted conclusion is that supplementation significantly affected fasting blood sugar and HOMA-IR and that vitamin K was associated with reduced risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

Vitamin K2 slows coronary calcium mainly in severe calcification; event benefit unproven

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 16 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Review of vitamin K2 (with or without vitamin D) and CAC. A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n=1,533) found a -17.3 Agatston units/year pooled difference. The largest RCT (389 men, 720 ug MK-7 + 1000 IU D vs placebo for 2 years) missed significance overall but slowed CAC at baseline CAC >=400; some CKD trials were null. Not yet a standard therapy.

K2 plus D3 slowed coronary calcium progression about 21% in a 2-year trial

Sep 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 14 Supplements & vitamins

In 398 adults with severe coronary calcification, two years of 720 mcg vitamin K2 (MK-7) plus 1,000 IU vitamin D3 daily slowed calcium-score progression versus placebo. However, total plaque volume rose similarly in both groups among the 143 assessed, so the trial does not show plaque reversal or fewer heart attacks.
