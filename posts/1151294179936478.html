Wall squat isometric holds may lower systolic blood pressure about 9 points in weeks

Magnesium supplements modestly lower blood pressure across 34 trials

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

A shared summary of an analysis of 34 trials with over 2,000 participants found modest reductions in systolic and diastolic pressure with magnesium supplementation, at a median 368 mg/day. Magnesium helps blood vessels relax and widen. Magnesium-rich foods include leafy greens, nuts, seeds and legumes.

ACE inhibitors can cause angioedema, even years after starting

Sep 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

A health-safety post warns that people taking ACE inhibitors (lisinopril, ramipril, benazepril) can develop angioedema, swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, even years later and often without itching. Risk is higher over 65, in smokers, people of African descent and those on immunosuppressants. Real swelling or breathing trouble needs emergency care.
