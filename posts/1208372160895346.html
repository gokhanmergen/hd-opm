Protein and mortality: BMJ 2020 meta-analysis finds weak links, plant protein favorable

Jan 06, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 5 ↩ 1 Protein

Summarizes a BMJ 2020 systematic review and meta-analysis of 32 prospective cohorts. Higher total protein was associated with slightly lower all-cause mortality, and plant protein with lower all-cause and CVD mortality, while animal protein had a minuscule effect up to about 20% of calories.

Six lines of evidence favor whole-food plant protein over red and processed meat

Nov 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Protein

Louis recommends an article by group member Mert Erogul MD on protein and aging. It argues six lines of evidence converge: growth-signaling genetics, IGF-1 biomarker data, rodent protein restriction through FGF21 and mTORC1, the roles of methionine, isoleucine and valine, short-term human trials, and epidemiology showing source matters more than amount. Plant protein from whole foods is favored.

Harvard cohorts: plant protein linked to lower CVD and total mortality over 30 years

Sep 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 15 Protein

Louis summarizes Harvard Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study findings that higher plant protein intake is strongly associated with lower CVD and all-cause mortality over 30 years, whereas animal protein is not, driven by red and processed meat. Seafood and yogurt look favorable. The shared text cites a Rotterdam Study plus meta-analysis of protein intake and mortality.

Another large cohort: higher plant protein linked to 35% lower all-cause mortality

Sep 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 3 Protein

Louis notes that another large prospective cohort confirms Harvard findings on protein. Higher plant protein intake was associated with 35% lower all-cause mortality, while animal protein showed no significant association. The shared summary adds that the effect likely reflects whole plant foods such as grains, legumes and vegetables.

Six converging evidence lines favor plant protein over red and processed meat

Aug 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 Protein

Louis shares an Ageproof article by group member Mert Erogul MD arguing that genetics, IGF-1 biomarkers, rodent experiments, amino-acid decomposition, short-term human trials and epidemiology converge: methionine, isoleucine and valine, not leucine or total protein, are implicated. Favoring whole-food plant protein over red and processed meat likely extends lifespan.

High insulin tells the liver to make cholesterol, tying insulin resistance to high LDL

Aug 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that insulin signals the liver to produce cholesterol, one reason insulin-resistant people tend to have high LDL. Losing visceral and especially liver fat to correct insulin resistance should therefore bring LDL down naturally. Supported by a Mass General Brigham explainer.
