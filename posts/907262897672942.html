Adding ezetimibe to a statin lowers CRP by an extra 10% in pooled trials

Gokhan Can · Nov 09, 2024 · ♥ 7 reactions · 9 comments · original Facebook post
PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugsInflammation & CRP
AI summary & key facts

Pooled analysis of placebo-controlled ezetimibe trials found ezetimibe alone lowered CRP numerically more than placebo (6%, not significant), while added to statin therapy it gave a significant extra 10% CRP reduction. Gokhan lists related studies on ezetimibe's anti-inflammatory effects.

  • Ezetimibe monotherapy: 6% greater CRP reduction vs placebo (p=0.09)
  • Ezetimibe plus statin: 10% additional CRP reduction (p<0.001)

Effects of Ezetimibe on Inflammation

Pooled Analyses of Effects on C-Reactive Protein and Low Density Lipoprotein Cholesterol in Placebo-Controlled Trials of Ezetimibe Monotherapy or Ezetimibe Added to Baseline Statin Therapy (2009)

Reduction in CRP by ezetimibe monotherapy was numerically greater than with placebo (treatment difference 6%, p = 0.09). Added to statin therapy, ezetimibe was associated with a significant additional reduction in CRP (treatment difference 10%, p <0.001). . In conclusion, the addition of ezetimibe to statin treatment provides significantly enhanced CRP reductions over and above those achieved with statin monotherapy.
