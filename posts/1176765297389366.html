COSMOS: cocoa flavanols cut CVD death 27% over 3.6 years in 21,442 older adults

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a summary of the COSMOS randomized trial of 21,442 older adults taking a 500 mg daily cocoa-extract supplement. It reduced CVD mortality by 27% (HR about 0.73) over 3.6 years as a secondary outcome, without a statistically significant reduction in the primary total CVD events endpoint.

  • n=21,442 older adults, 500 mg/day cocoa extract (standardized flavanols)
  • CVD mortality HR about 0.73 (27% lower) over 3.6 years
  • No significant reduction in total cardiovascular events (primary endpoint)
  • Mortality benefit was a secondary outcome

Shared from Healthy Harbor · original post

The COSMOS randomized clinical trial of 21,442 older adults found that taking a daily 500 mg cocoa-extract supplement resulted in a 27% reduction in cardiovascular-disease mortality (hazard ratio ≈ 0.73) over 3.6 years, while showing no statistically significant reduction in the primary endpoint of total cardiovascular events; the supplement contained standardized cocoa flavanols, and the mortality reduction was observed as a secondary outcome of the trial.

Cocoa flavanol extract cut CVD death 27% in the 21,000-person COSMOS trial

May 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 53 ↩ 4 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis summarizes the COSMOS randomized trial of 500 mg/day cocoa flavanols (and multivitamin) in over 21,000 adults for 3.6 years. Total cardiovascular events fell 10%, which was not statistically significant, but CVD death fell a significant 27% and major CV events were also significantly reduced. Adherence-adjusted analyses showed 15% fewer total events and 39% fewer CVD deaths; authors urge cautious interpretation.
