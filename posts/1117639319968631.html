AI summary & key facts

The NIA Interventions Testing Program found epicatechin raised median lifespan of male mice by about 5% and mitoglitazone, a next-generation TZD, by about 9%. Effects were seen only in males, and this is mouse data.

  • Epicatechin: about +5% median lifespan in male mice
  • Mitoglitazone: about +9% median lifespan in male mice
  • No lifespan benefit in female mice
  • Six compounds tested in total

Cocoa Flavonol and TZD extended mice lifespan in ITP

Good news for chocolate lovers and a hopeful sign for metabolic health. A new study just published by the prestigious Interventions Testing Program (ITP) has found some exciting keys to a longer lifespan, and one of them is found in cocoa.

Researchers with the National Institute on Aging's Interventions Testing Program (ITP), a major initiative to find real anti-aging compounds, tested six different substances in mice to see if they could extend life

➡️ Cocoa Power! A compound called Epicatechin, a healthy flavanol found in high concentrations in cocoa, extended the median lifespan of male mice by ~5%! It also increased their survival rate late in life. This adds more weight to the idea that the compounds in cocoa have powerful health benefits.

➡️ Next-Gen Diabetes Drugs Show Promise! A drug called Mitoglitazone had an even stronger effect, increasing the median lifespan of male mice by a remarkable ~9%! Mitoglitazone is a next-generation TZD (thiazolidinedione), a class of drugs that includes some used to treat type 2 diabetes by improving insulin sensitivity. This finding highlights the powerful link between metabolic health and longevity.

Pioglitazone in this class is one of my favorite off label drugs. Also earlier TZDs showed significant life extension in other mice studies, so this adds further evidence.

These lifespan-extending effects were only seen in male mice, not females. This continues a trend seen in these studies where many interventions have different effects depending on sex, showing just how complex the biology of aging is.

Paper · GeroScience · 2025Extension of lifespan by epicatechin, halofuginone and mitoglitazone in male but not female genetically heterogeneous miceMice bred in 2021 were tested by the Interventions Testing Program (ITP) for possible lifespan benefits of 2BAct (2BA), dichloroacetate (DCA), Epicatechin (EPI), Forskolin (FSK), Halofuginone (HAL) and Mitoglitazone (MIT). All agents were administered in the diet ad libitum beginning at 7 months of age. In male mice, EPI increased median lifespan by ~ 5%, and HAL and MIT each increased median lifespan by ~ 9%.
