Statins after early breast cancer diagnosis linked to 10% lower breast cancer death

Louis Nervegna · Nov 06, 2025 · ♥ 4 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post

A Medscape-reported Danish registry study found that starting statins within 3 years of early-stage breast cancer diagnosis was associated with a 10% lower risk of breast cancer death and 8% lower all-cause mortality.

Shared from Medscape · original post

Initiating statins within 3 years of diagnosis of early-stage breast cancer was associated with a modest but clinically meaningful 10% lower risk of dying from breast cancer and an 8% lower risk of dying from any cause, a recent Danish registry study suggests.

The researchers calculated that 59 patients would need to take statins to prevent one additional breast cancer death at 10 years. Statins May Improve Breast Cancer Survival Outcomes (Medscape)

Statins neither cause nor cure cancer: meta-analyses of randomized trials show no effect

Dec 31, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Statins

Reviews the evidence on statins and cancer. A 2012 meta-analysis of 134,537 participants in 22 RCTs found identical cancer incidence (1.4% per year) and mortality (0.5% per year) in statin users and non-users, and a 2023 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs found the same. Anticancer effects in specific contexts remain preclinical or suggestive.

Statin use linked to 35% lower odds of liver cancer in UK database study

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Statins

A UK Clinical Practice Research Datalink case-control study of 3,719 liver cancer cases and 14,876 controls found statin users had 35% lower odds of liver cancer, and cholesterol absorption inhibitors (ezetimibe class) 31% lower odds. This is an association, not proof, and non-statin findings need replication.

Statin plus sildenafil may block cancer metastasis in preclinical models

Jul 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 4 Cancer

A 2026 Cancer Research study found sildenafil (a PDE5 inhibitor) caused cholesterol to accumulate in lysosomes of mouse and human cancer models, limiting cholesterol available to cancer cells and impairing their migration and spread. Combining it with a statin, which blocks cholesterol production, gave stronger anti-metastatic effects than either alone. Louis notes the research is preclinical but promising.

Ezetimibe-type absorption inhibitors linked to 31% lower liver cancer odds; statins 35%

Aug 01, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 3 ↩ 2 Statins

A genetic/drug-target analysis reported by Medical Xpress found cholesterol absorption inhibitors associated with 31% lower odds of liver cancer and statins with 35% lower odds. Fibrates, omega-3 and niacin showed no association, while bile acid sequestrants showed inconsistent results needing replication.

Statin RCT meta-analyses show no increase or decrease in cancer risk

Dec 31, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 13 Statins

Louis compiles meta-analyses of randomized trials showing statins neither raise nor lower cancer incidence or mortality, plus cohort studies suggesting neutral or lower cancer risk, e.g. prostate cancer. He adds that RCTs and prospective cohorts sit at the top of the evidence hierarchy.

Mendelian randomization shows genetically lower LDL does not raise cancer risk

May 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that old observational links between low LDL and cancer are largely reverse causality. MR studies find genetically lowered LDL does not increase overall cancer risk, and genetically higher LDL is linked to more breast and prostate cancer; statin proxies lower colorectal cancer risk.
