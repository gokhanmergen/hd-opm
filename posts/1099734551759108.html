Each 10 g of daily fiber lowers colon cancer risk about 10%, favoring Mediterranean diets

Louis Nervegna · Aug 27, 2025 · ♥ 3 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis quotes a claim that high fiber intake can cut colon cancer risk by up to 60%, with each 10 g/day lowering risk by about 10%. He ties this to why a fiber-rich Mediterranean diet outperforms others for cancer prevention.

  • High fiber may lower colon cancer risk by up to 60% (per quote)
  • Each 10 g/day of fiber lowers risk roughly 10%

A traditional Mediterranean diet protects against cancer, as well as heart disease.

Quoted:

“A high-fiber diet can slash your risk of colon cancer by up to 60%.

Each 10 grams of daily fiber intake lowers your risk by roughly 10%, making fiber one of the most powerful and under-appreciated tools for cancer prevention.

This is exactly why a Mediterranean-style diet— packed with fiber-rich fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and legumes— consistently outperforms other diets for lowering cancer risk.”
