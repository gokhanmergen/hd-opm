Could berberine actually protect against heart disease? New research suggests its effects may extend beyond improving blood sugar and cholesterol.

In two randomized trials, 500 mg of berberine twice daily reduced ApoB, a marker of cholesterol-containing particles in the blood. The reduction appeared nearly three times greater in women, although the comparison came from separate trials.

A newer study gave berberine to 84 men and measured 92 cardiovascular-related proteins, identifying a distinct pattern called the “Berberine Signature.”

When researchers examined this signature in thousands of UK Biobank participants, a stronger signature was associated with approximately 13% lower odds of ischemic heart disease.

However, the UK Biobank participants weren’t necessarily taking berberine. This means the findings do not prove that berberine supplementation prevents cardiovascular disease.

How to Use This: Berberine has evidence supporting improvements in cardiovascular risk factors, but clinical trials are still needed to establish whether it prevents actual cardiovascular events.
