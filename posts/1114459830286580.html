COSMOS cocoa extract lowers hsCRP in older adults, possibly explaining CVD benefit

Louis Nervegna · Sep 17, 2025 · ♥ 19 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
Inflammation & CRPSupplements & vitaminsNuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods
AI summary & key facts

Medical Xpress reports that daily cocoa extract supplementation lowered hsCRP, a key inflammation marker, in older adults in the COSMOS randomized trial. Louis notes this anti-inflammatory effect may partly explain the reduction in cardiovascular mortality seen in the trial.

  • COSMOS: 21,442 participants over age 60, 2014-2020
  • Double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized trial
  • Cocoa extract lowered hsCRP
  • CVD mortality reduction of almost 30% reported (per Louis)

Cocoa reduces CVD mortality by almost 30% over a 6 year period, and its anti-inflammatory effect may partially explain why.

Results of the COSMOS randomized trial at Harvard Medical School and Brigham and Women’s Hospital in Boston.

Quoted:

“Between 2014 and 2020, Brigham and Women's Hospital led the COSMOS trial, a large-scale, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial with 21,442 participants over 60 years old, finding that cocoa extract supplementation decreased cardiovascular disease mortality by 27%.

In this new study, researchers collected and analyzed blood samples of 598 COSMOS participants to measure several inflammaging biomarkers: three pro-inflammatory proteins (hsCRP, IL-6, and TNF-α), one anti-inflammatory protein (IL-10), and one immune-mediating protein (IFN-γ).

Comparing changes in these biomarkers measured at baseline, one, and two years follow-up, hsCRP levels decreased by 8.4% each year compared with placebo, while the other biomarkers remained relatively consistent or increased modestly.

The decrease in hsCRP may help explain the cardio-protective effects seen with cocoa extract supplement in the larger COSMOS trial, where participants experienced a reduction in cardiovascular disease death.”

Cocoa flavanol extract cut CVD death 27% in the 21,000-person COSMOS trial

May 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 53 ↩ 4 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis summarizes the COSMOS randomized trial of 500 mg/day cocoa flavanols (and multivitamin) in over 21,000 adults for 3.6 years. Total cardiovascular events fell 10%, which was not statistically significant, but CVD death fell a significant 27% and major CV events were also significantly reduced. Adherence-adjusted analyses showed 15% fewer total events and 39% fewer CVD deaths; authors urge cautious interpretation.
