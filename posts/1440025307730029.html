Layne Norton rebuts seed-oil claims with human evidence on inflammation, LDL and oxidation

Louis Nervegna · Nov 03, 2026 · ♥ 3 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Inflammation & CRPLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

A shared post argues that human trial evidence does not support the claim that seed oils are harmful. Systematic reviews of randomized trials find that raising linoleic acid does not consistently increase inflammatory markers, replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL and cuts coronary events, and trials show no significant increase in lipid peroxidation. It adds that LDL oxidation mostly occurs after retention in the artery wall, so LDL concentration still matters.

  • Replacing saturated with polyunsaturated fat: about 19% fewer coronary heart disease events (meta-analysis of RCTs)
  • Linoleic acid increases did not consistently raise inflammatory markers in humans (PMIDs 22889633, 28752873)
  • Trials increasing linoleic acid found no significant rise in lipid peroxidation or oxidative DNA damage

Shared from Layne Norton · original post

HOW DELICIOUSLY EVIL 😈🤣

“Seed oils are industrial lubricants!” So is water. Please stop lubricating your opinions with bullshit. 😂

Let’s look at HUMAN evidence:

🔥 INFLAMMATION: Systematic reviews of randomized trials find that increasing linoleic acid, the main omega-6 fat in many seed oils, does NOT consistently increase inflammatory markers in HUMANS. (PMIDs: 22889633 & 28752873)

❤️ HEART DISEASE: Replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat (PUFA) lowers LDL cholesterol. A meta-analysis of randomized trials found ~19% fewer coronary heart disease events with this substitution. What you REPLACE matters. (PMIDs: 37297374 & 20351774)

🧪 OXIDATION: “PUFAs can oxidize” does not establish that eating them increases oxidative damage in humans. Controlled trials increasing linoleic acid found no significant increase in measured lipid peroxidation or oxidative DNA damage. Your body is not a bottle of oil sitting on a windowsill. (PMIDs: 12399265 & 12504167)

And the “only OXIDIZED LDL matters” crowd misses a crucial step: LDL enters the artery wall and gets RETAINED beneath the endothelium, where it can undergo oxidation and other modifications. Most LDL oxidation is thought to occur there; abundant antioxidants strongly limit oxidation in circulating plasma. (PMID: 19888833)

More circulating LDL particles means more opportunities for arterial entry, retention, and subsequent modification. Oxidation doesn’t make LDL concentration irrelevant, it helps explain why retaining LDL is a problem & how much LDL gets retained is driven by its concentration in blood. PUFAs from seed oils reduce LDL (PMID: 17938300)

🫀 LIVER FAT: During matched overfeeding, saturated fat caused substantially more liver fat accumulation than PUFA, despite similar weight gain (PMIDs: 24550191 & 31369090)

📉 GLUCOSE CONTROL: A meta-analysis of controlled feeding trials found improvements in several glucose/insulin measures when PUFA replaced saturated fat (PMID: 27434027)

You don’t need to chug canola oil. But replacing it with butter because an influencer yelled “INFLAMMATION” isn’t evidence-based nutrition.

Fear is easy to sell. Human outcome data are harder to bullshit. 🤷‍♂️
