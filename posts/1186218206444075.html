Shared from Medscape · original post

A trio of large observational studies reported at the San Antonio Breast Cancer Symposium (SABCS) 2025 suggest that GLP-1 receptor agonists may improve outcomes in some women with breast cancer.

Two studies reported an overall survival benefit of GLP-1 use in certain patients with breast cancer, including those with ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS) and invasive hormone receptor (HR)-positive nonmetastatic disease, and a third found improvements in a range of toxicities among patients receiving chemotherapy. Statins neither cause nor cure cancer: meta-analyses of randomized trials show no effect

Dec 31, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Statins

Reviews the evidence on statins and cancer. A 2012 meta-analysis of 134,537 participants in 22 RCTs found identical cancer incidence (1.4% per year) and mortality (0.5% per year) in statin users and non-users, and a 2023 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs found the same. Anticancer effects in specific contexts remain preclinical or suggestive.
