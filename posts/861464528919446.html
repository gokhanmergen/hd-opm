Keto beat Mediterranean and low-fat diets on liver fat and glycemia at equal weight loss

Aug 27, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Petersen et al. (Cell Metabolism, 2026) fed 42 adults with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver all meals in a controlled trial until ~10% weight loss. All diets improved muscle insulin sensitivity and body composition, but the very-low-carb ketogenic diet gave clearly larger improvements in liver metabolic function, intrahepatic fat and glycemic control.

Fatty liver can scar the liver years before ALT rises; 10% weight loss resolves it in 90%

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Routine ALT testing can miss advanced fatty liver disease: a Hepatology study biopsied 51 patients with normal ALT and found 12 with bridging fibrosis and 6 with cirrhosis. The post notes selection bias in that sample but says fibrosis behind a normal ALT is well established. Ten percent body weight loss resolved fatty liver in 90 percent and regressed fibrosis in 45 percent, and Mediterranean diet, resistance training and insulin-resistance management reduce liver fat independent of weight.

Keto cut liver fat 67% vs 45% on Mediterranean or plant-forward diets in a small RCT

Aug 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 3 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis highlights a Cell Metabolism randomized trial of 42 people with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver assigned to ketogenic, Mediterranean or low-fat plant-forward diets with weight loss matched. Keto cut liver fat 67% versus 45% for the other diets. He notes it is small and wonders about a Mediterranean keto diet.

At least six strategies have reversed plaque in randomized trials

Aug 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists six strategies shown to reverse plaque in human RCTs: major weight loss, Mediterranean/DASH diet, LDL-lowering drugs (statins, PCSK9 inhibitors, ezetimibe), colchicine, exercise and high-dose EPA fish oil. He would like to see a trial combining all of them.

Vegans with insulin resistance should target liver fat; white potatoes are not health food

Louis advises vegans with insulin resistance, high blood sugar or high triglycerides that the main cause is excess visceral and liver fat, even in lean people. He also cautions against white potatoes, citing a Harvard trial testing whether they harm arterial endothelium, and prefers sweet potatoes.

PREDIMED-Plus: energy-reduced Mediterranean diet plus exercise better prevented diabetes

Aug 31, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a prespecified secondary analysis of the PREDIMED-Plus trial in adults with obesity and metabolic syndrome. An energy-reduced Mediterranean diet with physical activity was more effective than an unrestricted Mediterranean diet at lowering type 2 diabetes incidence, with modest weight loss.
