Sumo wrestlers stay metabolically healthy because heavy exercise shifts fat under the skin

Mar 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis explains why sumo wrestlers, despite huge calorie and refined-carbohydrate intake, store little fat in the liver and pancreas and little visceral fat. He attributes this to up to 7 hours of daily exercise, which releases adiponectin and steers fat storage to subcutaneous tissue, a reason exercise protects against metabolic syndrome and diabetes.

Louis shares a Harvard Health item on a JAMA Cardiology study finding that high omega-6 levels in subcutaneous fat, mostly reflecting dietary linoleic acid from plant oils, correlated with lower death rates in older men.

Metabolic syndrome, insulin resistance and visceral fat drive plaque growth beyond LDL

Sep 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 Insulin resistance & diabetes

An annotated evidence summary (reshared from Ken Gursky, with a long analysis) argues that residual plaque growth despite low LDL is linked to metabolic syndrome, insulin resistance and visceral fat. It cites imaging studies in which these factors predicted greater plaque progression, even on statins.

BRIDGE trial: Mediterranean diet cut weight and visceral fat but did not improve cognition

Jul 06, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 6 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

In a 14-month randomized trial of older, mainly African American adults with obesity, an eight-month Mediterranean diet intervention improved diet quality, weight and visceral fat, but no cognitive benefit appeared versus control.

MESA: CAC under 100, hsCRP under 2 and low visceral fat may protect those with high Lp(a)

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 6 Lp(a)

Louis summarizes MESA findings that three markers seem to protect people with high Lp(a): CAC score below 100, hsCRP below 2 mg/L and low visceral fat. He warns MESA is an observational cohort that shows associations, not causality.

Visceral fat had the strongest inflammatory protein signature of any trait in VITAL/COSMOS

Nov 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a cross-sectional proteomics analysis of 896 adults from VITAL and COSMOS. Visceral fat measured by DXA was associated with 221 circulating proteins, more than blood pressure, HDL, triglycerides or insulin resistance. It cannot prove causation, but suggests visceral fat is a meaningful target for reducing chronic inflammation.
