Willett's three diet changes: less refined starch and sugar, no soda, less processed meat

Louis Nervegna · Nov 06, 2026 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsCarbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a Walter Willett clip with his top three changes to the typical American diet: reduce refined starch, sugar and white potatoes; eliminate sugar-sweetened beverages; and reduce processed meat. Replacing processed meat with soy, beans or nuts is a double win.

  • About half of calories come from carbs, 80% of that refined starch, sugar and potatoes
  • Americans average about three sugary drink servings a day
  • Swap processed meat for soy, beans or nuts

If you could change only 3 things about the typical American diet, what would they be?

Harvard Professor Walter Willett’s answer:

  1. Reduce refined starch, refined sugar, white potatoes
  1. Eliminate sugar-sweetened beverages
  1. Reduce processed meat

Quoted from the original post:

Walter Willett has spent decades studying what actually keeps people healthy, so when we asked him for the only three diet changes worth making, we listened.

His list, in order: fix your carbs (half our calories come from carbs, and 80% of that is refined starch, sugar, and potatoes), cut sugar sweetened beverages (Americans average three servings a day), and pull back on processed and red meat.

The move that matters most: what you replace it with. Swap processed meat for soy, beans, or nuts and you get what he calls the "double win," less of the bad stuff, more of a plant based protein that shows up at the top of nearly every health outcome we study.
