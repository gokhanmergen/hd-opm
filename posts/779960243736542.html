A CAC score of zero does not eliminate risk from high Lp(a), especially in younger adults

Louis Nervegna · Apr 01, 2024 · ♥ 10 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Lp(a)Plaque, CAC & CT angiography
AI summary & key facts

Louis relays Thomas Dayspring's quote from the new NLA Lp(a) update: Lp(a) is not consistently associated with baseline CAC or CAC progression, implying distinct pathobiology, so a zero calcium score does not remove the risk of elevated Lp(a), particularly in younger adults. This contrasts with his earlier MESA-based post focused on 10-year risk.
