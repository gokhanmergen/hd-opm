MIND diet: 20% less grey matter loss per 3-point score rise in Framingham MRI study

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

In 1,647 Framingham Offspring participants followed about 12 years with repeated MRI scans, closer adherence to the MIND diet (Mediterranean-DASH hybrid) was linked to slower grey matter loss. Each 3-point rise in MIND score matched 20% less age-related grey matter decline, equal to about 2.5 years of slower brain aging. This is an observational association.
