Ezetimibe beyond statins: fewer events, no mortality gain in trials, registry benefits

Nov 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Evidence digest on ezetimibe. A meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (123,785 patients) found add-on lipid lowering cut cardiovascular events 19% versus statins alone but did not improve all-cause mortality. Other data show moderate statin plus ezetimibe matches high-intensity statins, and registry studies hint at lower mortality, with strong LDL-target attainment.

Platelet reactivity score (PRESS) predicts cardiovascular events in PAD patients

Aug 20, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 19 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

NYU researchers developed PRESS, combining platelet aggregation responses and RNA sequencing, to detect platelet hyperreactivity. In PAD patients, hyperreactive platelets predicted more major adverse cardiac or limb events within 30 days of revascularization, and PRESS above the median carried a 90% higher risk of major events over 18 months. It might help target aspirin.

SELECT trial: semaglutide cut major cardiovascular events 20% in obese non-diabetic adults

Dec 24, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 6 ↩ 1 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Summarizes the SELECT trial of 2.4 mg weekly semaglutide in about 17,000 adults with obesity but without diabetes, followed for 40 months. The primary composite of CV death, nonfatal MI and nonfatal stroke fell from 8.0% to 6.5%, with benefits for nonfatal MI, all-cause death, blood pressure and weight.
