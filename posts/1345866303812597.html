Epstein-Barr virus appears to drive multiple sclerosis via T cells

Louis highlights new research on how EBV provokes T cells that may drive autoimmunity in MS, and a Boston trial of an EBV vaccine for early MS. He asks how many other diseases might be virus-caused.

  • EBV appears to cause MS
  • Clinical trial of an EBV vaccine for early MS in the Boston area

One of the most remarkable discoveries in medicine during the last several years is the fact that multiple sclerosis appears to be caused by the Epstein Barr virus.

Scientists are now working out the mechanisms by which this occurs.
