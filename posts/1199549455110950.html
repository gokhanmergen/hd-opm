Video doubts Lp(a) causality; Louis still favors it based on Mendelian randomization

Louis Nervegna · Jan 11, 2026 · ♥ 11 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
Lp(a)How to read research
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a video citing reasons for caution that Lp(a) directly causes cardiovascular disease. He disagrees, noting that Mendelian randomization studies strongly point to causality while acknowledging the tool has a short track record.

  • Mendelian randomization studies strongly hint Lp(a) is causal (Louis's view)

He cites some reasons to be cautious about the idea that Lp(a) is directly causal in CVD.

(I don’t share his skepticism. Mendelian randomization studies do strongly hint at causality. The MR studies are what keep me optimistic. But he makes some good points. And mendellian randomization is still a new tool with a short track record.)

HDL and ApoA-I raising therapies repeatedly failed, so HDL is probably not directly causal

Mar 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 9 ↩ 22 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis reviews why HDL or ApoA-I is probably not causal for heart disease. CETP inhibitor trials failed (ILLUMINATE with torcetrapib was stopped for harm; dal-OUTCOMES with dalcetrapib and ACCELERATE with evacetrapib showed no benefit), as did fibrate and niacin trials and CSL112 infusions, and Mendelian randomization agrees. He proposes HDL and ApoA-I mainly act as a proxy for insulin sensitivity, which is the real causal factor.
