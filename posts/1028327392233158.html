In the United States, heart disease is the leading cause of death for men, women, and people of most racial and ethnic groups. As a PCP, your understanding of heart disease is critical so you can translate the information to your patients. One sometimes misunderstood factor in heart disease is inflammation — what it is, how it raises heart health risks, and how patients can keep its impact to a minimum. Dr Carvalho: evidence for vitamin K2 supplements and arterial calcium is weak

Nov 16, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 · 💬 27 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Carvalho video: arterial calcium is a marker of long-standing plaque, not the cause of risk, so lowering a calcium score can mislead, just as statins raise calcium while lowering risk. No large properly powered trials show K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, and he warns of false reassurance. The admin adds that supplement use is fine but expectations should stay grounded.

Measuring kidney health: add cystatin C and urine albumin-to-creatinine to creatinine eGFR

Gokhan reminds that creatinine-based eGFR alone is insufficient. Cystatin C eGFR can differ in about 30% of people, so the combined CKD-EPI creatinine-cystatin C equation is best, and urine protein and albumin-to-creatinine ratio catch early disease. Test at least yearly if on many meds or supplements.

FLOWPROMOTE: combination therapy shrank soft plaque 28% vs 20% with LDL near 42 vs 70

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A shared post on FLOWPROMOTE (JAMA Cardiology, Oct 30, 2026): 104 statin-naive people with coronary narrowing were scanned with CT, then given atorvastatin 40 mg or rosuvastatin 40 mg plus ezetimibe 10 mg. After 18 months LDL fell to 42.5 versus 69.5 mg/dL, and soft plaque shrank 28.2% versus 20.0%. Louis shares it with hashtags only.
