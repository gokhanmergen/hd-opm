For some people, this is a cure for type 2 diabetes.

It is not a remission or a temporary reversal.

Not everyone gets a full curative response like this patient did, but some do.

This is proven in a well-done randomized controlled trial:

↪ Roy Taylor shows the pancreas can regenerate in type 2 diabetes, by diet alone

↪ Roy Taylor trial: clearing liver and pancreas fat via 8-week low-calorie diet reversed T2D

This was an advanced case of type 2 diabetes. This patient had enormous physical damage to the pancreas from years of steadily-progressing diabetes, as the images clearly show.

After 2 years, all clinical signs of diabetes disappeared and the pancreas was almost fully regenerated back to normal. Beta cell function was fully restored.

This was accomplished entirely by diet alone. There were no drugs involved.

You can read past posts about this by searching the group for the tag #roytaylorHD.

It is time to stop pretending that type 2 diabetes cannot be cured. That is a lie. These images prove it.

Here are the details of the diet:

For 8 weeks, it was an extreme calorie restriction diet using meal replacement shakes. The shakes contained normal proportions of carbs, protein, fat. It was not low carb. But it was only 800 calories. People were also allowed unlimited amounts of low calorie salad vegetables.

After 8 weeks on this diet, people lost around 30lbs, and the liver and pancreas were completely cleared of fat.

Then people were transitioned to a normal calorie traditional Mediterranean diet for maintenance. They stayed on the Mediterranean diet for the following 2 years, during which time the pancreas gradually regenerated.

The key to the regeneration was keeping the liver and pancreas free of fat for the entire 2 years.

If they regained any significant weight during the 2 years, they were immediately put back on the 800 calorie diet for a few weeks, until the weight was lost again. Then back on the normal calorie Mediterranean diet again for maintenance.

Exercise was encouraged, but not mandatory.
