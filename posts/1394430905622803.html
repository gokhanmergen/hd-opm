Plaque starts in neck and leg arteries decades before the heart; calcium scores miss it

Sep 17, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post summarises a video describing an NEJM study of 16,000 asymptomatic people showing plaque accumulates first in the neck and leg arteries, during the 20s to 40s, before the heart. Heart scans such as coronary calcium may therefore miss early disease; carotid intima-media thickness ultrasound can detect peripheral plaque, and the ankle-brachial index offers a simple at-home check for leg artery blockage.

New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Aug 31, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 38 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified. Carvalho argues calcium stabilises plaque, so slowing calcification without reducing plaque could even be harmful, and no evidence shows K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, so he advises relying on proven therapies, diet and exercise.

LDL-C is cumulative exposure: lowering it earlier and longer matters most

Jul 02, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 6 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Reposts an infographic arguing that LDL-C should be seen as plaque-years: every year of elevated LDL adds arterial burden that cannot be undone, so starting treatment at 30 beats starting at 45, and early intervention may matter more than drug strength.

Vitamin C has not reduced heart attacks or strokes and plaque data are mixed

Apr 12, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Admin summarises that vitamin C alone has been tested widely without reducing hard outcomes (MACE), and plaque-reversal data are mixed: supportive in animals, better/worse/no effect in human trials. He does not advise it as a heart-disease cure, though physiological doses appear safe; he takes 250 mg/day to avoid deficiency. Links an (largely AI-generated, author-vetted) summary document.

Olezarsen cut triglycerides 64% and remnant cholesterol 72%, but plaque did not change

Mar 30, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 10 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Quotes Ethan Weiss on a surprising null result: at 6 months olezarsen lowered triglycerides 63.9%, remnant cholesterol 71.9% and apoB 16.0% versus placebo with no LDL-C difference, yet there was no difference in plaque at one year. This suggests triglyceride/remnant lowering alone may not shrink plaque.
