Shared from JAMA Neurology · original post

Plasma p-tau217 demonstrated robust accuracy across assay platforms and study settings, reinforcing its potential role in early #Alzheimer screening once effective preventive therapies emerge. Current limitations include heterogeneity and underrepresentation of diverse populations.
