MORE MISINFORMATION

The claim that cholesterol/LDL is simply there to “repair” damaged arteries LITERALLY gets the biology BACKWARD. Even native, unoxidized LDL by itself has been shown to harm the function of the inner blood vessel linings.

In one study, researchers exposed endothelial cells to native LDL and found that it increased superoxide production and interfered with normal nitric oxide signaling by promoting eNOS uncoupling. In plain English: normal LDL, before it is oxidized, can directly disrupt the machinery that helps the endothelium maintain healthy blood-vessel function.

Another study found that native LDL increased endothelial superoxide production more than fourfold through an eNOS-dependent mechanism, with evidence of increased oxidative stress without significant oxidation of the LDL itself. In other words, you don’t have to first turn LDL into oxidized LDL for native LDL to adversely affect endothelial biology.

And then there’s human evidence. In 15 people with familial hypercholesterolemia, a single LDL-apheresis treatment reduced LDL-C by about 87% and immediately improved endothelium-dependent dilation of both the coronary arteries and coronary microvasculature.

Paper · J Clin Apher · 2004Improvement of endothelium-dependent coronary vasodilation after a single LDL apheresis in patients with hypercholesterolemia.The purpose of this study was to determine whether a single LDL apheresis would improve impaired endothelium-dependent dilation of the coronary artery in patients with hypercholesterolemia. Hypercholesterolemia is associated with impaired endothelial function, and human studies using cholesterol-lowering drugs indicate that endothelial function in the coronary arteries improves with reduction of serum LDL…pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

So yes, endothelial injury and inflammation also matter. But the simplistic story that “the artery gets damaged first, then LDL shows up to heal it” isn’t supported by any evidence.

Work on everything involved and don’t buy into the 🐂 💩 narratives out there trying to argue against decades of research from every conceivable angle, all pointing at the same thing.
