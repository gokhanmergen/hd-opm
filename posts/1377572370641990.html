CoQ10 may reduce gum bleeding and inflammation in periodontitis (11-trial meta-analysis)

Shared from Genuinely Healthy · original post

Research suggests that Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10) may significantly reduce gum bleeding and inflammation in people with chronic periodontitis, especially when used alongside standard periodontal treatment such as scaling and root planing.

Clinical studies using CoQ10—either applied directly to periodontal pockets or taken orally—have reported improvements in measures such as gingival inflammation, bleeding on probing, periodontal pocket depth, and clinical attachment.

A 2022 systematic review and meta-analysis of 11 clinical trials found significant reductions in both bleeding and gingival inflammation with CoQ10 supplementation.
