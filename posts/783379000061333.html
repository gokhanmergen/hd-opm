Walter Willett: refined sugar and starch, especially sugary drinks, threaten heart health

Louis Nervegna · Apr 08, 2024 · ♥ 7 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a 10-minute ZOE video of Harvard's Walter Willett on the danger of sugar-sweetened beverages, arguing refined sugar and refined starch are major threats to health and heart health. He notes refined starch is just chains of sugar molecules.

  • Refined starch is a chain of sugar molecules
  • Sugar-sweetened beverages are a particular concern

Refined sugar is a major threat to human health, and especially heart health— particularly in the form of sugar-sweetened beverages. There is a significant amount of evidence to back this up.

The same is true for refined starch. (Refined starch is just a chain of sugar molecules.)

Walter Willett has published over 2000 peer reviewed journal articles, and was for many years the second most cited journal paper author on all of pubmed.

He explains the danger in this 10 minute video.
