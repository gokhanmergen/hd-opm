"My whole world revolved around food."

In a new JAMA Network Open study from the Stanford Center for Digital Health, researchers interviewed 30 adults across the U.S. about what it is actually like to take GLP-1 medications.

Participants described reduced "food noise," renewed agency around eating, side effects, stigma and barriers to access.

For many, the story was not only about weight loss. It was about what life felt like when food was no longer in charge.
