Statin plus sildenafil may block cancer metastasis in preclinical models

Jul 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 4 Cancer

A 2026 Cancer Research study found sildenafil (a PDE5 inhibitor) caused cholesterol to accumulate in lysosomes of mouse and human cancer models, limiting cholesterol available to cancer cells and impairing their migration and spread. Combining it with a statin, which blocks cholesterol production, gave stronger anti-metastatic effects than either alone. Louis notes the research is preclinical but promising.

Hormone therapy has no proven dementia benefit and may add risk in APOE4 carriers with MCI

Mar 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares a post by Hussein Yassine on hormone therapy, brain health and APOE4. There is no strong randomized evidence that HRT reduces dementia risk; benefits are best supported for bone protection and menopausal symptoms, and APOE4 carriers who already have MCI or dementia may face specific risks.

Shingles vaccine tied to 46% fewer major cardiac events in high-risk people

Mar 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 13 Infections & vaccines

Louis notes growing evidence that the shingles vaccine protects against dementia, and a new study suggests it also protects the heart. In high-risk people it was associated with 46% fewer major cardiac events and 66% fewer deaths within a year, but this is observational and confounding by healthier behavior is possible.
