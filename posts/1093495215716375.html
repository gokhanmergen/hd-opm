COSMOS: a standard multivitamin slowed cognitive decline in older adults, maybe via B12

Louis Nervegna · Aug 19, 2025 · ♥ 10 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaSupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Louis states that the Harvard COSMOS RCT showed an RDA-level multivitamin slows cognitive decline in older people and that a linked study hints folate and vitamin B12 may explain it.

  • RDA-level multivitamin slowed cognitive decline in COSMOS
  • Folate and B12 may be responsible

A large well-done randomized controlled trial at Harvard (COSMOS) showed that a basic RDA-level multivitamin slows cognitive decline in older people.

You can read more about this study by clicking the tags #cosmosHD and #multivitaminHD.

The study below hints that folate and vitamin B12 might explain why.
