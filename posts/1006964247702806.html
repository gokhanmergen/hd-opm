Keto-CTA vs DISCO-CT: plaque progression on keto in LMHR versus DASH diet plus statins

Oct 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

A comparison of the KETO-CTA and DISCO-CT trials. The original 2025 JACC Advances KETO-CTA paper in lean mass hyper-responders was retracted over concerns about the Cleerly plaque software. Fact-checking keto and carnivore influencer claims about LDL, ApoB and saturated fat

May 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis lists what keto/carnivore influencers get right and wrong: ApoB is the best lipid marker, LDL-C usually tracks ApoB, particle size doesn't matter once particle number is counted, and most saturated fats raise LDL-P, ApoB and LDL-C. Studies finding saturated fat harmless used a standard Western diet as comparator.

Keto-CTA: lean keto dieters with plaque progressed; Mediterranean plus statins did not

Apr 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 32 · 💬 37 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis says the published Keto-CTA (LMHR) study settles his earlier hesitation: lean, metabolically healthy people with significant plaque whose LDL is raised by keto are very likely to see progression. He contrasts this with large trials of a traditional Mediterranean diet plus statins that showed halted progression or regression.

Mandrola critique: KETO-CTA did not upend the causal evidence for LDL in atherosclerosis

May 31, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Gokhan shares a Medscape review by John Mandrola of the KETO-CTA study in lean mass hyper-responders on keto. The review says the data are nowhere near supporting the authors' outsized claims that high LDL-C does not relate to atherosclerosis.
