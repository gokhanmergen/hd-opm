Video: large cohorts show no link between seed oils and prostate or colon cancer

Louis Nervegna · Dec 23, 2024 · ♥ 5 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Cancer
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a Nutrition Made Simple video analyzing two headline-making studies tying seed oils to cancer. Large prospective cohorts show no association with prostate cancer, and with colon cancer once ultra-processed food intake is corrected for. He adds that decades of research show no obvious relationship between fat intake and cancer.

  • No association with prostate cancer in large prospective cohorts
  • No association with colon cancer in cohorts that adjust for ultra-processed food
  • Applies to saturated and omega-6 fats according to Louis

This video examines the relationship between seed oil consumption and prostate / colon cancer.

The video points out:

Large prospective cohort studies show no association between seed oil consumption and prostate cancer.

When you look only at large prospective cohort studies that properly correct for ultra-processed food consumption, there is no association between seed oil consumption and colon cancer.

The video also addresses two studies that were published last week, and explains why they did not show that seed oil consumption causes either prostate or colon cancer.

Harvard review: fat not clearly linked to cancer; red and processed meat raise colon risk

Dec 20, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 2 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes the Harvard Nutrition Source summary on dietary fat and cancer. Total fat percentage and fat types show no clear association with cancer, and the Women's Health Initiative low-fat trial did not reduce breast or colon cancer. Red and processed meat do increase colon cancer risk, and animal fat in young women was linked to higher breast cancer risk in Nurses' Health Study II. He notes a later Harvard study updates this.

Replacing 10 g/day of butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality

Oct 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares Harvard research on 221,054 people from the Nurses' Health Studies I and II and the Health Professionals Follow-Up Study, published in JAMA Internal Medicine. Higher plant-oil intake (especially soybean, canola, olive) was linked to lower total, cancer and cardiovascular mortality, while butter was linked to higher total and cancer mortality; a modeled swap of 10 g butter for plant oils lowered mortality by 17%.

Plant oils instead of butter linked to lower mortality in 200,000 people over 30 years

Sep 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A Mass General Brigham study following more than 200,000 people for 30 years found diets higher in butter and low in plant oils were associated with elevated risk of premature death, while replacing butter with plant oils was linked to lower mortality.

NY Times: higher omega-6 linked to lower CVD, diabetes, cancer and mortality

Louis shares a NY Times article on the seed oil myth, noting it quotes researchers who studied the question in humans. Higher blood linoleic acid linked to lower inflammation and cardiometabolic risk

Jun 04, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A shared summary reports observational data presented at NUTRITION 2025 (Kevin Maki) that people with higher blood linoleic acid had lower CRP, better glucose control and healthier BMI. It cites meta-analyses linking higher omega-6 to lower CVD, cancer and all-cause mortality, and stresses whole-food sources.
