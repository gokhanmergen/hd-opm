Vitamin C, NAC and glutathione can give falsely high readings on glucose meters and CGMs

Louis Nervegna · Feb 26, 2024 · ♥ 15 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesSupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Louis warns that antioxidants including vitamin C, N-acetyl-cysteine and glutathione can cause false high readings on glucose meters and continuous glucose monitors. Lab blood tests are not affected.

  • Vitamin C, NAC and glutathione can cause false high readings on meters and CGMs
  • Lab tests are not affected

For those of you regularly using blood glucose meters and CGM’s, you should be aware that antioxidants including vitamin C, N-acetyl-cysteine, and glutathione can cause false high readings.

This is not a problem with lab tests, just meters and CGM’s.
