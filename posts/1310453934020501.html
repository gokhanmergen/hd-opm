Normal liver enzymes can hide fatty liver; use FibroScan and look beyond FIB-4

Louis Nervegna · Jun 05, 2026 · ♥ 9 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatRisk assessment & lab tests
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a dietitian post and note that half of people with fatty liver have normal liver enzymes, so enzymes and enzyme-based scores like FIB-4 poorly predict liver fat. He recommends FibroScan, with fatty liver diagnosed above 5% liver fat and insulin resistance linked to levels above 1.85%.

  • Half of people with fatty liver have normal liver enzymes
  • Fatty liver diagnosed at liver fat above 5%
  • Liver fat above 1.85% linked to insulin resistance

You can have normal liver enzymes and still have fatty liver.

Half of people with fatty liver have normal liver enzymes.

Liver enzymes are not a great predictor of liver fat. The best test for measuring liver fat is a specialized ultrasound, called fibroscan.

Fatty liver is diagnosed when liver fat exceeds 5%.

Levels above 1.85% are linked with insulin resistance. Lose the extra liver fat above this threshold and the insulin resistance typically disappears. See this post: ↪ Liver fat as low as 1.85% is linked to insulin resistance (Yale).

Happy Fatty Liver Friday!

Very important message today for those living with fatty liver disease trying to monitor their progress - you cannot rely on just looking at liver enzymes.

While it is true that certain predictive equations around liver fibrosis like the FIB4 use liver enzyme levels, they are not great predictors of your liver fat.

I explain why in today’s post and what you should look at instead when you are trying to reverse fatty liver.
