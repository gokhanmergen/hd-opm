Kidney health tracking: cystatin C, urine albumin/creatinine and more beyond creatinine

Gokhan Can · Sep 28, 2024 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan argues creatinine and eGFR are imperfect kidney markers and ranks five tests worth tracking, especially for people on many supplements or drugs: cystatin C (very useful, independent of muscle mass), CBC/chemistry/lipids, urine albumin/creatinine ratio, urine protein/creatinine and urinalysis.

  • Cystatin C eGFR rated most useful; independent of muscle mass
  • Five tests: cystatin C, CBC/chem/lipids, ACR, protein/creatinine, urinalysis

What Kidney Health Markers Should I Track?

(Creatinine & eGFR are relatively imperfect markers of kidney health. If you're on multiple supplements and drugs, I highly recommend thorough monitoring of kidney health trends. Ideally you should get all five tests semi-regularly).

  1. Cystatin C
  2. CBC / Chemistry / Lipids
  3. Albumin / Creatinine Ratio, Random Urine Test
  4. Protein and Creatinine Urine Test
  5. Urinalysis Urine Test

Rank these tests from most useful to least useful to monitor kidney HEALTH

  1. Cystatin C (includes cystatin C eGFR)
  • Usefulness: Very High
  • Cystatin C is a protein used to estimate glomerular filtration rate (eGFR), which is a direct measure of kidney function. Cystatin C may provide a more accurate assessment of GFR than creatinine alone, particularly in individuals with mild or early-stage kidney disease. It is independent of muscle mass, making it especially useful for detecting subtle declines in kidney function.
  1. CBC / Chemistry / Lipids (includes creatinine and creatinine eGFR)
  • Usefulness: Very High
  • This panel includes creatinine, which is commonly used to estimate GFR and kidney function, making it a key test for Kidney monitoring.
  1. Albumin / Creatinine Ratio, Random Urine Test
  • Usefulness: High
  • The albumin-to-creatinine ratio (ACR) is a crucial test to detect and monitor albuminuria (protein in the urine), which is an early marker of kidney damage. Albuminuria can indicate worsening kidney function, and tracking this regularly can help monitor Kidney Health deterioration before it becomes serious.
  1. Protein and Creatinine Urine Test (including the ratio)
  • Usefulness: High
  • This test measures total protein excreted in the urine and gives a protein-to-creatinine ratio. It's similar to the albumin/creatinine test but provides broader information about total protein excretion, which can be valuable for monitoring kidney function.
  1. Urinalysis Urine Test - Routine urine test
  • Usefulness: Moderate
  • Routine urinalysis can detect blood, protein, and other abnormalities in the urine. While less specific than the above tests for Kidney function, it is still useful for spotting general signs of kidney damage or infection. However, it's more of a general screening tool and may be redundant if you're doing the other, more specific tests.
