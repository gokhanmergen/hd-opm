Regular coffee drinkers have about 18% lower cancer risk, video says

Louis Nervegna · Oct 06, 2025 · ♥ 15 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a video on coffee and cancer, stating that regular coffee drinkers have around 18% lower risk of any cancer and that heavy coffee drinkers have around 50% lower risk of liver or endometrial cancer.

  • About 18% lower risk of any cancer in regular coffee drinkers
  • Around 50% lower risk of liver or endometrial cancer with large amounts of coffee

Coffee likely protects against cancer— particularly liver cancer and endometrial cancer.

People who drink coffee regularly have about an 18% lower risk of developing any kind of cancer, compared to people who do not.

People who drink large amounts of coffee have around a 50% lower risk of liver cancer or endometrial cancer.

Louis highlights the widely cited 2020 NEJM review on dairy and disease risk. Effects depend on the comparison food: dairy fares better than processed red meat or sugary drinks but worse than nuts.
