Want to take care of your brain? Maintain your blood pressure!

A new study from China finds that keeping your blood pressure below 130/80 is associated with a 15% reduced risk of dementia and a 16% reduction in overall cognitive impairment. Blood Pressure Goal: 120/80 Is the Number Your Doctor Wants to See (Yale Medicine)

Avoiding high blood pressure, diabetes and smoking tied to 13 more dementia-free years

Aug 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Brain health & dementia

An observational study found adults who avoided high blood pressure, diabetes and smoking averaged 30 dementia-free years after baseline vs 17 years for those with all three. The study cannot prove causation.

Nearly 1 in 4 young adults has high blood pressure, raising later dementia risk

Dec 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis quotes a clip (Rhonda Patrick) noting that nearly 1 in 4 young adults has hypertension and that earlier, cumulative exposure to high blood pressure impairs cerebral blood flow and speeds brain atrophy. He links exercise as a protective factor for both heart and brain.

Lowering blood pressure cut dementia risk in a cluster RCT in uncontrolled hypertension

Apr 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a Nature Medicine cluster-randomized trial in people with uncontrolled hypertension, reporting that intensive blood pressure reduction substantially lowered risk of dementia and of cognitive impairment without dementia. It links brain health to blood pressure control.

Anticholinergic drugs such as antihistamines and benzodiazepines linked to dementia risk

Jun 29, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

The shared article discusses antihistamines and other anticholinergic or sedating drugs (e.g. Benadryl, Tylenol PM, benzodiazepines, Ambien) that reduce acetylcholine signalling and correlate with higher dementia risk. Evidence is observational and relevance to heart health is indirect.

Two weekly servings of processed meat linked to 14% higher dementia risk; unprocessed not

Sep 09, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 12 ↩ 1 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis shares a finding reported by the NY Times that eating about two servings of processed meat per week was associated with a 14% higher dementia risk. This included celery-juice-cured versions, and no association was seen for unprocessed meat. Observational data.

Intensive BP control in SPRINT: 2.9 more years of survival and less white matter damage

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 2 ↩ 2 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Quotes follow-up analyses of the SPRINT trial comparing a systolic target under 120 with under 140 mm Hg. Intensive treatment was projected to add 2.9 years of survival from age 50 and was linked to fewer white matter lesions and better blood flow, markers of better brain health.
