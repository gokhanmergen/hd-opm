Louis shares a video by a statistician who reanalyzed the publicly released Keto-CTA data and alleges misuse of statistics. The paper was voluntarily retracted over alleged Cleerly data integrity issues, and the authors had not yet responded.

  • Reanalysis possible because authors released their data
  • Paper voluntarily retracted by authors

Accusations of misuse of statistics in the Keto-CTA study, after independent re-analysis

The presenter is a statistician and data scientist who has independently re-analyzed the raw data in the study.

To the credit of the authors of the Keto-CTA study, this reanalysis was only possible because they released their dataset publicly. They were very open with their raw data.

As far as I know, the authors have not yet responded to this.

Given that the paper was recently voluntarily retracted by the authors (due to alleged issues with the integrity of the Cleerly data), they will have an opportunity to correct these statistical issues when and if they choose to republish.
