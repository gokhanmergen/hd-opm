Louis notes heart disease deaths have started dropping after flatlining, citing an AHA report. It remains the leading US cause of death.

  • First drop in five years
  • Still leading cause of death in the US

Death rates from heart disease have finally started dropping again.

(They had flatlined over the last few years, due in part to Covid.)

#cvddeathHD

Shared from ABC News · original post

Fewer people are dying from heart disease, but the condition is still the leading cause of death in the U.S., a new report from the American Heart Association finds.

Although death rates from heart disease have dropped for the first time in the past five years, it still kills more Americans than any other condition, according to the report, published early Wednesday in the journal Circulation.

Baby aspirin during and after Covid may be worth discussing if you have heart disease

Jan 06, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 12 Infections & vaccines

Louis suggests people with heart disease who get Covid ask their doctor about baby aspirin during infection and for a few weeks after. Infections temporarily raise heart attack and stroke risk through inflammation, and Covid raises clot risk more than other infections. A linked 2022 meta-analysis found aspirin use associated with lower Covid mortality without more bleeding.

Vitamin C has not reduced heart attacks or strokes and plaque data are mixed

Apr 12, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Admin summarises that vitamin C alone has been tested widely without reducing hard outcomes (MACE), and plaque-reversal data are mixed: supportive in animals, better/worse/no effect in human trials. He does not advise it as a heart-disease cure, though physiological doses appear safe; he takes 250 mg/day to avoid deficiency. Links an (largely AI-generated, author-vetted) summary document.

North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. Biggest US heart disease death declines were in ages 55-64, men and African Americans

Sep 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 Cardiovascular disease: the big picture

Louis notes that over the last 60 years, the sharpest reductions in US heart disease death rates were among people aged 55-64, men and African Americans. The cited paper adds that premature heart disease mortality in adults 25-64 fell 70% since 1968 but has stagnated since 2011.
