AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes the ESC/EAS LDL-C targets by risk tier, including who counts as very high risk (clinical or imaging-proven ASCVD, severe diabetes, FH with ASCVD, eGFR below 30). Imaging evidence of ASCVD, including CT, counts as very high risk.

  • Very high risk: below 55 mg/dL (below 40 in recurrent ASCVD)
  • High risk: below 70 mg/dL
  • Moderate risk: below 100 mg/dL (consider)
  • Low risk: below 116 mg/dL (may consider)
  • Very high risk includes 10-year SCORE above 10%

The full report from the ESC can be found here:

EscardioLipidology update: targets and timing of well-established therapiesSummary Low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) is the primary target in lipid-lowering strategies. The goal of treatment depends on the level of individual risk in each patient, the goal of which is going to be lower in higher risk patients. The latest European Society of Cardiology (ESC) Guidelines and other recent literature underline that the…escardio.org

DETAILS

————— 1. VERY HIGH RISK —————

Clinical Data

ASCVD, either clinical or unequivocal on imaging (CT scan included in these guidelines)

Diabetes mellitus with target organ damage, long standing duration (>20 years), or associated with other risk factors

eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m2

Familial hypercholesterolemia (FH) with ASCVD or with another major risk factor

10 year risk SCORE >10%

LDL-C Target

<55 mg/dL both in primary and secondary prevention

<40 mg/dL in selected patients with recurrent ASCVD

————— 2. HIGH RISK —————

Clinical data

Markedly elevated single risk factors, including total cholesterol >310 mg/dL, LDL-C >190 mg/dL or blood pressure >180/110 mmHg

FH without other risk factors

Diabetes with a duration >10 years or with another risk factor

eGFR 30-59 mL/min/1.73 m2

Calculated 10 year risk SCORE >5% and <10%

LDL-C Target

<70 mg/dL

————— 3. MODERATE RISK —————

Clinical Data

Younger patients (Type 1 diabetes mellitus <35 years; Type 2 diabetes mellitus <50 years) with diabetes mellitus duration <10 years, without other risk factors

Calculated 10 year risk SCORE >1 % and <5%;

LDL-C Target

<100 mg/dL should be considered

————— 4. LOW RISK —————

Clinical data

Low-risk calculated 10 year risk SCORE <1% for fatal cardiovascular disease

LDL-C Target

<116 mg/dL may be considered
