Diabetes & CVD: The Core Mechanisms

Hyperglycemia drives CVD via:

  1. Atherosclerosis: Systemic inflammation & endothelial dysfunction accelerate plaque progression & impair regression.
  2. Cardiomyopathy: Glucolipotoxicity causes mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, & pathological fibrosis.
  3. Microvascular Disease: Coronary microvascular ischemia & impaired angiogenesis (VEGF resistance) heighten risk for HF and CLI.

Ref: Kalra, et al. Cell 2024. DOI:10.1016/j.cell.2024.05.044
