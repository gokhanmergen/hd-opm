Keto may beat Mediterranean on blood sugar, weight and waist, but only short term

Gokhan Can · Jan 13, 2025 · ♥ 3 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Low-carb, keto & carnivoreMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan comments that keto-like diets work amazingly well short term but often stop working as cheating and adherence problems accumulate, linking a report comparing keto and Mediterranean diets. It found keto improved blood sugar, gut microbiota, BMI and waist circumference more, but only in the short term.

  • Keto improved blood sugar, BMI, waist and microbiota composition more than Mediterranean
  • Advantage seen only in the short term

I know few diets like keto that work so amazingly well short term but eventually (unfortunately) stop working due to cheating and other adherence challenges.
