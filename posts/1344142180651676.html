BRAIN FOOD: Older adults who followed a Mediterranean diet reported significantly higher psychological well-being than their peers — even during COVID-19 lockdowns, a new study finds.

Researchers tracked more than 3,000 adults ages 50 to 90 in England and found the mental health boost held after accounting for income, education, physical activity and smoking habits.

The study, published in BMJ Open, suggests the specific types of food — not just caloric intake — played a key role in protecting emotional resilience during the pandemic's worst months.

MIND diet: 20% less grey matter loss per 3-point score rise in Framingham MRI study

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

In 1,647 Framingham Offspring participants followed about 12 years with repeated MRI scans, closer adherence to the MIND diet (Mediterranean-DASH hybrid) was linked to slower grey matter loss. Each 3-point rise in MIND score matched 20% less age-related grey matter decline, equal to about 2.5 years of slower brain aging. This is an observational association.
