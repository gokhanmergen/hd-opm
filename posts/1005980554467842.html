GLP-1 agonists and SGLT2 inhibitors linked to lower dementia risk in type 2 diabetes

Louis Nervegna · Apr 13, 2025 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)Brain health & dementiaInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

JAMA Neurology report shared by Louis: in people with type 2 diabetes, both GLP-1 receptor agonists and SGLT2 inhibitors were associated with decreased risk of Alzheimer disease and related dementias compared with other glucose-lowering drugs. Louis sees this as evidence for benefits beyond diabetes treatment.

  • Population: type 2 diabetes
  • GLP-1RAs and SGLT2is both associated with lower dementia risk vs other glucose-lowering drugs

More evidence for benefits of GLP-1 agonists and SGLT2 inhibitors that go well beyond just treating diabetes.
