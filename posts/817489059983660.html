Eggs and phosphatidylcholine did not raise TMAO in an RCT, unlike choline bitartrate

Louis Nervegna · Jun 10, 2024 · ♥ 13 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
Meat, eggs & dairyGut microbiomeSupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

A Cleveland Clinic randomized trial (not egg-industry funded, with TMAO discoverer Stanley Hazen) found choline bitartrate supplements raised TMAO and platelet reactivity, but four large eggs a day or phosphatidylcholine supplements did not.

  • Choline bitartrate arms: TMAO and platelet reactivity up (all P < 0.0001 / < 0.01)
  • Four eggs daily: TMAO p = 0.20; platelet reactivity p = 0.10
  • Phosphatidylcholine: TMAO p = 0.27; platelet reactivity p = 0.79

Phosphatidylcholine supplements (or natural sources of phosphatidylcholine like eggs) do not seem to raise TMAO levels.

This is the form of choline typically found in whole food.

However, other kinds of choline supplements like choline bitartrate do raise TMAO.

This paper is from researchers at the Cleveland Clinic. It was NOT funded by the egg industry.

One of the authors on this paper is Stanley Hazen. It was his research group at the Cleveland Clinic that first discovered in 2011 that blood levels of TMAO are associated with increased risk for CVD.

—-

Dietary Choline Supplements, but Not Eggs, Raise Fasting TMAO Levels in Participants with Normal Renal Function: A Randomized Clinical Trial

Quoted:

“Participant’s plasma TMAO levels increased significantly in all three intervention arms containing choline bitartrate (all P<0.0001), but daily ingestion of four large eggs p=0.20 or phosphatidylcholine supplements (P=0.27) failed to increase plasma TMAO levels. Platelet reactivity also significantly increased in the three intervention arms containing choline bitartrate (all P<0.01), but not with eggs (P=0.10) or phosphatidylcholine supplements (P=0.79).”

“Despite high choline content in egg yolks, healthy participants consuming four eggs daily showed no significant increase in TMAO or platelet reactivity. However, choline bitartrate supplements providing comparable total choline raised both TMAO and platelet reactivity, demonstrating that the form and source of dietary choline differentially contributes to systemic TMAO levels and platelet responsiveness.”
