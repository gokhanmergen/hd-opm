Shared from Healthy Harbor · original post

A double-blind randomized controlled trial evaluated the effectiveness of honey plus coffee compared with systemic prednisolone and a control treatment in 97 adults with persistent post-infectious cough (PPC). Participants were randomly assigned to receive a honey–coffee mixture, prednisolone, or guaifenesin for one week. Cough severity was measured using a validated visual analogue questionnaire before treatment and one week after completion. The primary outcome was the change in cough frequency, which served as a measure of symptom severity.

The results showed that the honey plus coffee group experienced a significantly greater reduction in cough scores (from 2.9 to 0.2) compared with the steroid group (from 3.0 to 2.4) and the control group (from 2.8 to 2.7). Statistical analysis demonstrated that the improvement in the honey plus coffee group was significantly superior to the other treatments (p < 0.001). The authors concluded that honey combined with coffee may serve as an effective alternative treatment for persistent post-infectious cough, although they recommended further studies with larger sample sizes to confirm these findings.
