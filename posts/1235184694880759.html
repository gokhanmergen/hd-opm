Aged garlic extract slowed plaque growth in a small CTA trial and cut colorectal adenomas

Louis notes aged garlic extract (Kyolic) slowed plaque growth and regressed high-risk soft plaque in a small CT angiography RCT, and a 2006 RCT showed about half as many colorectal adenomas on the high dose.

Research cited

  • Aged garlic extract and colorectal adenomas · RCT · Journal of Nutrition · 2006
Shared from Genuinely Healthy · original post

A double-blind, randomized clinical trial published in The Journal of Nutrition (2006) found that patients taking high-dose aged garlic extract (2.4 mL/day) for 12 months had about 50% fewer colorectal adenomas than the control group. By the end of the study, the control group averaged 1.44 adenomas, while the high-dose group averaged 0.68 adenomas — a statistically significant reduction (P < 0.05).

Even more impressive, among patients who had adenomas at the start of the study, the total polyp size was about 66% smaller in the high-dose group compared to control (0.86 mm vs. 2.56 mm, P = 0.03). While polyps continued to grow in the control group over the year, high-dose aged garlic extract significantly suppressed both polyp number and growth — suggesting a meaningful protective effect for colon health.
