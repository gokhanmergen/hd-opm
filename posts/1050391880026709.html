Sabatini on diet and mTOR: limit amino acids and insulin; ~18 h fasting may inhibit mTORC1

Louis shares notes on a David Sabatini interview about how diet affects mTOR and autophagy. Sabatini suggests low amino acid or low carbohydrate diets lower mTOR, favors a higher-carbohydrate diet of low-glycemic carbs, and thinks mTORC1 senses total daily insulin. Stanford finds BHB-amino acids, a new keto metabolite family that suppresses appetite

Stanford researchers led by Jonathan Long discovered a third ketosis pathway in which enzymes attach beta-hydroxybutyrate to amino acids, forming BHB-amino acids that suppress appetite and promote weight loss. It may explain reduced hunger during fasting or keto diets; heart relevance is indirect.
