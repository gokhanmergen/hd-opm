Two tricks to lower glucose and insulin spikes from whole grains

Louis Nervegna · Feb 26, 2025 · ♥ 28 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grainsInsulin resistance & diabetesGut microbiome
AI summary & key facts

Louis recommends eating whole grains as intact as possible (steel-cut oats, wheat berries rather than finely ground flour) and refrigerating cooked grains for 24 hours to convert starch into resistant starch. Resistant starch is digested slowly and feeds gut bacteria.

  • Choose intact grains over instant or finely ground
  • Refrigerate cooked grains 24 hours to raise resistant starch

Two tricks for lowering both the glycemic index and insulin index of whole grains:

  1. Eat the whole grain as intact as possible.

For example, choose slow-cook steel cut oats instead of instant oatmeal. As another example, choose a cereal made with wheat berries, instead of crackers made with finely ground whole wheat baking flour. (Wheat berries are the intact grain that whole wheat flour is made from.)

  1. Refrigerate the whole grains for 24 hours, after cooking them.

This turns a lot of the carbohydrate into resistant starch, which takes much longer to digest. The resistant starch passes through the small intestine unabsorbed, and feeds the healthy bacteria in your gut.

————-

Doing both of these things will lower both the glucose spike and the insulin spike that you get after eating whole grains.
