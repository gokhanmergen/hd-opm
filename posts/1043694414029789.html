Only lipophilic statins like atorvastatin enter the brain; PCSK9 drugs and ezetimibe don't

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 10 Brain health & dementia

Louis explains the blood-brain barrier and cholesterol: the brain makes its own cholesterol separately from blood, so non-statin LDL drugs and hydrophilic statins like rosuvastatin have little brain effect. Lipophilic statins do enter the brain, with promising observational data against Alzheimer's, and a 20,000-person atorvastatin trial is under way. Two prior trials showed statins do not worsen existing Alzheimer's, and midlife high LDL is a leading modifiable dementia risk (2024 Lancet Commission, about 7% of US cases per Louis).

Two RCTs of statins in Alzheimer's disease were neutral: no benefit, no harm

Aug 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Statins

Louis summarises two randomized trials of statins as treatment for existing Alzheimer's disease, one with simvastatin and one with atorvastatin. Neither slowed progression, and neither worsened it. This concerns treating established AD, not preventing it.

Two RCTs found statins neither helped nor harmed Alzheimer's disease progression

Jun 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 5 ↩ 3 Statins

Louis summarises two randomized trials of statins as a treatment for established Alzheimer's: simvastatin in 406 people with normal lipids over 18 months, and atorvastatin 80 mg (LEADe) in people already on donepezil over 72 weeks. Both were neutral on cognition and safe.

PREVENTABLE: 20,000-person trial of atorvastatin 40 mg to prevent dementia over age 75

Jun 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 5 Statins

Louis describes the ongoing PREVENTABLE trial, which randomizes about 20,000 community-dwelling adults aged 75+ to atorvastatin 40 mg or placebo to test whether statins prevent dementia. Atorvastatin was chosen because it is lipophilic and crosses the blood-brain barrier; the trial began in 2020 and is still recruiting.
