Canadian studies: Portfolio Diet linked to lower CVD risk and mortality in diverse groups

Louis Nervegna · Jun 17, 2025 · ♥ 1 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares news of two Canadian studies suggesting the plant-based Portfolio Diet, built from cholesterol-lowering foods such as nuts, seeds and plant oils, reduces CVD and mortality in more diverse populations than earlier work with mostly older, White participants. No numbers are given.

  • Two new Canadian studies
  • Previously evaluated mainly in older White participants

Two new Canadian studies suggest that the plant-based Portfolio Diet, which features cholesterol-lowering food choices, can reduce the risk for cardiovascular disease (CVD) and mortality across diverse groups. The diet previously had been evaluated mostly among older, White participants. 2 New Studies Show CVD Benefit of Portfolio Diet (Medscape)

Replacing 10 g/day of butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality

Louis shares Harvard research on 221,054 people from the Nurses' Health Studies I and II and the Health Professionals Follow-Up Study, published in JAMA Internal Medicine. Higher plant-oil intake (especially soybean, canola, olive) was linked to lower total, cancer and cardiovascular mortality, while butter was linked to higher total and cancer mortality; a modeled swap of 10 g butter for plant oils lowered mortality by 17%.
