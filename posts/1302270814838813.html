“Saturated fat might make you stupid” sounds like rage bait… but there’s actually some interesting data here.

A new prospective cohort study looked at dietary fat intake and long-term risk of dementia in adults with an average age of 68 over ~6 years of follow-up (PMID: 40998020).
What they found:

  • Total fat intake wasn’t associated with dementia risk
  • But the TYPE of fat mattered
  • Higher saturated fat intake was associated with increased dementia risk

Now before the carnivore crowd starts screaming “EPIDEMIOLOGY!!!” Yes, this is observational data. It does NOT prove causation. BUT… it also lines up with what we know mechanistically about saturated fat, LDL cholesterol/ApoB, vascular health, inflammation, and cerebrovascular disease risk. Your brain is incredibly energy-demanding and heavily dependent on healthy blood flow. Anything that worsens vascular health over decades probably isn’t doing your cognition any favors either.

Meanwhile, replacing saturated fat with unsaturated fats (especially polyunsaturated fats) has consistently been associated with improved cardiovascular outcomes and lower LDL cholesterol. So no, butter in your eggs isn’t going to instantly lower your IQ by 20 points. But the internet’s obsession with treating saturated fat like a “health food” despite decades of data is getting pretty detached from reality.

Nutrition isn’t about demonizing foods. It’s about understanding tradeoffs, patterns, and long-term risk.And before somebody comments, “my grandpa ate bacon every day and lived to 90” cool. Anecdotes are not data. Someone else’s grandpa smoked a pack a day and lived to 90, does that mean smoking isn’t bad for you? Stop it…

Meta-analysis of 102 feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat gives the best glycemic benefit

Nov 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarises a systematic review of 102 randomized feeding trials (4,220 participants) on 5% energy swaps. Replacing carbs with MUFA lowered HbA1c 0.09% and HOMA-IR 2.4%; with PUFA lowered HbA1c 0.11% and fasting insulin 1.6 pmol/L. PUFA alone improved insulin secretion capacity; saturated fat swaps did not change fasting glucose. Authors favor replacing refined carbs and saturated fat with unsaturated fats.

Dietary fat and mortality meta-analysis: polyunsaturated favorable, trans fat harmful

Dec 01, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarizes a meta-analysis of 19 prospective cohorts (over 1 million participants) on dietary fat and mortality. Higher total, monounsaturated and especially polyunsaturated fat were associated with lower mortality, trans fat with higher mortality, and saturated fat showed a non-linear pattern with excess risk up to about 11% of energy.
