Hong Kong's 86-year life expectancy goes with a grain, vegetable and fruit heavy diet

Sep 03, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 15 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis notes Hong Kong life expectancy of 86 years versus 76 in the US and cites a 2011 government Total Diet Study of 5,008 adults aged 20-84. Daily intake by weight was led by cereal grains (491 g), then vegetables (177 g), fruit (147 g), meat and poultry (113 g) and fish (71 g). Dairy, eggs, legumes, nuts, sugars and added fats were low, and he uses it to counter carnivore claims. It is an ecological observation.
