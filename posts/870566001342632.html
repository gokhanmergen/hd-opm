North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. The shared cardiologist's post describes North Karelia's 700 per 100,000 CVD death rate in 1972, with 24% of calories from saturated fat, and a 20% cholesterol drop with coronary deaths in working-age men falling 82%.

Swapping 1 tbsp butter for plant oil linked to 17% lower cancer and all-cause death

Sep 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 12 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares an AHA press release on Harvard cohort data (Nurses' Health Study I and II, Health Professionals) for 221,054 adults. Replacing a daily tablespoon of butter with unsaturated plant oil was associated with lower cancer and total mortality, and each extra tablespoon of butter with higher cancer risk. Olive, canola and soybean oil showed the biggest benefits.

Swapping ghee for rapeseed oil cleared liver fat in NAFLD without cutting calories

Apr 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A March 2024 RCT replaced ghee/butter with an equal number of calories of canola (rapeseed) oil in NAFLD patients; liver fat fell and nearly all glycemic and insulin resistance measures improved. Louis notes over 100 RCTs show replacing saturated with unsaturated fat improves glucose, A1C and HOMA-IR.
