Human ancestry traces to a bottleneck of about 1,300 individuals, Louis speculates

Louis Nervegna · Aug 27, 2025 · ♥ 10 reactions · 20 comments · original Facebook post
How to read research
AI summary & key facts

Louis speculates that a near-extinction event in human evolution, leaving about 1,300 genetically similar individuals, explains why humans differ so much from primates, with implications for monkey research. The linked content was unavailable.

  • About 1,300 breeding individuals in the bottleneck (per post)

There is a strong case to be made that this unique event in our evolutionary history was responsible for making humans very different from other primates.

We are literally the product of inbreeding from about 1300 different genetically-similar individuals.

This has major implications on medical research where monkeys are routinely used in experiments to predict how well a particular intervention will work in humans. While monkeys are certainly better than mice, there are huge evolutionary differences with humans. This near extinction event in our history as a species may explain why we are so different.
