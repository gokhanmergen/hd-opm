Statins are unlikely to cause meaningful weight gain via GLP-1 effects

Louis Nervegna · Feb 01, 2026 · ♥ 16 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsGLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)
AI summary & key facts

Louis dismisses the claim that statins reduce GLP-1 in a way that matters, arguing that if it did statins would cause obvious weight gain. Studies mostly show none, or very small increases.

  • Most studies show no causal weight gain with statins
  • Studies that found a link show very small increases

This whole “statins reduce GLP1” thing is kind of stupid.

If statins really had a meaningful effect on GLP1 in humans then they would cause obvious weight gain.

Many studies have tested whether statins cause weight gain. Most failed to show any causal effect. The ones that did associate statin use with weight gain only showed a very small increase.

Even if it is true that statins reduce GLP1 in humans, the effect on weight for most people is either zero or so minor that it’s not even worth thinking about.
