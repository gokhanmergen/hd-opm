Each daily yogurt serving linked to 14% lower CVD mortality in a 17-cohort meta-analysis

Feb 16, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 · 💬 12 Meat, eggs & dairy

A systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of 17 cohort studies (896,871 participants) found higher yogurt intake was associated with lower all-cause and cardiovascular mortality, with the largest benefit around 0.5 servings per day and no clear further gain above that. No significant association was seen with cancer mortality. As an observational analysis, it shows association, not causation.

Bisphosphonates for bone loss: fracture reductions summarized

Oct 13, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Review of bisphosphonate evidence for osteoporosis. Alendronate cuts vertebral and non-vertebral fractures by about 50% in women with prior vertebral fractures; yearly IV zoledronic acid cuts vertebral fractures 70% and hip fractures 40%; a 2017 meta-analysis found overall fracture OR 0.62. Not a heart topic but relevant to aging.

Protein and mortality: BMJ 2020 meta-analysis finds weak links, plant protein favorable

Jan 06, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 5 ↩ 1 Protein

Summarizes a BMJ 2020 systematic review and meta-analysis of 32 prospective cohorts. Higher total protein was associated with slightly lower all-cause mortality, and plant protein with lower all-cause and CVD mortality, while animal protein had a minuscule effect up to about 20% of calories.

Meta-analysis: lowering LDL with statins had no effect on cancer incidence or death

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Statins

Summarizes an individual-participant meta-analysis from the Cholesterol Treatment Trialists showing that about five years of statin therapy did not change new cancer diagnoses or cancer deaths. This is reassuring evidence against fears that lowering LDL promotes cancer.
