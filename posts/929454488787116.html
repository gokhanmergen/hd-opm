High LDL does not protect the brain: 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL as top dementia risk

Louis Nervegna · Dec 15, 2024 · ♥ 18 reactions · 38 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis counters the claim that high blood LDL protects against dementia. The 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL cholesterol as the biggest modifiable dementia risk factor, accounting for 7% of cases among 14 factors that together account for 45%. He adds circulating cholesterol does not cross the blood-brain barrier; the brain makes its own.

  • High LDL responsible for 7% of dementia cases (2024 Lancet Commission)
  • 14 modifiable risk factors together account for 45% of dementia cases
  • Brain makes almost all its own cholesterol; blood cholesterol is filtered by the BBB

I’m posting this again because of how important it is.

The claim that high blood levels of LDL magically protect your brain against dementia is not only wrong— but it is the opposite of the truth.

LDL is now the #1 modifiable risk factor for dementia.

Basic physiology: Circulating cholesterol can NOT even cross the blood brain barrier to get into the brain in the first place. The brain makes almost all its own cholesterol. The BBB filters out circulating cholesterol. (See comments for references.)

So the next time someone tells you that your circulating cholesterol is protecting your brain, you know that they don’t know what they’re talking about.
