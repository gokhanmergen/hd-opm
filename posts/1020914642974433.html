Reaction to Eric Topol's Axios piece on longevity breakthroughs

Gokhan Can · May 05, 2025 · ♥ 6 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevityGuidelines, policy & public health
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan criticizes Topol's framing of lifestyle advice as a longevity breakthrough, arguing public health outcomes have stagnated since 1980 guidelines. Opinion without cited data.

This kind of rhetoric is so wrong -- it's borderline nonsense. People with zero vision making policies for the broad public.

Framing decades-old lifestyle advice as some revolutionary breakthrough ignores the sobering reality: public health outcomes have stagnated or worsened since the first major dietary guidelines were introduced around 1980. Clearly, that advice hasn't been enough at the population level. While Dr. Topol is right to emphasize the importance of lifestyle and to acknowledge there's no miracle drug yet, portraying our current understanding as the pinnacle of longevity science downplays the real gaps and the urgent need for true innovation.
