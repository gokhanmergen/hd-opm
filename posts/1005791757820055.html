Opinion: keto is not heart healthy if LDL rises; never ignore LDL

Gokhan Can · Apr 13, 2025 · ♥ 29 reactions · 74 comments · original Facebook post
Low-carb, keto & carnivoreLDL, ApoB & lipid panelFats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan argues keto often raises LDL through saturated fat and that people ignore it, citing the recent keto plaque-progression study. His suggestions are to track LDL and use a nut, fish and olive-oil heavy version of keto.

I'm increasingly convinced that keto diet isn't heart healthy. Why? People often see their LDL go up on keto due to high saturated fat intake. Then thanks to all the internet lies, they ignore it! Some even experience sky high LDL, and they still ignore it. The end result is that some people on keto look great on the outside but their arteries are getting clogged faster everyday.

You can do a few things: 1) Never ignore your LDL 2) maybe cut back on saturated fat, and do a more nut heavy, fish heavy, olive oil rich etc version of keto.

The recent study that looked at the plaque progression of people on keto has been the latest nail in the coffin. It clearly showed these people are experiencing accelerated atherosclerosis on the inside, while looking great on the outside.
