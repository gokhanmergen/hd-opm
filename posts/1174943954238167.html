French cohort of 22.7 million: mRNA COVID shots cut COVID death 74%, all-cause death 25%

Gokhan Can · Dec 05, 2025 · ♥ 27 reactions · 47 comments · original Facebook post
Infections & vaccines
AI summary & key facts

Reports a French cohort of 22.7 million vaccinated and 5.9 million unvaccinated adults followed nearly four years: 74% lower COVID death risk and 25% lower risk of death overall, with no turbocancer signal. The admin adds that vaccination protects against heart disease deaths in high-risk groups and that he prefers flu vaccine to COVID mRNA.

  • 22.7 million vaccinated vs 5.9 million unvaccinated, ~4 years
  • 74% lower COVID-19 death risk
  • 25% lower all-cause death risk
  • No evidence of turbo cancers

The researchers followed 22.7 million vaccinated individuals and 5.9 million unvaccinated individuals for nearly four years. Vaccinated adults had a 74 percent lower risk of dying from COVID-19, and a 25 percent lower risk of dying. No evidence of turbocancers.

PS: Vaccination against viruses is highly protective against heart disease deaths if you're in the high risk group. I personally prefer flu vaccine (little sides) over covid mRNA vaccine (too much sides).

Personalized mRNA vaccine succeeds in phase 3 melanoma trial after ~50% phase 2 benefit

Aug 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 12 Cancer

A personalized mRNA vaccine plus Keytruda improved recurrence-free and distant metastasis-free survival in a phase 3 trial of more than 1,100 patients with melanoma. The earlier phase 2 trial showed roughly a 50% reduction in recurrence or death at 5 years. Approval might come as early as 2027, and trials are under way in other cancers.
