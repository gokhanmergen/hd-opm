High LDL does not protect the brain: 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL as top dementia risk

Dec 15, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 38 Brain health & dementia

Louis counters the claim that high blood LDL protects against dementia. The 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL cholesterol as the biggest modifiable dementia risk factor, accounting for 7% of cases among 14 factors that together account for 45%. He adds circulating cholesterol does not cross the blood-brain barrier; the brain makes its own.

CHOICE trial: hearing aids gave nearly six-fold cognitive improvement in MCI

Sep 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

A secondary outcome of the CHOICE randomized trial found cognitive improvement in 15.34% of older adults with mild cognitive impairment given hearing aids versus 2.36% of controls. The primary outcome was null and the authors say the surprisingly large effect needs replication.

A shared post says people who used hearing aids before 70 had a 61% lower dementia risk and that the 60s may be the key window to test hearing. The poster adds that correcting hearing loss lowers dementia risk. The studies are cited only by PubMed ID.
