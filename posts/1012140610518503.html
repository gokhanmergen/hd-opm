Long-term ADHD medication linked to 4% higher CVD risk per year of use

Dec 07, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a report on a JAMA Psychiatry nested case-control study from Karolinska Institutet. Long-term ADHD drug use was associated with 4% greater CVD risk per year of use, with the largest rise in the first three years, in children and adults, females and males. Hypertension and arterial disease drove most of the association, and stimulants were also linked. Observational, so association not proof of causation.

Rosuvastatin lowers LDL slightly more than atorvastatin; LODESTAR outcomes were similar

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 10 Statins

Comparison of the two high-intensity statins: both lower LDL-C by 50% or more, rosuvastatin is more potent per milligram, but in the randomized LODESTAR trial 3-year clinical outcomes did not differ significantly. The goal is reaching the appropriate LDL target on maximally tolerated therapy.

Plaque starts in neck and leg arteries decades before the heart; calcium scores miss it

Sep 17, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post summarises a video describing an NEJM study of 16,000 asymptomatic people showing plaque accumulates first in the neck and leg arteries, during the 20s to 40s, before the heart. Heart scans such as coronary calcium may therefore miss early disease; carotid intima-media thickness ultrasound can detect peripheral plaque, and the ankle-brachial index offers a simple at-home check for leg artery blockage.

Vitamin K2 MK-7 slowed coronary calcification in a 2-year RCT; soft plaque not measured

Sep 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

A shared post on a new 2-year randomized trial notes that MK-7 reduced progression of coronary calcification versus placebo. It cautions that calcified plaque is generally more stable, soft plaque was not measured, and the trial was not designed to show fewer cardiovascular events.
