Over 99% of Heart Attacks Linked to At Least One Known Risk Factor

New research confirms that heart disease almost never happens without clear risk signals.

Over 99% of people studied across two countries showed at least one elevated risk factor before their first cardiac event, and more than 93% had two or more.

Hypertension was the dominant culprit, affecting nearly all patients.

The study stresses that prevention and management of blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, and smoking habits are critical to lowering cardiovascular disease rates.
