Cocoa failed to slow cognitive decline in the COSMOS trial at Harvard, but other studies have shown a cognitive benefit.

Cocoa flavanol extract cut CVD death 27% in the 21,000-person COSMOS trial

May 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 53 ↩ 4 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis summarizes the COSMOS randomized trial of 500 mg/day cocoa flavanols (and multivitamin) in over 21,000 adults for 3.6 years. Total cardiovascular events fell 10%, which was not statistically significant, but CVD death fell a significant 27% and major CV events were also significantly reduced. Adherence-adjusted analyses showed 15% fewer total events and 39% fewer CVD deaths; authors urge cautious interpretation.

Shin bone loading may speed brain injury recovery via PIEZO1 in mice and pigs

Oct 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

A Nature Neuroscience paper found that compressing the tibia for a few minutes a day after traumatic brain injury improved movement and memory in mice and pigs. Bone cells sensed the force through the PIEZO1 sensor, and turning it off removed the benefit. The poster calls it the most interesting bone study he has read in a long time.

8-hour eating window improved cognition in postmenopausal women (conference data)

A 6-month trial of 47 women (50-79) on a 500 kcal deficit compared an 8.2-hour window with 12.3 hours. Both lost ~15 lb but only the shorter window improved memory and problem solving. No paper yet.
