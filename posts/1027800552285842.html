The solution to diabetes, fatty liver, and metabolic disease is simple:

Lose your visceral fat

(This is the fat in your abdomen that is intermixed with your organs, underneath the ab muscles.)

This will REVERSE all 3 of these diseases.

How much is enough to reverse these diseases? It varies from person to person. But on average, it requires about 30lbs of weight loss.

Roy Taylor has proven that this works in large randomized controlled trials:

↪ Roy Taylor's trial: diet-induced weight loss restores pancreas function in type 2 diabetes

↪ Liver fat is the biggest driver of insulin resistance in most people, per a Harvard expert

↪ Roy Taylor trial: clearing liver and pancreas fat via 8-week low-calorie diet reversed T2D

There is no reason to complicate this.

Find a diet that works for you.
Lose 30lbs.

If necessary, use supplements and drugs to help, like the newer GLP1 agonists.

————-

Taylor used a 700 calorie liquid diet to do this. It contained normal proportions of carbohydrate, fat, and protein. Unlimited salad greens and non-starchy vegetables were allowed in addition to the low-calorie shakes. No weight loss drugs were used.

This very low number of calories induced rapid weight loss. The liver was cleared of fat in just a couple weeks, and the pancreas in about 8 weeks. This regenerated the pancreas and cured the patients of their diabetes, fatty liver, and metabolic syndrome.

After that, the patients were transitioned to a normal calorie traditional Mediterranean diet for maintenance.

The patients remained cured long term, as long as they continued to keep their visceral fat low.
