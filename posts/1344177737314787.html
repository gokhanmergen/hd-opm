UCLA mouse study: creatine may boost cancer-fighting immune cells

A UCLA study found creatine may supercharge key immune cells, significantly slowing tumor growth in mice. Louis notes earlier preclinical work suggested creatine might promote existing cancers, so evidence is mixed.

  • Tumor growth slowed in mice
  • Earlier preclinical research mixed

There has been mixed preclinical research on creatine and cancer. While some earlier research has suggested that creatine might promote existing cancers, this newer research from UCLA suggests a protective effect.

Creatine and cancer metastasis: preclinical signal, but human evidence is reassuring

Sep 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 3 Supplements & vitamins

A summary of the claim that creatine promotes cancer spread. Animal and cell models showed more metastasis and worse survival, but human data are much less alarming: higher dietary creatine was linked to lower cancer risk, and a trial giving creatine to cancer patients found no survival difference. The poster says people with cancer should discuss supplementation with their oncology team.

Creatine 20 g/day improved cognition in Alzheimer's patients by nearly 5% in 8 weeks

Sep 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 2 Brain health & dementia

A FoundMyFitness summary reports that creatine at 20 grams per day improved cognitive function in Alzheimer's patients by nearly 5% after eight weeks, with gains in memory, fluid cognition, inhibitory control and attention. Louis frames it as something to consider for a loved one with Alzheimer's.

Pilot: 20 g/day creatine raised brain creatine 11% in Alzheimer's patients

Jun 20, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 3 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a summary of a pilot study in which older adults with probable Alzheimer's took high-dose creatine monohydrate for 8 weeks. Brain creatine measured by MR spectroscopy rose, serum creatine rose, and compliance was high with no serious adverse events. It is open-label and tests feasibility, not efficacy.
