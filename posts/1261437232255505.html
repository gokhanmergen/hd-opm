Taurine 1.5 g/day for 2 weeks reversed endothelial dysfunction in type 1 diabetes

Louis Nervegna · Apr 04, 2026 · ♥ 26 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
Blood vessels, circulation & endotheliumSupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

A 2010 study found two weeks of taurine reduced vascular stiffness and improved brachial artery relaxation (FMD) to healthy control levels, with a proposed mechanism of improved nitric oxide bioavailability.

  • 1.5 g/day for 2 weeks
  • Improved FMD and reduced vascular stiffness

Two weeks of taurine supplementation (1.5g/day) reverses early endothelial disfunction in young men with type 1 diabetes, improving blood vessel function. This treatment specifically reduces vascular stiffness and improves brachial artery relaxation (FMD), reversing early damage to the same levels as healthy controls.

Key Findings:

  • Rapid Reversal: In a 2010 study, 2 weeks of taurine supplementation reversed early, detectable conduit vessel abnormalities.
  • Mechanism of Action: Taurine increases nitric oxide bioavailability and reduces oxidative stress/inflammation (e.g., reduces LDL oxidation), which directly protects the endothelium.
  • Vascular Improvements: Specifically, arterial stiffness (measured by the augmentation index) and brachial artery reactivity (measured by FMD) were normalized.
  • Target Group: The study focused on young asymptomatic male diabetics with early-stage vascular impairment.
  • Potential Long-term Impact: These findings suggest taurine may aid in preventing the accelerated progression of cardiovascular disease in type 1 diabetics, and possibly non-diabetics as well.

Notes on Evidence:

  • This study was a double-blind cross-over trial with a small sample size (n=9 diabetics, n=10 controls).
  • Endothelial disfunction means that the inner lining of blood vessels is not working properly. This vessel lining (the endothelium) is supposed to help blood vessels relax, widen, and stay healthy. When it is disfunctional, the vessels become stiffer and do not widen normally. This is an early warning sign of future blood vessel disease, including atherosclerosis and heart problems
  • Taurine has previously been shown to improve various diabetes-related complications, including kidney function and diabetes-induced sexual dysfunction.

Quoted:

"Assessed diabetics had detectable, statistically significant abnormalities when compared with controls, in both arterial stiffness (augmentation index) and brachial artery reactivity (FMD). Both of these parameters were returned to control levels with 2 weeks taurine supplementation. In conclusion, 2 weeks taurine supplementation reverses early, detectable conduit vessel abnormalities in young male diabetics. This may have important implications in the long-term treatment of diabetic patients and their subsequent progression towards atherosclerotic disease."
