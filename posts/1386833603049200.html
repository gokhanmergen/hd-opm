Novartis Lp(a)HORIZON: pelacarsen failed to reduce heart attacks and strokes

Louis Nervegna · Sep 04, 2026 · ♥ 20 reactions · 24 comments · original Facebook post
Lp(a)PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares the first outcome-trial result for an Lp(a)-lowering drug: topline data from Novartis say pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) but did not show reduced cardiovascular risk overall. He says the result seriously questions whether Lp(a) is directly causal, noting only a press announcement is available so far.

  • First randomized outcome trial of an Lp(a)-lowering drug
  • Lp(a) lowered but no demonstrated reduction in MACE in the overall population
  • Only a topline press release available

The first randomized outcome trial to test Lp(a) lowering drugs has failed to show any benefit at reducing heart attacks and strokes (MACE).

We only have a topline press announcement from Novartis at this point, but nevertheless, this result is going to seriously call into question the assumption that Lp(a) is directly causal in heart disease.

NovartisNovartis announces Lp(a)HORIZON Phase III topline results for pelacarsen in patients with elevated Lp(a) and established cardiovascular disease (CVD)novartis.com

Quoted from the press release:

“Although lower Lp(a) levels were observed with pelacarsen, the findings did not demonstrate that this translated into reduced cardiovascular risk in the overall study population. These are not the results we hoped for, but they provide important evidence that advances scientific understanding of the relationship between Lp(a) lowering and cardiovascular outcomes and may help inform future approaches to cardiovascular risk management.”

Shared summary of two NEJM trials. Pelacarsen lowered Lp(a) sharply but event rates were 12.3% vs 12.1% on placebo, and ziltivekimab cut CRP and IL-6 but events were 17.6% vs 17.7%. Cleveland Clinic: pelacarsen did not cut CV events in Lp(a)HORIZON (12.3% vs 12.1%)

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Lp(a)

Louis shares the Cleveland Clinic press release on Lp(a)HORIZON, a 797-site randomized trial in 8,322 patients with prior CVD and Lp(a) of at least 70 mg/dL. The primary endpoint occurred in 12.3% on pelacarsen versus 12.1% on placebo after a median 57 months. Senior author Steven Nissen said a single neutral trial does not prove lack of benefit from lowering Lp(a).
