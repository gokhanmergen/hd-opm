Statins cut deaths 31% in people 80+, showing absolute benefit depends on baseline risk

Apr 02, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 5 Statins

Quotes Dr Carvalho on a new analysis in people aged 80 or older: statin users had a 31% lower relative risk of dying and, as stated, a 17% lower absolute risk. The key principle is that absolute benefit depends on control-group risk, so it looks small in young people over the short term but grows with time, age or illness.

TARGET-D: tailored vitamin D dosing missed its main endpoint but cut heart attack 52%

Summarises TARGET-D (630 adults with acute coronary syndrome, ~4.2 years): doses adjusted to reach 40-80 ng/mL did not significantly reduce the composite of death, heart failure hospitalization or stroke, though heart attack risk was 52% lower. Authors call for validation to rule out data mining.

VESALIUS-CV: Repatha cut first heart attack or stroke 25% in high-risk primary prevention

Nov 08, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 3 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summarises VESALIUS-CV in 12,000+ high-risk patients without prior MI or stroke (median age 66, LDL-C >=90 mg/dL on therapy). Adding evolocumab cut 3-point MACE 25% over ~4.5 years, with LDL-C reduced about 55% to a median 45 mg/dL versus 109 mg/dL on placebo.

Shingles vaccine linked to 28% fewer heart attack and 42.5% fewer stroke hospitalizations

Aug 29, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 28 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A Kaiser Permanente Southern California cohort matched 102,766 Shingrix recipients to 411,064 unvaccinated patients. Vaccinated people had lower shingles complications and fewer hospitalizations for heart attack and stroke. Gokhan notes the effect sizes are striking and that flu vaccine has similar heart-protective effects.
