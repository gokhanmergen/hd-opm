Coffee linked to lower liver and endometrial cancer risk and less DNA damage

Louis Nervegna · Jun 18, 2025 · ♥ 0 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsCancer
AI summary & key facts

Louis quotes a FoundMyFitness episode summary claiming each daily cup is linked to lower liver and endometrial cancer risk and that coffee may reduce DNA double-strand breaks. Three PMIDs are listed but the post itself does not describe those studies.

  • Each daily cup: 15-20% lower liver cancer risk, about 10% lower endometrial cancer risk (optimal around 4-5 cups/day)
  • One study: 23% fewer DNA breaks with daily dark roast coffee
  • PMIDs cited: 28490552, 19585497, 30448878

Quoted:

Coffee actively reduces your cancer risk by protecting your DNA

Every daily cup is linked to 15–20% lower liver cancer risk and ~10% lower endometrial cancer risk (optimal benefits around 4–5 cups/day)

The primary mechanism?

Actively reducing DNA damage—specifically DNA double-stranded breaks, the most severe form of genetic damage, directly threatening chromosomes and accelerating cellular aging

One study even found a 23% drop in these harmful DNA breaks from drinking dark roast coffee daily
