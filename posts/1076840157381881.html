Deidre Tobias is a professor at Harvard Medical School and the Harvard School of Public Health, with a PhD (ScD) from Harvard.

She studies the relationship between nutrition and disease.

She is not a vegan. She eats a traditional mediterranean diet (including animal products), like many in this group.

In this twitter thread, she explains how independent studies (RCT’s) funded by government and academic sources have mostly found that red meat worsens cardiometabolic health.

She explains that the exceptions to this rule have been studies entirely funded by the beef and pork industries.

You can read her editorial on this subject here:

PaperAm J Clinical Nutrition articleajcn.nutrition.org
