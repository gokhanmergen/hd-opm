Glycine may be geroprotective and protective against CVD, per a narrative review

Louis Nervegna · Dec 28, 2025 · ♥ 15 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBlood vessels, circulation & endotheliumAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis quotes a narrative review saying glycine may mitigate risks in acute MI, aortic conditions and endothelial dysfunction. Glycine modestly extends rodent lifespan.

  • Modest lifespan extension in animals
  • Narrative review, not a trial

The amino acid glycine has geroprotective properties, meaning that it may slow certain aspects of the aging process. It modestly extends lifespan in animal models, including rodents. It might be protective against CVD.

Quoted:

Glycine, the simplest proteinogenic amino acid, exhibits significant metabolic and bioactive properties.

Emerging evidence suggests glycine may be a conditionally essential amino acid under specific physiological conditions.

Glycine administration shows promise in mitigating risks and symptoms associated with acute myocardial infarction, aortic conditions, endothelial dysfunction, cardiac hypertrophy, and insulin resistance, among other cardio-metabolic disorders.

Glycine enhances glutathione production, reduces oxidative stress, and improves mitochondrial function and inflammation, offering therapeutic potential for aging- and age-related diseases.

Its multifaceted benefits suggest new therapeutic applications for cardiovascular health and systemic disorders.
