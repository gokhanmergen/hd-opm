Daily multivitamin slowed cognitive aging by about two years in a COSMOS meta-analysis

Louis shares a summary of COSMOS cognition results: in the main analysis of 573 adults 60+, a daily multivitamin modestly improved overall cognition and episodic memory over two years. Pooling with two earlier COSMOS cognition studies (more than 5,000 older adults) gave clear benefits, estimated at about two years of slower cognitive aging. Effects were modest, it is unclear which nutrient matters, dementia prevention was not tested, and the manufacturer donated tablets. He adds that this was an RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver).

Shared from Science Sphere · original post

💊 A daily multivitamin was linked to slower memory decline in older adults, equal to about two years of cognitive aging, in a large set of trials.

The findings come from COSMOS, a major US trial led by researchers at Mass General Brigham.

In the main analysis, 573 adults aged 60 and older were randomly given either a daily multivitamin or a placebo. They took in person thinking tests over two years.

👉 The multivitamin group showed a modest benefit in overall thinking skills. Their episodic memory, which helps you remember events and experiences, also improved. There was no clear benefit for attention and planning.

📊 The team then combined these results with two earlier COSMOS studies. One tested people by phone and the other online. Together, they included more than 5,000 older adults.

The combined results showed clear benefits for both overall thinking and memory. The researchers estimate the multivitamin slowed cognitive aging by about two years compared with placebo.

"The meta-analysis of three separate cognition studies provides strong and consistent evidence," said lead author Chirag Vyas.

⚠️ The effects were modest, and the "two years" figure is an estimate, not a direct measure.

The multivitamin contains more than 20 nutrients, so it is unclear which ones matter.

The trial did not test whether multivitamins prevent dementia.

The tablets were donated by the manufacturer, and the studies were partly funded by Mars Edge as well as the NIH.

🩺 A multivitamin is not a replacement for a healthy diet, exercise and sleep. Check with a doctor before starting supplements.

📃 RESEARCH PAPER
📌 Chirag Vyas et al., "Effect of multivitamin-mineral supplementation versus placebo on cognitive function: Results from the clinic sub-cohort of the COSMOS randomized clinical trial and meta-analysis of three cognition studies within COSMOS," The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition (2024).
