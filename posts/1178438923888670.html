Replace saturated fat with unsaturated fats; yogurt and cheese may be exceptions

Louis Nervegna · Dec 10, 2025 · ♥ 19 reactions · 9 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis links a New York Times piece on whether saturated fat is actually good for you, after Robert F. Kennedy Jr. signaled a shift in dietary advice. His short answer is to replace saturated fat with unsaturated fats from olive oil, nuts, seeds and avocados, noting fermented dairy may blunt saturated fat's effects but this is still debated.

  • Better to replace saturated fat with unsaturated fats (olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado)
  • Yogurt and cheese may be exceptions, still debated
  • Fermentation may blunt some negative effects of dairy saturated fat

Short answer: You’re better off replacing saturated fat with unsaturated fats from olive oil, nuts, seeds, avocados, etc. Yogurt and cheese might be exceptions, but there is still debate about that.

Swapping 10% of carb calories for vegetable fats cut lethal prostate cancer 29%

Dec 20, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes a UCSF-led study of nearly 4,600 men with non-metastatic prostate cancer. Substituting healthy vegetable fats (olive and canola oil, nuts, seeds, avocado) for carbohydrates or animal fats was associated with lower lethal prostate cancer and all-cause mortality. The study adjusted for age, treatment, BMI, smoking, exercise and other factors.

Overfeeding saturated fat raised liver fat 55%, sugar 33%, unsaturated fat 15% in an RCT

A randomized trial overfed three groups by 1000 calories for 3 weeks from butter/dairy, olive oil/nuts or added sugar. Liver fat rose 55%, 15% and 33% respectively, showing calories are not equal for the liver.
