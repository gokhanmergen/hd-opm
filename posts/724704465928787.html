JACC statement on geroscience: why aging drives CVD and how to slow cardiovascular aging

Louis Nervegna · Dec 16, 2023 · ♥ 11 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevityCardiovascular disease: the big picture
AI summary & key facts

Louis calls the JACC Scientific Statement on geroscience the best review so far of why age is the largest CVD risk factor. It argues aging is a common root of chronic disease, explaining multimorbidity, frailty and poorer CVD prognosis in older adults, and describes gerotherapeutic approaches, some already used in CVD care, that could transform management.

  • JACC Scientific Statement, 2023
  • Aging as a shared root of chronic diseases explains multimorbidity and frailty in CVD
  • Presents resilience mechanisms of mammalian aging and gerotherapeutic approaches

Age is (by far) the biggest risk factor for CVD.

This review article discusses why, and what can be done to slow the aging of the cardiovascular system.

It is probably the best review article written on the subject to date.

———-

Impact of Geroscience [the science of aging] on Therapeutic Strategies for Older Adults With Cardiovascular Disease: JACC Scientific Statement

“This hypothetical common root to chronic diseases [aging] explains why patients with CVD are often affected by multimorbidity and frailty and why older age negatively affects CVD prognosis and treatment response.”

“Here, we describe the main resilience mechanisms of mammalian aging, with a focus on how they can affect CVD pathophysiology. We next present novel gerotherapeutic approaches, some of which are already used in management of CVD, and explore their potential to transform care and management of CVD. The geroscience paradigm is gaining traction broadly in medical specialties, with potential to mitigate premature aging, reduce health care disparities, and improve population healthspan.”
