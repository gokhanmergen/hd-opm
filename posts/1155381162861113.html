James Watson, who took aspirin and metformin, died at 97 without ever getting cancer

Louis Nervegna · Nov 08, 2025 · ♥ 17 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
CancerAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis recalls DNA pioneer James Watson's self-designed regimen of aspirin, metformin, no sugar and regular exercise, which he guessed halved his cancer risk. It is an anecdote rather than evidence.

  • Regimen: aspirin, metformin, no sugar, regular exercise
  • Watson estimated a 50% cancer risk reduction (his own guess)

He just died at 97.

For many years, he followed his own anti-cancer regimen: aspirin, metformin, no sugar, regular exercise. He frequently played tennis. He guessed that this reduced his chances of getting cancer by 50%.

It seems to have worked. He never got cancer.

He said all this in a 2016 interview, which I distinctly remember reading at the time:

Platelet reactivity score (PRESS) predicts cardiovascular events in PAD patients

Aug 20, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 19 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

NYU researchers developed PRESS, combining platelet aggregation responses and RNA sequencing, to detect platelet hyperreactivity. In PAD patients, hyperreactive platelets predicted more major adverse cardiac or limb events within 30 days of revascularization, and PRESS above the median carried a 90% higher risk of major events over 18 months. It might help target aspirin.

Centella asiatica and pine bark extract: small trials on arterial plaque stability

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes small trials of Centella asiatica and French maritime pine bark for arterial plaque, including a 1-year study of 60 patients where soft plaque did not grow compared with a 23% increase on placebo. A 3-year study of over 300 patients reported plaque progression in 5.3% with the combination versus over 20% in controls.
