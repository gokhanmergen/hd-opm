Lancet Commission 2024: high LDL and hearing loss each account for 7% of dementia cases

Louis Nervegna · Jun 16, 2025 · ♥ 15 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes the 2024 Lancet Commission on dementia: untreated high LDL in midlife and untreated hearing loss each account for about 7% of dementia cases, vision loss in later life is also a modifiable risk, and nearly half of dementia is potentially preventable through 14 risk factors.

  • 14 modifiable risk factors, about half of dementia potentially preventable
  • High LDL in midlife: about 7% of cases
  • Hearing loss in midlife: another 7%
  • Vision loss in later life newly identified

Untreated high LDL in midlife is a major risk factor for developing dementia.

It is estimated to be responsible for 7% of all dementia cases.

Surprisingly, untreated hearing loss in midlife is also responsible for another 7% of dementia cases.

Vision loss in later life has also been identified as a modifiable risk factor.

Half of dementia cases are likely preventable by addressing 14 modifiable risk factors at different stages of life.

High LDL and hearing loss in midlife are the two biggest modifiable risk factors for dementia.

As life expectancies increase, the number of people living with dementia worldwide continues to rise.

📄 The 2024 Lancet Commission report on dementia prevention, intervention, and care adds compelling new evidence that untreated vision loss and high LDL cholesterol are risk factors for dementia.

Additionally, nearly half of cases of dementia are potentially preventable by addressing 14 modifiable risk factors at different stages of life.
