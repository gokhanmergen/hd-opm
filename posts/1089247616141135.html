Shared from Louis Nervegna · original post

If you’ve already had a heart attack, taking arginine supplements to raise nitric oxide levels can be very dangerous.

A well done randomized controlled trial at Johns Hopkins nearly 20 years ago showed that arginine can precipitate a second heart attack in someone that has already had one. This surprised the researchers, because they were expecting the opposite result.

This might apply to citrillune as well, since it is directly converted into arginine by your body.

Quoted:

“L-arginine provides the raw material from which the body makes nitric oxide, a tiny, transient molecule that helps relax and open blood vessels. Johns Hopkins researchers put it to the test in a rigorous, two-year clinical trial of 153 heart attack survivors. Neither the volunteers nor their doctors knew who was taking three grams of the amino acid three times a day and who was taking a placebo. After just six months the researchers stopped the trial when a peek at the data showed six deaths among volunteers assigned to L-arginine compared with none in the placebo group.”
