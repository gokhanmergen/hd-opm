Commentary on a public figure's reported labs (LDL 51 on rosuvastatin and ezetimibe)

Louis Nervegna · Apr 13, 2025 · ♥ 21 reactions · 39 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis comments on the reported blood panel of a political figure, noting LDL of 51 mg/dL, a favorable TG/HDL ratio and normal glucose and A1C despite poor lifestyle. It is speculation about an individual's health from reported numbers, not research.

  • Reported LDL 51 mg/dL on rosuvastatin 20 mg plus ezetimibe
  • TG/HDL suggests low liver fat
  • Fasting glucose and A1C below prediabetes thresholds

I would be surprised if Donald Trump has a heart attack with these numbers (assuming they are true).

Despite being significantly overweight, sedentary and eating a SAD diet, his blood panel indicates:

  1. An LDL of 51 mg/dL, which is low enough to slow or stop progression of soft plaque.

This is likely due to the 20mg dose of rosuvastatin that he reportedly takes, along with ezetimibe.

  1. A ratio of TG/HDL suggestive of very low ectopic fat in the liver, the main cause of insulin resistance and metabolic syndrome.

This is probably a result of genetics, in his case. (Certainly not diet or exercise.)

  1. Fasting glucose and A1C well below the threshold for prediabetes.
