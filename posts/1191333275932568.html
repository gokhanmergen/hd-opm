Admin asks cardiologist about rosuvastatin raising insulin, and pitavastatin plus PCSK9i

Gokhan Can · Dec 30, 2025 · ♥ 13 reactions · 39 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsInsulin resistance & diabetesPCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs
AI summary & key facts

Personal message to his cardiologist: on rosuvastatin 5 mg plus ezetimibe 10 mg his LDL was 90 then 63 mg/dL, not very different from low-dose rosuvastatin alone. He is concerned that fasting insulin rose roughly 50% on any rosuvastatin dose and wants to explore pitavastatin and a PCSK9 inhibitor. Anecdotal, n-of-1.

  • LDL 90 and 63 mg/dL on rosuvastatin 5 mg + ezetimibe 10 mg
  • Earlier LDL on rosuvastatin 2.5-5 mg: 71, 70, 91
  • Fasting insulin rose from ~14 to ~21 uIU/mL on rosuvastatin
  • Fasting glucose crept from ~90 to ~100 over years

<Saga continues. Sent the following message to my cardiologist>

Dear Dr Shoa,

After my last appointment with you, I've been taking 5mg Rosuvastatin + 10mg Ezetimibe everyday consistently. I got two blood tests so far (2 months apart) LDL = 90 mg/dL and 63 mg/dL.

The best indication is that the results I'm getting on the combo (LDL) aren't very different from what I had on 2.5-5mg Rosuvastatin monotherapy --- LDL ranged: 71, 70, 91 before.

I have two thoughts/questions:

  1. My fasting insulin jumped after any dose of rosuvastatin. From ~14 to ~21 uIU/mL -- almost 50%. My FBG crept up over the years from ~90 to ~100 (Tirzepatide pushes it down, but even on Tirz I had values like 104).

I'm worried about pancreatic exhaustion on Rosuva (on it for ~5 yrs). Am interested in exploring Pitavastatin.

  1. Trying a PCK9-i option instead of Ezetimibe (on top of low dose statin).

Should we try something like Pitavastatin 2mg + an injectible to optimize for longevity? I'm very interested in optimizing for the long term.

Thank you!
Gokhan

EDIT: Attaching my Dr's response as well. I feel so lucky to be his patient!
