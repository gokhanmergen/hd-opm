Shared from Louis Nervegna · original post

Beware of “doctors” who prescribe testosterone for “anti-aging”. The only proven benefit of testosterone therapy is to treat clinically diagnosed hypogonadism. No credible study has ever demonstrated any longevity benefit to testosterone therapy beyond treating hypogonadism, in humans or in any other mammal.

Serum testosterone concentration seems to show an inverse U-shaped relationship with human longevity in older men. Levels that are too high are associated with increased mortality, particularly from heart disease.

This data comes from 3690 men ages 70 to 92.

The optimal free testosterone is around 200 in that age range, and the probability of dying goes up linearly above that level.
