SGLT2 inhibitors slow CKD progression regardless of diabetes status, per meta-analyses

Louis Nervegna · Jan 20, 2026 · ♥ 8 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Kidney healthInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a JAMA editorial: meta-analyses show SGLT2 inhibitors consistently reduce kidney disease progression and hospitalizations across eGFR and albuminuria subgroups, regardless of diabetes.

  • Benefits across eGFR and albuminuria subgroups
  • Independent of diabetes status
  • CKD is a major heart disease risk factor

Meta-analyses of randomized trials show SGLT2 inhibitor drugs slow progression of chronic kidney disease.

It’s becoming clear that this class of drugs does much more than just treat diabetes.

Chronic kidney disease is a major risk factor for heart disease.

Slowing its progression not only protects the kidneys, but the whole cardiovascular system as well.
