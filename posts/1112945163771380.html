Layne Norton rebuts seed-oil claims with human evidence on inflammation, LDL and oxidation

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A shared post argues that human trial evidence does not support the claim that seed oils are harmful. Systematic reviews of randomized trials find that raising linoleic acid does not consistently increase inflammatory markers, replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL and cuts coronary events, and trials show no significant increase in lipid peroxidation. It adds that LDL oxidation mostly occurs after retention in the artery wall, so LDL concentration still matters.

Higher blood linoleic acid predicts lower CVD risk in 68,000-person pooled analysis

Jul 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Johns Hopkins piece citing the 2019 Marklund pooled analysis of over 68,000 participants in 30 cohorts across 13 countries. People with the highest blood linoleic acid had lower CVD risk, especially for CV mortality and stroke; controlled trials also show LA lowers LDL and may improve glucose metabolism. The association is observational, which the author notes.

Swapping saturated fat for polyunsaturated seed oils cut CVD events 29% in strict RCTs

Jun 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis states adipose omega-6 is linked to lower mortality in cohorts, and that RCTs meeting strict criteria (no trans fats, supplied food, 2+ years, biomarker-verified adherence, validated events) show a 29% reduction in heart disease events from replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat. He explains why the Minnesota Coronary Experiment, Sydney Heart Study and Rose Corn Oil Trial fail those criteria.

Medical schools push back on seed-oil myths; pooled cohorts link omega-6 to lower risk

Jun 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 26 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says major medical and public health schools are countering influencer claims about seed oils. He notes a 2019 pooled analysis of 30 prospective cohorts found higher blood omega-6 (linoleic acid) predicted lower risk of heart disease and diabetes over 2.5 to 30 years, and points to earlier group posts.

Over 100 randomized trials show polyunsaturated seed-oil fats improve insulin sensitivity

Nov 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis states that over 100 human randomized trials in aggregate show polyunsaturated fats improve insulin sensitivity and blood sugar control. The shared FoundMyFitness post adds that these omega-6 fats can lower LDL and have not been shown to raise inflammation in trials, but repeatedly reheated oil forms harmful oxidation products.
