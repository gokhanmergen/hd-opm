High CAC score is not a death sentence: weight loss, LDL below 70, diet and exercise

Louis Nervegna · May 28, 2025 · ♥ 31 reactions · 21 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyLDL, ApoB & lipid panelWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Louis tells group members with high CAC that the risk is high but manageable: about 30 lb weight loss clears liver and pancreas fat and can reverse soft plaque, LDL below 70 mg/dL often halts or reverses plaque, a Mediterranean diet has the most evidence, and exercise lowers risk at the same CAC.

  • About 30 lb weight loss clears ectopic fat
  • LDL below 70 mg/dL often stops or reverses plaque growth
  • Exercisers with high CAC have lower heart attack risk

We have lots of people in the group with high CAC scores. It is not a death sentence.

But it does put you at high risk, so it is important to manage that risk.

The magic number for weight loss is around 30lbs for most people. This clears the liver and pancreas of ectopic fat and reverses metabolic syndrome and insulin resistance. Studies have also shown that 30lbs of weight loss can reverse soft plaque.

↪ Roy Taylor trial: clearing liver and pancreas fat via 8-week low-calorie diet reversed T2D

↪ Six strategies shown to regress plaque in human randomized trials

↪ Liver fat is the biggest driver of insulin resistance in most people, per a Harvard expert

The diet that has the most evidence to prevent heart attacks and strokes is the traditional Mediterranean diet.

↪ Meta-analysis of 4 Mediterranean diet RCTs: MACE down 48%, CVD death down 46%

You can learn about it in this book:

↪ Free book 'Eat, Drink, and Be Healthy' recommended for the Mediterranean diet

A good summary of the diet can be found in this post:

↪ How to follow a traditional Mediterranean diet: do's and don'ts

When LDL is pushed below 70 mg/dL using diet and medication, plaque growth often stops and even reverses. Many studies have now proven this. So getting your LDL low is imperative to stop the progression of your disease.

↪ Six strategies shown to regress plaque in human randomized trials

You must manage all your risk factors. Not just LDL. You can do this with diet, medication, lifestyle, supplements, etc. This is what we focus on in this group.

↪ Seventeen modifiable heart-disease risk factors: target as many as possible

Exercise is important. People with high CAC scores who exercise regularly are at much lower risk of a heart attack than people with the same CAC score who do not exercise.

↪ With the same CAC score, fitter people have much lower heart attack risk over 20-25 years
