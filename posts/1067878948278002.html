Resistance exercise preserves muscle during calorie restriction and GLP-1 weight loss

Mar 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 8 Exercise & fitness

Louis argues that most muscle lost during calorie restriction can be prevented by resistance exercise without raising protein intake, citing over 70 RCTs in which exercise drives nearly all strength gains and extra protein adds only about 5% over 22 weeks. He applies this to GLP-1 drugs, saying their muscle loss comes from calorie restriction rather than a drug side effect. The shared study (16 weeks, 82 people with type 2 diabetes) showed that high-dose exercise with diet fully preserved muscle.

GLP-1 agonists cut heart attack risk 14% and CV mortality 13% in a 25-trial meta-analysis

Jul 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 11 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis summarises a meta-analysis of 25 randomized trials showing GLP-1 agonists reduce MI, cardiovascular death, MACE and stroke versus placebo over about 3.5 years, with and without diabetes. Benefits were on top of optimal treatment with statins and blood pressure drugs and were larger at higher BMI.
