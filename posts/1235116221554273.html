Louis: processed meat contains nitrosamines that are carcinogenic and atherogenic

Louis Nervegna · Feb 27, 2026 · ♥ 10 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Meat, eggs & dairyCancer
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues curing meat, even with celery powder, forms nitrosamines linked to cancer, atherosclerosis and diabetes, and that processed meat is a very recent addition in evolutionary terms. He links WHO/IARC classification of processed meat as carcinogenic.

  • Hominids have existed 6-7 million years; cured meat only about 5,000 years
  • IARC classifies processed meat as carcinogenic

Processed meat is not “real food”.

Your hominid ancestors did not eat processed meat. When they successfully hunted animals, they ate the meat fresh and unprocessed.

Hominids have existed on earth for 6-7 million years. We have only been eating cured meat for the last 5000 years. That is less than 0.1% of our evolutionary history.

Curing meat chemically transforms it.

Toxic nitrosamines are formed, which are carcinogenic, atherogenic, and diabetagenic.

This includes the processed meats that are deceptively labeled as “natural” or “uncured”, which use celery powder or sea salt to chemically transform the meat in place of sodium nitrite:

↪ 'Uncured' and 'nitrate-free' processed meats still get nitrates from celery and sea salt

↪ 'Uncured' and 'nitrate-free' processed meats can carry as many or more nitrosamines

Any meat that is smoked, cured, or chemically transformed with salt is classified as processed meat. All of these preparation methods chemically transform the meat, creating harmful chemicals as a result.

These processed meats are considered ultra-processed foods.

In large epidemiological studies, these processed meats have the worst hazard ratios of any food category that you can possibly eat, with the only exception being trans fats. They raise mortality more than any other food:

facebook.com/share/v/18NzYS1pLQ

The WHO has repeatedly warned that they directly cause colon cancer. They classify processed meat as a group 1 carcinogen, in the same category as asbestos, smoking, and ionizing radiation:

No amount of advertising or PR will change these basic facts.
