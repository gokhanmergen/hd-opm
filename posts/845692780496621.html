Simple metabolic health markers: waist-to-height ratio, adiponectin and TG/HDL ratio

Louis Nervegna · Aug 02, 2024 · ♥ 8 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesRisk assessment & lab testsWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

The post names three simple measures of metabolic health (waist-to-height ratio as a visceral fat proxy, adiponectin, TG/HDL ratio) from a Christos Mantzoros podcast clip. Metabolic health means good insulin sensitivity and glycemic control, and metabolic syndrome adds to residual CVD risk.

  • Tests: waist/height ratio, adiponectin, TG/HDL
  • Metabolic health = good insulin sensitivity and glycemic control without metabolic syndrome features
  • Metabolic syndrome contributes to residual CVD risk

What simple tests give you a measure of your “metabolic health” ?

waist-circumference/height ratio (visceral fat)
Adiponectin
TG/HDL ratio

“Metabolic health” essentially means good insulin sensitivity and glycemic control with the absence of symptoms of metabolic syndrome.

Metabolic syndrome is a major risk factor for CVD, which contributes to residual risk.
