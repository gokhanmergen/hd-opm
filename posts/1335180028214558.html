Shared from BoxLife Magazine · original post

Brian, 43, reversed significant arterial plaque in 16 weeks using a targeted diet devised by Dr. Michael Diamonds.

His daily plate combined six powerhouse foods—wild salmon, leafy greens, extra virgin olive oil, berries or pomegranate, raw garlic, and ground flax seeds—to prevent new plaque and stabilize soft deposits.

These ingredients act through varied mechanisms—omega-3 EPA integration, nitrate-driven nitric oxide production, oleocanthal’s anti-inflammatory effect, antioxidant polyphenols, allicin’s blood-thinning, and flax’s LDL-binding fiber.

The article also warns that seemingly healthy items like flavored yogurts, granola bars, and juices can undermine progress, and invites readers to explore full preparation and lifestyle guidance.

DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed ~40 mm3 of soft plaque in one year

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains the DISCO-CT randomized trial, where a DASH diet plus statins produced the largest soft (non-calcified) plaque regression of any dietary trial on CT angiography. Adjusted to one year it was -39.5 mm3, versus an average at-risk person growing about 5 mm3 per year, so roughly 8 years of progression reversed. He notes the DASH diet was about 30% fat, mostly unsaturated, and adds caveats about interpretation.

At least six strategies have reversed plaque in randomized trials

Aug 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists six strategies shown to reverse plaque in human RCTs: major weight loss, Mediterranean/DASH diet, LDL-lowering drugs (statins, PCSK9 inhibitors, ezetimibe), colchicine, exercise and high-dose EPA fish oil. He would like to see a trial combining all of them.
