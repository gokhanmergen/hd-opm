Personalized mRNA vaccine succeeds in phase 3 melanoma trial after ~50% phase 2 benefit

A personalized mRNA vaccine plus Keytruda improved recurrence-free and distant metastasis-free survival in a phase 3 trial of more than 1,100 patients with melanoma. The earlier phase 2 trial showed roughly a 50% reduction in recurrence or death at 5 years. Approval might come as early as 2027, and trials are under way in other cancers.

COVID mRNA vaccination before surgery tied to doubled glioblastoma survival (preprint)

Aug 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 3 Cancer

A preprint from UAB found that 187 glioblastoma patients vaccinated against COVID within 100 days before biopsy or surgery had median survival of 743 days vs 349 days unvaccinated. The association was not seen with flu, pneumonia or shingles vaccines. Louis cautions about healthy-user bias but notes all patients were treated at one hospital, and says a randomized trial is needed.

MeMeMe trial: metformin cut diabetes 80-92% in metabolic syndrome; Med diet did not

Aug 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

In 1,442 people with metabolic syndrome followed about 3 years, 1,700 mg/day metformin prevented type 2 diabetes, which fully explained the difference in major noncommunicable diseases. The Mediterranean diet intervention did not. Gokhan notes no heart disease or cancer prevention from metformin either.

Meta-analysis: lowering LDL with statins had no effect on cancer incidence or death

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Statins

Summarizes an individual-participant meta-analysis from the Cholesterol Treatment Trialists showing that about five years of statin therapy did not change new cancer diagnoses or cancer deaths. This is reassuring evidence against fears that lowering LDL promotes cancer.
