Blending fruit into smoothies does not raise the blood sugar spike and may lower it

Louis Nervegna · Oct 25, 2026 · ♥ 4 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grainsInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

Louis disputes the claim that blended fruit spikes blood sugar, saying randomized trials show blending binds soluble fiber to sugar and slows absorption, reducing the glucose spike. The quoted post says smoothies may be better for you than the whole fruit.

  • Multiple human randomized trials claimed, not named in post
  • Blending said to bind soluble fiber to sugar and reduce glucose spike

Blending whole fruit into smoothies does NOT increase the blood sugar spike.

This is nonsense.

In fact, the exact opposite happens: The blending causes the soluble fiber in the fruit to bind more tightly to the sugar, which actually reduces the glucose spike. Multiple randomized trials in humans have proven this.

Enjoy your fruit smoothies. They are good for you.

Quoted:

SMOOTHIES MAY BE BETTER FOR YOU THAN THE FRUIT IN THEM.

It appears that drinking smoothies slows the sugar absorption, than just eating the fruit.
