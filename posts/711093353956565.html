Raw garlic's allicin lowered TMAO and improved gut microbiome in a human trial and mice

Louis Nervegna · Nov 17, 2023 · ♥ 6 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Gut microbiomeNuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsBlood vessels, circulation & endothelium
AI summary & key facts

Louis highlights an npj Biofilms and Microbiomes paper showing raw garlic juice and allicin reduce TMAO production via gut microbiota. In people with high TMAO production, a week of raw garlic juice reduced TMAO formation, increased microbial diversity and beneficial bacteria; in mice allicin ameliorated atherosclerosis. He notes cooking destroys allicin.

  • One week of raw garlic juice in high-TMAO-producing humans reduced TMAO formation
  • Allicin inhibited gamma-butyrobetaine and trimethylamine production in vitro and ex vivo
  • Atherosclerosis reversed in mouse models
  • Cooked garlic loses allicin

Allicin from garlic reduces TMAO levels and improves the health of the gut microbiome. In mouse models, it reverses atherosclerosis by this TMAO mechanism.

Note that the garlic must be RAW for this to work. Cooked garlic is still very good for you, but the heat destroys the allicin.

“In human subjects exhibiting high-TMAO production, raw garlic juice intake for a week reduced TMAO formation, improved gut microbial diversity, and increased the relative abundances of beneficial bacteria. In in vitro and ex vivo studies, raw garlic juice and allicin inhibited γ-butyrobetaine (γBB) and trimethylamine production by the gut microbiota. Thus, raw garlic juice and allicin can potentially prevent cardiovascular disease by decreasing TMAO production via gut microbiota modulation.”
