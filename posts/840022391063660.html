Statins neither cause nor cure cancer: meta-analyses of randomized trials show no effect

Dec 31, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Statins

Reviews the evidence on statins and cancer. A 2012 meta-analysis of 134,537 participants in 22 RCTs found identical cancer incidence (1.4% per year) and mortality (0.5% per year) in statin users and non-users, and a 2023 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs found the same. Anticancer effects in specific contexts remain preclinical or suggestive.

Meta-analysis: lowering LDL with statins had no effect on cancer incidence or death

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Statins

Summarizes an individual-participant meta-analysis from the Cholesterol Treatment Trialists showing that about five years of statin therapy did not change new cancer diagnoses or cancer deaths. This is reassuring evidence against fears that lowering LDL promotes cancer.

Statin use linked to 35% lower odds of liver cancer in UK database study

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Statins

A UK Clinical Practice Research Datalink case-control study of 3,719 liver cancer cases and 14,876 controls found statin users had 35% lower odds of liver cancer, and cholesterol absorption inhibitors (ezetimibe class) 31% lower odds. This is an association, not proof, and non-statin findings need replication.

Statins associated with lower advanced and fatal prostate cancer and longer survival

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis cites a 2022 review of the epidemiology of statins and prostate cancer. Registry and cohort studies find statin use linked to lower advanced and fatal prostate cancer risk and longer survival in men already diagnosed. These are observational associations.
