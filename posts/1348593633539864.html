Bempedoic acid cut CV events and may also dampen age-related inflammation

Louis Nervegna · Jul 21, 2026 · ♥ 10 reactions · 14 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares Gokhan Can's summary of bempedoic acid in 4,206 statin-intolerant primary prevention patients: the primary composite fell 30%, MI 39%, and all-cause mortality 27%. It lowers LDL by 21.3% and hsCRP by 21.5%, and inhibits ACLY, which drives senescence inflammation in aged mice.

  • Primary outcome HR 0.70 (0.55-0.89); CV death/MI/stroke HR 0.64
  • All-cause mortality HR 0.73 (0.54-0.98)
  • LDL-C reduced 21.3%, hsCRP 21.5%
  • More gout and gallstones; higher creatinine, uric acid, liver enzymes

Bempedoic Acid as A Potential Life Extension Drug

Study Overview: Bempedoic Acid Cardiovascular Outcomes Study

  • Population: 4,206 statin-intolerant primary prevention patients at high cardiovascular risk
  • Intervention: Bempedoic acid 180 mg daily vs. placebo
  • Primary Outcome: Composite of cardiovascular death, nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke, or coronary revascularization

Key Results

  1. Primary outcome: 30% reduction (HR 0.70, 95% CI 0.55-0.89)
  2. Cardiovascular death, MI, or stroke: 36% reduction (HR 0.64, 95% CI 0.48-0.84)
  3. Myocardial infarction (MI): 39% reduction (HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.39-0.98)
  4. Cardiovascular death: 39% reduction (HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.41-0.92)
  5. All-cause mortality: 27% reduction (HR 0.73, 95% CI 0.54-0.98)

Mechanism of Action

  • LDL cholesterol reduction: 21.3%
  • High-sensitivity C-reactive protein reduction: 21.5%

Adverse Effects

  • Increased incidence of gout and cholelithiasis
  • Elevated serum creatinine, uric acid, and hepatic enzyme levels

ACLY and Cellular Senescence Study

  • Identified ATP-citrate lyase (ACLY) as a key driver of age-related inflammation
  • ACLY activates the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP)
  • Inhibiting ACLY reduced inflammation in aging cells and aged mice

Bempedoic Acid as a Potential Life Extension Drug

Mechanism

  • Known action: Inhibits ATP-citrate lyase (ACLY)
  • Dual action potential:Lowers cardiovascular risk through LDL reduction May suppress age-related inflammation by inhibiting ACLY

Addressing Hallmarks of Aging

  • Dyslipidemia
  • Chronic inflammation
  • Potentially cellular senescence

Life Extension Potential

  • Significant reductions in cardiovascular events and all-cause mortality suggest potential life extension in high-risk primary prevention patients intolerant to statins
  • May have broader anti-aging effects beyond cardiovascular health
  • Could influence other age-related conditions (e.g., dementia, diabetes)

Implications and Future Directions

  1. Need for long-term studies to confirm effects on lifespan and healthspan
  2. Importance of assessing long-term safety and optimal use in different populations
  3. Potential to extend healthspan by reducing age-related diseases

Conclusion

Bempedoic acid shows promise as a life extension drug due to its demonstrated cardiovascular benefits and potential to address fundamental aging processes through ACLY inhibition. Further research is needed to fully understand its long-term effects on overall lifespan and health span.
