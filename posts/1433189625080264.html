High B12 blood levels may reflect disease rather than cause it: reverse causality

Louis Nervegna · Oct 27, 2026 · ♥ 2 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsHow to read research
AI summary & key facts

Louis notes observational studies link high B12 to various diseases, but certain disease processes themselves raise blood B12 even without supplementation. This is reverse causality, and the shared infographic covers persistently elevated serum B12 without supplementation.

  • Some diseases raise B12 levels independent of supplementation

There are observational studies that link high blood levels of vitamin B12 to risk of various diseases.

But the problem is that certain disease processes themselves can raise blood levels of B12, even in the absence of supplementation. This is an example of reverse causality.
