The idea that big pharmaceutical companies are making enormous amounts of money by pushing statins makes no sense.

Most statins are off patent. There are cheap generic forms for most of them.

I can get a 3 month supply of 20mg Atorvastatin tablets (Lipitor) for $7.70, without using insurance. That is cheaper than most supplements. It is almost free.

I split them in half and it lasts me 6 months.

Nobody is making any real money from off-patent statins anymore. They are now no more profitable than selling vitamins.

For example, Atorvastatin has been generic for almost 15 years now. Nobody owns the patent for it anymore. Anybody can sell it.

Since atorvastatin is on the WHO list of essential medicines, pharmaceutical companies are essentially forced to make it, even though the profit margins on it are near zero.

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 41 Statins

Evidence review concluding statins reduce events and soft plaque but do not slow Agatston CAC progression, and that long-term cohorts show calcification accelerating without plateau, interpreted as plaque repair into stable scar. Trials: St. Francis (CAC unchanged, events down 33%), SALTIRE (progression 26% vs 18%) and BELLES (similar progression, ~11-14%).

Meta-analysis: lipid-lowering therapy regresses coronary plaque (Li 2021, SATURN)

Summarizes Li et al. 2021, a meta-analysis of 31 IVUS studies (4,997 patients) showing lipid-lowering therapy lowers atheroma volume, with maximal regression at LDL below 80 and HDL above 45 mg/dL. It also covers the SATURN analysis, where rosuvastatin 40 mg regressed plaque more than atorvastatin 80 mg.
