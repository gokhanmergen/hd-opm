Vitamin C and E did not blunt resistance training gains in older women with sarcopenia

Louis Nervegna · Sep 08, 2025 · ♥ 21 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsExercise & fitness
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a trial summary challenging the idea that antioxidants blunt exercise adaptation. Older women with sarcopenia who added vitamins C and E to 12 weeks of resistance band training gained more muscle and strength with lower inflammation.

  • 12 weeks of resistance band training; 1000 mg vitamin C plus 335 mg vitamin E
  • Greater gain in skeletal muscle index (+0.71 kg/m^2)
  • Grip strength +3.7 kg and knee extension +2.3 kg
  • Lower IL-6 and improved oxidative stress marker (GSH/GSSG)

So much for the idea that antioxidants blunt the adaptive effects of exercise…

It’s certainly not true in older women doing resistance exercise.
