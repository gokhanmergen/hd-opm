Clearing senescent 'zombie cells' from blood vessels improved insulin resistance in mice

Gokhan Can · Nov 22, 2025 · ♥ 9 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Shares a news report of a mouse study in which removing aged cells from blood vessels reduced inflammation, improved blood sugar and countered insulin resistance. The admin notes insulin resistance is multifactorial and finds it interesting that old blood-vessel cells appeared to be the main driver in this study.

Video: immunoglobulin G buildup in fat tissue drives insulin resistance and aging

Gokhan summarizes a Mike Lustgarten video on research showing IgG accumulates in fat tissue with age and obesity, impairs insulin signaling, promotes inflammation and fibrosis, and is reduced by calorie restriction. Niacin breakdown products 2PY and 4PY linked to higher cardiovascular risk (Ferrell 2024)

Feb 19, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 6 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes Ferrell et al. 2024: untargeted metabolomics found that the niacin end-products 2PY and 4PY predict major adverse cardiac events, with 4PY promoting vascular inflammation.
