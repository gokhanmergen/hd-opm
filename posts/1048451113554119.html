Shared from Gokhan Can · original post

This video by Physionic discusses a study (Cardioprev) on the Mediterranean diet's impact on atherosclerosis. The study compared the Mediterranean diet to a low-fat diet in people with coronary heart disease over seven years. The Mediterranean diet showed a significant reduction in arterial plaque buildup, particularly in the first five years, and fewer cardiovascular events like heart attacks and strokes compared to the low-fat diet.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet reversed carotid plaque and cut recurrent events ~25%

Apr 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis summarizes the 2021 CORDIOPREV RCT in patients with prior heart attack or stroke on standard medical care: a Mediterranean diet rich in olive oil, nuts and fish reduced carotid thickness at 5 and 7 years, unlike a low-fat diet, and reduced maximum plaque height. Recurrent major cardiovascular events were roughly 25% lower, even though participants were already on statins and other drugs.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet shrank carotid plaque and cut recurrent events about 25%

Mar 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 13 ↩ 27 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes CORDIOPREV, an RCT of 1002 coronary patients comparing a Mediterranean diet rich in extra virgin olive oil with a low-fat diet. The Mediterranean diet reduced carotid intima-media thickness at 5 and 7 years while the low-fat diet showed no change, and also lowered carotid plaque height. He also cites roughly 25% fewer recurrent major cardiovascular events over 7 years, with benefits seen despite statin treatment and mean LDL-C below 90.

Mediterranean-diet RCT meta-analysis: 48% fewer MACE; WFPBNO only has one small RCT

Jun 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 5 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares a May 2024 meta-analysis of the four large Mediterranean diet RCTs (Lyon, Indo-Mediterranean, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV) with substantial reductions in cardiovascular events, and argues the only RCT of a whole-food plant-based no-oil diet is Ornish's very small trial, with other WFPBNO evidence observational.

Meta-analysis of 4 Mediterranean diet RCTs: MACE down 48%, CVD death down 46%

Jan 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 20 ↩ 18 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes a 2024 meta-analysis pooling four long RCTs (Lyon, Indo-Mediterranean, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV; 10,054 participants) comparing a Mediterranean diet to control diets. It lowered major cardiovascular events, MI, stroke and CV death but not all-cause mortality or revascularization.

At least six strategies have reversed plaque in randomized trials

Aug 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists six strategies shown to reverse plaque in human RCTs: major weight loss, Mediterranean/DASH diet, LDL-lowering drugs (statins, PCSK9 inhibitors, ezetimibe), colchicine, exercise and high-dose EPA fish oil. He would like to see a trial combining all of them.
