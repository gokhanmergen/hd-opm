JAHA cohort: higher ketone body levels linked to more cardiovascular events and death

Gokhan Can · Mar 02, 2026 · ♥ 9 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
Low-carb, keto & carnivoreHeart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

Shares a JAHA report that higher circulating ketone body levels are associated with increased cardiovascular events and mortality in a large population cohort. The admin finds it worrying for people who use keto diets therapeutically; the post does not say whether ketones came from diet, and association does not show causation.

  • Higher ketone body levels associated with more CV events and mortality
  • Large population cohort

Kinda scary for those of us who tout keto diet as a therapeutic lifestyle.

Each daily yogurt serving linked to 14% lower CVD mortality in a 17-cohort meta-analysis

Feb 16, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 · 💬 12 Meat, eggs & dairy

A systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of 17 cohort studies (896,871 participants) found higher yogurt intake was associated with lower all-cause and cardiovascular mortality, with the largest benefit around 0.5 servings per day and no clear further gain above that. No significant association was seen with cancer mortality. As an observational analysis, it shows association, not causation.
