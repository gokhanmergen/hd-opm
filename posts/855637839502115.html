Black cumin meta-analyses show better lipids, BP and glucose; LDL fell 27% in one trial

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 31 · 💬 13 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares a NutritionFacts summary that daily black cumin improved cholesterol, triglycerides, blood pressure and blood sugar across meta-analyses of RCTs. In one trial, postmenopausal women taking 1 g/day lowered LDL by 27% in two months, which rebounded after stopping.

New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Aug 31, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 38 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified. Carvalho argues calcium stabilises plaque, so slowing calcification without reducing plaque could even be harmful, and no evidence shows K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, so he advises relying on proven therapies, diet and exercise.

Dr Carvalho: evidence for vitamin K2 supplements and arterial calcium is weak

Nov 16, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 · 💬 27 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Carvalho video: arterial calcium is a marker of long-standing plaque, not the cause of risk, so lowering a calcium score can mislead, just as statins raise calcium while lowering risk. No large properly powered trials show K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, and he warns of false reassurance. The admin adds that supplement use is fine but expectations should stay grounded.

North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. The shared cardiologist's post describes North Karelia's 700 per 100,000 CVD death rate in 1972, with 24% of calories from saturated fat, and a 20% cholesterol drop with coronary deaths in working-age men falling 82%.

Some with high LDL avoid plaque, but heart disease is multifactorial and most aren't lucky

Jun 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 6 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis recommends a short video (Dr. Daniel Chong with lipidologist Tom Dayspring) on why some people with high LDL avoid plaque. He stresses genetic protection is the exception; LDL/ApoB is one major factor among many, including insulin resistance, inflammation, endothelial dysfunction and blood pressure.
