One-year RCT: low-carb and whole-food high-carb diets removed liver and visceral fat alike

AI summary & key facts

Louis describes a one-year randomized trial comparing a low-carb diet with two higher-carb diets built on whole foods, one with intact whole grains. All three removed liver and visceral fat, and diets with intact grains or low carb did slightly better than one with grains milled into flour.

  • Calories matched (2000 women, 2500 men), protein 17% of calories
  • Low carb: 8% carbs, 75% fat (30% saturated); good-carb diets: 45% carbs, 38% fat (10-12% saturated)
  • All 3 diets effectively reduced liver and visceral fat
  • Intact whole grains and low carb performed similarly, slightly better than flour-based whole grains

What is the best diet to lose liver fat?

As we’ve talked about many times in the group, liver fat is the primary driver of insulin resistance in most people:

↪ Liver fat is the biggest driver of insulin resistance in most people, per a Harvard expert

↪ Liver fat as low as 1.85% is linked to insulin resistance (Yale)

The randomized controlled trial described in this 10 minute video directly compared 3 different diets over a period of 1 year:

  • Low carb
  • Good carbs from whole food
  • Good carbs from whole food, but all whole grains must be eaten intact

Each diet was designed to be the same number of calories: 2000 for women, 2500 for men.

Each group was asked to consume the same amount of protein: 17% of calories.

The low carb group was asked to consume only 8% of calories from carbs, and 75% from fat (30% saturated). The two good carb groups were asked to consume 45% of calories from carbs, and 38% from fat (10-12% saturated).

The final result was that all 3 diets were very effective at removing liver fat, and at removing visceral fat in general.

The low carb and good carb diet with intact whole grains worked the same at removing liver fat and visceral fat. Both did a little bit better than the good carb diet that allowed whole grains mechanically processed into flour.

A key takeaway from this study was that eating whole grains and fruit was fine when trying to lose liver fat. A diet that included intact whole grains and fruit worked just as well as a diet that dramatically cut all carbs

However, to get the best results, the study showed that the whole grains should be eaten as intact as possible. (For example, eat intact brown rice instead of crackers made from brown rice flour. Or eat slow cook steel cut oats instead of instant whole oats.)
