Case report: rapid CAD progression after stopping statin and starting keto (LMHR)

Louis Nervegna · Oct 28, 2024 · ♥ 6 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a Circulation abstract describing a patient with a lean mass hyper-responder profile whose coronary disease progressed rapidly on a ketogenic diet without lipid treatment. The authors caution that people with known CAD and LMHR should be careful with popular diets and consult a provider. A single case, but a cautionary one.

  • Single patient (case report)
  • Rapid CAD progression after stopping a statin and starting keto
  • Authors: LMHR with known CAD should be very cautious

Some people with heart disease do not respond well to ketogenic diets. Maybe it’s just a minority, but the potential for a bad response is clearly there in some vulnerable people.

“Abstract 17807: Rapid Progression of CAD After Stopping Statin and Starting a Ketogenic Diet in a Phenotypic Lean Mass Hyper-Responder

Conclusions: Social media has influenced many to try ketogenic diet to manage metabolic health. Some influencers have questioned high-LDL association with ASCVD and have recommended avoiding pharmacotherapy. Despite popular opinion that high-LDL in this phenotype does not have clinical implication, our patient with a similar profile had rapid progression of CAD while on a KD and was untreated for HLD. Patients with known CAD and LMHR should be very cautious when starting popular diets and should discuss the possible implications with their provider.”
