Shared from MedPage Today · original post

Maintaining three key health factors during midlife -- normal blood pressure, no diabetes, and not smoking -- was associated with nearly 13 additional dementia-free years, a study of 12,000 people in the U.S. showed.

Individuals who met these criteria at age 55 lived an average 30.1 more years dementia-free, reported Josef Coresh, MD, PhD, of NYU Langone Health in New York City, and co-authors.
