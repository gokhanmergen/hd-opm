Admin opinion: avoid pesticides because they may disrupt the gut microbiome

Personal opinion urging avoidance of pesticides and non-organic food, with a Science news article suggesting pesticides may disrupt the gut microbiome and contribute to diabetes. No specific study is described.

My personal opinion: In USA, humans are like lab rats --there aren't adequate safeguards to ensure the long term safety of our food supply. My advice: steer clear of toxic chemicals at all costs, including pesticides and non-organic food. Why? At minimum they likely mess up your microbiome; potentially causing numerous diseases like diabetes (see the link). Potentially far worse outcomes are well within the realm of possibility.

DIABMET-FMT: lean-donor fecal transplant improved insulin resistance in type 2 diabetes

Oct 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Gut microbiome

The shared description of the DIABMET-FMT randomized trial says repeated fecal microbiota transplant capsules from lean, metabolically healthy donors improved insulin sensitivity, microbiome diversity and short-chain fatty acid production in adults with type 2 diabetes, without diet, drug or exercise changes. The poster sees it as evidence that the microbiome contributes to diabetes in some people. The details come from the post only and could not be verified here.
