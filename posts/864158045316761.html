Review: nuts, olive oil and nutraceuticals can lower small dense LDL

Gokhan Can · Sep 04, 2024 · ♥ 7 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
LDL, ApoB & lipid panelSupplements & vitaminsNuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods
AI summary & key facts

Clinical review of foods and supplements affecting small dense LDL, an independent risk factor. Pistachios, hazelnuts and mixed nuts in a Mediterranean diet consistently improve sdLDL and particle size, and high-polyphenol olive oil (25 mL/day for 3 weeks) cut small LDL particles 15.3%.

  • Nuts: strong RCT evidence for reducing small dense LDL
  • 25 mL/day high-polyphenol olive oil for 3 weeks: small LDL particles -15.3%

Foods and Natural Supplements for Reducing Small Dense LDL

This review article examines the effects of various nutraceuticals and natural products on small dense LDL (sdLDL) levels, LDL particle number, and LDL particle size. The authors note that sdLDL particles are considered an independent risk factor for cardiovascular disease due to their greater susceptibility to oxidation and ability to penetrate arterial walls.

NUTS

  • Pistachios, hazelnuts, and mixed nuts in a Mediterranean diet, have consistently shown beneficial effects on reducing small dense LDL particles and improving LDL particle size.
  • Evidence: Evidence from randomized controlled trials is generally strong.

Olive Oil (High-Polyphenol-Content)

  • Intervention: 25 mL/day raw high-polyphenol-content olive oil for 3 weeks.
  • Result: Significant reduction in small LDL particles (-15.3%).
  • Evidence: Significant differences noted between groups.

Vegetable Oils (Olive, Rapeseed, Sunflower)

  • Intervention: High-fat diet with vegetable oils for 4 weeks.
  • Result: Significant reduction in LDL size (-0.36 nm), but no significant differences between oils.
  • Evidence: Moderate; significant reduction in LDL size observed, but group differences were not significant.

Avocado Diet

  • Intervention: Fresh Hass avocado (136 g/day) for 5 weeks.
  • Result: Significant reduction in LDL particle number (-80.1 nmol/L) and small dense LDL cholesterol (-4.1 mg/dL).
  • Evidence: Randomized crossover study with significant improvements compared to control.

Strawberries

  • Intervention: 50 g freeze-dried strawberries for 8 weeks.
  • Result: Significant reduction in small LDL particles (14%).
  • Evidence: Controlled trial with significant effects compared to control.

Eicosapentaenoic Acid (EPA) and Docosahexaenoic Acid (DHA)

  • Intervention: 4 g/day fish oil for 12 weeks.
  • Result: Increase in LDL particle sizes (LDL1, LDL2, LDL3) but no change in LDL4.
  • Evidence: Inconclusive; significant increase in larger LDL particles but mixed results on smaller particles.

Curcumin

  • Intervention: 1000 mg/day curcuminoids plus 5 mg piperine for 8 weeks.
  • Result: Significant decrease in small dense LDL levels.
  • Evidence: Randomized controlled trial with significant reductions observed.

Soybean Oil (SBO) and Rice Bran Oil (RBO/PO mixture)

  • Intervention: 20% of total energy as SBO or RBO/PO for 10 weeks.
  • Result: Significant reduction in small dense LDL cholesterol (-10% for SBO, -5% for RBO/PO).
  • Evidence: Significant effects observed compared to palm oil.
