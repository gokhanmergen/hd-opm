Science is verifiable, and what cannot be fact-checked is ideology

Louis Nervegna · Apr 04, 2025 · ♥ 13 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
How to read researchMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues that nothing in science needs to be 'believed' because it can be fact-checked and verified, and that claims which cannot be verified are ideology that merely sounds like science.

When it comes to science, there is really nothing to “believe” in because— by definition— everything in science can be carefully fact-checked and verified.

Either compelling evidence exists, or it does not. That’s what the process of fact-checking and verification uncovers.

If it can’t be fact-checked and verified, then it’s not science— it’s ideology.

There are lots of things that sound like science. It’s easy to be fooled. But when you actually do the hard work to fact-check and verify them, you realize that they are ideology and not science.
