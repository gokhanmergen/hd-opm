Genetics suggest statins' small diabetes risk is tied to LDL lowering (Gemini-assisted)

Sep 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 11 Statins

Louis shares a post, written with Gemini AI, arguing that the small rise in new-onset type 2 diabetes with intensive lipid lowering reflects target-mediated effects rather than an off-target drug side effect. Genetic variants mimicking HMGCR (statin) and NPC1L1 (ezetimibe) inhibition show modestly higher glucose and diabetes risk.

Statin use linked to 35% lower odds of liver cancer in UK database study

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Statins

A UK Clinical Practice Research Datalink case-control study of 3,719 liver cancer cases and 14,876 controls found statin users had 35% lower odds of liver cancer, and cholesterol absorption inhibitors (ezetimibe class) 31% lower odds. This is an association, not proof, and non-statin findings need replication.

Statin-related liver enzyme elevations are rare (about 1 in 1,000) and usually transient

Apr 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 Statins

Louis explains that lasting liver enzyme elevation from statins affects about 1 in 1,000, short-term rises are usually transient, and newer research suggests statins may protect the liver long term. Quotes Harvard Health that abnormal tests were about as common on placebo.

Serious liver problems from statins are rare and routine liver monitoring is unnecessary

Feb 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 17 ↩ 2 Statins

Louis quotes Harvard Health: among statin users about 1 in 1,000 has an abnormal liver test attributable to the drug, and abnormal results are about as common on placebo. A single baseline test is recommended and routine monitoring is not useful for most people.
