Six RCTs show statins reverse total plaque volume by 5% to 17%

Louis lists six randomized trials showing reversal of total atheroma volume with statins: COSMOS ~5%, SATURN ~5.5%, ASTEROID ~6.5%, IBIS-4 ~7.5%, STABLE ~8%, JAPAN-ACS ~17%. About 95% of acute-thrombosis heart attacks involve soft plaque, not calcified plaque

Oct 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post says about 64% of heart attacks from acute thrombosis come from rupture of soft lipid-rich plaque and about 25% from erosion of soft plaque, while only about 5% involve calcified nodules. Because statins and other lipid-lowering drugs stabilize and reverse soft plaque, the poster argues they address the plaque type most responsible for heart attacks.

YELLOW trials: intensive rosuvastatin cut lipid-core plaque within 7-8 weeks

Aug 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 19 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The YELLOW study found that short-term intensive lipid lowering with rosuvastatin reduced lipid-core burden (yellow plaque) in coronary lesions in as little as 7-8 weeks, stabilizing vulnerable plaque rather than shrinking blockages. YELLOW III added the PCSK9 inhibitor evolocumab to statins with greater improvement in plaque composition. Generic rosuvastatin costs under $10/month.
