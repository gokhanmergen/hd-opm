Omega-3 Supplements and Muscle Health: What the Science Says
A new review of 33 systematic studies finds little evidence that omega-3 fatty acid supplementation improves lean mass, muscle strength, or physical function in healthy adults or clinical populations, including cancer patients.

While omega-3s are essential for overall health, their role in muscle growth and preservation remains inconclusive. Some studies suggest small benefits in older adults, but overall, the data do not support omega-3 supplements as a reliable strategy for maintaining muscle mass or strength.

More research is needed to clarify the impact of omega-3s on muscle health, particularly regarding dose, duration, and individual response. For now, exercise and protein intake remain the most effective ways to support muscle function.

Study by Nunes et al., 2025. Published in Clinical Nutrition ESPEN. Link in bio
