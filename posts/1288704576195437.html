Plant phytochemicals act as hormetic signals linked to resilience and healthy aging

How plant compounds act as hormetic signals that activate protective pathways linked to resilience, metabolic health, and healthy aging.

Shared from Eric Verdin · original post

We often think of food as calories, protein, fat, or carbohydrates.

But plants are also delivering molecular signals.

Phytochemicals such as sulforaphane, EGCG, quercetin, curcumin, fisetin, and resveratrol evolved as protective compounds in plants exposed to environmental stress. When we consume them, many interact with conserved cellular pathways linked to antioxidant defense, autophagy, mitochondrial function, inflammation, and metabolic regulation.

This is one reason why diets rich in diverse plant foods consistently associate with healthier aging and lower chronic disease risk.

Dietary fiber as a longevity intervention: 15-30% lower mortality at 25-29 g/day

Dec 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 12 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Long summary arguing fiber is a bioactive longevity tool. The WHO-commissioned Reynolds 2019 meta-analysis found 15-30% lower all-cause mortality at 25-29 g/day, with benefits continuing per extra 8 g; newer NHANES data suggest diminishing returns past ~30 g/day. It describes mechanisms including butyrate-driven epigenetic effects and gut barrier reinforcement against toxins such as microplastics.
