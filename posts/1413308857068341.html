Explainer on false balance bias in science communication

Louis Nervegna · Oct 05, 2026 · ♥ 17 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
How to read researchMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

A definition of false balance bias, where opposing sides are presented as equally valid even when evidence supports only one. The poster tags influencers and evidence, but cites no specific research.

“False balance bias” is a cognitive and journalistic error where opposing sides of an issue are presented as having equal weight, validity, or evidence, even when overwhelming scientific or factual consensus supports only one side.

Often driven by a well-meaning desire for fairness or neutrality, this false equivalency misleads the public by making fringe, unsupported claims appear just as credible as established fact.

Ultimately, rather than achieving true impartiality, this practice distorts reality and undermines public understanding of well-established truths.
