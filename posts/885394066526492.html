Cleveland Clinic: pelacarsen did not cut CV events in Lp(a)HORIZON (12.3% vs 12.1%)

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Lp(a)

Louis shares the Cleveland Clinic press release on Lp(a)HORIZON, a 797-site randomized trial in 8,322 patients with prior CVD and Lp(a) of at least 70 mg/dL. Eggs did not raise TMAO in a Cleveland Clinic randomized trial, unlike choline bitartrate

Jul 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 13 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis rebuts claims that eggs raise AFib risk via TMAO by pointing to an earlier randomized trial from the same Cleveland Clinic group. In that trial, eating eggs or taking phosphatidylcholine did not raise TMAO, whereas choline bitartrate supplements did. The trial was not funded by the egg industry.

Daily Mail egg-AFib headline is wrong: the study linked TMAO, not eggs, to AFib

Jul 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis corrects a Daily Mail headline claiming eggs raise AFib risk. The Cleveland Clinic study associated blood TMAO with AFib, not egg intake, and the same group had earlier shown in a human trial that eggs do not raise TMAO.

Cleveland Clinic links gut-bacteria byproduct TMAO to atrial fibrillation risk

Jul 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 ↩ 2 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Cleveland Clinic researchers analyzed blood samples from over 5,000 people undergoing heart procedures and found TMAO, made by gut bacteria from nutrients in red meat and other animal products, was linked to higher AFib risk. Cell experiments suggested TMAO may alter the heart's electrical environment. Louis frames it as TMAO possibly raising AFib risk.
