UK cancer death rates down 29% since 1989 and 11% in the past decade

Louis Nervegna · Mar 12, 2026 · ♥ 7 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Cancer
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares Cancer Research UK data showing overall cancer death rates have fallen despite rises in younger groups, with stomach cancer down 34% and lung 22% over a decade.

  • Overall -29% since 1989; -11% in past 10 years
  • Stomach -34%, lung -22%, ovarian -19%, breast -14%
  • Bowel -6%

Overall cancer death rates in the UK are down 29% since 1989.

While there have been troubling increases in certain subgroups such as younger people, the overall trend has been down.

These are the cancer types with the biggest drops in death rates in the UK over the past 10 years:

  • Stomach cancer: -34%
  • Lung: -22%
  • Ovarian: -19%
  • Breast: -14%
  • Esophageal: -12%
  • Cervical: -11%
  • Leukemia: -9%
  • Bowel: -6%
  • Overall: -11%
