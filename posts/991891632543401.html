CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet slowed atherosclerosis and cut events vs low-fat diet

Gokhan Can · Mar 24, 2025 · ♥ 5 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

A video summary of the CORDIOPREV study comparing a Mediterranean diet with a low-fat diet in coronary heart disease patients. The Mediterranean arm showed less plaque buildup, mostly in the first five years, and fewer heart attacks and strokes.

  • 7-year follow-up in people with coronary heart disease
  • Greater plaque reduction on Mediterranean diet, mainly in first 5 years
  • Fewer cardiovascular events than low-fat diet

This video by Physionic discusses a study (Cardioprev) on the Mediterranean diet's impact on atherosclerosis. The study compared the Mediterranean diet to a low-fat diet in people with coronary heart disease over seven years. The Mediterranean diet showed a significant reduction in arterial plaque buildup, particularly in the first five years, and fewer cardiovascular events like heart attacks and strokes compared to the low-fat diet.
