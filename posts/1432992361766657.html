Calorie for calorie, whey and beef raise insulin more than white bread or oatmeal

Louis Nervegna · Oct 27, 2026 · ♥ 5 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
ProteinInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues excess protein spikes insulin: calorie for calorie, whey protein raises insulin more than white bread or pure glucose, and beef more than oatmeal or white pasta. He criticizes influencers who ignore the insulin index of protein and warns that frequent extra protein keeps insulin high.

  • Whey raises insulin more than white bread or glucose per calorie
  • Beef raises insulin more than oatmeal or white pasta

Excess protein spikes insulin.

Influencers constantly mislead people about this basic fact.

Calorie for calorie, whey protein powder raises insulin more than white bread or pure glucose. Beef raises insulin more than oatmeal (porridge) or white pasta.
