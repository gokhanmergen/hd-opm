Angus Barbieri fasted 382 days under medical supervision, showing what body fat sustains

Louis Nervegna · Oct 19, 2025 · ♥ 14 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
Fasting & meal timingWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Louis comments on the documented case of Angus Barbieri, who survived 382 days on tea, coffee and vitamins under medical monitoring. He cautions that prolonged fasts are very dangerous, but says the case shows that humans evolved to tolerate periods without food if they have enough body fat.

  • Fast lasted 382 days, monitored by doctors, on tea, coffee and vitamins
  • Louis: the only documented fast beyond one year he knows of
  • Prolonged fasts are very dangerous

This is the only documented case that I’ve ever seen of someone fasting continuously beyond one year.

Previous post on this: ↪ Angus Barbieri survived a 382-day fast and lost 276 pounds under medical supervision

Prolonged fasts like this are very dangerous. But cases like this do prove that it is medically possible, if a person has enough stored body fat.

It’s also a good reminder that we evolved to tolerate reasonable periods of time without access to food. This was a normal part of our evolutionary history as a species. It’s the reason why we gain weight in the first place.
