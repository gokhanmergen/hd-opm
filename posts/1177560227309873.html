A 3-year randomized, double-blind clinical trial known as the DO-HEALTH study was led by Dr. Heike A. Bischoff-Ferrari of the University of Zurich. It was published on April 25, 2022, in Frontiers in Aging.

The study found that healthy adults aged 70 and older who took vitamin D3 (2000 IU per day), omega-3 fatty acids (1 g per day), and followed a home-based strength and balance exercise program experienced a 61% reduction in invasive cancer risk compared with a placebo group.

Out of 2,157 participants across five European countries, only 4 cancer cases occurred in the combined-intervention group versus 12 cases in the placebo group.

The number needed to treat was 35. This shows that the combined lifestyle and nutritional approach, rather than any single intervention alone, was responsible for the significant cancer risk reduction.
