List of olive oils certified by the North American Olive Oil Association for purity

Louis Nervegna · Jul 14, 2024 · ♥ 17 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)
AI summary & key facts

A share of the NAOOA's list of 78 olive oils enrolled in its random testing and certification program for conformity with International Olive Council purity and quality standards. It is useful for people worried about olive oil authenticity, though it comes from an industry trade group.

  • Oils bought from US/Canadian supermarkets and tested several times a year
  • Tested against International Olive Council standards
  • List updated for 2024

“For consumers seeking extra assurance of olive oil quality, here is a list of the olive oils that are enrolled in the North American Olive Oil Association's (NAOOA's) Certified Olive Oil Seal program for random testing and certification.

The NAOOA olive oil certification program is the nation's largest and most complete olive oil testing and certification program. We purchase olive oils from supermarkets in the USA and Canada and test them multiple times per year for adherence to the physico-chemical purity and quality standards set by the International Olive Council (IOC).”
