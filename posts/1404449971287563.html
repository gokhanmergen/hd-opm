Hearing aids before age 70 linked to a 61% lower dementia risk

Louis recommends treating hearing loss with a hearing aid before age 70, citing news of a study of nearly 3,000 participants over up to two decades. Those with hearing loss who used hearing aids had 61% lower dementia risk among people younger than 70. He says multiple studies now show this.

Louis summarizes the 2024 Lancet Commission on dementia: untreated high LDL in midlife and untreated hearing loss each account for about 7% of dementia cases, vision loss in later life is also a modifiable risk, and nearly half of dementia is potentially preventable through 14 risk factors.

High LDL does not protect the brain: 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL as top dementia risk

Dec 15, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 38 Brain health & dementia

Louis counters the claim that high blood LDL protects against dementia. The 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL cholesterol as the biggest modifiable dementia risk factor, accounting for 7% of cases among 14 factors that together account for 45%. He adds circulating cholesterol does not cross the blood-brain barrier; the brain makes its own.
