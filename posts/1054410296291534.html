I want to make sure people understand this.

It is at the heart of why CGM’s are a waste of money for most people, and may actually cause harm. See below.

——-

My A1C is 4.7 because my ectopic liver fat is virtually undetectable in elastography (fibroscan). It is less than 1%.

Elastography (fibroscan) is a special type of ultrasound of the liver that measure its fattiness and stiffness.

I do NOT use a CGM.

Ectopic liver fat is the true cause of insulin resistance:

↪ Liver fat is the biggest driver of insulin resistance in most people, per a Harvard expert

Certainly not whole grains and fruit, which hundreds of prospective studies and RCT’s show reduce the risk of IR. I eat whole grains and fruit at nearly every single meal, and will continue to do so— because eating healthy foods like this is exactly the reason why my liver fat is near zero in the first place. In someone with no liver fat, the blood sugar spikes from whole grains and fruit are short in duration and contribute very little to the total area under the glucose curve from which average glucose level throughout the day is calculated.

(To minimize the spikes even further, it is best to consume the whole grains as intact as possible, and to consume the fruit either in whole form or in smoothies where all the fiber is retained. Studies have actually shown that blending whole fruit into smoothies lowers the glycemic index because the soluble fiber better binds to the sugar, slowing its absorbtion.)

Using CGM’s to choose (unhealthy) foods that lower A1C in non-diabetic patients with metabolic syndrome is covering up the true cause of their underlying problem— ectopic liver fat. All this is doing is hiding the underlying insulin resistance, instead of addressing it head on. This gives patients a false sense of security. The only way to truly reverse insulin resistance is to eliminate ectopic fat in the liver. A CGM does absolutely nothing to facilitate that. It is a waste of money for most people. You are better off spending your money on a fibroscan to measure the fat in your liver, since this actually addresses the root cause.

What really does work is eliminating visceral fat through a diet that effectively lowers calories. This is how you get rid of your liver fat and cure insulin resistance and metabolic syndrome. See links below:

↪ Liver fat is the biggest driver of insulin resistance in most people, per a Harvard expert

↪ Roy Taylor trials: 15 kg weight loss cleared liver and pancreas fat, reversing diabetes

↪ Roy Taylor trial: clearing liver and pancreas fat via 8-week low-calorie diet reversed T2D

↪ Roy Taylor's trial: diet-induced weight loss restores pancreas function in type 2 diabetes

If you are using a CGM to justify replacing healthy foods like slow-cook steel cut oats and fruit with processed meat and red meat— like the cured venison jerky sticks that Peter Attia sells (which are loaded with carcinogenic nitrosamines that cause colon cancer)— you are harming your health in the long term. Many non-diabetic people using these CGM’s are causing themselves far more harm than good by making stupid food substitutions like this based on what the CGM tells them. See links below:

↪ 'Uncured' and 'nitrate-free' processed meats can carry as many or more nitrosamines

↪ Swapping red and processed meat for beans, nuts, fish or poultry lowers CVD risk

If you know exactly what you are doing, a CGM can be a useful tool. But unfortunately most people do not, and are being mislead by companies and influencers who are making a lot of money by promoting these devices.
