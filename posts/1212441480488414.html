People who survive cancer have lower Alzheimer's risk; mouse study suggests why

Louis Nervegna · Jan 29, 2026 · ♥ 10 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaCancer

Louis shares a mouse study suggesting some cancers release signals that help the brain clear Alzheimer's-linked proteins, a possible explanation for the observed inverse association.

  • Cancer survivors have lower Alzheimer's risk
  • Mouse study: cancer-released signals aid clearance of Alzheimer's-linked proteins

People who have had cancer are at lower risk for developing Alzheimer’s.

Until now, the reasons for this have been a mystery.

New research is starting to uncover why. This could lead to new treatments.
