Mouse and human data suggest atherosclerosis meets criteria for an autoimmune disease

Gokhan Can · Oct 03, 2026 · ♥ 13 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post
Inflammation & CRPBlood vessels, circulation & endothelium
AI summary & key facts

The post highlights a news report of a new study in mice and patients proposing that atherosclerosis meets the criteria for an autoimmune disease. Evidence is mostly from mouse models with supporting human correlational data, so it is early; more work is needed to learn how much of the disease this process drives and whether interrupting it could slow progression.

  • Evidence mainly from mouse models plus human correlational data
  • Unclear how much of atherosclerosis is autoimmune-driven
  • Whether blocking the process slows progression is untested

Early evidence, mostly from mouse models with supporting human correlational data, suggests an autoimmune component in atherosclerosis. Further studies are needed to establish how much of the disease is driven by this process, and whether interrupting it could meaningfully slow progression.
