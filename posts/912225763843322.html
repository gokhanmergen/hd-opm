Vitamin C may accelerate wound healing.

#vitamincHD #woundhealingHD

Shared from Ergo-Log Webmagazine · original post

Supplementation with fairly large, but by no means irresponsible, doses of vitamin C accelerates the healing of wounds. Malaysian researchers came to this conclusion in a meta-analysis, for which they collected all available trials.

Vitamin C has not reduced heart attacks or strokes and plaque data are mixed

Apr 12, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Admin summarises that vitamin C alone has been tested widely without reducing hard outcomes (MACE), and plaque-reversal data are mixed: supportive in animals, better/worse/no effect in human trials. He does not advise it as a heart-disease cure, though physiological doses appear safe; he takes 250 mg/day to avoid deficiency. Links an (largely AI-generated, author-vetted) summary document.

EVITA trial: vitamin C lowered deaths in clonal blood conditions (exploratory result)

Sep 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 8 Supplements & vitamins

In the small phase 2 EVITA randomized trial of oral vitamin C in clonal cytopenia and lower-risk myeloid cancers, the vitamin C group had 65% lower risk of death (11 vs 24 deaths) and fewer serious adverse events. Survival was exploratory and the primary endpoint of clonal growth showed no difference. The poster speculates relevance to CHIP and cardiovascular disease but calls it a big unproven leap.

Vitamin C slowed bone marrow aging in monkeys in a 3-year trial

Jul 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 2 Aging & longevity

A 3+ year trial in aged cynomolgus monkeys found oral vitamin C partly offset bone marrow aging, expanding common lymphoid progenitors and rebalancing lineage output, with about a 4-year reduction in transcriptomic age estimates. Louis notes bone marrow stem cell decline contributes to aging and heart disease.

Low blood vitamin C linked to brain changes, likely a proxy for fruit and vegetables

Jul 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 6 Brain health & dementia

A PLOS One study of 2,000+ older Japanese adults linked low blood vitamin C to less grey matter and weaker memory networks. Louis says it likely reflects overall fruit and vegetable intake.

Vitamin C blood levels follow an S-curve and plateau, with saturation near 1000 mg/day

Jun 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a pharmacokinetics explanation: blood vitamin C rises steeply from 30 to 100 mg/day, then flattens, so higher doses do not proportionally raise levels. These figures are for healthy people; infection and stress raise the saturation point.

Neutrophil vitamin C saturates near 200 mg twice daily; infection raises the need

May 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

William Wallace's shared explanation: neutrophils use vitamin C as an internal antioxidant pool to survive the respiratory burst they use to kill microbes. In healthy people neutrophil levels saturate at around 200 mg twice per day, and the dose needed to saturate them rises during infections.
