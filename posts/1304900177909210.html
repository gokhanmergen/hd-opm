He uses it to illustrate appropriate CVD treatment versus health misinformation and asks that politics be kept out.

  • LDL 53 mg/dL on rosuvastatin + ezetimibe
  • Fasting glucose 83, A1C 5.3%, TG/HDL 104/70, hsCRP 1.05 mg/L
  • CT angiography used to track plaque burden

The White House released a new memo summarizing Donald Trump’s latest health screening.

I have been commenting on these in the past, as they relate to cardiac health, and will do so again…

Like last time, the pattern is clear:

The president is listening carefully to the cardiologists who are treating him at Walter Reed.

He is on both rosuvastatin and ezetimibe and has pushed his LDL down to 53 mg/dL, a level reserved for people at high risk.

This is in spite of having “good metabolic health”, as measured by fasting glucose (83 mg/dL), A1C (5.3%), and TG/HDL ratio (104/70). And in spite of having normal inflammatory markers, like hsCRP (1.05 mg/L).

He has had CT-angiography to track his plaque burden.

He is up to date on all vaccinations.

Despite the stream of cholesterol denialism and anti-vax nonsense that has come out of his own administration, it is clear that Trump does not truly believe any of it when it comes to his own personal health.

When it comes to his own health, he is doing what his cardiologists are telling him to do.

His own medical report is proof of that.

Like the last time that I did this, I would politely ask people to please not turn this into a post about politics. He is a famous person who influences a lot of people. This is about treating CVD appropriately, and avoiding health misinformation.

Ezetimibe beyond statins: fewer events, no mortality gain in trials, registry benefits

Nov 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Evidence digest on ezetimibe. A meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (123,785 patients) found add-on lipid lowering cut cardiovascular events 19% versus statins alone but did not improve all-cause mortality. Other data show moderate statin plus ezetimibe matches high-intensity statins, and registry studies hint at lower mortality, with strong LDL-target attainment.

Atorvastatin is generally preferred over rosuvastatin in chronic kidney disease

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Statins

Louis shares an MRCP UK explainer on statins in CKD: atorvastatin needs no renal dose adjustment (under 2% renal excretion), while rosuvastatin is 30% renally excreted, needs dose reduction at low eGFR and can cause reversible proteinuria at high doses. KDIGO advises a statin with or without ezetimibe in CKD stages 3-5 not on dialysis.
