Louis argues that greater height, driven largely by higher IGF-1 during development, is linked to shorter lifespan and higher cancer risk across mammals and, after adjusting for confounders, in humans. He uses it to dispute carnivore influencers who say height signals longevity. A video clip from Optispan is shared; no study is cited.

  • Smaller dogs live longer than larger ones
  • After adjusting for socioeconomic confounders, taller people live shorter and have higher cancer risk (per Louis)
  • Height is partly driven by IGF-1 levels during development

Being tall decreases lifespan, and increases risk for cancer.

Not just in humans, but in most mammals.

Being taller is due in large part to higher levels of IGF1 during development.

Anyone that has ever owned dogs knows this. Smaller dogs live longer than bigger ones.

This is the sad reality.

Carnivore influencers like Shawn Baker who claim that being tall reflects longer lifespan don’t know what they’re talking about.

After correcting for socioeconomic factors and other confounders, people who are taller live shorter lifespans than people who are shorter, and are at higher risk for cancer.
