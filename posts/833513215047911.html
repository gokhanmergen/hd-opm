COSMOS trials: a daily multivitamin improved memory and slowed cognitive aging

Louis Nervegna · Jul 10, 2024 · ♥ 7 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBrain health & dementia
AI summary & key facts

The poster says the COSMOS trials at Harvard show a standard multivitamin slows cognitive decline, citing a Mass General Brigham release on a third COSMOS cognition study and a combined analysis. It reports consistent, statistically significant benefits for memory and global cognition versus placebo.

  • Combined analysis of three COSMOS cognition studies
  • Meta-analysis ~5,000 participants, >500 with in-person assessments over 2 years
  • Benefit for memory and global cognition vs placebo

Can a simple RDA level multivitamin slow down cognitive decline in older people as they age?

A continuing series of large well-run randomized controlled trials (COSMOS) at Harvard have shown yes.

This video discusses these trials, and compares them to other trials of various vitamins and minerals in the past which did not show a benefit.

Quoted from a recent press release from Massachusetts General and Brigham and Women’s hospitals in Boston:

“Two previously published studies of cognition in COSMOS suggested a positive effect for a daily multivitamin. COSMOS researchers now report the results of a third study of cognition in COSMOS, which focused on participants who underwent in-person assessments, together with the results of a combined analysis from the three separate studies. The results from this latest report confirm consistent and statistically significant benefits of a daily multivitamin versus placebo for both memory and global cognition. Results are published today in The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition.”
