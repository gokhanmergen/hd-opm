CBD and CBG reduced liver fat and improved blood sugar in obese mice after 4 weeks

Louis Nervegna · Sep 05, 2026 · ♥ 18 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatSupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a mouse study in which CBD and CBG reduced liver fat and improved blood glucose, with CBG also boosting insulin sensitivity, via phosphocreatine buffering rather than cannabinoid receptors. He emphasizes it is just a mouse study, so human relevance is unproven.

  • Obese mice treated 4 weeks
  • Reduced liver fat; improved blood sugar
  • CBG improved insulin sensitivity and reduced body fat
  • Mechanism: phosphocreatine buffering and lysosomal restoration

The cannabinoids CBD and CBG look like they might be promising for reducing liver fat.

This was just a mouse study, but the results were very impressive.

CBD and CBG are found naturally in hemp, and are available as dietary supplements.

A potential breakthrough for a condition affecting one in three adults with no approved treatments.
Scientists found CBD and CBG significantly reduced liver fat and improved blood sugar levels in obese mice after just four weeks. CBG boosted insulin sensitivity and reduced body fat. These compounds work by increasing phosphocreatine levels to help cells manage energy more efficiently, not through traditional cannabinoid receptors.
