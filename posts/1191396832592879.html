Limiting diabetes risk on LDL-lowering drugs: statin risk is modest, alternatives exist

Nov 26, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 17 Statins

Review explaining that statins modestly increase new-onset diabetes in a dose-dependent way, mostly in people with existing metabolic risk factors, and that cardiovascular benefit clearly outweighs this. It suggests statins mainly bring forward diagnosis in predisposed people, outlines mechanisms (beta-cell dysfunction, insulin resistance) and proposes ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors and pitavastatin as options that limit glycemic harm.

CTT meta-analysis: of 66 label-listed statin side effects, 62 showed no causal link

Feb 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Statins

Shared summary of the Cholesterol Treatment Trialists meta-analysis in The Lancet using individual data from 23 double-blind RCTs. Most side effects on statin labels were not caused by the drug, while muscle symptoms, new-onset diabetes, mild liver enzyme rises and a few minor effects were confirmed.

Small dense HDL tied to coronary calcium in insulin resistance; drugs may offset 40%

Nov 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis highlights a paper in the International Journal of Cardiology reporting that small HDL particles are associated with prediabetes, diabetes and subclinical coronary atherosclerosis. The authors suggest lipid-lowering drugs may reduce up to 40% of the coronary calcification linked to high small HDL. Louis concludes that high HDL is not always good and that people with insulin resistance considering a statin should read the paper.
