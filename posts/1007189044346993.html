KETO-CTA compared high-LDL to very-high-LDL keto dieters with no low-LDL control group

Gokhan Can · Apr 15, 2025 · ♥ 21 reactions · 20 comments · original Facebook post
Low-carb, keto & carnivorePlaque, CAC & CT angiographyLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan argues media coverage misrepresented KETO-CTA: it compared keto dieters with LDL around 200 to those around 300, with no low-LDL control, and still showed substantial new plaque, progressing like high-risk conditions. He says he would not keto as a lean mass hyper-responder and controls his own LDL with low-dose rosuvastatin.

  • One-year study of keto dieters with LDL about 200 vs about 300 mg/dL
  • No control group with healthy low LDL
  • Plaque progression reported at a rate comparable to type 2 diabetes (per post)

It's deeply concerning how this study is being portrayed by major outlets as some kind of major finding, likely due to how it was presented. This is academic fraud.

If you look at what the study actually found, in plain language:

  1. It compared keto dieters with high LDL (around 200) to those with very high LDL (around 300) and found similar negative outcomes over a year. This is like comparing people smoking 2 packs a day versus 3 packs a day, and finding no difference --- you're just comparing two high-risk groups. Crucially, there was no control group with healthy, low LDL levels (similar to non-smokers).
  1. These outwardly "healthy" keto dieters showed significant new plaque buildup (atherosclerosis), progressing at a rate similar to people with Type 2 diabetes or other known high-risk conditions.

So, far from downplaying the risk, the study clearly showed that maintaining very high LDL levels, even in the context of a ketogenic diet, is significantly harmful and seems to put these individuals on a fast track to heart disease. The disconnect between this data and the resulting headlines is shocking.

My story:

  • I keto dieted on and off for ~7 years, with some stretches as long as 2 yrs of continuous ketosis. I did not experience sky high LDL, luckily.
  • Would I keto diet again? Yes, sure -- it works really well for my health conditions. That said, I also control my LDL via low dose rosuvastatin. I also don't do carnivore. More veggie heavy, nut heavy.
  • Would I keto if I were a LMHR? No way.

The lack of integrity from these news outlets and authors is shocking.
