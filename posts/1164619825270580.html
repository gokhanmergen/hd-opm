ASPREE sub-analysis: aspirin tied to ~70% lower dementia in a platelet-genetics subgroup

Oct 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

A genetic analysis of 13,541 ASPREE participants found aspirin did not prevent dementia overall, but in the top 20% of a platelet-count genetic score dementia occurred in about 1% on aspirin versus 3.3% on placebo. Major bleeding was more than twice as common in this subgroup, so the post cautions against starting aspirin for dementia prevention.

Obicetrapib gets first approval worldwide in the EU, lowering LDL up to 40-50%

Sep 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 5 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

The European Commission approved obicetrapib, a low-dose oral CETP inhibitor, on 21 September 2026 in two versions: obicetrapib 10 mg alone and combined with ezetimibe 10 mg. Phase 3 trials reported LDL-C reductions of about 40% alone and about 50% with ezetimibe versus placebo. The poster says the drug has shown promise for both cardiovascular disease and Alzheimer's.

Lower cardiac output predicts faster brain shrinkage in APOE4 carriers

Aug 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

Researchers followed older adults for over a decade and found that in APOE4 carriers, lower cardiac output was a risk factor for faster brain atrophy. Louis notes heart and brain health are linked.
