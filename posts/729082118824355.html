Links a Medscape report on FDA approval of low-dose colchicine for a broad cardiovascular indication, with a note that the approval extends to primary prevention in people with multiple risk factors. No specific trial is described in the post.

Please note that the drug has been approved even for primary prevention with multiple risk factors.

PCSK9 inhibitor plus statin nearly halted coronary calcium progression versus statin alone

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 4 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summary of alirocumab 75 mg biweekly added to moderate statins in 61 CAD patients over 18 months: median CAC +2.9% vs +10.6%, with CAC not rising significantly from baseline. A retrospective study (Ikegami, n=120) found about half the annual calcium progression. Mechanism is thought to be stronger LDL and Lp(a) lowering.

High Lp(a) raises CVD death risk 16% per 50 mg/dL, independent of other risk factors

May 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 22 Lp(a)

Louis argues Lp(a) alone raises heart disease mortality even in people with no other risk factors. Citing a Current Opinion in Lipidology review, he notes each 50 mg/dL increase corresponds to a hazard ratio of 1.16 for CVD mortality, and works an example: Lp(a) of 250 mg/dL would imply roughly an 80% higher risk, taking a 20% lifetime risk to about 36%. The linear extrapolation is his own illustration.

WOSCOPS 20-year follow-up: 5 years of pravastatin gave lasting mortality and event benefit

Apr 07, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Statins

Describes the 20-year follow-up of the West of Scotland Coronary Prevention Study: 5 years of pravastatin in middle-aged men with high cholesterol and no prior CHD produced a lasting legacy effect, with reduced all-cause, CV and coronary mortality and fewer heart failure and MI hospitalizations. No excess cancer or non-CV mortality was seen.
