Shared from Laboratory Sciences · original post

Glucose transporters (GLUT1–GLUT14) help regulate glucose and other sugar movement across cell membranes. Each transporter has a specific tissue distribution, affinity, and physiological function. Understanding their differences is essential for learning glucose metabolism, cellular physiology, and clinical biochemistry.

Exercise activates AS160 and GLUT4 for muscle glucose uptake; Zierath wins 2024 EASD prize

Aug 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares news that Prof. Juleen Zierath won the 2024 EASD Diabetes Prize for Excellence for 30 years of work on exercise and muscle insulin signalling. Her group found exercise activates AS160, which boosts GLUT4 to move glucose into muscle and fat cells, a pathway still working when insulin's effect is impaired in type 2 diabetes. This supports exercise for lowering blood sugar.

Low-fat vegan diet improved blood sugar and liver fat without calorie restriction

A human trial covered by Lifespan.io found that a low-fat plant-based diet improved glucose control, reduced liver fat and lowered skin AGEs even without calorie restriction. The poster adds that Mediterranean, keto and low-fat vegan diets all reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity, and argues the common mechanism is loss of visceral fat.
