Excess protein is not simply “pee’d out”.

That is a blatant lie.

The extra nitrogen is removed and the excess protein is converted to carbohydrate in the liver. That carbohydrate is then stored as fat, if it exceeds the body’s caloric needs.

Too many calories from any source— including protein— causes weight gain.
