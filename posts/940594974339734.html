How to deal with bone loss: exercise, hormones, prunes, labs and bisphosphonates

Gokhan Can · Jan 02, 2025 · ♥ 8 reactions · 15 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan's checklist for osteopenia/osteoporosis: weight-bearing exercise first, discuss hormones with an endocrinologist, eat 5-10 prunes a day, have labs reviewed, and consider bisphosphonates if nothing else helps. He warns against starting calcium on your own because of cardiovascular risk.

  • Weight-bearing exercise is the top lifestyle measure
  • 5-10 prunes/day suggested
  • Do not start calcium without medical advice (heart risk)
  • Bisphosphonates considered if other measures fail

How to deal with bone loss? (Osteopenia/Osteoporosis)

  1. Weight bearing exercise is the #1 lifestyle modification to fight bone loss.
google.comGoogle Searchgoogle.com
  1. Hormones are hugely impactful. As your hormone levels drop, you lose a lot of bone (both estrogen and testosterone are important). Definitely talk to an endocrinologist about it.
  1. As for natural hacks, I'd immediately start eating 5-10 prunes/day religiously. This is the most powerful natural hack that should move things in the right direction.

↪ Prunes help maintain bone density in postmenopausal women, researcher says

  1. Next up your endocrinologist and/or PCP should take a deep look at your labs and make some recommendations. A very common recommendation is to increase calcium (+ Vit D) intake via foods and supplements. But never ever start calcium on your own. It's a risky supplement actually (too much is harmful for the heart).
  1. If nothing else helps, I believe Bisphosphonates are generally helpful and have limited downside. They're usually used after trying out other options. ↪ Bisphosphonates for bone loss: fracture reductions summarized

Bisphosphonates are broadly considered a life extension drug (similar to statins, RAS inhibition, aspirin etc).
