Respiratory Infections Can Be Especially Dangerous for People With Heart Conditions

COVID-19, flu, and RSV circulate widely during respiratory season and can increase cardiac vulnerability, particularly in those with established heart conditions.
Even mild viral infections can destabilize hemodynamics, provoke inflammatory cascades, and increase the likelihood of acute coronary syndromes or decompensated heart failure.

Prevention through vaccination, early recognition of symptoms, and adherence to prescribed therapies remains essential.
