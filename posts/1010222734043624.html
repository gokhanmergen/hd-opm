Meat-heavy, low-carb health influencers rarely reach 90, per a video survey

Louis Nervegna · Apr 19, 2025 · ♥ 13 reactions · 13 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevityLow-carb, keto & carnivoreMeat, eggs & dairy
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a video series surveying ages at death of over 100 health influencers and says those advocating diets high in red and processed meat and low in whole grains, fruit, vegetables and legumes did not live long. Richard Bernstein, who died at 90, is the only one he found who reached 90. This is an anecdotal tally, not controlled evidence.

  • Series surveys over 100 deceased health influencers
  • Richard Bernstein died this week at age 90
  • Louis found no other meat-heavy, low-carb influencer reaching 90 in 10 months of searching

For the last 10 months, I have been searching for health influencers who lived past 90yo, and recommended a diet high in red or processed meat and low in “healthy” carbs (whole grains, fruits, vegetables, legumes, etc.).

There is only one influencer that I could find: Richard Bernstein, who is mentioned in the video below.

He just died this week at age 90.

So far, I believe that he is the only health influencer in history who recommended such a diet to live to age 90, as best as I can tell.
