Natural Alternatives or Statins?

Many people are swapping statins for natural cholesterol-lowering supplements, but is it the right choice?

Ultimately, both statins and natural supplements offer unique benefits and drawbacks. If you're a cancer patient, it's crucial to weigh your choices as you have a condition that means you need an effective option. It is important to choose something (natural or pharmaceutical) that may synergise with other treatments to have the best effect. Finally more scientific studies back up the dipyridamole/statin combinations as well as the synergy between statins and metformin.
You should carefully weigh up your choices and seek medical advice from a doctor. Statins have a huge amount of evidence against cancer but a combination might be the most effective approach.
