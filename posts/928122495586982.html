Dozens of RCTs show seed oils are not inflammatory; viral tumor study involved no diet

AI summary & key facts

Louis says dozens of human RCTs show seed oils are not inflammatory and some show anti-inflammatory effects. He shares Andrea Love's explanation that a viral colorectal cancer study was a molecular screen of 40 tumor biopsies with no dietary component, and that only 0.2% of linoleic acid is converted into arachidonic acid.

  • Dozens of human RCTs: seed oils not inflammatory
  • The cancer study analyzed 40 biopsy samples and did not involve diet
  • Only 0.2% of ingested linoleic acid converts to arachidonic acid, per shared post
  • Arachidonic acid is also found in meat, liver and eggs

Dozens of randomized controlled trials in humans have proven that seed oils are not inflammatory. Some of these studies showed an anti-inflammatory effect.

(We now know that omega 6 fats inhibit NF-kB which is the body’s master generator of inflammation. This NF-kB pathway is considerably more important in generating inflammation than the arachadonic acid pathway. There is a very tight negative feedback loop that regulates levels of arachadonic acid in the human body, anyway, and dietary linoleic acid has only a minor effect on perturbing it.)

Shared from Dr. Andrea Love · original post

No, a new study DIDN’T say seed oils are causing colorectal cancer.

The study has ZERO to do with seed oils.
Or ANY dietary components.
Diet wasn’t even involved.

The study being circulated was a molecular screen of tumor biopsies that looked at patterns of gene expression & metabolism. Basically: researchers collected huge data sets from 40 biopsy samples and sifted through it.

But grifters don’t know basic cell biology, or that lipids are cell components & signaling molecules, so they see the words lipid and arachidonic acid and make WILD extrapolations.

This study was an observational assessment of a variety of molecules involved in physiological processes. They compared patterns of expression between tumor biopsies and healthy tissue biopsies.

That’s it. That’s ALL the conclusion you can make. This study is to generate HYPOTHESES about possible things that might be involved in colorectal cancer pathology - where controlled and more extensive studies can follow up on.

There was NOTHING that involved dietary intake, diet changes, or individual foods in this study.

Wellness influencers want to demonize seed oils because linoleic acid is metabolized into arachidonic acid and that’s their evil inflammation villain.

Did they miss the part where arachidonic acid is found in animal food products like meat and liver and eggs?

And that only 0.2% of linoleic acid you ingest is converted into arachidonic acid?

And that arachidonic acid is part of EVERY CELL in your body?

Yes. Yes, they did. Because they don’t understand basic biology. And they definitely didn’t read the paper.
