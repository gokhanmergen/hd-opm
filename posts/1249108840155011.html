Elaine LaLanne turns 100, a plant-heavy, active lifestyle example

Louis Nervegna · Mar 19, 2026 · ♥ 33 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevityMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Louis marks Elaine LaLanne's 100th birthday, saying diet, exercise and lifestyle can slow aging; she and her husband ate at least 10 fruits and vegetables daily, two meals per day, and mainly fish for animal protein. It is anecdotal.

  • At least 10 fruits and vegetables per day
  • Two meals per day
  • Fish the main animal protein

She turned 100 today.

It’s not a coincidence that she has lived an extraordinarily long life, just like her husband. Diet, exercise, and lifestyle factors can slow aging. She is living proof.

In addition to all the exercise, they both ate a plant heavy diet, consuming at least 10 fruits and vegetables per day, but usually a lot more. They only ate 2 meals per day, and their main source of animal protein was fish.

Dietary fiber as a longevity intervention: 15-30% lower mortality at 25-29 g/day

Dec 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 12 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Long summary arguing fiber is a bioactive longevity tool. The WHO-commissioned Reynolds 2019 meta-analysis found 15-30% lower all-cause mortality at 25-29 g/day, with benefits continuing per extra 8 g; newer NHANES data suggest diminishing returns past ~30 g/day. It describes mechanisms including butyrate-driven epigenetic effects and gut barrier reinforcement against toxins such as microplastics.

A Queen Mary University of London study in Microbial Cell found that caffeine acts via AMPK, the cellular fuel gauge, influencing growth, DNA repair and stress response. It was done in fission yeast, so human relevance is unproven.
