Ancel Keys' 1975 Mediterranean diet book: plants, olive oil, fish, little red meat

Follow-up to the longevity post, summarizing (via a ChatGPT summary by Pankaj Kapahi) the diet Ancel and Margaret Keys described in their 1975 book. It emphasizes vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, extra virgin olive oil as the main fat, fish, limited red meat and modest dairy, herbs instead of salt, fruit for dessert and wine in moderation.
