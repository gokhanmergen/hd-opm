Statins' Lasting Impact on Cardiovascular Health

The 20-year follow-up of the West of Scotland Coronary Prevention Study (WOSCOPS) demonstrates a significant, long-lasting "legacy effect" of statin therapy for the primary prevention of cardiovascular disease. Originally a 5-year trial comparing pravastatin to a placebo in middle-aged men with hypercholesterolemia but no prior coronary heart disease, the extended 20-year observational follow-up revealed that the initial 5-year treatment period led to persistent health benefits. Specifically, the pravastatin group experienced significant reductions in all-cause mortality, cardiovascular and coronary heart disease mortality, and hospitalizations for heart failure and myocardial infarction. Furthermore, the long-term data confirmed the safety of pravastatin, showing no increased risk of cancer or non-cardiovascular mortality, ultimately supporting the early use of statins to positively alter the lifetime trajectory of atherosclerotic disease.
