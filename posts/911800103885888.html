Harvard Health: anti-inflammatory foods to include and inflammatory foods to avoid

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan reposts a Harvard Health list. Include tomatoes, olive oil, leafy greens, nuts, fatty fish, berries and other fruit, and coffee; avoid refined carbohydrates, fried foods, sugary drinks, red and processed meat, and margarine, shortening and lard. He adds two daily drinks, plus green tea, hibiscus tea and coffee, to raise his Mediterranean score.

  • Include: tomatoes, olive oil, greens, nuts, fatty fish, fruit, coffee
  • Avoid: refined carbs, fried foods, soda, red and processed meat, margarine and lard

From Harvard Health

Anti-inflammatory Foods to Include:

  1. Tomatoes
  1. Olive oil
  1. Green leafy vegetables: Spinach, Kale , Collards...
  1. Nuts: Almonds, Walnuts...
  1. Fatty fish: Salmon, Mackerel, Tuna, Sardines
  1. Fruits: Strawberries, Blueberries, Cherries, Oranges
  1. Coffee (contains polyphenols and anti-inflammatory compounds)

Foods to Avoid (Inflammatory Foods):

  1. Refined carbohydrates (white bread, pastries)
  1. French fries and other fried foods
  1. Soda and sugar-sweetened beverages
  1. Red meat (burgers, steaks)
  1. Processed meat (hot dogs, sausage)
  1. Margarine, shortening, and lard
