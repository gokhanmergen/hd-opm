Coffee lovers, this one’s worth celebrating.

A recent lab study found that compounds in espresso may help prevent tau proteins, one of the hallmarks of Alzheimer’s, from clumping and damaging brain cells.

It’s early research, but it reinforces what we already know: our daily choices, even something as simple as a cup of coffee, can influence brain health at the molecular level.
