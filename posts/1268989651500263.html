Weight loss is the most effective way to lose liver fat.

(Specifically, weight loss from visceral fat.)

But there is decent evidence that making the kinds of diet and lifestyle changes suggested in this post can reduce liver fat, even in the absence of weight loss. These kinds of changes can enhance the effects of weight loss, leading to more reduction in liver fat than weight loss alone. For example, see these previous posts:

Normal liver enzymes can hide fatty liver; use FibroScan and look beyond FIB-4

Louis shares a dietitian post and note that half of people with fatty liver have normal liver enzymes, so enzymes and enzyme-based scores like FIB-4 poorly predict liver fat. He recommends FibroScan, with fatty liver diagnosed above 5% liver fat and insulin resistance linked to levels above 1.85%.

Overfeeding saturated fat raised liver fat 55%, sugar 33%, unsaturated fat 15% in an RCT

Apr 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A randomized trial overfed three groups by 1000 calories for 3 weeks from butter/dairy, olive oil/nuts or added sugar. Liver fat rose 55%, 15% and 33% respectively, showing calories are not equal for the liver.

Choline deficiency can cause fat to accumulate in the liver

Sep 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A shared post explains that choline is needed for phosphatidylcholine, which packages and exports liver fat in lipoproteins. Severely inadequate intake can cause liver fat to accumulate in controlled feeding studies and is reversible with adequate choline. Good sources include eggs, liver, meat, fish and soybeans.

High protein intake linked to MASLD in NHANES; amino acids fuel liver fat synthesis

Dec 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 7 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a Cell Metabolism study suggesting high-quality protein may drive fatty liver. In NHANES (4,693 participants), carbohydrate and total fat were not associated with MASLD, while medium/high protein intake raised MASLD risk about 1.45-fold and high protein doubled MASH risk; low carbohydrate intake was associated with about 2-fold higher MASH progression. Amino acids serve as carbon sources for liver fatty acid synthesis, and mTOR may play a role.

Gut-microbe D-lactate raises blood sugar and liver fat; an oral 'trap' helped obese mice

Jul 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Gut microbiome

Canadian researchers found D-lactate made by gut bacteria signals the liver to produce excess glucose and fat in obese mice and humans. An oral polymer trap binding it in the gut improved glucose control, insulin resistance and liver inflammation in mice without changing diet or weight.
