Over half a tablespoon of olive oil daily linked to 28% lower dementia death over 28 years

Louis Nervegna · Jul 27, 2025 · ♥ 8 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaFats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)
AI summary & key facts

Louis cites a prospective cohort of 92,383 adults in the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study. Consuming over 7 g/day of olive oil was associated with 28% lower risk of dementia-related death.

  • 92,383 adults followed 28 years
  • More than 7 g/day olive oil: 28% lower risk of dementia-related death

Consumption of more than half a tablespoon of olive oil per day is associated with 28% lower risk for dementia-related death over a 28 year period.

This data cones from the Harvard Nurses Study and Harvard Physicians Followup Study.

Quoted:

“In a prospective cohort study of 92 383 adults observed over 28 years, the consumption of more than 7 g/d [half a tablespoon] of olive oil was associated with a 28% lower risk of dementia-related death compared with never or rarely consuming olive oil, irrespective of diet quality.”

“This prospective cohort study examined data from the Nurses’ Health Study (NHS; 1990-2018) and Health Professionals Follow-Up Study (HPFS; 1990-2018). The population included women from the NHS and men from the HPFS who were free of cardiovascular disease and cancer at baseline. Data were analyzed from May 2022 to July 2023.”
