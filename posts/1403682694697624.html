Why ApoB, Lp(a), UACR and HbA1c add to a standard cholesterol panel

Louis Nervegna · Sep 25, 2026 · ♥ 2 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

A promotional explainer says ApoB counts atherogenic particles and predicts heart attack and stroke risk better than LDL, while Lp(a), UACR and HbA1c reveal risks that standard labs miss. It gives no studies or numbers beyond a claim that 20% of people carry Lp(a) risk.

  • ApoB counts bad particles; LDL estimates volume
  • Lp(a) is a genetic risk factor said to affect 20% of people and not routinely tested
  • UACR and HbA1c detect early kidney and metabolic changes

Your standard annual physical tests basic LDL and blood pressure. But did you know ApoB is a significantly more accurate predictor of future heart attack and stroke risk? 🧠

Here is why your basic cholesterol panel falls short:

  • ApoB: Counts the actual number of bad particles (LDL only estimates volume).
  • Lp(a): A genetic risk factor 20% of people carry that standard labs never check.
  • UACR & HbA1c: Detects early kidney and metabolic shifts long before standard glucose spikes.
