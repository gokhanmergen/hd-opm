Comparison of Keto-CTA (LMHR) study to Nature-CT Study

Keto diet verses standard American diet

The whole aim of the the Nature-CT study was to evaluate the progression of atherosclerosis in a healthy population, using CT-angiogram.

They had zero diabetes, and zero use of statins (or any other lipid lowering drug).

Their age was identical to the LMHR keto group in the Keto-CTA study, 55yo on average.

The Nature-CT study was conducted by Mathew Budoff at the same institute at UCLA, using the same CCTA technology and Cleerly software for analysis.

The Nature-CT study was done very recently, published November 2024.

This was a true apples-to-apples comparison.

Mathew Budoff agrees that this is a good group for comparison, and says that he is planning a paper comparing the two.

Here are the results for soft plaque progression (NCPV) after 1 year:

LMHR: 18.8 mm^3
Nature-CT: 4.9 mm^3

The LMHR group on the keto diet saw 3.8x faster soft plaque progression than the people in the Nature-CT group eating a standard American diet.
