This is the most remarkable case of extreme longevity that I have ever heard of.

The math on this is astonishing (see comments).

——

The traditional Mediterranean diet was originally popularized in the USA during the 1970’s.

This was a result of the famous “7 countries study” which first discovered that groups of people following this diet were living considerably longer with much lower rates of heart disease than in the United States.

Once learning this, the scientists working on the 7 countries study all personally switched to a traditional Mediterranean diet themselves. They were highly motivated and followed the diet religiously for the rest of their lives.

As a result, 3 out of the total 18 scientists in this research group lived to the age of 100. The most famous of these 18 was Ancel Keys who made it to a few days short of 101. His wife also followed the diet religiously and lived to 98.

The odds of 3 out of 18 people (all men born in the early 1900’s) living to 100 by pure chance is about 1 in 10 billion.

Historically, only about 1 in 10,000 people living in the USA have ever made it to age 100.

Louis argues the traditional Mediterranean diet was mostly cheap plant food (legumes, nuts, seeds, whole grains) with about 10% seafood, yogurt, cheese, poultry and eggs, and rare unprocessed red meat.
