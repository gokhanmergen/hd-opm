Video: immunoglobulin G buildup in fat tissue drives insulin resistance and aging

Feb 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Gokhan summarizes a Mike Lustgarten video on research showing IgG accumulates in fat tissue with age and obesity, impairs insulin signaling, promotes inflammation and fibrosis, and is reduced by calorie restriction. Blocking IgG recycling through the FcRn receptor in mice improved metabolic health and extended lifespan. He calls it potentially Nobel-worthy 'if it holds up'.

Obesity is strongly heritable: twin studies estimate 40-80% genetic contribution

Jan 25, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Gokhan argues that obesity is not merely a willpower issue. He cites twin-study heritability of 40-80%, BMI heritability of 47-80%, and the higher obesity rate of children with two obese parents, plus weight setpoint theory, while noting lifestyle still matters.

Boosting VEGF signaling to improve circulation extended lifespan 45% in old mice

Dec 19, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 36 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Gokhan highlights a mouse study in which restoring youthful VEGF signaling kept blood vessels healthier with age, improving metabolism, muscle, bone and inflammation and extending lifespan by 45%. He speculates about human applications such as walking and daily activity, but the finding is in mice.

Berberine modestly lowers LDL, triglycerides and ApoB: meta-analysis of 18 RCTs

Sep 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials in adults with dyslipidemia found berberine produced small reductions in LDL cholesterol, triglycerides and apoB, with possible sex-specific effects on HDL. The conclusion calls for larger trials assessing clinical outcomes.
