Bale-Doneen protocol: aggressive multi-drug risk management reduced carotid IMT

Gokhan Can · Dec 03, 2023 · ♥ 11 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Describes the Bale and Doneen approach to reversing plaque: lifestyle counseling, regular imaging and biomarker testing, statin-based lipid control with ezetimibe or niacin, SBP under 120, low-dose aspirin, insulin sensitizers and anti-inflammatories. In the 2016 community-based paper, carotid IMT measures fell markedly over a median of 3 years.

  • Median time to last CIMT: 3.0 years
  • At 2 years: 52.7% decrease in max CIMT, 3.0% decrease in mean CIMT (p<0.001)
  • Treatment included statin, ezetimibe, niacin, fibrates, omega-3, ACE inhibitor, metformin or pioglitazone, colchicine

What does an Optimal Treatment look like?

The following is the Drs. Brad Bale and Amy Doneen methodology for reversing heart disease plaque from a paper in 2016 (paper, recent video).

  • [Lifestyle] All patients received extensive behavioral, dietary and lifestyle counseling in keeping with accepted guideline practice.
  • [Regular testing] Clinicians review laboratory data, CAC and CIMT imaging. Markers like inflammation and fasting insulin are regularly tested monitored.
  • [Lipid Control] Treat patients with statin, statin plus niacin, statin plus ezetimibe, or statin plus niacin and ezetimibe. Statin-intolerant patients were treated with ezetimibe plus niacin.
  • [Omega 3 and additional metabolic drugs] Additional therapy with fibrates, bile acid sequestrants, and fish oils was also used as needed to achieve the more aggressive NCEP ATP III lipid goals.
  • [SBP < 120, ideally using an ACE Inhibitor] Patients with a history of coronary artery disease (CAD) and/or hypertension were treated with angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (ACEi), angiotensin receptor blocker, β-blocker, diuretic, renin inhibitor, and/or clopidogrel as indicated.
  • [Anti-platelet] Use low dose aspirin for secondary prevention and other high risk individuals.
  • [Diabetic drugs for obese and/or insulin resistant] Patients with newly diagnosed insulin resistance, metabolic syndrome, and diabetes mellitus were started on oral insulin sensitizing agents, primarily metformin and pioglitazone.
  • [Anti-inflammatories] Treat chronic inflammation with colchicine (already FDA approved for secondary prevention), and perhaps others (high dose omega 3, low dose pioglitazone, curcumin, 4 herb synergy...).
  • Additional supplements that might help with longevity, help avoid vitamin deficiencies etc.

Results (paper)

Median time to last carotid intima-media thickness (CIMT) was 3.0 years. Compared to baseline, follow-up analysis of all treatment groups at 2 years showed a 52.7% decrease in max CIMT, a 3.0% decrease in mean CIMT, and an 87.0% decrease in the difference between max and mean CIMT (p < 0.001). Plaque composition changes occurred, including a decrease in lipid-rich plaques of 78.4% within the first 2 years (p < 0.001). After the first 2 years, CIMT and lipid-rich plaques continued to decline at reduced rates.
