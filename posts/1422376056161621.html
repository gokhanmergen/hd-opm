STAREE trial: atorvastatin in people 70+ cut major CV events 30% without raising dementia

Louis Nervegna · Oct 15, 2026 · ♥ 10 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsBrain health & dementiaHeart attack, stroke & procedures
↻ This is a repost. The main version (highest ranked) is STAREE: atorvastatin cut major CV events 30% in adults 70+ with no rise in dementia · Nov 01, 2026.
AI summary & key facts

In the NEJM-published STAREE primary-prevention trial, 9,971 adults aged 70 or older without cardiovascular disease, diabetes or dementia received atorvastatin 40 mg or placebo for a median 5.9 years. Statin users had fewer major cardiovascular events, heart attacks and revascularizations, with no increase in dementia, mortality or serious adverse events. The post argues that claims that statins cause dementia and do not prevent heart disease are not evidence-based.

  • n = 9,971 adults aged 70+, atorvastatin 40 mg vs placebo, median follow-up 5.9 years
  • 30% lower risk of major cardiovascular events
  • 43% lower risk of MI and 43% lower risk of coronary revascularization
  • Dementia HR 1.03 (no significant increase); no significant increase in all-cause mortality
  • Serious adverse events 2.7% in both groups; investigator-initiated, no industry involvement

Statins & Dementia - The STAREE trial

Quoted:

Statins and Dementia? Well let’s have a look see in a new primary prevention trial of older adults that looked at Dementia risk in addition to cardiac risk.

The new STAREE trial, published in the New England Journal of Medicine, randomized 9,971 adults age 70+ with no known cardiovascular disease, diabetes, or dementia to atorvastatin 40 mg or placebo and followed them for a median of 5.9 years.

This was an investigator-initiated study with no commercial or industry involvement.

The results:

  • 30% lower risk of major cardiovascular events with atorvastatin
  • 43% lower risk of MI
  • 43% lower risk of coronary revascularization
  • No significant increase in dementia: HR 1.03
  • No significant increase in all-cause mortality
  • Serious adverse events: 2.7% in BOTH groups

You’re allowed to dislike statins.

You’re allowed to discuss their limitations and adverse effects.

But you don’t get to invent your own data.

And the claim that statins cause dementia and don’t prevent heart disease is simply not an evidence-based position.

📚 Reference:
Zoungas S, et al. Atorvastatin, Cardiovascular Events, and Disability-free Survival in Older Adults. N Engl J Med. 2026;395:1259-1272. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2607314.
