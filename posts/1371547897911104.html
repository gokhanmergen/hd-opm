COSMOS multivitamin matches Centrum-type RDA doses except B12 at 10x the RDA

Nov 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 8 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shows the ingredient list of the multivitamin used in the COSMOS trial next to the one used in Physicians' Health Study II. Nearly all nutrients are near RDA levels, but B12 is about 10x the RDA, and he suspects that dose may have driven the cognitive benefit seen in COSMOS. COSMOS showed slower cognitive decline in older adults, and PHS II showed an 8% cancer reduction.

Daily multivitamin slowed cognitive aging by about two years in a COSMOS meta-analysis

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a summary of COSMOS cognition results: in the main analysis of 573 adults 60+, a daily multivitamin modestly improved overall cognition and episodic memory over two years. Pooling with two earlier COSMOS cognition studies (more than 5,000 older adults) gave clear benefits, estimated at about two years of slower cognitive aging. Effects were modest, it is unclear which nutrient matters, dementia prevention was not tested, and the manufacturer donated tablets. He adds that this was an RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver).

COSMOS: daily multivitamin slowed biological aging 2.7-5.1 months over two years

Mar 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Rhonda Patrick reports that in COSMOS, older adults on a multivitamin for two years slowed biological aging by 2.7 to 5.1 months and 3.6 years slowed brain aging by 2.1 years (global) and 4.9 years (episodic).

COSMOS trial: daily multivitamin may slow biological aging markers, with editorial caveats

Mar 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

Quotes a MedPage Today report and editorial on the COSMOS trial, in which a daily multivitamin-multimineral was linked to slower epigenetic-clock aging. The editorialists (Belsky and Ryan) say the data bring the field closer to evidence that a simple multivitamin can slow aging, but uncertainties remain.
