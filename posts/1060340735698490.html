Coffee isn't poisoning you with mold toxins (it's probably protecting you instead)

Despite widespread fear of mold-produced mycotoxins (particularly ochratoxin A, or OTA) in coffee, large-scale testing consistently shows that over 95% of coffee samples fall far below safety limits

Moreover, roasting beans eliminates 70–90% of OTA, and brewing removes even more, leaving levels too low to pose real risk

In fact, regular coffee consumption correlates strongly with lower rates of liver and kidney disease (this is the exact opposite of what you'd expect if mold toxins were harming you)
