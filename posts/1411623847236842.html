Urolithin A improved heart function up to 80% in mouse models of stiff-heart failure

Sep 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 13 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A newly published mouse study strengthens the preclinical case that urolithin A, a compound made from pomegranates, walnuts and some berries, helps stiff heart tissue relax in a hard-to-treat form of heart failure. It improved heart function measures by up to 80% in animal models; human data are lacking.

Urolithin A, a pomegranate gut metabolite, improved HFpEF in mice and human heart tissue

Aug 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 9 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A Science Advances study from King's College London found that urolithin A activates the heart-protective protein PKGIα at cysteine 42, improving heart relaxation, fibrosis and exercise activity in mice with HFpEF and relaxing engineered human heart tissue. About half of people produce urolithin A from pomegranate; human trials are lacking, so benefit for patients is unproven.

Urolithin A looks promising for heart failure in animals but is untested in humans
