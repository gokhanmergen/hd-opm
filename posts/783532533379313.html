Aging stem cells open a direct platelet pathway that raises thrombosis risk (Cell 2024)

Shares the abstract of a Cell paper showing that with age, hematopoietic stem cells increasingly make platelets through a direct route that bypasses the usual progenitor cascade. The platelets from this pathway are more prone to thrombosis and cause higher platelet counts. It offers a possible cellular explanation for why clotting-related cardiovascular disease rises so steeply in older people.
