Commentary on a public figure's CT angiogram and 325 mg daily aspirin

Louis comments on news that a public figure had a CT angiogram, not an MRI, and takes full-dose aspirin, suggesting the aspirin explains hand bruising. This is mostly news about an individual.

  • Report says 325 mg aspirin daily

Looks like he actually underwent a CT-angiogram at Walter Reed, not an MRI.

They were screening his coronary arteries for possible blockages.

He also states in this same interview that he is continuing to take daily aspirin to thin his blood, and that his main physical exercise is golf.

Per this particular report, he is taking a FULL dose of 325mg aspirin: Trump tells WSJ he is taking more aspirin than doctors recommend (ksl.com)

My note:
That high 325mg dose of aspirin does seem to be a reasonable explanation for the bruising on his hands. Aspirin is standard of care for anyone at high risk of DVT from chronic veinous insufficiency, as Trump seems to be. But usually an 81mg baby dose is recommended. Not a full dose, especially in older people. The risk of bleeding from aspirin in older people is much higher than in younger people. They are also probably encouraging him to walk daily for the CVI, elevate his legs whenever possible, and wear compression stockings. I notice that he often uses a cart when playing golf, which they are probably discouraging him from doing for both his CVI and his heart health.
