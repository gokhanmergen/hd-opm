💊 How does amlodipine lower blood pressure?

Amlodipine blocks L-type calcium channels mainly in vascular smooth muscle.

Less calcium enters the cells → the smooth muscle relaxes → arteries widen → vascular resistance falls → blood pressure decreases.

In simple terms:

Amlodipine helps the arteries relax.

That is why understanding the mechanism makes pharmacology much easier than memorizing drug names alone.
