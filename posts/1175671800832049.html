Shared from MedPage Today · original post

Alzheimer's patients treated with the GLP-1 receptor agonist semaglutide (Rybelsus) in two phase III trials showed no significant improvement in cognitive or functional decline over 2 years compared with placebo, according to new data presented at the Clinical Trials on Alzheimer's Disease annual meeting.
