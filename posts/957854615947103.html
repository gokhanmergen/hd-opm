Unhealthy Diet Linked to Faster Biological Aging in Young Adults

A new study found that consuming a diet high in fast food, processed meat, and sugary drinks accelerates biological aging, even in young adults.

Researchers used epigenetic clocks to measure biological aging, revealing that those with unhealthy diets aged faster than their chronological age.

In contrast, diets rich in fruits and vegetables were linked to slower aging.
