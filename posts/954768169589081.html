Four long-term RCTs show the Mediterranean diet cuts CVD death by 46% in aggregate

AI summary & key facts

Louis argues the traditional Mediterranean diet has the strongest evidence of any diet: four randomized trials of 4-7 years (Lyon Heart Study, Indo-Mediterranean Diet Heart Study, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV) showed lower heart attack, stroke and CVD mortality. Prospective cohorts of up to 40 years, including the Harvard Nurses and Health Professionals studies, confirm this over the long run. Other diets may work better, but none has comparable evidence.

  • Four RCTs of 4-7 years: Lyon, Indo-Mediterranean, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV
  • In aggregate, CVD death reduced by 46% in these trials
  • Cohorts tracked people up to 40 years; over a dozen more cohorts of 10-30 years agree
  • Responds to a claim that no long-term research exists on any diet

Four large randomized controlled trials running 4-7 years have proven that the traditional Mediterranean diet lowers risk of heart attack, stroke, and cardiovascular mortality.

In aggregate, the risk of dying from CVD is reduced by 46% in these trials.

  • Lyon Heart Study
  • Indo-Mediterranean Diet Heart Study
  • PREDIMED
  • CORDIOPREV

↪ Meta-analysis of 4 Mediterranean diet RCTs: MACE down 48%, CVD death down 46%

Prospective cohort studies tracking groups of people for up to 40 years of their lives have confirmed that this holds true in the long run.

This includes the famous:

  • Harvard Nurses Study (HNS)
  • Harvard Physicians Followup Study (HPFS)

Over a dozen more large prospective cohort studies, tracking people for 10-30 years, have also confirmed this longer term.

These studies are at the very top of the evidence hierarchy in medicine. That is why this diet is now recommended by the vast majority of cardiologists.

↪ Check where a study sits on the evidence hierarchy before citing it

Are there other diets that might work better? Maybe… but all of that is mostly just guesses and opinions. No other diet has anywhere close to this level of scientific evidence supporting it.

Olive oil has the strongest trial evidence, but canola and soybean oil also have support

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.
