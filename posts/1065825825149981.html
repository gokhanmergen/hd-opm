AI summary & key facts

Louis quotes an interview claim that running often matches or surpasses SSRIs for depression, especially in people with high inflammation, and notes depression is a CVD risk factor. These are secondhand claims from a podcast without named studies.

  • Claim: running matches or beats SSRIs, especially with high CRP
  • Depression is a CVD risk factor

Running may treat depression more effectively than SSRI’s.

This is important because depression is a major risk factor for CVD.

Quoted:

Running may treat depression as effectively (or better) than common antidepressant medications

Studies directly comparing running to SSRIs show that running often matches or surpasses medication effectiveness, especially for those with high inflammation (measured by biomarkers like C-reactive protein), who typically respond poorly to antidepressants

Not only is running an effective and completely free alternative, but its "side effects" are universally positive: reduced Alzheimer’s disease risk, reduced cardiovascular disease risk, and a longer lifespan
