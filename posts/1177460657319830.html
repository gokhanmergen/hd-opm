Urolithin A is one of the reasons why pomegranates, walnuts, and berries are good for you.

It is a mild mitochondrial toxin, like statins and metformin. It creates hormetic stress in mitochondria through a process called mitohormesis:

This hormetic stress turns on defense pathways in the mitochondria that improve the health of the cell.

Shared from Dale Bredesen, MD · original post

Urolithins, particularly urolithin A, are compounds that have a growing body of research supporting their role in healthy aging. Now, they've been found to "de-age" our immune systems, enhancing mitochondria in specific T-lymphocytes. These healthy compounds are created in your gut from polyphenols found in pomegranates, walnuts, and some berries, and are thus postbiotics. Similarly, prevention of immunosenescence occurs with Himalayan tartary buckwheat, also due to polyphenols. Scientists Found a Compound That Can De-Age Our Immune Systems (Popular Mechanics)
