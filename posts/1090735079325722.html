📊 JAMA Data Brief: Although nearly half of US adults have #hypertension, a recent health survey from The Annenberg Public Policy Center of the University of Pennsylvania shows only 13% can correctly identify what blood pressure reading clinicians consider high, one of many knowledge gaps related to the condition.

“If you don’t know what it means to have high blood pressure or why it’s a problem or how you can prevent it, you might not realize that you have a problem that you could correct,” Kathleen Hall Jamieson, PhD, the center’s director, told JAMA Medical News.
