Keto-CTA: high LDL with good metabolic health still saw 3.8x faster plaque progression

Louis Nervegna · Aug 21, 2025 · ♥ 4 reactions · 9 comments · original Facebook post
Low-carb, keto & carnivorePlaque, CAC & CT angiographyLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Louis says Keto-CTA tested whether good metabolic health protects against very high ApoB and LDL (average LDL 254 mg/dL) and found no protection. A copied LMHR-group chart also shows the DISCO study (statins, other drugs and a DASH diet) achieved significant plaque reversal in one year.

  • Average LDL 254 mg/dL in Keto-CTA participants
  • Plaque progression 3.8x worse than Nature-CT
  • DISCO intervention group showed significant plaque reversal after one year

The Keto-CTA (LMHR) study directly tested the hypothesis that good metabolic health protects against very high ApoB and very high LDL.

It failed to show any protection.

Lean metabolically healthy people with very low TG/HDL saw rapid plaque progression in this study. These people had an average LDL of 254 mg/dL.

Their plaque progression was 3.8x worse than a comparable group of normal people eating the standard American diet with considerably worse metabolic health, but with normal LDL (the Nature-CT study).

Both groups were tested using the same CT-angiogram scanners and the same Cleerly analysis software. The average age was the same in both studies (55yo). The principle investigator on both trials was the same (Mathew Budoff). This was an apples to apples comparison.

I copied this post from the LMHR group.

It is referring to the Keto-CTA study, which tested the effects of a keto diet on arterial plaque in a cohort of lean metabolically-healthy individuals with very high LDL and low TG/HDL (LMHR).

It compares plaque progression (NCPV) in the Keto-CTA study to multiple other studies.

The best results came from the intervention group in the DISCO study. This group showed significant plaque REVERSAL after one year, using statins, other medications, and a DASH diet very similar to the traditional mediterranean diet. See graph in photo.

The Keto-CTA group saw significant plaque progression.

Here are the studies being compared in the graph in the photo:

  • NATURE-CT: Healthy individuals, no special diet (age: 54.9 ‡ 10.2 yrs, LDL-C: 111.6 mg/dL)
  • KETO-CTA: Lean Mass Hyper-Responders on ketogenic diet (age: 55.5 yrs, LDL-C: 237 mg/dL) - lean, no diabetes, no high BP, no CVD risk factors except high LDL
  • Nakanishi: Non-diabetic vs. diabetic patients, standard diets (age: not reported, LDL-C: not reported)
  • SMARTool: Multi-center project, clinical characteristics and statin use impact (age: not reported, LDL-C: 110 ‡ 42 mg/dL)
  • DISCO-CT Control: Advanced heart disease, standard medication (age: 60 ‡ 7.7 yrs, LDL-C: not reported)
  • DISCO-CT Intervention: Advanced heart disease, standard medication + strict DASH diet (age: 60 ‡ 7.7 yrs, LDL-C: not reported)
