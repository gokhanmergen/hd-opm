Diet soda is a better choice than regular soda, and aspartame fear is overblown

Louis Nervegna · Jul 29, 2025 · ♥ 12 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

Louis endorses Layne Norton's argument on aspartame metabolism and says diet soda beats regular soda, noting decades of prospective tracking in Harvard cohorts, while preferring neither.

  • Aspartame is metabolised to aspartic acid, phenylalanine and methanol; steak has far more of the amino acids

The basic biology of what he is saying here is correct.

There are certainly better choices for beverages than diet soda, but the amount of fear mongering on this topic is ridiculous.

If you have to choose between the two, diet soda is a much better choice than regular soda.

(Ideally you would choose neither.)

They have prospectively tracked diet soda consumption for over 40 years in both the Harvard Nurses Study and Harvard Physicians Followup Study. After all this time, they have repeatedly been unable to associate diet soda or aspartame with weight gain or with any disease whatsoever. Multiple other large prospective cohort studies have confirmed this.

Regular soda consumption, on the other hand, is consistently associated with both weight gain and risk for multiple major diseases in large prospective cohort studies:

↪ Walter Willett: refined sugar and starch, especially sugary drinks, threaten heart health

Equating the proven harms of regular soda consumption with alleged harms of diet soda is a big mistake.

Don’t Hate Me, Hate the Data 🤷🏼‍♂️

When people get butt hurt about aspartame claiming it will give you cancer, I always ask them how? Via what mechanism? Aspartame is metabolized into aspartic acid, phenylalanine, & methanol. Aspartic acid & phenylalanine are both amino acids & you get 20-30x more of each in a steak. So I’m gonna assume no one is going to claim those are carcinogenic. So it’s got to be the methanol right? Well our body has systems to eliminate methanol. It’s not a forever chemical, it does not accumulate. And did I mention you get 15x more methanol in a glass of tomato juice?

Further, the upper limit of methanol intake is 100mg/kg. So for someone my size that is 9300mg of methanol or 9.3g. A 12 oz can of diet soda produces about 20mg of methanol. So you’d need to consume approximately 465 cans at once to reach the limit. You would die from electrolyte dilution after a 10th or 20th of that intake LOL.

Methanol is eliminated from the body at approximately 8.5mg/hour. So even if you had a diet soda every hour you are awake (let’s say 16 diet sodas), so you can eliminate 204mg of methanol per day on average. So unless you are drinking more than 10 diet soda’s every single day, you don’t need to worry about methanol exposure.
