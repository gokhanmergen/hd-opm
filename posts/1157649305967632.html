Even a cure for heart disease would show only 2-5% absolute risk reduction short-term

Louis Nervegna · Nov 10, 2025 · ♥ 15 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsHow to read research
AI summary & key facts

Louis explains that over a few years the absolute risk of a heart attack is only about 2-5%, so no intervention, even a complete cure, can cut absolute risk by more than that in a short trial. A 1-2% absolute reduction from statins or from diet and lifestyle trials is therefore a meaningful result.

  • Absolute heart attack risk is ~2-5% over a few years, even in higher-risk people
  • Best possible absolute risk reduction in such a trial is therefore 2-5%
  • 1-2% reductions are typical of both statin and diet/lifestyle trials

Even a complete CURE for heart disease will only reduce absolute risk by 2-5% in a trial that lasts just a few years.

Why?

Because the absolute risk of having a heart attack itself is only 2-5% during that time frame, even for people at higher risk. You cannot reduce absolute risk to less than zero!

Don’t be fooled by the quacks and charlatans claiming that absolute risk risk reductions of 1-2% are meaningless in trials lasting only a few years.

This is exactly what you see from statins, and it’s also what you see from the best trials on diet and lifestyle. The best you could possibly get from a full CURE is just 2-5% absolute risk reduction in a trial lasting only a few years. Getting 1-2% reduction in that time frame is pretty darn good.
