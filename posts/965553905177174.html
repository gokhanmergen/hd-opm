Low-carb diets lose their diabetes advantage by 12 months, trials and meta-analyses show

Nov 25, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 8 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Argues low-carb gives better remission and HbA1c at 6 months, but the edge fades. A 2021 BMJ meta-analysis (23 RCTs) found benefit at 6 months diminishing by 12; Silverii 2020 (37 RCTs, 3,301 patients) found HbA1c differences vanishing and reversing by 24 months; a 2-year calorie-matched RCT showed identical weight loss. Concludes the benefit comes from adherence, not metabolic magic.

Cheese umbrella review: modestly lower mortality, CVD, stroke and diabetes risk

Aug 06, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Meat, eggs & dairy

An umbrella review of 54 meta-analyses found cheese consumption associated with lower risk of all-cause mortality, cardiovascular outcomes, type 2 diabetes, fractures and dementia, with moderate evidence quality. Gokhan flags this as interesting given the group's usual caution on saturated fat.

MeMeMe trial: metformin cut diabetes 80-92% in metabolic syndrome; Med diet did not

Aug 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

In 1,442 people with metabolic syndrome followed about 3 years, 1,700 mg/day metformin prevented type 2 diabetes, which fully explained the difference in major noncommunicable diseases. The Mediterranean diet intervention did not. Gokhan notes no heart disease or cancer prevention from metformin either.
