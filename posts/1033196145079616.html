Pet cat lifespan has doubled in 45 years

AI summary & key facts

A shared post says average pet cat lifespan rose from 7-9 years to well into the teens thanks to vet care and indoor living. It is not about human heart health.

A remarkable 2x extension of average lifespan.

#agingHD #longevityHD #catsHD

Shared from Greatest Reactions · original post

In the last 45 years, the average lifespan of pet cats has doubled—rising from just 7 to 9 years to well into the teens, with some living into their twenties.

This jump is thanks to better vet care, improved nutrition, and more informed pet owners. Routine check-ups, vaccines, and the safety of indoor living have all played a part in helping our feline companions stick around longer as true members of the family.

Dietary fiber as a longevity intervention: 15-30% lower mortality at 25-29 g/day

Dec 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 12 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Long summary arguing fiber is a bioactive longevity tool. The WHO-commissioned Reynolds 2019 meta-analysis found 15-30% lower all-cause mortality at 25-29 g/day, with benefits continuing per extra 8 g; newer NHANES data suggest diminishing returns past ~30 g/day. It describes mechanisms including butyrate-driven epigenetic effects and gut barrier reinforcement against toxins such as microplastics.
