SIX older randomized trials have shown reversal of total plaque (TAV) with statins
(If we count very recent trials, it is even more. For example, the FLOWPROMOTE trial: facebook.com/share/v/1My2cZxnm7.)

The reversals in total plaque have ranged anywhere from 5% to 17%.

I listed each of the trials by name below, and the amount of the reversal:

COSMOS ~5%

SATURN ~5.5%

ASTEROID ~6.5%

IBIS-4 ~7.5%

STABLE ~8%

JAPAN-ACS ~17%

(FLOWPROMOTE ~14%)

The reversals were proven with either CT-angiogram or IVUS (the gold standard).

Note that these reversals in total plaque were a result of reversing soft plaque, not calcified plaque.

The reality is that plaque reversal with statins is fairly common in the first few years after starting. You can read the many past posts about this by searching the group for the tag #statinreverseHD.
