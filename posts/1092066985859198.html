Weighted vest during weight loss halved regain at 18 months in a pilot study

Gokhan Can · Aug 17, 2025 · ♥ 6 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatExercise & fitness
AI summary & key facts

A pilot study tested whether a weighted vest helps maintain weight loss. Both groups lost about 10-11 kg, but resting metabolic rate fell 238 kcal/day without the vest versus 16 with it, and regain was far smaller with the vest. Gokhan notes his own vest use did not produce much weight loss.

  • Weight loss about 10-11 kg in both groups over 6 months
  • RMR drop: -238 kcal/day (diet only) vs -16 kcal/day (diet plus vest)
  • 18 months after: diet-only group regained all weight; vest group stayed 4.8 kg below baseline

I wore a weight vest & ankle weights for ~1.5 yrs everyday during 1-2 hrs of walking. Can't say I lost a ton of weight at the end of this period!

A recent pilot study explored whether wearing a weighted vest could improve long-term weight loss maintenance based on the "gravitostat" hypothesis, which suggests the body defends against weight loss by sensing a reduction in gravitational load. Over a six-month diet, both a weight-loss only group (WL) and a weight-loss plus weighted vest group (WL+WV) lost similar amounts of weight (~10-11 kg). However, the WL group's resting metabolic rate (RMR) dropped by 238 calories/day, while the WL+WV group's RMR decreased by only 16 calories/day. Eighteen months after the intervention ended, the WL group had regained all their lost weight, whereas the WL+WV group had regained only about half, remaining 4.8 kg below their starting weight. Despite these results, the article deems the study inconclusive due to methodological shortcomings, such as a high follow-up dropout rate and the lack of monitoring for diet or activity.
