Magnesium supplementation has lowered blood pressure in randomized controlled trials

An analysis of 34 trials involving over 2,000 participants found modest reductions in both systolic and diastolic pressure, with a median dose of 368 mg/day

The mechanism: magnesium helps blood vessels relax and widen, reducing the resistance the heart pumps against

Magnesium-rich foods are another practical starting point, including dark leafy greens, nuts, seeds, and legumes
