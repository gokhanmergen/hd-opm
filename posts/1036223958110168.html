Looking healthy on keto or carnivore does not rule out silent plaque, citing KETO-CTA

Gokhan Can · May 29, 2025 · ♥ 20 reactions · 30 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan recalls group members with very early heart attacks after years of keto or carnivore eating and cites KETO-CTA in lean mass hyper-responders. His advice is to follow LDL and ApoB rather than how you look or feel.

  • Anecdote: 95% blockage of the widowmaker after 5 years carnivore plus 5 years keto
  • Anecdote: widowmaker heart attack at 35

I have a soft spot for people who look amazing, and claim to feel amazing.

We unfortunately ended up with stories of people who had very early heart attacks while looking and feeling amazing.

He followed the carnivore diet for 5 years. And 5 years keto before that. The end result: 95% blockage of his widowmaker.
↪ Five years of carnivore and five of keto ended in 95% left main-branch blockage

Chiropractor had a massive widow-maker heart attack at only 35yo.
↪ Chiropractor had a widow-maker heart attack at 35 with CAC 0 and LDL 372 on carnivore/keto

Then the Keto-CTA study came along, and proved to any unbiased observer that these people who look amazing from outside, are often getting sicker inside (LMHR profile).

What can you do? Trust the science at this point. Follow the well established markers like LDL, ApoB. Don't play games with your health, that is, if the markers are moving in the wrong direction.
