Natto: spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase in one food, with mortality and plaque data

Gokhan Can · Dec 09, 2023 · ♥ 25 reactions · 12 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsPlaque, CAC & CT angiographySupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Summarizes natto as a food rich in spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase. It cites a Frontiers clinical study where high-dose nattokinase slowed carotid plaque progression, an association with lower all-cause mortality, and mouse lifespan data for spermidine.

  • Natto consumption associated with 18% lower all-cause mortality (per the post)
  • A 50 g serving has about 5-13 mg spermidine, 1-2k FU nattokinase and 200-1200 mcg vitamin K2
  • Nattokinase above 8k FU/day may reverse coronary plaque progression
  • Spermidine increased mouse lifespan by about 10%

Natto (Superfood)

  • Regular & high dose consumption of Natto is associated with] 18% reduction in all cause mortality — a spermidine, vit K2 and nattokinase rich food (ref).
  • A single serving of Natto (50g) has about 5-13mg Spermidine, 1-2k FU Nattokinase, 200 - 1200 mcg of vitamin K2.
  • High dose nattokinase (> 8k FU / day) may reverse the progression of Coronary Artery Plaque (ref).
  • Regular spermidine consumption increased mice lifespan by +10% (ref).
  • Autophagy is required for the anti-aging effect of spermidine – inhibition of autophagy abolishes the longevity-extending effects of spermidine on yeast, worms and flies.
  • You can find Natto in japanese food stores in single servings or ferment it yourself from soybeans.

Spermidine linked to lower mortality and cognitive decline, and longer lifespan in animals
