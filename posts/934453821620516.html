Vitamin D 2000 IU cut autoimmune disease risk 40% in the VITAL trial, MS a key target

Nov 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis resurfaces a three-year-old post: the strongest evidence for higher-dose vitamin D is in autoimmune disease, especially MS, and he notes a new JAMA publication on vitamin D treating MS. The VITAL trial of about 25,000 people at Harvard found that 2000 IU vitamin D daily for over 2 years cut autoimmune disease risk by about 40%. CNN coverage of the same trial reports about 30% lower risk with vitamin D and fish oil supplements.

Autoimmune and inflammatory diseases linked to elevated Lp(a) in ~50,000 patients

A JACC Advances study of nearly 50,000 patients found people with gout, ankylosing spondylitis and IBD are more likely to have elevated Lp(a), with rates approaching those in established heart disease. Louis notes this fits Lp(a) being highly responsive to acute-phase inflammation.
