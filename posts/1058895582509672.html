Buck Institute: neurons burn sugar differently, hinting how calorie restriction helps

Louis Nervegna · Jul 01, 2025 · ♥ 18 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares work from group member Pankaj Kapahi's lab at the Buck Institute, published in Nature Metabolism, describing a new mechanism by which calorie restriction protects the brain from Alzheimer's-related damage. It may help explain why GLP-1 drugs show promise against dementia.

  • Mechanism relates to how neurons metabolise sugar (glycogen)
  • Published in Nature Metabolism
  • Possible link to GLP-1 drugs and dementia

This work was done by one of our group members Pankaj Kapahi.

His research group has discovered a new mechanism by which calorie restriction protects the brain from damage that leads to Alzheimer’s and dementia.

Glycation and AGEs in cardiovascular aging: Kapahi on methylglyoxal and the GLYLO suppleme

Dec 18, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis says he underestimated glycation and AGEs in aging after Pankaj Kapahi's work at the Buck Institute. Kapahi's post explains methylglyoxal as a reactive glycolysis byproduct that drives AGEs and aging, and describes GLYLO, a combination of lipoic acid, nicotinamide, piperine, pyridoxine and thiamine that lowered methylglyoxal and AGEs and extended mouse lifespan even when started late. Louis says Kapahi plans a paper showing it reduces arterial stiffening. Evidence is preclinical and the product is commercial.
