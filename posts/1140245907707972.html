Nature: tools to edit mitochondrial DNA are emerging, relevant to disease and aging

Louis Nervegna · Oct 19, 2025 · ♥ 13 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a 2025 Nature article noting that CRISPR cannot easily reach mitochondria but newer techniques are beginning to edit the mitochondrial genome. He notes faulty mitochondria contribute to many diseases and to aging itself.

  • CRISPR does not easily edit mitochondrial DNA; newer precise methods have had recent success
  • Faulty mitochondria contribute to multiple diseases and aging (Louis)

This is exciting. We may soon be able to edit DNA in mitochondria.

Faulty mitochondria contribute to the cause of multiple different diseases, and to the aging process itself.

So far, editing DNA in mitochondria has been impossible.

CRISPR gene editing has made its way into every corner of modern biology, but there’s one place CRISPR can’t easily reach: mitochondria. Researchers have been looking for other ways to precisely edit the mitochondrial genome and the past few years have brought some success.
