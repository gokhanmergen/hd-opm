Coronary CT angiography modestly improved lifestyle and therapy uptake vs risk scoring

Louis Nervegna · Jun 22, 2025 · ♥ 16 reactions · 12 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyRisk assessment & lab tests
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a JAMA finding that, compared with cardiovascular risk scoring, coronary CT angiography was associated with modest improvements in healthier lifestyle behaviors, acceptance of recommended preventive therapy and risk factor modification. His comment is that higher-risk people become more motivated once they see their scan results.

  • CCTA vs risk scoring: modest improvements in lifestyle behaviors
  • Greater acceptance of recommended preventive therapy
  • Modest improvement in risk factor modification

Higher risk people are more motivated to change their diet and lifestyle and to take appropriate medication, after they know the results of a CT-angiogram scan.

REACT: silent atherosclerosis found in 57% of 16,808 adults, 7-9% of 18-29-year-olds

Sep 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The NEJM REACT study used carotid and femoral ultrasound plus coronary CT angiography in 16,808 adults aged 18-70 without known disease. Plaque was present in 57% overall, appeared in young adults, and a zero calcium score often missed early non-calcified plaque. Ten-year risk scores detected very little silent disease, so keeping risk factors favorable throughout life is the priority.

DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed ~40 mm3 of soft plaque in one year

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains the DISCO-CT randomized trial, where a DASH diet plus statins produced the largest soft (non-calcified) plaque regression of any dietary trial on CT angiography. Adjusted to one year it was -39.5 mm3, versus an average at-risk person growing about 5 mm3 per year, so roughly 8 years of progression reversed. He notes the DASH diet was about 30% fat, mostly unsaturated, and adds caveats about interpretation.

Six statin trials show 5 to 17% total plaque reversal, with the best results at lowest LDL

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 ↩ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a chart of randomized trials showing plaque reversal with statins, ezetimibe and PCSK9 inhibitors, with total atheroma volume change plotted against LDL-C. Total plaque reversal ranged from about 5% to 17% in six older statin trials, measured by CT angiography or IVUS, and came from soft rather than calcified plaque. The chart suggests total plaque reversal begins once LDL falls below roughly 90 mg/dL.
