Psyllium lowers LDL 13 mg/dL and fasting glucose 37 mg/dL in type 2 diabetes

Louis Nervegna · Jun 15, 2026 · ♥ 14 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grainsLDL, ApoB & lipid panelInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

Louis quotes a post summarizing meta-analyses of psyllium: it lowers LDL and ApoB and, in type 2 diabetes, HbA1c and fasting glucose, with a larger effect when baseline glucose is higher. The quoted author also reports his own HbA1c falling from 5.9 to 5.3 in 8 months (anecdote).

  • LDL -13 mg/dL and ApoB -5 mg/dL (28 RCTs, Jovanovski 2018, AJCN)
  • HbA1c -0.97% in type 2 diabetes (35 RCTs, Gibb 2015, AJCN)
  • Fasting glucose -37 mg/dL in type 2 diabetes
  • Effect scales with baseline glucose; little effect if blood sugar is normal

The higher your blood sugar, the more psyllium fiber reduces it.

In type 2 diabetics, psyllium reduces fasting glucose by a whopping 37 mg/dL.

Quoted:

Psyllium husk is doing two jobs at once:

📉 LDL: −13 mg/dL (28 RCTs, Jovanovski 2018 AJCN)
📉 ApoB: −5 mg/dL (same study)
📉 HbA1c: −0.97% in T2DM (35 RCTs, Gibb 2015 AJCN)
📉 Fasting glucose: −37 mg/dL in T2DM

Effect scales with baseline. If your blood sugar is fine, it won't drop it further. If your HbA1c is creeping up (5.7-6.2% range), psyllium before meals is quietly one of the most evidence-backed low-cost tools available.

My HbA1c went from 5.9 (prediabetic) to 5.3 in 8 months. Psyllium was part of the reason.

Psyllium lowers LDL about 13 mg/dL across 28 trials by trapping bile acids

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 4 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared explainer says psyllium lowers LDL by about 13 mg/dL across 28 randomized trials, and that the mechanism is mechanical: its viscous gel traps bile acids so the liver pulls cholesterol from the blood to make more, upregulating LDL receptors. It does not absorb cholesterol directly or scrub the gut, which is why most other soluble fibers do not work the same way.

Only gel-forming fibers such as psyllium and beta-glucan lower LDL

Nov 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 3 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares a video explaining that fibers lower LDL by forming a gel that traps bile acids, forcing the liver to pull LDL from blood. Psyllium, oat and barley beta-glucan and guar gum gel, while inulin, FOS and wheat bran do not. Psyllium lowered LDL about 13 mg/dL across 28 trials and about 9 mg/dL in a 2025 analysis of 41 trials.
