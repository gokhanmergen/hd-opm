Two Harvard studies found no extra birth-defect risk with metformin in men or women

Louis Nervegna · Aug 24, 2024 · ♥ 7 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesHormones, women's & men's health
AI summary & key facts

Two Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health studies reported that infants born to men or women taking metformin did not appear to be at higher risk of birth defects. The post is a brief share of the news coverage.

  • Two studies led by Harvard Chan School
  • No increased birth defect risk with metformin taken by men or by women trying to conceive

Two recent studies from the Harvard School of Public Health have both shown that metformin does not increase the risk of birth defects, when taken by either men or women trying to conceive.

Metformin's glucose lowering is regulated by Rap1 in brain neurons, mouse study finds

Aug 03, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A Baylor College of Medicine study in Science Advances found metformin acts largely through the protein Rap1 in SF1 neurons of the hypothalamus. Mice lacking Rap1 in the brain did not respond, and a tiny dose given into the brain lowered glucose. This is preclinical mouse research.

MeMeMe trial: metformin cut diabetes 80-92% in metabolic syndrome; Med diet did not

Aug 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

In 1,442 people with metabolic syndrome followed about 3 years, 1,700 mg/day metformin prevented type 2 diabetes, which fully explained the difference in major noncommunicable diseases. The Mediterranean diet intervention did not. Gokhan notes no heart disease or cancer prevention from metformin either.

One zoledronate infusion cut vertebral fractures about 44% over 10 years in women 50-60

Jan 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 9 Hormones, women's & men's health

Gokhan shares the NEJM 10-year randomized trial of infrequent zoledronate in early postmenopausal women (50-60 years) with normal-to-low-normal bone density. Zoledronate roughly halved new morphometric vertebral fractures versus placebo, and he calls it a longevity-type drug like statins or aspirin. The trial concerns bone rather than heart outcomes.

GLP-1 drugs vs metformin in obese prediabetes: 31% less diabetes, 41% lower mortality

Nov 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares a large observational AHA 2026 study of 256,492 adults with prediabetes and obesity: GLP-1 users had 31% lower risk of type 2 diabetes and 41% lower mortality than metformin users. They also had fewer strokes, heart attacks and heart failure cases. The data are unpublished, observational and not yet peer reviewed, and Louis ties the results to ectopic fat and calorie restriction.

Metformin linked to less dementia in 20-year Diabetes Prevention Program follow-up

Sep 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 6 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A shared report on 1,483 people from the Diabetes Prevention Program followed for over 20 years found dementia in 1.9% of those randomized to metformin, versus 4.2% with intensive lifestyle and 3.5% with placebo. The mechanism is unknown and the results do not establish metformin as a dementia treatment.

Metformin associated with over 50% lower dementia risk in 20-year DPP/DPPOS follow-up

Aug 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 5 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a New Scientist report on observational analysis of the Diabetes Prevention Program randomized trial and its follow-up, suggesting metformin may cut dementia risk by more than half. It aligns with monkey data on slower brain aging. The finding is not conclusive and the mechanism is unclear.
