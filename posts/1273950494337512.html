Group warning about influencer-promoted treatments with no evidence for CVD

Louis Nervegna · Apr 22, 2026 · ♥ 34 reactions · 24 comments · original Facebook post
Myths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

Louis warns against relying on unproven influencer protocols for heart disease; an opinion post with no cited studies.

If you participate in these heart disease groups long enough, you will see this same sad story repeated over and over again.

Aside from the exercise, there is no credible evidence that any of this stuff effectively treats CVD. It is mostly just guesses. All the treatments that do have real evidence are conspicuously missing.

Is it possible that some of these things actually do help to slow CVD? Yes. But betting your entire life on these unproven things alone without hard evidence is a very risky thing to do.
