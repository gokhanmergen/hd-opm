Shared from BoxLife Magazine · original post

Fit runner Jeff collapsed mid-race, revealing a silent 50–60% blockage in his “widowmaker” artery.

This near–fatal event was triggered by atrial fibrillation, not exercise, exposing how undetected plaque can lurk without symptoms.

The article unpacks the stages of atherosclerosis, how plaque ruptures, and why collateral vessels from training can mask serious occlusions.

It also highlights essential screenings—like lipid panels, calcium scans, and CT angiograms—to catch risks early.

Emergency angioplasty and lifestyle tweaks saved Jeff’s life and illustrate why monitoring heart health matters for all athletes.
