Ideas to improve your Mediterranean diet adherence score (MEDAS)

Gokhan Can · Dec 02, 2024 · ♥ 18 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsNuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsFats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan's practical MEDAS tips: morning cucumber-celery juice, evening low-sodium V8 for lycopene, a tablespoon of high-polyphenol olive oil at lunch and dinner, and nuts in place of cheese. He warns that a green juice containing grapefruit juice may interact with statins.

  • Cucumber-celery juice in the morning
  • Low-sodium V8 after dinner
  • 1 tbsp high-polyphenol extra virgin olive oil at lunch and dinner
  • Grapefruit juice may raise statin absorption

Please see the first image to understand what it means to be adherent to med diet. Some ideas to get there:

1. Start Your Day with Fresh Vegetable Juice

1 cup cucumber + celery juice (first thing in the morning). I've been doing this for about four years. It provides a nice energy boost.

Warning: Suja Uber Greens has added grapefruit juice, and may enhance the absorption of certain medications, like statins. Try another brand if you don't want this side effect.

Eating one medium sized cucumber also works similarly. (I've done that while traveling)

2. Evening Anti-oxidants & Lycopene

1 cup low-sodium vegetable V8 (after dinner). I've been drinking this for the past five months, and it’s worked very well. It gives a mild increase in energy and helps keep my evening meals light and high nutrient content.

3. Add High-Polyphenol Olive Oil

1 tablespoon of high-polyphenol extra virgin olive oil. Inspired by Bryan Johnson, I've started adding this to both lunch and dinner. While it’s a new addition to my routine, there’s nothing negative to report so far. I'm curious to see how (or if) it affects my labs over time.

4. Boost Your Nut Intake

Try swapping out cheese with mixed nuts in your meals? For example, I found out that a mixed nut burrito can be surprisingly tasty and satisfying.
