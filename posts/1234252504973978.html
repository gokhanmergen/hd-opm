In less than a minute, he correctly explains the connection between low LDL cholesterol and higher mortality in old age.

This has been repeatedly studied, and it is now well understood: Diseases in old age that kill people cause metabolic disregulation which lowers LDL as a side effect. This is called reverse causality. The low LDL does not cause the disease— it’s the reverse— the disease causes the low LDL. Reverse causality creates an association between low LDL and higher mortality in old age.
