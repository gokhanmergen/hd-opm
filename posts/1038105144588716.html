Group guidelines: the group is for reading and digesting science, mainly human trials

Gokhan Can · Jun 01, 2025 · ♥ 30 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
Group news & community
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan explains that the group focuses on human clinical trial evidence, treats observational and preclinical data as suggestive, and does not claim to know everything. A group announcement, not health content.

I've seen way too many posts recently questioning whether heart disease treatments are on the right track, whether what we're doing in this group is the right approach etc.

I want to clarify that this group is mainly for reading and digesting science. When I mean science, I mostly mean evidence from human clinical trials. Other scientific studies too are acceptable e.g. observational evidence, preclinical evidence (mice or cell studies), anectodes etc but they come with a huge asterisk attached to them. So not as trustworthy and needs to be taken as suggestive but not definitive.

Does this group claim to know everything? Not at all.

Do the members occassionally get overly excited about things that aren't yet definitively proven to work? Yes, for sure -- we're all human.

Are things in the right track? Yes, most definitely. Science, as it's practiced, has made tremendous progress with improving the human condition since the enlightenment. Age-normalized cardiovascular deaths are coming down consistently.

Should we go back to the pre-industrial age, reject modern medicine, move to far away villages or something? No not at all. There's no data to support such claims (that I know).

Discuss the latest scientific research on preventing, managing, and potentially reversing cardiovascular disease through evidence-based interventions.

We focus on proven and emerging therapies: optimizing blood markers, optimal treatment using lifestyle changes, medications, supplements and off label drugs that may extend lifespan.

Key Principles:

  • Our goal is to learn and work together to address CVD scientifically.
  • We believe peer-reviewed research and clinical trials should guide our knowledge.
  • We are open to proven pharmaceuticals like statins, ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors as well as lifestyle changes.
  • We help address risk factors like obesity, high blood pressure, diabetes, and high cholesterol.
  • We embrace supplements & drugs shown to slow down atherosclerosis or reverse metabolic disorders.
  • Our ultimate goal is maximizing your disease-free years and lifespan.
  • Having diverse opinions, we share a commitment to rigorous analysis.
  • No conspiracies allowed. Defend claims via facts or this group may not be the best place for you.
