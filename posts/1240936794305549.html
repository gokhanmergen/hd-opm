The golden trio of health conspiracy theory nonsense:

  1. Anti-vax
  2. Cholesterol denialism
  3. Seed oils cause pretty much every major chronic disease

These three topics have absolutely nothing to do with one another scientifically.

Yet anyone who believes one of them, very likely believes all three.

The only thing these 3 topics have in common is that they are the subject of the same crackpot conspiracy theories about how mainstream medicine is intentionally trying to kill you. That’s why people who believe 1 of them usually believe all 3.
