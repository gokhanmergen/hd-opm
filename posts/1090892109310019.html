How to accurately estimate your visceral fat

You don’t need to waste money on DEXA scans… and expose yourself to unnecessary ionizing radiation (even if very small).

As this video explains:

It turns out that calculating your waste circumference minus your thigh circumference is a better estimate of your total visceral fat than the number you get from a DEXA scan.

The intuitive reason why this works is very simple:

Measuring your waste circumference is a very good estimate of the total fat in your abdomen. And measuring your thigh circumference gives you a good estimate of the subcutaneous fat in your abdomen. By subtracting ‘total’ minus ‘subcutaneous’ you get ‘visceral’.
