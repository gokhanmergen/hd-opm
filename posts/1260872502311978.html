AHA's new dietary guidance: nine features of a Mediterranean/DASH-style pattern

AI summary & key facts

Louis lists the AHA guidance: control calories and weight, eat fruits and vegetables and whole grains, favor legumes, nuts and seafood over red meat, avoid processed meat, choose unsaturated fats, whole foods, minimize added sugar and salt, and limit alcohol.

  • 9 features including unsaturated over saturated fat
  • Avoid processed meat; limit alcohol

The new dietary guidelines from the AHA.

They are basically recommending a traditional Mediterranean / DASH diet.

  1. Control calories and weight
  2. Eat lots of fruits & vegetables
  3. Eat whole grains, instead of refined grains
  4. Favor legumes, nuts, and seafood for protein, instead of red meat. Avoid processed meat. Choose lower fat dairy products.
  5. Favor unsaturated fat, instead of saturated fat. For example, nuts, seeds, liquid vegetable oils (like olive oil).
  6. Choose whole foods, instead of overly processed foods.
  7. Minimize added sugar
  8. Minimize excess salt
  9. Limit alcohol consumption

Healthy eating is about balance, not one meal or one type of food.

A dietary pattern that supports a healthy heart features vegetables and fruits, whole grains, healthy sources of protein, nontropical oils and minimally processed foods.

New dietary guidance from the American Heart Association highlights the importance of adopting these healthy habits early in life and maintaining them into adulthood. Here are the 9 features that can help you choose a heart-healthy eating pattern.
