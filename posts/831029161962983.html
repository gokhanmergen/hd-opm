Eggs did not raise TMAO in a Cleveland Clinic randomized trial, unlike choline bitartrate

Jul 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 13 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis rebuts claims that eggs raise AFib risk via TMAO by pointing to an earlier randomized trial from the same Cleveland Clinic group. In that trial, eating eggs or taking phosphatidylcholine did not raise TMAO, whereas choline bitartrate supplements did. The trial was not funded by the egg industry.

Daily Mail egg-AFib headline is wrong: the study linked TMAO, not eggs, to AFib

Jul 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis corrects a Daily Mail headline claiming eggs raise AFib risk. The Cleveland Clinic study associated blood TMAO with AFib, not egg intake, and the same group had earlier shown in a human trial that eggs do not raise TMAO.

Even 1-3 eggs per month tied to 17% lower Alzheimer's risk in the Adventist Health Study

Jun 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis corrects a shared claim: in the Adventist Health Study analysis, eggs eaten only 1-3 times per month were associated with 17% lower Alzheimer's risk, and nuts and legumes gave the same reduction in a substitution analysis. So daily eggs are not required.

Adventist Health Study: nuts and beans protected against dementia as much as eggs

May 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 6 Brain health & dementia

Louis notes this is the fifth large cohort linking eggs to lower dementia risk, but the same analysis showed nuts and beans gave equally large protection. Swap eggs for red and processed meat, not for nuts or beans. The shared post says roughly 12 almonds or a third cup of lentils matched the egg effect, undermining a choline-only explanation.
