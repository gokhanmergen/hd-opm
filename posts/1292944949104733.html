An experimenter’s 10-day high-polyphenol extra-virgin olive oil protocol delivered surprising health benefits beyond its usual culinary role.

Groundbreaking studies like PREDIMED and a Nature report highlight oleocanthal’s ibuprofen-like anti-inflammatory properties and its ability to reduce visceral fat, stabilize blood sugar, and increase calorie burn.

Initial days brought digestive adjustments and slight bloating, but by day three cravings vanished and the waistline began to shrink.

Inflammation markers dropped, energy steadied, and snacks lost their appeal by day five.

By day seven, weight fell without changing diet or exercise.

The article details precise dosing, timing strategies, and olive oil selection tips.
