PCSK9 inhibitors regress plaque without the calcification surge seen with statins

Jan 01, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 44 · 💬 35 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Explains the 'statin paradox' (statins reduce events and plaque but raise coronary calcium as plaque densifies) and argues PCSK9 inhibitors regress plaque without a calcific surge. Cites GLAGOV (evolocumab, LDL 36.6 mg/dL, PAV -0.95% vs +0.05%), PACMAN-AMI (alirocumab triple regression) and Gao 2022 (CAC +2.9% vs +10.6%).

Alirocumab plus statin slowed coronary calcium progression: +2.9% vs +10.6% at 18 months

Jan 01, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 1 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summarises a 2022 RCT in CAD patients on moderate statins: alirocumab 75 mg every 2 weeks for 18 months limited CAC progression, with Lp(a) change correlating with CAC change. A retrospective study (Ikegami) found about half the annual CAC progression with PCSK9 inhibitor plus statin; studies are small and larger trials are needed.

PCSK9 inhibitor plus statin nearly halted coronary calcium progression versus statin alone

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 4 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summary of alirocumab 75 mg biweekly added to moderate statins in 61 CAD patients over 18 months: median CAC +2.9% vs +10.6%, with CAC not rising significantly from baseline. A retrospective study (Ikegami, n=120) found about half the annual calcium progression. Mechanism is thought to be stronger LDL and Lp(a) lowering.

The case for LDL lowering: statins, ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors and diet all reduce events

Sep 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 13 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Long article arguing skepticism about statins is no longer warranted. About 16 FDA-approved drugs lower LDL, nearly every method of lowering LDL reduces cardiovascular events, and high-dose statins combined with other agents can slow or possibly halt plaque progression. Diet changes such as reducing saturated fat also lower LDL.

Plaque reduction: statins, PCSK9 inhibitors and ezetimibe have the strongest evidence

May 18, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 32 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Ranks interventions for regressing or stabilizing atherosclerotic plaque. Statins, PCSK9 inhibitors and ezetimibe have the strongest trial evidence, with high-dose EPA and pioglitazone fairly good; colchicine, aged garlic, Centella, pine bark, nattokinase and plant-based diets are promising but need rigorous imaging RCTs, and evidence for exercise, L-arginine and vitamin K2 is limited.
