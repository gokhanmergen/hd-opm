Supplements and foods can raise statin levels through cytochrome P450 interactions

Dec 01, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 13 Statins

Louis explains that an underappreciated cause of statin side effects is combining the drug with foods or supplements that raise its blood level via cytochrome P450 interactions in the gut and liver. Grapefruit juice is the classic example, and herbs and botanicals are the likeliest supplement culprits. Practical steps: separate dosing times and adjust statin dose to LDL. A reader note says the grapefruit interaction applies only to certain statins.

Platelet reactivity score (PRESS) predicts cardiovascular events in PAD patients

Aug 20, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 19 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

NYU researchers developed PRESS, combining platelet aggregation responses and RNA sequencing, to detect platelet hyperreactivity. In PAD patients, hyperreactive platelets predicted more major adverse cardiac or limb events within 30 days of revascularization, and PRESS above the median carried a 90% higher risk of major events over 18 months. It might help target aspirin.
