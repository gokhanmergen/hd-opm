Seed-oil profit claim rebutted with an industry note on thin refining margins

AI summary & key facts

Louis argues the idea that selling edible seed oils is highly profitable is false, citing an oil-refinery equipment vendor's FAQ that calls edible oil refining a low-margin commodity business. It is a short opinion with no health evidence.

  • Cited source says refining is low-margin due to commodity nature, competition and raw-material price volatility

It’s a lie that selling edible seed oils is highly profitable.

Most seed oils are priced as a commodity.

The profit margins are terrible.

Olive oil has the strongest trial evidence, but canola and soybean oil also have support

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.

North Karelia: swapping butter for vegetable oils credited with 80%+ fewer coronary deaths

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis says replacing saturated with unsaturated fat is believed to be a main reason heart deaths dropped in Finland in the 1970s and 1980s. The shared cardiologist's post describes North Karelia's 700 per 100,000 CVD death rate in 1972, with 24% of calories from saturated fat, and a 20% cholesterol drop with coronary deaths in working-age men falling 82%.

Swapping 1 tbsp butter for plant oil linked to 17% lower cancer and all-cause death

Sep 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 12 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares an AHA press release on Harvard cohort data (Nurses' Health Study I and II, Health Professionals) for 221,054 adults. Replacing a daily tablespoon of butter with unsaturated plant oil was associated with lower cancer and total mortality, and each extra tablespoon of butter with higher cancer risk. Olive, canola and soybean oil showed the biggest benefits.

A new study shared via MedPage Today links whole grains, vegetables, vegetable oils, nuts and legumes to lower dementia risk and refined grains, juices, white potatoes and added sugars to higher risk. Middle-age and older adults who improved diet quality had an 11% reduced dementia diagnosis risk over 10 years.

100+ feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose control, saturated fat does not

Dec 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes the 2016 PLoS Medicine meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat, mainly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowered fasting glucose and A1C and improved insulin resistance and secretion, MUFA did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit. He pairs it with the 20-cohort biomarker study showing a 43% lower diabetes risk with high omega-6.

Seed oil use rose 5x since 1950 while heart disease deaths fell over 4x

Nov 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis reshares a post pointing out that since 1950 vegetable oil consumption rose about 5x and butter and lard fell 2-3x, while heart disease death rates fell over 4x and life expectancy rose from 60 to 78. This is a raw, unadjusted correlation, and he criticizes influencers who post fake graphs on the topic.
