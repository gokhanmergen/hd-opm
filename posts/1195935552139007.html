Louis questions whether John McDougall's teenage stroke was ischemic

AI summary & key facts

Louis relays neuroradiologist Peter Rogers' view that an ischemic stroke at 18 is very rare and arterial dissection is the usual cause. The post is speculation about an individual rather than research.

  • Ischemic strokes at age 18 are very rare
  • Arterial dissection is the usual cause at that age (per Rogers)

It is very unlikely that John McDougal actually did have an ischemic stroke at age 18, like he repeatedly claimed.

Ischemic strokes are very rare in 18yo’s. Strokes at that age are almost always caused by arterial dissection.

This is coming directly from Peter Rogers, who is a Harvard/Stanford trained neuroradiologist and image-guided surgeon. He is an expert on strokes. And he practices a diet similar to the McDougal diet himself. So this is not a hit piece. He believes that the clinical evidence suggests McDougal had an arterial dissection at 18yo, not an ischemic stroke.
