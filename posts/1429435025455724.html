Microsoft's MAI-DxO AI beat physicians on complex NEJM diagnostic cases

Louis Nervegna · Oct 23, 2026 · ♥ 8 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
AI & digital tools
AI summary & key facts

A news item reports initial results from Microsoft's MAI-DxO, a virtual panel of AI agents that more than doubled physician accuracy on 304 complex NEJM cases while ordering fewer, cheaper tests. It is news about an AI tool, not about heart disease.

  • 304 complex NEJM cases
  • More than double physician accuracy with reference tools

#aiHD

Shared from The Rundown · original post

Microsoft just published initial results from its MAI-DxO AI diagnostic system, revealing that it beats physicians on both complex medical cases and everyday ER visits while ordering fewer, cheaper tests at the same time.

MAI-DxO works like a virtual panel of doctors, with AI agents proposing diagnoses, ordering tests, and challenging each other as new results come in.

On 304 complex ‘New England Journal of Medicine’ cases, it more than doubled physicians’ accuracy with reference tools; on typical ER cases, it hit 80% vs. 47%.

One setup reached 81% accuracy on the NEJM cases at less than half the cost of comparable systems, with the gains holding even with newer models like GPT-5.5.

Microsoft will test the system in real clinical settings at Beth Israel Deaconess in Boston, measuring how its patient diagnoses fare against clinicians’ assessments.

Microsoft’s ER findings make a strong case for AI in diagnosis, but getting the assessment right is only one step in care. For AI to truly help patients, it will also need to guide treatment, track health over time, and fit into how doctors and patients make decisions together, all of which still have to be proven at scale.
