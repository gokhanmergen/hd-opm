AHA: unsaturated fats from avocado, fish and nuts lower bad cholesterol and triglycerides

Louis Nervegna · Apr 01, 2025 · ♥ 9 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)LDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares an American Heart Association post noting that unsaturated fats found in avocado, fish and nuts can help lower LDL and triglycerides. It is a brief public-health message without study detail.

  • Unsaturated fats can lower bad cholesterol and triglycerides

#unsaturatedfatHD #fatHD #ldlHD #triglyceridesHD #avocadoHD #seafoodHD #nutsHD

Shared from American Heart Association · original post

Eating foods with fat is part of a healthy diet, and some are definitely healthier than others. Unsaturated fats – found in avocado, fish and nuts – can help lower bad cholesterol and triglycerides, and give your body the fuel it needs to live a long, healthy life.

Walter Willett: unsaturated fats, Mediterranean eating and daily habits at 81

Oct 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 4 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A Washington Post interview with Harvard nutrition researcher Walter Willett. He says his group's data showed total fat was irrelevant, trans fats were bad, saturated fat versus refined carbohydrate was only weakly related to heart disease, and unsaturated fats were linked to lower heart disease and mortality. He also describes his own diet, including oats, nuts, blueberries and a plant-based lunch.
