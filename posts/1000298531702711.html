Generative AI sounds convincing even when wrong; be careful with medical decisions

Louis Nervegna · Apr 05, 2025 · ♥ 4 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
AI & digital tools
AI summary & key facts

Louis warns that generative AI tools are designed to sound highly convincing even when they are wrong and urges great care before using them for medical decisions. He shares a short Terence Tao video on AI's plausibility problem.

  • AI tools can sound plausible while being wrong

The problem with generative AI tools is that they are designed to sound extremely convincing, even when they are wrong.

You need to be extremely careful when using them to make medical decisions.
