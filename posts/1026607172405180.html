Shared from Rip Esselstyn · original post

Don’t let the fearmongering scare you away from eating oats. 🥣

Levels, a company that specializes in glucose monitors, found that the average blood sugar spike 1-2 hours after eating a bowl of oats is only 34mg per deciliter – which falls in the moderate to mild range.

In fact, oats contain a soluble fiber called beta-glucan that actually stabilizes blood sugar levels.
