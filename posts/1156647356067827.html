Cardiovascular Disease is Multi-Factorial

Managing cardiovascular disease requires a multi-faceted approach. While controlling traditional risk factors like LDL , BP, BMI, and Trigs etc is fundamental, emerging research highlights a far more complex picture driven by chronic inflammation. This systemic inflammation can be triggered by a wide range of factors, including viral infections like Flu, COVID-19 and shingles, as well as bacterial loads originating from poor oral hygiene and untreated gum disease. Furthermore, the gut microbiome plays a critical role, with metabolites like TMAO and Imidazole Propionate directly contributing to atherosclerosis. Finally, environmental toxins like microplastics and novel biological processes, such as plaque exhibiting tumor-like growth, demonstrate that a comprehensive heart health strategy must look beyond cholesterol and address all these interconnected risk factors.

What I personally aim for:

  • Lots of high fiber foods and supplemental fiber
  • Brush 2x, Floss daily (water pick is great)
  • Get dental cleanings 3x yearly.
  • Get yearly flu vaccine. Mask without feeling embarresed.
  • Have an air purifier running 24x7 in my bedroom (not sure how much it helps with this, but I figured it cannot hurt).
  • Don't eat outside (when you can avoid it). Eat at home, and buy organic. Avoid conventionally grown food as much as possible.
