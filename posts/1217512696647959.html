Olezarsen cut triglycerides 64% and remnant cholesterol 72%, but plaque did not change

Mar 30, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 10 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Quotes Ethan Weiss on a surprising null result: at 6 months olezarsen lowered triglycerides 63.9%, remnant cholesterol 71.9% and apoB 16.0% versus placebo with no LDL-C difference, yet there was no difference in plaque at one year. This suggests triglyceride/remnant lowering alone may not shrink plaque.

Metabolic syndrome, insulin resistance and visceral fat drive plaque growth beyond LDL

Sep 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 Insulin resistance & diabetes

An annotated evidence summary (reshared from Ken Gursky, with a long analysis) argues that residual plaque growth despite low LDL is linked to metabolic syndrome, insulin resistance and visceral fat. It cites imaging studies in which these factors predicted greater plaque progression, even on statins.

Ezetimibe lowers LDL-C about 18-20% alone and a further 21-30% on top of a statin

Sep 24, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 19 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

A quick reference on ezetimibe: it blocks cholesterol absorption in the small intestine and is most useful added to a statin. It also modestly lowers apoB and triglycerides and slightly raises HDL.

Kimchi meta-analysis: lower triglycerides, glucose and blood pressure across nine studies

Sep 21, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

A University of Connecticut meta-analysis of nine studies linked regular kimchi intake to better cardiometabolic markers. The blood pressure benefit is attributed to potassium and fermentation bacteria despite kimchi's high sodium, though most studies were done in Korea.

Metabolic health beyond the scale: waist-to-height ratio, TG/HDL and adiponectin

Sep 17, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 12 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A reshared guide (from Louis Nervegna) explaining metabolic syndrome and simple markers of metabolic health. It stresses waist-to-height ratio, the triglyceride-to-HDL ratio as an indirect marker of insulin sensitivity, and adiponectin, with visceral fat loss as the key lever.

Staying keto while still tracking ApoB and plaque markers (shared from Dominic D'Agostino)

Aug 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 6 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Gokhan reshares a keto practitioner's account: 15 years of keto lowered blood pressure, triglycerides, HbA1c, insulin and hsCRP but raised LDL and ApoB until ezetimibe was added. Because of high Lp(a) and a decade of very high LDL, he used a carotid IMT test instead of CTA. The message is to keep monitoring markers on keto.
