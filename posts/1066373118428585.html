  • Smoldering cardiovascular crisis (Harvard Gazette) · other · Harvard Gazette
Shared from Harvard University · original post

Starting in the late 1960s, cardiovascular disease deaths began to fall. Mortality decreased by 70% and the share of all premature deaths attributable to heart disease fell, too.

The trends no longer look so promising. Around 2010, the long decline in cardiovascular deaths began to level off and appear to have stalled. Harvard experts are exploring why that may be happening.
