PREDIMED: nuts regressed carotid plaque and a nut-rich Mediterranean diet cut stroke 50%

AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes the PREDIMED carotid substudy: a Mediterranean diet with 30 g/day mixed nuts (walnuts, hazelnuts, almonds) regressed carotid intima-media thickness over 2.4 years while the control diet progressed. In the main trial, the Mediterranean diet cut MACE by 30% and the nut group had a 50% lower stroke risk; the trial was stopped early on ethics grounds.

  • Substudy of 164 subjects: ICA-IMT progressed 0.052 mm on control, regressed 0.084 mm on MedDiet+nuts (P=0.024)
  • Nut dose: 30 g/day (15 g walnuts, 7.5 g hazelnuts, 7.5 g almonds); regression after 2.4 years
  • Main trial: MACE reduced 30%; stroke about 50% lower in the nuts group
  • Trial stopped early so controls could be moved to the Mediterranean diet

Nuts reduce plaque in the carotid artery.

This result is from a large randomized controlled trial (PREDIMED) run for multiple years.

This study compared a control diet (similar to AHA recommendations at the time), a Mediterranean diet + additional EVOO, and a Mediterranean diet + nuts.

The recommended dose of nuts in the intevention group was 30g per day (a small handful), consisting of 15g walnuts, 7.5g hazelnuts, 7.5g almonds.

Only the group with nuts saw regression of plaque in the carotid.

The reversal occurred after 2.4 years of daily nut consumption.

“We evaluated 164 subjects with complete data. In a multivariate model, mean ICA-IMT progressed in the control diet group (mean [95% confidence interval], 0.052 mm [−0.014 to 0.118 mm]), whereas it regressed in the MedDiet+nuts group (−0.084 mm [−0.158 to −0.010 mm]; P=0.024 versus control).”

This study demonstrated that the Mediterranean diet including nuts reduced MACE events by 30% compared with the control diet, which was the primary endpoint.

In particular, the group consuming nuts had a 50% reduced risk of having a stroke than the control group. This was consistent with the reversal of plaque seen in the carotid artery.

MACE (Major Adverse Cardiac Events) is a composite endpoint representing serious heart/vessel problems, typically including cardiovascular death, heart attack (MI), stroke, and unplanned revascularization (PCI/CABG) or heart failure hospitalization.

Because the results were so striking in such a short time, the PREDIMED trial was stopped early. The ethics committee felt that the control group should also be placed on the Mediterranean diet.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet reversed carotid plaque and cut recurrent events ~25%

Apr 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis summarizes the 2021 CORDIOPREV RCT in patients with prior heart attack or stroke on standard medical care: a Mediterranean diet rich in olive oil, nuts and fish reduced carotid thickness at 5 and 7 years, unlike a low-fat diet, and reduced maximum plaque height. Recurrent major cardiovascular events were roughly 25% lower, even though participants were already on statins and other drugs.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet shrank carotid plaque and cut recurrent events about 25%

Mar 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 13 ↩ 27 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes CORDIOPREV, an RCT of 1002 coronary patients comparing a Mediterranean diet rich in extra virgin olive oil with a low-fat diet. The Mediterranean diet reduced carotid intima-media thickness at 5 and 7 years while the low-fat diet showed no change, and also lowered carotid plaque height. He also cites roughly 25% fewer recurrent major cardiovascular events over 7 years, with benefits seen despite statin treatment and mean LDL-C below 90.
