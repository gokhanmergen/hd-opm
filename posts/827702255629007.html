At least 7 g/day of olive oil linked to 28% lower dementia-related death over 28 years

Louis Nervegna · Jun 29, 2024 · ♥ 20 reactions · 23 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaFats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)
AI summary & key facts

A May 2024 analysis of the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study found at least 7 g of olive oil daily was associated with 28% lower dementia-related mortality versus never or rarely using it, regardless of genetic predisposition and overall diet quality.

  • Olive oil >= 7 g/day: 28% lower dementia-related death
  • Follow-up 1990-2018 (28 years)
  • Independent of genetics and diet quality
  • Observational cohort

Consuming at least 7g of olive oil per day is associated with a 28% lower risk of dementia-related death later in life. (Developing dementia and ultimately dying from its consequences.)

This is the latest paper published from the Harvard Nurses Study and Harvard Physician’s Health Study. (May 2024)

The followup period was 28 years (1990 to 2018), long enough to see both the onset of dementia and its ultimate consequences.

Quoted:

“The study found that, regardless of genetic predisposition and overall diet quality, participants who consumed at least 7 grams of olive oil per day had a 28% lower risk of dementia-related death compared to participants who never or rarely consumed olive oil.
