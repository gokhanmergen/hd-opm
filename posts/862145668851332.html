How to reduce microplastic exposure: less plastic, glass, natural fibers, HEPA filters

Gokhan Can · Aug 31, 2024 · ♥ 13 reactions · 15 comments · original Facebook post
Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)
AI summary & key facts

Practical tips from a Bloomberg article: use less single-use plastic, avoid microwaving in plastic, choose glass or stainless steel, wear natural-fiber clothing, use HEPA filters and remove shoes indoors. Complete avoidance is impossible, and links to heart disease are potential.

  • Avoid microwaving food in plastic
  • Choose glass or stainless steel
  • Natural-fiber clothing; HEPA filters; remove shoes indoors

How to Reduce Your Exposure to Microplastics

  1. Microplastics have been found throughout the human body and may pose health risks, including potential links to heart disease, cancer, and other conditions.
  1. While completely avoiding microplastics is impossible, there are steps individuals can take to minimize exposure:
  • Use less plastic overall, especially single-use plastics
  • Avoid microwaving food in plastic containers
  • Choose glass or stainless steel kitchenware over plastic
  • Opt for clothes made of natural fibers instead of synthetics
  • Use HEPA air filters at home
  • Remove shoes when entering the house to reduce dust circulation

Choose clothes made of natural fibers

Textiles made of synthetic fibers are a frequent and often-forgotten source of microplastic exposure, particularly when they’re washed or dried. Plastic-free alternatives include natural fibers such as wool, cotton and linen.

Microplastics raised plaque 63% in male mice, with no change in cholesterol or weight

Summarises a UC Riverside mouse study: atherosclerosis-prone mice given 10 mg/kg/day microplastics for nine weeks. Males had 63% more plaque in the aortic root and 624% more in the brachiocephalic artery; females had no significant increase.
