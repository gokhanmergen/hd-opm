Nuts and seeds are a healthier alternative to ultra-processed protein powders

Louis Nervegna · Dec 12, 2024 · ♥ 25 reactions · 27 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsProtein
AI summary & key facts

Louis suggests nuts and seeds instead of protein powders, since their fat and fiber are cardioprotective, improve insulin sensitivity and are filling. He points to research showing protective effects of nuts and seeds against CVD.

A much healthier alternative to ultra-processed protein powders.

The additional fat and fiber in nuts and seeds is cardioprotective, improves insulin sensitivity, and is highly satiating.
