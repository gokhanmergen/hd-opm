This p-tau217 blood test has now become standard of care in Alzheimer’s.

#ptau217HD #alzheimersHD #dementiaHD

Shared from Medical Xpress - Medical and Health News · original post

A blood test measuring the protein p-tau217 has increased Alzheimer's diagnosis accuracy to 94.5% in clinical settings, compared to 75.5% with standard methods. This advancement offers a less invasive, more accessible, and highly reliable approach to identifying Alzheimer's disease in patients with cognitive symptoms.
