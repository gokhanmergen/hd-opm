  • Kidney function and complete revascularization in older MI patients · other · JACC International
Shared from JACC Journals · original post

Kidney function remains a strong prognostic factor in older MI patients. Physiology-guided complete revascularization is effective regardless of renal function and may provide greater absolute clinical benefit in patients with chronic kidney disease due to their elevated baseline risk.

Lubiprostone preserved kidney function in a 118-patient phase II CKD trial

Aug 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 2 Kidney health

A phase II trial in Science Advances found that the constipation drug lubiprostone (16 ug daily) preserved eGFR over 24 weeks versus decline on placebo. Surprisingly, it did not cut uremic toxins; instead it shifted the gut microbiome and raised spermidine. Kidney disease commonly accompanies heart disease.

Gut-bacteria peptide corisin drives diabetic kidney fibrosis in mice

Aug 26, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Kidney health

A study found the gut-bacteria peptide corisin is elevated in diabetic patients with CKD and tracks with severity. In mice, corisin travels to the kidney on albumin and accelerates aging, scarring and cell death, and a neutralizing antibody reduced kidney damage. Gokhan notes CKD almost always accompanies heart disease.
