Recombinant shingles vaccine linked to fewer cardiovascular events in Nature Medicine

Louis Nervegna · Aug 30, 2026 · ♥ 22 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Infections & vaccinesHeart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a 2026 Nature Medicine study comparing recombinant (Shingrix) and live attenuated shingles vaccines as a natural experiment. The recombinant vaccine was associated with lower incidence of cardiovascular events, adding to possible Alzheimer's protection.

  • Natural experiment comparing recombinant vs live attenuated vaccine
  • Recombinant vaccine associated with lower CV event incidence

This was just published.

In addition to possible protection against Alzheimer’s, the latest shingles vaccine (Shingrix) might also protect against CVD.

Link to journal paper:

Shingles vaccine linked to 46% fewer major cardiac events in heart disease (ACC.26)

Aug 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 6 Infections & vaccines

In 246,822 US adults over 50 with ASCVD, vaccinated patients had 46% fewer major cardiac events and 66% lower mortality at one year. It is observational, not yet peer reviewed, and may reflect healthy-user bias.

Shingles vaccine tied to slower biological aging and less inflammation in 3,800 elders

Feb 28, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 22 · 💬 11 Infections & vaccines

Reports a large national study of more than 3,800 Americans aged 70+, in which shingles-vaccinated people showed slower biological aging, lower chronic inflammation and slower aging-related changes in gene activity. This suggests the vaccine may calm 'inflammaging', the low-grade inflammation tied to heart disease, frailty and cognitive decline.

BMJ: evidence mounts that COVID-19 disrupts immunity, challenging 'immunity debt' idea

Aug 20, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A BMJ feature reports that the immunity debt theory is increasingly challenged and that SARS-CoV-2 may cause lasting immune disruption. Evidence includes more infections after covid than after flu or RSV, viral reactivation, T-cell exhaustion and persistent inflammation.

Shingles vaccination linked to slower biological aging in over 3,800 older adults

Oct 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 Infections & vaccines

A US Health and Retirement Study cohort analysis of over 3,800 adults aged 70+ found those vaccinated against shingles between 2008 and 2018 had lower inflammation scores, slower genetic aging and lower overall biological aging scores. The poster links this with evidence the vaccine may lower dementia risk, with reduced inflammaging a possible mechanism.
