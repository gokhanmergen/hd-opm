Three randomized controlled trials have rigorously proven that a traditional Mediterranean / DASH diet can reverse plaque in arteries:

DISCO-CT

↪ DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed soft plaque by about 40 mm3 in a year

CORDIOPREV

↪ CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet shrank carotid plaque and cut recurrent events about 25%

↪ CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet reduced atherosclerosis versus low-fat diet over 7 years

PREDIMED

↪ PREDIMED: nuts regressed carotid plaque and a nut-rich Mediterranean diet cut stroke 50%

In all three cases, the main focus was on eating whole plant foods like fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds, legumes, whole grains. These were primarily plant-based diets.

But some healthier forms of animal food were allowed, and this might have contributed to the plaque reversal. Seafood was encouraged, especially fatty fish. Poultry and eggs were allowed in moderation. Low-fat dairy was also allowed in moderation, especially fermented forms like yogurt and cheese.

However, red and processed meat were strongly discouraged, even lean varieties.

The main fat source was olive oil, and other unsaturated plant fats. Saturated fat was kept fairly low.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet shrank carotid plaque and cut recurrent events about 25%

Mar 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 13 ↩ 27 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes CORDIOPREV, an RCT of 1002 coronary patients comparing a Mediterranean diet rich in extra virgin olive oil with a low-fat diet. The Mediterranean diet reduced carotid intima-media thickness at 5 and 7 years while the low-fat diet showed no change, and also lowered carotid plaque height. He also cites roughly 25% fewer recurrent major cardiovascular events over 7 years, with benefits seen despite statin treatment and mean LDL-C below 90.

Mediterranean diet has the strongest evidence of any diet: 4 RCTs and decades of cohorts

May 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis argues the traditional Mediterranean diet has unmatched evidence: four 4-7 year RCTs show lower heart attack, stroke and CV death (46% lower CV mortality overall), and Nurses' Health and Health Professionals cohorts plus a dozen more large cohorts confirm long-term benefit. He allows other diets might work better but says none has comparable evidence.

Meta-analysis of 4 Mediterranean diet RCTs: MACE down 48%, CVD death down 46%

Jan 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 20 ↩ 18 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes a 2024 meta-analysis pooling four long RCTs (Lyon, Indo-Mediterranean, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV; 10,054 participants) comparing a Mediterranean diet to control diets. It lowered major cardiovascular events, MI, stroke and CV death but not all-cause mortality or revascularization.
