This article delves into how GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy are transforming eating habits by dampening cravings for ultraprocessed snacks and heightening taste for whole foods.

It highlights Big Food’s rapid pivot toward satiety-driven products, from high-protein brownie bites to individually packaged frozen meals designed for GLP-1 users.

Experts weigh in on whether food scientists can override medication-induced rewiring of reward pathways and what that means for future clean-label innovations.
