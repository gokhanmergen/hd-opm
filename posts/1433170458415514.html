Quoted from the original post:

Can the fasting mimicking diet help protect heart health?

Taking care of your heart includes staying active and making thoughtful food choices.
High blood pressure, elevated cholesterol, and high blood sugar can affect heart and metabolic health, especially as we age. The encouraging news is that healthier habits can help bring these measures into healthier ranges.

Research led by Prof. Valter Longo suggests that periodic cycles of the fasting mimicking diet may help lower blood sugar, cholesterol, and blood pressure, and reduce inflammation—all factors linked to heart and metabolic health.

A personalized approach is key. A healthcare professional can help determine whether the fasting mimicking diet is appropriate for your age, health needs, and any medications you take. The goal is to understand how nutrition and lifestyle can support heart health and healthier aging.
