Shared from Science of Health · original post

The DO-HEALTH study, a 3-year randomized, double-blind trial led by Dr. Heike A. Bischoff-Ferrari, included 2,157 healthy adults aged 70 and older across five European countries.

Participants who took vitamin D3 (2,000 IU/day), omega-3 fatty acids (1 g/day), and followed a home-based strength and balance exercise program experienced a 61% reduction in invasive cancer risk compared with placebo.

Only 4 cancer cases occurred in the combined-intervention group versus 12 in the placebo group.

The study highlights that a combined nutritional and lifestyle approach, rather than a single intervention, is responsible for significantly lowering cancer risk in seniors.

Bottom Fact: Combined vitamin D3, omega-3 supplementation, and home exercises reduced invasive cancer risk by 61% in adults aged 70+ in the DO-HEALTH trial.

Source: DO-HEALTH study, Frontiers in Aging, 2022.
