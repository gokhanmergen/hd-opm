Baylor guide to cutting liver fat: fewer calories, intact grains, vegetables, good fats

Louis Nervegna · Sep 04, 2025 · ♥ 9 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Weight, visceral & liver fatMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes a Baylor College of Medicine NAFLD diet guide. The most effective strategy is reducing calories because liver fat is lost first, and food choices make it easier or harder, favoring vegetables, intact whole grains, fish, legumes and unsaturated oils.

  • 4-5 servings of non-starchy vegetables per day
  • Whole grains 2-4 servings daily, as intact as possible; 2-3 servings of whole fruit
  • Avoid refined sugar, fruit juice, processed meat and trans fat; limit red meat
  • Fish at least 2x per week; use olive or canola oil instead of animal fats
  • Walk at least 30 minutes daily

How do you reduce liver fat by diet?

(As discussed many times in the group, fat accumulation in the liver is the main cause of insulin resistance in most people.)

The most effective way is to reduce calories. The first place that you lose fat when you go on a calorie-restricted diet is in the liver. You must lose most of your visible visceral fat to clear your liver of ectopic fat.

The types of foods that you eat will make this process easier or harder.

This guide from the Baylor College of Medicine assembles the best evidence from high quality studies.

Reference:

bcm.eduA guide to what and how to eat non alcoholic fatty liver disease.pdfbcm.edu

Summary:

  • Eat at least 4-5 servings of non-starchy vegetables per day.
  • Choose whole grains, instead of refined grains. (Eat them as intact as possible.)
  • Limit whole grains to 2-4 servings daily.
  • Don’t mix starchy vegetables and whole grains in the same meal. Pick one or the other.
  • Eat 2-3 servings of fruit daily, in a whole or blended form that doesn’t remove the fiber.
  • Half your plate should be (non-starchy) vegetables and fruit.
  • Avoid refined sugar (and added natural sugars).
  • Avoid fruit juice. Limit dried fruit.
  • Choose plant protein, like legumes, nuts, seeds.
  • Choose healthy forms of animal protein, like seafood, poultry, eggs, yogurt, cheese.
  • If you eat animal protein, prioritize fish by eating it at least 2x per week (baked or broiled) in place of other animal proteins.
  • Limit red meat, and avoid processed meat.
  • Choose unsaturated fats, over saturated fats. Prepare foods with unsaturated plant oils like olive or canola, instead of animal fats.
  • Make sure to get enough omega 3 fat, from foods like fatty fish or plant sources.
  • Avoid trans fat.
  • Exercise. At least, go for a 30 minute walk each day.
