🧪 HOW TO INTERPRET LIVER FUNCTION TEST (LFT)

📚 An Easy Stepwise Clinical Approach

💡 LFTs help assess hepatocellular injury, cholestasis, bile flow, and hepatic synthetic function.

👉 Easy approach:

BILIRUBIN → AST/ALT → ALP/GGT → ALBUMIN/INR → PATTERN → CAUSE

1️⃣ BILIRUBIN 🟡

🔹 Total Bilirubin

▪️ ↑ Bilirubin → Jaundice

Then determine:

Unconjugated ↑
→ Hemolysis
→ Gilbert syndrome
→ Impaired bilirubin conjugation

Conjugated ↑
→ Hepatocellular disease
→ Cholestasis / biliary obstruction

💡 Clinical pearl:
Dark urine + conjugated bilirubin ↑ → think hepatobiliary disease.

2️⃣ AST & ALT — HEPATOCELLULAR INJURY 🧬

🔺 ALT > AST

Commonly seen in:
▪️ Viral hepatitis
▪️ MASLD/NAFLD
▪️ Many drug-induced liver injuries

🔺 AST > ALT

Think of:
▪️ Alcohol-associated liver disease
▪️ Cirrhosis
▪️ Muscle injury can also raise AST

💡 Classic clue:
AST:ALT ≥ 2 → strongly suggests alcohol-associated liver disease
—but it is not diagnostic by itself.

3️⃣ ALP + GGT — CHOLESTASIS 🚨

🔺 ALP ↑ + GGT ↑

→ Hepatobiliary source likely

Think:
▪️ Biliary obstruction
▪️ Cholestasis
▪️ Cholangitis
▪️ Infiltrative liver disease

🔺 ALP ↑ + GGT normal

→ Consider non-hepatic ALP source, especially:
▪️ Bone disease

💡 GGT helps confirm hepatic origin of an elevated ALP.

4️⃣ ALBUMIN + PT/INR — SYNTHETIC FUNCTION 🧪

🔻 Albumin

→ Reduced hepatic synthetic function or other causes such as:
▪️ Malnutrition
▪️ Protein loss
▪️ Inflammation

⚠️ Albumin changes slowly → not a good marker of acute liver injury.

🔺 PT/INR

→ Impaired synthesis of clotting factors
→ Important marker of hepatic synthetic dysfunction

⚠️ Prolonged PT/INR may also occur with vitamin K deficiency or anticoagulant use.

5️⃣ IDENTIFY THE PATTERN 🔍

🔥 HEPATOCELLULAR PATTERN

AST/ALT ↑↑ > ALP

Think:
▪️ Viral hepatitis
▪️ Drug-induced liver injury
▪️ Ischemic hepatitis
▪️ MASLD
▪️ Autoimmune hepatitis

⬇️

🚧 CHOLESTATIC PATTERN

ALP ↑↑ > AST/ALT

Think:
▪️ Biliary obstruction
▪️ Cholangitis
▪️ Primary biliary cholangitis
▪️ Primary sclerosing cholangitis
▪️ Infiltrative disease

⬇️

🔄 MIXED PATTERN

Both AST/ALT + ALP significantly elevated

→ Consider mixed hepatocellular + cholestatic injury.

6️⃣ PUT IT ALL TOGETHER 🧠

🩺 QUICK INTERPRETATION

Bilirubin ↑
⬇️
Conjugated or unconjugated?

AST/ALT ↑
⬇️
Hepatocellular injury?

ALP/GGT ↑
⬇️
Cholestatic pattern?

Albumin/INR abnormal
⬇️
Assess synthetic function

⬇️

🔎 THEN LOOK FOR THE CAUSE

▪️ History: alcohol, drugs, medications, supplements
▪️ Viral hepatitis risk
▪️ Metabolic risk factors
▪️ Autoimmune disease
▪️ Ultrasound when indicated
▪️ Further targeted investigations

🧠 REMEMBER

AST/ALT → INJURY
ALP/GGT → CHOLESTASIS
BILIRUBIN → EXCRETION
ALBUMIN/INR → SYNTHETIC FUNCTION
