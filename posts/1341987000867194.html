What an aortic dissection is, its symptoms and risk factors

Louis shares an explanation that an aortic dissection can be fixed if caught early. It occurs in about 3 per 100,000 people.

  • About 3 per 100,000
  • Symptoms: chest pain, sudden breathlessness, fainting, unequal arm pulse, stroke-like symptoms
  • Risks: age, hypertension, atherosclerosis, connective tissue disorders

A nice simple explanation of what an aortic dissection actually is… and what some of the early symptom are.

It can be fixed, if caught quickly before it ruptures.

Note that aortic dissections are rare. They typically occur at a rate of only around 3 per 100,000 people.

Some of the symptoms:

Chest pain, sudden shortness of breath, fainting/dizziness, a weak pulse in one arm compared to the other, stroke-like symptoms, raspy voice (uncommon)

Some of the big risk factors:

Age, high blood pressure, atherosclerotic disease, genetic conditions (such as those that weaken connective tissue)
