LDL-C vs LDL-P vs ApoB: particle number predicts risk better than cholesterol content

Jul 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

LDL-C measures cholesterol inside LDL particles while LDL-P counts the particles; they can be discordant (low LDL-C with many small particles), and particle number predicts risk better. Particle size adds little once number is accounted for, and ApoB, which counts all atherogenic particles, is the best test.

NLA consensus clarifies when to measure ApoB and matching LDL-C thresholds

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 19 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Shared from Gokhan Can: a summary of the National Lipid Association's Expert Clinical Consensus on apoB testing. ApoB is a more accurate predictor than LDL-C when the two diverge, particularly after statins, and testing is suggested for borderline-risk patients deciding on a statin or those considering intensifying therapy, with apoB thresholds matching LDL-C goals.

Fact-checking keto and carnivore influencer claims about LDL, ApoB and saturated fat

Louis lists what keto/carnivore influencers get right and wrong: ApoB is the best lipid marker, LDL-C usually tracks ApoB, particle size doesn't matter once particle number is counted, and most saturated fats raise LDL-P, ApoB and LDL-C. Very low LDL-C cancels much of the risk from high Lp(a), possibly explaining HORIZON

Sep 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 3 Lp(a)

Louis explains that Lp(a) carries LDL cholesterol, so lowering LDL-C reduces the cholesterol available to deposit in arteries even from Lp(a). He speculates this may help explain why the HORIZON Lp(a) trial failed: once LDL-C is low enough, there may be too little to cause major damage.

Lp(a) carries LDL cholesterol, so lab LDL-C includes cholesterol from Lp(a) particles

Oct 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 37 · 💬 23 Lp(a)

Louis explains that Lp(a) particles are 'boats' carrying the same LDL cholesterol 'cargo' as LDL particles, so a lab LDL-C value includes cholesterol carried by Lp(a); in people with very high Lp(a), a significant portion of LDL-C comes from Lp(a). Lp(a)'s sticky apo(a) protein deposits cholesterol in artery walls and is prothrombotic.
