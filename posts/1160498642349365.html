Biology is too complex to predict outcomes from mechanisms alone

Louis Nervegna · Nov 14, 2025 · ♥ 13 reactions · 16 comments · original Facebook post
How to read researchNuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues that human outcome evidence over long periods beats theoretical mechanisms, with no specific study.

Biology is complex. You can’t make accurate predictions based on theoretical mechanisms alone. This leads to failure, more often than not. Human biology is just too complicated to be able to reliably do this. It’s a stupid strategy.

You have to look at what happens in actual humans over long periods of time.

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains the DISCO-CT randomized trial, where a DASH diet plus statins produced the largest soft (non-calcified) plaque regression of any dietary trial on CT angiography.
