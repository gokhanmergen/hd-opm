CORDIOPREV and PREDIMED showed olive oil and nuts caused plaque regression on imaging

AI summary & key facts

Countering low-fat vegan claims that olive oil and nuts cause heart disease, the poster says two large randomized trials, CORDIOPREV and PREDIMED, showed plaque regression in high-risk patients with olive oil and nuts, proven on imaging. Details are in linked group posts.

  • CORDIOPREV and PREDIMED: plaque regression with olive oil and nuts
  • Confirmed by imaging in high-risk patients

The claims from low-fat vegan health influencers that olive oil and nuts cause heart disease are not believable.

Two very large long-running randomized controlled trials for heart disease — CORDIOPREV and PREDIMED— showed that olive oil and nuts cause plaque regression in high risk patients. This was proven on imaging in both of these studies.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet shrank carotid plaque and cut recurrent events about 25%

Mar 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 13 ↩ 27 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes CORDIOPREV, an RCT of 1002 coronary patients comparing a Mediterranean diet rich in extra virgin olive oil with a low-fat diet. The Mediterranean diet reduced carotid intima-media thickness at 5 and 7 years while the low-fat diet showed no change, and also lowered carotid plaque height. He also cites roughly 25% fewer recurrent major cardiovascular events over 7 years, with benefits seen despite statin treatment and mean LDL-C below 90.

Olive oil has the strongest trial evidence, but canola and soybean oil also have support

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.

Olive oil is the only fat tested in several large trials: Lyon, PREDIMED and CORDIOPREV

Louis explains why he uses olive oil in all his cooking: it is the only fat source tested in multiple large, roughly 5-year randomized trials with hard heart outcomes (Lyon Diet Heart Study, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV), each finding fewer heart attacks and strokes versus supposedly heart-healthy control diets. He acknowledges other fats or no oil might work better but lack equivalent RCT proof.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet reversed carotid plaque and cut recurrent events ~25%

Apr 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis summarizes the 2021 CORDIOPREV RCT in patients with prior heart attack or stroke on standard medical care: a Mediterranean diet rich in olive oil, nuts and fish reduced carotid thickness at 5 and 7 years, unlike a low-fat diet, and reduced maximum plaque height. Recurrent major cardiovascular events were roughly 25% lower, even though participants were already on statins and other drugs.
