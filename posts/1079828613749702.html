Louis shares a 2-minute video of Roy Taylor presenting images of pancreas regeneration and restored beta cell function after diet-induced weight loss, with no drugs involved. The post also outlines the 8-week very-low-calorie diet.

  • 8-week extreme calorie restriction with meal replacement shakes
  • No drugs involved

Roy Taylor proved in a large randomized controlled trial that the pancreas can be regenerated in type 2 diabetics.

The tissue in the pancreas literally regenerated itself in his trial.

Beta cell function returned to normal.

He shows the images in this 2 minute video.
They are stunning.

There were no drugs involved. This was accomplished entirely by diet alone:

▶youtu.beLink to youtu.beyoutu.be

Here are the details of the diet:

For 8 weeks, it was an extreme calorie restriction diet using meal replacement shakes. The shakes contained normal proportions of carbs, protein, fat. It was not low carb. But it was only 800 calories. People were also allowed unlimited amounts of low calorie salad vegetables.

After 8 weeks on this diet, people lost around 30lbs, and the liver and pancreas were completely cleared of fat.

Then people were transitioned to a normal calorie traditional Mediterranean diet for maintenance. They stayed on the Mediterranean diet for the following 2 years when the pancreas regenerated.

The key to the regeneration was keeping the liver and pancreas free of fat for the entire 2 years.

If they regained any significant weight during the 2 years, they were immediately put back on the 800 calorie diet for a few weeks, until the weight was lost again. Then back on the normal calorie Mediterranean diet again for maintenance.

Exercise was encouraged, but not mandatory.
