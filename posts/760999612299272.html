Interview: insulin resistance and LDL/ApoB are both independent causes of heart disease

Louis Nervegna · Feb 23, 2024 · ♥ 18 reactions · 23 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares an interview with William Cromwell, an expert in both metabolic disorders and lipidology, who walks through evidence that insulin resistance and LDL (ApoB) are both independent causative risk factors for heart disease. Louis's takeaway is that neither is the 'true cause' and both need addressing.

  • Insulin resistance and ApoB/LDL are both independent causal risk factors (per interviewee)
  • Both should be addressed for best prevention and treatment

This is an excellent interview that addresses an important question about the roles that insulin resistance and LDL play in heart disease.

The clinical researcher being interviewed, William Cromwell MD, is an expert on both metabolic disorders AND lipidology, who has published extensively in both areas.

He walks through the evidence that demonstrates that insulin resistance and LDL (ApoB) are BOTH independent causative risk factors for heart disease.

Neither appears to be the “true cause”. Both need to be addressed to have the best chances of preventing and treating this disease.

High HDL tracks with lower risk in UK Biobank, but HDL-raising drugs have failed

Oct 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that a viral UK Biobank graph shows higher HDL linked to lower ASCVD risk at any LDL/ApoB, as dozens of earlier observational studies have, though the effect size here is larger. Every randomized HDL-raising drug trial failed on hard outcomes, and Mendelian randomization shows no lifetime benefit from genes raising HDL, so HDL is thought to be a proxy for insulin resistance, which is better measured directly.

Lowering triglycerides alone does not shrink plaque: olezarsen cut TG 64%, plaque flat

May 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 12 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that unless triglycerides exceed about 400-500 mg/dL, drug-lowering them makes little difference to plaque or hard outcomes. In BRIDGE-TIMI 73a olezarsen cut triglycerides 64% with no plaque volume change; VLDL particles are atherogenic but are already counted by apoB.

Low HDL is mostly a sign of insulin resistance, and HDL itself is likely not causal

Dec 31, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 10 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains low HDL usually stems from insulin resistance and visceral fat. Losing visceral fat and replacing refined carbs with healthy fats raises it. A shared post argues randomized trials suggest HDL/ApoA-1 itself is unlikely a major causal driver.
