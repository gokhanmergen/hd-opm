Anti-parasitic drugs for cancer: promising in animals, few human trials

Louis notes preclinical promise for ivermectin, mebendazole and fenbendazole but few human trials, sharing Brian Lawenda's review.

  • Animal evidence promising; few human trials

Do anti-parasitic drugs like ivermectin, mebendazole, or fenbendazole help treat cancer?

There is promising preclinical evidence in animal models, but very few human trials so far to draw conclusions from. It remains an intriguing open question in oncology.
