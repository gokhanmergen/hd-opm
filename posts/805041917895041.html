Rhonda Patrick's morning and evening supplement list

Gokhan Can · May 18, 2024 · ♥ 11 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Reposts a list of the supplements Rhonda Patrick takes, including multivitamin, fish oil, vitamin D, K2, ubiquinol, cocoa extract, sulforaphane, berberine and urolithin A, plus evening magnesium and more fish oil. It is a personal regimen with no supporting studies attached, so it is context rather than evidence.

  • Morning: fish oil 3 g EPA, vitamin D 5,000 IU, vitamin K2 (MK7) 45 mcg, ubiquinol 100 mg, berberine 1,000 mg
  • Evening: additional 3 g fish oil (higher DHA), magnesium glycinate 120 mg

Rhonda's Morning Supplements

  1. Multivitamin: She takes two types, changing them monthly
  1. B-complex: from Swanson
  1. Fish Oil: 3 grams of Omega-3s (EPA)
  1. Vitamin D: 5,000 IU daily
  1. Vitamin K2: 45 mcg of K2, MK7 together with Omega-3
  1. Lutein & Zeaxanthin (eye health)
  1. Ubiquinol: 100 mg for cellular energy support
  1. Cocoa Extract (Cocoavia): 1 scoop or 3 capsules
  1. Sulforaphane: 20 mg of free-form
  1. PQQ: Rhonda uses for potential cognitive function and memory enhancement
  1. Berberine: 1,000 mg for metabolic support
  1. Acetyl-L-Carnitine: 500 mg (energy production)
  1. Alpha Lipoic Acid: 600 mg (antioxidant)
  1. Urolithin A: 250 mg, possibly supports muscle health as we age
  1. Protein Powder – Whey Isolate

Rhonda's Evening Supplements

  1. Fish Oil: An additional 3 grams with a higher DHA version
  1. Magnesium (Magnesium Glycinate): 120 mg to support sleep quality and muscle function
  1. Melatonin: 10 mg per night, taken for sleep enhancement and to prevent night terrors
  1. Inositol: 2 grams per night

Review of 33 systematic studies finds little evidence omega-3 supplements build muscle

Mar 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares Stuart Phillips' summary of a 2025 Clinical Nutrition ESPEN review of 33 systematic studies, which found little evidence that omega-3 supplements improve lean mass, muscle strength or physical function in healthy adults or clinical groups. Some small benefits in older adults were suggested, but exercise and protein remain the best strategies.
