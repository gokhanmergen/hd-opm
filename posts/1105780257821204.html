Omega-3s May Protect Against Daytime Sleepiness

A large multi-ethnic study uncovered seven molecules in the blood associated with excessive daytime sleepiness.

Protective factors included omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids, while other metabolites such as tyramine and sex steroids were tied to increased risk.

The results show both diet and hormonal pathways influence daytime drowsiness.

Researchers say future clinical trials could test whether a targeted diet or supplements can ease symptoms.

100+ feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose control, saturated fat does not

Louis summarizes the 2016 PLoS Medicine meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat, mainly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowered fasting glucose and A1C and improved insulin resistance and secretion, MUFA did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit. He pairs it with the 20-cohort biomarker study showing a 43% lower diabetes risk with high omega-6.
