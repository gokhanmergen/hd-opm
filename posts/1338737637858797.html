Retired NBA star Shaquille O’Neal recently revealed he’s using the GLP-1 medication Zepbound to treat obstructive sleep apnea rather than for weight loss.

Diagnosed in 2011, he credits the drug for improved sleep, energy, and focus while juggling new ventures like a professional dunking league, Reebok expansions, and two master’s degrees.

Beyond medication, he emphasizes simple routines—cardio, strength training, balanced nutrition, and daily walks.

His openness joins other high-profile athletes normalizing GLP-1 therapies for various health needs.
