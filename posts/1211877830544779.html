Two days of only oatmeal reprogrammed the gut microbiome in metabolic syndrome

Louis Nervegna · Jan 28, 2026 · ♥ 27 reactions · 22 comments · original Facebook post
Gut microbiomeCarbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains
AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes the oatmeal study, noting participants had BMI over 30 with metabolic syndrome and ate 1100-1200 kcal/day; controls on the same diet did not see the same benefit.

  • Participants had average BMI above 30 with metabolic syndrome
  • Calorie intake 1100-1200 kcal/day in both groups
  • Controls without oatmeal did not see the same benefit

Eating nothing but oatmeal for 2 days appears to rapidly reprogram the gut microbiome, producing longer-lasting metabolic benefits.

Note: This study was conducted in overweight individuals (average BMI > 30) with metabolic syndrone, and in conjunction with a very-low calorie diet (1100 - 1200/d). The controls who did not eat oatmeal were also on the same low calorie diet, but did not see the same benefit.

74 RCTs show oats lower LDL, blood sugar, weight and waist circumference

Louis shares a summary of a 2022 meta-analysis of 74 randomized trials of oat consumption. Oats versus non-oat controls lowered LDL-C by 0.29 mmol/L, total cholesterol by 0.42 mmol/L, blood sugar, weight and waist, though apoB did not change significantly in just 2 studies. He recommends slow-cook steel-cut oats over instant oats.
