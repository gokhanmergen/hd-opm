After acute coronary syndrome, lower LDL sooner is better, down to about 30 mg/dL

Gokhan Can · Sep 23, 2025 · ♥ 3 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
LDL, ApoB & lipid panelHeart attack, stroke & proceduresPCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs
AI summary & key facts

Shares a new European Heart Journal viewpoint on combination lipid-lowering after acute coronary syndrome. The takeaway: the earlier and lower LDL goes after an ACS, the better, with continued improvement down to around 30 mg/dL.

  • Benefit continues down to LDL of about 30 mg/dL after ACS

Acute coronary syndrome (ACS) is a term for serious events like a heart attack, where blood flow to the heart is suddenly blocked. The lower LDL the sooner the better if you had ACS. The improvement continues all the way to LDL ~ 30mg/dL .
