Is canola oil really bad for you? Or is this just a bunch of nonsense made up by social media influencers and people writing pop nutrition books?

What do the randomized controlled trials on canola oil in humans actually show?

I don’t use canola oil myself— but as you know— I am very suspicious in general of the typical BS claims made by social media influencers. These people often have no idea what they’re talking about, so you have to fact check pretty much every single statement that they make.

Turns out… when you actually look carefully at randomized controlled trials, the data on canola oil is very good. Go figure.

Olive oil has the strongest trial evidence, but canola and soybean oil also have support

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.

Swapping 1 tbsp butter for plant oil linked to 17% lower cancer and all-cause death

Sep 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 12 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares an AHA press release on Harvard cohort data (Nurses' Health Study I and II, Health Professionals) for 221,054 adults. Replacing a daily tablespoon of butter with unsaturated plant oil was associated with lower cancer and total mortality, and each extra tablespoon of butter with higher cancer risk. Olive, canola and soybean oil showed the biggest benefits.

Swapping ghee for rapeseed oil cleared liver fat in NAFLD without cutting calories

Apr 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A March 2024 RCT replaced ghee/butter with an equal number of calories of canola (rapeseed) oil in NAFLD patients; liver fat fell and nearly all glycemic and insulin resistance measures improved. Louis notes over 100 RCTs show replacing saturated with unsaturated fat improves glucose, A1C and HOMA-IR.

Replacing 10 g/day of butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality

Oct 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares Harvard research on 221,054 people from the Nurses' Health Studies I and II and the Health Professionals Follow-Up Study, published in JAMA Internal Medicine. Higher plant-oil intake (especially soybean, canola, olive) was linked to lower total, cancer and cardiovascular mortality, while butter was linked to higher total and cancer mortality; a modeled swap of 10 g butter for plant oils lowered mortality by 17%.

Higher adipose linoleic acid linked to lower CVD mortality, countering seed-oil claims

A shared critique argues that influencers dismiss research showing higher adipose-tissue linoleic acid (omega-6) is associated with lower cardiovascular and all-cause mortality. It says the contradicting evidence was lower quality and excluded from the meta-analysis for not meeting inclusion criteria.
