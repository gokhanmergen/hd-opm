Nitrates in vegetables versus processed meats: nitrosamines and protective compounds

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan shares a video comparing nitrate sources. Processed meats can form harmful N-nitrosamines during processing and cooking, whereas vegetable nitrates come with antioxidants that may offset any downside.

  • Processed meats can form N-nitrosamines; vegetable-derived nitrates typically do not

Great video! Comparing nitrates in vegetables with those in processed meats.

N-nitrosamine Formation:
Processed meats can form harmful N-nitrosamines during processing and cooking. This isn’t typically a concern with vegetable-derived nitrates.

Protective Compounds:
Vegetables also come packed with antioxidants and other beneficial nutrients that may help neutralize any potential negative effects of nitrates.
