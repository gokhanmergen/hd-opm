A Medscape report on new SELECT data: semaglutide (Wegovy) was associated with a 41% lower risk of major adverse cardiovascular events within 6 months of starting, before substantial weight loss or reaching the full 2.4 mg dose. This suggests benefits beyond weight loss.

Semaglutide (Wegovy) is associated with a 41% reduction in the risk for major adverse cardiovascular events (MACEs) within 6 months of initiation — well before patients achieve substantial weight loss or reach the full 2.4 mg weekly dose — according to new data from the SELECT trial. A new analysis of the SELECT trial asks why semaglutide reduced cardiovascular events. Inflammation markers fell 38% versus placebo, early and even in people who lost little weight, and those with the biggest drops had the fewest events. Modelling suggests part of the drug's benefit runs through lower inflammation, though this shows a link rather than proof of cause.
