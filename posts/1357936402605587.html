Post defends COVID-19 vaccine effectiveness against political revisionism

Gokhan Can · Aug 01, 2026 · ♥ 76 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post
Infections & vaccines
AI summary & key facts

Admin states that data from cities, counties and states in 2021-2022 showed lower hospitalization and death among the vaccinated and that politicians' attempts to rewrite this are wrong. No specific study is cited.

This group is rooted in science, not politics, and it is important that we address this topic. The evidence is clear: COVID-19 vaccines saved millions of lives. Throughout 2021 and 2022, data from cities, counties, and states worldwide consistently showed that hospitalization and death rates were significantly lower among the vaccinated. These findings were supported by hundreds of independent analyses conducted by local health authorities and peer-reviewed papers across various demographics and countries. Despite some measurement noise, the conclusion remains consistent.

Attempts by politicians to rewrite this history are factually incorrect. If you stay grounded in the data you can do much better and arrive at more intelligent conclusions.

NYU researchers developed PRESS, combining platelet aggregation responses and RNA sequencing, to detect platelet hyperreactivity. In PAD patients, hyperreactive platelets predicted more major adverse cardiac or limb events within 30 days of revascularization, and PRESS above the median carried a 90% higher risk of major events over 18 months. It might help target aspirin.
