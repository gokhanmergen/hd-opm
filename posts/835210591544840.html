American Cancer Society: 40% of US cancers and ~half of cancer deaths are modifiable

Louis Nervegna · Jul 13, 2024 · ♥ 7 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
CancerGuidelines, policy & public health
AI summary & key facts

A new ACS-led study estimates that four in ten cancer cases and about half of cancer deaths in US adults 30 and older are attributable to modifiable risk factors. Many of these, such as smoking, weight and diet, overlap with heart-disease prevention.

  • 40% of cancer cases attributable to modifiable risk factors
  • About 50% of cancer deaths attributable to modifiable risk factors
  • US adults aged 30 and over

Microplastics raised plaque 63% in male mice, with no change in cholesterol or weight

Dec 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

Summarises a UC Riverside mouse study: atherosclerosis-prone mice given 10 mg/kg/day microplastics for nine weeks. Males had 63% more plaque in the aortic root and 624% more in the brachiocephalic artery; females had no significant increase. Weight and cholesterol were unchanged, suggesting direct endothelial damage and inflammation. Admin suggests high fiber and Finnish sauna as defenses (not from the study).

PCSK9 inhibitor plus statin nearly halted coronary calcium progression versus statin alone

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 4 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summary of alirocumab 75 mg biweekly added to moderate statins in 61 CAD patients over 18 months: median CAC +2.9% vs +10.6%, with CAC not rising significantly from baseline. A retrospective study (Ikegami, n=120) found about half the annual calcium progression. Mechanism is thought to be stronger LDL and Lp(a) lowering.

CARDIA: low-carb diets in young adults tied to more coronary calcium if animal-based

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 3 · 💬 7 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Posts the abstract of a CARDIA analysis of 2,226 participants (mean age 40.4) followed 8.3 years. Higher carbohydrate intake was inversely associated with CAC progression (HR 0.731); an animal-based low-carb score predicted higher progression (HR 1.456) but a plant-based score did not (HR 1.016). 26.5% had CAC progression.

Mendelian randomization in 1 million people: statin-like genetics cut dementia risk 76-82%

Nov 06, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 17 Brain health & dementia

Summarises Dr Stanfield's video on a Mendelian randomization study of over a million people.
