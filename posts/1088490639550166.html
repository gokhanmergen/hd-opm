Hypothyroidism is a risk factor for CVD.

#hypothyroidismHD

Shared from Cleveland Clinic · original post

Hypothyroidism is a condition that happens when your thyroid gland doesn’t make or release enough hormone into your bloodstream. As a result, your metabolism slows down. This can cause unintentional weight gain and make you feel exhausted all the time. In general, hypothyroidism is very treatable. Most people can manage the condition with medication and regular follow-up visits with their endocrinologist.

Over a third of 'low-risk' adults had coronary plaque; low LDL and ApoB were protective

Aug 18, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A JACC Advances study of 1,033 asymptomatic adults aged 40-65 classified as low risk found 35.9% had coronary plaque and 25.3% early calcification, increasing with higher LDL, non-HDL cholesterol and apoB, even below guideline levels. Gokhan reads it as showing that keeping LDL under 70 mg/dL greatly reduces risk.

Niacin breakdown products 2PY and 4PY linked to higher cardiovascular risk (Ferrell 2024)

Summarizes Ferrell et al. 2024: untargeted metabolomics found that the niacin end-products 2PY and 4PY predict major adverse cardiac events, with 4PY promoting vascular inflammation. The link was supported by genetics (the ACMSD gene) in humans and mice, which raises questions about heavy niacin intake from food fortification.

Oatmeal eaters had 21% lower coronary heart disease risk in observational studies

A summary of observational research finding that oatmeal two or more times a week was linked to about 21% lower coronary heart disease risk versus under once a month, with whole oat intake linked to lower all-cause mortality. The authors note these studies cannot prove cause because oat eaters may differ in other habits.
