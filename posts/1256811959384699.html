First placebo-controlled CTO PCI trial: less chest pain, better quality of life

Heart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

JACC Journals reports what is believed to be the first randomized placebo-controlled trial of reopening a chronic total coronary occlusion by PCI. Patients receiving the procedure had statistically significant reductions in chest pain and better quality of life than those given a placebo procedure; presented at ACC.26.

Shared from JACC Journals · original post

In what is believed to be the first randomized placebo-controlled trial of its kind, patients with a chronic total blockage of a coronary artery who received a nonsurgical procedure to reopen the artery showed statistically significant reductions in chest pain and improvements in quality of life compared with patients who received a placebo procedure. The research was presented at #ACC26.
