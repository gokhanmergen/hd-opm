Multivitamin trials slow cognitive decline; possible relevance to heart disease patients

Louis Nervegna · Jan 19, 2024 · ♥ 10 reactions · 12 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBrain health & dementia
AI summary & key facts

Louis notes large randomized trials now show a basic RDA-level multivitamin can slow cognitive decline in older people. He speculates this could matter for heart disease patients because cognitive decline and heart disease are closely linked in older people, so a simple multivitamin might mitigate some added risk. The heart-specific benefit is a hypothesis, not shown in the post.

  • Linked news describes this as the third major study finding multivitamins improve memory and slow cognitive aging
  • RDA-level multivitamin, older adults
  • People with heart disease have higher cognitive-decline risk (per the post)

There is now good evidence from large randomized controlled trials that a basic RDA-level multivitamin can slow cognitive decline in older people.

Is there possibly any relevance of this to heart disease? Maybe… as cognitive decline and heart disease are inextricably linked in older people. If you have heart disease, you are at higher risk of cognitive decline as you get older. A simple RDA-level multivitamin might mitigate some of that additional risk.
