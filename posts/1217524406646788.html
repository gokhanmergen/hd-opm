Grip strength predicts heart attack like blood pressure, but reflects reverse causality

Louis Nervegna · Feb 05, 2026 · ♥ 7 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevityHow to read research
AI summary & key facts

Louis notes grip strength predicts heart attack risk comparably to blood pressure, but argues this is reverse causality: aging weakens grip, and training grip will not lower heart disease risk.

  • Grip strength predicts heart attack about as well as blood pressure
  • Doing hand exercises will not lower heart disease risk

Grip strength predicts risk of heart attack as well as blood pressure.

Note that this is a classic example of reverse causality. Weak grip does not cause aging and age-related diseases. It’s the other way around; aging causes weak grip. Unfortunately, doing hand exercises to strengthen your grip will not lower your risk of heart disease.
