Red meat and whey raise insulin, countering claims in a Lustig clip

Louis Nervegna · Oct 29, 2024 · ♥ 13 reactions · 19 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesProteinMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

Louis says Robert Lustig and Levels are wrong to imply red meat does not raise insulin. He states red meat raises insulin as much as white pasta or oatmeal and whey more than white bread, so switching from carbs to protein mainly lowers glucose, not insulin. He agrees excess insulin may promote vascular smooth muscle growth and some cancers.

  • Red meat raises insulin about as much as white pasta or oatmeal
  • Whey raises insulin more than white bread or glucose
  • Fat is the only macronutrient that does not significantly raise insulin
  • Protein switch lowers postprandial glucose, not insulin

Someone needs to tell Robert Lustig and the folks at Levels that:

  • Red meat raises insulin as much as white pasta or oatmeal.
  • Whey protein raises insulin far more than white bread or pure glucose.

Reference:
facebook.com/share/p/t7Yf6UKZi4EZwcV3

The video clip is misleading but correct: Excessive insulin does cause vascular smooth muscle growth in arteries, which likely contributes to heart disease. It does cause glandular growth. It does likely raise the risk for both breast and prostate cancer. All of this is right.

But here is the truth - High protein diets skyrocket postprandial insulin.

Influencers have been lying about this for years.

Switching from carbs to protein does very little to lower postprandial insulin levels. It only lowers postprandial glucose.

The only macronutrient that does not significantly raise insulin is fat.

If you are wearing a CGM and switching to high protein foods like red meat and whey protein to supposedly lower your postprandial insulin levels, then you are likely doing the exact opposite.
