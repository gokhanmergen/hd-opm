Salt Substitutes Can Reduce Stroke Recurrence and Mortality

New findings from the Salt Substitute and Stroke Study (SSaSS) highlight a simple yet powerful intervention for stroke survivors: replacing regular salt with a low-sodium, potassium-enriched alternative.

doi:10.1001/jamacardio.2024.5417

In this large-scale, cluster randomized trial involving 15,249 stroke patients across northern China, those using salt substitutes experienced:

✅ 14% lower risk of recurrent stroke
✅ 12% lower risk of death, with a 21% reduction in stroke-related deaths
✅ A small but meaningful drop in blood pressure
✅ No increased risk of hyperkalemia

These results reinforce salt substitution as a low-cost, scalable strategy to improve outcomes for stroke survivors worldwide. Given the global burden of stroke, implementing such a simple dietary change could drive major public health benefits.
