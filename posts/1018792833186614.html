ApoB and Lp(a) capture lipid risk; LDL particle size adds nothing once apoB is known

Gokhan Can · May 02, 2025 · ♥ 13 reactions · 34 comments · original Facebook post
LDL, ApoB & lipid panelLp(a)Risk assessment & lab tests
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan advises against paying for NMR particle-size panels, pointing to Gil Carvalho's summary of a genetic analysis in about 200,000 people over 14 years. ApoB and Lp(a) best captured lipid-driven cardiovascular risk and LDL particle size added no further information.

  • Genetic analysis in about 200,000 people over 14 years
  • ApoB plus Lp(a) best capture lipid-mediated CV risk; particle size adds no information once apoB is accounted for

I'd stop wasting money on NMR (particle size) panels.
