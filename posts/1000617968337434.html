Keto causes bigger temporary insulin resistance than statins, so statin fear is overplayed

Sep 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 16 Statins

Louis argues that keto influencers who fixate on statins slightly reducing insulin sensitivity overlook that a keto diet causes the same effect to a much greater degree. Without carbohydrate, skeletal muscle becomes insulin resistant to preserve glucose for the brain and heart, so a glucose tolerance test on keto looks poor. Both effects are temporary and reverse on stopping.

Why diet-health epidemiology may mislead: reverse causation and socioeconomic confounding

Jan 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 17 How to read research

Gokhan argues that dramatic personal improvements on low-carb diets undermine trust in guidelines, and that observational nutrition studies can be distorted by reverse causation (people with insulin resistance may prefer fat and protein) and socioeconomic confounding.
