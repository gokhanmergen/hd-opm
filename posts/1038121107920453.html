Life Extension founder Bill Faloon discussees a significant 2025 UK study which found that modifiable lifestyle changes, such as increased physical activity and social engagement, can reduce sudden cardiac arrest risk by 40-63%, while factors like unemployment and smoking elevate it.

He contrasts this with historical figures like FDR and Eisenhower, who died relatively young from conditions like severe hypertension and heart attacks due to the limited medical interventions of their time, noting FDR's death spurred the vital Framingham Heart Study.

In stark contrast, modern former presidents like Clinton, Bush, Cheney, and Jimmy Carter (cured of terminal cancer) have benefited from advanced medical technologies, allowing them to survive serious health issues and live longer.

In an enjoyable presentation, Faloon emphasizes how these advancements demonstrate technology's power to overcome previously fatal conditions, contributing to increased longevity and improved quality of life.
