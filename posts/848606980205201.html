2024 Lancet Commission: high LDL is the top modifiable dementia risk factor (7% of cases)

AI summary & key facts

The 2024 Lancet Commission added high LDL cholesterol and vision loss to its dementia risk factors. It attributes 7% of dementia cases to high LDL, among 14 modifiable risk factors that together account for 45% of cases.

  • High LDL responsible for 7% of dementia cases
  • 14 modifiable risk factors account for 45% of cases
  • Two new factors vs 2020: high LDL and failing eyesight

The 2024 Lancet Commission has identified LDL cholesterol as the single biggest modifiable risk factor for dementia.

According to the report, high LDL is responsible for 7% of dementia cases.

The report identifies 14 modifiable risk factors, which in aggregate account for 45% of dementia cases.
