Six randomized diet trials showed plaque reversal, from Ornish to CORDIOPREV and DISCO

Louis Nervegna · Oct 16, 2024 · ♥ 32 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Louis lists the only six human RCTs in which diet reversed plaque: Lifestyle Heart Trial (Ornish diet), STARS and SCRIP (AHA diet), PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV (Mediterranean) and DISCO (DASH-style). Successful diets emphasized produce, legumes, whole grains, nuts, olive oil, fish and some dairy. They limited junk food, refined grains, butter, fatty red meat and heavy alcohol. He links each paper.

  • Lifestyle Heart Trial 1990 (Ornish), STARS 1992 and SCRIP 1994 (AHA diet)
  • PREDIMED (carotid IMT/plaque, Mediterranean diet plus nuts), CORDIOPREV 2021, DISCO 2021
  • Shared themes: fruits, vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts, olive oil, fish
  • All limited refined grains, junk food, butter, fatty red meat, alcohol

There have been 6 randomized controlled trials in humans that have demonstrated that diet can reverse arterial plaque.

They were each successful to varying degrees.

This video goes through each of them, one by one:

Lifestyle Heart Trial (1990) - Ornish diet
Stars Trial (1992) - AHA diet
Scrip Trial (1994) - AHA diet
PREDIMED (2013) - Mediterranean diet
CORDIOPREV (2021) - Mediterranean diet
DISCO (2021) - DASH diet

These are the ONLY randomized diet trials in humans that have ever demonstrated plaque reversal.

The common dietary themes in the trials that worked to reverse plaque were:

  • Fruits & vegetables
  • Legumes
  • Whole grains
  • Yogurt, and some other dairy
  • Unsaturated fats
  • Nuts
  • Olive oil
  • Fish & seafood
  • Some eggs & poultry

Foods that ALL of the trials limited:

  • Junk food
  • Refined grains
  • Cookies, cakes, sugary cereal, sugary drinks, pop tarts, potato chips, etc.
  • Large amounts of butter
  • Large amounts of fatty red meats
  • Large amounts of alcohol

DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed ~40 mm3 of soft plaque in one year

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains the DISCO-CT randomized trial, where a DASH diet plus statins produced the largest soft (non-calcified) plaque regression of any dietary trial on CT angiography. Adjusted to one year it was -39.5 mm3, versus an average at-risk person growing about 5 mm3 per year, so roughly 8 years of progression reversed. He notes the DASH diet was about 30% fat, mostly unsaturated, and adds caveats about interpretation.
