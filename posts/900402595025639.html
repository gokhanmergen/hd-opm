Epigenetic clock pioneer Horvath reportedly cut his biological age 4 years in 9 months

Louis introduces UCLA's Steve Horvath, discoverer of the epigenetic clock, and shares a Business Insider piece on how he lowered his own epigenetic age by 4 years. Reported changes were less bread and pasta, more vegetables, a higher statin dose, several supplements and a nightly uphill walk. It is one person's anecdote.

  • Horvath's clock estimates cell age; whole-organism accuracy still debated
  • 4 years younger in 9 months (self-reported, n=1)
  • Changes: diet, statin dose, vitamin D, zinc, fish oil, 20-minute uphill walks

If you don’t know who Steven Horvath is, you probably should.

He is a geneticist and biostatistician at UCLA who discovered a clock inside human cells that accurately shows how old the cell is— much like rings on a tree.

At the cellular level, this clock is very accurate. (There is an on-going debate on how accurate it will be at the level of the entire organism. This is being intensely studied right now.)

The clock works not only on human cells, but also on cells from hundreds of different invertebrates, which includes mammals.

Many now believe that it is only a matter of time before he wins a Nobel prize in medicine for this discovery.

Steve was able to reverse this clock in his own cells by 4 years by making some very simple changes to his diet, medications, lifestyle, and supplements.

4 ways a top biological age expert reversed his own biological age by 4 years

1. He cut down on bread and pasta, and started eating more frozen vegetables

Vegetables like leafy greens, crunchy sprouts, beets, and asparagus are nutrient rich foods that can naturally reduce inflammation, staving off a whole host of age-related diseases, including cancer and diabetes.

  1. Horvath increased his dosage of a cheap cholesterol drug that many doctors say can help out with healthy aging

Statins work to do two things: They lower LDL [bad cholesterol] and they also lower inflammation.

  1. He started taking at least 5 different supplements, including vitamin D, zinc, fish oil ets.

4. And he takes a brisk, uphill walk for about 20 minutes every night after dinner

In just 9 months, he shaved 4 years off his biological age — whatever that means.

The good news is there's no biological age test required to start doing more of these healthy behaviors. Just get moving, and eat more veggies.
