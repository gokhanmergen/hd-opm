CoQ10 trial in chronic kidney failure: 36% vs 90% needed dialysis

Louis Nervegna · Oct 16, 2026 · ♥ 18 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Kidney healthSupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

The post highlights an older randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of coenzyme Q10 in chronic renal failure in which far fewer CoQ10 patients needed dialysis than placebo patients. The poster stresses he is not claiming CoQ10 reverses kidney disease, only that the research exists. The linked paper is from 2000, so confirmation in larger recent trials matters.

  • 36.2% of kidney patients on CoQ10 required dialysis vs 90% on placebo
  • Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled design
  • Post claims the finding has been replicated in other trials (not detailed in the post)

Shared from Kidneysolution · original post

A randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial published in the journal Renal Failure found that only 36.2% of kidney patients taking CoQ10 required dialysis, compared to 90% in the placebo group.

That is a significant finding.

We are not saying CoQ10 reverses kidney disease.

We are saying the research exists, it has been replicated across multiple trials and populations, and most kidney patients have never been told it does.

CoQ10 improved tinnitus severity and sleep in presbycusis patients in a small 2025 RCT

A 2025 double-blind randomized trial in the Iranian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology tested coenzyme Q10 added to routine care in patients with tinnitus due to age-related hearing loss. It reported reduced tinnitus disability and improved sleep. It is not related to heart health.

Pooled blinded trials: statins cause only about 1 in 15 reported muscle aches

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 12 Statins

A pooled analysis of 19 blinded trials with 123,940 people found that over about four years, 27.1 of 100 on a statin reported muscle pain or weakness versus 26.6 of 100 on placebo. This implies that the vast majority of muscle complaints on statins are not caused by the drug, consistent with a nocebo effect.

CoQ10 rejuvenated aging platelet-producing cells in mice via COPS3 and autophagy

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 Supplements & vitamins

A mouse study in Aging Cell found dietary CoQ10 restored function of aged megakaryocytes, the bone marrow cells that make platelets, through a COPS3-autophagy pathway. Aging makes platelets stickier and more clot-prone, raising heart attack and stroke risk. The poster calls it promising but it is animal-only.

CoQ10 may reduce gum bleeding and inflammation in periodontitis (11-trial meta-analysis)

Aug 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 35 · 💬 8 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes the CoQ10 taken for heart health might also reduce gum disease. A 2022 meta-analysis of 11 clinical trials found CoQ10, topical or oral, lowered gum bleeding and gingival inflammation alongside standard periodontal treatment.
