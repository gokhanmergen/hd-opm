Louis argues that weight loss, not a specific magic diet, explains cured prediabetes and metabolic syndrome in keto, carnivore or vegan anecdotes. He shares the Twinkie diet story, where a professor lost 27 pounds on junk food with calories under 1,800 a day.

  • Roughly 30 lb of loss dramatically reduces liver fat and reverses insulin resistance (per post)
  • Twinkie diet: 27 lb lost in 10 weeks with improved cholesterol and triglycerides (per shared article)

Virtually any diet that causes weight loss of around 30lbs or more will dramatically reduce liver fat and reverse insulin resistance.

Think about this the next time that you see someone claiming that their magic carnivore/keto diet or magic raw vegan diet cured their prediabetes and metabolic syndrome.

It was the weight loss. Duh.

Shared from BoxLife Magazine · original post

A Kansas State nutrition professor tested a radical idea: could you lose weight eating almost nothing but junk food?

For 10 weeks, Mark Haub lived on Twinkies, chips, and sugary snacks while cutting calories to under 1,800 a day. The result was 27 pounds lost, improved cholesterol, and lower triglycerides—despite the highly processed diet.

His experiment highlights the power of calorie reduction, but it also raises questions about long-term health, nutrient deficiencies, and whether portion control with junk food can ever replace truly balanced eating.
