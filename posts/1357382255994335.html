Targeting senescent-cell inflammation (senomorphics) may beat killing the cells

Louis Nervegna · Jul 31, 2026 · ♥ 9 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Aging & longevityInflammation & CRP
AI summary & key facts

Louis says senescent cells cause multiple diseases including CVD and argues that shutting off their inflammation may be better than senolytics. Mayo researchers found a mitochondrial pathway driving this inflammation.

Accumulation of senescent cells with advancing age plays a direct causal role in multiple diseases, including CVD.

Instead of killing senescent cells (with drugs called senolytics), a better strategy may be shutting off the inflammation-generating pathways that make these cells dangerous in the first place (with drugs called senomorphics).

Can we reduce harmful inflammation from aging cells without destroying the cells themselves?

Researchers have identified a new mitochondrial pathway that helps drive the chronic inflammation associated with aging. The findings could help pave the way for new therapies to combat age-related diseases driven by chronic inflammation.
