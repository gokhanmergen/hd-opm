Harvard review: fat not clearly linked to cancer; red and processed meat raise colon risk

Dec 20, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 2 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes the Harvard Nutrition Source summary on dietary fat and cancer. Total fat percentage and fat types show no clear association with cancer, and the Women's Health Initiative low-fat trial did not reduce breast or colon cancer. Red and processed meat do increase colon cancer risk, and animal fat in young women was linked to higher breast cancer risk in Nurses' Health Study II. He notes a later Harvard study updates this.

Statins after high-risk prostate cancer diagnosis linked to 42% lower cancer death

Dec 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 9 Statins

A shared summary of a cohort study of high-risk prostate cancer patients: post-diagnosis statin use (atorvastatin, pravastatin, rosuvastatin, not lovastatin) lowered prostate cancer mortality 42%, statins plus metformin 54%, and metformin alone showed no benefit.

ERSPC 23-year follow-up: PSA screening cut prostate cancer deaths 13% with overdiagnosis

Nov 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Cancer

Summarises the 23-year European Randomized Study of Screening for Prostate Cancer follow-up: 13% lower prostate cancer mortality (22 fewer deaths per 10,000 men), one death prevented per 456 men invited (628 at 16 years), with higher diagnosis rate (14% vs 12%) reflecting overdiagnosis. Authors call for risk-based screening.

Statins associated with lower advanced and fatal prostate cancer and longer survival

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis cites a 2022 review of the epidemiology of statins and prostate cancer. Registry and cohort studies find statin use linked to lower advanced and fatal prostate cancer risk and longer survival in men already diagnosed. These are observational associations.
