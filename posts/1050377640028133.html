74 RCTs show oats lower LDL, blood sugar, weight and waist circumference

Jun 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 49 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares a summary of a 2022 meta-analysis of 74 randomized trials of oat consumption. Oats versus non-oat controls lowered LDL-C by 0.29 mmol/L, total cholesterol by 0.42 mmol/L, blood sugar, weight and waist, though apoB did not change significantly in just 2 studies. He recommends slow-cook steel-cut oats over instant oats.

Berberine 500 mg twice daily lowered ApoB in two trials; CVD prevention unproven

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Supplements & vitamins

A shared summary says two randomized trials of 500 mg berberine twice daily reduced ApoB, with a reduction nearly three times greater in women, though across separate trials. A newer study of 84 men identified a 'Berberine Signature' of 92 proteins linked to about 13% lower odds of ischemic heart disease in UK Biobank, but those participants were not necessarily taking berberine, so it does not prove prevention.

Berberine modestly lowers LDL, triglycerides and ApoB: meta-analysis of 18 RCTs

Sep 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials in adults with dyslipidemia found berberine produced small reductions in LDL cholesterol, triglycerides and apoB, with possible sex-specific effects on HDL. The conclusion calls for larger trials assessing clinical outcomes.
