Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT) is booming, but it comes with serious hidden health risks that doctors rarely discuss.
While TRT can provide short-term benefits like more energy and muscle, it may increase your risk of blood clots, heart attacks, sleep apnea, and infertility—especially if you don't actually have low testosterone.
For men with genuine testosterone deficiency, TRT under medical supervision can be life-changing and even preventative.
The key? Only pursue TRT if you're clinically deficient, work with a qualified healthcare provider, and monitor your health regularly.
