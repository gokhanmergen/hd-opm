High-dose omega-3 raises AFib risk in high-CVD-risk people, not at low doses (2026)

Sep 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 12 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis warns that the atrial fibrillation risk from high-dose omega-3 is real. A 2021 meta-analysis of 7 RCTs (81,210 people) found higher AF risk, and a 2026 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs (114,592 people) confirmed it at higher doses in people at high CVD risk, but not in those without high risk, and not at low doses in either group.

Daily Mail egg-AFib headline is wrong: the study linked TMAO, not eggs, to AFib

Jul 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis corrects a Daily Mail headline claiming eggs raise AFib risk. The Cleveland Clinic study associated blood TMAO with AFib, not egg intake, and the same group had earlier shown in a human trial that eggs do not raise TMAO.

Cleveland Clinic links gut-bacteria byproduct TMAO to atrial fibrillation risk

Jul 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 ↩ 2 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Cleveland Clinic researchers analyzed blood samples from over 5,000 people undergoing heart procedures and found TMAO, made by gut bacteria from nutrients in red meat and other animal products, was linked to higher AFib risk. Cell experiments suggested TMAO may alter the heart's electrical environment. Louis frames it as TMAO possibly raising AFib risk.

Omega-3 index tracked mortality like classic risk factors; high intake may raise AFib

Jul 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 6 Supplements & vitamins

A shared summary says US average omega-3 index is 4.12% and under 2% of people reach 8%. In a 2,500-person cohort and 42,466-person pooled analysis, highest-index groups had lower all-cause mortality. Louis cautions evidence is observational and high omega-3 intake may raise AFib.

Undiagnosed sleep apnea may drive resistant hypertension, AFib and cardiometabolic disease

Jun 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Sleep, stress & social health

A shared Medscape piece notes that clinicians often escalate drugs in resistant hypertension, recurrent AFib, type 2 diabetes, heart failure or CKD without considering obstructive sleep apnea. Many patients lack classic snoring or sleepiness and instead show hard-to-control blood pressure or fragmented sleep. Louis added only a hashtag.
