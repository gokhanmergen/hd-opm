Shared from Prof Luigi Fontana MD PhD FRACP · original post

A breakthrough in type 1 diabetes treatment: A 25-year-old woman with type 1 diabetes is now producing her own insulin following a autologous transplant of reprogrammed stem cells (CiPSC islets) beneath the abdominal anterior rectus sheath. Conducted by Deng Hongkui’s team at Peking University, this pioneering procedure eliminated her need for insulin within 2.5 months, with glycemic control improving from 43.18% to 96.21% time-in-target range by month 4, and stable control thereafter (>98% time-in-range, ~5% glycated hemoglobin). At 1 year, all study endpoints were met with no transplant-related issues. These promising results support further clinical trials of CiPSC-islet transplantation for type 1 diabetes. These advancements could revolutionize type 1 diabetes care, offering hope to millions worldwide.

DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2024.09.004
