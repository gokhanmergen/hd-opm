More bad news on the natural sweetener erythritol.

It may damage the blood brain barrier.

Erythritol is a sugar alcohol found naturally in certain foods. It became popular as a low calorie sweetener, particularly in keto desserts.

Previous observational research by Stanley Hazen’s group at the Cleveland Clinic has linked it with increased risk of stroke.
