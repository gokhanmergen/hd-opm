A personal list of anti-inflammatory drugs and supplements, with low-dose colchicine next

Gokhan Can · Feb 18, 2024 · ♥ 13 reactions · 12 comments · original Facebook post

The author catalogs the anti-inflammatory drugs and supplements he has used (curcumin, fish oil, aspirin, Wobenzym and others) with a line on each mechanism, and says he plans to try low-dose colchicine. It is a personal experiment list without cited trials.

  • Curcumin inhibits TNF-alpha and NF-kB; fish oil EPA/DHA produce resolvins and protectins
  • Aspirin inhibits COX but carries bleeding risk and needs medical supervision

I tried to make a list of every anti-inflammatory drug and supplement I've been on. Would love to experiment with low dose Colchine after I get some this summer.

  • Curcumin: Potent anti-inflammatory effects through inhibition of inflammatory molecules like TNF-alpha and NF-kB. This spice extract is commonly used to address a wide range of inflammatory conditions.
  • Fish Oil: Rich in omega-3 fatty acids (EPA and DHA), which reduce inflammation by producing anti-inflammatory molecules called resolvins and protectins. Benefits cardiovascular health and mood.
  • Aspirin: Inhibits the COX enzymes responsible for inflammation, pain, and fever, however due to bleeding risk, it should be used cautiously and under medical supervision.
  • Wobenzym (Digestive Enzymes): Contains a mix of enzymes with anti-inflammatory properties, shown to lower TGF-beta, a key player in chronic inflammation and fibrosis. Also, bromelain and trypsin have direct anti-inflammatory activities and can improve the absorption of curcumin.
  • Singulair: Blocks leukotrienes, inflammatory molecules involved in asthma and allergic reactions. Research suggests potential anticancer and anti-NAFLD effects.
  • Aged Garlic Extract: Contains allicin-like compounds with various health benefits. It shows anti-inflammatory action by reducing the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines.
  • Milk Thistle: Active ingredient silymarin exhibits anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, particularly beneficial for liver health.
  • NAC: Antioxidant properties and boosts glutathione levels and modulates inflammatory pathways. It is also used for respiratory health and has been shown to decrease inflammatory markers such as CRP in PCOS.
  • Rosuvastatin: Beyond cholesterol control, it exhibits anti-inflammatory effects by reducing CRP levels and inhibiting inflammatory pathways. Further research indicates an association with anticancer effects and possible antiaging benefits.
  • Hibiscus & Green Tea: Contain polyphenols, which are antioxidants with anti-inflammatory properties. Hibiscus tea has shown potential in lowering blood pressure, while green tea's catechins have demonstrated numerous health benefits. The combination makes for a powerful anti-inflammatory beverage.
  • Oat Fiber + Green Banana Flour: Soluble fiber is fermented by gut bacteria into short-chain fatty acids like butyrate, which possess anti-inflammatory activities. Beta-glucans in oat fiber are specifically linked to reduced inflammation.
  • Clostridium butyricum: Improves the gut microbiome and produces butyrate which has anti-inflammatory effects by inhibiting NF-kB and promoting regulatory T cells.
