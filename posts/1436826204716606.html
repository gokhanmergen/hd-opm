Review: amyloid-beta may cross a leaky blood-brain barrier and promote heart failure

Louis Nervegna · Oct 31, 2026 · ♥ 3 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Heart rhythm & heart failureBrain health & dementia
AI summary & key facts

A shared Frontiers in Medicine review argues that the amyloid protein that accumulates in Alzheimer's brains can cross a leaky blood-brain barrier, accumulate in the heart and help drive heart failure, linking brain and cardiac disease. It is a hypothesis-generating review rather than outcome evidence.

  • Proposed link between amyloid-beta and heart failure in Alzheimer's disease

The same abnormal amyloid protein that accumulates in the brain of Alzheimer’s patients might also harm the heart.

Higher midlife vitamin D linked to lower brain tau 16 years later in Framingham

Nov 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 Brain health & dementia

A Framingham Heart Study analysis followed 793 adults for 16 years, starting at average age 39. Vitamin D was measured in 2002-2005 and brains were scanned in 2016-2019, and higher midlife vitamin D was linked to lower tau tangles but not to amyloid. The post notes vitamin D acts like a steroid hormone with receptors in memory regions.

FDA approved the first blood test for Alzheimer's (Lumipulse, pTau217/amyloid ratio)

Aug 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 9 Brain health & dementia

Louis explains Lumipulse, approved in 2025, measures the pTau217/beta-amyloid 1-42 ratio, one of three tests (with PET and spinal tap) that can confirm amyloid for diagnosis.

Midlife vitamin D linked to later tau burden in Framingham participants

Aug 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Researchers analyzed vitamin D in nearly 800 Framingham adults without dementia (mean age 39) and tau-PET scans about 16 years later. Louis notes a 20-year RCT is unlikely, so cohorts are the best option, though they cannot prove causation.

MIND diet slowed cognitive decline most in people with high Alzheimer's blood biomarkers

Jul 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Brain health & dementia

A secondary analysis of the 3-year MIND trial in 602 adults aged 65-84 found that cognitive effects of the MIND diet differed by baseline blood levels of amyloid-beta 40 and p-tau181. Louis highlights the largest benefit in those with high biomarkers suggestive of early Alzheimer's.

Anti-amyloid antibodies slow early Alzheimer's decline ~30%, with modest benefit and risks

Jun 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a JAMA perspective noting that lecanemab and donanemab clear brain amyloid and slow cognitive decline by roughly 30% in early disease, supporting a causal role for amyloid. The effects remain modest and are limited by amyloid-related imaging abnormalities.

Obicetrapib improved Alzheimer's biomarkers, most in APOE4/4 carriers (BROADWAY substudy)

May 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a pre-specified BROADWAY substudy (1,535 patients) in which obicetrapib improved five Alzheimer's biomarkers, with the largest benefit in APOE4/4 homozygotes. Caveats are that these are biomarkers, not cognitive outcomes, in a subgroup needing replication.
