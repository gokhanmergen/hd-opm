Talk on arterial stiffening, elastin damage and reversing calcification in animal models

Louis Nervegna · Jul 11, 2024 · ♥ 13 reactions · 18 comments · original Facebook post
Blood vessels, circulation & endotheliumAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

A Longevity Summit Dublin talk covers what makes arteries stiffen with age, with damage to elastin proteins central and a precursor to calcification. The speaker's company reports reversing arterial calcification in animal models with an elastin-repairing therapy.

  • Elastin damage central to arterial stiffening and later calcification
  • Calcification reversed in animal models (company claim)

What causes arteries to stiffen as we age?

Can this fundamental aging process of the arteries be slowed down or reversed?

What interventions are being studied to address this?

This talk addresses all of these questions.

As the name of the speaker’s company implies, damage to elastin proteins in the arteries is central to this process. Damage to elastin is a key part of why calcification ultimately forms in the artery. His company has reversed arterial calcification in animal models with a therapy that repairs the elastin in the arteries.

Protein restriction and aging: animal benefits, no proven human lifespan gain

Louis shares a summary of a review (Knopf and Lamming) of protein and amino-acid restriction.
