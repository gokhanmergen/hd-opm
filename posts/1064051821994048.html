Louis notes that Andrew Saul, who wrote books claiming vitamins alone protect against heart disease, died of congestive heart failure in his 60s, and that advanced ischemic heart disease is the leading cause of congestive heart failure. This is anecdotal, not research.

Vitamin C has not reduced heart attacks or strokes and plaque data are mixed

Apr 12, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Admin summarises that vitamin C alone has been tested widely without reducing hard outcomes (MACE), and plaque-reversal data are mixed: supportive in animals, better/worse/no effect in human trials. He does not advise it as a heart-disease cure, though physiological doses appear safe; he takes 250 mg/day to avoid deficiency. Links an (largely AI-generated, author-vetted) summary document.

Vitamin C blood levels follow an S-curve and plateau, with saturation near 1000 mg/day

Jun 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a pharmacokinetics explanation: blood vitamin C rises steeply from 30 to 100 mg/day, then flattens, so higher doses do not proportionally raise levels. These figures are for healthy people; infection and stress raise the saturation point.

TARGET-D: tailored vitamin D dosing missed its main endpoint but cut heart attack 52%

Nov 10, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 25 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises TARGET-D (630 adults with acute coronary syndrome, ~4.2 years): doses adjusted to reach 40-80 ng/mL did not significantly reduce the composite of death, heart failure hospitalization or stroke, though heart attack risk was 52% lower. Authors call for validation to rule out data mining.

Tom Seest's CAC drop from 66 to 42 over three years, and the debate about reversal

Dec 02, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 16 ↩ 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summarizes a video on whether coronary calcium can be reversed. Tom's score fell from 66 to 42 (36%) between 2018 and 2021 amid many simultaneous changes (keto diet, fasting, supplements, less exercise, weight gain), and he admits he does not know which mattered.

Personalized vitamin D3 dosing halved repeat heart attacks in an Intermountain trial

Nov 11, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

A shared summary describes an Intermountain Health trial of 630 recent heart attack survivors in which vitamin D3 was dosed individually to keep blood levels above 40 ng/mL. Repeat heart attacks were lower, though total major cardiovascular events were not, and larger trials are needed.
