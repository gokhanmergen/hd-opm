Major weight loss clearly and strongly slows biological aging

In an analysis of a 32-week GLP-1 agonist (semaglutide) trial in adults with excess visceral fat, multiple epigenetic aging clocks picked up the improvement

The likely mechanism: less visceral fat means less adipose-driven inflammatory and metabolic signaling that can accelerate aging biology

That fits a hard truth about obesity: it actively pushes aging biology in the wrong direction, raising risk for cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, cancer, and shorter life expectancy
