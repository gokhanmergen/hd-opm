COSMOS trials: daily multivitamin slowed brain aging about 2.1 years in adults 65+

Louis Nervegna · Feb 04, 2026 · ♥ 7 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBrain health & dementia
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues older adults may harm brain health by skipping an RDA-level multivitamin, citing the Harvard COSMOS randomized trials. The shared post reports one year of a standard multivitamin slowed global cognitive aging about 2.1 years.

  • Three large randomized trials in adults 65+
  • Global brain aging slowed about 2.1 years; episodic memory aging almost 5 years (per shared post)

Older adults over the age of 65yo might be putting their brains at risk by not taking a basic RDA level multivitamin.

This statement is justified by results from the large COSMOS randomized trials at Harvard. You can learn more about this by searching the group for the tags #multivitaminHD and #cosmosHD.

Shared from FoundMyFitness · original post

For brain health in older adults, a multivitamin is no longer “optional” - it’s a high-priority basic

In three large randomized trials of adults 65+, taking a standard multivitamin like Centrum Silver for one year slowed global brain aging by about 2.1 years and episodic memory aging by almost 5 years

That means better retention of everyday experiences, names, faces, and events (the things families actually notice when memory starts to fade)

Given how common poor diets and micronutrient gaps are, a single daily multivitamin is an easy, low-effort way to protect aging brains
