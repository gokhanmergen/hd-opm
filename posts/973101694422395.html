Serious liver problems from statins are rare and routine liver monitoring is unnecessary

Louis Nervegna · Feb 23, 2025 · ♥ 9 reactions · 17 comments · original Facebook post
Statins
AI summary & key facts

Louis quotes Harvard Health: among statin users about 1 in 1,000 has an abnormal liver test attributable to the drug, and abnormal results are about as common on placebo. A single baseline test is recommended and routine monitoring is not useful for most people.

  • About 1 in 1,000 abnormal liver tests attributable to statins
  • Routine blood testing does not identify at-risk people
  • One baseline liver test recommended before starting

How common are elevated liver enzymes in people taking a statin?

Quoted:

“Studies of very large numbers of people estimate that among those taking a statin, only one in 1,000 of them would have an abnormal liver test result attributable to the medication. Other studies found that abnormal test results were about as common in people who took a statin or a placebo. Since statins cause so few liver problems, and many other things—such as viral infections and drinking alcohol—can affect liver tests, most abnormal liver test results in people taking statins are unrelated.”

“When statin medications were first approved, doctors did periodic blood tests to check for liver injury. Three decades later, it is clear that serious liver injury from statins is a rare side effect and that routine blood testing does not help identify people at risk for statin-related liver problems. Therefore, routine monitoring is not a good use of money and time.”

“There have been rare reports of serious liver problems associated with statins, but the people this happens to often have liver disease already or were also taking other medications that worsen the effect of statins on the liver. Therefore, a single baseline liver test is recommended before starting on a statin, and periodic checks might be continued in people at high risk of problems.”

Statin use linked to 35% lower odds of liver cancer in UK database study

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Statins

A UK Clinical Practice Research Datalink case-control study of 3,719 liver cancer cases and 14,876 controls found statin users had 35% lower odds of liver cancer, and cholesterol absorption inhibitors (ezetimibe class) 31% lower odds. This is an association, not proof, and non-statin findings need replication.
