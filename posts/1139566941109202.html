Visceral fat, via aromatase, likely drives population-level testosterone decline

Louis and a Barbell Medicine post argue that visceral fat contains aromatase, which converts testosterone to estrogen, and that BMI-adjusted studies underestimate this because waist circumference has risen faster than BMI. Excess body fat can cut testosterone by up to half a reference range; fat loss restores it

Mar 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis posts that overweight and obesity can lower testosterone by as much as half a reference range (about 350 ng/dL) and that losing visceral fat often returns levels to normal. The shared FoundMyFitness post explains that extra fat raises aromatization of testosterone to estrogen, which signals the brain to reduce testosterone production.
