WOSCOPS 20-year follow-up: 5 years of pravastatin gave lasting mortality and event benefit

Apr 07, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Statins

Describes the 20-year follow-up of the West of Scotland Coronary Prevention Study: 5 years of pravastatin in middle-aged men with high cholesterol and no prior CHD produced a lasting legacy effect, with reduced all-cause, CV and coronary mortality and fewer heart failure and MI hospitalizations. No excess cancer or non-CV mortality was seen.

2025 ACC statement: inflammation is actionable in CVD, with universal hsCRP screening

Oct 09, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 13 Inflammation & CRP

Summarises the 2025 ACC Scientific Statement (JACC) concluding the inflammation-ASCVD link is now clinically actionable. It recommends universal hsCRP screening in primary and secondary prevention alongside cholesterol, notes IL-6 and fibrinogen also predict risk, and says CT perivascular fat attenuation is promising but not routine. It contrasts this with a more cautious 2002 CDC/AHA workshop.

NLA consensus: apoB testing helps when LDL-C and risk are discordant

Oct 15, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 6 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Summary of a National Lipid Association expert consensus on apoB testing. ApoB counts all atherogenic particles and may predict risk better than LDL-C, especially after statin treatment when the two diverge, and testing is suggested for borderline-risk patients deciding on a statin and in other discordant cases.

The case for LDL lowering: statins, ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors and diet all reduce events

Sep 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 13 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Long article arguing skepticism about statins is no longer warranted. About 16 FDA-approved drugs lower LDL, nearly every method of lowering LDL reduces cardiovascular events, and high-dose statins combined with other agents can slow or possibly halt plaque progression. Diet changes such as reducing saturated fat also lower LDL.

Meta-analysis: lowering LDL with statins had no effect on cancer incidence or death

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Statins

Summarizes an individual-participant meta-analysis from the Cholesterol Treatment Trialists showing that about five years of statin therapy did not change new cancer diagnoses or cancer deaths. This is reassuring evidence against fears that lowering LDL promotes cancer.
