COSMOS multivitamin matches Centrum-type RDA doses except B12 at 10x the RDA

Louis Nervegna · Nov 15, 2026 · ♥ 7 reactions · 8 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBrain health & dementiaAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis shows the ingredient list of the multivitamin used in the COSMOS trial next to the one used in Physicians' Health Study II. Nearly all nutrients are near RDA levels, but B12 is about 10x the RDA, and he suspects that dose may have driven the cognitive benefit seen in COSMOS. COSMOS showed slower cognitive decline in older adults, and PHS II showed an 8% cancer reduction.

  • COSMOS multivitamin ingredients come from the trial's supplementary material

  • COSMOS: slowing of cognitive decline and memory loss in older people

These were the exact ingredients in the multivitamin used in the Harvard COSMOS trial at MGH/Brigham hospital in Boston.

This is from the supplementary material in the journal publication on the trial: Effect of multivitamin-mineral supplementation versus placebo on cognitive function:… (The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2024).

The COSMOS trial demonstrated a slowing of cognitive decline and memory loss in older people, and an overall reduction in cognitive aging: ↪ Daily multivitamin slowed cognitive aging by about two years in a COSMOS meta-analysis.

You can read past posts about this trial by searching the group for the tag #cosmosHD.

The first column shows the multivitamin used in COSMOS.

The second column shows the multivitamin used in the Physician’s Health Study II randomized trial, which demonstrated an 8% reduction in cancer rates compared to placebo: ↪ Physicians' Health Study II: a basic multivitamin cut total cancer 8% over 14 years.

All the ingredients are close to the RDA, except B12 which is 10x the RDA. Given the known neurological benefits of B12, it is hard not to suspect that the 10x RDA dose played an outsized role in the results: ↪ Even 'normal' vitamin B12 may be too low for older brains; active B12 tied to lesions.

Daily multivitamin slowed cognitive aging by about two years in a COSMOS meta-analysis

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a summary of COSMOS cognition results: in the main analysis of 573 adults 60+, a daily multivitamin modestly improved overall cognition and episodic memory over two years. Pooling with two earlier COSMOS cognition studies (more than 5,000 older adults) gave clear benefits, estimated at about two years of slower cognitive aging. Effects were modest, it is unclear which nutrient matters, dementia prevention was not tested, and the manufacturer donated tablets. He adds that this was an RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver).

Rhonda Patrick reports that in COSMOS, older adults on a multivitamin for two years slowed biological aging by 2.7 to 5.1 months and 3.6 years slowed brain aging by 2.1 years (global) and 4.9 years (episodic).

COSMOS trial: daily multivitamin may slow biological aging markers, with editorial caveats

Mar 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

Quotes a MedPage Today report and editorial on the COSMOS trial, in which a daily multivitamin-multimineral was linked to slower epigenetic-clock aging. The editorialists (Belsky and Ryan) say the data bring the field closer to evidence that a simple multivitamin can slow aging, but uncertainties remain.

COSMOS: 2 years of a basic multivitamin slowed biological aging by about 4 months

Mar 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 10 Supplements & vitamins

Latest COSMOS publication from Harvard and Mass General Brigham: a daily RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver) slowed five epigenetic-clock measures of biological aging, with the largest gains in participants who were biologically older than their age at baseline. The poster stresses this was a standard multivitamin, not a megadose, and recalls that COSMOS earlier showed slower cognitive decline.
