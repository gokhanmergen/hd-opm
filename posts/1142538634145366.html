Vitamin D3 trials: 5% lower all-cause mortality (borderline), 15% lower cancer mortality

Notes pooled randomized data for vitamin D3: all-cause mortality trend HR 0.95 (0.91-1.00, p=0.07) and cancer mortality HR 0.85 (0.74-0.97). Also links a ScienceDaily report that vitamin D slowed telomere shortening, with the ideal dose unclear.
