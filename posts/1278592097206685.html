Alzheimer's biological changes may begin in the late 50s, per Mayo Clinic

Alzheimer’s begins decades before symptoms first appear.

#alzheimersHD #dementiaHD #mayoclinicHD

Shared from Mayo Clinic · original post

Subtle biological changes linked to Alzheimer’s disease may begin as early as the late 50s — decades before memory loss or other symptoms appear — according to new research from Mayo Clinic.

The study maps when key brain and blood-based changes tend to accelerate across the lifespan, offering new insight into when detection and prevention efforts could have the greatest impact.
