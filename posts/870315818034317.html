Metabolic acidosis: low blood CO2 may signal excess acid, treatable with citrate or diet

Gokhan Can · Sep 14, 2024 · ♥ 11 reactions · 5 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan says a low or low-normal CO2 on labs suggests mild metabolic acidosis, which he links to bone and muscle loss. He suggests potassium or magnesium citrate and a more fruit, vegetable, nut and legume diet, but cites no studies.

  • Low or low-normal CO2 on labs suggests mild acidosis
  • Potassium or magnesium citrate; citrate is converted to bicarbonate

Metabolic acidosis is a big deal.

You can see it in your labs. If your CO2 measurement is coming in too low or right on top of the low-normal, you're experiencing some degree of "too much acid".

This acidic state triggers bone loss, muscle deterioration, and can even create an environment favorable for tumor growth.

The nice thing is that there are simple solutions --- potassium citrate and magnesium citrate are two supplements that can nip mild metabolic acidosis in the bud, make your body more alkaline, protect bones & muscles. Mind you it has to be "citrate", which is converted into a base (bicarbonate) internally.

Alternatively, you can adjust your diet to consume more base-forming foods (fruits, vegetables, nuts, legumes), and less acid-forming foods (most other foods), to push that CO2 up. This is likely one of the biggest benefits of Med diet.
