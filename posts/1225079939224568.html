Personal anecdote about eating large amounts of roasted sesame seeds as a snack and carb replacement; the admin considers nuts and seeds among the healthiest foods. No evidence cited.

Sesame by the bucket? Apparently, it’s a thing

My wife discovered sesame seeds a while back, and now she’s fully converted. She used to snack on sunflower seeds, but over time they started triggering allergies. So she switched to sesame seeds instead.

And not casually. I’m talking serious amounts. They’ve become a staple in her diet, almost a carb replacement (though she’s not keto or anything extreme). She genuinely believes they support her health and help her maintain her weight.

I occasionally join her, and I'd say roasted sesame seeds are quite good. Toast them properly and they’re almost dessert-like after a meal.

I’ve always believed nuts and seeds are among the healthiest foods you can eat. Nutrient-dense, satisfying, and metabolically friendly. Sesame definitely fits the bill.
