Meeting AHA's 7 ideal health targets is linked to lower heart disease and cancer risk

Louis Nervegna · Jan 01, 2025 · ♥ 17 reactions · 9 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis notes that achieving the AHA 7 cardiovascular health targets associates with lower cancer as well as heart disease risk, citing the ARIC study.

  • Based on ARIC cohort and AHA ideal cardiovascular health metrics

Achieving these 7 targets according to the American Heart Assiciation guidelines is associated with lower risk of BOTH heart disease AND cancer.
