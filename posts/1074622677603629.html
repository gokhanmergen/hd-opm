Today, less than 10% of heart attacks result in death.

This reflects the huge advancements that have been made in treating heart attacks in the last several decades.

However, there is still about a 90% chance that a cardiac arrest or widowmaker heart attack will result in death, if it occurs outside of a hospital.

Note that a cardiac arrest is not the same as a heart attack.

A cardiac arrest is when your heart completely stops beating.

Most heart attacks do not result is cardiac arrest— but when they do, they are usually deadly.
