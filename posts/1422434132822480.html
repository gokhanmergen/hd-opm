What if one of the most demonized ingredients in your kitchen isn’t actually the problem? 👀

Seed oils have been blamed for everything from inflammation to chronic disease. But does the science actually support the fear?

According to Stanford nutrition researcher Dr. Christopher Gardner (Christopher Gardner), the evidence tells a very different story.

🔥 Here’s where it gets interesting: Decades of research show that replacing saturated fats with omega-6 polyunsaturated fats can improve cardiovascular health outcomes.

Yet social media keeps telling us to throw out the seed oils and start cooking everything in butter, lard, and beef tallow.

What if that supposedly healthier swap is doing the opposite of what you think?

In this podcast conversation, Christopher Gardner challenges one of nutrition’s biggest controversies and explains why decades of research deserve more attention than viral wellness claims.
