A day of 'Mediterranean low-carb' eating with LDL of 71 mg/dL after 30 days

Gokhan Can · Sep 07, 2025 · ♥ 16 reactions · 19 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan shares a typical day of food (vegetable juice, tea, coffee, chicken bibimbap without rice, cheese, nuts, low-carb chocolate) and reports LDL of 71 mg/dL and high energy after about 30 days. It is a personal anecdote.

  • LDL 71 mg/dL after about 30 days on this pattern

Food menu in a day of "mediterrenean low carb"?

  1. Start the day with cucumber + celery juice (Suja Uber Greens) - Breakfast
  2. Green tea + hibiscus tea. Instant Coffee.
  3. Chicken bibimbop (no rice) - Lunch
  4. Double shot Espresso
  5. 1-2 small pieces of low carb chocolate -- snack
  6. ~2 large cuts of cheese (pepper jack and brie) -- dinner
  7. 1-2 handfuls of mixed nuts -- dinner

The more I can stick to this diet, the healthier I get. It's been ~30 days only :) Wish me luck that I can continue forever. LDL was 71 mg/dL past Monday. Lots of energy.

Green Mediterranean diet cut visceral fat about 14% in an 18-month randomized trial
