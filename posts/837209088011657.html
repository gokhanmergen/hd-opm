LODESTAR: rosuvastatin and atorvastatin had comparable outcomes in 4,400 patients with CAD

Louis Nervegna · Jul 17, 2024 · ♥ 12 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsHeart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

In the head-to-head LODESTAR trial, rosuvastatin and atorvastatin showed comparable three-year rates of death, MI, stroke or revascularization in patients with coronary artery disease. Rosuvastatin lowered LDL more but had higher new-onset diabetes needing treatment and cataract surgery.

  • 4,400 adults with CAD, 12 South Korean hospitals, 2016-2019
  • Rosuvastatin n = 2204, atorvastatin n = 2196
  • Comparable 3-year composite of death, MI, stroke, revascularization
  • Rosuvastatin: lower LDL-C but more new-onset diabetes and cataract surgery

Which statin drug is best for lowering risk of heart attack, stroke, and all cause mortality in higher risk patients?

The recently published LODESTAR randomized trial directly compared Rosuvastatin (Crestor) and Atorvastatin (Lipitor) head to head.

Here are the results:

“Conclusions: In adults with coronary artery disease, rosuvastatin and atorvastatin showed comparable efficacy for the composite outcome of all cause death, myocardial infarction, stroke, or any coronary revascularisation at three years. Rosuvastatin was associated with lower LDL cholesterol levels but a higher risk of new onset diabetes mellitus requiring antidiabetics and cataract surgery compared with atorvastatin.”

This trial was run in 12 hospitals in South Korea, September 2016 to November 2019 in 4400 adults (age ≥19 years) with coronary artery disease. Participants were assigned to receive either rosuvastatin (n=2204) or atorvastatin (n=2196) using 2×2 factorial randomisation. The primary outcome was a three year composite of all cause death, myocardial infarction, stroke, or any coronary revascularisation.

Rosuvastatin lowers LDL slightly more than atorvastatin; LODESTAR outcomes were similar

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 10 Statins

Comparison of the two high-intensity statins: both lower LDL-C by 50% or more, rosuvastatin is more potent per milligram, but in the randomized LODESTAR trial 3-year clinical outcomes did not differ significantly. The goal is reaching the appropriate LDL target on maximally tolerated therapy.

Rosuvastatin can stress kidneys at high dose; atorvastatin appears kidney-friendly

Jul 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Statins

Louis explains rare dose-related proteinuria, hematuria or AKI with rosuvastatin, especially 40 mg or with CKD. PLANET I and II found rosuvastatin 40 mg lowered eGFR whereas atorvastatin 80 mg reduced proteinuria and preserved function.

Atorvastatin is generally preferred over rosuvastatin in chronic kidney disease

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Statins

Louis shares an MRCP UK explainer on statins in CKD: atorvastatin needs no renal dose adjustment (under 2% renal excretion), while rosuvastatin is 30% renally excreted, needs dose reduction at low eGFR and can cause reversible proteinuria at high doses. KDIGO advises a statin with or without ezetimibe in CKD stages 3-5 not on dialysis.
