High-carb diet raised insulin yet people ate 689 fewer calories a day in an NIH ward study

Nov 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 0 · 💬 3 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a post arguing that normal insulin spikes from healthy carbohydrate do not drive hunger or overeating. In a controlled NIH study, 20 adults lived in a research facility on a high-carb low-fat diet (about 75% carbohydrate) and a ketogenic diet (about 10% carbohydrate) for two weeks each. Despite higher glucose and insulin, they ate an average of 689 fewer calories per day on the high-carb diet, contradicting the carbohydrate-insulin model's prediction, although the diets differed in other ways.

Low-carb diets are no better for fat loss than other diets when calories are matched

Feb 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 2 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis explains that controlled metabolic ward studies by Kevin Hall found equal fat loss on low-carb and normal-carb diets with equal calories; low-carb works mainly by controlling appetite. James Krieger recounts changing his view.

Long-term keto in mice caused high cholesterol, fatty liver and poor insulin secretion

Oct 04, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 12 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Because lifelong diet RCTs are impossible in humans, Louis points to a long-term mouse study. Keto protected against weight gain and kept insulin sensitivity at first, but over the long run caused hypercholesterolemia, liver fat and glucose intolerance from impaired insulin secretion in male and female mice.
