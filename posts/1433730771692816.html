Insulin resistance by itself does NOT cause type 2 diabetes.

You need insulin resistance in combination with pancreatic insufficiency (insulin deficiency) to cause diabetes.

There are many people who are severely insulin resistant who will never develop diabetes. A properly functioning pancreas is capable of pumping out very large amounts of insulin to compensate for severe insulin resistance. This is not a good thing, but it certainly does happen in many cases.

A true cure for diabetes has to address pancreatic insufficiency, not just insulin resistance. To truly cure diabetes, beta cell function in the pancreas has to be restored back to normal again.
