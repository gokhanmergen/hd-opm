New recommendations from the American College of Cardiology (ACC) on testing for low-grade inflammation with hsCRP.

Shared from Dr. Tara Narula · original post

One of the key drivers of cardiovascular disease is inflammation. New recs out this week support universal testing for hsCRP - a simple, widely available blood test that is a marker of inflammation and can be a powerful predictor of heart disease. Ask your doctor to be tested for this!
