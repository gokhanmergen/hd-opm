Yogurt has long-term evidence behind it; processed whey supplements do not

Louis Nervegna · Jul 09, 2026 · ♥ 52 reactions · 27 comments · original Facebook post
Meat, eggs & dairyProtein
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues yogurt has consistent long-term epidemiology and trials like Lyon, PREDIMED and CORDIOPREV encouraged it, whereas processed whey has no long-term data.

  • Long-term epidemiology on yogurt is consistent
  • Almost no long-term data on whey supplements

Someone who gets it…

We have lots of long-term epidemiology on yogurt. It consistently does well. And we have big randomized heart trials like the Lyon Heart Study, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV, etc. that encouraged yogurt consumption and produced excellent outcomes after years of followup.

On the other hand, we have pretty much ZERO long-term epidemiology on processed whey protein supplements. And no big randomized trials that lasted for multiple years. What these highly-processed whey protein supplements do in the long term is a big mystery.

Why take the risk?

If you do choose to include dairy protein in your diet, the science strongly favors yogurt over the various other options.
