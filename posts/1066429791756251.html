No credible RCT shows TRT extends life or lowers disease risk in men without hypogonadism

Louis Nervegna · Jul 12, 2025 · ♥ 7 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Hormones, women's & men's healthAging & longevity
AI summary & key facts

Louis argues no randomized trial has shown testosterone therapy lowers disease risk or extends lifespan in men without clinical hypogonadism, some studies show harm, and animal experiments show raised testosterone shortens lifespan. He cites a Scientific American feature on testosterone therapy risks.

  • No credible RCT of benefit in men without hypogonadism
  • Raising testosterone shortened lifespan in animal experiments, per the post

No credible randomized controlled trial in humans has ever shown that TRT lowers risk for any disease or extends lifespan in men without clinically diagnosed hypogonadism. Some studies have shown evidence of harm.

And here is the dark truth that these TRT clinics will not tell you:

In animal experiments— including primates and other mammals like rodents— raising testosterone consistently shortens lifespan. See the reference linked-to above.

Testosterone for anti-aging lacks proven longevity benefit; high levels tied to deaths

Mar 23, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 34 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis warns that the only proven benefit of testosterone therapy is treating clinically diagnosed hypogonadism and no credible study shows longevity benefit. He cites data from 3,690 men aged 70-92 showing an inverse U-shaped relation between testosterone and mortality, with risk rising above an optimal level, plus animal studies showing androgens shorten and castration extends lifespan. The animal evidence and sex-difference argument are suggestive, not proof in humans.

He argues this undermines claims that raising testosterone in older men is anti-aging, noting no randomized trial shows benefit in men without clinical hypogonadism. It is mouse preprint data, not a human option.
