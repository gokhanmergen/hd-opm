Vitamin C and Plaque

Vit C alone has been tested extensively in clinical trials and it unfortunately did not reduce any "hard outcomes" (mace ~ heart attacks, strokes, CVD death etc). Data regarding reversing plaque is mixed (supportive in animal experiments, human trials show better/worse/no effect, depending on which trial you look at).

VIt C as a cure for heart disease is not advisable at this point. If you feel your body is in a highly oxidative state and/or as general protection, supplementation with physiological doses appear safe. I myself supplement with vit C to avoid deficiency (250mg/day), and find it beneficial.
