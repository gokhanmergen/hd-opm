Super-oxidized fish oil fed for 7 weeks caused no harm in human trial

Sep 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

The poster shares a Bill Harris clip arguing oxidized fish oil is likely not harmful. In the trial described, researchers deliberately super-oxidized fish oil and gave it to participants for 7 weeks, finding no difference in oxidation, peroxidation, inflammation, glucose, lipids or liver enzymes versus non-oxidized fish oil.
