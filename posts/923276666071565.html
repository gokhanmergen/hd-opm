Statins after high-risk prostate cancer diagnosis linked to 42% lower cancer death

Louis Nervegna · Dec 05, 2024 · ♥ 10 reactions · 9 comments · original Facebook post
StatinsCancer
AI summary & key facts

A shared summary of a cohort study of high-risk prostate cancer patients: post-diagnosis statin use (atorvastatin, pravastatin, rosuvastatin, not lovastatin) lowered prostate cancer mortality 42%, statins plus metformin 54%, and metformin alone showed no benefit.

  • Statin alone: 42% lower risk of dying from prostate cancer
  • Statin plus metformin: 54% lower
  • Metformin alone: no reduction
  • High-risk defined as T2c or higher, PSA 20 or higher, or Gleason 8 or higher

Research cited

  • Individual and joint effects of metformin and statins on mortality in high-risk prostate cancer · cohort · Cancer Medicine · 2020
Shared from Integrative Oncology-Essentials Fan Page · original post

—Taking a statin (atorvastatin, pravastatin, or rosuvastatin, but not lovastatin) alone after a diagnosis of high-risk prostate cancer reduced the risk of dying from prostate cancer by 42% compared to those who did not use statins or metformin.
—Taking metformin plus statins after their diagnosis was associated with a 54% reduction in dying from prostate cancer.
—Taking metformin alone did not show any reduction in prostate cancer mortality.

High‐risk prostate cancer was defined as: (T category ≥T2c or prostate specific antigen level ≥20 or Gleason score ≥8; equivalent to overall cancer stage ≥IIB)

Rosuvastatin lowers LDL slightly more than atorvastatin; LODESTAR outcomes were similar

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 10 Statins

Comparison of the two high-intensity statins: both lower LDL-C by 50% or more, rosuvastatin is more potent per milligram, but in the randomized LODESTAR trial 3-year clinical outcomes did not differ significantly. The goal is reaching the appropriate LDL target on maximally tolerated therapy.

Jul 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Statins

Louis explains rare dose-related proteinuria, hematuria or AKI with rosuvastatin, especially 40 mg or with CKD. PLANET I and II found rosuvastatin 40 mg lowered eGFR whereas atorvastatin 80 mg reduced proteinuria and preserved function.
