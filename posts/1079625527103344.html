AI summary & key facts

Louis argues that any diet producing large weight loss clears liver and pancreas fat, which explains remission on carnivore, keto, vegan, rice or GLP-1 approaches, rather than any food-specific effect.

How are people on carnivore or keto diets able to reverse their insulin resistance and diabetes?

Very simple. The people on carnivore or keto diets who truly reverse their diabetes and insulin resistance lose lots of weight and completely clear their liver and pancreas of ectopic fat.

That’s why it works.

It’s the same reason why a WFPBNO vegan diet works to reverse diabetes— major weight loss.

Or the same reason why crazy diets like the “sugar diet” or “rice diet” work.

And it’s the same reason why GLP1 drugs reverse diabetes.

Search the group for the tag #liverfatsummaryHD and read the posts. This is proven in large RCT’s.

Any diet will reverse diabetes if it leads to big enough weight loss to clear the liver and pancreas of fat.

There is nothing magical about any of these diets, when it comes to insulin resistance and diabetes. It’s the weight loss that really makes them work— specifically, the clearance of fat in the liver and pancreas.
