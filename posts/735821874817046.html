Baby aspirin during and after Covid may be worth discussing if you have heart disease

Louis Nervegna · Jan 06, 2024 · ♥ 14 reactions · 12 comments · original Facebook post
Infections & vaccinesAspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners
AI summary & key facts

Louis suggests people with heart disease who get Covid ask their doctor about baby aspirin during infection and for a few weeks after. Infections temporarily raise heart attack and stroke risk through inflammation, and Covid raises clot risk more than other infections. A linked 2022 meta-analysis found aspirin use associated with lower Covid mortality without more bleeding.

  • Infection temporarily raises heart attack and stroke risk due to inflammation
  • Covid increases blood clot risk more than other infections
  • Meta-analysis: aspirin associated with reduced mortality in Covid-19, no increased bleeding

If you have heart disease and get Covid, you may want to talk to your doctor about taking a baby aspirin during the infection and for a few weeks afterward.

Any kind of infection temporarily raises the risk of a heart attack or stroke during the time period of the infection, because of the very high levels of inflammation that occur. This temporary increase in risk is very large and is one of the reasons why heart disease is a major concern with Covid.

In addition, Covid is well known to increase the risk of blood clots— more so than other kinds of infections.

There is now some evidence that baby aspirin may reduce the risk of dying during a Covid infection.
