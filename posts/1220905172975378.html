Aspartame is not absorbed and breaks down into common food components

Louis Nervegna · Feb 09, 2026 · ♥ 8 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsMyths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

Louis, who says there are better sweeteners, shares a quoted explainer that aspartame is not toxic: it is broken down in the gut into phenylalanine, aspartic acid and methanol, with exposures far below normal foods.

  • Aspartame is not absorbed intact and does not enter circulation
  • Steak provides 20-30x the phenylalanine and aspartic acid of a diet soda
  • Methanol from diet soda is often lower than from fruit juice or vegetables

There are better choices than aspartame to sweeten food and drinks, but it’s important to be scientifically accurate about what it actually is and what it actually does.

Quoted:

3 Reasons Aspartame is NOT Toxic

People love hottakes saying “aspartame is toxic” or that it “causes cancer”, etc.

O’Rly? How?

Aspartame NEVER gets absorbed into the body (PMID: 27753624).
It doesn’t enter circulation
And it doesn’t poison you

Aspartame is rapidly and completely broken down in the gut into:

  • Phenylalanine
  • Aspartic acid
  • Methanol

The two amino acids are consumed in far greater amounts from normal protein-containing foods. You get 20-30x as much phenylalanine and aspartic acid in a steak than you do a diet soda. So let’s just put those to the side (PMID: 17828671 & 2566887)

What we are left with as a possible culprit for negative effects is methanol… but the methanol exposure from diet soda is tiny, often lower than what you get from fruit juice or vegetables.

Even in studies using extremely high doses of aspartame, blood methanol levels stay far below ranges associated with toxicity, and the metabolic fate of methanol from aspartame is well understood (EFSA Panel on Food Additives Scientific Opinion on the re-evaluation of aspartame (E 951). EFSA Journal. 2013) (PMIDs: 12180494 & 2566887)

Additionally, the negative effects that people claim from aspartame are very different from the negative effects of methanol poisoning so the argument breaks down at the most basic level.

You don’t have to like aspartame.
You don’t have to drink diet soda.

But if you’re claiming it accumulates in the body or causes harm via methanol poisoning…
that claim isn’t supported by human data.
