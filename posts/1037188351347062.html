Gokhan shares a Medscape review by John Mandrola of the KETO-CTA study in lean mass hyper-responders on keto. The review says the data are nowhere near supporting the authors' outsized claims that high LDL-C does not relate to atherosclerosis.

Apr 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 21 · 💬 20 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Gokhan argues media coverage misrepresented KETO-CTA: it compared keto dieters with LDL around 200 to those around 300, with no low-LDL control, and still showed substantial new plaque, progressing like high-risk conditions. A comparison of the KETO-CTA and DISCO-CT trials. The original 2025 JACC Advances KETO-CTA paper in lean mass hyper-responders was retracted over concerns about the Cleerly plaque software.
