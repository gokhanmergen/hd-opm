Sweetener evidence links: NutriNet-Santé cancer cohort, WHO review, beverage meta-analysis

Louis Nervegna · Jun 13, 2025 · ♥ 0 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grainsCancer
AI summary & key facts

A link-only post gathering three sources on non-sugar sweeteners: the NutriNet-Santé cohort associating aspartame and acesulfame-K with cancer risk, a WHO systematic review and meta-analysis, and a 2022 Diabetes Care meta-analysis finding no cardiometabolic harm when low/no-calorie drinks substitute for sugary ones. There is no commentary.

  • NutriNet-Santé: aspartame and acesulfame-K associated with higher cancer risk (observational)
  • Diabetes Care 2022 meta-analysis: low/no-calorie beverages not linked to cardiometabolic harm in substitution analyses
