Myeloperoxidase (MPO) from visceral fat immune cells stiffens arteries and may be causal

Louis Nervegna · Sep 04, 2025 · ♥ 11 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Inflammation & CRPBlood vessels, circulation & endotheliumWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares an 8-minute video explaining MPO, an enzyme secreted into the blood by immune cells in visceral fat. He says it attacks and stiffens arteries, can be lowered by losing visceral fat and reducing inflammation, and is now measurable with commercial blood tests.

  • MPO is secreted by immune cells in visceral fat
  • Lowered by reducing visceral fat and inflammation
  • Commercial MPO blood tests exist (per post)

MPO is an enzyme that is secreted into blood circulation by immune cells in visceral fat.

It attacks and stiffens arteries, and is likely causal in heart disease.

You can lower your blood levels of this MPO enzyme by getting rid of visceral fat and reducing levels of inflammation.

There are now commercial blood tests available for MPO.
