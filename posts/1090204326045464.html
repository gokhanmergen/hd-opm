A man once went 382 days without food—and survived.

Living off body fat, water, and vitamins under medical supervision, Angus Barbieri lost 276 pounds and kept it off, with no major health complications.

This article breaks down the science behind his extreme fast, the medical safeguards that made it possible, and why modern experts still debate its legacy.

It’s a remarkable case that challenges assumptions about obesity, metabolism, and the limits of the human body.
