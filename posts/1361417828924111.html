Welsh birthday rule natural experiment suggests shingles vaccine protects against dementia

Shared from Dr. Kevin Tran · original post

Most health news you read is correlation. This dementia study is different.

A Welsh birthday policy split two groups that were identical in every way, except one side got the shingles vaccine and one did not. The uptake cliff: 0.01% vs 47.2%. That is as close to a randomized test as you get without actually running one.
