Louis shares a 7-minute video explaining that the PREDIMED randomized trial of a Mediterranean diet with extra virgin olive oil showed a lower risk of atrial fibrillation, a major cause of stroke. The post does not give effect sizes.

  • PREDIMED: 5-year RCT of Mediterranean diet for heart attack and stroke prevention
  • Afib is a major cause of stroke

Short video (7 mins) explaining how olive oil lowers the risk for atrial fibrillation.

Afib is a major cause of stroke.

The video explains that the proof for this comes from the PREDIMED study, which was a 5 year randomized control trial testing whether a traditional Mediterranean diet could lower the risk for heart attacks and strokes. This video does a good job of briefly explaining the study, and describes the results for Afib in particular.

Louis explains why he uses olive oil in all his cooking: it is the only fat source tested in multiple large, roughly 5-year randomized trials with hard heart outcomes (Lyon Diet Heart Study, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV), each finding fewer heart attacks and strokes versus supposedly heart-healthy control diets. Louis summarizes a 2024 meta-analysis pooling four long RCTs (Lyon, Indo-Mediterranean, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV; 10,054 participants) comparing a Mediterranean diet to control diets. It lowered major cardiovascular events, MI, stroke and CV death but not all-cause mortality or revascularization.
