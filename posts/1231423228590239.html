Sugar-sweetened beverages are especially potent drivers of liver fat via fructose

Mar 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 · 💬 2 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Quotes a Medscape review explaining that sugary drinks deliver fast-absorbed free sugars, and fructose is cleared mainly by the liver where it boosts de novo lipogenesis and triglyceride production. Frequent intake is tied to weight gain, type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular disease.

Whole fruit is linked to less fatty liver; fiber may protect the liver from fructose

Jun 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis notes high fructose causes fatty liver in animals, yet whole fruit is linked to lower fatty liver risk in humans. He cites a 2025 mouse study where inulin fiber changed gut bacteria so fructose was broken down in the intestine before reaching the liver, which could partly explain it; no human trial has tested that mechanism.

Glucose plus fructose promotes colorectal cancer metastasis via SORD (Nature Metabolism)

Sep 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Cancer

A Nature Metabolism study shows the glucose and fructose combination found in sugar-sweetened beverages promotes colorectal cancer metastasis through sorbitol dehydrogenase (SORD). Louis notes large prospective cohorts have repeatedly linked sugary drinks with colon cancer and that this may explain a mechanism.

University of Michigan food study: ranking foods by minutes of healthy life gained or lost

Sep 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 6 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis points to a previous post on a University of Michigan study that scored foods by their effect on healthy life. Per the shared news text, soda costs about 12 minutes per serving while nuts, seeds and some fruit add minutes.

Nature Food 2021: swapping 10% of beef and processed meat calories adds 48 healthy minutes

Sep 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 ↩ 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis quotes the University of Michigan Nature Food analysis of more than 5,800 American foods by health and environmental impact. Soda and hot dogs subtract healthy life minutes, whereas nuts and seeds add them, and substituting a small share of red and processed meat with whole plant foods and seafood yields gains.

One sugar-sweetened soda daily for 3 weeks doubled CRP and raised small dense LDL

Apr 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 9 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares a FoundMyFitness clip citing a study in healthy young men: drinking a 20-ounce sugar-sweetened beverage daily for 3 weeks produced a 100% rise in hsCRP and a spike in small dense LDL, linking sugary drinks to inflammation and worsening insulin resistance.
