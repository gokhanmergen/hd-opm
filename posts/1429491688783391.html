Resistance training lowers liver fat and insulin resistance markers despite no weight loss

Louis Nervegna · Oct 23, 2026 · ♥ 7 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis notes that resistance training reduces liver fat and insulin resistance markers even when body weight does not fall, citing a Gut 2011 study in non-alcoholic fatty liver disease. The shared post adds that a Mediterranean-style diet and 150 minutes of walking or biking a week help reverse fatty liver.

  • Liver fat fell independent of weight change
  • 150 minutes per week of walking or biking suggested

Resistance training reduces liver fat and markers of insulin resistance, even if total body weight does not go down.

Fatty liver can scar the liver years before ALT rises; 10% weight loss resolves it in 90%

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Routine ALT testing can miss advanced fatty liver disease: a Hepatology study biopsied 51 patients with normal ALT and found 12 with bridging fibrosis and 6 with cirrhosis. The post notes selection bias in that sample but says fibrosis behind a normal ALT is well established. Ten percent body weight loss resolved fatty liver in 90 percent and regressed fibrosis in 45 percent, and Mediterranean diet, resistance training and insulin-resistance management reduce liver fat independent of weight.

Louis shares a dietitian post and note that half of people with fatty liver have normal liver enzymes, so enzymes and enzyme-based scores like FIB-4 poorly predict liver fat. He recommends FibroScan, with fatty liver diagnosed above 5% liver fat and insulin resistance linked to levels above 1.85%.

Overfeeding saturated fat raised liver fat 55%, sugar 33%, unsaturated fat 15% in an RCT

A randomized trial overfed three groups by 1000 calories for 3 weeks from butter/dairy, olive oil/nuts or added sugar. Liver fat rose 55%, 15% and 33% respectively, showing calories are not equal for the liver.
