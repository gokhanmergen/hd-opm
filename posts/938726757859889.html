Eliquis and supplements question; mother's suspected DVT proved a false positive

Louis Nervegna · Dec 30, 2024 · ♥ 10 reactions · 21 comments · original Facebook post
Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinnersSupplements & vitaminsBlood vessels, circulation & endothelium
AI summary & key facts

Louis asks whether group members take Eliquis and how supplements interact with it, since some supplements add to the anticoagulant effect and it is metabolized by CYP3A4. In an edit he reports his mother's calf DVT diagnosis was wrong: repeat ultrasound and D-dimer in a vascular clinic showed clear veins, noting ultrasound alone is only about 60% accurate for DVT.

  • Some supplements compound Eliquis's anticoagulant effect; metabolized by CYP3A4
  • Ultrasound alone is about 60% accurate for DVT, per Louis
  • Repeat ultrasound plus D-dimer by experienced staff found no DVT

Edit: It turns out this was not a DVT after all. Ultrasound is only about 60% accurate in diagnosing DVT by itself. So she had a repeat ultrasound and D-dimer test today by more experienced staff in a vascular clinic, and her deep calf veins are perfectly clear. So no DVT after all. And no more Eliquis.

——

Anybody in the group taking Eliquis?

What side effects have you experienced?

My 83yo mom was just diagnosed with a DVT in her right calf, and is starting on it until the clot resolves.

She is going to temporarily stop all supplements, except for her multivitamin and probiotic.

I worry about combining supplements with this drug. A number of supplements compound its anticoagulant effect. And it is metabolized by CYP3A4 in the cytochrome P450 which many supplements modulate.

I’d be curious if anyone does safely take any other supplements with Eliquis?
