Newer treatments available for people experiencing early signs of Alzheimer's disease highlight the need for accessible and cost-effective tests that can diagnose the disease earlier. In a new study, researchers at Mayo Clinic have affirmed the accuracy of a blood test that can be used in outpatient memory clinics.
