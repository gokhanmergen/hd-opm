PREDIMED: Mediterranean diet with olive oil or nuts cut major CV events by 30% and 28%

Louis Nervegna · Aug 26, 2025 · ♥ 10 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsHeart attack, stroke & proceduresFats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)
AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes the PREDIMED randomized trial of about 7,500 people testing the traditional Mediterranean diet for primary prevention. Groups supplemented with extra-virgin olive oil or nuts had a 30% and 28% lower rate of heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death after 5 years versus an AHA-style control diet. A shared Annals post adds that Mediterranean diet plus calorie reduction and exercise cut diabetes risk by 31% in a large trial.

  • ~7,500 participants, primary prevention
  • Composite of heart attack, stroke, CV death reduced 30% (olive oil) and 28% (nuts) after 5 years vs AHA-style diet
  • Shared Annals of Internal Medicine post: Mediterranean diet + calorie reduction + exercise lowered diabetes risk 31%

The PREDIMED study was a very large randomized controlled trial (7500 people) testing the traditional mediterranean diet as an intervention for primary prevention of heart attacks, strokes, and cardiovascular mortality.

In the mediterranean groups supplemented with extra-virgin olive oil or nuts, the main composite endpoint (heart attack, stroke, or cardiovascular death) was reduced by 30% and 28% respectively after 5 years, as compared with the control group eating an AHA style diet:

Paper · N Engl J Med · 2018Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease with a Mediterranean Diet Supplemented with Extra-Virgin Olive Oil or Nutsnejm.org

(Note that the vast majority of patients in the trial were taking statins and other medications, both in the control and intervention groups. So these benefits were in addition to the benefits already achieved from medication.)

A subsampling of the group eating nuts showed a reversal of plaque in the carotid artery:

↪ PREDIMED: nuts regressed carotid plaque and a nut-rich Mediterranean diet cut stroke 50%

In a substudy of PREDIMED designed with diabetes as the primary endpoint (PREDIMED-Reus), the incidence of diabetes was reduced by 52% after 4 years in the mediterranean groups supplemented with olive oil or nuts, as compared to the control group eating the AHA style diet:

Paper · Diabetes Care · 2011Reduction in the incidence of type 2 diabetes with the Mediterranean diet: results of the PREDIMED-Reus nutrition intervention randomized trial.Conclusion: MedDiets without calorie restriction seem to be effective in the prevention of diabetes in subjects at high cardiovascular risk.pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

A secondary analysis of the very large PREDIMED trial was just published which showed that combining the traditional mediterranean diet with exercise and calorie limitation lowered risk of diabetes by an additional 31%, compared to following the mediterranean diet alone without limiting calories (ad libitum).

This new paper shows that the benefits of the traditional mediterranean diet can be further magnified by combining it with exercise and modest calorie restriction.
