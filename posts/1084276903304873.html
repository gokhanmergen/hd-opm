Home HEPA air purifiers lowered systolic blood pressure by about 3 mm Hg (JACC)

Louis Nervegna · Aug 06, 2025 · ♥ 11 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Blood pressure, salt & potassiumEnvironment (microplastics, pollution, heat)
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a JACC study finding portable HEPA purifiers at home lowered systolic blood pressure in adults with elevated baseline readings, even in areas of relatively low pollution. He calls the effect small but real.

  • Effect size about 3 mm Hg systolic
  • Benefit seen even with low overall air pollution

It’s a small effect (3 mm Hg), but appears to be real.

Using portable high-efficiency particulate air purifiers at home can significantly lower systolic blood pressure in adults with elevated baseline readings — even in areas with relatively low overall air pollution levels, according to a study published today in #JACC.
