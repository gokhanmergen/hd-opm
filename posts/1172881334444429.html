Keto beat Mediterranean and low-fat diets on liver fat and glycemia at equal weight loss

Aug 27, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Petersen et al. (Cell Metabolism, 2026) fed 42 adults with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver all meals in a controlled trial until ~10% weight loss. All diets improved muscle insulin sensitivity and body composition, but the very-low-carb ketogenic diet gave clearly larger improvements in liver metabolic function, intrahepatic fat and glycemic control.

MeMeMe trial: metformin cut diabetes 80-92% in metabolic syndrome; Med diet did not

Aug 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

In 1,442 people with metabolic syndrome followed about 3 years, 1,700 mg/day metformin prevented type 2 diabetes, which fully explained the difference in major noncommunicable diseases. The Mediterranean diet intervention did not. Gokhan notes no heart disease or cancer prevention from metformin either.

BRIDGE trial: Mediterranean diet cut weight and visceral fat but did not improve cognition

Jul 06, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 6 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

In a 14-month randomized trial of older, mainly African American adults with obesity, an eight-month Mediterranean diet intervention improved diet quality, weight and visceral fat, but no cognitive benefit appeared versus control.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet slowed atherosclerosis and cut events vs low-fat diet

Mar 24, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 8 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A video summary of the CORDIOPREV study comparing a Mediterranean diet with a low-fat diet in coronary heart disease patients. The Mediterranean arm showed less plaque buildup, mostly in the first five years, and fewer heart attacks and strokes.
