Discussion prompt: small changes to reduce cardiovascular risk in the new year

Friends,
As we embark on a new year, many of us set goals to improve our health. What are some small, sustainable changes you're considering to reduce cardiovascular risk factors? I'm aiming to walk more by taking the stairs, parking farther away and tracking my steps daily. Share what's worked for you or where you're struggling. Let's have an open dialogue to support each other in making positive lifestyle changes. Wishing everyone motivation and perseverance in their 2024 health goals!

Lipoprotein(a) explained: 80-90% genetic, stable for life, a risk factor beyond LDL

Oct 05, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 8 Lp(a)

Explainer on Lp(a): an LDL-like particle with apo(a) attached, 80-90% genetically determined, fully expressed by 1-2 years of age, and elevated in about 1 in 5 US individuals (1.4 billion globally). Levels above 50 mg/dL or 125 nmol/L raise ASCVD risk, possibly via impaired fibrinolysis and cholesterol deposition, and may occur with normal lipid panels.
