mRNA-driven Myc and Cyclin T1 restarted heart muscle cell division after heart attack

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis shares a summary of a Nature Cardiovascular Research study from Cambridge using temporary modified mRNA to activate Myc and Cyclin T1 in heart muscle cells so mature cardiomyocytes re-enter division. The aim is to regenerate heart tissue after a heart attack instead of leaving scar tissue and chronic heart failure. This is early-stage regeneration research.

Mitoquinone (MitoQ) may reverse early heart failure signs in type 2 diabetes

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 3 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A Medical Xpress news item reports that mitoquinone, an improved form of CoQ10, showed potential to reverse early signs of heart failure in people with type 2 diabetes, improving cardiac energy efficiency and relaxation without major side effects. Larger trials are planned to confirm the result.

WOSCOPS 20-year follow-up: 5 years of pravastatin gave lasting mortality and event benefit

Apr 07, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Statins

Describes the 20-year follow-up of the West of Scotland Coronary Prevention Study: 5 years of pravastatin in middle-aged men with high cholesterol and no prior CHD produced a lasting legacy effect, with reduced all-cause, CV and coronary mortality and fewer heart failure and MI hospitalizations. No excess cancer or non-CV mortality was seen.

TARGET-D: tailored vitamin D dosing missed its main endpoint but cut heart attack 52%

Nov 10, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 25 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises TARGET-D (630 adults with acute coronary syndrome, ~4.2 years): doses adjusted to reach 40-80 ng/mL did not significantly reduce the composite of death, heart failure hospitalization or stroke, though heart attack risk was 52% lower. Authors call for validation to rule out data mining.

Rapamycin for 8 weeks improved diastolic and endothelial function in six older men

Oct 31, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 2 Aging & longevity

Summarises a proof-of-concept open-label pilot in six healthy men aged 70-76 taking rapamycin 1 mg daily for 8 weeks. It was safe and well tolerated; systolic function was unchanged but cardiac MRI showed better diastolic function (peak filling rate p=0.014) and endothelial function improved (p=0.020). Very small and uncontrolled; larger placebo-controlled trials are needed.

BMJ trial: stem cells after heart attack lowered heart failure risk in weak hearts

Oct 31, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Shares a news report of a BMJ clinical trial in which patients with weak heart function given stem cell therapy shortly after a heart attack had lower risk of heart failure and related hospital stays than with standard care. No numbers in the post.
