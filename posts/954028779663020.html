Nuts protect against CVD and diabetes and cause less weight gain than expected

Louis Nervegna · Jan 24, 2025 · ♥ 16 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a ZOE episode explaining why nuts are linked to lower CVD and diabetes risk. About 30% of the calories in nuts pass through the gut undigested, which helps explain why they cause less weight gain than their calories suggest.

  • Around 30% of calories in nuts pass through the GI tract undigested
  • Nuts are associated with protection against CVD and diabetes

Nuts protect against CVD and diabetes.

She spends a lot of time explaining why it is harder for nuts to cause weight gain than people realize, despite their higher calories. For example, around 30% of the calories in nuts pass through the GI tract undigested. A lot of the research that she cites on nuts and lack of weight gain is new to me. I had not heard of it before.
