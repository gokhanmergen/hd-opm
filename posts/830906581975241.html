In diabetes, GLP-1 drugs or metformin linked to lower cancer risk than insulin, 15 years

Louis Nervegna · Jul 05, 2024 · ♥ 12 reactions · 14 comments · original Facebook post
GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)CancerInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

A retrospective cohort study with 15 years of follow-up found that diabetic patients on GLP-1 agonists or metformin had considerably lower cancer risk than those on insulin; GLP-1 drugs lowered risk of 10 obesity-associated cancers versus insulin. The poster attributes this to insulin's growth-promoting effects.

  • Retrospective cohort, 15-year follow-up
  • GLP-1 RA lowered risk of 10 obesity-associated cancers versus insulin
  • Metformin also associated with lower cancer risk than insulin

In diabetics, use of GLP-1 agonist drugs or metformin was associated with considerably lower risk of cancer than use of insulin.

The followup period was 15 years. This was a retrospective cohort study.

(This result is not surprising, given insulin’s well-known growth promoting properties, which stimulate cancer cell division.)
