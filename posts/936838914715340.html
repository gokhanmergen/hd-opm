AHA's updated high blood pressure definition reflects trials of early treatment

Louis Nervegna · Dec 27, 2024 · ♥ 18 reactions · 27 comments · original Facebook post
Blood pressure, salt & potassiumGuidelines, policy & public health
AI summary & key facts

Louis comments on an AHA post that nearly half of US adults have high blood pressure. He explains the definition was lowered because several large RCTs showed aggressive early treatment greatly reduces long-term heart attack and stroke risk.

  • Nearly half of American adults have high blood pressure (AHA)
  • Several large RCTs support treating earlier

The latest definition of high blood pressure from the AHA.

Several very large randomized controlled trials have demonstrated that aggressively addressing high blood pressure in its earliest stages results in a large reduction in long term risk for heart attack and stroke. That’s why the definition of high blood pressure was updated.

Nearly half of American adults have high blood pressure, putting them at higher risk for stroke and heart disease. The only way to know if BP is in a normal range is to measure it. Small steps can make a big difference.
