Esselstyn's olive oil FMD claim challenged: exercise also dips FMD short term but helps

Louis Nervegna · Aug 12, 2024 · ♥ 22 reactions · 11 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

A video clip shows Gil Carvalho challenging Caldwell Esselstyn's claim that olive oil damages arteries because it temporarily suppresses flow-mediated dilation (FMD). The poster notes exercise does the same temporarily and is hormetic, and says over 30 human RCTs show olive oil improves FMD and endothelial function long term.

  • Refined olive oil temporarily suppresses FMD after ingestion
  • Exercise causes the same transient FMD drop, thought hormetic
  • Over 30 human RCTs show longer-term FMD/endothelial improvement with olive oil

Gil Carvalo politely confronts Caldwell Esselstyn about his claim that olive oil damages arteries.

Esselstyn claims that the fact that refined olive oil temporarily suppresses flow mediated dilation (FMD) in arteries after ingestion means that it damages endothelial cells.

Gil correctly points out to Esselstyn that exercise does the exact same thing.

I did an earlier post on this:

facebook.com/share/p/t1SVuqgP6WvBSNKc

Studies have shown that the temporary drop in FMD following exercise is hormetic and improves the health of arteries in the long run. The same is likely true with refined olive oil, as Gil alludes to in this video clip. I said the same thing— with references— in my previous post (link above).

Over 30 randomized trials in humans have shown that olive oil improves flow mediated dilation (FMD) and endothelial function (EF) in the longer term:

Meta-analysis of 30 trials: olive oil lowers CRP and IL-6 and improves FMD

Mar 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 6 ↩ 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis argues that over 30 human RCTs show olive oil improves flow-mediated dilation and endothelial function, quoting a systematic review of 30 studies and 3,106 participants. Olive oil lowered CRP by 0.64 mg/L and IL-6 by 0.29 and raised FMD by 0.76 percentage points versus controls. Refined olive oil may briefly impair FMD after a meal, while EVOO improves it acutely and over the long term.
