Fish oil dosing: aiming for 3 g EPA and 1 g DHA per day

Gokhan Can · Nov 07, 2024 · ♥ 4 reactions · 7 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsFats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan shares his fish oil plan, combining mixed EPA-DHA capsules with pure EPA for 3 g EPA, 1 g DHA and 4.9 g total omega-3 daily. He notes EPA is the main vascular contributor but DHA may also help, and asks for others' thoughts.

  • 3 g EPA/day
  • 1 g DHA/day
  • 4.9 g total omega-3/day

Fish oil dosage. What are your thoughts? Here's what I'm aiming for these days:

  • Mixed EPA-DHA supplement x N capsules
  • Pure EPA supplement x M capsules
  • Totaling 3g EPA/day, 1g DHA/day, 4.9g/day of omega 3.

It appears that DHA has some benefits too, though EPA is the main contributor to vascular health.
