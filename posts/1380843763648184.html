Louis shares another study linking the shingles vaccine to lower dementia risk, notable because it examined the newer Shingrix rather than Zostavax.   • Over 500,000 Medicare recipients aged 66+
  • 24% lower dementia risk over four years with at least one Shingrix dose

Yet another study links the shingles vaccine with lower risk of dementia. This one was just published.

What is notable about this particular study is that it analyzed the newer shingles vaccine Shingrix, whereas previous studies mostly focused on the older vaccine Zostavax.

BONUS PROTECTION: The shingles vaccine has been linked to a 24% lower risk of dementia in older adults over a four-year period.

Brown University researchers analyzed health records of more than 500,000 Medicare recipients aged 66 and older and found those who received at least one dose of Shingrix saw a significant reduction in cognitive decline compared to unvaccinated individuals.

A clinical neurologist says this is the third or fourth study to reach the same conclusion — calling the finding "icing on the cake" atop a growing body of evidence.
