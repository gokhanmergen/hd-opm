Plant-based diets lower CVD risk but may modestly raise hemorrhagic stroke risk

Louis Nervegna · Jun 18, 2024 · ♥ 7 reactions · 32 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsHeart attack, stroke & procedures
AI summary & key facts

The poster flags that plant-based diets, though linked to lower CVD, may modestly increase hemorrhagic stroke risk. He cautions this is observational and may be confounded, noting lifetime heart attack risk is about 20% versus about 3% for hemorrhagic stroke.

  • Lifetime heart attack risk ~20%; hemorrhagic stroke ~3% in the US
  • Observational, cannot prove causality

Plant-based diets have many benefits, including lower risk of CVD, but they may modestly raise the risk for hemorrhagic stroke.

This is something to be aware of.

Disclaimer: This is an observational study that can not prove causality. Often results like this are explained by non-causal factors like confounding. In the USA, the lifetime risk of having a heart attack is around 20%. The lifetime risk for hemorrhagic stroke is around 3%.

Swapping refined carbs for unsaturated fat lowers LDL: meta-analysis of 60 feeding trials

Oct 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A meta-analysis of 60 controlled human feeding trials found that replacing carbohydrate calories with monounsaturated or polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL cholesterol, while saturated fat raises it. The effect looked largest for polyunsaturated fat, which is why choosing oils and nuts over refined starch and sugar helps the lipid profile.
