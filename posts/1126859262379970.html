Higher adipose linoleic acid linked to lower CVD mortality, countering seed-oil claims

Louis Nervegna · Oct 03, 2025 · ♥ 7 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)Myths, misinformation & controversies
AI summary & key facts

A shared critique argues that influencers dismiss research showing higher adipose-tissue linoleic acid (omega-6) is associated with lower cardiovascular and all-cause mortality. It says the contradicting evidence was lower quality and excluded from the meta-analysis for not meeting inclusion criteria.

  • Higher adipose-tissue linoleic acid associated with lower CVD and all-cause mortality
  • Contrary studies were of lower quality and did not meet meta-analysis inclusion criteria

A perfect example of how influencers blatantly lie about seed oils.

Shared from Dr. Matthew Nagra, ND · original post

When @paulsaladinomd is faced with research destroying his anti-seed oil position, he engages in some serious mental gymnastics. He claims that adipose tissue linoleic acid (omega-6 fat in seed oils) is a good marker of dietary intake, and the research shows that higher levels are associated with lower risks of cardiovascular (and all-cause) mortality.

Instead of acknowledging that his position is flawed, he tries to claim that “other research” (which is of much lower quality and looking at a completely different outcome) was left out of a meta-analysis (it didn’t meet the inclusion criteria) and then commits a false balance fallacy to suggest the data is “conflicting and confusing.” The reality is, it’s only confusing to those who don’t understand how to assess study quality.

Replacing 10 g/day of butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality

Louis shares Harvard research on 221,054 people from the Nurses' Health Studies I and II and the Health Professionals Follow-Up Study, published in JAMA Internal Medicine. Higher plant-oil intake (especially soybean, canola, olive) was linked to lower total, cancer and cardiovascular mortality, while butter was linked to higher total and cancer mortality; a modeled swap of 10 g butter for plant oils lowered mortality by 17%.
