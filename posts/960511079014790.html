Ketosis does not prove humans are carnivores; most carnivore dieters are not in ketosis

Louis argues all primates can enter ketosis as a starvation defense, so it is not evidence of a carnivorous evolution. He claims high protein intake and insulin prevent ketosis on carnivore diets unless fasting, needing about 60-65% of calories from fat.

  • Ketosis needs roughly 60-65% of calories from fat, per Louis

Ketosis is not evidence that humans are carnivores.

That idea— promoted by carnivore influencers— is laughably ridiculous.

All primates have the ability to operate in ketosis, even though they eat little to no meat. It protects them against starvation.

(Modern humans are the only primate that eats large amounts of meat.)

Primates evolved the ability to operate in ketosis in order to survive off stored fat when zero food was available. It also allows us to survive primarily on dietary fat as a side effect. No other primate except humans uses ketosis to survive primarily on dietary fat, even though they theoretically could if forced.

Most people on carnivore diets can’t even get into ketosis in the first place— because all the extra protein they eat is converted by the liver into glucose. And because of the huge post-prandial insulin spikes that animal protein cause. This blocks ketosis.

↪ Beef and whey raise insulin more than pasta, oatmeal or glucose: use the insulin index

You need to be eating at least 60-65% of calories from fat to be in ketosis, which leaves only 30-35% for protein. Most people on carnivore diets eat way more protein than that, which prevents the them from ever getting into ketosis in the first place.

The only way to get into ketosis by eating that much protein is to fast— but fasting will get you into ketosis no matter what you eat.

↪ Fasting is the most effective way to generate ketones, without eating a keto diet

The carnivore influencers claiming to be in ketosis while eating 60% or more of their calories from protein are doing so by fasting. They are cheating the test by doing it after a fast.
