Esselstyn's diet reduces risk but is not a guaranteed cure, Louis says

Louis Nervegna · Nov 14, 2025 · ♥ 20 reactions · 54 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Louis criticizes claims that everyone following the diet halts heart disease, citing group members who progressed, often refusing medication.

  • Healthy diet reduces risk; it is not a cure

This is why I find it hard to take anything that he says seriously.

He asserts that EVERYONE who is compliant with his diet sees the progression of their heart disease completely STOP. In other words, that his diet is the cure for heart disease.

This is untrue. I’ve seen many cases of people in these online health groups who followed his diet religiously, yet still saw progression of their heart disease. In most cases, these people refused to take medication, believing his diet was a magic cure.

The reality is that it is a healthy diet that reduces risk. It is not a cure:

↪ Esselstyn's plant-based diet is supported only by case series, not high-level evidence

The sad truth is that Caldwell Esselstyn is not a scientist. He is an ideologue. He has been cherry picking his best cases for years pretending like this is the norm, while simultaneously ignoring the failures. He blaims every single failure on non-compliance.

This is not science. This is ideology.

Yes, there are some great success stories. But many people still see their disease progress on the Esselstyn diet, especially if they refuse proper medical treatment.
