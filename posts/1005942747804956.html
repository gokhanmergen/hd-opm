Ezetimibe beyond statins: fewer events, no mortality gain in trials, registry benefits

Nov 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Evidence digest on ezetimibe. A meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (123,785 patients) found add-on lipid lowering cut cardiovascular events 19% versus statins alone but did not improve all-cause mortality. Other data show moderate statin plus ezetimibe matches high-intensity statins, and registry studies hint at lower mortality, with strong LDL-target attainment.

Rosuvastatin lowers LDL slightly more than atorvastatin; LODESTAR outcomes were similar

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 10 Statins

Comparison of the two high-intensity statins: both lower LDL-C by 50% or more, rosuvastatin is more potent per milligram, but in the randomized LODESTAR trial 3-year clinical outcomes did not differ significantly. The goal is reaching the appropriate LDL target on maximally tolerated therapy.

FLOWPROMOTE: combination therapy shrank soft plaque 28% vs 20% with LDL near 42 vs 70

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A shared post on FLOWPROMOTE (JAMA Cardiology, Oct 30, 2026): 104 statin-naive people with coronary narrowing were scanned with CT, then given atorvastatin 40 mg or rosuvastatin 40 mg plus ezetimibe 10 mg. After 18 months LDL fell to 42.5 versus 69.5 mg/dL, and soft plaque shrank 28.2% versus 20.0%. Louis shares it with hashtags only.

One zoledronate infusion cut vertebral fractures about 44% over 10 years in women 50-60

Gokhan shares the NEJM 10-year randomized trial of infrequent zoledronate in early postmenopausal women (50-60 years) with normal-to-low-normal bone density. Zoledronate roughly halved new morphometric vertebral fractures versus placebo, and he calls it a longevity-type drug like statins or aspirin. The trial concerns bone rather than heart outcomes.
