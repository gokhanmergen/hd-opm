Shared from Vital Human- Dr. Daniel Chong · original post

Oats are one of those foods where the average social media opinion on their health effects is often far from reality. The classic approach of “anti-oat” health influencers is to point out components of oats that in mechanistic (vs real world) studies show they may cause harm, and then completely ignore the literal mountain of real world observational and interventional data showing how beneficial they are.
