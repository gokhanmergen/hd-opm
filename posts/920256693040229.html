Grounding lacks scientific backing; look at the totality of evidence

Gokhan Can · Nov 30, 2024 · ♥ 12 reactions · 41 comments · original Facebook post
Myths, misinformation & controversiesHow to read research
AI summary & key facts

Gokhan warns that 'grounding' is a dubious practice without scientific support and that one paper is never enough, reposting a FoundMyFitness note that better-proven options for inflammation and oxidative stress include omega-3s, exercise and sulforaphane. No studies are cited.

  • One paper is not sufficient for a scientific inference
  • Better-proven options: omega-3s, exercise, sulforaphane

Please look for scientific evidence, and scrutinize deeply (if at all possible) during these dangerous times where anything goes on the internet. Grounding is one of those dubious practices which lacks scientific backing as far as I can tell. Usually one paper isn't sufficient to make a scientific inference. You need to look at the totality of evidence (and develop a strong sense of what constitutes valid scientific evidence).

Shared from FoundMyFitness · original post

Proponents of "grounding" claim it offers a variety of health benefits, but the scientific evidence just isn't there

If you're trying to reduce inflammation and oxidative stress, you have way more potent (and proven) options:

  • Omega-3 fatty acids
  • Exercise
  • Sulforaphane
