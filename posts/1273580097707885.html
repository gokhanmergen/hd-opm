The MIND diet blends Mediterranean and DASH for brain health

Louis Nervegna · Apr 22, 2026 · ♥ 9 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward dietsBrain health & dementia
AI summary & key facts

Louis explains MIND stands for Mediterranean-DASH Intervention for Neurodegenerative Delay, recommended by neurologists and also lowers heart disease risk.

  • Combines Mediterranean and DASH elements

The MIND diet is designed and tested to promote brain health and reduce the risk of developing neurological diseases like Alzheimer's and Parkinson’s.

You can read more about it by searching the group for the tag #minddietHD.

The acronym MIND stands for Mediterranean-DASH Intervention for Neurodegenerative Delay.

It is the diet most recommended by neurologists who treat these diseases, as it has the strongest evidence to date from randomized controlled trials and prospective cohort studies. It combines elements of the Mediterranean and DASH diets, emphasizing natural plant-based foods, while limiting unhealthy ones. It is also a diet that is proven to reduce the risk of heart disease and diabetes.

MIND Diet Guidelines (Quick Reference)

Brain-healthy foods (eat more):

  • Green leafy vegetables (6+ servings/week)
  • Other vegetables (1+ per day)
  • Berries (especially blueberries & strawberries, 2+ servings/ week)
  • Whole grains (3/day)
  • Nuts (5/week)
  • Olive oil (main fat)
  • Fish (1+/week)
  • Legumes (3+/week)
  • Poultry (2+/week)
  • Wine (optional, 1/day)

Limit these:

  • Processed meat
  • Red meat
  • Butter & margarine made with trans fat
  • Full-fat cheese
  • Whole milk / cream
  • Pastries / sweets
  • Ultraprocessed & fast foods
  • Foods fried in saturated or trans fat

7-Day MIND Diet Plan

Day 1

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with blueberries and walnuts
  • Lunch: Quinoa salad with chickpeas, spinach, tomatoes, olive oil vinaigrette
  • Snack: Handful of almonds
  • Dinner: Grilled salmon, steamed broccoli, brown rice
  • Optional: 1 glass of red wine

Day 2

  • Breakfast: Whole grain toast with avocado and boiled egg
  • Lunch: Lentil soup with side of mixed greens
  • Snack: Greek yogurt with raspberries
  • Dinner: Stir-fried tofu with kale, bell peppers, and quinoa

Day 3

  • Breakfast: Smoothie with spinach, banana, blueberries, flaxseeds
  • Lunch: Turkey lettuce wraps with hummus and veggie sticks
  • Snack: A pear and a few walnuts
  • Dinner: Baked trout, roasted carrots, farro

Day 4

  • Breakfast: Greek yogurt parfait with granola and strawberries
  • Lunch: Chickpea and kale stew
  • Snack: Baby carrots and hummus
  • Dinner: Grilled chicken breast, sautéed greens, sweet potato

Day 5

  • Breakfast: Whole grain cereal with almond milk and blueberries
  • Lunch: Black bean and corn salad
  • Snack: Handful of pistachios
  • Dinner: Baked cod, quinoa, asparagus

Day 6

  • Breakfast: Scrambled eggs with spinach and mushrooms, whole grain toast
  • Lunch: Tuna salad over mixed greens with olive oil dressing
  • Snack: Apple with almond butter
  • Dinner: Roasted turkey, mashed cauliflower, steamed green beans

Day 7

  • Breakfast: Chia pudding with mixed berries
  • Lunch: Mediterranean lentil salad
  • Snack: Mixed nuts
  • Dinner: Grilled shrimp, brown rice, zucchini
