Hyperkalemia can stop the heart, so kidney disease patients must watch potassium

Excess potassium in the blood can stop your heart.

This is why people with kidney problems have to be especially careful about dietary intake of potassium.

#potassiumHD #kidneysHD

Shared from InsideBiology · original post

Your kidneys play a vital role in keeping your heart beating normally.

They continuously regulate potassium levels in your blood. Potassium is essential for normal nerve signalling, muscle contraction and the electrical activity of the heart.

When kidney function declines, potassium can accumulate in the bloodstream, causing hyperkalemia. Severe hyperkalemia can disrupt the heart's electrical rhythm and lead to life-threatening cardiac arrhythmias.
