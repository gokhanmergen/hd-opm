Olive oil raised GLP-1 and lowered post-meal glucose versus butter in type 1 diabetes

Aug 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Olive oil, like all fats, raises GLP-1, and the quoted crossover data suggest it does so more than butter, helping people feel full sooner. After a high-glycemic meal in people with type 1 diabetes, glucose exposure was lower with EVOO than with butter or a low-fat meal, plausibly via slower gastric emptying and glucose-lipid competition.
