Optimal Life's Essential 8 health offset dementia risk in type 2 diabetes over 13 years

Louis Nervegna · Dec 03, 2025 · ♥ 8 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Brain health & dementiaInsulin resistance & diabetesCardiovascular disease: the big picture
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares an AHA release showing that adults with type 2 diabetes and high genetic dementia risk who had moderate or high Life's Essential 8 cardiovascular health had much lower risk of mild cognitive impairment and dementia over 13 years. The eight factors are blood pressure, cholesterol, fasting glucose, smoking, BMI, exercise, diet and sleep.

  • Life's Essential 8: BP, cholesterol, glucose, smoking, BMI, exercise, diet, sleep
  • Moderate/high vs low CV health: greatly lower MCI and dementia risk over 13 years
  • Held among people with type 2 diabetes and high genetic dementia risk

The same lifestyle factors that protect diabetics from heart disease also seem to protect them from dementia:

The 8 lifestyle factors that need to be optimized to potentially gain this protection are: blood pressure, cholesterol, fasting glucose, smoking, BMI, exercise, diet, sleep.

Quoted:

“Optimal cardiovascular health, based on the American Heart Association’s Life’s Essential 8 metrics, may decrease the risk of mild cognitive impairment and dementia for adults with Type 2 diabetes.

Among adults with both Type 2 diabetes and a high genetic risk for dementia, researchers found that having moderate or high cardiovascular health, compared to having low cardiovascular health, greatly lowered the risk of developing mild cognitive impairment and dementia over 13 years.”

Shared from Dale Bredesen, MD · original post

Diabetes has long been associated with dementia, which is a disease of brain energetics and inflammation. Epidemiological studies show that better cardiovascular health is associated with improved cognitive health, and new research indicates that it offsets dementia risk in people with type 2 diabetes.
