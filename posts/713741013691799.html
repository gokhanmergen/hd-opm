Peter Attia's current supplement stack

Gokhan Can · Nov 23, 2023 · ♥ 8 reactions · 14 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Summarizes the supplements Peter Attia says he takes: B6, high-EPA fish oil aimed at a red-cell EPA+DHA level near 12%, vitamin D 5,000 IU, several forms of magnesium, methylfolate and B12 to keep homocysteine below nine, and baby aspirin. This is a personal regimen, not evidence.

  • Fish oil about 2 g EPA and 1.5 g DHA daily, aiming for RBC EPA+DHA near 12%
  • Vitamin D 5,000 IU daily; magnesium about 1 g elemental total
  • Methylfolate and methyl B12 to keep homocysteine below 9

The main supplements Attia currently takes.

Vitamin B6:

  • Initially: 50mg daily.
  • Adjusted to: 50mg three times a week due to potential neuropathy concerns and re-evaluation of B6 needs.

Fish Oil (EPA & DHA):

  • Brand: Carlson's (highest EPA version).
  • Intake: 4 capsules daily (around 2g of EPA and 1.5g of DHA).
  • Aim: Achieve a red blood cell membrane concentration of EPA and DHA of about 12%.

Vitamin D:

  • Intake: 5,000 IU daily.
  • Brand: Not specified.
  • Mentioned the need for more rigorous studies on vitamin D's benefits.

Magnesium:

  • Types: Slow Mag, Magnesium L-Threonate, Magnesium Oxide.
  • Aim: About 1g of total/elemental magnesium daily.

Folate & B12:

  • Type: Methyl folate and Methyl B12.
  • Brand: Jarrow.
  • Dosage: Standard dose.
  • Aim: Maintain homocysteine below nine.

Baby Aspirin:

  • Intake: 1 a day.
  • Purpose: Cardio protection (though he admits the evidence is weak).

Night Supplements:

  • Ashwagandha: 600mg (Brand: Solgar).
  • Glycine: 2g (Brand: Thorne).
  • Magnesium L-Threonate: NOW Foods brand with "Magtein".
  • Occasionally, for travel: Phosphatidylserine from Jarrow's (400mg).

Others:

  • Athletic Greens (AG1): Taken in the morning.
  • Probiotic: "Glucose Control" by Pendulum (2 in the morning with AG1).

Personalized vitamin D3 dosing halved repeat heart attacks in an Intermountain trial

Nov 11, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

A shared summary describes an Intermountain Health trial of 630 recent heart attack survivors in which vitamin D3 was dosed individually to keep blood levels above 40 ng/mL. Repeat heart attacks were lower, though total major cardiovascular events were not, and larger trials are needed.

A shared summary of a University of Surrey, John Innes Centre and Quadram Institute study reporting that vitamin D2 supplementation can reduce D3 levels below those of people taking no supplements. D3 is the more effective form.
