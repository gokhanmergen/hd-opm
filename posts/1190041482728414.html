Statins, bempedoic acid, ezetimibe, fibrates and PCSK9 inhibitors all lower hsCRP

Louis Nervegna · Dec 28, 2025 · ♥ 23 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
Inflammation & CRPLDL, ApoB & lipid panelPCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs
AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes meta-analysis data on average hsCRP reductions from lipid-lowering drugs, noting part of the effect is that high LDL itself is inflammatory.

  • Statins -17.3%
  • Bempedoic acid -20%
  • Ezetimibe -3.2%
  • Fibrates -10.3%
  • PCSK9 inhibitors -6%; fish oil about -12.8%

Statins, bempedoic acid, ezetimibe, fibrates, and PCSK9 inhibitors, all lower inflammation and hsCRP.

Multiple trials have proven this.

Here are the average amounts by which they lower hsCRP:

Statins - 17.3%
Bempedoic acid - 20%
Ezetimibe - 3.2%
Fibrates - 10.3%
PCSK9i - 6%

(For comparison, fish oil lowers hsCRP by about 12.8%.)

Part of the reason why they all do this is because high LDL itself can be inflammatory. When LDL gets inside the arterial wall and oxidizes, it attracts immune cells which generate inflammation. This raises levels of hsCRP and other inflammatory markers.
