Plaque starts in neck and leg arteries decades before the heart; calcium scores miss it

Sep 17, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post summarises a video describing an NEJM study of 16,000 asymptomatic people showing plaque accumulates first in the neck and leg arteries, during the 20s to 40s, before the heart. Heart scans such as coronary calcium may therefore miss early disease; carotid intima-media thickness ultrasound can detect peripheral plaque, and the ankle-brachial index offers a simple at-home check for leg artery blockage.

New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Aug 31, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 38 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified. Carvalho argues calcium stabilises plaque, so slowing calcification without reducing plaque could even be harmful, and no evidence shows K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, so he advises relying on proven therapies, diet and exercise.
