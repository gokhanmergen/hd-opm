Shared from Genuinely Healthy · original post

According to a 2006 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial published in Osteoarthritis and Cartilage, 50 men and women aged 40–76 years with knee osteoarthritis who took methylsulfonylmethane (MSM) at a dose of 3 grams twice daily (6 grams per day total) for 12 weeks experienced significant reductions in knee pain and physical function impairment compared with participants who received a placebo.

Researchers measured these improvements using the WOMAC assessment, a standard tool for evaluating osteoarthritis symptoms, and found that MSM helped participants move more comfortably and perform daily activities more easily. The study also reported improvements in overall physical functioning on the SF-36 quality-of-life assessment, while no major adverse events were observed during the trial, suggesting that MSM was well tolerated over the 12-week treatment period.

PMID: 16309928
