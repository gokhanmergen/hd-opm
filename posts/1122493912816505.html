Quote on holding beliefs on evidence while staying open to revision

Louis Nervegna · Sep 28, 2025 · ♥ 25 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
How to read research
AI summary & key facts

Louis uses a quote to urge group members to base health beliefs on high-quality scientific evidence and to stay open to updating them. No specific research is discussed.

This quote directly applies to beliefs about health and medical topics.

It’s important to have sound reasons for what you believe, based on high-quality scientific evidence.

It’s also important to keep an open mind and not believe anything dogmatically. Always be open to adjusting your beliefs based on newer and better evidence.
