DISCO-CT diet-plus-medication group showed plaque reversal, unlike progression in Keto-CTA

Louis Nervegna · Jul 17, 2025 · ♥ 6 reactions · 6 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyLow-carb, keto & carnivoreLDL, ApoB & lipid panel
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a comparison of non-calcified plaque volume changes across several CT angiography studies. The DISCO-CT intervention group (statins, other medications and a DASH diet) showed significant plaque reversal at one year, while the Keto-CTA group of lean mass hyper-responders with LDL-C around 237 mg/dL showed progression. The linked Keto-CTA longitudinal paper is retracted per link metadata.

  • DISCO-CT intervention: standard medication plus strict DASH diet, significant plaque reversal at 1 year
  • Keto-CTA: lean mass hyper-responders, mean LDL-C 237 mg/dL, age 55.5
  • NATURE-CT healthy individuals LDL-C 111.6 mg/dL
  • Plotted outcome: non-calcified plaque volume (NCPV)

I copied this post from the LMHR group.

It is referring to the Keto-CTA study, which tested the effects of a keto diet on arterial plaque in a cohort of lean metabolically-healthy individuals with very high LDL and low TG/HDL (LMHR).

It compares plaque progression (NCPV) in the Keto-CTA study to multiple other studies.

The best results came from the intervention group in the DISCO study. This group showed significant plaque REVERSAL after one year, using statins, other medications, and a DASH diet very similar to the traditional mediterranean diet. See graph in photo.

The Keto-CTA group saw significant plaque progression.

Here are the studies being compared in the graph in the photo:

  • NATURE-CT: Healthy individuals, no special diet (age: 54.9 ‡ 10.2 yrs, LDL-C: 111.6 mg/dL)
  • KETO-CTA: Lean Mass Hyper-Responders on ketogenic diet (age: 55.5 yrs, LDL-C: 237 mg/dL) - lean, no diabetes, no high BP, no CVD risk factors except high LDL
  • Nakanishi: Non-diabetic vs. diabetic patients, standard diets (age: not reported, LDL-C: not reported)
  • SMARTool: Multi-center project, clinical characteristics and statin use impact (age: not reported, LDL-C: 110 ‡ 42 mg/dL)
  • DISCO-CT Control: Advanced heart disease, standard medication (age: 60 ‡ 7.7 yrs, LDL-C: not reported)
  • DISCO-CT Intervention: Advanced heart disease, standard medication + strict DASH diet (age: 60 ‡ 7.7 yrs, LDL-C: not reported)

Repatha alone reversed soft plaque in 37 of 47 patients over 18 months at LDL near 30

Nov 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis explains a small uncontrolled study of the PCSK9 inhibitor evolocumab with CT angiography. Average LDL was about 30 mg/dL, 37 of 47 patients had some non-calcified plaque regression, and many had large reversals worth years of typical growth (about 5 mm3 per year). He cautions it was small with no randomization or control group, and that PCSK9 monotherapy is not yet proven to reduce heart attacks.
