Shared from Fact Point · original post

When you're hungry—especially during periods of fasting—your body initiates a natural process called autophagy. This is a cellular "cleanup" mechanism where damaged, sick, or aging cells are broken down and recycled. Autophagy helps improve overall health, boost immunity, and may even slow aging. It's a survival strategy that not only conserves energy during food scarcity but also maintains cellular health by removing harmful buildup.
