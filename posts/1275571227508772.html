More than 3 servings of refined grains a day tied to worse heart failure outcomes

Medscape reports a JACC: Heart Failure study linking over 3 daily refined grain servings to higher hospitalization risk, while vegetables and legumes were associated with better outcomes.

  • >3 refined grain servings/day: higher hospitalization
  • Vegetables and legumes: fewer complications

Not exactly a shocker…

(They’re not good for people WITHOUT heart failure either.)

Research cited

  • Refined grains and heart failure outcomes · cohort · JACC: Heart Failure
Shared from Medscape · original post

A new study published in JACC: Heart Failure indicates that consuming more than three servings of refined grains per day is associated with a significantly higher risk of hospitalization and adverse clinical outcomes. Conversely, patients who prioritized a diet rich in vegetables and legumes showed improved health metrics and a lower incidence of heart failure complications. No long-lived population ate a carbohydrate-free diet; long-term effects unknown

Jun 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 4 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis argues that every long-lived population studied includes carbohydrate from vegetables, fruits, legumes, nuts, seeds, whole grains or fermented dairy, and that the few carbohydrate-free-eating groups, such as certain Eskimo populations, were not notably long-lived. He says low-carb diets may help short-term but long-term heart-disease effects are speculative, so he suggests including low-GI carbohydrates.
