Biggest US heart disease death declines were in ages 55-64, men and African Americans

Louis Nervegna · Sep 14, 2025 · ♥ 9 reactions · 3 comments · original Facebook post
Cardiovascular disease: the big picture
AI summary & key facts

Louis notes that over the last 60 years, the sharpest reductions in US heart disease death rates were among people aged 55-64, men and African Americans. The cited paper adds that premature heart disease mortality in adults 25-64 fell 70% since 1968 but has stagnated since 2011.

  • Premature mortality (ages 25-64) down 70% since 1968
  • Rate has been stagnant from 2011 on
  • Still nearly 1 in 5 deaths in that age group in 2017

The most striking reductions in death rates from heart disease in the USA over the last 60 years have been in people aged 55-64yo, and in men and African Americans.
