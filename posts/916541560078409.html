Sitosterolemia may be more common than doctors realize, WSJ essay says

Louis Nervegna · Nov 24, 2024 · ♥ 5 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Plaque, CAC & CT angiographyLDL, ApoB & lipid panelRisk assessment & lab tests
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a Wall Street Journal essay about a patient with clogged arteries despite good health, found to have sitosterolemia. People with this condition over-absorb plant sterols from the gut, and high blood sterol levels then cause plaque. The article suggests it may be underdiagnosed.

  • Sitosterolemia: over-absorption of sterols in the GI tract
  • High blood sterol levels cause plaque
  • Article suggests the condition may be more common than recognized

I thought that this condition Sitosterolemia was rare until Stephen Marolda posted a link to this article. The article says that it may be a lot more common than doctors realize.

People with this condition over-absorb sterols in the GI tract. High levels in the blood then cause plaque.
