PREDIMED nut eaters had about 40% lower total and cancer mortality in under 5 years

Louis Nervegna · Mar 03, 2026 · ♥ 42 reactions · 14 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Louis highlights that no drug has been shown to cut both total and cancer mortality by about 40% in healthy people within 5 years, yet this was seen with nuts in PREDIMED. He stresses the caveat that it was a post-hoc observational analysis of an RCT.

  • About 40% lower total and cancer mortality with nuts
  • Occurred in under 5 years
  • Post-hoc analysis; hints at but cannot prove causation

Nuts are a life extension drug.

See this older post:
↪ Nut eating >3 times a week tied to 39% lower all-cause mortality in PREDIMED

There’s no medication that can reduce both total mortality and cancer mortality by 40% in healthy people. This happened in less than 5 years in PREDIMED with nuts! If there were a pharmaceutical that could do this, it would be a blockbuster.

How much would you pay for a drug that reduces your chances of dying from cancer by 40%? And what if they also told you that it reduces your chances of dying from any cause by 40%? People would pay a fortune.

(The caveat here is that this was a post-hoc observational analysis of the PREDIMED randomized controlled trial. It can’t prove causality, but it can strongly hint at it.)

Swapping 1 tbsp butter for plant oil linked to 17% lower cancer and all-cause death

Sep 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 12 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares an AHA press release on Harvard cohort data (Nurses' Health Study I and II, Health Professionals) for 221,054 adults. Replacing a daily tablespoon of butter with unsaturated plant oil was associated with lower cancer and total mortality, and each extra tablespoon of butter with higher cancer risk. Olive, canola and soybean oil showed the biggest benefits.

Replacing 10 g/day of butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality

Oct 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares Harvard research on 221,054 people from the Nurses' Health Studies I and II and the Health Professionals Follow-Up Study, published in JAMA Internal Medicine. Higher plant-oil intake (especially soybean, canola, olive) was linked to lower total, cancer and cardiovascular mortality, while butter was linked to higher total and cancer mortality; a modeled swap of 10 g butter for plant oils lowered mortality by 17%.

Plant oils instead of butter linked to lower mortality in 200,000 people over 30 years

Sep 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A Mass General Brigham study following more than 200,000 people for 30 years found diets higher in butter and low in plant oils were associated with elevated risk of premature death, while replacing butter with plant oils was linked to lower mortality.
