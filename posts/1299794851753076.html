COSMOS: cocoa flavanols improved cognition only in older adults with low baseline intake

Louis Nervegna · May 24, 2026 · ♥ 4 reactions · 2 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsBrain health & dementiaSupplements & vitamins
AI summary & key facts

Louis summarizes the Harvard COSMOS randomized trial: cocoa flavanol supplements showed cognitive benefit, particularly for age-related memory loss, in people whose diets were poor in flavanols at baseline, not in those with normal intake.

  • Benefit seen only in low baseline flavanol intake
  • Especially for age-related memory loss

In the COSMOS randomized trial at Harvard, a cocoa flavanol supplement showed cognitive benefits in older people IF they had low baseline intake to begin with.

This was especially true for age-related memory loss.

People with more normal intakes at baseline did not see benefits.

Is dark chocolate good for your brain?

In the latest episode of Change Your Mind, a new podcast all about how to look after your brain, we spoke to Professor JoAnn Manson, from Harvard University.

She is a world-leading expert on nutrition and brain health, and has conducted a huge trial on the effects of cocoa-flavanols, an antioxidant found in dark chocolate. While the trial did find a positive effect, the answer isn’t as simple as eating lots of chocolate.
