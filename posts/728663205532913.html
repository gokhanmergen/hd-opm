Case: carotid plaque regression with salmon, niacin and low-dose rosuvastatin

AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a video by Dr. Ford Brewer describing his own plaque reversal. He ate salmon daily, added niacin and low-dose rosuvastatin and switched from an ARB to an ACE inhibitor, with a major decrease in carotid plaque volume and CIMT arterial age falling from 72 to 52. It is a single self-reported case in a video from a clinic that also sells services and supplements.

What did he do:

Ate salmon every day (for omega 3), added niacin (nicotinic acid), added low dose statin (rosouvastatin), switched from an ARB to an ACEi for blood pressure.

Results:

Major decrease in plaque volume in the carotid atery. Arterial age dropped from 72 to 52 as measured on CIMT.

Adventist study: vegans 85+ had higher neurological death risk than non-vegetarians

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Among AHS-2 participants (about 88,000, over 12,000 deaths), vegans aged 85 and older had higher risk of death from stroke (17%), dementia (13%) and Parkinson's (37%) than healthy non-vegetarians, while pesco-vegetarians had the lowest risk. The comparison was against very healthy non-vegetarians, not the standard American diet.
