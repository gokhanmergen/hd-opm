High-dose vitamin B1 (benfotiamine) is being tested in a trial for Alzheimer's

Louis Nervegna · Mar 10, 2026 · ♥ 6 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsBrain health & dementia
AI summary & key facts

Louis notes high-dose vitamin B1 may slow Alzheimer's and a large trial is testing benfotiamine, a fat-soluble B1, which may reach the brain better. Evidence is not yet in.

  • Trial uses benfotiamine

High dose vitamin B1 supplementation may slow the progression of Alzheimer’s disease.

A large scale clinical trial is now underway to test this hypothesis. The study is using a synthetic fat-soluble form of vitamin B1 (benfotiamine), which the researchers believe will better penetrate into the brain than ordinary thiamine.
