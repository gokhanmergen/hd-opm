Group reminder: cite studies and favor top-tier evidence

Louis Nervegna · Feb 02, 2025 · ♥ 28 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Group news & communityHow to read research
AI summary & key facts

An admin reminder that members should cite studies so they can be read and should prioritize high-level evidence. Group housekeeping with no medical content.

Reminder:

If you are going to reference a study, please provide a citation so we can actually look it up and read it firsthand.

This is a science-based group. We don’t take people’s words on things— especially influencers— we look up the study and read it for ourselves. This is the only way to know for sure what conclusion can be drawn from the study, if any.

Please try to prioritize studies near the top of the evidence hierarchy in medicine. These are the most reliable, and the only studies that we should be making important health decisions based upon.
