Louis links the full PREDIMED NEJM publication and corrects an AI summary: the control diet was essentially the AHA diet, not low-fat.

  • Control diet: AHA-style, lower fat than the Mediterranean diets

The full publication of the PREDIMED trial results in the NEJM can be found here:

Paper · N Engl J Med · 2018Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease with a Mediterranean Diet Supplemented with Extra-Virgin Olive Oil or Nutsnejm.org

(Correction to the AI summary in the photos: The control diet was essentially the American Heart Association diet at the time. It was not a low fat diet, although it was lower in fat than the traditional Mediterranean diets used in the study.)

Meta-analysis of 4 Mediterranean diet RCTs: MACE down 48%, CVD death down 46%

Jan 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 20 ↩ 18 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes a 2024 meta-analysis pooling four long RCTs (Lyon, Indo-Mediterranean, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV; 10,054 participants) comparing a Mediterranean diet to control diets. It lowered major cardiovascular events, MI, stroke and CV death but not all-cause mortality or revascularization.

PREDIMED: more olive oil linked to 48% lower CVD mortality; each 10 g/day cut CVD 10%

Jun 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 42 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis reports a PREDIMED analysis in which participants with the highest olive oil intake had much lower cardiovascular mortality, with a clear dose-response relationship. It was a primary prevention cohort, and most olive oil consumed in the intervention group was extra virgin.

PREDIMED: Mediterranean diet with nuts regressed carotid plaque and cut stroke 50%

Jun 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 45 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis describes a PREDIMED sub-study comparing a control diet, Mediterranean plus EVOO, and Mediterranean plus nuts. Only the nut group showed carotid intima-media thickness regression after about 2.4 years, consistent with a lower stroke rate and 30% fewer MACE.

Mediterranean diet has the strongest evidence of any diet: 4 RCTs and decades of cohorts

May 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis argues the traditional Mediterranean diet has unmatched evidence: four 4-7 year RCTs show lower heart attack, stroke and CV death (46% lower CV mortality overall), and Nurses' Health and Health Professionals cohorts plus a dozen more large cohorts confirm long-term benefit. He allows other diets might work better but says none has comparable evidence.
