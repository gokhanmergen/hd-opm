How to Eat for a Long and Healthy Life

(As appeared in NYTimes, and summarized by gpt4o. I believe the article is reflective of the best available science, but I felt compelled to add some potential caveats in the last paragraph).

  • Prioritize protein, especially from plants: Plant-based proteins (e.g., legumes, nuts) are linked with longer life, while red/processed meats are associated with shorter life spans.
  • Incorporate bone-strengthening nutrients: Ensure sufficient calcium and vitamin D from foods like dairy, fortified plant milks, leafy greens, and sunlight exposure.
  • Pump up the polyphenols: Polyphenol-rich foods like fruits, vegetables, whole grains, coffee, and olive oil may support healthy aging and reduce risks of chronic diseases.
  • Focus on healthy fats: Unsaturated fats from sources like olive oil, nuts, seeds, and fatty fish are linked to longer life, while saturated fats from red meat should be limited.
  • Limit ultraprocessed foods: These foods, high in fast carbs and sodium, are associated with greater risks of chronic diseases and shorter life expectancy.
  • Consider the big picture: Overall diet quality matters more than any one food. Patterns like the Mediterranean diet, plant-based diets, and balanced eating are associated with better longevity and health outcomes.
  • One size doesn't fit all. Dietary advice is subject to change. Take everything you read in popular literature with a grain of salt. Reverse causality in observational diet studies is a strong possibility. Other confounders too are ever present (eg wrt socioeconomic status and personality).
