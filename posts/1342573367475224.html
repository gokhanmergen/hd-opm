✅✅ATORVASTATIN IS THE PREFERRED STATIN IN CKD PATIENTS

The most common cause of mortality in patients with CKD is cardiovascular, and therefore statins are recommended.

The PLANET (Prospective Evaluation of Proteinuria and Renal Function in Diabetic [and Nondiabetic] Patients With Progressive Renal Disease) trial showed that rosuvastatin was associated with decreased glomerular filtration rate (GFR) and increased proteinuria, whereas atorvastatin improved GFR and had no effect on proteinuria.

Atorvastatin improved kidney function dose-dependently across 6 RCTs with 30,621 patients

Oct 13, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis cites a JAHA post hoc analysis of six double-blind outcome trials in patients at risk of or with cardiovascular disease. Atorvastatin improved kidney function over time in a dose-dependent way, and improvement in kidney function was associated with lower cardiovascular risk. The authors suggest kidney function could serve as a surrogate end point.

Atorvastatin is generally preferred over rosuvastatin in chronic kidney disease

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Statins

Louis shares an MRCP UK explainer on statins in CKD: atorvastatin needs no renal dose adjustment (under 2% renal excretion), while rosuvastatin is 30% renally excreted, needs dose reduction at low eGFR and can cause reversible proteinuria at high doses. KDIGO advises a statin with or without ezetimibe in CKD stages 3-5 not on dialysis.

2026 ESC guidelines: manage CVD and CKD together as one cardiorenal syndrome

Aug 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Kidney health

Louis shares a summary of the 2026 ESC guideline on cardiovascular disease and CKD. Key messages include managing CVD and CKD together, testing both eGFR and urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio, treating albuminuria as a cardiovascular vital sign, and staging CKD with both measures.
