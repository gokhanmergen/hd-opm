Louis shares an MRCP UK explainer on statins in CKD: atorvastatin needs no renal dose adjustment (under 2% renal excretion), while rosuvastatin is 30% renally excreted, needs dose reduction at low eGFR and can cause reversible proteinuria at high doses. KDIGO advises a statin with or without ezetimibe in CKD stages 3-5 not on dialysis.

  • Atorvastatin: under 2% renal excretion, no dose change for CKD 1-5 (non-dialysis)
  • Rosuvastatin: about 30% renal excretion, dose reduction at eGFR <30, usually max 10 mg in severe CKD
  • High-dose rosuvastatin can cause reversible proteinuria/hematuria
  • KDIGO: statin plus or minus ezetimibe in CKD stages 3-5 not on dialysis

Atorvastatin vs Rosuvastatin in CKD

Core Concept

Statins in CKD are used for CV risk reduction.
Renal benefits are modest, not primary endpoints.

Atorvastatin is generally preferred in moderate–advanced CKD due to:

  • Less renal clearance (safe in low eGFR)
  • No dose adjustment
  • Lower incidence of statin-associated proteinuria
  • Some evidence of reduced proteinuria + slower eGFR decline

Rosuvastatin is very potent but requires dose reduction in CKD and high doses may cause proteinuria/hematuria.

Atorvastatin

  • Hepatic clearance
  • <2% renal excretion
  • No dose modification for CKD stages 1–5 (non-dialysis)

Rosuvastatin

  • 30% renal excretion
  • Requires dose reduction in eGFR <30
  • Maximum recommended dose often ≤10 mg/day in severe CKD
  • High doses associated with tubular proteinuria (reversible)

Renal Effects of statins

Atorvastatin

  • Shown to reduce proteinuria modestly
  • May slow eGFR decline
  • No significant renal toxicity reports

Rosuvastatin

  • Dose-dependent proteinuria + microscopic hematuria
  • Mechanism: proximal tubular dysfunction
  • Usually reversible with dose reduction or cessation

Guideline-Based (KDIGO)

KDIGO Recommendations:

  • Use statin ± ezetimibe in CKD stages 3–5 (not on dialysis)
  • Avoid high-dose rosuvastatin in advanced CKD
  • Atorvastatin commonly preferred because of pharmacokinetics

Which Statin to Start in CKD?
Preferred First-Line in CKD (Moderate–Advanced):

Atorvastatin (10–40 mg/day)
Why?

  • Safe in low eGFR
  • No dose limits
  • Less proteinuria risk
  • Good LDL reduction

When to Use Rosuvastatin

  • Need stronger LDL lowering
  • Patient has early CKD (Stage 1–2)
  • Start low dose (5–10 mg) if eGFR <30

Exam clue: Severe CKD + high ASCVD risk → Atorvastatin preferred to avoid rosuvastatin-induced proteinuria.

Only lipophilic statins like atorvastatin enter the brain; PCSK9 drugs and ezetimibe don't

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 10 Brain health & dementia

Louis explains the blood-brain barrier and cholesterol: the brain makes its own cholesterol separately from blood, so non-statin LDL drugs and hydrophilic statins like rosuvastatin have little brain effect. Lipophilic statins do enter the brain, with promising observational data against Alzheimer's, and a 20,000-person atorvastatin trial is under way. Two prior trials showed statins do not worsen existing Alzheimer's, and midlife high LDL is a leading modifiable dementia risk (2024 Lancet Commission, about 7% of US cases per Louis).
