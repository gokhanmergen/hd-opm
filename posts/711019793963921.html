Gotu kola extract reportedly stabilized high-risk plaque in small studies

Louis Nervegna · Nov 17, 2023 · ♥ 16 reactions · 16 comments · original Facebook post
Supplements & vitaminsPlaque, CAC & CT angiographyBlood vessels, circulation & endothelium
AI summary & key facts

Louis flags, as needing larger trials, Life Extension's summary that 60 mg of Centella asiatica extract three times daily hardened soft carotid plaque by 30% over 12 months, with fewer cardiovascular events in the randomized phase, and hardened femoral plaque by 63% with no size increase versus 23% in controls. He says it may target endothelial health. Evidence comes from small studies summarized by a supplement-company magazine.

  • 60 mg Centella asiatica extract three times daily
  • Carotid plaque 30% denser after 12 months in the first study
  • Randomized phase: 41% fewer cardiovascular events; reduced brain blood flow in 7% vs 17% of controls
  • Femoral plaque 63% harder; plaque size +23% in controls, none in treated

This needs to be tested in larger higher quality studies— but if the result holds up— it could be very important. One of the underlying mechanisms appears to be targeting the health of the arterial endothelium, for which no drug currently exists:

Gotu Kola stabilizes high-risk arterial plaque, and slows accumulation of new plaque.

“In a previous human study, participants with high-risk (soft) plaque took 60 mg of an extract of Centella asiatica three times daily. After 12 months, their carotid-artery plaque was denser (harder) by an average of 30%.

Phase two of this same study used the same dose but in a randomized, placebo-controlled trial. Carotid-artery plaque stability significantly improved. MRI scans showed reduced blood flow to the brain in 17% of controls but only in 7% of the Centella asiatica group. And supplemented subjects experienced 41% fewer cardiovascular events.

A similar placebo-controlled study of individuals with high-risk (soft) plaque in their femoral (main leg) arteries found that 60 mg of an extract of Centella asiatica three times daily produced a 63% harder plaque—indicating a lower rupture risk—in just 12 months. Critically, plaque size increased 23% in controls while supplemented patients showed zero plaque-size increase.”

Plaque starts in neck and leg arteries decades before the heart; calcium scores miss it

Sep 17, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post summarises a video describing an NEJM study of 16,000 asymptomatic people showing plaque accumulates first in the neck and leg arteries, during the 20s to 40s, before the heart. Heart scans such as coronary calcium may therefore miss early disease; carotid intima-media thickness ultrasound can detect peripheral plaque, and the ankle-brachial index offers a simple at-home check for leg artery blockage.

New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Aug 31, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 38 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified. Carvalho argues calcium stabilises plaque, so slowing calcification without reducing plaque could even be harmful, and no evidence shows K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, so he advises relying on proven therapies, diet and exercise.
