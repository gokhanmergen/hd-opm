What does a diet linked to longer life actually look like?

In one Harvard study, diet quality was one of 5 lifestyle factors linked to ~12–14 extra years of life expectancy at age 50

Add more of these:

  • Fatty fish —2 servings/week, ~4 oz each, like salmon or mackerel
  • Whole grains — oats, barley, quinoa, brown rice, farro
  • Whole fruit — with the fiber intact
  • Fiber-rich plants

Limit:

  • Red meat — avoid going above ~12–18 oz/week
  • Processed meat — close to zero
  • Sugar-sweetened beverages — zero, including juice
  • Sodium — ideally closer to 1,500 mg/day, not above ~2,300 mg/day
  • Ultra-processed foods — as low as possible
