Gerald Shulman honored for showing hidden fat in muscle and liver drives diabetes

Louis Nervegna · Nov 10, 2025 · ♥ 6 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Insulin resistance & diabetesWeight, visceral & liver fat
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares the American Liver Foundation's 2025 award to Gerald Shulman, whose team showed that fat stored in the wrong places, such as muscle and liver cells, disrupts insulin signaling and contributes to type 2 diabetes and liver disease. The quoted citation stresses that where fat is stored matters more than total body fat.

  • Shulman: 38 years of research on diabetes, lipid metabolism and liver disease
  • Where fat is stored matters more than total body fat

His work has revolutionized the understanding of what fundamentally causes diabetes.

Quoted:

“With 38 years of groundbreaking research on diabetes, lipid metabolism and liver disease, Dr. Shulman’s team discovered how “hidden fat” in the wrong places (like muscle and liver cells) disrupts insulin signaling and contributes to the development of type 2 diabetes as well as liver disease. This exciting research illustrates that where fat is stored is more important than total body fat and also explains how liver fat leads to disease. As a leading physician-scientist, Dr. Shulman’s discoveries have reshaped the landscape of hepatology, earning him international acclaim and deep respect among his peers.”
