Hunter-gatherers have LDL of 50-70 mg/dL and no atherosclerosis, per a review

Louis Nervegna · Aug 16, 2024 · ♥ 15 reactions · 20 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

The post counters carnivore/keto claims by citing a review stating that native hunter-gatherers, healthy neonates, free-living primates and wild mammals have LDL of 50-70 mg/dL and do not develop atherosclerosis. Per the review, RCTs show events minimized when LDL is below 70 mg/dL with no major safety concerns in that range.

  • Normal LDL 50-70 mg/dL in hunter-gatherers, neonates, primates, wild mammals
  • Atherosclerosis progression and CHD events minimized with LDL <70 mg/dL in trials
  • No major safety concerns in studies lowering LDL to 50-70 mg/dL

Traditional hunter-gatherer tribes have very low total cholesterol.

Carnivore and keto influencers have been lying about this for years now.

PaperScienceDirect articlesciencedirect.com

Quoted from the review article:

“The normal low-density lipoprotein (LDL) cholesterol range is 50 to 70 mg/dl for native hunter-gatherers, healthy human neonates, free-living primates, and other wild mammals (all of whom do not develop atherosclerosis). Randomized trial data suggest atherosclerosis progression and coronary heart disease events are minimized when LDL is lowered to <70 mg/dl. No major safety concerns have surfaced in studies that lowered LDL to this range of 50 to 70 mg/dl.”
