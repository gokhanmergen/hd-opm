Shared from Dr. Louis Ignarro · original post

Why was nitric oxide considered worthy of a Nobel Prize? Because its discovery as a signaling molecule fundamentally changed our understanding of how the cardiovascular system works.

Nearly three decades after receiving the Nobel Prize, I continue to believe that understanding how our bodies produce and use nitric oxide is essential to maintaining good cardiovascular health.

This is also the science behind my book, NO More Heart Disease, where I explore how everyday lifestyle choices can support nitric oxide production and cardiovascular health.

For more stories about the discoveries, research, and science behind nitric oxide, visit drignarro.com.
