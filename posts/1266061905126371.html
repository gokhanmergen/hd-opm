Omega 6 polyunsaturated fats activate the PPAR-gamma pathway.

“They are ligands for PPAR-gamma, which is an insulin sensitizer.” — Walter Willett, Harvard (video clip)

These are the main fats found in nuts and seeds (and their oils).

This PPAR-gamma pathway appears to be the principle mechanism by which omega 6 fats reduce insulin resistance and diabetes risk:

↪ Why omega-6 lowers diabetes risk: 43% lower risk in top vs bottom blood level quintile

↪ Omega-6 linoleic acid improves insulin sensitivity by activating PPAR-gamma

Activation of this PPAR-gamma pathway is the same mechanism by which the thiazolidinedione (“glitazone”) class of diabetes drugs work, including the medication pioglitazone (Actos):

↪ Linoleic acid activates PPAR-gamma like pioglitazone, which may reverse plaque

↪ Scripps team designs PPAR-gamma diabetes drugs with fewer side effects than glitazones
