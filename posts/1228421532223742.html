IARC classifies processed meat as a Group 1 carcinogen, like tobacco and asbestos

🤔🥓 Health authorities classify bacon and other processed meats — including ham, sausages, and hot dogs — as Group 1 carcinogens, meaning there is strong evidence they can cause cancer in humans, especially colorectal cancer.

This classification comes from the International Agency for Research on Cancer, the cancer research arm of the World Health Organization, which places processed meat in the same evidence category as tobacco smoking, asbestos, and arsenic based on studies linking regular consumption to increased cancer risk.

Experts stress that Group 1 describes the certainty of evidence — not the level of danger. Bacon is not as risky as smoking a cigarette, even though both fall under the same classification.

The message isn’t abstinence, but moderation: limiting processed meat as part of a balanced diet can help reduce long-term cancer risk.
