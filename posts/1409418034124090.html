Stanford: most Americans already meet the revised 1.2-1.6 g/kg protein guideline

Louis Nervegna · Oct 01, 2026 · ♥ 6 reactions · 10 comments · original Facebook post
ProteinGuidelines, policy & public health
AI summary & key facts

A Stanford Medicine piece separating protein hype from science says national data show most adults already consume protein at or above both previous and newly revised recommendations. The poster adds that Americans eat more total and high-quality animal protein than any other major region.

  • Revised protein guideline: 1.2-1.6 g/kg per day
  • Most adults already meet or exceed old and new recommendations

Despite the nonsense you’ve heard from influencers, most Americans already meet or exceed the revised protein guideline of 1.2g/kg to 1.6g/kg per day.

Americans consume more total protein and more high-quality animal protein than any other major region of the world: ↪ The US leads the world in protein intake, about 1.5 g/kg per day.

The idea that Americans are not consuming enough protein is patently ridiculous.

Quoted:

“National data show that most adults already consume protein amounts that meet or exceed both the previous and newly revised recommendations.”
