Shared from Louis Nervegna · original post

LDL particle size is not associated with risk for CVD, after the total number of LDL particles is properly adjusted for.

The number of LDL particles is measured by LDL-P or ApoB.

In simple terms, if you have high LDL or ApoB (indicating a large number of LDL particles) having a larger LDL particle size is unlikely to protect you.

Particle size is still useful as a measure of insulin sensitivity and glycemic control, but there are more accurate tests for this.
