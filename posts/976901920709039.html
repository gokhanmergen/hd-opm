Standing desks may not offset the health risks of sitting, a new study suggests

Gokhan Can · Mar 01, 2025 · ♥ 20 reactions · 28 comments · original Facebook post

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan argues that 'sitting is the new smoking' confused association with causation, and shares a CNN report on a study finding standing desks may not improve the health risks of sitting all day. The takeaway is that standing still is not a substitute for moving.

  • CNN report: standing desks may not improve the health risks tied to prolonged sitting

Humanity confused association with causation circa 2015 and made hyperbolic claims like "sitting is the new smoking". It turns out standing still isn't what it's made out to be.
