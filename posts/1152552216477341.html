Adventist study: vegans 85+ had higher neurological death risk than non-vegetarians

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Among AHS-2 participants (about 88,000, over 12,000 deaths), vegans aged 85 and older had higher risk of death from stroke (17%), dementia (13%) and Parkinson's (37%) than healthy non-vegetarians, while pesco-vegetarians had the lowest risk. The comparison was against very healthy non-vegetarians, not the standard American diet.

A 2021 meta-analysis of 40 randomized trials in 135,267 participants found omega-3 EPA/DHA reduced several cardiac outcomes, with suggested doses of 1000-2000 mg.

The poster says plant-based eaters who also ate some seafood lived longer than pure vegans in the Adventist Health Study and the Harvard Nurses' and Physicians' follow-up studies. Vegans still did very well, just not as well. The shared text says the Adventist study followed over 100,000 Americans for 30 years.

Walter Willett: unsaturated fats, Mediterranean eating and daily habits at 81

Oct 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 4 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A Washington Post interview with Harvard nutrition researcher Walter Willett. He says his group's data showed total fat was irrelevant, trans fats were bad, saturated fat versus refined carbohydrate was only weakly related to heart disease, and unsaturated fats were linked to lower heart disease and mortality. He also describes his own diet, including oats, nuts, blueberries and a plant-based lunch.

Framingham data link a high omega-3 index to about 5 more years of life expectancy

Dec 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a FoundMyFitness claim that people with a high omega-3 index (about 8%) have roughly 5 more years of life expectancy than those with a low index (about 4%) in cohort data from the Framingham Heart Study. It also says very low omega-3 may carry mortality risk similar to smoking and that nearly 90% of Americans fall short of EPA and DHA targets.

Adventists: pesco-vegetarians had 18% lower mortality, vegans only 3%

Oct 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 12 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares Adventist Health Study results showing pesco-vegetarian diets had the lowest mortality: 18% lower versus non-vegetarians, 15% for lacto-ovo-vegetarians and 3% for vegans. He notes similar patterns in the Harvard cohorts.
