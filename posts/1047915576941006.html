If you want to learn about the traditional Mediterranean diet and its many health benefits, this is the best book on the subject, in my opinion.

It is available free online here:

klimareporter.de7c0b 4fcf 84c8 85aaa63683c4.pdfklimareporter.de

Quoted from the book:

“Eating well— teamed with keeping your weight in the healthy range, exercising regularly, and not smoking— can prevent 80 percent of heart attacks, 90 percent of type 2 diabetes, and 70 percent of colorectal cancer. It can also help you avoid stroke, osteoporosis, constipation and other digestive woes, cataracts, and aging-related memory loss or dementia. And the benefits aren't just for the future. A healthy diet can give you more energy and help you feel good today.”

What’s unique about this book is that it covers the science backing-up the diet in a very accurate way, but also in a manner that any lay person can understand. The author has published over 2000 scientific papers on the subject (that’s not a typo), and for many years, was the second most cited journal paper author on all of pubmed. This book itself is widely cited in the medical literature, which is rare for a book intended for a non-scientific audience.

The book explains in simple terms how the basic principles of the Mediterranean diet can be modified to fit virtually any preference. You can do a low-carb version of the diet, a Mexican food version, an Asian version, or many other possibilities— all based on the flexible principles described.

I bought the first edition of this book in 2001 and have followed the diet that it recommends for the last 23 years. It is the only formal diet that I have ever followed. It’s easy and delicious. This is not fad diet. This is a diet that you can stick to for the rest of your life.
