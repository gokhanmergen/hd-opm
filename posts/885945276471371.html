Personal labs on tirzepatide/retatrutide and 5 mg rosuvastatin: LDL 61, ApoB 58, eGFR 86

AI summary & key facts

Gokhan shares his labs at 46: liver numbers normalized after about three years, LDL 61 and ApoB 58 mg/dL on 5 mg rosuvastatin, eGFR down to 86 (creatinine plus cystatin C), and CRP up from 0.2-0.4 to 0.8.

  • LDL 61 and ApoB 58 mg/dL on rosuvastatin 5 mg
  • eGFR 86 (combined estimate) vs creatinine eGFR near 100
  • CRP 0.8 vs prior 0.2-0.4

My recent labs. The good:

  • Liver numbers are normal for the first time in ~3 years. (Tirzepatide/Retatrutide helped a lot).
  • Most things normal.
  • LDL of 61mg/dL, ApoB of 58 md/dL thanks to 5mg Rosuvastatin.

The bad:

  • Realized that my kidneys are in slightly worse shape than I thought. eGFR of 86 (combined estimate from Creatinine and C Cystatin). Creatinine eGFR was averaging 100 before.
  • CRP was 0.2-0.4 range before. Slight increase to 0.8, maybe random but it's ok.

Grateful to have mostly normal labs, historically that wasn't always the case. (Age 46. High risk for blood clots and CVD)

Kidney health tracking: cystatin C, urine albumin/creatinine and more beyond creatinine

Gokhan argues creatinine and eGFR are imperfect kidney markers and ranks five tests worth tracking, especially for people on many supplements or drugs: cystatin C (very useful, independent of muscle mass), CBC/chemistry/lipids, urine albumin/creatinine ratio, urine protein/creatinine and urinalysis.

Measuring kidney health: add cystatin C and urine albumin-to-creatinine to creatinine eGFR

Sep 09, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 2 Kidney health

Gokhan reminds that creatinine-based eGFR alone is insufficient. Cystatin C eGFR can differ in about 30% of people, so the combined CKD-EPI creatinine-cystatin C equation is best, and urine protein and albumin-to-creatinine ratio catch early disease. Test at least yearly if on many meds or supplements.
