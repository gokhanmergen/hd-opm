Nature Food 2021: swapping 10% of beef and processed meat calories adds 48 healthy minutes

Louis Nervegna · Sep 08, 2025 · ♥ 8 reactions · 0 comments · original Facebook post
Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foodsMeat, eggs & dairyMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Louis quotes the University of Michigan Nature Food analysis of more than 5,800 American foods by health and environmental impact. Soda and hot dogs subtract healthy life minutes, whereas nuts and seeds add them, and substituting a small share of red and processed meat with whole plant foods and seafood yields gains.

  • A can of soda: about -12 minutes of healthy life; a hot dog: -36 minutes
  • 30 g of nuts and seeds: +25 minutes
  • Replacing 10% of daily calories from beef and processed meat with whole grains, fruits, vegetables, nuts, legumes and select seafood: +48 minutes per day
  • Modeling analysis of more than 5,800 foods

Small targeted dietary changes can yield substantial gains for human health

Quoted:

“Drinking a can of soda could cost you roughly 12 minutes of "healthy life," while eating a hot dog could reduce it by 36, according to a [University of Michigan] study.

On the other hand, the authors of the paper, published in the journal Nature Food, also found that eating a 30g (1oz) serving of nuts and seeds provides a gain of 25 minutes of healthy life—which in this case refers to good quality and disease-free life expectancy.”

“The researchers analyzed the individual foods based on their composition, finding that substituting only 10 percent of daily caloric intake of beef and processed meats for a diverse mix of whole grains, fruits, vegetables, nuts, legumes and select seafood could add 48 minutes of healthy life per person per day.”
