Measuring Kidney Health

A reminder: Creatinine and eGFR based on creatinine levels alone are insufficient markers to fully assess kidney health. Ideally, you should also measure

  • Cystatin C and calculate eGFR from that as well. These two eGFR values can be discordant (mismatching) in about 30% of people. The best estimate of your kidney filtration rate may be an average or a combination of the eGFR values -- sometimes called CKD-EPI creatinine-cystatin C equation (available online).
  • In addition to eGFR, it's crucial to monitor urine markers like urine protein and the albumin-to-creatinine ratio. These tests can help detect early-stage kidney disease and deterioration as soon as possible.

For those of us on multiple medications and supplements, it's recommended to track these markers at least annually, or more frequently if you're at high risk for kidney disease. Kidney disease and heart disease are closely linked, and Cystatin C is also considered a marker for heart health.

Swapping refined carbs for unsaturated fat lowers LDL: meta-analysis of 60 feeding trials

Oct 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A meta-analysis of 60 controlled human feeding trials found that replacing carbohydrate calories with monounsaturated or polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL cholesterol, while saturated fat raises it. The effect looked largest for polyunsaturated fat, which is why choosing oils and nuts over refined starch and sugar helps the lipid profile.

A phase II trial in Science Advances found that the constipation drug lubiprostone (16 ug daily) preserved eGFR over 24 weeks versus decline on placebo.
