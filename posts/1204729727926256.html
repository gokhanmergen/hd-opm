SGLT2 inhibitors linked to delayed progression in prostate cancer on hormone therapy

Louis Nervegna · Jan 19, 2026 · ♥ 6 reactions · 4 comments · original Facebook post
CancerInsulin resistance & diabetes
AI summary & key facts

Louis shares a JAMA Oncology finding that men with prostate cancer on hormone therapy using SGLT2 inhibitors had lower risk of treatment failure and delayed progression.

  • SGLT2 inhibitors excrete up to 100 g sugar per day
  • Association with lower treatment failure

This class of diabetes drugs help your kidneys excrete up to 100 grams of sugar per day. They may be helpful in treating cancer, particularly prostate cancer.

Nov 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Cancer
