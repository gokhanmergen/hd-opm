AHA's updated dietary guidance shifts from meat to plants while still recommending seafood

Louis Nervegna · Apr 24, 2026 · ♥ 11 reactions · 1 comments · original Facebook post
Guidelines, policy & public healthMediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets
AI summary & key facts

Louis clarifies the new AHA scientific statement shifts from red and processed meat toward plant proteins, essentially a Mediterranean/DASH pattern.

  • Still recommends seafood
  • Starts heart-healthy eating early in life

To clarify, they are still strongly recommending seafood. They are mainly recommending a shift away from red and processed meat towards plant proteins like legumes, nuts, seeds, whole grains.

Med News: In newly updated guidance, the American Heart Association advises shifting from meat to a plant-based diet for improved #cardiovascular health.
