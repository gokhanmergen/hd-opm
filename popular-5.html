  88. VLDL cholesterol (triglycerides / 5) may be more atherogenic than LDL

    May 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 11 ↩ 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

    Louis explains that VLDL is an emerging atherosclerosis risk factor and may be more atherogenic than LDL, so people with low LDL but high VLDL may still be at risk. VLDL can be roughly estimated by dividing triglycerides by 5 on a standard lipid panel, which is one reason to watch triglycerides. A normal VLDL is below 30 mg/dL, corresponding to triglycerides below 150 mg/dL, though optimal is likely lower in high-risk people.
