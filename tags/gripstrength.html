Grip strength predicts mortality better than blood pressure, but is a marker, not a cause

Jun 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 2 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares a post on the PURE study, where lower grip strength tracked with higher mortality, and adds a caution from William Wallace: blood pressure is causal while grip strength is not. Treating blood pressure lowers risk, whereas doing handgrip exercises just games the test without reducing risk.

Grip strength predicts heart attack like blood pressure, but reflects reverse causality

Feb 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Aging & longevity

Louis notes grip strength predicts heart attack risk comparably to blood pressure, but argues this is reverse causality: aging weakens grip, and training grip will not lower heart disease risk.
