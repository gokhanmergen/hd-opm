Evidence that plaque can shrink: CENIT, PRECISE-IVUS, ZEUS, EVAPORATE and GLAGOV

Jun 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 72 · 💬 42 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Post by group member Daniel Chong summarising trials showing plaque change: supervised HIIT in CENIT, ezetimibe in PRECISE-IVUS, ezetimibe added to statin in ZEUS, icosapent ethyl in EVAPORATE and evolocumab in GLAGOV. It notes some research suggests a 1% reduction in plaque volume may equate to a 20% reduction in event risk.

Two-year high-intensity exercise reversed cardiac aging by up to 20 years in an RCT

Feb 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 30 · 💬 19 Exercise & fitness

A shared summary of a 2-year randomized trial in sedentary middle-aged adults combining HIIT, moderate endurance and strength sessions. It improved VO2 max, baroreflex sensitivity and heart elasticity versus a stretching/balance control, though in healthy participants only.

Exercise, including HIIT, can reduce coronary plaque burden and add to LDL lowering

Jul 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 17 ↩ 8 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A shared review states that regular moderate-to-high intensity exercise reduces coronary plaque burden in CAD patients, with HIIT and moderate continuous training both leading to regression. Combining exercise with aggressive LDL control below 70 mg/dL gives additive benefit, while extreme volumes may increase calcification though with more stable plaque.
