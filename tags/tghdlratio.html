Atherogenic index of plasma (log TG/HDL) predicts rapid plaque progression in PARADIGM

Apr 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 18 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A PARADIGM sub-study found higher AIP, the base-10 log of triglycerides divided by HDL, was associated with faster plaque progression and greater plaque burden in diabetic and non-diabetic adults. Rapid plaque progression occurred in 26.1% over a median 3.4 years of follow-up.

HDL and ApoA-I raising therapies repeatedly failed, so HDL is probably not directly causal

Mar 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 9 ↩ 22 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis reviews why HDL or ApoA-I is probably not causal for heart disease. CETP inhibitor trials failed (ILLUMINATE with torcetrapib was stopped for harm; dal-OUTCOMES with dalcetrapib and ACCELERATE with evacetrapib showed no benefit), as did fibrate and niacin trials and CSL112 infusions, and Mendelian randomization agrees. He proposes HDL and ApoA-I mainly act as a proxy for insulin sensitivity, which is the real causal factor.

Oct 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that a viral UK Biobank graph shows higher HDL linked to lower ASCVD risk at any LDL/ApoB, as dozens of earlier observational studies have, though the effect size here is larger.
