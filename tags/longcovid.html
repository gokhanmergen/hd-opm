BMJ: evidence mounts that COVID-19 disrupts immunity, challenging 'immunity debt' idea

Aug 20, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A BMJ feature reports that the immunity debt theory is increasingly challenged and that SARS-CoV-2 may cause lasting immune disruption. Evidence includes more infections after covid than after flu or RSV, viral reactivation, T-cell exhaustion and persistent inflammation.

Metformin cut SARS-CoV-2 viral load 3.6-fold in the COVID-OUT randomized trial

Aug 23, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 4 ↩ 2 Infections & vaccines

Quoting a Clinical Infectious Diseases analysis of the COVID-OUT trial, the post says metformin suppresses viral protein translation via host mTOR and reduced viral load versus placebo. Metformin also lowered hospitalization/death and long Covid odds.

COVID-OUT: early metformin cut long Covid risk by 42% in a large randomized trial

Aug 22, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 4 ↩ 2 Infections & vaccines

The COVID-OUT phase 3 trial (>1000 mostly non-diabetic outpatients) found metformin lowered long Covid incidence by 42% relative to placebo, and by 63% when started within 4 days of symptoms. Ivermectin and fluvoxamine did not prevent long Covid.

VIVID: high-dose vitamin D3 did not reduce COVID-19 severity in a randomized trial

Mar 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 5 Supplements & vitamins

Louis reports the Harvard-led VIVID trial: 1,747 COVID patients and 277 contacts randomized to vitamin D3 or placebo for four weeks showed no difference in healthcare use, death or symptoms, though a signal for less long COVID emerged.

Hypothesis: disrupted iron handling after SARS-CoV-2 infection may trigger long COVID

Mar 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 5 Infections & vaccines

Shares a news summary proposing that infection makes the body sequester iron, which if prolonged could reduce iron available for red blood cells and contribute to persistent symptoms. Louis calls it one of the more interesting long-COVID hypotheses; it is not yet clinically proven.

TOGETHER metformin trial retracted; COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6 still support metformin

Mar 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Infections & vaccines

The TOGETHER trial of metformin for early COVID-19 was retracted by its authors in October 2025 after an analysis error led to premature stopping. Louis notes that two other large randomized trials, COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6, independently showed metformin lowers long-COVID risk, and hopes for a corrected re-publication.

Long COVID linked to higher risk of venous and arterial blood clots in meta-analysis

Jun 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis finding that people with post-COVID syndrome have an increased risk of thrombosis. This raises risk of heart attack, stroke, DVT and pulmonary embolism.

Metformin cuts long COVID risk in two large randomized trials, COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6

Feb 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 ↩ 2 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis notes the evidence that metformin protects against long COVID is impressive: two independent large randomized trials each showed a big risk reduction.

Fluvoxamine reduced fatigue in Long COVID; metformin did not

Apr 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares an Annals of Internal Medicine report that fluvoxamine significantly reduced fatigue in Long COVID while metformin did not, and notes fluvoxamine's early COVID promise was not confirmed by better trials.

Starting metformin at acute COVID infection lowers long COVID risk in two large RCTs

Feb 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 ↩ 2 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a Clinical Infectious Diseases review concluding that two randomized placebo-controlled trials, COVID-OUT and ACTIV-6, show that starting metformin during acute infection is safe and lowers long COVID risk.

Metformin and UDCA did not help people who already had long COVID (396-patient trial)

Mar 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Infections & vaccines

A new trial found neither metformin nor UDCA relieved symptoms in people with established long COVID. Earlier randomized trials showed metformin lowers the risk of developing long COVID when started early in infection, so timing appears to matter.
