Carotid plaque burden on ultrasound predicted mortality better than CAC in 5,700 people

Jun 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 30 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a prospective study of about 5,700 people without symptoms showing carotid ultrasound plaque burden (cPB) predicted all-cause mortality better than coronary calcium score. After adjustment, cPB had a hazard ratio of 1.23 per trend versus 1.15 for CAC, and Louis notes the test is cheap and radiation-free.

Atherosclerotic plaques contain large clonal, mutated cell populations, like tumors

Jun 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 14 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A JCI Insight study used deep whole-exome sequencing of carotid plaques and non-atherosclerotic arteries and found somatic mutations in plaques but not in healthy arterial tissue. This suggests plaques grow by clonal expansion, paralleling clonal hematopoiesis (CHIP), which is causally linked to cardiovascular disease.

Cumulative LDL-C thresholds: endothelial damage above 4000, plaque from 6000 mg-year/dL

Sep 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 9 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

A synthesis of three studies showing cumulative LDL exposure predicts atherosclerosis better than one reading. In a cross-sectional study of 8,000+ people not on lipid drugs (Age x LDL-C), vascular damage appeared in stages as cumulative exposure rose, suggesting even moderate LDL sustained for decades crosses these tipping points.

Plaque starts in neck and leg arteries decades before the heart; calcium scores miss it

Sep 17, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post summarises a video describing an NEJM study of 16,000 asymptomatic people showing plaque accumulates first in the neck and leg arteries, during the 20s to 40s, before the heart. Heart scans such as coronary calcium may therefore miss early disease; carotid intima-media thickness ultrasound can detect peripheral plaque, and the ankle-brachial index offers a simple at-home check for leg artery blockage.

Nattokinase 10,800 FU/day improved carotid plaque and lipids; 3,600 FU/day did not

Quotes a clinical study of 1,062 participants in which nattokinase at 10,800 FU/day managed atherosclerosis and hyperlipidemia, shrinking carotid intima-media thickness and plaque size. The lower dose of 3,600 FU/day was ineffective.

Micronanoplastics 50 times higher in carotid plaque than in plaque-free arteries

Apr 23, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

A small preliminary study presented at an AHA meeting found carotid plaque, especially from people with stroke, TIA or transient vision loss, held 50 times more micronanoplastics than plaque-free arteries. It cannot prove causation and no direct link to inflammation markers was found, though gene activity related to plaque stability differed.
