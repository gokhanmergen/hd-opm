Vitamin C and E did not blunt resistance training gains in older women with sarcopenia

Sep 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 6 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a trial summary challenging the idea that antioxidants blunt exercise adaptation. Older women with sarcopenia who added vitamins C and E to 12 weeks of resistance band training gained more muscle and strength with lower inflammation.

Regular nut eaters have 30-50% lower risk of heart attack and CVD in major cohorts

Aug 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis quotes Harvard's Nutrition Source on large cohort studies, including the Adventist, Iowa Women's, Nurses' Health and Physicians' Health studies, which consistently link regular nut intake to lower risk of heart attack, sudden cardiac death and CVD.

Vitamin C plus E did not blunt resistance training gains in older women with sarcopenia

Apr 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

A shared trial summary: older women with sarcopenia doing 12 weeks of resistance band training who added vitamin C (1000 mg) and E (335 mg) gained more muscle and strength with lower IL-6. Louis uses it to question the claim that antioxidants blunt exercise adaptations.
