Unhealthy plant-based diets raise CHD risk, healthy ones lower it, in Harvard cohorts

Dec 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis stresses that not all plant-based diets are healthy. In the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study, a plant-based index rich in healthy plant foods was linked to substantially lower CHD risk while one emphasizing sugary drinks, refined grains, potatoes and sweets was linked to higher risk, worse than the standard diet baseline.

Small MSK trial: high-fiber plant-based diet delayed progression of precursor myeloma

Dec 23, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 12 Cancer

A first-of-its-kind Memorial Sloan Kettering trial gave 20 overweight people with precursor blood disorders 12 weeks of plant-based meals with coaching. None had progressed at one year, and two with previously progressing disease improved. Researchers think increased gut butyrate may be involved; a 150-person trial (NUTRIVENTION-3) is enrolling.

Harvard cohort: unhealthy low-carb diets raise diabetes risk, plant-based ones lower it

Nov 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 15 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Summarises a video on a 30-year Harvard study of over 200,000 participants: low-carb diets high in animal protein, saturated fat and refined carbs like white potatoes were linked to higher type 2 diabetes risk, while plant-based versions (nuts, avocado, whole grains) lowered it. It also discusses rapid weight loss without exercise causing 'fat overshooting' on regain.

CARDIA: low-carb diets in young adults tied to more coronary calcium if animal-based

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 3 · 💬 7 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Posts the abstract of a CARDIA analysis of 2,226 participants (mean age 40.4) followed 8.3 years. Higher carbohydrate intake was inversely associated with CAC progression (HR 0.731); an animal-based low-carb score predicted higher progression (HR 1.456) but a plant-based score did not (HR 1.016). 26.5% had CAC progression.

Swapping refined carbs for unsaturated fat lowers LDL: meta-analysis of 60 feeding trials

Oct 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A meta-analysis of 60 controlled human feeding trials found that replacing carbohydrate calories with monounsaturated or polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL cholesterol, while saturated fat raises it. The effect looked largest for polyunsaturated fat, which is why choosing oils and nuts over refined starch and sugar helps the lipid profile.
