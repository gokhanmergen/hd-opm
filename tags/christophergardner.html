Extra protein cannot be stored: the body burns it for energy or converts it to fat

Nov 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Protein

Louis shares a clip of Stanford's Christopher Gardner explaining that unlike fat, the body has no dedicated storage for excess dietary protein. Surplus amino acids are broken down and used for energy, with carbon skeletons possibly becoming glucose or fat. Adequate protein matters, but more is not automatically better.
