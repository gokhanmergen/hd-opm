Dietary choline may protect mitochondria and slow brain-cell aging, a dementia mechanism

Aug 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 31 · 💬 10 Brain health & dementia

Louis revisits a study offering a more plausible mechanism for why prospective studies link choline, especially from eggs, with lower dementia risk. Controlled feeding shows dietary choline barely raises brain acetylcholine, so the new proposal is that choline protects mitochondria from premature aging via phosphatidylcholine.

Five large cohort studies link egg and choline intake to lower Alzheimer's risk

May 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 16 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis notes that five large prospective cohort studies have associated egg or choline consumption with lower Alzheimer's risk, suggesting a genuine signal. The post links to an earlier discussion and gives no further detail.

Eggs and phosphatidylcholine did not raise TMAO in an RCT, unlike choline bitartrate

Jun 10, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 10 ↩ 5 Meat, eggs & dairy

A Cleveland Clinic randomized trial (not egg-industry funded, with TMAO discoverer Stanley Hazen) found choline bitartrate supplements raised TMAO and platelet reactivity, but four large eggs a day or phosphatidylcholine supplements did not.

Eggs did not raise TMAO in a Cleveland Clinic randomized trial, unlike choline bitartrate

Jul 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 13 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis rebuts claims that eggs raise AFib risk via TMAO by pointing to an earlier randomized trial from the same Cleveland Clinic group. In that trial, eating eggs or taking phosphatidylcholine did not raise TMAO, whereas choline bitartrate supplements did. The trial was not funded by the egg industry.

Falling phosphatidylcholine may fragment mitochondria with age; choline may restore them

May 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

William Wallace's shared post describes a Nature Communications paper proposing that mitochondria age partly because PC-synthesizing enzymes decline, causing fragmented networks. Adding PC or choline reconnected the network in preclinical models; human rescue evidence is not yet established.

Fatty liver affects over 50% of US adults over 50; food choline does not raise TMAO

Jul 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares data that fatty liver prevalence exceeds 50% in over-50s in a study of about 4,000 people, and stresses choline helps export liver fat. Phosphatidylcholine did not raise TMAO in a Cleveland Clinic RCT.
