High insulin/IGF-1 tied to cancer, diabetes; Laron syndrome and low-IGF-1 mice live longer

May 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 13 Aging & longevity

Louis praises a video on height, insulin/IGF-1 and longevity and lays out his argument: diet (refined starch/sugar and excess animal protein) is the main driver of IGF-1, and low IGF-1 is linked to lower cancer and diabetes risk. He cites Laron syndrome, with no documented cancer deaths, little diabetes and less heart disease and Alzheimer's, and a long-lived low-IGF-1 mouse. Claims come from the post and videos, not named papers.

Taller people tend to live shorter lives, likely via growth hormone, IGF-1 and mTOR

Aug 23, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 14 ↩ 2 Aging & longevity

The post notes that taller people have shorter lifespans and higher cancer risk, as with larger versus smaller dogs, probably because of growth hormone, IGF-1 and mTOR signaling. It uses this to criticize a carnivore influencer video about nutrition and height; the claims are not backed by cited studies.

Taller people tend to live shorter lives and have higher cancer risk, Louis says

May 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 11 Aging & longevity

Louis argues that being tall, driven largely by IGF1 during development, shortens lifespan in most mammals and raises cancer risk after adjusting for socioeconomic confounders. He links a video and disputes carnivore influencers' claims.

One zoledronate infusion cut vertebral fractures about 44% over 10 years in women 50-60

Jan 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 9 Hormones, women's & men's health

Gokhan shares the NEJM 10-year randomized trial of infrequent zoledronate in early postmenopausal women (50-60 years) with normal-to-low-normal bone density. Zoledronate roughly halved new morphometric vertebral fractures versus placebo, and he calls it a longevity-type drug like statins or aspirin. The trial concerns bone rather than heart outcomes.
