DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed ~40 mm3 of soft plaque in a year on CT

Aug 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 38 · 💬 21 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

In the DISCO-CT randomized trial, a DASH-style diet with statins reduced non-calcified plaque volume by 39.5 mm3 (adjusted to one year from 66 weeks), the largest dietary regression on CT angiography to date. An at-risk person typically grows about 5 mm3 per year, so this equals roughly 8 years of progression. DASH fat was ~30% of calories, mostly unsaturated.

A CT angiogram shows the lumen, so it can miss early plaque inside the artery wall

Apr 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 25 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains that coronary CT angiography is 'lumenography': contrast dye fills only the lumen, so early plaque growing inside the artery wall is mostly invisible until it narrows the lumen. A perfect CCTA therefore does not rule out early-stage plaque. He links a Dr Alo video on this blind spot.

A normal angiogram can hide large plaque burden because of Glagov remodeling

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains that angiography is a 'luminogram' that misses plaque inside the artery wall because early plaque causes outward (Glagov) remodeling that preserves the lumen. Modern plaque-progression studies use IVUS or CT angiography instead, and he cites this as a flaw of Dean Ornish's small 1990s trial.

Same CT plaque, lower MI risk in women than men in a 10,003-person Danish cohort

Aug 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A Danish population study of 10,003 asymptomatic adults over 40 found subclinical coronary plaque less frequent in women at every age, for example 6% vs 20% at age 40-44. After 6 years, obstructive disease carried 4.1-fold higher MI risk in women versus 12-fold in men, though the sex interaction was not statistically significant.

REACT: silent atherosclerosis found in 57% of 16,808 adults, 7-9% of 18-29-year-olds

Sep 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The NEJM REACT study used carotid and femoral ultrasound plus coronary CT angiography in 16,808 adults aged 18-70 without known disease. Plaque was present in 57% overall, appeared in young adults, and a zero calcium score often missed early non-calcified plaque. Ten-year risk scores detected very little silent disease, so keeping risk factors favorable throughout life is the priority.
