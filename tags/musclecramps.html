Vitamin K2 reduced nocturnal leg cramps in a clinical study, per Life Extension

Mar 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a Life Extension article stating that a clinical study found vitamin K2 reduced the frequency, duration and severity of nocturnal leg cramps, starting after one week. The source is a supplement-company magazine, and no trial details are given.

Vitamin K2 reduced nighttime leg cramps in older adults in a 2024 RCT

Dec 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in JAMA Internal Medicine found vitamin K2 significantly reduced nocturnal leg cramps in adults 65 and older.
