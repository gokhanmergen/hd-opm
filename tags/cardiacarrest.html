Under 10% of heart attacks are now fatal, but out-of-hospital cardiac arrest is ~90% fatal

Aug 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 7 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Modern treatment means less than 10% of heart attacks result in death, versus up to half of victims decades ago. However, a cardiac arrest or widowmaker heart attack outside a hospital still has about a 90% chance of death, and cardiac arrest (heart stops) is distinct from a heart attack.

A summary of an ESC press release on 20,286 male bodybuilders found 121 deaths, with sudden cardiac death causing 38%. Professionals had more than five times the sudden cardiac death risk of amateurs.
