Urolithin A overview: gut-made pomegranate metabolite, mostly preclinical heart evidence

Oct 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A shared summary of research on urolithin A, a compound gut bacteria make from ellagitannins in pomegranate, walnuts and berries. The poster notes most of the evidence is preclinical in animals and says it may have value in heart failure. The summary also mentions human trials published in Nature Medicine.

Urolithin A looks promising for heart failure in animals but is untested in humans

Sep 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 2 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis says urolithin A is a compound to watch for heart failure based on animal evidence, but it has not been properly tested in humans with the condition. Small human trials in healthy people suggest improved muscle function and exercise capacity; the shared post mentions 500-1,000 mg daily and mitophagy effects.
