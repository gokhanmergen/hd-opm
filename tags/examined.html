Full-fat yogurt lowered ApoB while low-fat yogurt plus butter raised it in a crossover RCT

Jun 07, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 15 ↩ 1 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis summarizes a randomized crossover trial matching saturated fat between full-fat yogurt and low-fat yogurt with added butter. Despite equal saturated fat, the butter arm raised ApoB while full-fat yogurt lowered it, which the study attributes to the milk fat globule membrane (MFGM) that is intact in yogurt but lost in butter. He suggests this may partly explain why yogurt looks better than other dairy in epidemiology.

Late-life folate restriction reduced biosynthesis without harming healthspan in mice

Aug 08, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 16 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Mice switched at 52 weeks to a folate- and choline-free diet held their weight, avoided anemia, and showed reduced protein synthesis pathways over 68 weeks. Gokhan stresses the work is in yeast, worms and mice, not humans.

Olive oil protected against cardiac hypertrophy in aging-model rats

Nov 23, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 11 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

In rats given D-galactose to mimic cardiac aging, eight weeks of olive oil reduced cardiac hypertrophy and oxidative stress (MDA), increased antioxidant activity (SOD) and improved expression of heart-health genes. It is an animal study, so human benefit is not shown.

Statins neither cause nor cure cancer: meta-analyses of randomized trials show no effect

Dec 31, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Statins

Reviews the evidence on statins and cancer. A 2012 meta-analysis of 134,537 participants in 22 RCTs found identical cancer incidence (1.4% per year) and mortality (0.5% per year) in statin users and non-users, and a 2023 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs found the same. Anticancer effects in specific contexts remain preclinical or suggestive.

Vitamin D lowered depression symptoms dose-dependently in meta-analysis of 31 RCTs

Apr 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Examine.com summary of a dose-response meta-analysis of 31 RCTs: vitamin D reduced depressive symptoms, with most benefit near 5,000 IU/day and benefit seen only in people already depressed and not vitamin D sufficient. Most trials were under six months. Off the cardiovascular focus.

A week of all-ultraprocessed food caused more weight gain than none in a crossover RCT

May 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Shared from Examine.com: a small randomized crossover trial in young men found a week of an entirely ultraprocessed diet led to more weight gain than a diet with no ultraprocessed food. The result fits observational data and an earlier RCT, though the post cautions that ultraprocessed food is a broad category and not all of it is equally harmful.

Higher fiber intake linked to 53% lower mortality in 3,894 US adults with diabetes

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared summary of a cohort of 3,894 US adults with diabetes found the third quartile of fiber intake had a 53% lower relative risk of death than the lowest (HR 0.47), with about 13% mediated by lower systemic immune inflammation index. It is observational and does not prove fiber extends life by 53%.

Louis: omega-6 does not cause inflammation in trials; omega-3:6 ratio is not predictive

A summary opinion post stating that controlled feeding studies and RCTs consistently show omega-6 fats do not cause inflammation, and that the omega-3 to omega-6 ratio does not predict heart disease or diabetes. It directs readers to group tags for the underlying posts and cites no specific study.
