Atherosclerosis drivers such as high apoB and blood pressure also raise dementia risk

Jun 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 5 Brain health & dementia

A shared post argues that atherosclerotic risk factors (atherosclerosis, endothelial dysfunction, high BP, high LDL/apoB) are consistently linked to Alzheimer's and dementia risk, with some genetic overlap between lipids, coronary disease and Alzheimer's. The message is that keeping apoB and blood pressure optimal, and improving endothelial function, insulin sensitivity and inflammation, protects both heart and brain.

PNAS: brain network destabilization begins in midlife, tied to insulin resistance and fat

Jun 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a summary of a PNAS study finding that brain network destabilization, a precursor to cognitive decline, begins in midlife and is associated with insulin resistance and elevated blood pressure. The summary argues that visceral fat drives these and that diet and exercise to reduce it may protect the brain.
