Semaglutide raised knee cartilage thickness 17% in 6 months in a small pilot

Mar 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 19 ↩ 2 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis highlights a Cell Metabolism study in mice and people with obesity and osteoarthritis. After six months on low-dose semaglutide plus hyaluronic acid, MRI showed a 17% increase in cartilage thickness versus under 1% in the comparison group, with better knee function; Science News notes bigger trials are needed.

Semaglutide cut inflammation 38% in SELECT; biggest drops tracked with fewer events

Aug 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 4 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

A new analysis of the SELECT trial asks why semaglutide reduced cardiovascular events. Inflammation markers fell 38% versus placebo, early and even in people who lost little weight, and those with the biggest drops had the fewest events. Modelling suggests part of the drug's benefit runs through lower inflammation, though this shows a link rather than proof of cause.

SELECT trial: semaglutide cut major cardiovascular events 20% in obese non-diabetic adults

Dec 24, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 6 ↩ 1 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Summarizes the SELECT trial of 2.4 mg weekly semaglutide in about 17,000 adults with obesity but without diabetes, followed for 40 months. The primary composite of CV death, nonfatal MI and nonfatal stroke fell from 8.0% to 6.5%, with benefits for nonfatal MI, all-cause death, blood pressure and weight.

Semaglutide extended female mouse lifespan 12%, but a 24% calorie drop likely drove it

Sep 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's take on a Nature study in which semaglutide started at 20 months extended median mouse lifespan by 12% (742 to 834 days). Food intake fell about 24%, so much of the benefit probably reflects calorie restriction. The shared text cautions that it is not evidence of 10 extra years in humans.

Louis shares an article saying low testosterone in middle-aged men is often caused by visceral fat, and that around 7-10% weight loss lowers leptin enough to raise testosterone. Semaglutide and other GLP-1 drugs reportedly raise testosterone 20-30% in men with abundant visceral fat.

Karl Nadolsky shares a real-world study where incretin-based drugs in men with obesity produced modest weight loss, better glycemic control and higher total and free testosterone, most in those who lost the most weight. Men and women lose weight similarly with calorie restriction and GLP-1 drugs

May 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Stuart Phillips debunks claims that women need special weight loss strategies. A meta-analysis of 13 long-term RCTs found no sex difference, low-carb reviews similar, and GLP-1 trials showed women losing about 1 kg more.

Semaglutide REMODEL trial: 40% less albuminuria, and a calorie-restriction interpretation

Oct 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

A Nature Medicine mechanistic study (REMODEL) found semaglutide in people with type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease cut albuminuria by 40% and reduced kidney and perirenal fat, with biopsy changes in glomerular endothelial cells. The poster argues the kidney benefit stems from negative energy balance rather than anything special about GLP-1 drugs, which simply make calorie restriction easier.
