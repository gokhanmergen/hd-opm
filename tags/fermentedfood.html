Butter and cheese affect LDL differently despite similar saturated fat: the food matrix

Sep 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 7 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis highlights that butter and cheese contain similar saturated fat but affect LDL differently because of food matrix. Churning strips the natural milk-fat wrapper, while in cheese fat stays in a protein mesh, with calcium and fermentation also possibly modifying effects. Swapping to a Western diet for weeks raised inflammation in a Tanzanian multiomics trial

Jun 04, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A Nature Medicine study of 77 healthy Tanzanian men tested a switch between a traditional Kilimanjaro diet and a Western diet, plus a Western diet with the fermented banana drink mbege. The Western diet quickly increased weight and inflammatory and cardiometabolic proteins, while the heritage diet and mbege reduced inflammatory markers.

Fermented foods do not appear to raise stomach cancer risk, but salt-preserved foods may

Oct 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Cancer

Louis says the totality of evidence suggests plant- and dairy-based fermented foods do not raise stomach cancer risk. Salt preservation does seem to be associated with higher risk, likely from the high sodium content rather than fermentation, and many fermented foods are also salty, which confounds studies.
