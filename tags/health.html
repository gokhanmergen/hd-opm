Tirzepatide beat testosterone therapy for raising testosterone in obese men (pilot, n=83)

Jul 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 2 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

ARBs, alone or with a calcium channel blocker, are best-tolerated BP drugs in 700+ trials

Jun 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis summarizes the JAMA network meta-analysis showing ARBs have fewer treatment discontinuations from side effects than even placebo.
