Carotid plaque burden on ultrasound predicted mortality better than CAC in 5,700 people

Jun 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 30 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a prospective study of about 5,700 people without symptoms showing carotid ultrasound plaque burden (cPB) predicted all-cause mortality better than coronary calcium score. After adjustment, cPB had a hazard ratio of 1.23 per trend versus 1.15 for CAC, and Louis notes the test is cheap and radiation-free.

Ultrasound carotid plaque burden predicted mortality better than CAC over 12 years

Nov 29, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 10 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A prospective cohort of about 5,700 asymptomatic people found that ultrasound carotid plaque burden (cPB) predicted all-cause mortality better than CAC score. Louis suggests cPB is a cheap, radiation-free test and says to pick it over carotid intima-media thickness.

Carotid plaque burden ultrasound predicted mortality better than CAC in 5,700 people

Aug 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 4 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis says CIMT does not predict heart attack risk, and recommends carotid plaque burden (cPB) ultrasound instead. A 12-year prospective cohort of 5,700 asymptomatic people found cPB predicted all-cause mortality more accurately than CAC score; the test is cheap and radiation-free.
