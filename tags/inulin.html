Psyllium at about 10 g/day lowers LDL by roughly 13 mg/dL across 28 randomized trials

Jun 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 14 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares an explanation that psyllium works mechanically: its viscous gel traps bile acids, so the liver pulls LDL from blood to make new ones, the same principle as bile acid sequestrants. It does not absorb cholesterol, and most other soluble fibers do not produce the same effect.

Psyllium lowers LDL about 13 mg/dL across 28 trials by trapping bile acids

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 4 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared explainer says psyllium lowers LDL by about 13 mg/dL across 28 randomized trials, and that the mechanism is mechanical: its viscous gel traps bile acids so the liver pulls cholesterol from the blood to make more, upregulating LDL receptors. It does not absorb cholesterol directly or scrub the gut, which is why most other soluble fibers do not work the same way.

Whole fruit is linked to less fatty liver; fiber may protect the liver from fructose

Jun 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis notes high fructose causes fatty liver in animals, yet whole fruit is linked to lower fatty liver risk in humans. He cites a 2025 mouse study where inulin fiber changed gut bacteria so fructose was broken down in the intestine before reaching the liver, which could partly explain it; no human trial has tested that mechanism.

Only gel-forming fibers such as psyllium and beta-glucan lower LDL

Nov 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 3 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares a video explaining that fibers lower LDL by forming a gel that traps bile acids, forcing the liver to pull LDL from blood. Psyllium, oat and barley beta-glucan and guar gum gel, while inulin, FOS and wheat bran do not. Psyllium lowered LDL about 13 mg/dL across 28 trials and about 9 mg/dL in a 2025 analysis of 41 trials.

Soluble vs insoluble fiber is an oversimplification

Jul 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 1 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared explainer says fiber varies independently in solubility, viscosity, fermentability and structure. Cellulose is insoluble, non-viscous, barely fermented; beta-glucan is soluble and highly viscous.

Psyllium and oat beta-glucan lower LDL because they gel; inulin does not

Jun 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis quotes a pharmacist's simple test: psyllium forms a thick gel in water while inulin dissolves clear. Only the gel-forming fibers trap bile acids, making the liver pull cholesterol from the blood, so for LDL choose psyllium or oat/barley beta-glucan, while inulin is a fine prebiotic.
