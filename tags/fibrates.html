Low HDL is mostly a sign of insulin resistance, and HDL itself is likely not causal

Dec 31, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 10 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains low HDL usually stems from insulin resistance and visceral fat. Losing visceral fat and replacing refined carbs with healthy fats raises it. A shared post argues randomized trials suggest HDL/ApoA-1 itself is unlikely a major causal driver.

HDL and ApoA-I raising therapies repeatedly failed, so HDL is probably not directly causal

Mar 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 9 ↩ 22 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis reviews why HDL or ApoA-I is probably not causal for heart disease. CETP inhibitor trials failed (ILLUMINATE with torcetrapib was stopped for harm; dal-OUTCOMES with dalcetrapib and ACCELERATE with evacetrapib showed no benefit), as did fibrate and niacin trials and CSL112 infusions, and Mendelian randomization agrees. He proposes HDL and ApoA-I mainly act as a proxy for insulin sensitivity, which is the real causal factor.

Lp(a) explained: an LDL particle with a tail, genetic, unaffected by standard drugs

Sep 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Lp(a)

Louis shares graphics explaining Lp(a). It is an LDL-like particle that is genetically set, affects about one in five people, and is not lowered by statins, ezetimibe, fibrates or fish oil. New drugs lower it by 80-99%, but benefit on outcomes was not yet shown.

Fibrates lower triglycerides and may reduce CVD risk in metabolic syndrome

Mar 01, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 3 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Brief overview of fibrates (fenofibrate, gemfibrozil), PPAR-alpha agonists that lower triglycerides, raise HDL and have anti-inflammatory effects. Meta-analyses and real-world data suggest a role for CVD risk reduction in people with metabolic syndrome, especially high triglycerides and low HDL. A 2020 review notes that several RCTs did not show clear benefit overall.
