Minnesota Coronary Experiment did not show that omega-6 fats cause heart disease

Jun 12, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 5 ↩ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

The poster says the 1989 report of the Minnesota trial found no difference in cardiovascular events, cardiovascular deaths or total mortality between treatment and control, and that the 2016 BMJ re-analysis added no new data changing this. The BMJ paper's observational cholesterol-mortality analysis is criticized as unreliable because causes of death were unknown, and hundreds of observational studies show omega-6 inversely related to heart disease.

Swapping saturated fat for polyunsaturated seed oils cut CVD events 29% in strict RCTs

Jun 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis states adipose omega-6 is linked to lower mortality in cohorts, and that RCTs meeting strict criteria (no trans fats, supplied food, 2+ years, biomarker-verified adherence, validated events) show a 29% reduction in heart disease events from replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat. He explains why the Minnesota Coronary Experiment, Sydney Heart Study and Rose Corn Oil Trial fail those criteria.
