Japan-ACS: pitavastatin and atorvastatin both regressed coronary plaque by about 17-18%

Sep 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 23 ↩ 21 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summary of the Japan-ACS trial, in which over 300 acute coronary syndrome patients were randomized to pitavastatin or atorvastatin and imaged with IVUS. Both statins produced similar, substantial regression of non-culprit plaque volume as LDL fell into the 80s mg/dL, which is one reason statins remain standard care after ACS.

Japan-ACS: modest statin doses cut non-culprit plaque volume by about 17-18%

Sep 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 5 Statins

The Japan-ACS randomized trial enrolled over 300 patients with acute coronary syndrome at 33 centers and compared 4 mg pitavastatin with 20 mg atorvastatin. Both produced similar major plaque regression over 8 to 12 months; Louis uses it to rebut claims that statins do nothing.

About 95% of acute-thrombosis heart attacks involve soft plaque, not calcified plaque

Oct 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post says about 64% of heart attacks from acute thrombosis come from rupture of soft lipid-rich plaque and about 25% from erosion of soft plaque, while only about 5% involve calcified nodules. Because statins and other lipid-lowering drugs stabilize and reverse soft plaque, the poster argues they address the plaque type most responsible for heart attacks.

JACC review: guidelines favor whole-food plant-based diets for CVD and cancer prevention

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis quotes a September 2025 JACC: CardioOncology state-of-the-art review noting that AHA, ACC, ACS, NCCN and WCRF/AICR guidelines all recommend a predominantly whole-food plant-based diet, as does the EAT-Lancet consensus. Patterns like Mediterranean and DASH are included, emphasizing fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, seeds and olive oil while minimizing red meat.
