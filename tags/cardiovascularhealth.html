Pooled blinded trials: statins cause only about 1 in 15 reported muscle aches

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 12 Statins

A pooled analysis of 19 blinded trials with 123,940 people found that over about four years, 27.1 of 100 on a statin reported muscle pain or weakness versus 26.6 of 100 on placebo. This implies that the vast majority of muscle complaints on statins are not caused by the drug, consistent with a nocebo effect.

Statins neither cause nor cure cancer: meta-analyses of randomized trials show no effect

Dec 31, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Statins

Reviews the evidence on statins and cancer. A 2012 meta-analysis of 134,537 participants in 22 RCTs found identical cancer incidence (1.4% per year) and mortality (0.5% per year) in statin users and non-users, and a 2023 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs found the same. Anticancer effects in specific contexts remain preclinical or suggestive.

Apr 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 1 Cardiovascular disease: the big picture

Luigi Fontana's summary of a NEJM pooled analysis of over 2 million people in 39 countries on blood pressure, non-HDL cholesterol, BMI, diabetes and smoking. At age 50, no risk factors versus all five meant up to 13.3 more CVD-free years and lower lifetime CVD risk; modifying risk factors at 55-60 still helped. Luigi Fontana's summary of an NEJM trial of lepodisiran, an siRNA that blocks hepatic Lp(a) production.
