Gokhan advises against paying for NMR particle-size panels, pointing to Gil Carvalho's summary of a genetic analysis in about 200,000 people over 14 years. ApoB and Lp(a) best captured lipid-driven cardiovascular risk and LDL particle size added no further information.

LDL-C vs LDL-P vs ApoB: particle number predicts risk better than cholesterol content

Jul 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

LDL-C measures cholesterol inside LDL particles while LDL-P counts the particles; they can be discordant (low LDL-C with many small particles), and particle number predicts risk better. Particle size adds little once number is accounted for, and ApoB, which counts all atherogenic particles, is the best test.

LDL particle size does not predict CVD risk once particle number or ApoB is accounted for

Jan 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 18 ↩ 3 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that LDL particle size is not associated with CVD risk after adjusting for total LDL particle number. A large particle size will not protect someone with high LDL or ApoB. Particle size can still reflect insulin sensitivity, but more accurate direct tests exist.

Eur Heart J 2025: apoB particle count, not size or type, drives coronary risk

Apr 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 10 ↩ 2 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis shares a 2025 European Heart Journal paper confirming that LDL particle size does not predict coronary risk once apoB is accounted for. Review: nuts, olive oil and nutraceuticals can lower small dense LDL

Sep 04, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 6 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Clinical review of foods and supplements affecting small dense LDL, an independent risk factor. Pistachios, hazelnuts and mixed nuts in a Mediterranean diet consistently improve sdLDL and particle size, and high-polyphenol olive oil (25 mL/day for 3 weeks) cut small LDL particles 15.3%.

Fact-checking keto and carnivore influencer claims about LDL, ApoB and saturated fat

May 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis lists what keto/carnivore influencers get right and wrong: ApoB is the best lipid marker, LDL-C usually tracks ApoB, particle size doesn't matter once particle number is counted, and most saturated fats raise LDL-P, ApoB and LDL-C. Studies finding saturated fat harmless used a standard Western diet as comparator.
