Nuts protect against CVD and diabetes and cause less weight gain than expected

Jan 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 11 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares a ZOE episode explaining why nuts are linked to lower CVD and diabetes risk. About 30% of the calories in nuts pass through the gut undigested, which helps explain why they cause less weight gain than their calories suggest.

A week of all-ultraprocessed food caused more weight gain than none in a crossover RCT

May 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Shared from Examine.com: a small randomized crossover trial in young men found a week of an entirely ultraprocessed diet led to more weight gain than a diet with no ultraprocessed food. The result fits observational data and an earlier RCT, though the post cautions that ultraprocessed food is a broad category and not all of it is equally harmful.
