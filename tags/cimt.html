Carotid plaque burden on ultrasound predicted mortality better than CAC in 5,700 people

Jun 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 30 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a prospective study of about 5,700 people without symptoms showing carotid ultrasound plaque burden (cPB) predicted all-cause mortality better than coronary calcium score. After adjustment, cPB had a hazard ratio of 1.23 per trend versus 1.15 for CAC, and Louis notes the test is cheap and radiation-free.

Pomegranate juice and heart health: CIMT, blood pressure and ACE evidence reviewed

Jan 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 16 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A review of pomegranate juice and cardiovascular disease credits its polyphenols (ellagitannins, punicalagins, anthocyanins). It cites a study where juice for up to a year reduced carotid intima-media thickness by up to 35% in carotid stenosis patients while controls rose 10%, and a meta-analysis of 8 RCTs showing lower blood pressure.

Plaque starts in neck and leg arteries decades before the heart; calcium scores miss it

Sep 17, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post summarises a video describing an NEJM study of 16,000 asymptomatic people showing plaque accumulates first in the neck and leg arteries, during the 20s to 40s, before the heart. Heart scans such as coronary calcium may therefore miss early disease; carotid intima-media thickness ultrasound can detect peripheral plaque, and the ankle-brachial index offers a simple at-home check for leg artery blockage.

Aged garlic extract slowed coronary calcium progression in three small RCTs

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 6 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes three small double-blind RCTs using Kyolic aged garlic extract (about 1.2 g/day) with CAC as a surrogate. In each, CAC progression over a year was lower than with placebo, though the studies were small and used some co-supplements.

Bale-Doneen protocol: aggressive multi-drug risk management reduced carotid IMT

Dec 03, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 3 ↩ 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Describes the Bale and Doneen approach to reversing plaque: lifestyle counseling, regular imaging and biomarker testing, statin-based lipid control with ezetimibe or niacin, SBP under 120, low-dose aspirin, insulin sensitizers and anti-inflammatories. In the 2016 community-based paper, carotid IMT measures fell markedly over a median of 3 years.

Staying keto while still tracking ApoB and plaque markers (shared from Dominic D'Agostino)

Aug 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 6 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Gokhan reshares a keto practitioner's account: 15 years of keto lowered blood pressure, triglycerides, HbA1c, insulin and hsCRP but raised LDL and ApoB until ezetimibe was added. Because of high Lp(a) and a decade of very high LDL, he used a carotid IMT test instead of CTA. The message is to keep monitoring markers on keto.

Case: carotid plaque regression with salmon, niacin and low-dose rosuvastatin

Dec 23, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a video by Dr. Ford Brewer describing his own plaque reversal. He ate salmon daily, added niacin and low-dose rosuvastatin and switched from an ARB to an ACE inhibitor, with a major decrease in carotid plaque volume and CIMT arterial age falling from 72 to 52. It is a single self-reported case in a video from a clinic that also sells services and supplements.
