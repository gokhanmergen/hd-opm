Taurine 3 g/day lowered insulin, HOMA-IR and inflammation in type 2 diabetes RCT

Jul 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 15 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A 120-person RCT gave 3 g taurine daily with a low-calorie diet for 8 weeks. Insulin, HOMA-IR, TNF, hs-CRP and MDA fell, but lipids, HbA1c and fasting glucose did not improve.

Higher blood linoleic acid linked to lower inflammation in about 1,900 people

Aug 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 18 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes a report on a biomarker study of nearly 1,900 people in which higher membrane linoleic acid, an omega-6 fat common in seed oils, tracked with lower inflammation and better glucose, insulin and HOMA-IR. Because it uses blood biomarkers rather than diet surveys, it is more robust.

One admin's story: high-risk blood disorder, LDL 70, hsCRP down from 1.2 to 0.2

Dec 05, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 22 · 💬 19 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Gokhan describes his personal regimen at 45 with a chronic blood disorder, tracking BMI, blood pressure, LDL, glucose and inflammation. His latest labs show LDL 70 and hsCRP 0.2, with a BMI of about 32.5 still being brought down and no calcification on heart CT.

Higher plasma linoleic acid tied to lower glucose, insulin and inflammation (n=1,894)

Jun 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 14 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A cross-sectional analysis of 1,894 people found higher plasma linoleic acid was consistently associated with lower glucose, insulin, HOMA-IR and inflammation markers (CRP, GlycA, SAA). Louis adds that dozens of prospective cohorts show higher omega-6 levels go with lower CVD and diabetes risk.

William Wallace shares the 2024 Tzang meta-analysis (Nutrition & Diabetes) of RCTs using 0.5 to 6 g/day taurine. Against placebo, every measured metabolic marker moved favourably, though effects were modest.

100+ trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose and insulin markers, saturated doesn't

Sep 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 6 ↩ 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes a meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials showing polyunsaturated fat, mostly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowers fasting glucose and A1C and improves insulin resistance and insulin secretion. Monounsaturated fat did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit.

Vitamin K improved fasting glucose and HOMA-IR and was linked to lower T2DM risk

Apr 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 6 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis suggests the main value of vitamin K may be improving insulin sensitivity and lowering risk of pre-diabetes and diabetes, citing a 2023 systematic review and meta-analysis. The quoted conclusion is that supplementation significantly affected fasting blood sugar and HOMA-IR and that vitamin K was associated with reduced risk of developing type 2 diabetes.

Insulin resistance precedes abnormal A1C by years; fasting insulin and OGTT catch it first

Jun 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares an analysis of the Whitehall II study showing that glucose and A1C stay normal for years while insulin resistance accumulates, because the pancreas compensates with extra insulin. He recommends fasting insulin, HOMA-IR and a 2-hour OGTT with insulin to detect it early.

100+ feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose control, saturated fat does not

Dec 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes the 2016 PLoS Medicine meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat, mainly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowered fasting glucose and A1C and improved insulin resistance and secretion, MUFA did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit. He pairs it with the 20-cohort biomarker study showing a 43% lower diabetes risk with high omega-6.

100+ RCTs and 20 cohorts: omega-6 improves glucose control, 43% lower diabetes risk

Oct 21, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes evidence that omega-6 polyunsaturated fat lowers fasting glucose and A1C and improves insulin resistance and secretion across more than 100 randomized trials. A pooled analysis of about 40,000 people from 20 prospective cohorts with biomarker-measured omega-6 found the top fifth had 43% lower diabetes risk than the bottom fifth. He lists the supporting meta-analyses.
