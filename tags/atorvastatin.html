Statins do not slow coronary calcium; severe-calcification odds hit 4.48 after 10+ years

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 41 Statins

Evidence review concluding statins reduce events and soft plaque but do not slow Agatston CAC progression, and that long-term cohorts show calcification accelerating without plateau, interpreted as plaque repair into stable scar. Trials: St. Francis (CAC unchanged, events down 33%), SALTIRE (progression 26% vs 18%) and BELLES (similar progression, ~11-14%).

Only lipophilic statins like atorvastatin enter the brain; PCSK9 drugs and ezetimibe don't

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 10 Brain health & dementia

Louis explains the blood-brain barrier and cholesterol: the brain makes its own cholesterol separately from blood, so non-statin LDL drugs and hydrophilic statins like rosuvastatin have little brain effect. Lipophilic statins do enter the brain, with promising observational data against Alzheimer's, and a 20,000-person atorvastatin trial is under way. Two prior trials showed statins do not worsen existing Alzheimer's, and midlife high LDL is a leading modifiable dementia risk (2024 Lancet Commission, about 7% of US cases per Louis).

Meta-analysis: lipid-lowering therapy regresses coronary plaque (Li 2021, SATURN)

Summarizes Li et al. 2021, a meta-analysis of 31 IVUS studies (4,997 patients) showing lipid-lowering therapy lowers atheroma volume, with maximal regression at LDL below 80 and HDL above 45 mg/dL. It also covers the SATURN analysis, where rosuvastatin 40 mg regressed plaque more than atorvastatin 80 mg.

Atorvastatin is generally preferred over rosuvastatin in chronic kidney disease

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Statins

Louis shares an MRCP UK explainer on statins in CKD: atorvastatin needs no renal dose adjustment (under 2% renal excretion), while rosuvastatin is 30% renally excreted, needs dose reduction at low eGFR and can cause reversible proteinuria at high doses. KDIGO advises a statin with or without ezetimibe in CKD stages 3-5 not on dialysis.

Amyloid-beta drives excess astrocyte cholesterol and impairs brain waste clearance in mice

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Brain health & dementia

A Nature Neuroscience mouse study found amyloid-beta raises calcium activity in astrocytes, boosting cholesterol production and removing AQP4 water channels, which impairs the glymphatic waste-clearing system. Calming astrocytes, blocking cholesterol synthesis or giving atorvastatin restored clearance and memory in mice. Human trials are needed, but the mechanism may partly explain observational links between statin use and lower dementia risk.

JAPAN-ACS sub-study: statins lowered AGEs, which were linked to plaque progression

Sep 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Statins

A sub-study of the JAPAN-ACS trial found high baseline advanced glycation end products (AGEs) were independently associated with plaque progression, and statin therapy significantly lowered AGE levels. Louis notes the trial produced the largest plaque regression, about -17%, in a drug trial.
