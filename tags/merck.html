Personalized mRNA vaccine succeeds in phase 3 melanoma trial after ~50% phase 2 benefit

Aug 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 12 Cancer

A personalized mRNA vaccine plus Keytruda improved recurrence-free and distant metastasis-free survival in a phase 3 trial of more than 1,100 patients with melanoma. The earlier phase 2 trial showed roughly a 50% reduction in recurrence or death at 5 years. Approval might come as early as 2027, and trials are under way in other cancers.

Personalized mRNA melanoma vaccine could extend to other cancers, with trials ongoing

Aug 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Cancer

Louis adds that the real promise of the personalized mRNA vaccine is its use across cancers beyond melanoma. The shared physician explanation stresses it treats cancer after tumor removal rather than preventing it. Trials are ongoing in non-small cell lung, kidney and bladder cancer.
