Tomato paste for 3 months improved attention and memory in a randomized crossover trial

Sep 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 37 · 💬 13 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a 2026 randomized crossover trial in healthy adults in which daily tomato intake improved cognitive performance and modulated functional brain networks. Media coverage describes modest gains in attention and memory after three months of tomato paste. The trial is small.

Lycopene may help prevent LDL oxidation inside the artery wall

Sep 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 6 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

The poster claims lycopene is one of very few antioxidants that might prevent oxidation of LDL within the artery wall, with an explanation in comments. No data are given in the post.

Lutein may modestly reverse plaque but has no proven effect on cardiovascular events

Jul 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

Lutein reduced plaque over 12 months in an RCT and higher blood lutein linked to less plaque over 18 months in ~500 people, but a large trial of lutein plus zeaxanthin did not reduce CV death over five years.

Lutein shrank carotid wall thickness in a small RCT but heart outcome benefit is unproven

Jul 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis cites a 144-person randomized trial in which 12 months of lutein, or lutein plus lycopene, reduced carotid intima-media thickness. The commentary notes no established evidence lutein reduces cardiovascular outcomes. Lutein plus zeaxanthin slows macular degeneration (AREDS2).

Two to three tablespoons of tomato paste daily improved brain speed in a small trial

Nov 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a report on a small randomized crossover trial in humans, published in Antioxidants, in which daily tomato paste may improve concentration and information processing speed. It is a small study and the post adds no further detail.
