Plant-based diet quality and dementia: whole plant foods protect, refined ones raise risk

Apr 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A new study shared via MedPage Today links whole grains, vegetables, vegetable oils, nuts and legumes to lower dementia risk and refined grains, juices, white potatoes and added sugars to higher risk. Middle-age and older adults who improved diet quality had an 11% reduced dementia diagnosis risk over 10 years.

CHOICE trial: hearing aids gave nearly six-fold cognitive improvement in MCI

Sep 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

A secondary outcome of the CHOICE randomized trial found cognitive improvement in 15.34% of older adults with mild cognitive impairment given hearing aids versus 2.36% of controls. The primary outcome was null and the authors say the surprisingly large effect needs replication.

Phase 2 trial begins testing benfotiamine (vitamin B1) in Alzheimer's

Apr 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares news of an 18-month Phase 2 trial at Rutgers testing benfotiamine, a fat-soluble thiamine form, to improve cognition in Alzheimer's.
