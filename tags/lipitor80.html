PROVE IT-TIMI 22: atorvastatin 80 mg early after a heart attack cut 30-day events 28%

Jul 31, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 11 Statins

The post explains why high-intensity atorvastatin is started right after a heart attack: in PROVE IT-TIMI 22, 30-day death/MI/rehospitalization was lower with atorvastatin 80 mg than pravastatin 40 mg. The rapid effect is attributed to pleiotropic effects (anti-inflammatory, plaque and endothelial stabilization), though LDL lowering itself may contribute.

High-dose atorvastatin after heart attack cut events ~30% in 30 days (PROVE IT-TIMI 22)

Jan 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 16 Statins

Louis explains why patients are immediately given atorvastatin 80 mg after a heart attack: PROVE IT-TIMI 22 showed an almost 30% reduction in death, repeat heart attack or rehospitalization within 30 days versus a lower-potency statin. The speed suggests pleiotropic effects (anti-inflammatory, plaque and endothelial stabilizing) rather than LDL lowering. Lipophilic statins at maximum dose work best in this setting.

Louis reposts a note explaining why patients are put on atorvastatin 80 mg right after a heart attack.
