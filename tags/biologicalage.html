Coffee linked to younger epigenetic age and up to 27% lower mortality, per FoundMyFitness

Sep 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 8 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A FoundMyFitness solocast summary says each extra daily cup is associated with a roughly 0.7-1 year younger epigenetic age, that 3+ cups daily links to 34-41% lower odds of accelerated biological aging, and coffee drinkers show up to 27% lower all-cause mortality. Decaf shows similar benefits.

Mediterranean and DASH diets slow biological aging most, per npj Aging perspective

Sep 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares an npj Aging review proposing that diet acts as a molecular signal modulating aging through inflammation, the microbiome and resilience, and says biological-age metrics show the traditional Mediterranean and DASH diets slow aging the most so far.

Mediterranean-like macronutrient mix lowered estimated biological age in 4 weeks

Sep 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 3 Aging & longevity

A University of Sydney study in 104 adults aged 65-75 found three diet groups reduced estimated biological age after four weeks, with the strongest evidence for an omnivorous diet of 28-29% fat, 14% protein and 53% carbohydrate. Biological age was estimated from 20 biomarkers including cholesterol, insulin and C-reactive protein.

CAC score may be a simple measure of overall biological aging

May 18, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares an article by Dr. Joel Kahn describing a study that proposes the coronary artery calcium score as a broad marker of biological aging, not just heart disease. The quoted authors note CAC is widely available, takes seconds and often costs $100 or less.

Muscle biological age is the top cell-type predictor of mortality in 60,542 people

Jun 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Aging & longevity

Louis shares a 2026 Nature Medicine study using plasma proteins from 60,542 people to estimate the biological age of over 40 cell types. Skeletal muscle aging best predicted all-cause mortality. He cautions about reverse causality but notes it offers a biological rationale for resistance training lowering mortality.
