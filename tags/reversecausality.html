U-shaped mortality curves for cholesterol, BMI, A1C and BP reflect reverse causality

Low LDL in cancer patients reflects reverse causality: tumours scavenge LDL

Why high cholesterol 'protects' in observational data: reverse causation, not low-LDL harm

Sep 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 7 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis quotes a Barbell Medicine explanation that low cholesterol in sick, older people reflects disease lowering cholesterol, not the reverse.
