Methylene blue stains the brain and heart blue-green and fails the Ames test, Louis warns

Louis warns against methylene blue supplementation, claiming it stains internal organs, especially the brain and heart, blue-green as shown in autopsies, and that it fails the Ames test, indicating carcinogenic potential. He links a Substack article on its dark side. The post gives no trial data.

Methylene blue is a toxic industrial dye with unknown long-term risk as an anti-aging pill

Dec 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 11 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis warns that methylene blue is an industrial textile dye that fails the Ames test, is non-biodegradable and is considered a water pollutant. In medicine it is used only short term or for serious diseases, so long-term anti-aging use has unknown harms.
