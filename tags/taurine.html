Taurine deficiency may drive aging; Columbia study spans worms, mice, monkeys and humans

Mar 01, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 28 ↩ 4 Aging & longevity

Louis highlights a 2023 Columbia University study on taurine and aging in worms, mice, monkeys and humans, noting taurine also benefits the cardiovascular system. Taurine and cardiovascular disease: anti-inflammatory and vascular effects

Jan 27, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 21 · 💬 26 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

A long evidence summary on taurine: deficiency causes cardiomyopathy in cats and in humans with transporter defects, higher taurine intake correlates with lower coronary disease, and animal studies show reduced atherosclerosis and vessel relaxation. The author is considering a supplement against chronic inflammation; most evidence cited is animal or observational.

Taurine 3 g/day lowered insulin, HOMA-IR and inflammation in type 2 diabetes RCT

Jul 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 15 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A 120-person RCT gave 3 g taurine daily with a low-calorie diet for 8 weeks. Insulin, HOMA-IR, TNF, hs-CRP and MDA fell, but lipids, HbA1c and fasting glucose did not improve.

Taurine 1.5 g/day for 2 weeks reversed endothelial dysfunction in type 1 diabetes

Apr 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 26 · 💬 11 ↩ 1 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A 2010 study found two weeks of taurine reduced vascular stiffness and improved brachial artery relaxation (FMD) to healthy control levels, with a proposed mechanism of improved nitric oxide bioavailability.

Mouse taurine doses of 500-1000 mg/kg scale to about 2.8-5.7 g for a 70 kg human

Oct 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 7 Aging & longevity

Louis explains that the 2023 Columbia taurine study used 500-1000 mg/kg in mice, and that allometric scaling for faster mouse metabolism converts this to about 2.8-5.7 g for a 70 kg person. He suggests that is the range to test in human trials, since mouse mg/kg doses cannot be applied directly to humans.

Nature study challenges the idea that taurine declines with age

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis summarizes a Nature paper from Rafael de Cabo's NIA group finding that taurine levels do not decline with age in humans, monkeys or mice, contradicting a high-profile Columbia study. He notes the new data are mostly longitudinal, whereas the Columbia data were largely cross-sectional, and that unchanged levels do not necessarily mean supplementation has no value.

Pooled data from 9 randomized trials found taurine supplementation lowered fasting insulin, triglycerides and total cholesterol. In overweight participants BMI fell by 1.14 kg/m2, and in obesity HbA1c and HOMA-IR improved.

Yale: taurine loss triggers NLRP3 inflammasome; raising taurine eased inflammaging in mice

Nov 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Inflammation & CRP

Louis shares a summary of a Yale Cell study showing macrophages release up to 80% of their taurine after inflammatory triggers, which helps activate the NLRP3 inflammasome. Restoring taurine suppressed NLRP3 activation, and raising taurine in older mice improved grip strength, coordination, some glucose measures and inflammatory markers. It is preclinical research.

Taurine lowers age-related inflammation via the NLRP3 inflammasome in mouse macrophages

Oct 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A Cell paper from Yale reports that taurine levels increase with age in mice and humans, and boosting taurine further in aged mice increased healthspan and dampened inflammation by regulating the NLRP3 inflammasome in macrophages.

Taurine is not a reliable aging biomarker, says new multi-species study

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A shared summary of a large longitudinal study across humans (26-100 years), monkeys and mice finds taurine levels do not consistently fall with age, often rise (especially in women), and vary more between individuals than with age. The post notes that taurine, like NAD+ boosters and resveratrol, shows promise in animals but human evidence is limited.
