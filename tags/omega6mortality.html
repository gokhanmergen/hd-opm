Seed oil intake rose 5x since 1950 while US heart disease death rates fell over 4x

Nov 27, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 30 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis argues that since 1950 vegetable oil consumption has risen about 5x and butter and lard intake has fallen 2-3x, while heart disease death rates fell by over 4x and life expectancy rose from 60 to 78. He presents the raw unadjusted correlation as evidence against claims that seed oils drive heart disease. It is an ecological correlation, not a controlled test.

Replacing 10 g/day of butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality

Oct 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares Harvard research on 221,054 people from the Nurses' Health Studies I and II and the Health Professionals Follow-Up Study, published in JAMA Internal Medicine. Higher plant-oil intake (especially soybean, canola, olive) was linked to lower total, cancer and cardiovascular mortality, while butter was linked to higher total and cancer mortality; a modeled swap of 10 g butter for plant oils lowered mortality by 17%.

NY Times: higher omega-6 linked to lower CVD, diabetes, cancer and mortality

Nov 24, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 10 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a NY Times article on the seed oil myth, noting it quotes researchers who studied the question in humans. The article states that people who eat more omega-6 or have higher blood levels have lower risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, cancer and earlier death, and links to the relevant meta-analyses.

Seed oils don't raise inflammation markers; butter linked to higher mortality

Mar 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 11 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes a news article in which Martha Belury says omega-6 linoleic acid does not significantly change blood inflammatory markers and that the omega-6 to omega-3 imbalance claim is oversimplified. It also reports the Brigham and Women's study of over 200,000 adults for 30+ years: highest butter intake linked to 15% higher mortality, highest plant oil intake to 16% lower.

Higher blood linoleic acid linked to lower inflammation and cardiometabolic risk

Jun 04, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A shared summary reports observational data presented at NUTRITION 2025 (Kevin Maki) that people with higher blood linoleic acid had lower CRP, better glucose control and healthier BMI. It cites meta-analyses linking higher omega-6 to lower CVD, cancer and all-cause mortality, and stresses whole-food sources.

Plant oils instead of butter linked to lower mortality in 200,000 people over 30 years

Sep 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A Mass General Brigham study following more than 200,000 people for 30 years found diets higher in butter and low in plant oils were associated with elevated risk of premature death, while replacing butter with plant oils was linked to lower mortality.

Scoping review of about 500 studies finds seed oil concerns lack scientific foundation

Louis shares a 2026 review in Critical Reviews in Food Science and Nutrition covering RCTs, cohorts, biomarker and Mendelian randomization data on seed oils and linoleic acid. It found no inflammatory effect of linoleic acid and supports replacing saturated with polyunsaturated fat for CVD.

Swapping saturated fat for polyunsaturated seed oils cut CVD events 29% in strict RCTs

Jun 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis states adipose omega-6 is linked to lower mortality in cohorts, and that RCTs meeting strict criteria (no trans fats, supplied food, 2+ years, biomarker-verified adherence, validated events) show a 29% reduction in heart disease events from replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat. He explains why the Minnesota Coronary Experiment, Sydney Heart Study and Rose Corn Oil Trial fail those criteria.
