'Grounding' during sleep is unnatural because primates evolved sleeping off the ground

Mar 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 36 Myths, misinformation & controversies

Louis argues that sleeping grounded (connected to the earth) is not natural, because primates slept in trees for tens of millions of years and wood is an insulator, so a raised bed mirrors natural primate behavior. This is an evolutionary argument and cites no study.

Review: aggressive LDL lowering outweighs theoretical risks, yet few reach <55 mg/dL

Oct 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 5 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Summarises a July 2025 review: guidelines now target LDL-C <55 mg/dL (<40 after a recurrent event), PCSK9 inhibitors add 59-62% LDL reduction, and evidence (FOURIER, CTT meta-analysis, PCSK9 genetics) supports benefit to very low levels. It says safety concerns such as hemorrhagic stroke, cognitive decline (EBBINGHAUS) and hormone disruption are unsupported, while fewer than one in four high-risk patients reach target.

Lp(a) has no known essential role in modern humans and may even have minor upsides

Sep 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 2 Lp(a)

Louis explains that people with very low or undetectable Lp(a) show no significant ill effects, so it does not appear essential today. It may have conferred an evolutionary advantage to hunter-gatherer ancestors, and high Lp(a) might lower the risk of some conditions such as diabetes and hemorrhagic stroke, even though it strongly raises CVD risk. He supports this with an AI-generated explanation.
