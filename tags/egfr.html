Obicetrapib lowered Alzheimer's marker p-tau217, most in APOE4 carriers (BROADWAY)

Jul 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 6 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

In the phase 3 BROADWAY trial, obicetrapib 10 mg cut LDL-C by 29.9%. A biomarker substudy of 1,535 participants (367 APOE4 carriers) found reduced p-tau217, greatest in APOE4 carriers, shaping a planned Alzheimer's prevention trial.

Rosuvastatin can stress kidneys at high dose; atorvastatin appears kidney-friendly

Jul 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Statins

Louis explains rare dose-related proteinuria, hematuria or AKI with rosuvastatin, especially 40 mg or with CKD. PLANET I and II found rosuvastatin 40 mg lowered eGFR whereas atorvastatin 80 mg reduced proteinuria and preserved function.

Measuring kidney health: add cystatin C and urine albumin-to-creatinine to creatinine eGFR

Sep 09, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 2 Kidney health

Gokhan reminds that creatinine-based eGFR alone is insufficient. Cystatin C eGFR can differ in about 30% of people, so the combined CKD-EPI creatinine-cystatin C equation is best, and urine protein and albumin-to-creatinine ratio catch early disease. Test at least yearly if on many meds or supplements.

Kidney health tracking: cystatin C, urine albumin/creatinine and more beyond creatinine

Sep 28, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Kidney health

Gokhan argues creatinine and eGFR are imperfect kidney markers and ranks five tests worth tracking, especially for people on many supplements or drugs: cystatin C (very useful, independent of muscle mass), CBC/chemistry/lipids, urine albumin/creatinine ratio, urine protein/creatinine and urinalysis.

Lubiprostone preserved kidney function in a 118-patient phase II CKD trial

Aug 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 2 Kidney health

A phase II trial in Science Advances found that the constipation drug lubiprostone (16 ug daily) preserved eGFR over 24 weeks versus decline on placebo. Surprisingly, it did not cut uremic toxins; instead it shifted the gut microbiome and raised spermidine. Kidney disease commonly accompanies heart disease.

Louis notes that kidney disease is a major risk factor for CVD and dementia and that the latest AHA risk calculator now includes eGFR. A shared review describes the kidney's crosstalk with the heart, brain, gut, liver and lungs.
