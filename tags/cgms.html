Beef and whey raise insulin more than pasta, oatmeal or glucose: use the insulin index

May 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 71 ↩ 27 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis points out that some proteins are more insulinogenic than carbs: beef raised insulin more than equal-calorie white pasta or oatmeal in the 1997 insulin index study, and whey more than glucose or white bread in a 2012 study. He says influencers have misled people about this and recommends learning the insulin index rather than assuming carbs alone drive insulin.

Glucose and insulin are different: CGMs show glucose only, and beef or whey spike insulin

Feb 21, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 40 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis explains that glucose is a sugar and insulin a hormone, so a CGM tells you nothing directly about insulin. Red meat and whey can produce high insulin with little or no glucose rise because amino acids need insulin to enter muscle.

CGM glucose estimates ran 0.6% above lab A1C in people without diabetes

May 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 50 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis highlights a study finding that the CGM-derived glucose management indicator (GMI) overestimates lab HbA1c in people without diabetes. A 0.6% gap is large enough to turn a normal A1C of 5.1% into an apparent prediabetic 5.7%, so CGM-based A1C estimates should not replace lab tests in healthy people.

RCT: 60 g of pistachios daily lowered HbA1c 0.2% and LDL 10 mg/dL in prediabetes

Mar 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a 12-week RCT in 120 people with prediabetes testing 60 g of pistachios daily (30 g before breakfast and dinner). Pistachios lowered HbA1c, post-meal glucose spikes, LDL, triglycerides and waist circumference, while blood pressure, fasting glucose and insulin did not change.
