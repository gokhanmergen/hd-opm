Mutated blood stem cells (CHIP) may be a major cause of heart disease, doubling MI risk

Apr 08, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 13 Inflammation & CRP

Louis explains clonal hematopoiesis (CHIP): age-related mutations in bone-marrow stem cells yield white blood cells that drive inflammation and plaque growth, and the same mutations link to leukemia. A quoted report says CHIP carriers had a 54% higher chance of dying within a decade, from heart attacks and strokes, and mouse transplants of CHIP-mutated cells produced fast-growing, inflamed plaques. Louis credits Gokhan Can for pointing him to this.

Prune Study: 50 g of prunes daily preserved hip bone density in postmenopausal women

Jan 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 9 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

The shared summary describes a 12-month RCT of 235 postmenopausal women randomized to no prunes, 50 g or 100 g of prunes per day. The 50 g group lost significantly less hip bone density than controls; 100 g added no benefit and had lower adherence.

Single dose of donor stem cells raised walking endurance about 20% in frail older adults

Feb 25, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 9 Aging & longevity

Describes a trial of donor bone-marrow mesenchymal stem cells in 148 walking adults aged 70-85 with frailty (118 on varying doses, 30 on placebo). The cells lack immune-triggering surface proteins so no immunosuppressants were needed; at nine months the highest dose group walked about 60 m farther in a six-minute walk test than at baseline.

One zoledronate infusion cut vertebral fractures about 44% over 10 years in women 50-60

Jan 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 9 Hormones, women's & men's health

Gokhan shares the NEJM 10-year randomized trial of infrequent zoledronate in early postmenopausal women (50-60 years) with normal-to-low-normal bone density. Cholesterol as a master regulator of blood stem cells, driving inflammatory hematopoiesis

Oct 19, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Inflammation & CRP

Review of preclinical evidence that high LDL pulls hematopoietic stem cells out of dormancy, causing proliferation, oxidative stress, telomere shortening, impaired repopulation and lasting epigenetic changes that program blood and immune cells toward chronic inflammation. This offers a molecular basis for residual inflammatory risk after cholesterol is treated.

Shin bone loading may speed brain injury recovery via PIEZO1 in mice and pigs

Oct 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

A Nature Neuroscience paper found that compressing the tibia for a few minutes a day after traumatic brain injury improved movement and memory in mice and pigs. Bone cells sensed the force through the PIEZO1 sensor, and turning it off removed the benefit. The poster calls it the most interesting bone study he has read in a long time.

Bisphosphonates for bone loss: fracture reductions summarized

Oct 13, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Review of bisphosphonate evidence for osteoporosis. Alendronate cuts vertebral and non-vertebral fractures by about 50% in women with prior vertebral fractures; yearly IV zoledronic acid cuts vertebral fractures 70% and hip fractures 40%; a 2017 meta-analysis found overall fracture OR 0.62. Not a heart topic but relevant to aging.

Mechanical stress on bones made mice live nearly 3 months longer via anti-aging signals

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Exercise & fitness

A news report on a mouse study found that mechanical strain on the skeleton, similar to what occurs in exercise and resistance training, makes osteocytes secrete anti-aging chemicals. Gently squeezing leg bones extended mouse lifespan by nearly 3 months, roughly nine human years. A related study found bone stress improved memory and motor function after traumatic brain injury in mice and pigs.

A shared summary of a University of Surrey, John Innes Centre and Quadram Institute study reporting that vitamin D2 supplementation can reduce D3 levels below those of people taking no supplements. D3 is the more effective form.
