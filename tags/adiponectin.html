Pioglitazone slowed coronary plaque progression and shrank necrotic core in diabetics

Jan 14, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 27 ↩ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Data-rich overview of pioglitazone's effects on plaque. In PERISCOPE (543 type 2 diabetes patients with CAD, 18 months, IVUS) pioglitazone lowered percent atheroma volume while glimepiride raised it. A smaller trial (Ogasawara 2009) found reduced necrotic core and higher adiponectin, suggesting benefit via reduced inflammation.

Sumo wrestlers stay metabolically healthy because heavy exercise shifts fat under the skin

Mar 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis explains why sumo wrestlers, despite huge calorie and refined-carbohydrate intake, store little fat in the liver and pancreas and little visceral fat. He attributes this to up to 7 hours of daily exercise, which releases adiponectin and steers fat storage to subcutaneous tissue, a reason exercise protects against metabolic syndrome and diabetes.

Over 30 RCTs show omega-6 linoleic acid does not raise inflammatory markers

Feb 07, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis argues that giving up seed oils will not slow heart disease and that swapping them for saturated fat would likely worsen it. Chronically high insulin drives aging biology, and calorie restriction lowers it

Aug 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Luigi Fontana explains that insulin is a nutrient-signaling hormone and that persistent activation of the insulin/IGF-1-AKT-mTOR network is linked to cell growth, aging biology and cancer-related pathways. Calorie restriction and weight loss are among the strongest ways to improve insulin sensitivity.
