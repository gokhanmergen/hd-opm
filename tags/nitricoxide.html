Humming boosts nasal nitric oxide about 15-fold; a small RCT hints at lower blood pressure

Oct 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 20 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis fact-checks a viral claim that humming raises nitric oxide 1400%: that equals about a 15-fold rise in nasal production (not 'brain' production as the post said), a finding from Weitzberg's 2002 work. A small randomized trial of bee-humming breathing in hypertension suggests blood pressure may fall, though whether nitric oxide is the mechanism is unclear.

Johns Hopkins arginine RCT stopped early: 6 deaths vs 0 in heart-attack survivors

Nov 29, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 25 ↩ 3 Supplements & vitamins

Louis warns that L-arginine supplements, taken to raise nitric oxide, can be dangerous after a heart attack. A Johns Hopkins randomized trial of 153 heart attack survivors given 3 g arginine three times daily was stopped after six months when six deaths occurred in the arginine group and none on placebo. Citrulline, which the body converts to arginine, might carry a similar concern, which is his speculation.

Daily peanuts improved brain blood flow, memory and BP in older adults in a crossover RCT

Jul 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A Maastricht randomized crossover trial of 31 adults aged 60-75 found 60 g of peanuts daily for 16 weeks raised cerebral blood flow 3.6%, verbal memory 5.8%, and lowered systolic BP 5 mmHg.

Native, non-oxidized LDL alone pushed endothelial cells into an oxidative state

Jun 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 13 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis shares a post describing a classic in-vitro study (PMID 7543827) where native LDL made endothelial cells release about four times more superoxide, partly from the enzyme that normally makes nitric oxide, forming peroxynitrite. It is cited to counter claims that LDL is harmless unless oxidized.

Black tea improved artery function in a Berlin trial, but adding milk erased the benefit

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 8 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A European Heart Journal study (2007) from Charite Berlin had 16 healthy postmenopausal women drink black tea, tea with skimmed milk, or water on three days. Flow-mediated dilation improved with plain tea but the benefit disappeared with milk. Rat artery experiments showed tea boosts nitric oxide and milk caseins block it. Louis notes many studies show polyphenols improve FMD.

White potatoes: short-term endothelial decline (MODERATE) and more hypertension in cohorts

Apr 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 7 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Summarizes the MODERATE trial, where participants who ate boiled, baked or mashed potatoes in the previous 24 hours had 1.7% lower flow-mediated dilation, roughly the 1.2%-per-decade aging decline for 14 years, as a short-term effect. It is linked to cohort data (Nurses' Health Studies and Health Professionals) tying baked, boiled or mashed potatoes to hypertension.
