Walter Willett: unsaturated fats, Mediterranean eating and daily habits at 81

Oct 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 4 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A Washington Post interview with Harvard nutrition researcher Walter Willett. He says his group's data showed total fat was irrelevant, trans fats were bad, saturated fat versus refined carbohydrate was only weakly related to heart disease, and unsaturated fats were linked to lower heart disease and mortality. He also describes his own diet, including oats, nuts, blueberries and a plant-based lunch.

Willett's three diet changes: less refined starch and sugar, no soda, less processed meat

Nov 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares a Walter Willett clip with his top three changes to the typical American diet: reduce refined starch, sugar and white potatoes; eliminate sugar-sweetened beverages; and reduce processed meat. Replacing processed meat with soy, beans or nuts is a double win.

Summary of how micronutrients such as vitamin C and CoQ10 affect endothelial function

Jul 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Louis shares a Linus Pauling Institute review summarising studies on micronutrients and endothelial dysfunction. Quoted examples include pooled RCTs showing vitamin C improved endothelial markers in heart failure, atherosclerosis or diabetes but not hypertension, and CoQ10 150-300 mg/day modestly improving function in five small trials.

Willett: protein source matters more than amount, from ~300,000 people over ~30 years

Jan 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Protein

Louis cites the Harvard Nurses' Health and Health Professionals cohorts behind Walter Willett's comments. Protein percentage of calories showed little relation to health outcomes across the studied range, but the source of protein did.
