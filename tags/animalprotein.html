Insulin stimulates liver cholesterol production, linking insulin resistance to high LDL

Jun 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 22 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

The post explains that insulin signals the liver to make more cholesterol by keeping HMG-CoA reductase active, one reason insulin resistance and metabolic syndrome go with high cholesterol. It argues protein, especially animal protein, also raises insulin, partly offset by glucagon, and that swapping animal for plant protein lowers cholesterol beyond saturated fat and fiber effects. Much of the protein argument is the poster's inference.

Harvard cohorts: plant protein linked to lower CVD and total mortality over 30 years

Sep 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 15 Protein

Louis summarizes Harvard Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study findings that higher plant protein intake is strongly associated with lower CVD and all-cause mortality over 30 years, whereas animal protein is not, driven by red and processed meat. Seafood and yogurt look favorable. The shared text cites a Rotterdam Study plus meta-analysis of protein intake and mortality.

Harvard cohort: unhealthy low-carb diets raise diabetes risk, plant-based ones lower it

High insulin tells the liver to make cholesterol, tying insulin resistance to high LDL

Louis explains that insulin signals the liver to produce cholesterol, one reason insulin-resistant people tend to have high LDL. Losing visceral and especially liver fat to correct insulin resistance should therefore bring LDL down naturally. Supported by a Mass General Brigham explainer.

Aug 31, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 15 ↩ 7 Protein

Louis summarizes a Nurses' Health Study analysis of midlife protein and later-life health. Total and animal protein were unfavorably associated with chronic disease, while plant and dairy protein were favorable. Replacing 3% of energy with plant protein (from fats, carbs, animal or dairy protein) raised odds of healthy aging by 22-58%, and the protein came mainly from food rather than supplements.

Six converging evidence lines favor plant protein over red and processed meat

Six lines of evidence favor whole-food plant protein over red and processed meat

Nov 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Protein

Louis recommends an article by group member Mert Erogul MD on protein and aging. It argues six lines of evidence converge: growth-signaling genetics, IGF-1 biomarker data, rodent protein restriction through FGF21 and mTORC1, the roles of methionine, isoleucine and valine, short-term human trials, and epidemiology showing source matters more than amount. Plant protein from whole foods is favored.

CARDIA: low-carb diets in young adults tied to more coronary calcium if animal-based

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 3 · 💬 7 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Posts the abstract of a CARDIA analysis of 2,226 participants (mean age 40.4) followed 8.3 years. Higher carbohydrate intake was inversely associated with CAC progression (HR 0.731); an animal-based low-carb score predicted higher progression (HR 1.456) but a plant-based score did not (HR 1.016). 26.5% had CAC progression.
