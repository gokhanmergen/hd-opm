Free book 'Eat, Drink, and Be Healthy' recommended for the Mediterranean diet

Sep 27, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 15 ↩ 9 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis recommends the Harvard Medical School guide 'Eat, Drink, and Be Healthy' as the best lay book on the Mediterranean diet and links a free PDF. He quotes it saying good eating plus healthy weight, exercise and not smoking can prevent 80% of heart attacks, 90% of type 2 diabetes and 70% of colorectal cancer. He notes the diet can be adapted to low-carb, Mexican, Asian and other versions.

How to follow a traditional Mediterranean diet: do's and don'ts

Apr 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 2 ↩ 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares Gokhan Can's summary of Mediterranean diet do's and don'ts for people asking how to do it properly, and recommends the book behind the food pyramid. Advice includes plenty of fruit and vegetables, whole grains, olive oil, nuts and fatty fish, legumes, limited red meat and minimal saturated fat such as butter and cream.
