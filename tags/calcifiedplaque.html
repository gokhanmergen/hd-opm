Calcified plaque is a marker, the goal should be reversing soft plaque

Aug 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 44 · 💬 51 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis argues a high CAC score predicts risk mainly because calcified plaque reflects the amount of soft plaque, which is the dangerous part. He says to focus on reversing or stabilizing soft plaque rather than lowering CAC, and notes randomized outcome trials are needed.

Vitamin K2 MK-7 slowed coronary calcification in a 2-year RCT; soft plaque not measured

Sep 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

A shared post on a new 2-year randomized trial notes that MK-7 reduced progression of coronary calcification versus placebo. It cautions that calcified plaque is generally more stable, soft plaque was not measured, and the trial was not designed to show fewer cardiovascular events.

Charles Grashow's protocol to regress coronary calcium over two years

Dec 02, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 7 ↩ 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Describes how Charles Grashow responded to worsening coronary calcium in 2020-2021 with an intensive regimen of lifestyle, high-dose nattokinase, LDL-lowering and anti-inflammatory supplements and off-label drugs. His later scans reportedly showed that the trajectory of his coronary disease had reversed. It is a single-patient story, not a trial.

About 95% of acute-thrombosis heart attacks involve soft plaque, not calcified plaque

Oct 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post says about 64% of heart attacks from acute thrombosis come from rupture of soft lipid-rich plaque and about 25% from erosion of soft plaque, while only about 5% involve calcified nodules. Because statins and other lipid-lowering drugs stabilize and reverse soft plaque, the poster argues they address the plaque type most responsible for heart attacks.

Staying keto while still tracking ApoB and plaque markers (shared from Dominic D'Agostino)

Aug 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 6 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Gokhan reshares a keto practitioner's account: 15 years of keto lowered blood pressure, triglycerides, HbA1c, insulin and hsCRP but raised LDL and ApoB until ezetimibe was added. Because of high Lp(a) and a decade of very high LDL, he used a carotid IMT test instead of CTA. The message is to keep monitoring markers on keto.

With a CAC score of zero, high LDL still deserves treatment over a 30-40 year horizon

Jun 05, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Q&A: a zero CAC is good news but only a current snapshot. LDL drives plaque over decades, and waiting for calcium to appear means significant plaque has already formed, so proactive LDL lowering still protects against future events.
