Nearly half of dementia cases may be preventable; Willett recommends the MIND diet

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a BBC Science Focus article noting the 2024 Lancet Commission estimated 45% of dementia cases are attributable to modifiable risk factors. Walter Willett says diet is most important and recommends the MIND diet to put dementia off for some years.

Daily blueberries altered 600+ blood-cell genes; metabolites tied to artery dilation

Oct 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A FoundMyFitness podcast summary says daily blueberry intake altered the expression of over 600 genes in blood cells and produced anthocyanin-derived metabolites, some associated with better artery dilation. The point is that blueberries do more than act as antioxidants.
