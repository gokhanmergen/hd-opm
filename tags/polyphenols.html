Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 8 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A European Heart Journal study (2007) from Charite Berlin had 16 healthy postmenopausal women drink black tea, tea with skimmed milk, or water on three days. Flow-mediated dilation improved with plain tea but the benefit disappeared with milk. Rat artery experiments showed tea boosts nitric oxide and milk caseins block it. Louis notes many studies show polyphenols improve FMD.

Gut microbes turn fiber and polyphenols into GLP-1 and other satiety hormones

Jan 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Gut microbiome

Louis quotes Scientific American: the body makes its own incretin hormones such as GLP-1 and PYY, and gut bacteria ferment indigestible fiber and polyphenols into molecules that stimulate them. These hormones help regulate blood sugar, appetite and the speed of food moving through the gut (the 'colonic brake'). This suggests whole plant foods support the same pathways targeted by Ozempic and Wegovy.
