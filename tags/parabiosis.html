Skepticism of a startup's claimed drug combo that mimics young blood for aging

Sep 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 9 Aging & longevity

Louis comments on a startup claiming a two-drug combination replicates the effect of young blood. He says the underlying parabiosis science is real but the commercial rollout, based on anecdotes and a pay-to-play trial, endangers the field's reputation. He speculates the drugs are anti-inflammatory agents targeting cytokines such as IL-11 and TGF-beta.

Transplanted hearts take on the biological age of their recipients, preprint finds

Oct 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Aging & longevity

A bioRxiv preprint reports that donor hearts assimilate the biological age of the recipient: older hearts in younger bodies showed signs of rejuvenation, and young hearts in older bodies showed accelerated aging, in mice and humans. The poster relates it to parabiosis experiments and notes implications for donor organ allocation.

Mechanical stress on bones made mice live nearly 3 months longer via anti-aging signals

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Exercise & fitness

A news report on a mouse study found that mechanical strain on the skeleton, similar to what occurs in exercise and resistance training, makes osteocytes secrete anti-aging chemicals. Gently squeezing leg bones extended mouse lifespan by nearly 3 months, roughly nine human years. A related study found bone stress improved memory and motor function after traumatic brain injury in mice and pigs.
