Niacin in a small glioblastoma pilot study: long survival, but historical controls

Feb 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 7 ↩ 2 Cancer

Louis notes a pilot study where niacin (vitamin B3) appeared to improve glioblastoma outcomes by rejuvenating immune function, probably via NAD. It was small and used historical controls.

COVID mRNA vaccination before surgery tied to doubled glioblastoma survival (preprint)

Aug 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 3 Cancer

A preprint from UAB found that 187 glioblastoma patients vaccinated against COVID within 100 days before biopsy or surgery had median survival of 743 days vs 349 days unvaccinated. The association was not seen with flu, pneumonia or shingles vaccines. Louis cautions about healthy-user bias but notes all patients were treated at one hospital, and says a randomized trial is needed.

Vitamin B3 forms and NAD: strong evidence in skin cancer, only preliminary in glioblastoma

Jun 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 ↩ 2 Cancer

Louis notes randomized trials show nicotinamide can reduce certain skin cancers, and early niacin trials are testing glioblastoma, with both likely acting through NAD. The shared analysis stresses the brain-cancer result comes from 15 patients with no control group, while the skin-cancer effect is real but modest.

High-dose niacin shows early promise in glioblastoma: 82% progression-free at 6 months

Oct 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Cancer

A small phase 1 pilot trial of high-dose, controlled-release niacin added to standard chemotherapy and radiotherapy for glioblastoma reported 82% of patients progression-free at six months. The poster notes the doses resemble those once used for high cholesterol and that results used historical controls, so a randomized phase 2 trial is ongoing.
