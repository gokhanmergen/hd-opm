What cardiologists say about statins: dementia, muscle pain, diabetes, primary prevention

Jan 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 18 Statins

A long point-by-point rebuttal of anti-statin claims. It cites a meta-analysis linking statins to lower dementia risk, the SAMSON trial on nocebo muscle pain, JUPITER's 44% reduction in major events, and a pooled analysis showing about 5 cardiac events prevented per extra diabetes case.

In JUPITER, rosuvastatin brought diabetes diagnosis forward by only 5.4 weeks

Feb 13, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis uses the JUPITER trial (rosuvastatin 20 mg vs placebo, 17,600 people, up to 5 years) to size the statin-diabetes link. Statin allocation advanced the time to diabetes diagnosis by an average of 5.4 weeks and raised HbA1c about 0.1 point at 24 months. His reading is that no one got diabetes who was not going to anyway, and that 20 mg is a high dose versus the 5 mg many take. The Lancet analysis concluded benefits exceeded the diabetes hazard.

Switching from rosuvastatin to pitavastatin removed a statin-related A1C rise in one case

Jun 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 19 Statins

Louis comments on a video of a doctor whose A1C rose slightly on rosuvastatin and normalized on pitavastatin. He notes this is a reasonable option for lower-risk people sensitive to glucose effects, but rosuvastatin has stronger large-RCT evidence in high-risk patients, so switching should not be taken lightly.

Probiotics might counter statin-linked blood sugar rise via gut microbiota, editorial says

Nov 09, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 11 Statins

Louis shares an ATVB editorial describing statin-induced gut microbiota changes as a candidate mechanism for statin-associated new-onset type 2 diabetes. The editorial says causality still needs larger sequencing studies. Louis adds that in JUPITER, high-dose rosuvastatin raised A1C by 0.1, bringing diabetes diagnosis about 5.4 weeks earlier over 2 years, a small but real effect.

Only about 9% of statin users see a measurable rise in blood sugar, per UCLA Health

Jan 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 8 Statins

Louis quotes UCLA Health saying statins raise blood glucose in about 9% of users, mostly with moderate-to-high doses and in people already trending toward prediabetes. He notes he is among the 91% with no effect.

Statins raise type 2 diabetes risk 9-12% relative, a tiny absolute effect for most people

May 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 Statins

Louis explains that in roughly 5-year RCTs, statins raise relative risk of new type 2 diabetes by 9-12% (20-36% at very high intensity). In absolute terms this is about 1 in 1000 going to 1.12 in 1000, so low-risk people see little change, while people already on the verge of diabetes may be nudged over the threshold sooner.
