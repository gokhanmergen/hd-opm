Claim that blending fruit causes bigger glucose spikes is untrue, per Louis

Dec 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 14 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis states that blending fruit does not cause a larger glucose spike than whole fruit and can lower or leave unchanged the glycemic index, criticizing Glucose Goddess and Gary Brecka's claims. The post links earlier group posts but cites no new study.

Daily bananas modestly lowered fasting glucose and LDL/HDL ratio in a 12-week study

Feb 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 11 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares a study where 30 hypercholesterolemic and 15 type 2 diabetic participants ate 250 or 500 g of banana daily for 12 weeks. Fasting glucose and the LDL/HDL ratio improved in the hypercholesterolemic group. He adds that greener bananas have a lower glycemic and insulin index because of resistant starch.

Blending fruit into smoothies does not spike blood sugar more than whole fruit

Aug 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 4 ↩ 4 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis argues that smoothies made from whole fruit do not spike blood sugar more than eating the fruit whole, and may lower the glycemic response. He cites several PubMed studies of whole vs blended fruit and offers a fiber-binding explanation.

Blending bananas with berries does not cancel the berries' benefits

Jul 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 6 Myths, misinformation & controversies

Louis shares a rebuttal of the claim that bananas in smoothies negate berry polyphenols. Banana polyphenol oxidase can break down some polyphenols such as epicatechins but does not degrade most anthocyanins, the main blueberry polyphenols, so the effect is likely minor.

Five foods with more potassium than a banana, plus tomato sauce and sweet potatoes

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 2 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a video listing potassium-rich foods that may support healthy blood pressure: white beans, baked potatoes with skin, Swiss chard, cooked spinach and avocado. He adds tomato sauce, tomato juice and sweet potatoes.
