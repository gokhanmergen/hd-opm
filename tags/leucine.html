Gly-Gly-Leu tripeptide reduced arterial calcification in non-human primates

Apr 22, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 24 · 💬 13 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A drug candidate previously effective in fatty liver disease reduced atherosclerosis in large mammals according to a news report, and Gokhan notes a glycine-glycine-leucine tripeptide significantly reduced calcification in non-human primates.

Sabatini on diet and mTOR: limit amino acids and insulin; ~18 h fasting may inhibit mTORC1

Dec 09, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 11 ↩ 3 Aging & longevity

Louis shares notes on a David Sabatini interview about how diet affects mTOR and autophagy. Sabatini suggests low amino acid or low carbohydrate diets lower mTOR, favors a higher-carbohydrate diet of low-glycemic carbs, and thinks mTORC1 senses total daily insulin. He guesses about 18 hours of fasting inhibits mTORC1, while stressing nobody has all the answers.

Leucine-rich protein caused atherosclerosis in engineered mice; human relevance unproven

Mar 04, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Protein

Louis shares a video review of a recent paper proposing that leucine-rich protein drives atherosclerosis. The conclusion, which Louis agrees with, is that it occurred in engineered mice fed 46% of calories from protein, is biologically plausible but remains far from proven in humans.
