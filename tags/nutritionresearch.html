High added sugar linked to over double the risk of heart disease death in a JAMA IM study

Aug 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 35 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared summary describes a 15-year JAMA Internal Medicine study in which people getting 25% or more of calories from added sugar had over twice the CVD death risk of those under 10%, regardless of weight or other factors. Louis clarifies that the comparison is with dietary cholesterol, not blood cholesterol.

Nuts, seeds and corn did not raise diverticulitis risk in nearly 30,000 women

May 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a large prospective cohort study in women showing that eating nuts, seeds, corn or seeded fruit did not increase diverticulitis risk, while healthy diet patterns (DASH, Mediterranean-style) were linked to lower risk. He notes his mother with diverticulosis was told to avoid nuts for years. Peripheral to heart health but relevant to nut-eating advice.

Review of 33 systematic studies finds little evidence omega-3 supplements build muscle

Mar 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares Stuart Phillips' summary of a 2025 Clinical Nutrition ESPEN review of 33 systematic studies, which found little evidence that omega-3 supplements improve lean mass, muscle strength or physical function in healthy adults or clinical groups. Some small benefits in older adults were suggested, but exercise and protein remain the best strategies.
