Red meat sugar Neu5Gc linked to 63% higher type 2 diabetes risk in 89,953 French adults

Oct 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 6 Meat, eggs & dairy

Preliminary results presented at EASD 2026 followed 89,953 NutriNet-Santé participants for about 7 years. Those eating the most Neu5Gc, a sugar found in red meat that humans do not make, had a 63% higher risk of type 2 diabetes, holding after adjusting for exercise, diet quality and smoking. Louis suggests this may partly explain why cohorts like the Nurses' Health Study link red meat to diabetes; the study is observational and not peer reviewed.

Meta-analysis of 102 feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat gives the best glycemic benefit

Nov 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarises a systematic review of 102 randomized feeding trials (4,220 participants) on 5% energy swaps. Replacing carbs with MUFA lowered HbA1c 0.09% and HOMA-IR 2.4%; with PUFA lowered HbA1c 0.11% and fasting insulin 1.6 pmol/L. PUFA alone improved insulin secretion capacity; saturated fat swaps did not change fasting glucose. Authors favor replacing refined carbs and saturated fat with unsaturated fats.

Low-carb diets lose their diabetes advantage by 12 months, trials and meta-analyses show

Nov 25, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 8 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Argues low-carb gives better remission and HbA1c at 6 months, but the edge fades. A 2021 BMJ meta-analysis (23 RCTs) found benefit at 6 months diminishing by 12; Silverii 2020 (37 RCTs, 3,301 patients) found HbA1c differences vanishing and reversing by 24 months; a 2-year calorie-matched RCT showed identical weight loss. Concludes the benefit comes from adherence, not metabolic magic.
