Magnesium plus potassium improved insomnia and sleep hormones in type 2 diabetes

Jul 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 8 Sleep, stress & social health

Louis suggests that taking magnesium at bedtime may work better when combined with an equal dose of potassium, especially in people with diabetes. The shared summary describes a 2024 Frontiers in Endocrinology randomized trial in adults with type 2 diabetes and insomnia, where the combination gave the biggest improvements.

Heart attacks peak in the early morning; bedtime dosing of BP drugs may help

Jun 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 14 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Louis explains that most heart attacks occur shortly after waking, when cortisol surges, vessels tighten and blood pressure spikes. He suggests taking blood pressure medication, aspirin and statins at bedtime, and corrects his post to say people are found dead in the 'bathroom' rather than 'toilet', since defecation syncope is much rarer than a standard heart attack.

Daily peanut butter improved muscle power in older adults and memory in young adults

Jun 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 26 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares two randomized trials of peanut butter. In 120 older adults at fall risk, 43 g/day for six months improved sit-to-stand speed and muscle power without changing weight; a separate 6-month trial in young adults (ARISTOTLE) found better memory and stress response.
