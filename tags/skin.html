hsCRP is not artery-specific: aging skin and visceral fat drive it in older people

Jun 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Inflammation & CRP

Louis shares an explanation that hsCRP is made by the liver in response to IL-6 and flags inflammation anywhere in the body. The post says age-related skin barrier breakdown and visceral fat are major contributors to 'inflamm-aging' and raised hsCRP in older people.

Vitamin C may reprogram epigenetics in skin cells, a lab finding on skin aging

Jul 26, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 13 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis links vitamin C's role in lab stem cell reprogramming to a Journal of Investigative Dermatology study suggesting it may restore more youthful gene expression in skin. It is early mechanistic evidence unrelated to heart disease.

Largest collagen review (113 trials): helps skin and osteoarthritis, little for muscle

Jun 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 3 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares an umbrella review of 16 systematic reviews covering 113 trials. Collagen improved skin elasticity and hydration and reduced osteoarthritis pain and stiffness with high-certainty evidence, plus a small muscle benefit in older adults. The shared post notes collagen is a poor muscle protein compared with whey.
