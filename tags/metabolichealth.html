Mar 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a 12-week RCT in 120 people with prediabetes testing 60 g of pistachios daily (30 g before breakfast and dinner). On statins, high ApoB with normal LDL-C raised MI risk 49%; high LDL-C alone did not

In Copenhagen General Population Study participants on statins (n=13,015, median 8 years), high apoB with normal LDL-C had 49% higher MI rate and 21% higher mortality. High LDL-C with normal apoB showed no excess risk; non-HDL-C behaved like apoB.

Louis agrees with a critique of influencers who treat VO2 max as the key heart-health metric. Video: immunoglobulin G buildup in fat tissue drives insulin resistance and aging

Feb 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Gokhan summarizes a Mike Lustgarten video on research showing IgG accumulates in fat tissue with age and obesity, impairs insulin signaling, promotes inflammation and fibrosis, and is reduced by calorie restriction. Blocking IgG recycling through the FcRn receptor in mice improved metabolic health and extended lifespan. He calls it potentially Nobel-worthy 'if it holds up'.
