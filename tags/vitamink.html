Vitamin K2 MK-4 improved insulin sensitivity in a small placebo-controlled trial

Mar 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 19 Supplements & vitamins

Louis explains he takes K2 for insulin sensitivity, not unproven calcification claims. In a Diabetes Care 2011 trial, 42 participants given 30 mg MK-4 for 4 weeks improved insulin sensitivity and disposition index, apparently via osteocalcin.

Vitamin K2 activates matrix Gla protein to limit arterial calcification, review says

Sep 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 21 Supplements & vitamins

A 2021 narrative review in Open Heart describes vitamin K2 as a regulator of calcium in the cardiovascular system by activating matrix Gla protein (MGP). Low vitamin K status leaves more inactive MGP, which has been linked to arterial stiffness and vascular calcification. It is a narrative review, not outcome trial evidence.

Vitamin K2 slows coronary calcium mainly in severe calcification; event benefit unproven

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 16 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Review of vitamin K2 (with or without vitamin D) and CAC. A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n=1,533) found a -17.3 Agatston units/year pooled difference. The largest RCT (389 men, 720 ug MK-7 + 1000 IU D vs placebo for 2 years) missed significance overall but slowed CAC at baseline CAC >=400; some CKD trials were null. Not yet a standard therapy.

K2 plus D3 slowed coronary calcium progression about 21% in a 2-year trial

Sep 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 14 Supplements & vitamins

In 398 adults with severe coronary calcification, two years of 720 mcg vitamin K2 (MK-7) plus 1,000 IU vitamin D3 daily slowed calcium-score progression versus placebo. However, total plaque volume rose similarly in both groups among the 143 assessed, so the trial does not show plaque reversal or fewer heart attacks.

Vitamin K2 trials show better glucose and HbA1c in type 2 diabetes, per Life Extension

Louis shares a Life Extension article summarizing RCTs of vitamin K and glucose metabolism. Three RCTs of MK-7 vitamin K2 (90-360 mcg/day for 3-6 months) lowered fasting glucose and HbA1c in type 2 diabetes versus placebo, and trials in healthy and prediabetic people improved insulin sensitivity. A shared summary of a randomized trial in 180 adults with symptomatic coronary artery disease and existing calcium found that 360 mcg/day MK-7 slowed CAC progression versus placebo over two years. Vitamin K2 slowed coronary calcium only when started early; two late-stage trials failed

Louis shares a summary of three RCTs of vitamin K2 (MK-7) on arterial calcification. K2 lowered the inactive MGP marker in all three, but calcification slowed only in the most recent trial, which enrolled people with early coronary disease; two trials in established aortic valve or dialysis calcification showed no change.

Vitamin D trials in heart disease mostly disappointing despite mechanistic promise

Mar 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 0 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a 2026 review describing a 'vitamin D paradox': promising cell, animal and observational data, but supplementation has not consistently reduced cardiovascular events in unselected populations unless people are truly deficient. Aside from bone health, he says the best RCT evidence is for autoimmune disease.
