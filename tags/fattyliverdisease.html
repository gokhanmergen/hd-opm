Normal liver enzymes can hide fatty liver; use FibroScan and look beyond FIB-4

Jun 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 11 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a dietitian post and note that half of people with fatty liver have normal liver enzymes, so enzymes and enzyme-based scores like FIB-4 poorly predict liver fat. He recommends FibroScan, with fatty liver diagnosed above 5% liver fat and insulin resistance linked to levels above 1.85%.

Fatty liver and liver stiffness reversed in one year on a Mediterranean diet

Oct 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A dietitian shares a single client's success story: complete reversal of fatty liver and liver scarring within a year on a high-polyphenol Mediterranean diet including beans and nuts. The poster stresses this is not just anecdote, since randomized trials show this type of diet reverses liver fat, and links earlier posts.

Weight loss, especially visceral fat loss, is the best way to reduce liver fat

Apr 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis says weight loss is the most effective way to lower liver fat, but diet and lifestyle changes like a green Mediterranean pattern can reduce liver fat even without weight loss and add to weight-loss effects. Shares a dietitian's beginner guide to fatty liver.
