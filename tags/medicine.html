A CAC score of zero does not rule out atherosclerosis, especially under age 45

Louis explains that a zero calcium score can coexist with visible calcium specks or with non-calcified soft plaque, particularly in people under 45. Because soft plaque takes 10-15 years to calcify, a 45-year-old with significant soft plaque might not show calcium until about age 60.

ARBs, alone or with a calcium channel blocker, are best-tolerated BP drugs in 700+ trials

Jun 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis summarizes the JAMA network meta-analysis showing ARBs have fewer treatment discontinuations from side effects than even placebo. ARB plus CCB was the best-tolerated regimen, ARBs appeared in four of the top five, and CCB monotherapy was worse, suggesting ARB-based regimens may improve long-term adherence.
