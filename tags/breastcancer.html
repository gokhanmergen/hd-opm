Menopausal hormone therapy is fairly safe for low-risk women, risky with established CVD

Apr 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 11 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares Cleveland Clinic guidance on hormone therapy and heart disease. HRT can be safe in healthy women under 60 or within 10 years of menopause, but risk rises when started over 10 years after menopause, and women with established CVD (10-year ASCVD risk over 10%) or breast cancer should avoid it. Low risk applies to recent menopause, normal weight and BP, active, and 10-year ASCVD risk under 5%.

Statins neither cause nor cure cancer: meta-analyses of randomized trials show no effect

Dec 31, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Statins

Reviews the evidence on statins and cancer. A 2012 meta-analysis of 134,537 participants in 22 RCTs found identical cancer incidence (1.4% per year) and mortality (0.5% per year) in statin users and non-users, and a 2023 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs found the same. Anticancer effects in specific contexts remain preclinical or suggestive.

Harvard review: fat not clearly linked to cancer; red and processed meat raise colon risk

Dec 20, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 2 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes the Harvard Nutrition Source summary on dietary fat and cancer. Total fat percentage and fat types show no clear association with cancer, and the Women's Health Initiative low-fat trial did not reduce breast or colon cancer. Red and processed meat do increase colon cancer risk, and animal fat in young women was linked to higher breast cancer risk in Nurses' Health Study II. He notes a later Harvard study updates this.

Bisphosphonates for bone loss: fracture reductions summarized

Oct 13, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Review of bisphosphonate evidence for osteoporosis. Alendronate cuts vertebral and non-vertebral fractures by about 50% in women with prior vertebral fractures; yearly IV zoledronic acid cuts vertebral fractures 70% and hip fractures 40%; a 2017 meta-analysis found overall fracture OR 0.62. Not a heart topic but relevant to aging.

High-dose vitamin D with chemo raised complete response 2.3-fold in breast cancer RCT

Jul 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Supplements & vitamins

A 2025 World Journal of Surgery randomized trial of 227 women receiving neoadjuvant chemotherapy compared chemotherapy alone with chemo plus 50,000 IU vitamin D3 weekly. Vitamin D was associated with higher odds of pathologic complete response, but it was an adjunct and survival was not studied.

Small RCT: vitamin D raised pathological complete response to 43% vs 24% in breast cancer

Jul 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Supplements & vitamins

In an 80-woman Brazilian trial, women aged 45+ starting neoadjuvant chemotherapy received 2,000 IU vitamin D daily or placebo for six months. Complete response occurred in 43% vs 24% among 75 who reached surgery. The commentary notes the benefit tracked correction of deficiency and that pathologic complete response is not a validated surrogate endpoint.

Vitamin D3 2,000 IU raised complete response to chemotherapy in breast cancer: 43% vs 24%

Oct 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Cancer

In an RCT of 80 women aged 45 or older receiving neoadjuvant chemotherapy for breast cancer, 2,000 IU daily vitamin D3 for six months was compared with placebo. Among 75 who completed treatment and had surgery, pathological complete response was 43% with vitamin D versus 24% with placebo. Small trial in cancer, not heart disease.
