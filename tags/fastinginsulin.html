Why fasting insulin is very low on a carnivore diet

Aug 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 10 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis explains that high protein and near-zero carbs force insulin very low and glucagon high when fasting, the hormonal setting needed for gluconeogenesis, which can look like pancreatic insufficiency on labs.

Fasting insulin mainly reflects liver fat-driven insulin resistance

Apr 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 2 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis argues that elevated fasting insulin is mostly a compensatory response to hepatic insulin resistance from liver fat, with muscle fat contributing too.
