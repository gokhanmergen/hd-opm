DO-HEALTH: vitamin D, omega-3 and exercise together linked to lower cancer risk

Dec 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 32 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

The 3-year trial of 2,157 adults aged 70+ tested vitamin D3, omega-3 and home exercise. The shared summary reports a 61% reduction in invasive cancer risk with the combination.

DO-HEALTH: vitamin D, omega-3 and exercise cut invasive cancer risk 61% in adults 70+

Dec 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares the 3-year DO-HEALTH randomized trial (2,157 adults aged 70+, five European countries) in which the combination of vitamin D3 2000 IU/day, omega-3 1 g/day and a home strength exercise program gave a 61% reduction in invasive cancer versus placebo. Only the combination was highlighted, with 4 cases versus 12.

DO-HEALTH: omega-3 slowed epigenetic aging clocks in older adults

Aug 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Aging & longevity

A post-hoc analysis of 777 adults over 70 randomized to vitamin D 2,000 IU, omega-3 1 g, home strength exercise or combinations for three years found omega-3 slowed three of four methylation clocks. These are surrogate markers.
