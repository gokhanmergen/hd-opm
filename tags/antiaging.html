Natto: spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase in one food, with mortality and plaque data

Dec 09, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 25 · 💬 12 ↩ 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Summarizes natto as a food rich in spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase. It cites a Frontiers clinical study where high-dose nattokinase slowed carotid plaque progression, an association with lower all-cause mortality, and mouse lifespan data for spermidine.

Alpha-ketoglutarate (AKG): longevity data in worms and mice, plus anti-thrombotic effects

Mar 02, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 13 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes the case for the supplement AKG, which extended lifespan in worms by about 50% and healthspan in aged mice, apparently by mildly suppressing mitochondrial function and mimicking calorie restriction. The linked EBioMedicine paper suggests AKG inhibits thrombosis and inflammation. The human evidence for cardiovascular benefit is limited and the author notes he personally uses it.

Epicatechin (cocoa flavanol) and mitoglitazone extended lifespan in male mice in the ITP

Sep 21, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 3 Aging & longevity

The NIA Interventions Testing Program found epicatechin raised median lifespan of male mice by about 5% and mitoglitazone, a next-generation TZD, by about 9%. Effects were seen only in males, and this is mouse data.

Mitteldorf's case that aging is a programmed, adaptive trait and not wear-and-tear

Sep 28, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 3 Aging & longevity

Gokhan endorses Josh Mitteldorf's iPAK 2024 talk arguing aging is an active, evolved process driven by inflammation, autoimmunity and telomere attrition, so we should intervene. This is a contested theoretical view, not a trial-based finding.

Metformin's glucose lowering is regulated by Rap1 in brain neurons, mouse study finds

Aug 03, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A Baylor College of Medicine study in Science Advances found metformin acts largely through the protein Rap1 in SF1 neurons of the hypothalamus. Mice lacking Rap1 in the brain did not respond, and a tiny dose given into the brain lowered glucose. This is preclinical mouse research.

Growth hormone is not a fountain of youth: lower GH linked to longer life

Sep 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Aging & longevity

A shared post citing a new review warns that GH boosting via peptides and supplements is marketed as anti-aging although low-GH states are associated with longer life and slower aging, while acromegaly brings many long-term harms. Louis added only hashtags; the post includes a course advertisement.

SGLT2 inhibitors: heart and kidney benefits and possible effects on aging pathways

Sep 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A shared post notes that SGLT2 inhibitors protect heart and kidneys beyond glucose control, and that emerging research suggests influences on inflammation, mitochondrial function, autophagy, AMPK and mTOR. It is largely promotional for a blog, and Louis added only hashtags.

Taurine is not a reliable aging biomarker, says new multi-species study

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A shared summary of a large longitudinal study across humans (26-100 years), monkeys and mice finds taurine levels do not consistently fall with age, often rise (especially in women), and vary more between individuals than with age. The post notes that taurine, like NAD+ boosters and resveratrol, shows promise in animals but human evidence is limited.
