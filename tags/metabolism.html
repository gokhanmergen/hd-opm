High protein intake linked to MASLD in NHANES; amino acids fuel liver fat synthesis

Dec 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 7 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a Cell Metabolism study suggesting high-quality protein may drive fatty liver. In NHANES (4,693 participants), carbohydrate and total fat were not associated with MASLD, while medium/high protein intake raised MASLD risk about 1.45-fold and high protein doubled MASH risk; low carbohydrate intake was associated with about 2-fold higher MASH progression. Amino acids serve as carbon sources for liver fatty acid synthesis, and mTOR may play a role.

Video: immunoglobulin G buildup in fat tissue drives insulin resistance and aging

Feb 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Gokhan summarizes a Mike Lustgarten video on research showing IgG accumulates in fat tissue with age and obesity, impairs insulin signaling, promotes inflammation and fibrosis, and is reduced by calorie restriction. Gut microbes turn fiber and polyphenols into GLP-1 and other satiety hormones

Jan 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Gut microbiome

Louis quotes Scientific American: the body makes its own incretin hormones such as GLP-1 and PYY, and gut bacteria ferment indigestible fiber and polyphenols into molecules that stimulate them. These hormones help regulate blood sugar, appetite and the speed of food moving through the gut (the 'colonic brake'). This suggests whole plant foods support the same pathways targeted by Ozempic and Wegovy.
