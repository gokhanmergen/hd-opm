Testosterone raised soft plaque growth by 41 mm3 in a 1-year RCT of older men

Louis covers a 2017 randomized trial by Matthew Budoff using CT angiography. Testosterone users had much more noncalcified plaque growth than placebo, compared with a typical ~5 mm3/year; TRT is beneficial only in true hypogonadism.

About 95% of acute-thrombosis heart attacks involve soft plaque, not calcified plaque

Oct 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The post says about 64% of heart attacks from acute thrombosis come from rupture of soft lipid-rich plaque and about 25% from erosion of soft plaque, while only about 5% involve calcified nodules. Because statins and other lipid-lowering drugs stabilize and reverse soft plaque, the poster argues they address the plaque type most responsible for heart attacks.

Testosterone therapy accelerated soft plaque growth by 41 mm3 in older men in a 2017 RCT

Aug 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 13 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis summarizes a 2017 RCT by Matthew Budoff in which older men on testosterone had about 41 mm3 greater soft plaque (NCPV) growth in a year versus placebo, equal to roughly 8 years of typical progression. He notes TRAVERSE (2023) found no increase in heart attacks or strokes with treated hypogonadism over a few years.

Louis predicts coronary CT angiography will become standard in preventive cardiology as cost and radiation fall. The shared review argues that total plaque volume and non-calcified plaque often outperform stenosis in large cohorts, supporting disease-based rather than risk-factor-based prevention.

Repatha alone reversed soft plaque in 37 of 47 patients over 18 months at LDL near 30

Nov 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis explains a small uncontrolled study of the PCSK9 inhibitor evolocumab with CT angiography. Average LDL was about 30 mg/dL, 37 of 47 patients had some non-calcified plaque regression, and many had large reversals worth years of typical growth (about 5 mm3 per year). He cautions it was small with no randomization or control group, and that PCSK9 monotherapy is not yet proven to reduce heart attacks.

Keto-CTA hyper-responders progressed soft plaque 3.8x faster than Nature-CT controls

Louis compares Keto-CTA (keto diet, LMHR) with the Nature-CT study, both from Budoff's UCLA group using CCTA and Cleerly software, with similar age (~55), no diabetes and no statins. Keto-CTA plaque growth matched diabetics' and far exceeded high-risk statin-treated groups

Apr 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis quotes an analysis of the Keto-CTA trial putting its plaque progression in context: median non-calcified plaque rose 18.8 mm3 in one year, comparable to type 2 diabetics (~17.4 mm3/yr), about triple PARADIGM metabolically normal people, and nearly 3x SMARTool high-risk non-statin users (6.4 mm3/yr).
