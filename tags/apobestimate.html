ApoB cannot be calculated from a lipid panel but is roughly 0.7 times non-HDL cholesterol

Sep 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 9 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that ApoB counts particles while non-HDL cholesterol (total minus HDL) measures the cholesterol they carry, so non-HDL is only a rough proxy. A crude estimate is ApoB approximately 0.7 x non-HDL-C, but a direct ApoB test is more accurate.

ApoB can be roughly estimated as 0.7 x (LDL + TG/5); non-HDL cholesterol approximates it
