Lp(a) carries LDL cholesterol, so LDL-C on a lab report includes Lp(a) cholesterol

Jun 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 49 ↩ 3 Lp(a)

Louis explains with a boats-and-cargo analogy that Lp(a) particles carry LDL cholesterol just as LDL particles do, so LDL-C counts cholesterol from both. Louis highlights the SATURN trial, where high-dose statins reversed plaque by about 1-2% PAV over two years, and each 1% PAV reversal is associated with about 20% fewer MACE. A shared meta-analysis of 31 studies and 4,997 patients (Li 2021) found lipid-lowering lowered TAV and PAV on IVUS.

LDL-C vs LDL-P vs ApoB: particle number predicts risk better than cholesterol content

Jul 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

LDL-C measures cholesterol inside LDL particles while LDL-P counts the particles; they can be discordant (low LDL-C with many small particles), and particle number predicts risk better. Particle size adds little once number is accounted for, and ApoB, which counts all atherogenic particles, is the best test.

ApoB is the 'sign' on atherogenic particles; LDL and Lp(a) carry LDL cholesterol

Jun 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 10 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis extends a boat-and-cargo analogy: LDL, VLDL and Lp(a) particles carry an ApoB tag that makes them atherogenic, and an ApoB test counts the total number of such particles. LDL and Lp(a) particles carry LDL cholesterol, while VLDL carries triglyceride, so LDL-C on a lab report includes cholesterol carried by Lp(a).

NLA consensus clarifies when to measure ApoB and matching LDL-C thresholds

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 19 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Shared from Gokhan Can: a summary of the National Lipid Association's Expert Clinical Consensus on apoB testing. ApoB is a more accurate predictor than LDL-C when the two diverge, particularly after statins, and testing is suggested for borderline-risk patients deciding on a statin or those considering intensifying therapy, with apoB thresholds matching LDL-C goals.

Fact-checking keto and carnivore influencer claims about LDL, ApoB and saturated fat

May 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis lists what keto/carnivore influencers get right and wrong: ApoB is the best lipid marker, LDL-C usually tracks ApoB, particle size doesn't matter once particle number is counted, and most saturated fats raise LDL-P, ApoB and LDL-C.
