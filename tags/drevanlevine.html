What cardiologists say about statins: dementia, muscle pain, diabetes, primary prevention

Jan 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 18 Statins

A long point-by-point rebuttal of anti-statin claims. It cites a meta-analysis linking statins to lower dementia risk, the SAMSON trial on nocebo muscle pain, JUPITER's 44% reduction in major events, and a pooled analysis showing about 5 cardiac events prevented per extra diabetes case.

Grip strength predicts heart attack like blood pressure, but reflects reverse causality

Feb 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Aging & longevity

Louis notes grip strength predicts heart attack risk comparably to blood pressure, but argues this is reverse causality: aging weakens grip, and training grip will not lower heart disease risk.

Thiazides are not just water pills, and calling them diuretics may scare people off

Jan 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Evan Levine argues thiazides should stop being called water pills or diuretics because that is not how they ultimately lower blood pressure and it deters use of a top hypertension drug.

ARBs, alone or with a calcium channel blocker, are best-tolerated BP drugs in 700+ trials

Jun 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis summarizes the JAMA network meta-analysis showing ARBs have fewer treatment discontinuations from side effects than even placebo. ARB plus CCB was the best-tolerated regimen, ARBs appeared in four of the top five, and CCB monotherapy was worse, suggesting ARB-based regimens may improve long-term adherence.
