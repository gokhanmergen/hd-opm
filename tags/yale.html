Liver fat as low as 1.85% is linked to insulin resistance (Yale)

Louis posts a Yale report showing that liver fat above 1.85%, well below the 5% fatty liver threshold, is linked to insulin resistance. Lean people may have enough liver fat to cause insulin resistance without knowing it.

Insulin resistance starts with fat accumulating in muscle, before liver fat

Nov 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis highlights Yale research (Kitt Petersen) showing insulin resistance begins with fat buildup inside muscle, even in lean young people predisposed to diabetes, and that liver fat accumulates later. A linked Yale article reports small amounts of liver fat are linked to insulin resistance and cardiometabolic risk factors.

Yale: taurine loss triggers NLRP3 inflammasome; raising taurine eased inflammaging in mice

Nov 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Inflammation & CRP

Louis shares a summary of a Yale Cell study showing macrophages release up to 80% of their taurine after inflammatory triggers, which helps activate the NLRP3 inflammasome. Restoring taurine suppressed NLRP3 activation, and raising taurine in older mice improved grip strength, coordination, some glucose measures and inflammatory markers. It is preclinical research.

Taurine lowers age-related inflammation via the NLRP3 inflammasome in mouse macrophages

Oct 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A Cell paper from Yale reports that taurine levels increase with age in mice and humans, and boosting taurine further in aged mice increased healthspan and dampened inflammation by regulating the NLRP3 inflammasome in macrophages.
