Olezarsen cut triglycerides 64% but did not change plaque in ESSENCE-TIMI 73b imaging

Mar 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The ESSENCE-TIMI 73b imaging study found olezarsen lowered triglycerides about 64% and remnant cholesterol about 72%, with ApoB down only about 16%, yet 12-month CCTA showed no difference in plaque. Louis reads it as showing TG lowering alone, without treating insulin resistance, does not slow plaque.

Standard cholesterol tests miss Lp(a), remnant and ApoB-rich particles

Dec 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis shares a brief explainer that heart risk goes beyond LDL and HDL, as hidden lipoprotein particles such as Lp(a), remnants and ApoB-rich particles cause many heart attacks and are invisible on standard panels.
