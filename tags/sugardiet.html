Carnivore, keto and vegan diets reverse diabetes via the same route: weight and fat loss

Jul 31, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 17 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis argues that any diet producing large weight loss clears liver and pancreas fat, which explains remission on carnivore, keto, vegan, rice or GLP-1 approaches, rather than any food-specific effect.

Isoleucine restriction extends mouse lifespan 33%, and may explain the old rice diet

Jun 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 15 ↩ 4 Aging & longevity

Louis cautions that the viral 'sugar diet' is not healthy long term but says there may be a mechanism: isoleucine restriction extended median lifespan by 33% in male HET3 mice and improved insulin sensitivity. He connects this to Kempner's 1940s rice diet, which had minimal isoleucine.
