DISCO-CT diet-plus-medication group showed plaque reversal, unlike progression in Keto-CTA

Jul 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a comparison of non-calcified plaque volume changes across several CT angiography studies. The DISCO-CT intervention group (statins, other medications and a DASH diet) showed significant plaque reversal at one year, while the Keto-CTA group of lean mass hyper-responders with LDL-C around 237 mg/dL showed progression. The linked Keto-CTA longitudinal paper is retracted per link metadata.

Keto-CTA: metabolic health and large LDL particles gave no protection from plaque

Louis says Keto-CTA tested whether good metabolic health and large LDL particle size protect against very high LDL (average 254 mg/dL) and found no protection. Plaque progression was 3x worse than in the Nature-CT comparison group, while DISCO showed reversal with DASH plus statins.

Keto-CTA hyper-responders progressed soft plaque 3.8x faster than Nature-CT controls

Apr 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis compares Keto-CTA (keto diet, LMHR) with the Nature-CT study, both from Budoff's UCLA group using CCTA and Cleerly software, with similar age (~55), no diabetes and no statins. After one year non-calcified plaque volume rose 18.8 mm3 in LMHR versus 4.9 mm3 in the Nature-CT standard-diet group. Budoff reportedly agrees it is a good comparison and plans a paper comparing them.

Louis shares a comparison of the Keto-CTA keto cohort with the Nature-CT study, which had no diabetes and no lipid drugs and was run by Matthew Budoff at UCLA. It argues the comparison is apples to apples, with both groups averaging 55 years old.
