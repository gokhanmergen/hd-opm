Olive oil may reduce clotting by raising apoA-IV, which limits platelet aggregation

Jul 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 10 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A Life Extension article describes mechanistic studies in which apoA-IV, a protective HDL component, reduces platelet aggregation and thrombosis, and monounsaturated fats such as extra virgin olive oil raise apoA-IV, especially after meals. This is mechanistic and partly animal evidence.

Dietary fat and mortality meta-analysis: polyunsaturated favorable, trans fat harmful

Dec 01, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarizes a meta-analysis of 19 prospective cohorts (over 1 million participants) on dietary fat and mortality. Higher total, monounsaturated and especially polyunsaturated fat were associated with lower mortality, trans fat with higher mortality, and saturated fat showed a non-linear pattern with excess risk up to about 11% of energy.

Fat type beats amount: PUFA tied to lower mortality, trans and saturated fat to higher

Aug 24, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 8 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A 2020 Clinical Nutrition meta-analysis of 19 prospective cohorts (1,013,273 participants, 195,515 deaths) found polyunsaturated and monounsaturated fats associated with lower mortality, and saturated and trans fats with higher. Gokhan cautions that data guide rather than prescribe.

Meta-analysis of 102 feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat gives the best glycemic benefit

Nov 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarises a systematic review of 102 randomized feeding trials (4,220 participants) on 5% energy swaps. Replacing carbs with MUFA lowered HbA1c 0.09% and HOMA-IR 2.4%; with PUFA lowered HbA1c 0.11% and fasting insulin 1.6 pmol/L. PUFA alone improved insulin secretion capacity; saturated fat swaps did not change fasting glucose. Authors favor replacing refined carbs and saturated fat with unsaturated fats.

100+ trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose and insulin markers, saturated doesn't

Sep 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 6 ↩ 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes a meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials showing polyunsaturated fat, mostly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowers fasting glucose and A1C and improves insulin resistance and insulin secretion. Monounsaturated fat did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit.

Meta-analysis of 119 trials: nuts did not cause weight gain and modestly lowered LDL

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials (154 articles, 119 unique RCTs) found adding about 120 kcal/day of nuts did not significantly change weight, BMI or waist; body fat fell slightly and lean mass rose. Nuts were associated with small reductions in total cholesterol, LDL and triglycerides, with no significant effect on HDL.

Examine: saturated fat raises LDL-C and apoB in WHO meta-analysis of 84 RCTs

Sep 05, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summary of Examine's article on saturated fat. A 2016 WHO meta-analysis of 84 RCTs (2,353 healthy adults) examined replacing 1% of calories from carbohydrates or unsaturated fat with saturated fat. Saturated fat raised LDL-C and apoB (suggesting higher risk), and HDL rose only about one-tenth as much as LDL-C, raising the LDL/HDL ratio.

Harvard: unsaturated fats protect, trans fats harm; PREDIMED backs olive oil and nuts

Sep 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes Harvard's Nutrition Source page correcting myths about dietary fat. It says polyunsaturated fats from vegetable oils, nuts and seeds help ward off type 2 diabetes, trans fats do the opposite, and the PREDIMED Mediterranean diet with olive oil or nuts reduced cardiovascular events.

100+ feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose control, saturated fat does not

Dec 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes the 2016 PLoS Medicine meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat, mainly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowered fasting glucose and A1C and improved insulin resistance and secretion, MUFA did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit. He pairs it with the 20-cohort biomarker study showing a 43% lower diabetes risk with high omega-6.

Palmitic acid impairs insulin action while olive oil's oleic acid counteracts it

Jun 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Fox News report on a review concluding that palmitic acid, the most common saturated fat in American foods, promotes inflammation, oxidative stress and insulin resistance, while oleic acid from olive oil, nuts and avocados counteracts these effects. Researchers back swapping saturated fats for extra-virgin olive oil rather than just cutting total fat.

Higher saturated fat intake linked to increased dementia risk in a prospective cohort

May 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Layne Norton's shared post summarizes a cohort of adults averaging 68 years followed about 6 years: total fat was not linked to dementia, but higher saturated fat was. It is observational but fits mechanisms involving LDL/apoB and vascular health, and Louis notes the pattern recurs across large cohorts.
