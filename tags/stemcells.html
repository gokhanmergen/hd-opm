Mutated blood stem cells (CHIP) may be a major cause of heart disease, doubling MI risk

Apr 08, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 13 Inflammation & CRP

Louis explains clonal hematopoiesis (CHIP): age-related mutations in bone-marrow stem cells yield white blood cells that drive inflammation and plaque growth, and the same mutations link to leukemia. A quoted report says CHIP carriers had a 54% higher chance of dying within a decade, from heart attacks and strokes, and mouse transplants of CHIP-mutated cells produced fast-growing, inflamed plaques. Louis credits Gokhan Can for pointing him to this.

Atherosclerotic plaques contain large clonal, mutated cell populations, like tumors

Jun 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 14 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A JCI Insight study used deep whole-exome sequencing of carotid plaques and non-atherosclerotic arteries and found somatic mutations in plaques but not in healthy arterial tissue. This suggests plaques grow by clonal expansion, paralleling clonal hematopoiesis (CHIP), which is causally linked to cardiovascular disease.

Single dose of donor stem cells raised walking endurance about 20% in frail older adults

Feb 25, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 9 Aging & longevity

Describes a trial of donor bone-marrow mesenchymal stem cells in 148 walking adults aged 70-85 with frailty (118 on varying doses, 30 on placebo). The cells lack immune-triggering surface proteins so no immunosuppressants were needed; at nine months the highest dose group walked about 60 m farther in a six-minute walk test than at baseline.

Aging stem cells open a direct platelet pathway that raises thrombosis risk (Cell 2024)

Jun 09, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 12 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Shares the abstract of a Cell paper showing that with age, hematopoietic stem cells increasingly make platelets through a direct route that bypasses the usual progenitor cascade. The platelets from this pathway are more prone to thrombosis and cause higher platelet counts. It offers a possible cellular explanation for why clotting-related cardiovascular disease rises so steeply in older people.

Cholesterol as a master regulator of blood stem cells, driving inflammatory hematopoiesis

Oct 19, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Inflammation & CRP

Review of preclinical evidence that high LDL pulls hematopoietic stem cells out of dormancy, causing proliferation, oxidative stress, telomere shortening, impaired repopulation and lasting epigenetic changes that program blood and immune cells toward chronic inflammation. This offers a molecular basis for residual inflammatory risk after cholesterol is treated.

Reprogrammed stem-cell islet transplant freed a woman with type 1 diabetes from insulin

Sep 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 6 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Shared from Luigi Fontana: a 25-year-old woman with type 1 diabetes received autologous chemically induced pluripotent stem cell islets under the abdominal rectus sheath. She stopped needing insulin within 2.5 months, with time-in-range rising from 43.18% to 96.21% by month 4 and above 98% afterward (HbA1c about 5%). At 1 year all endpoints were met with no transplant-related issues. A single patient, and about type 1 not cardiovascular disease.

Shin bone loading may speed brain injury recovery via PIEZO1 in mice and pigs

Oct 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

A Nature Neuroscience paper found that compressing the tibia for a few minutes a day after traumatic brain injury improved movement and memory in mice and pigs. Bone cells sensed the force through the PIEZO1 sensor, and turning it off removed the benefit. The poster calls it the most interesting bone study he has read in a long time.
