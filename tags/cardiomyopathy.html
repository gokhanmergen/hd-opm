Taurine and cardiovascular disease: anti-inflammatory and vascular effects

Jan 27, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 21 · 💬 26 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

A long evidence summary on taurine: deficiency causes cardiomyopathy in cats and in humans with transporter defects, higher taurine intake correlates with lower coronary disease, and animal studies show reduced atherosclerosis and vessel relaxation. The author is considering a supplement against chronic inflammation; most evidence cited is animal or observational.

Anabolic steroid users had 3x heart attack and 8.9x cardiomyopathy risk (Circulation)

Dapagliflozin linked to lower heart failure risk in carriers of cardiomyopathy variants

Jun 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis shares a Nature Medicine study of over 12,000 adults with type 2 diabetes in which dapagliflozin was associated with significantly lower heart failure risk in those carrying inherited cardiomyopathy-related variants. Fox News describes an 8-fold reduction in heart failure hospitalization, and suggests genetic screening could eventually guide early treatment.

HCM, athlete's heart and LVH look alike on imaging but differ greatly in risk

Apr 03, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis explains HCM, athlete's heart and left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH) can look alike on EKG or ultrasound. HCM is usually genetic and serious, potentially causing sudden cardiac arrest; athlete's heart follows long-term intense exercise and is usually benign; LVH is usually due to uncontrolled hypertension or aortic stenosis. A person can have both HCM and athlete's heart.
