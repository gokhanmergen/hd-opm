Chiropractor had a widow-maker heart attack at 35 with CAC 0 and LDL 372 on carnivore/keto

Oct 21, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 43 ↩ 2 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis describes a lean, active 35-year-old with a CAC score of 0 who had a massive heart attack after a carnivore/keto diet pushed his LDL to 372 mg/dL without familial hypercholesterolemia. He notes the man still denies cholesterol's role. It is an anecdote; a CAC of 0 does not exclude soft plaque.

Looking healthy on keto or carnivore does not rule out silent plaque, citing KETO-CTA

May 29, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 20 · 💬 30 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Gokhan recalls group members with very early heart attacks after years of keto or carnivore eating and cites KETO-CTA in lean mass hyper-responders. His advice is to follow LDL and ApoB rather than how you look or feel.

Under 10% of heart attacks are now fatal, but out-of-hospital cardiac arrest is ~90% fatal

Aug 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 7 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Modern treatment means less than 10% of heart attacks result in death, versus up to half of victims decades ago. However, a cardiac arrest or widowmaker heart attack outside a hospital still has about a 90% chance of death, and cardiac arrest (heart stops) is distinct from a heart attack.

Runner's heart attack revealed a silent 50-60% blockage in a 'widowmaker' artery

Feb 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Article about a fit runner who collapsed mid-race with undiagnosed 50-60% blockage, triggered by atrial fibrillation rather than exercise. It explains plaque rupture, collateral vessels that can mask blockage, and screening via lipid panels, calcium scans and CT angiograms.
