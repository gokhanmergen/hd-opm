PREDIMED nut eaters had about 40% lower total and cancer mortality in under 5 years

Mar 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 42 · 💬 14 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis highlights that no drug has been shown to cut both total and cancer mortality by about 40% in healthy people within 5 years, yet this was seen with nuts in PREDIMED. He stresses the caveat that it was a post-hoc observational analysis of an RCT.

PREDIMED: free nuts roughly halved stroke risk, and walnuts may improve artery function

Mar 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 8 ↩ 7 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

NutritionFacts summarizes PREDIMED, in which over 7,000 high-risk people were randomized and one group received free nuts weekly for four years. The nuts group appeared to cut stroke risk about in half, and higher nut intake overall was linked with lower premature death; walnuts may improve artery relaxation.

Green Mediterranean diet with walnuts cut liver fat about 39% in an 18-month RCT

Sep 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 ↩ 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares the DIRECT-PLUS trial in Gut, where a Mediterranean diet enriched with green tea, the aquatic plant Mankai and walnuts and restricted in red and processed meat reduced liver fat most among 294 adults with abdominal obesity. He emphasizes liver fat as a root cause of insulin resistance for most people.

DIRECT-PLUS: green Mediterranean diet cut fatty liver prevalence to 31.5% over 18 months

Sep 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 ↩ 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis quotes a Healio report on the DIRECT-PLUS trial, in which 294 people with abdominal obesity or dyslipidemia were randomized to healthy guidelines, Mediterranean or green-Mediterranean diets. The green-MED diet (walnuts, green tea, Mankai, little red/processed meat) roughly doubled intrahepatic fat loss.

Nut and walnut intake linked to lower mortality, with most benefit near 15 g/day

Jul 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A shared summary reports that across studies nut consumption consistently tracked with lower all-cause mortality, with most benefit around 15 g per day and a plateau beyond. Walnuts showed lower mortality in women and men, and the association persisted among people with healthy diets.

Green Mediterranean diet slowed brain aging in a randomized trial of 300 adults

Sep 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

In a randomized worksite trial in Israel, a calorie-controlled green-Mediterranean diet with exercise support shaped the brain age gap on MRI. Earlier reports said both Mediterranean diets slowed brain atrophy by about 50% over 18 months, and the green-MED diet lowered two proteins linked to brain aging.

Green Mediterranean diet cut visceral fat about 14% in an 18-month randomized trial

Oct 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A shared summary of an 18-month RCT comparing a healthy diet, a Mediterranean diet and a Green Mediterranean diet enriched with green tea and Wolffia globosa (mankai duckweed). The Green Mediterranean diet reduced visceral fat by about 14%, the largest effect of the three arms. Visceral fat drives inflammation and metabolic dysfunction.

Green Mediterranean diet with green tea and mankai slowed brain atrophy in people over 50

Oct 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

In an 18-month randomized trial of nearly 300 participants, a standard healthy diet, a Mediterranean diet, and a Green Mediterranean diet with green tea and mankai (duckweed) were compared on hippocampal occupancy, a marker of brain aging. Louis highlights that Mediterranean diets slowed brain aging in people over 50. Linked paper is the DIRECT-PLUS Green-MED brain atrophy analysis.
