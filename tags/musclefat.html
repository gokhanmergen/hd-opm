Liver fat as low as 1.85% is linked to insulin resistance (Yale)

Jun 29, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 25 ↩ 14 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis posts a Yale report showing that liver fat above 1.85%, well below the 5% fatty liver threshold, is linked to insulin resistance. Lean people may have enough liver fat to cause insulin resistance without knowing it.

Diabetes researchers cite fat in muscle and liver as the main cause of insulin resistance

Oct 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 13 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis says leading diabetes researchers increasingly agree that fat build-up inside muscle and liver is what causes insulin resistance in most people. Food types can make accumulation easier or harder, but the main cause is overall calorie overconsumption, and very lean people with no ectopic fat rarely develop insulin resistance whatever they eat.

Omega-6 linoleic acid improves insulin sensitivity by activating PPAR-gamma

The post shares a 2023 review arguing that linoleic acid, the main omega-6 fat in nuts, seeds and vegetable oils, lowers cardiometabolic risk. The review suggests understanding how LA affects PPAR activity may overturn the dogma that omega-6 promotes inflammation.

Louis reshares a figure comparing lean and obese skeletal muscle, showing differences in fiber type, mitochondria and lipid content that shift muscle from burning to storing energy. He notes insulin resistance starts with fat in muscle and only later in the liver.

Insulin resistance starts with fat accumulating in muscle, before liver fat

Nov 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis highlights Yale research (Kitt Petersen) showing insulin resistance begins with fat buildup inside muscle, even in lean young people predisposed to diabetes, and that liver fat accumulates later. A linked Yale article reports small amounts of liver fat are linked to insulin resistance and cardiometabolic risk factors.

Gerald Shulman honored for showing hidden fat in muscle and liver drives diabetes

Nov 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares the American Liver Foundation's 2025 award to Gerald Shulman, whose team showed that fat stored in the wrong places, such as muscle and liver cells, disrupts insulin signaling and contributes to type 2 diabetes and liver disease. The quoted citation stresses that where fat is stored matters more than total body fat.
