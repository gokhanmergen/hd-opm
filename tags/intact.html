Two tricks to lower glucose and insulin spikes from whole grains

Feb 26, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 11 ↩ 1 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis recommends eating whole grains as intact as possible (steel-cut oats, wheat berries rather than finely ground flour) and refrigerating cooked grains for 24 hours to convert starch into resistant starch. Resistant starch is digested slowly and feeds gut bacteria.

Low-carb and whole-food high-carb diets cleared liver and visceral fat equally in an RCT

Aug 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 8 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis explains a randomized trial comparing three isocaloric diets over one year. All three cleared liver fat, and a whole-food higher-carb diet with intact whole grains worked as well as low carb. He draws the practical point that whole grains and fruit are fine for liver fat loss.

Vegans with insulin resistance should target liver fat; white potatoes are not health food

Sep 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 17 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis advises vegans with insulin resistance, high blood sugar or high triglycerides that the main cause is excess visceral and liver fat, even in lean people. He also cautions against white potatoes, citing a Harvard trial testing whether they harm arterial endothelium, and prefers sweet potatoes.
