Japan-ACS: pitavastatin and atorvastatin both regressed coronary plaque by about 17-18%

Sep 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 23 ↩ 21 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summary of the Japan-ACS trial, in which over 300 acute coronary syndrome patients were randomized to pitavastatin or atorvastatin and imaged with IVUS. Both statins produced similar, substantial regression of non-culprit plaque volume as LDL fell into the 80s mg/dL, which is one reason statins remain standard care after ACS.

Louis shares a Cleveland Clinic cardiologist explaining that statins can reverse plaque, mainly by shrinking the cholesterol-rich lipid core, which also makes plaque more stable. He adds that six large randomized trials showed total plaque volume decreases of 5-17% from baseline.

SATURN: high-dose statins reversed plaque 1-2%; each 1% PAV cut means 20% fewer events

Aug 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 20 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis highlights the SATURN trial, where high-dose statins reversed plaque by about 1-2% PAV over two years, and each 1% PAV reversal is associated with about 20% fewer MACE. A shared meta-analysis of 31 studies and 4,997 patients (Li 2021) found lipid-lowering lowered TAV and PAV on IVUS.

Six RCTs show statins reverse total plaque volume by 5% to 17%

Aug 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists six randomized trials showing reversal of total atheroma volume with statins: COSMOS ~5%, SATURN ~5.5%, ASTEROID ~6.5%, IBIS-4 ~7.5%, STABLE ~8%, JAPAN-ACS ~17%. Reversals were measured by CT angiography or IVUS and came from soft rather than calcified plaque. A shared chart relates LDL to TAV change across statin, PCSK9 and ezetimibe trials.

Japan-ACS: modest statin doses cut non-culprit plaque volume by about 17-18%

Sep 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 5 Statins

The Japan-ACS randomized trial enrolled over 300 patients with acute coronary syndrome at 33 centers and compared 4 mg pitavastatin with 20 mg atorvastatin. Both produced similar major plaque regression over 8 to 12 months; Louis uses it to rebut claims that statins do nothing.

YELLOW-III reversed total atheroma volume 4.4% in 6 months at LDL of about 39 mg/dL

Louis adds a green dot for YELLOW-III to his plaque-reversal chart. High-dose statin plus a PCSK9 inhibitor reduced total atheroma volume from 137.2 to about 131 mm3 in only 6 months and stabilized plaque, with post-treatment LDL averaging 39.1 mg/dL.

CT angiography plaque burden often predicts events better than stenosis severity

Jun 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis predicts coronary CT angiography will become standard in preventive cardiology as cost and radiation fall. The shared review argues that total plaque volume and non-calcified plaque often outperform stenosis in large cohorts, supporting disease-based rather than risk-factor-based prevention.
