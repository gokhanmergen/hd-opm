Jul 31, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 11 Statins

The post explains why high-intensity atorvastatin is started right after a heart attack: in PROVE IT-TIMI 22, 30-day death/MI/rehospitalization was lower with atorvastatin 80 mg than pravastatin 40 mg. High-dose atorvastatin after heart attack cut events ~30% in 30 days (PROVE IT-TIMI 22)

Jan 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 16 Statins

Louis explains why patients are immediately given atorvastatin 80 mg after a heart attack: PROVE IT-TIMI 22 showed an almost 30% reduction in death, repeat heart attack or rehospitalization within 30 days versus a lower-potency statin. The speed suggests pleiotropic effects (anti-inflammatory, plaque and endothelial stabilizing) rather than LDL lowering. Lipophilic statins at maximum dose work best in this setting.

Statins have pleiotropic effects beyond LDL, including endothelial and plaque benefits

Jul 20, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 6 Statins

Louis quotes a review stating that besides lowering LDL-C, statins have non-lipid effects such as improved endothelial function, reduced oxidative stress and atherosclerotic plaque stabilization. It is a useful overview of why statin benefit may extend beyond LDL lowering.

Meta-analysis of 26 RCTs: statins lowered hs-CRP by about 1 mg/L in patients with CVD

Jul 12, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 6 Inflammation & CRP

A 2022 meta-analysis pooled randomized trials and found statins significantly reduce hs-CRP and CRP in patients with cardiovascular disease, in both high-intensity and lower-intensity regimens, with treatment over 10 weeks. For someone with hs-CRP of 2 mg/L this is about a 50% drop.

Statins, especially with ezetimibe, lower Lp-PLA2, a plaque-specific inflammation marker

Jun 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Inflammation & CRP

Louis explains that Lp-PLA2 reflects inflammation inside atherosclerotic plaque (unlike systemic hsCRP) and is recognised as a CVD risk factor. A quoted review reports pravastatin, atorvastatin, rosuvastatin and simvastatin significantly lower it, with ezetimibe added lowering it further.
