Why the LMHR phenotype happens: starvation-like physiology in lean keto dieters

Louis proposes that the lean mass hyper-responder (LMHR) pattern, with extremely high LDL-C on keto/carnivore diets, reflects an ancient starvation response described in calorie-restriction literature.
