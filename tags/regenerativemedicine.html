Semaglutide raised knee cartilage thickness 17% in 6 months in a small pilot

Mar 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 19 ↩ 2 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis highlights a Cell Metabolism study in mice and people with obesity and osteoarthritis. After six months on low-dose semaglutide plus hyaluronic acid, MRI showed a 17% increase in cartilage thickness versus under 1% in the comparison group, with better knee function; Science News notes bigger trials are needed.

How semaglutide appears to repair cartilage in mice and humans, independent of weight loss

Mar 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 2 ↩ 1 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares a ScienceAlert article on the semaglutide osteoarthritis study. Semaglutide reprogrammed the metabolism of cartilage-forming cells, reduced pain and cartilage degeneration, and in mice caused fewer bone spurs; the authors describe a weight-loss-independent repair mechanism.

Human heart muscle cells divide after heart attack, University of Sydney finds

Jan 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Louis shares a Circulation Research study showing cardiomyocyte mitosis in human heart tissue after myocardial infarction, first direct proof of human intrinsic repair capacity, though regeneration is limited.

mRNA-driven Myc and Cyclin T1 restarted heart muscle cell division after heart attack

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis shares a summary of a Nature Cardiovascular Research study from Cambridge using temporary modified mRNA to activate Myc and Cyclin T1 in heart muscle cells so mature cardiomyocytes re-enter division. The aim is to regenerate heart tissue after a heart attack instead of leaving scar tissue and chronic heart failure. This is early-stage regeneration research.
