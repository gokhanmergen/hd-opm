GLP-1 drugs may curb alcohol and nicotine cravings; semaglutide cut drinking in a trial

Dec 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares a science-news piece noting that in a recent trial adults with alcohol-use disorder on weekly low-dose semaglutide for nine weeks drank less and reported fewer cravings than placebo, and that rodent studies show reduced nicotine and alcohol intake. Larger long-term trials are needed.
