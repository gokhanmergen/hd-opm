Blending whole fruit does not cause bigger blood-sugar spikes than eating it whole

May 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 13 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis rebuts the claim that blending fruit spikes blood sugar. Per the shared response, an RCT comparing whole apples and blackberries with a smoothie of the same fruits found a slightly smaller glucose rise with the smoothie, and blending mangoes also did not raise the spike. Fiber remains in the blend and still slows sugar absorption.

French fries, but not other potato dishes, linked to higher diabetes risk

Louis reports that Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study analysis found french fries raised diabetes risk over 30 years, apparently due to trans fats, while boiled or baked potatoes did not versus the standard diet. Olive oil raised GLP-1 and lowered post-meal glucose versus butter in type 1 diabetes

Aug 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Olive oil, like all fats, raises GLP-1, and the quoted crossover data suggest it does so more than butter, helping people feel full sooner. After a high-glycemic meal in people with type 1 diabetes, glucose exposure was lower with EVOO than with butter or a low-fat meal, plausibly via slower gastric emptying and glucose-lipid competition.
