ASPREE: aspirin tied to 70% lower dementia with high platelet-count genetic scores

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

Monash researchers analysed genetic data from 13,541 ASPREE participants and found that among those in the top 20% of a platelet-count polygenic score, 1% on 100 mg aspirin developed dementia versus 3.3% on placebo. Aspirin is not recommended for dementia prevention, and this is a genetic subgroup finding that needs confirmation, with bleeding risk to weigh.

ASPREE sub-analysis: aspirin tied to ~70% lower dementia in a platelet-genetics subgroup

Oct 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

A genetic analysis of 13,541 ASPREE participants found aspirin did not prevent dementia overall, but in the top 20% of a platelet-count genetic score dementia occurred in about 1% on aspirin versus 3.3% on placebo. Major bleeding was more than twice as common in this subgroup, so the post cautions against starting aspirin for dementia prevention.
