No trial has shown PCSK9 inhibitors lower heart attack risk without statins

Aug 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 53 ↩ 3 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis stresses that Repatha and Praluent proved their benefit only on top of statins, so claims that they should replace statins are unsupported. They might work better but outcome data are lacking, which is why guidelines keep statins first-line and add PCSK9 inhibitors.

Repatha for 18 months reduced non-calcified plaque on CT angiography in a 47-patient study

Mar 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 11 ↩ 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

In a small imaging study, intensive LDL lowering with the PCSK9 inhibitor evolocumab (average LDL about 30 mg/dL) shrank non-calcified plaque while total plaque volume was unchanged. Louis compares this with the roughly 5 uL/year soft plaque growth in people untreated at risk.

VESALIUS-CV: Repatha cut first heart attack or stroke 25% in high-risk primary prevention

Nov 08, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 3 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summarises VESALIUS-CV in 12,000+ high-risk patients without prior MI or stroke (median age 66, LDL-C >=90 mg/dL on therapy). Adding evolocumab cut 3-point MACE 25% over ~4.5 years, with LDL-C reduced about 55% to a median 45 mg/dL versus 109 mg/dL on placebo.

PCSK9 inhibitors on top of statins regressed plaque in GLAGOV and PRECISE-IVUS

Dec 25, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 3 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summarizes IVUS trials of PCSK9 inhibition for plaque regression. In GLAGOV, adding evolocumab to statins significantly reduced percent atheroma volume versus statins alone, PRECISE-IVUS showed a similar direction, and ODYSSEY J-IVUS with alirocumab was a small, non-significant reduction over a short 36 weeks.

Keto-CTA plaque progression vs plaque regression on Repatha in Budoff's other study

Apr 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis compares non-calcified plaque volume (NCPV) changes in the Keto-CTA (LMHR) study with a second Matthew Budoff study in higher-risk people whose LDL was lowered to about 30 mg/dL with the PCSK9 inhibitor Repatha. The lean, healthy keto group with LDL 254 mg/dL progressed, while the hypertensive, higher-risk Repatha group mostly showed major regression. He also notes that uL and mm^3 are the same unit when comparing NCPV across studies.

Repatha alone reversed soft plaque in 37 of 47 patients over 18 months at LDL near 30

Nov 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis explains a small uncontrolled study of the PCSK9 inhibitor evolocumab with CT angiography. Average LDL was about 30 mg/dL, 37 of 47 patients had some non-calcified plaque regression, and many had large reversals worth years of typical growth (about 5 mm3 per year). He cautions it was small with no randomization or control group, and that PCSK9 monotherapy is not yet proven to reduce heart attacks.
