100 mg vitamin B6 reduced depression symptoms in a new randomized trial; B12 failed

Aug 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes a new RCT reports that 100 mg vitamin B6 significantly reduced depression symptoms while B12 did not. The same authors found in 2022 that B6 reduces anxiety. He notes depression and anxiety are CVD risk factors and warns of nerve toxicity with prolonged high doses.

Daily peanut butter improved muscle power in older adults and memory in young adults

Jun 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 26 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares two randomized trials of peanut butter. In 120 older adults at fall risk, 43 g/day for six months improved sit-to-stand speed and muscle power without changing weight; a separate 6-month trial in young adults (ARISTOTLE) found better memory and stress response.

Magnesium chloride 248 mg/day improved mild-to-moderate depression in a crossover RCT

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

A randomized crossover trial in 126 adults with mild-to-moderate depression gave 248 mg elemental magnesium daily as magnesium chloride for 6 weeks. PHQ-9 depression scores improved with a net difference of about 6 points versus the control period. Louis shared the paper with tags on magnesium, depression and anxiety.

Cold pack on the neck raised heart rate variability in a small RCT; viral post overstates

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Sleep, stress & social health

Louis links the actual study behind a viral post claiming an ice pack on the back of the neck calms anxiety in seconds via the vagus nerve. The randomized trial in healthy participants found cold stimulation at the lateral neck raised heart rate variability and lowered heart rate versus control, but Louis says the post exaggerates and it still seems a safe thing to try.
