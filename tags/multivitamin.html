COSMOS: RDA-level multivitamin slowed epigenetic aging by about 4 months

Mar 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 9 Supplements & vitamins

Louis calls the COSMOS epigenetic-aging analysis arguably the highest quality RCT on multivitamins ever done. High vitamin B12 in a multivitamin did not raise disease risk in a 14-year Harvard trial

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 9 Supplements & vitamins

The poster argues it is unlikely high B12 intake causes cancer or clonal disease. The Physicians' Health Study multivitamin trial, which contained 10 times the RDA of B12, found no increase in major disease over 14 years and an 8% lower overall cancer risk. Nurses' and Physicians' cohorts also found no B12-disease link, except combined B12 and B6 with hip fracture, which vanished for B12 alone.

Daily multivitamin slowed cognitive aging by about two years in a COSMOS meta-analysis

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a summary of COSMOS cognition results: in the main analysis of 573 adults 60+, a daily multivitamin modestly improved overall cognition and episodic memory over two years. Pooling with two earlier COSMOS cognition studies (more than 5,000 older adults) gave clear benefits, estimated at about two years of slower cognitive aging. Effects were modest, it is unclear which nutrient matters, dementia prevention was not tested, and the manufacturer donated tablets. He adds that this was an RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver).

Multivitamin cohort study found 4% higher mortality, but bias makes it uninformative

Aug 11, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

Peter Attia's critique of a study of over 390,000 adults in three US cohorts followed up to 27 years found daily multivitamin users had 4% higher mortality, inconsistent across follow-up and not dose-dependent. Healthy and sick user biases and confounding mean it neither proves a benefit nor shows harm, and multivitamins can correct deficiencies.
