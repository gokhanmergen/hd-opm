Long-term melatonin use linked to higher heart failure risk in an observational analysis

Nov 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 29 Supplements & vitamins

Louis comments on an AHA-presented analysis of more than 130,000 adults with chronic insomnia, in which melatonin use over a year was associated with about 90% higher odds of heart failure. He warns it is retrospective, cannot prove causation, and that melatonin is a hormone whose long-term high-dose effects are not well studied.

Magnesium plus potassium improved insomnia and sleep hormones in type 2 diabetes

Jul 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 8 Sleep, stress & social health

Louis suggests that taking magnesium at bedtime may work better when combined with an equal dose of potassium, especially in people with diabetes. The shared summary describes a 2024 Frontiers in Endocrinology randomized trial in adults with type 2 diabetes and insomnia, where the combination gave the biggest improvements.

Ergo-Log doubts an unpublished study linking melatonin to heart failure

Nov 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

A shared Ergo-Log item notes an unpublished epidemiological study presented at AHA in October 2025 suggesting melatonin may raise heart failure risk. The site doubts it, citing trials that show a positive effect of melatonin on heart failure.
