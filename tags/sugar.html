Buck Institute: neurons burn sugar differently, hinting how calorie restriction helps

Jul 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 4 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares work from group member Pankaj Kapahi's lab at the Buck Institute, published in Nature Metabolism, describing a new mechanism by which calorie restriction protects the brain from Alzheimer's-related damage. It may help explain why GLP-1 drugs show promise against dementia.

Omega-3 fats may blunt the post-meal inflammatory spike after high saturated fat and sugar

Mar 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 1 Inflammation & CRP

Explains that eating increases gut permeability, letting bacterial lipopolysaccharide (LPS) enter the blood and trigger inflammation, especially after meals high in saturated fat and sugar and low in fiber. Human studies suggest omega-3s reduce this response, though the clinical significance of the spikes is unclear.

Coffee linked to lower type 2 diabetes risk: 29% lower at 5 cups a day, decaf included

Nov 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares a SuppVersity summary of a Journal of Functional Foods review. A meta-analysis found 5 cups a day versus none was associated with a 29% lower type 2 diabetes risk, with each extra cup about 6% lower, and decaf showed a similar benefit to caffeinated coffee. Data beyond 6 cups are murky.
