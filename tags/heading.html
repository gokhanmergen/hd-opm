Statins do not slow coronary calcium; severe-calcification odds hit 4.48 after 10+ years

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 41 Statins

Evidence review concluding statins reduce events and soft plaque but do not slow Agatston CAC progression, and that long-term cohorts show calcification accelerating without plateau, interpreted as plaque repair into stable scar. Trials: St. Francis (CAC unchanged, events down 33%), SALTIRE (progression 26% vs 18%) and BELLES (similar progression, ~11-14%).

Louis points members to Gokhan Can's document 'Plaque Reduction / CVD', a March 2026 review of plaque regression and stabilization interventions. Tags indicate it covers statins, PCSK9 inhibitors, pioglitazone, Mediterranean and DASH diets, and EPA.

Strategies for lowering TMAO: diet, supplements and repurposed drugs

Aug 29, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 22 · 💬 18 ↩ 1 Gut microbiome

A written review of how TMAO forms from choline and carnitine via gut bacteria and the liver, and how to lower it. It points to less red meat and egg yolk, a fiber-rich Mediterranean-style diet, polyphenols and allicin, and statins and metformin. Gokhan says TMAO is not his top concern but worth knowing; he mentions a small human berberine trial that lowered TMAO and plaque.

Infectious hypothesis of heart disease: oral streptococci in about 42% of coronary plaques

Sep 13, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 7 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A long essay argues that chronic hidden infections may help drive plaque growth and rupture. It highlights a 2025 JAHA study finding viridans-group oral streptococci in roughly 42% of coronary plaque samples, reinforcing the oral health and heart link.

Organic food: clear pesticide reduction, less certain evidence of health benefit

Sep 07, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 8 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

A review-style explainer on organic versus conventional food. It clarifies that the USDA Organic label certifies farming practices rather than health claims, and that organic farming still permits some pesticides. The core tension is a clear drop in pesticide exposure against more complex evidence for long-term health benefit.

Cholesterol as a master regulator of blood stem cells, driving inflammatory hematopoiesis

Oct 19, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Inflammation & CRP

Review of preclinical evidence that high LDL pulls hematopoietic stem cells out of dormancy, causing proliferation, oxidative stress, telomere shortening, impaired repopulation and lasting epigenetic changes that program blood and immune cells toward chronic inflammation. This offers a molecular basis for residual inflammatory risk after cholesterol is treated.

Glyphosate: IARC classification and the case for avoiding it

Sep 07, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

An advocacy-style essay arguing that glyphosate should be avoided. It cites the 2015 IARC classification as probably carcinogenic to humans, based on limited human evidence of non-Hodgkin lymphoma, sufficient animal evidence and strong mechanistic evidence. Not specific to heart disease.
