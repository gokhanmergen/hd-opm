French cohort of 22.7 million: mRNA COVID shots cut COVID death 74%, all-cause death 25%

Dec 05, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 27 · 💬 47 Infections & vaccines

Reports a French cohort of 22.7 million vaccinated and 5.9 million unvaccinated adults followed nearly four years: 74% lower COVID death risk and 25% lower risk of death overall, with no turbocancer signal. The admin adds that vaccination protects against heart disease deaths in high-risk groups and that he prefers flu vaccine to COVID mRNA.

Personalized mRNA vaccine succeeds in phase 3 melanoma trial after ~50% phase 2 benefit

Aug 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 12 Cancer

A personalized mRNA vaccine plus Keytruda improved recurrence-free and distant metastasis-free survival in a phase 3 trial of more than 1,100 patients with melanoma. The earlier phase 2 trial showed roughly a 50% reduction in recurrence or death at 5 years. Approval might come as early as 2027, and trials are under way in other cancers.

Flu vaccination linked to lower all-cause mortality, per admin's earlier evidence summary

Dec 14, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 13 Infections & vaccines

Reminder to get vaccinated, linking an earlier admin post. The shared summary lists the flu vaccine with 11-42% lower all-cause mortality in RCTs and prospective cohorts, 43% lower in consistent associations across 40+ studies, and 25-40% lower Alzheimer's risk, citing a cohort study and a systematic review and meta-analysis. Also notes Novavax (non-mRNA) COVID vaccine is no longer available to him.

BMJ: evidence mounts that COVID-19 disrupts immunity, challenging 'immunity debt' idea

Aug 20, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A BMJ feature reports that the immunity debt theory is increasingly challenged and that SARS-CoV-2 may cause lasting immune disruption. Evidence includes more infections after covid than after flu or RSV, viral reactivation, T-cell exhaustion and persistent inflammation.

Flu vaccination and mortality: the author's reading of the evidence

Nov 18, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 7 · 💬 14 ↩ 3 Infections & vaccines

The author describes getting his yearly flu shot plus a Novavax COVID shot, then summarizes evidence that flu vaccination is associated with lower all-cause and cardiovascular mortality. Reported reductions come from both RCTs and cohort studies, though the post flags observational limits.

Repair Biotechnologies mRNA therapy cleared free cholesterol and cut plaque 17% in mice

Aug 10, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Presentation by Reason on a fusion protein delivered by mRNA in lipid nanoparticles to clear free cholesterol from liver cells. In mice it reduced plaque volume 17% in six weeks and stabilized plaques; primate studies are planned, with human trials aimed at early 2026.

Personalized mRNA melanoma vaccine could extend to other cancers, with trials ongoing

Aug 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Cancer

Louis adds that the real promise of the personalized mRNA vaccine is its use across cancers beyond melanoma. The shared physician explanation stresses it treats cancer after tumor removal rather than preventing it. Trials are ongoing in non-small cell lung, kidney and bladder cancer.

mRNA therapy REP-0003 aims to reverse plaque in homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia

Jul 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis shares a note that Repair Biotechnologies' investigational mRNA therapy REP-0003 received FDA eligibility under the Rare Disease Evidence Principles Pathway for homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia. It aims to remove intracellular cholesterol rather than lower LDL.

mRNA-driven Myc and Cyclin T1 restarted heart muscle cell division after heart attack

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis shares a summary of a Nature Cardiovascular Research study from Cambridge using temporary modified mRNA to activate Myc and Cyclin T1 in heart muscle cells so mature cardiomyocytes re-enter division. The aim is to regenerate heart tissue after a heart attack instead of leaving scar tissue and chronic heart failure. This is early-stage regeneration research.
