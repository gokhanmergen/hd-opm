6-month intermittent fasting RCT: ~8% weight loss, non-HDL down 11%, TG down 23%

Jan 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 3 Fasting & meal timing

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's Nature Communications secondary-outcomes paper on 6 months of intermittent fasting in middle-aged adults with overweight. Lipids and body fat improved, but glucose, insulin resistance, blood pressure, inflammation and carotid IMT did not change.

Nuts, seeds and corn did not raise diverticulitis risk in nearly 30,000 women

May 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a large prospective cohort study in women showing that eating nuts, seeds, corn or seeded fruit did not increase diverticulitis risk, while healthy diet patterns (DASH, Mediterranean-style) were linked to lower risk. He notes his mother with diverticulosis was told to avoid nuts for years. Peripheral to heart health but relevant to nut-eating advice.

Metformin may work partly by reshaping the gut microbiome

Nov 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 Gut microbiome

A shared post says studies show metformin increases helpful gut species such as Akkermansia and Bacteroides that improve glucose control. Findings are mostly in animals and organoids; no bacteria have replaced metformin in large human trials.
