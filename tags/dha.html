Omega-3 supplementation across 40 RCTs lowered heart attack 13% and fatal heart attack 35%

Dec 20, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 26 Supplements & vitamins

A 2021 meta-analysis of 40 randomized trials in 135,267 participants found omega-3 EPA/DHA reduced several cardiac outcomes, with suggested doses of 1000-2000 mg.

PREVENT-E4: 2 g/day DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 365 at-risk older adults

Jun 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis reports the PREVENT-E4 randomized trial found no cognitive or hippocampal-volume difference between DHA and placebo over two years, though it showed 2 g DHA reached spinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. The trial was small and designed mainly to test brain penetration.

Adventist study: vegans 85+ had higher neurological death risk than non-vegetarians

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Among AHS-2 participants (about 88,000, over 12,000 deaths), vegans aged 85 and older had higher risk of death from stroke (17%), dementia (13%) and Parkinson's (37%) than healthy non-vegetarians, while pesco-vegetarians had the lowest risk. The comparison was against very healthy non-vegetarians, not the standard American diet.

High-dose DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 2-year APOE4 trial, but reached the brain

Jun 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Brain health & dementia

Louis comments on a Medscape report of a trial of high-dose omega-3 in older adults at high dementia risk. It found no improvement in memory, cognition or hippocampal volume, although DHA reached the cerebrospinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. He notes the trial was short at two years, and APOE4 carriers with the highest DHA to arachidonic acid ratio declined less.

Higher stored EPA linked to 36% lower bleeding stroke risk in a 21-year Danish study

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Louis shares a 21-year Danish study of 54,546 adults using fat-tissue samples of EPA and DHA. People with the highest EPA had a 36% lower risk of bleeding stroke, DHA was neutral, and ALA was linked to 32% higher risk (needs confirmation). Pooled RCTs found no extra brain bleeding, except small risk with high-dose prescription EPA. The study is observational.

Fuhrman: vegans should supplement algae-based DHA and EPA for brain protection

Mar 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Supplements & vitamins

Joel Fuhrman argues that vegans converting plant ALA poorly may have an omega-3 index below 4%, linked to higher stroke, dementia and Parkinson's risk in older vegans, and recommends algae-based DHA/EPA to reach 6-9%. Claims come from a promotional blog without cited studies.
