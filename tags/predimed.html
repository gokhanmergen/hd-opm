Olive oil is the only fat tested in several large trials: Lyon, PREDIMED and CORDIOPREV

Feb 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 31 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains why he uses olive oil in all his cooking: it is the only fat source tested in multiple large, roughly 5-year randomized trials with hard heart outcomes (Lyon Diet Heart Study, PREDIMED, CORDIOPREV), each finding fewer heart attacks and strokes versus supposedly heart-healthy control diets. He acknowledges other fats or no oil might work better but lack equivalent RCT proof.

Pooled blinded trials: statins cause only about 1 in 15 reported muscle aches

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 12 Statins

A pooled analysis of 19 blinded trials with 123,940 people found that over about four years, 27.1 of 100 on a statin reported muscle pain or weakness versus 26.6 of 100 on placebo. This implies that the vast majority of muscle complaints on statins are not caused by the drug, consistent with a nocebo effect.
