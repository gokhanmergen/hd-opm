Lyon Diet Heart Study: Mediterranean diet cut cardiac events and deaths about 70%

Dec 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 16 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis comments on a video clip about the Lyon Heart Study, a randomized secondary-prevention trial in people with a prior heart attack. The traditional Mediterranean diet cut cardiovascular events about 70% and all-cause death about 70% over 2.5 years, which he calls the largest total mortality reduction reported in a CVD prevention trial and about twice the benefit of statins in secondary prevention.

Adventist study: vegans 85+ had higher neurological death risk than non-vegetarians

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Among AHS-2 participants (about 88,000, over 12,000 deaths), vegans aged 85 and older had higher risk of death from stroke (17%), dementia (13%) and Parkinson's (37%) than healthy non-vegetarians, while pesco-vegetarians had the lowest risk. The comparison was against very healthy non-vegetarians, not the standard American diet.

Rutgers trial tests plant omega-3 ALA for early Alzheimer's in APOE4 carriers

May 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 5 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a Rutgers clinical trial of alpha-linolenic acid in APOE4 carriers. The rationale is that APOE4 carriers transport DHA poorly across the blood-brain barrier, which may explain weak DHA trial results, while ALA enters the brain more easily and can be converted locally to DHA. ALA is found in plant foods such as flaxseed.
