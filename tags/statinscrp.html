ACC urges measuring hsCRP; statins cut hsCRP about 1 mg/L in a 26-RCT meta-analysis

Oct 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 21 Inflammation & CRP

Louis notes that the latest American College of Cardiology scientific statement recommends measuring and treating hsCRP and points out that statins do this effectively. He reshares a 2022 meta-analysis of 26 RCTs showing statins lowered hs-CRP and CRP in patients with CVD.

Meta-analysis of 26 RCTs: statins lowered hs-CRP by about 1 mg/L in patients with CVD

Jul 12, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 6 Inflammation & CRP

A 2022 meta-analysis pooled randomized trials and found statins significantly reduce hs-CRP and CRP in patients with cardiovascular disease, in both high-intensity and lower-intensity regimens, with treatment over 10 weeks. For someone with hs-CRP of 2 mg/L this is about a 50% drop.

Meta-analysis: statins cut arterial wall inflammation on PET-CT, high-intensity most

Oct 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 Statins

Louis highlights a 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis of 10 clinical trials (373 subjects) using 18F-FDG PET-CT, a marker of arterial wall inflammation. Statins markedly reduced uptake (target-to-background ratio), significantly so for high-intensity therapy and non-significantly for low-to-moderate intensity.
