AHA PREVENT calculator replaces Pooled Cohort Equations for ages 30-79

Jan 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 21 ↩ 12 Risk assessment & lab tests

The AHA updated its cardiovascular risk calculator, PREVENT, for adults aged 30-79 without known CVD. It uses newer, larger and more diverse data than the 2013 Pooled Cohort Equations and includes kidney and metabolic disease, giving 10- and 30-year risk of both ASCVD and heart failure. Statins for all could halve heart attacks yet add only 10-20 days to life over 5 years

May 14, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 Statins

Louis works through why universal statin use could cut lifetime heart attack and stroke risk from about 20% to 10% while extending average lifespan by only days over 5 years: only about 10% of heart attacks and strokes are fatal, and only 1 in 5 US deaths are from them, so the reduction in overall deaths is about 50 times smaller. His arithmetic suggests a 0.022% to 0.094% reduction in overall deaths over 5 years. Note that this is a population-averaged back-of-envelope calculation.

Statins neither cause nor cure cancer: meta-analyses of randomized trials show no effect

Dec 31, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Statins

Reviews the evidence on statins and cancer. A 2012 meta-analysis of 134,537 participants in 22 RCTs found identical cancer incidence (1.4% per year) and mortality (0.5% per year) in statin users and non-users, and a 2023 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs found the same. Anticancer effects in specific contexts remain preclinical or suggestive.
