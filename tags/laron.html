Willett shares concern that whey powders may raise long-term risk via insulin/IGF1/mTOR

Jan 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 13 ↩ 2 Protein

Louis says Walter Willett shares his concern that processed whey could raise insulin, IGF-1 and mTOR, which are implicated in cancer, heart disease and aging. He notes whey has short-term benefits but little long-term data.

Laron syndrome, with lifelong low IGF-1 and mTOR, shows low cancer, diabetes and CVD risk

Jan 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 3 ↩ 5 Aging & longevity

Louis reposts an explanation of Laron syndrome, a mutation preventing IGF-1 production, and notes affected people have almost no cancer or diabetes, lower Alzheimer's risk and low CVD risk.

Lowering IGF1 extends lifespan in mice, so be wary of 'anti-aging' growth hormone claims

Jun 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 2 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis argues that growth hormone or IGF1 should not be marketed as anti-aging, because lowering them consistently extends lifespan in rodents. The record mouse, a growth hormone receptor knockout, lived almost 5 years versus about 2.5 for a normal lab mouse.

People with Laron syndrome (low IGF1) appear to have low cardiovascular risk

Jun 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis notes that people with genetically low IGF1 (Laron syndrome) have very low rates of cancer, diabetes and Alzheimer's, and a recent study suggests lower cardiovascular risk too. The linked Nature news piece describes lower blood sugar and insulin resistance in these individuals, hinting at new treatment strategies.
