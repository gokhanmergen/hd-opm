Moli-sani: more than 3 tablespoons of olive oil daily linked to 28% lower cancer mortality

Mar 20, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a summary of a 2024 Moli-sani Study analysis of 22,895 Italian adults followed for 12 years. Consuming over 3 tablespoons of olive oil daily was associated with 28% lower cancer mortality versus under 1.5 tablespoons, partly mediated by lower blood pressure and resting heart rate. The summary stresses the observational design and self-reported diet.

Conventional produce beats no produce: natural plant carcinogens dwarf pesticide residues

Louis argues that, while organic is fine if affordable, eating conventionally grown fruit and vegetables is much better than skipping them. He cites Ames's PNAS papers showing 99.99% by weight of dietary pesticides are plants' own natural defense chemicals, and that natural carcinogens in produce far exceed synthetic residues.

Olive oil protected against cardiac hypertrophy in aging-model rats

Nov 23, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 11 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

In rats given D-galactose to mimic cardiac aging, eight weeks of olive oil reduced cardiac hypertrophy and oxidative stress (MDA), increased antioxidant activity (SOD) and improved expression of heart-health genes. It is an animal study, so human benefit is not shown.

Spinach raises blood antioxidant activity more than 1250 mg vitamin C

Jun 03, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 12 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis argues against the claim that vitamin C supplements are harmful because antioxidants can blunt exercise adaptations in short-term studies. He notes that a large serving of spinach raises blood antioxidant activity more than a 1250 mg vitamin C supplement, and that short-term biomarker studies often predict long-term outcomes poorly. He does not claim antioxidant supplements prevent disease; human RCTs have mostly failed, with exceptions like age-related macular degeneration.
