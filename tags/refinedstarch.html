Insulin stimulates liver cholesterol production, linking insulin resistance to high LDL

Jun 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 22 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

The post explains that insulin signals the liver to make more cholesterol by keeping HMG-CoA reductase active, one reason insulin resistance and metabolic syndrome go with high cholesterol. It argues protein, especially animal protein, also raises insulin, partly offset by glucagon, and that swapping animal for plant protein lowers cholesterol beyond saturated fat and fiber effects. Much of the protein argument is the poster's inference.

Swapping refined carbs for unsaturated fat lowers LDL: meta-analysis of 60 feeding trials

Oct 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A meta-analysis of 60 controlled human feeding trials found that replacing carbohydrate calories with monounsaturated or polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL cholesterol, while saturated fat raises it. The effect looked largest for polyunsaturated fat, which is why choosing oils and nuts over refined starch and sugar helps the lipid profile.

Why insulin resistance lowers HDL, and why improving insulin sensitivity beats chasing HDL

Mar 21, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 ↩ 10 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis lists six mechanisms by which insulin resistance lowers HDL: impaired HDL maturation (lower LCAT), reduced apoA-I production, faster HDL catabolism (hepatic lipase, CETP), altered HDL composition and function, higher triglycerides making small dense HDL, and inflammation and oxidative stress. He concludes the best way to raise HDL is improving insulin sensitivity (lose visceral fat, exercise, cut refined starch and sugar), since insulin resistance is causal in heart disease while large RCTs suggest HDL itself is probably not.
