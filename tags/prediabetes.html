RCT: 60 g of pistachios daily lowered HbA1c 0.2% and LDL 10 mg/dL in prediabetes

Mar 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a 12-week RCT in 120 people with prediabetes testing 60 g of pistachios daily (30 g before breakfast and dinner). Pistachios lowered HbA1c, post-meal glucose spikes, LDL, triglycerides and waist circumference, while blood pressure, fasting glucose and insulin did not change.

Twice-weekly resistance training reversed prediabetes in a third of adults in 12 weeks

Jun 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 6 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares the 2017 Resist Diabetes randomized trial in sedentary adults aged 50-69 with prediabetes. Two days a week of machine-based, low-to-moderate-weight resistance training returned over a third of participants to normal blood sugar within 12 weeks.

Almost any diet with about 30 lb weight loss reverses liver fat and insulin resistance

Louis argues that weight loss, not a specific magic diet, explains cured prediabetes and metabolic syndrome in keto, carnivore or vegan anecdotes. He shares the Twinkie diet story, where a professor lost 27 pounds on junk food with calories under 1,800 a day.

Keto cut liver fat 67% vs 45% on Mediterranean or plant-forward diets in a small RCT

Aug 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 3 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis highlights a Cell Metabolism randomized trial of 42 people with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver assigned to ketogenic, Mediterranean or low-fat plant-forward diets with weight loss matched. Keto cut liver fat 67% versus 45% for the other diets. He notes it is small and wonders about a Mediterranean keto diet.

Twinkie diet shows metabolic gains come from fat loss, not food type

Nov 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 6 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis uses the Mark Haub 'Twinkie diet' case, where a professor lost 27 pounds in 10 weeks on junk food at 1,800 kcal/day, to argue that metabolic improvement follows loss of liver and visceral fat. He does not recommend such a diet.

Daily avocado plus mango for 8 weeks improved vascular function in prediabetes

Apr 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 4 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A randomized trial found adding avocado and mango to the diet raised flow-mediated dilation to 6.7% while it fell to 4.6% in controls, with lower diastolic BP and some improvement in eGFR.

Keto beat Mediterranean and low-fat diets on liver fat and glycemia at equal weight loss

Aug 27, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Petersen et al. (Cell Metabolism, 2026) fed 42 adults with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver all meals in a controlled trial until ~10% weight loss. All diets improved muscle insulin sensitivity and body composition, but the very-low-carb ketogenic diet gave clearly larger improvements in liver metabolic function, intrahepatic fat and glycemic control.

Insulin resistance may be an adaptive response to nutrient excess

Jul 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 2 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis discusses a Cell Metabolism paper proposing insulin resistance and T2D as allostatic responses to chronic nutrient excess. He notes glucose-only drugs (sulfonylureas, insulin) don't improve outcomes consistently while metformin, SGLT2i and GLP-1 agonists do.

Small dense HDL tied to coronary calcium in insulin resistance; drugs may offset 40%

Nov 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis highlights a paper in the International Journal of Cardiology reporting that small HDL particles are associated with prediabetes, diabetes and subclinical coronary atherosclerosis. The authors suggest lipid-lowering drugs may reduce up to 40% of the coronary calcification linked to high small HDL. Louis concludes that high HDL is not always good and that people with insulin resistance considering a statin should read the paper.

Prediabetes remission depends on visceral fat loss, not total weight (Nature Medicine)

Oct 04, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 3 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A Nature Medicine analysis found prediabetes can go into remission without overall weight loss if fat is distributed to subcutaneous rather than visceral deposits. Louis stresses that even normal-weight people can have excess liver fat that must be lost to reverse prediabetes.

RCTs show skipping breakfast does not cause weight gain and cuts about 260 calories a day

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 Fasting & meal timing

Observational studies link skipping breakfast to weight gain, but a 2019 BMJ review pooling 13 randomized trials found no evidence that eating breakfast aids weight loss or that skipping it causes weight gain. Breakfast skippers ate about 260 fewer calories per day overall. The possible downside is poorer glycemic control later in the day in people with diabetes or prediabetes.

Magnesium oxide lowered fasting glucose in older adults with prediabetes and low magnesium

Mar 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A small 16-week RCT of 360 mg/day magnesium oxide in older adults with prediabetes and magnesium deficiency modestly reduced fasting glucose, but HbA1c did not change significantly. The post notes benefit appears to occur only in those who are deficient.
