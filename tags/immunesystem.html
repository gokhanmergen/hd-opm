Niacin in a small glioblastoma pilot study: long survival, but historical controls

Feb 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 7 ↩ 2 Cancer

Louis notes a pilot study where niacin (vitamin B3) appeared to improve glioblastoma outcomes by rejuvenating immune function, probably via NAD. It was small and used historical controls.

Persistently low lymphocyte count is linked to higher mortality; check ALC on your CBC

Aug 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 11 Risk assessment & lab tests

Louis notes persistent lymphopenia is associated with higher mortality, possibly via thymus involution, and that absolute lymphocyte count is on a standard CBC. The quoted explanation links low ALC with worse outcomes in critically ill patients including those with heart failure.

Vitamin B3 forms and NAD: strong evidence in skin cancer, only preliminary in glioblastoma

Jun 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 ↩ 2 Cancer

Louis notes randomized trials show nicotinamide can reduce certain skin cancers, and early niacin trials are testing glioblastoma, with both likely acting through NAD. The shared analysis stresses the brain-cancer result comes from 15 patients with no control group, while the skin-cancer effect is real but modest.
