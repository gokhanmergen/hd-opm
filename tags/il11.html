Blocking IL-11 extended mouse lifespan ~25%; diets and drugs that may lower it listed

Jul 17, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 15 ↩ 1 Inflammation & CRP

A news share on the study showing IL-11 blockade extends mouse lifespan by about 25%. IL-11 is a pro-inflammatory cytokine from the IL-6 family that rises with age; the poster suggests Mediterranean diet, exercise, statins, aspirin, metformin and ACE inhibitors may reduce IL-6 family signaling, and colchicine may too (speculative).

Colchicine may work partly by inhibiting IL-11, a lifespan-linked pro-fibrotic cytokine

Jul 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 15 ↩ 4 Inflammation & CRP

The poster speculates that because colchicine, the first approved anti-inflammatory heart drug, has anti-fibrotic effects (it was studied in pulmonary fibrosis), it may act partly through IL-11 inhibition. Blocking IL-11 extended mouse lifespan by 25%, and anti-IL-11 therapies are entering pulmonary fibrosis trials. This is a hypothesis.

Skepticism of a startup's claimed drug combo that mimics young blood for aging

Sep 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 9 Aging & longevity

Louis comments on a startup claiming a two-drug combination replicates the effect of young blood. He says the underlying parabiosis science is real but the commercial rollout, based on anecdotes and a pay-to-play trial, endangers the field's reputation. He speculates the drugs are anti-inflammatory agents targeting cytokines such as IL-11 and TGF-beta.

First IL-11 blocking longevity drug enters human trials after 22-25% mouse lifespan gain

Jan 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Aging & longevity

Louis reports that the first patient has been dosed with an IL-11 blocking drug backed by Calico. He cites a Nature study in which IL-11 blockade produced a 22-25% median lifespan extension in mice and notes IL-11 is linked to heart disease.

Louis reposts a mouse lifespan graph comparing IL-11 inhibition with other aging interventions. IL-11 is an inflammatory molecule produced in excess with age and linked to numerous diseases including heart disease.
