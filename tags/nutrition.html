Vitamin K2 slows coronary calcium mainly in severe calcification; event benefit unproven

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 16 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Review of vitamin K2 (with or without vitamin D) and CAC. A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n=1,533) found a -17.3 Agatston units/year pooled difference. The largest RCT (389 men, 720 ug MK-7 + 1000 IU D vs placebo for 2 years) missed significance overall but slowed CAC at baseline CAC >=400; some CKD trials were null. Not yet a standard therapy.

Whey protein raised IGF-1 but not muscle or strength in 24-week RCT of older adults

Jul 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 17 Protein

Meta-analysis of 119 trials: nuts did not cause weight gain and modestly lowered LDL

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials (154 articles, 119 unique RCTs) found adding about 120 kcal/day of nuts did not significantly change weight, BMI or waist; body fat fell slightly and lean mass rose. Nuts were associated with small reductions in total cholesterol, LDL and triglycerides, with no significant effect on HDL.

MIND diet: 20% less grey matter loss per 3-point score rise in Framingham MRI study

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

In 1,647 Framingham Offspring participants followed about 12 years with repeated MRI scans, closer adherence to the MIND diet (Mediterranean-DASH hybrid) was linked to slower grey matter loss. Each 3-point rise in MIND score matched 20% less age-related grey matter decline, equal to about 2.5 years of slower brain aging. This is an observational association.

Swapping refined carbs for unsaturated fat lowers LDL: meta-analysis of 60 feeding trials

Oct 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A meta-analysis of 60 controlled human feeding trials found that replacing carbohydrate calories with monounsaturated or polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL cholesterol, while saturated fat raises it. The effect looked largest for polyunsaturated fat, which is why choosing oils and nuts over refined starch and sugar helps the lipid profile.

Protein restriction and aging: animal benefits, no proven human lifespan gain

Louis shares a summary of a review (Knopf and Lamming) of protein and amino-acid restriction.
