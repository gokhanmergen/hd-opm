Colchicine 0.5 mg cut plaque volume by over 1% in one year on CT angiography (Budoff)

May 11, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 37 ↩ 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis highlights a Budoff-led CCTA trial in which low-dose colchicine reduced percent atheroma volume by over 1% in a year, with lower CRP and a slowing of calcified plaque. He contrasts this with the Keto-CTA study, where PAV rose 0.8% on a keto diet. Budoff notes it was a small study but supports colchicine in chronic stable coronary disease.

Statin plaque regression RCTs: SATURN, REVERSAL, ASTEROID, COSMOS, JAPAN-ACS

Jan 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 29 ↩ 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists human trials in which statins reversed or halted atheroma growth measured as percent atheroma volume (PAV). High-dose atorvastatin and rosuvastatin regressed PAV in SATURN and ASTEROID, while REVERSAL showed halted progression versus pravastatin. COSMOS added canakinumab to rosuvastatin, and PROSPECT and JAPAN-ACS showed lower progression on statins.

SATURN: high-dose statins reversed plaque 1-2%; each 1% PAV cut means 20% fewer events

Aug 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 20 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis highlights the SATURN trial, where high-dose statins reversed plaque by about 1-2% PAV over two years, and each 1% PAV reversal is associated with about 20% fewer MACE. A shared meta-analysis of 31 studies and 4,997 patients (Li 2021) found lipid-lowering lowered TAV and PAV on IVUS.

Six RCTs show statins reverse total plaque volume by 5% to 17%

Aug 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists six randomized trials showing reversal of total atheroma volume with statins: COSMOS ~5%, SATURN ~5.5%, ASTEROID ~6.5%, IBIS-4 ~7.5%, STABLE ~8%, JAPAN-ACS ~17%. Reversals were measured by CT angiography or IVUS and came from soft rather than calcified plaque. A shared chart relates LDL to TAV change across statin, PCSK9 and ezetimibe trials.

Keto-CTA extrapolation: LMHRs with CAC over 100 hit 'severe plaque' (PAV >15%) in ~3 years

Apr 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 5 ↩ 4 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis extrapolates the Keto-CTA data: PAV above 15% is severe plaque. At the study's progression rates, people with CAC over 100 cross it in 3 years, those with 10-year risk over 5% in 8 years, the average participant in 17 years and CAC=0 in 29 years. He cautions these linear projections are likely upper bounds.

Letter to JACC: Keto-CTA omitted pre-specified outcome, a 43% rise in non-calcified plaque

Apr 21, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a letter to the editor critiquing the Keto-CTA study of lean mass hyper-responders. It argues the primary outcome (non-calcified plaque volume) was not reported in the paper, but the lead author later shared a median increase of 18.8 mm3 (~43%), several-fold above normal annual progression and in contrast to regression seen in DISCO-CT. The 0.8% rise in percent atheroma volume also exceeds typical annual change in PARADIGM.
