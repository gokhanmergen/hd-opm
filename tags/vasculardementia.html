Statin users had 14% lower dementia risk in a meta-analysis of 7 million people

Aug 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Statins

A systematic review of observational studies found statin use associated with 14% lower all-cause dementia and 18% lower Alzheimer's. Louis corrects that these were cohort and case-control studies, not RCTs.

Statin use linked to 20% lower dementia risk in heart-failure patients (Hong Kong study)

Jan 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Statins

Louis shares a news report on a University of Hong Kong study in heart-failure patients finding statin use associated with lower incident dementia. The observational association was described by the lead author as larger than expected, and prior research has suggested possible neuroprotective effects.

Statins started within a year of type 2 diabetes tied to 15% lower dementia risk

Sep 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Statins

Louis shares a Danish observational study of over 132,000 patients with type 2 diabetes; starting statins within a year was tied to 15% lower dementia risk over a decade, driven by vascular dementia. He notes it hints at starting early, as the STAREE trial showed no effect when starting in old age.

Proposed mechanism for coffee's protection against neurodegenerative disease

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Quoted podcast excerpt: lifelong coffee drinking is associated with slower cognitive decline and lower risk of Parkinson's, Alzheimer's and vascular dementia, possibly because caffeine blocks adenosine receptors and makes the brain work harder.
