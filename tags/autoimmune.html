Mouse and human data suggest atherosclerosis meets criteria for an autoimmune disease

Oct 03, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 8 Inflammation & CRP

The post highlights a news report of a new study in mice and patients proposing that atherosclerosis meets the criteria for an autoimmune disease. Evidence is mostly from mouse models with supporting human correlational data, so it is early; more work is needed to learn how much of the disease this process drives and whether interrupting it could slow progression.

JAMA RCT: high-dose vitamin D reduced disease activity in early multiple sclerosis

Mar 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 6 ↩ 5 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a JAMA trial in which oral cholecalciferol 100,000 IU every two weeks significantly reduced disease activity in clinically isolated syndrome and early relapsing-remitting MS. He speculates it may help other autoimmune diseases. The topic is not cardiovascular.

Vitamin D is not a panacea: big trials missed primary endpoints

Jul 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

Louis says he takes vitamin D, but large trials failed primary endpoints, with some secondary benefits. The strongest human evidence is for autoimmune disease protection.

EBV likely causes multiple sclerosis and may contribute to other autoimmune diseases

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 6 Infections & vaccines

Louis says it is now fairly clear Epstein-Barr virus causes MS, and EBV might play a role in other autoimmune diseases, possibly even heart disease, though that evidence is weaker. The shared post summarizes a 2018 Nature Genetics study linking the EBV protein EBNA2 to genetic risk regions in seven autoimmune diseases, without proving causation for all of them.

VITAL: vitamin D 2000 IU for over 2 years cut autoimmune disease risk about 40%

Mar 11, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 ↩ 4 Supplements & vitamins

Louis resurfaces a 2022 CNN report on the VITAL trial (about 25,000 people), saying 2000 IU/day of vitamin D for over two years reduced autoimmune disease risk by 40%, and ties it to a new JAMA MS trial. The CNN summary describes older adults on vitamin D and fish oil as about 30% less likely to develop autoimmune disorders.

Autoimmune and inflammatory diseases linked to elevated Lp(a) in ~50,000 patients

Sep 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 2 Lp(a)

A JACC Advances study of nearly 50,000 patients found people with gout, ankylosing spondylitis and IBD are more likely to have elevated Lp(a), with rates approaching those in established heart disease. Louis notes this fits Lp(a) being highly responsive to acute-phase inflammation.

Harvard Gazette on JoAnn Manson: VITAL trial of vitamin D and omega-3 in 25,000 adults

Jun 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Supplements & vitamins

Louis quotes a Harvard Gazette profile of JoAnn Manson, whose VITAL randomized trial of 25,000 people aged 50 and older tested vitamin D and fish oil omega-3. The quoted text reports a 28% reduction in heart attack with omega-3 (about 40% in those with low fish intake) and little effect of vitamin D on heart disease.

Vitamin D 2000 IU cut autoimmune disease risk 40% in the VITAL trial, MS a key target

Nov 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis resurfaces a three-year-old post: the strongest evidence for higher-dose vitamin D is in autoimmune disease, especially MS, and he notes a new JAMA publication on vitamin D treating MS. The VITAL trial of about 25,000 people at Harvard found that 2000 IU vitamin D daily for over 2 years cut autoimmune disease risk by about 40%. CNN coverage of the same trial reports about 30% lower risk with vitamin D and fish oil supplements.
