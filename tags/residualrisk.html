In statin-treated PCI patients with LDL <70, inflammation, not TG, gave ~1.8x more MACE

Apr 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 10 Inflammation & CRP

A shared study summary: among more than 9,400 statin-treated PCI patients with LDL under 70 mg/dL, elevated hs-CRP (2 mg/L or more) carried about 1.8x higher MACE risk, whereas elevated triglycerides alone showed no significant increase. Louis frames inflammation as a major driver of residual risk.

Lowering LDL does not eliminate heart attack risk: residual risk persists in every trial

Apr 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 15 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis counters the idea that driving LDL to zero makes heart attack and stroke risk vanish. Risk keeps falling as LDL goes lower, but every randomized trial still shows substantial residual risk, which is a major research focus.

On statins, high ApoB with normal LDL-C raised MI risk 49%; high LDL-C alone did not

Jul 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 2 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

In Copenhagen General Population Study participants on statins (n=13,015, median 8 years), high apoB with normal LDL-C had 49% higher MI rate and 21% higher mortality. High LDL-C with normal apoB showed no excess risk; non-HDL-C behaved like apoB.

Visceral fat drives residual CV risk through inflammation; GLP-1 agonists may help

Aug 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a Frontiers in Cardiovascular Medicine review linking visceral adipose tissue to residual cardiovascular risk via chronic inflammation. It discusses GLP-1 receptor agonists as promising therapies.
