Replacing saturated with polyunsaturated fat cut non-fatal heart attacks 25%

Dec 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 19 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes an Annals of Internal Medicine systematic review of 17 randomized trials with hard endpoints. Replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat lowered non-fatal heart attacks by 25% in higher-risk people over 5 years, similar to a lower-dose statin; other outcomes trended favorably but were not fully significant when the replacement was the general diet.

Super-oxidized fish oil fed for 7 weeks caused no harm in human trial

Sep 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

The poster shares a Bill Harris clip arguing oxidized fish oil is likely not harmful. In the trial described, researchers deliberately super-oxidized fish oil and gave it to participants for 7 weeks, finding no difference in oxidation, peroxidation, inflammation, glucose, lipids or liver enzymes versus non-oxidized fish oil.

Finland's North Karelia Project: less saturated fat, CHD deaths down nearly 80%

Dec 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 16 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Tom Rifai summary of Finland's 1970s turnaround, led by the North Karelia Project, where the population cut saturated fat and shifted to rapeseed, sunflower and soybean-based margarines and oils. Serum cholesterol fell about 15-20% and CHD mortality fell nearly 80% in working-age men, although smoking and blood pressure improvements also contributed.

Dietary fat and mortality meta-analysis: polyunsaturated favorable, trans fat harmful

Dec 01, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 9 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarizes a meta-analysis of 19 prospective cohorts (over 1 million participants) on dietary fat and mortality. Higher total, monounsaturated and especially polyunsaturated fat were associated with lower mortality, trans fat with higher mortality, and saturated fat showed a non-linear pattern with excess risk up to about 11% of energy.

Fat type beats amount: PUFA tied to lower mortality, trans and saturated fat to higher

Aug 24, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 8 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A 2020 Clinical Nutrition meta-analysis of 19 prospective cohorts (1,013,273 participants, 195,515 deaths) found polyunsaturated and monounsaturated fats associated with lower mortality, and saturated and trans fats with higher. Gokhan cautions that data guide rather than prescribe.

Meta-analysis of 102 feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat gives the best glycemic benefit

Nov 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarises a systematic review of 102 randomized feeding trials (4,220 participants) on 5% energy swaps. Replacing carbs with MUFA lowered HbA1c 0.09% and HOMA-IR 2.4%; with PUFA lowered HbA1c 0.11% and fasting insulin 1.6 pmol/L. PUFA alone improved insulin secretion capacity; saturated fat swaps did not change fasting glucose. Authors favor replacing refined carbs and saturated fat with unsaturated fats.

Meta-analysis of 119 trials: nuts did not cause weight gain and modestly lowered LDL

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials (154 articles, 119 unique RCTs) found adding about 120 kcal/day of nuts did not significantly change weight, BMI or waist; body fat fell slightly and lean mass rose. Nuts were associated with small reductions in total cholesterol, LDL and triglycerides, with no significant effect on HDL.

Examine: saturated fat raises LDL-C and apoB in WHO meta-analysis of 84 RCTs

Sep 05, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summary of Examine's article on saturated fat. A 2016 WHO meta-analysis of 84 RCTs (2,353 healthy adults) examined replacing 1% of calories from carbohydrates or unsaturated fat with saturated fat. Saturated fat raised LDL-C and apoB (suggesting higher risk), and HDL rose only about one-tenth as much as LDL-C, raising the LDL/HDL ratio.

Layne Norton rebuts seed-oil claims with human evidence on inflammation, LDL and oxidation

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A shared post argues that human trial evidence does not support the claim that seed oils are harmful. Systematic reviews of randomized trials find that raising linoleic acid does not consistently increase inflammatory markers, replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL and cuts coronary events, and trials show no significant increase in lipid peroxidation. It adds that LDL oxidation mostly occurs after retention in the artery wall, so LDL concentration still matters.

Olive oil has the strongest trial evidence, but canola and soybean oil also have support

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.
