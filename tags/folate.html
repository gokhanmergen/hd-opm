Methylfolate is not 'natural', and higher blood levels may promote cancer

Mar 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 33 Supplements & vitamins

Louis explains food folate is polyglutamate-bound and less bioavailable than folic acid, which is less bioavailable than 5-MTHF supplements. Raising methylfolate too high may fuel precancerous cells and shortened lifespan in worms and mice.

Network meta-analysis: only grape seed extract and folate reduced arterial stiffness

Oct 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 9 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

A network meta-analysis of 48 randomized trials in 2,719 people with heart disease or high blood pressure compared 31 diets and supplements on pulse wave velocity. Grape seed extract lowered PWV 1.70 m/s versus placebo and folate 1.46 m/s. Louis notes grape seed extract is likely safer than megadosing folate.

Late-life folate restriction reduced biosynthesis without harming healthspan in mice

Aug 08, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 16 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Mice switched at 52 weeks to a folate- and choline-free diet held their weight, avoided anemia, and showed reduced protein synthesis pathways over 68 weeks. Gokhan stresses the work is in yeast, worms and mice, not humans.

Louis shares a short Cleveland Clinic explainer that homocysteine is an amino acid broken down by vitamins B12, B6 and folate, and that high levels may indicate deficiency and increase clot, heart disease and stroke risk. It is basic background without trial evidence.

Even light alcohol raises breast cancer risk, but high folate erased it in Nurses' Study

Louis shares a Walter Willett clip noting wine, beer and spirits all raise breast cancer risk at under one drink a day, replicated in over 100 studies. B12 lowers homocysteine but has not cut heart attacks; B vitamins may lower stroke risk

Nov 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a review summary showing B12 supplements reliably lower homocysteine, but large randomized trials did not consistently reduce heart attacks or cardiac deaths. The strongest benefit was for stroke, mainly where foods are not folic-acid fortified, but most trials combined B12 with folic acid and B6. Routine B12 solely for CVD prevention is not supported, though it is essential if deficient.
