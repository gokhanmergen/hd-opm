Berberine lowered plaque score 3.2% and TMAO in a small 21-patient trial

Jun 10, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 23 Gut microbiome

Louis highlights a small human trial in which berberine reduced atherosclerotic plaque score, with a proposed mechanism of lowering gut-derived TMAO by acting on TMAO-producing gut bacteria. The sample is small and the comparison group was not randomized to the same degree as a large trial, so this is hypothesis-generating.

Berberine inhibits PCSK9 and modestly lowers LDL, far less than PCSK9 drugs

Jun 21, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 10 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes berberine is a cheap oral compound that inhibits PCSK9 and modestly lowers cholesterol. He would never discourage PCSK9 drugs for high-risk people, but suggests berberine may be worth considering for primary prevention in someone at low 10-year risk, while stressing its LDL lowering is much smaller.

Under 1% of oral berberine reaches the blood, so it likely acts in the gut

May 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 7 Gut microbiome

Louis argues berberine's very low oral bioavailability (<1%) means its main anti-diabetic action is via gut bacteria, unlike metformin. The shared post cites human pharmacokinetics (Spinozzi 2014) finding mostly the metabolite berberrubine in plasma.

Berberine modestly lowers LDL, triglycerides and ApoB: meta-analysis of 18 RCTs

Sep 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials in adults with dyslipidemia found berberine produced small reductions in LDL cholesterol, triglycerides and apoB, with possible sex-specific effects on HDL. The conclusion calls for larger trials assessing clinical outcomes.

Cleveland Clinic: berberine may reduce body fat, but evidence is early

Jun 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a Cleveland Clinic article saying early research suggests berberine may help reduce body fat, while a specialist cautions it may not be the miracle drug hoped for.

Berberine 500 mg twice daily lowered ApoB in two trials; CVD prevention unproven

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Supplements & vitamins

A shared summary says two randomized trials of 500 mg berberine twice daily reduced ApoB, with a reduction nearly three times greater in women, though across separate trials. A newer study of 84 men identified a 'Berberine Signature' of 92 proteins linked to about 13% lower odds of ischemic heart disease in UK Biobank, but those participants were not necessarily taking berberine, so it does not prove prevention.
