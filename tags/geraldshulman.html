Liver fat as low as 1.85% is linked to insulin resistance (Yale)

Jun 29, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 25 ↩ 14 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis posts a Yale report showing that liver fat above 1.85%, well below the 5% fatty liver threshold, is linked to insulin resistance. Lean people may have enough liver fat to cause insulin resistance without knowing it.

Gerald Shulman honored for showing how ectopic fat causes insulin resistance

Louis highlights Yale's Gerald Shulman, recognised for discovering molecular mechanisms by which fat in liver and muscle directly causes insulin resistance, including in lean people.

Gerald Shulman honored for showing hidden fat in muscle and liver drives diabetes

Nov 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares the American Liver Foundation's 2025 award to Gerald Shulman, whose team showed that fat stored in the wrong places, such as muscle and liver cells, disrupts insulin signaling and contributes to type 2 diabetes and liver disease. The quoted citation stresses that where fat is stored matters more than total body fat.

Shulman: about 10% weight loss and ~1,200 kcal/day can reverse liver insulin resistance

Nov 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a Yale Q&A with Gerald Shulman on reversing insulin resistance. Shulman says modest caloric restriction lowers liver fat and reverses liver insulin resistance and type 2 diabetes, and that this is likely how GLP-1 drugs work.
