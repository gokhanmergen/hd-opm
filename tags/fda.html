FDA keeps icosapent ethyl (Vascepa) approved for CV risk reduction based on REDUCE-IT

May 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 8 Supplements & vitamins

Shared summary that the FDA's approval of high-dose EPA stays in place despite debate over the mineral oil placebo in REDUCE-IT. It is indicated as add-on to statins for adults with triglycerides at least 150 mg/dL and established CVD, or diabetes plus two risk factors. Atrial fibrillation and bleeding were more common.

Lowering LDL does not guarantee fewer events: niacin and CETP inhibitors failed on MACE

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis explains that not every LDL-lowering therapy reduces heart attacks and strokes. Niacin added to high-dose statins lowered LDL more but two large RCTs showed no extra MACE benefit, and the CETP inhibitors torcetrapib and dalcetrapib lowered LDL but failed in outcome trials. This is why the FDA requires outcome trials.

FDA approved low-dose colchicine for CV risk reduction, including multiple risk factors

Jun 20, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 Inflammation & CRP

Louis notes that the FDA approved low-dose colchicine to reduce cardiovascular events, working by lowering arterial inflammation. Per the Medscape report, it is the first specific anti-inflammatory drug for this purpose in people with established atherosclerotic disease or multiple risk factors.
