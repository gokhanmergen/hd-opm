Blood donation halved cancer deaths in an RCT but did not lower heart attacks

Nov 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 13 Cancer

Louis reports a randomized trial where blood donation did not reduce heart attacks or strokes but halved cancer deaths after 6 years, possibly through lower iron.

Skepticism of a startup's claimed drug combo that mimics young blood for aging

Sep 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 9 Aging & longevity

Louis comments on a startup claiming a two-drug combination replicates the effect of young blood. He says the underlying parabiosis science is real but the commercial rollout, based on anecdotes and a pay-to-play trial, endangers the field's reputation. He speculates the drugs are anti-inflammatory agents targeting cytokines such as IL-11 and TGF-beta.
