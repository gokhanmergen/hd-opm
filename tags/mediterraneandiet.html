Prof. Valter Longo: the true Mediterranean diet hardly exists anymore, even in Italy

Jan 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 6 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares a quote from Valter Longo's 'At Longevity's Table' saying the true Mediterranean diet (extra virgin olive oil, legumes, vegetables, fruit, whole grains, less fish, cheese, eggs and moderate red wine) has largely been replaced by refined and processed foods. Louis adds that what people eat in the Mediterranean today is no longer a traditional Mediterranean diet.

PREDIMED: Mediterranean diet with olive oil or nuts cut major CV events by 30% and 28%

Aug 26, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 ↩ 7 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes the PREDIMED randomized trial of about 7,500 people testing the traditional Mediterranean diet for primary prevention. Groups supplemented with extra-virgin olive oil or nuts had a 30% and 28% lower rate of heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death after 5 years versus an AHA-style control diet. A shared Annals post adds that Mediterranean diet plus calorie reduction and exercise cut diabetes risk by 31% in a large trial.

A carnivore dieter's heart problems highlight the risk of social-media advice

Louis comments on a shared video about a man who followed carnivore influencers, avoided medication and developed heart disease despite a family history and high LDL-C. The shared commentary argues such people are victims of misinformation.
