Louis explains FDA approval needs three phases (safety, preliminary efficacy, full efficacy), typically a decade or more and at least $100 million, sometimes near $1 billion. Phase 3 trials are large and long and often fail, so promising therapies may never be tested. Meanwhile doctors can use GRAS supplements and prescribe drugs off-label, which lets science-based clinicians go beyond the standard of care.
