First epigenetic reprogramming trial in the eye; cardiovascular use is speculative

Louis comments on the start of a clinical trial using Yamanaka-factor epigenetic reprogramming to restore youthful cell states in the eye. COSMOS: 2 years of a basic multivitamin slowed biological aging by about 4 months

Mar 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 10 Supplements & vitamins

Latest COSMOS publication from Harvard and Mass General Brigham: a daily RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver) slowed five epigenetic-clock measures of biological aging, with the largest gains in participants who were biologically older than their age at baseline. The poster stresses this was a standard multivitamin, not a megadose, and recalls that COSMOS earlier showed slower cognitive decline.

Twin study: exercise benefits are real but smaller after controlling genetics and bias

Apr 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 5 Exercise & fitness

A 30-year Finnish twin study of ~22,000 participants found exercise improves healthy aging but offers a maximum of about 7% lower mortality, with both sedentary and highly active people showing faster biological aging. Louis argues observational exercise-longevity literature is confounded.
