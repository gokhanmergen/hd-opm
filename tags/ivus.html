Japan-ACS: pitavastatin and atorvastatin both regressed coronary plaque by about 17-18%

Summary of the Japan-ACS trial, in which over 300 acute coronary syndrome patients were randomized to pitavastatin or atorvastatin and imaged with IVUS. Pioglitazone slowed coronary plaque progression and shrank necrotic core in diabetics

Jan 14, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 27 ↩ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Data-rich overview of pioglitazone's effects on plaque. In PERISCOPE (543 type 2 diabetes patients with CAD, 18 months, IVUS) pioglitazone lowered percent atheroma volume while glimepiride raised it. A smaller trial (Ogasawara 2009) found reduced necrotic core and higher adiponectin, suggesting benefit via reduced inflammation.

SATURN: high-dose statins reversed plaque 1-2%; each 1% PAV cut means 20% fewer events

Aug 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 20 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis highlights the SATURN trial, where high-dose statins reversed plaque by about 1-2% PAV over two years, and each 1% PAV reversal is associated with about 20% fewer MACE. A shared meta-analysis of 31 studies and 4,997 patients (Li 2021) found lipid-lowering lowered TAV and PAV on IVUS.

Six RCTs show statins reverse total plaque volume by 5% to 17%

Aug 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis lists six randomized trials showing reversal of total atheroma volume with statins: COSMOS ~5%, SATURN ~5.5%, ASTEROID ~6.5%, IBIS-4 ~7.5%, STABLE ~8%, JAPAN-ACS ~17%. Reversals were measured by CT angiography or IVUS and came from soft rather than calcified plaque. A shared chart relates LDL to TAV change across statin, PCSK9 and ezetimibe trials.

YELLOW trials: intensive rosuvastatin cut lipid-core plaque within 7-8 weeks

Aug 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 19 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The YELLOW study found that short-term intensive lipid lowering with rosuvastatin reduced lipid-core burden (yellow plaque) in coronary lesions in as little as 7-8 weeks, stabilizing vulnerable plaque rather than shrinking blockages. YELLOW III added the PCSK9 inhibitor evolocumab to statins with greater improvement in plaque composition. Generic rosuvastatin costs under $10/month.

Ornish trial's 'plaque reversal' may be x-ray angiography measurement error

Dec 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 12 ↩ 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains criticisms of the small 1986-1992 Ornish trial: it used x-ray angiography, which outlines the blood-flow lumen rather than measuring plaque, so the small reported reversal may largely be measurement error. He contrasts this with modern CT angiography and IVUS, and notes the trial's tiny size as another major weakness.

A normal angiogram can hide large plaque burden because of Glagov remodeling

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains that angiography is a 'luminogram' that misses plaque inside the artery wall because early plaque causes outward (Glagov) remodeling that preserves the lumen. Modern plaque-progression studies use IVUS or CT angiography instead, and he cites this as a flaw of Dean Ornish's small 1990s trial.

Meta-analysis: lipid-lowering therapy regresses coronary plaque (Li 2021, SATURN)

Summarizes Li et al. 2021, a meta-analysis of 31 IVUS studies (4,997 patients) showing lipid-lowering therapy lowers atheroma volume, with maximal regression at LDL below 80 and HDL above 45 mg/dL. It also covers the SATURN analysis, where rosuvastatin 40 mg regressed plaque more than atorvastatin 80 mg.

IVUS-guided PCI did not beat angiography alone in two NEJM trials

Apr 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Two NEJM trials found IVUS-guided PCI gave no clinical benefit: IVUS-CHIP in complex high-risk PCI showed 13.9% vs 11.1% events (HR 1.25) and OPTIMAL in left main PCI 33.7% vs 30.9% (HR 1.11), despite better stent optimization.
