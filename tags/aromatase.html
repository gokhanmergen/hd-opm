Visceral fat, via aromatase, likely drives population-level testosterone decline

Apr 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 5 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis and a Barbell Medicine post argue that visceral fat contains aromatase, which converts testosterone to estrogen, and that BMI-adjusted studies underestimate this because waist circumference has risen faster than BMI. Lean, active men without chronic illness are said to keep stable testosterone into their 70s and 80s.

Tirzepatide raised testosterone more than TRT in men with obesity (pilot, n=83)

Jun 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares a small controlled pilot study in obese men with metabolic hypogonadism. Two months of tirzepatide gave greater weight loss and increases in testosterone, LH, FSH and SHBG while lowering estradiol, compared with no treatment or transdermal testosterone.

US testosterone decline disappears after adjusting for assay method and visceral fat

Apr 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 3 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares a clip from a Barbell Medicine physician review arguing the supposed decades-long drop in US testosterone vanishes once assay method and visceral fat are accounted for.
