Fatty liver in older adults mostly signals multimorbidity, not cirrhosis

Jul 26, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis notes that the real problem of liver fat is usually the cardiometabolic disease it drives, not liver damage. A shared Nature study found steatotic liver in 33.3% of older adults but only 2.4% had significant fibrosis.

Wikipedia cites 'low cholesterol causes mortality' as a correlation-causation fallacy

Dec 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 How to read research

Louis notes that the Wikipedia entry on correlation not implying causation uses low cholesterol and higher mortality as an example: diseases such as cancer lower cholesterol and increase mortality, so causality runs the opposite way.

Coffee over 3 cups/day linked to lower liver stiffness but not liver fat

Jun 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares a report that drinking more than three cups of coffee daily was associated with lower liver stiffness (fibrosis) but not with less liver fat accumulation.

Fatty liver can scar the liver years before ALT rises; 10% weight loss resolves it in 90%

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Routine ALT testing can miss advanced fatty liver disease: a Hepatology study biopsied 51 patients with normal ALT and found 12 with bridging fibrosis and 6 with cirrhosis. The post notes selection bias in that sample but says fibrosis behind a normal ALT is well established. Ten percent body weight loss resolved fatty liver in 90 percent and regressed fibrosis in 45 percent, and Mediterranean diet, resistance training and insulin-resistance management reduce liver fat independent of weight.
