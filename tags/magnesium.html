Review of 884 supplement RCTs: omega-3, folic acid and CoQ10 helped; beta-carotene harmed

Nov 30, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 7 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis quotes a JACC systematic review of 884 randomized trials (883,627 participants) on 27 micronutrients. Omega-3 reduced CVD mortality, heart attack and CHD events; folic acid reduced stroke; CoQ10 reduced all-cause mortality; vitamins C, D, E and selenium had no effect on CVD or type 2 diabetes; beta-carotene increased mortality and stroke. Several others improved risk factors with moderate-to-high quality evidence.

All magnesium forms, even oxide, are absorbed; form-specific marketing is hype

Apr 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

A shared piece argues that every magnesium salt dissociates in stomach acid and Mg2+ is what is absorbed, so threonate-for-brain or taurate-for-heart claims lack human data. Louis says differences between forms are fairly minor.

Magnesium at 400 mg/day or more for 12+ weeks lowered blood pressure by about 6/4 mmHg

Jan 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 4 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a summary of an umbrella meta-analysis finding that magnesium supplementation at doses of at least 400 mg/day for 12 weeks or longer significantly reduced blood pressure, mostly in people with metabolic disorders and likely poor magnesium status. Magnesium is not strong enough to be monotherapy for hypertension.

Magnesium glycinate and rapid depression recovery: a 2006 case series, not a trial

Magnesium 100 mg/day tied to 22% lower heart failure risk, but not CHD, in a meta-analysis

A shared summary of a 2016 BMC Medicine meta-analysis of 40 studies covering over a million people: magnesium was not linked to total CVD or CHD, but each extra 100 mg/day was linked to lower heart failure, stroke, type 2 diabetes and mortality.
