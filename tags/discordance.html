LDL-C vs LDL-P vs ApoB: particle number predicts risk better than cholesterol content

Jul 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

LDL-C measures cholesterol inside LDL particles while LDL-P counts the particles; they can be discordant (low LDL-C with many small particles), and particle number predicts risk better. Particle size adds little once number is accounted for, and ApoB, which counts all atherogenic particles, is the best test.

VLDL cholesterol (triglycerides / 5) may be more atherogenic than LDL

May 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 11 ↩ 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that VLDL is an emerging atherosclerosis risk factor and may be more atherogenic than LDL, so people with low LDL but high VLDL may still be at risk. VLDL can be roughly estimated by dividing triglycerides by 5 on a standard lipid panel, which is one reason to watch triglycerides. A normal VLDL is below 30 mg/dL, corresponding to triglycerides below 150 mg/dL, though optimal is likely lower in high-risk people.
