At high risk of a heart attack? Find a good cardiologist and add, don't replace, treatment

Feb 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 46 · 💬 15 ↩ 2 Myths, misinformation & controversies

Louis advises high-risk people to follow a good cardiologist rather than social media claims, which often cherry-pick one study out of many. He argues the window to experiment has passed once risk is high, and that natural or alternative approaches are safest as additions, with treatment reduced later with the cardiologist if risk markedly improves. It is opinion, not research.

A rule for deciding: don't bet your life on anything not proven in high-quality RCTs

Oct 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 37 · 💬 6 How to read research

Louis shares his personal approach to heart disease decisions: rely first on interventions proven in multiple high-quality randomized trials, such as the Mediterranean diet and LDL lowering, ignore advice that conflicts with RCTs, and add less-proven measures only on top of standard care in tiers. It is an opinion piece with no new data.

Men and women lose weight similarly with calorie restriction and GLP-1 drugs

May 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Stuart Phillips debunks claims that women need special weight loss strategies. A meta-analysis of 13 long-term RCTs found no sex difference, low-carb reviews similar, and GLP-1 trials showed women losing about 1 kg more.
