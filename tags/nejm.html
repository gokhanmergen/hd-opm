N-of-1 trial: most statin side effects arose with placebo too, pointing to nocebo

Feb 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 15 Statins

Louis highlights a NEJM N-of-1 trial where people who had stopped statins for side effects received four months of statin, four of placebo and four of nothing, randomly ordered. Symptoms with placebo were about the same as with statin, and half could restart statins; Louis infers about 90% of side effects were nocebo.

NEJM: hs-CRP, LDL and Lp(a) each predicted 30-year CVD risk in women, best combined

Aug 31, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 14 Risk assessment & lab tests

Louis highlights a NEJM Women's Health Study analysis of initially healthy women followed 30 years with 3,662 first major cardiovascular events. Top versus bottom quintile hazard ratios were 1.70 for hs-CRP, 1.36 for LDL and 1.33 for Lp(a), each independent, with the combination predicting best. The authors argue prevention should extend beyond 10-year risk estimates.

NEJM 2020 dairy review: a mixed bag, best for fermented forms, weak for fractures

Jan 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 21 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis highlights the widely cited 2020 NEJM review on dairy and disease risk. It concludes that high dairy intake is not supported for preventing fractures, is not clearly linked to weight, diabetes or CVD, and may raise prostate and endometrial cancer risk while lowering colorectal cancer risk. Effects depend on the comparison food: dairy fares better than processed red meat or sugary drinks but worse than nuts.

REACT in NEJM: 57% of adults have atherosclerosis and age is the biggest risk factor

Aug 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 4 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares the REACT study in NEJM of more than 16,000 adults aged 18-70 without known CVD, imaged by vascular ultrasound and CT. About 57% had plaque, rising from 1 in 13 in their 20s to about 9 in 10 in their 60s. Men developed silent disease 5-10 years earlier than women. He concludes age is the biggest risk factor.

VESALIUS-CV: Repatha cut first heart attack or stroke 25% in high-risk primary prevention

Nov 08, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 3 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summarises VESALIUS-CV in 12,000+ high-risk patients without prior MI or stroke (median age 66, LDL-C >=90 mg/dL on therapy). Adding evolocumab cut 3-point MACE 25% over ~4.5 years, with LDL-C reduced about 55% to a median 45 mg/dL versus 109 mg/dL on placebo.

REACT: silent atherosclerosis found in 57% of 16,808 adults, 7-9% of 18-29-year-olds

Sep 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The NEJM REACT study used carotid and femoral ultrasound plus coronary CT angiography in 16,808 adults aged 18-70 without known disease. Plaque was present in 57% overall, appeared in young adults, and a zero calcium score often missed early non-calcified plaque. Ten-year risk scores detected very little silent disease, so keeping risk factors favorable throughout life is the priority.

Statin use linked to less liver cancer and decompensation in 16,500 liver disease patients

Mar 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Statins

Louis shares an NEJM Journal Watch summary of a Boston study of 16,500 patients with chronic liver disease (mostly MASLD, viral and alcoholic hepatitis) at intermediate or high fibrosis risk. Statin users had lower 10-year incidence of hepatocellular carcinoma and less hepatic decompensation than nonusers. The data are observational.

Atrial fibrillation overview shared from NEJM

Jan 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A shared NEJM explainer describes atrial fibrillation as an irregular, rapid rhythm of the upper heart chambers that can cause clots in the left atrial appendage and stroke. It lists typical symptoms and the main treatments: rate and rhythm control, catheter ablation and anticoagulants.
