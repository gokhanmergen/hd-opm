Harvard cohort: unhealthy low-carb diets raise diabetes risk, plant-based ones lower it

Nov 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 15 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Summarises a video on a 30-year Harvard study of over 200,000 participants: low-carb diets high in animal protein, saturated fat and refined carbs like white potatoes were linked to higher type 2 diabetes risk, while plant-based versions (nuts, avocado, whole grains) lowered it. It also discusses rapid weight loss without exercise causing 'fat overshooting' on regain.

Sabatini on diet and mTOR: limit amino acids and insulin; ~18 h fasting may inhibit mTORC1

Dec 09, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 11 ↩ 3 Aging & longevity

Louis shares notes on a David Sabatini interview about how diet affects mTOR and autophagy. Sabatini suggests low amino acid or low carbohydrate diets lower mTOR, favors a higher-carbohydrate diet of low-glycemic carbs, and thinks mTORC1 senses total daily insulin. He guesses about 18 hours of fasting inhibits mTORC1, while stressing nobody has all the answers.

No long-lived population ate a carbohydrate-free diet; long-term effects unknown

Jun 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 4 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis argues that every long-lived population studied includes carbohydrate from vegetables, fruits, legumes, nuts, seeds, whole grains or fermented dairy, and that the few carbohydrate-free-eating groups, such as certain Eskimo populations, were not notably long-lived. He says low-carb diets may help short-term but long-term heart-disease effects are speculative, so he suggests including low-GI carbohydrates.

Keto beat Mediterranean and low-fat diets on liver fat and glycemia at equal weight loss

Aug 27, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Petersen et al. (Cell Metabolism, 2026) fed 42 adults with obesity, prediabetes and fatty liver all meals in a controlled trial until ~10% weight loss. All diets improved muscle insulin sensitivity and body composition, but the very-low-carb ketogenic diet gave clearly larger improvements in liver metabolic function, intrahepatic fat and glycemic control.

Meta-analysis of 102 feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat gives the best glycemic benefit

Nov 18, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Summarises a systematic review of 102 randomized feeding trials (4,220 participants) on 5% energy swaps. Replacing carbs with MUFA lowered HbA1c 0.09% and HOMA-IR 2.4%; with PUFA lowered HbA1c 0.11% and fasting insulin 1.6 pmol/L. PUFA alone improved insulin secretion capacity; saturated fat swaps did not change fasting glucose. Authors favor replacing refined carbs and saturated fat with unsaturated fats.

100+ trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose and insulin markers, saturated doesn't

Sep 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 6 ↩ 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes a meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials showing polyunsaturated fat, mostly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowers fasting glucose and A1C and improves insulin resistance and insulin secretion. Monounsaturated fat did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit.

CARDIA: low-carb diets in young adults tied to more coronary calcium if animal-based

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 3 · 💬 7 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Posts the abstract of a CARDIA analysis of 2,226 participants (mean age 40.4) followed 8.3 years. Higher carbohydrate intake was inversely associated with CAC progression (HR 0.731); an animal-based low-carb score predicted higher progression (HR 1.456) but a plant-based score did not (HR 1.016). 26.5% had CAC progression.

100+ feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat improves glucose control, saturated fat does not

Dec 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes the 2016 PLoS Medicine meta-analysis of randomized feeding trials: polyunsaturated fat, mainly omega-6 from vegetable oils, lowered fasting glucose and A1C and improved insulin resistance and secretion, MUFA did well but less so, and saturated fat showed no appreciable benefit. He pairs it with the 20-cohort biomarker study showing a 43% lower diabetes risk with high omega-6.

Men and women lose weight similarly with calorie restriction and GLP-1 drugs

May 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Stuart Phillips debunks claims that women need special weight loss strategies. A meta-analysis of 13 long-term RCTs found no sex difference, low-carb reviews similar, and GLP-1 trials showed women losing about 1 kg more.
