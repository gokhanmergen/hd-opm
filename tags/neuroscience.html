APOE4 drives cholesterol buildup in brain astrocytes, promoting toxic protein deposits

Oct 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 4 Brain health & dementia

Mount Sinai researchers using human stem-cell-derived miBrain tissue found APOE4 causes cholesterol to accumulate in astrocytes, impairing lysosomal clearance of alpha-synuclein and promoting amyloid and alpha-synuclein deposits. A second study showed APOE4 damages brain blood vessels through mechanisms unrelated to cholesterol. The poster reads this as cholesterol being potentially causal in APOE4 carriers.

Shin bone loading may speed brain injury recovery via PIEZO1 in mice and pigs

Oct 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

A Nature Neuroscience paper found that compressing the tibia for a few minutes a day after traumatic brain injury improved movement and memory in mice and pigs. Bone cells sensed the force through the PIEZO1 sensor, and turning it off removed the benefit. The poster calls it the most interesting bone study he has read in a long time.

Coffee, not caffeine alone, changes brain connectivity in visual and executive networks

Jul 20, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 7 Brain health & dementia

The shared text summarises a Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience study finding increased functional connectivity in higher visual and executive control networks after coffee but not caffeine alone. It is a small brain-imaging finding with no direct cardiovascular content.

Moderate coffee (3-4 cups/day) linked to thicker cortex and healthier white matter

Sep 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis quotes a Hamburg City Health Study analysis where regular moderate coffee drinkers had thicker cortex and less white matter microstructural alteration, whereas 5 or more cups per day did not differ from under 1 cup. The pattern is U-shaped, and the study is observational.

Senolytic dasatinib plus quercetin caused brain demyelination in a 2026 PNAS study

Mar 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's warning that senolytics are not a magic bullet. A 2026 PNAS study found dasatinib plus quercetin induced demyelination and oligodendrocyte dysfunction without killing cells; Louis adds that old senescent cells still serve functions such as skin barrier integrity.

PNAS: brain network destabilization begins in midlife, tied to insulin resistance and fat

Jun 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a summary of a PNAS study finding that brain network destabilization, a precursor to cognitive decline, begins in midlife and is associated with insulin resistance and elevated blood pressure. The summary argues that visceral fat drives these and that diet and exercise to reduce it may protect the brain.

Amyloid-beta drives excess astrocyte cholesterol and impairs brain waste clearance in mice

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Brain health & dementia

A Nature Neuroscience mouse study found amyloid-beta raises calcium activity in astrocytes, boosting cholesterol production and removing AQP4 water channels, which impairs the glymphatic waste-clearing system. Calming astrocytes, blocking cholesterol synthesis or giving atorvastatin restored clearance and memory in mice. Human trials are needed, but the mechanism may partly explain observational links between statin use and lower dementia risk.
