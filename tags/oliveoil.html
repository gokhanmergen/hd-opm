Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.
