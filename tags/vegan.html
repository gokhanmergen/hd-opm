ESC scientific statement: diet is a primary factor in CVD prevention and treatment

Dec 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 23 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares the European Society of Cardiology/EAPC scientific statement on diet and cardiovascular disease. It concludes that diet is a primary factor, that plant-based whole-food patterns lower risk, and that ultraprocessed food, processed and red meat, salt, sugar and saturated fat raise it.

Esselstyn's plant-based diet is supported only by case series, not high-level evidence

Nov 11, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 22 ↩ 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis argues the Esselstyn diet is probably healthy and likely lowers cardiovascular risk, but that claims for it are overhyped. Esselstyn's published work consists of case reports and case series, which sit low on the evidence hierarchy, so the strong evidence for plant-based diets in general should be allowed to speak for itself.

Louis shares Adventist Health Study results showing pesco-vegetarian diets had the lowest mortality: 18% lower versus non-vegetarians, 15% for lacto-ovo-vegetarians and 3% for vegans. He notes similar patterns in the Harvard cohorts.

Plant-based diets lower CVD risk but may modestly raise hemorrhagic stroke risk

The poster flags that plant-based diets, though linked to lower CVD, may modestly increase hemorrhagic stroke risk. He cautions this is observational and may be confounded, noting lifetime heart attack risk is about 20% versus about 3% for hemorrhagic stroke.

Unhealthy plant-based diets raise CHD risk, healthy ones lower it, in Harvard cohorts

Dec 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis stresses that not all plant-based diets are healthy. In the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study, a plant-based index rich in healthy plant foods was linked to substantially lower CHD risk while one emphasizing sugary drinks, refined grains, potatoes and sweets was linked to higher risk, worse than the standard diet baseline.

Countering low-fat vegan claims that olive oil and nuts cause heart disease, the poster says two large randomized trials, CORDIOPREV and PREDIMED, showed plaque regression in high-risk patients with olive oil and nuts, proven on imaging. Details are in linked group posts.

Vegans with insulin resistance should target liver fat; white potatoes are not health food

Sep 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 17 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis advises vegans with insulin resistance, high blood sugar or high triglycerides that the main cause is excess visceral and liver fat, even in lean people. Adventist study: vegans 85+ had higher neurological death risk than non-vegetarians

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Among AHS-2 participants (about 88,000, over 12,000 deaths), vegans aged 85 and older had higher risk of death from stroke (17%), dementia (13%) and Parkinson's (37%) than healthy non-vegetarians, while pesco-vegetarians had the lowest risk. The comparison was against very healthy non-vegetarians, not the standard American diet.

Optimal diets vary: Mediterranean first, with individualized low-carb or low-fat options

Jun 30, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Gokhan says no scientist can tell you the single best diet. The Mediterranean diet has the strongest evidence for average people, while those with insulin resistance may try low-carb or low-fat vegan diets. Swapping refined carbs for unsaturated fat lowers LDL: meta-analysis of 60 feeding trials

Oct 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A meta-analysis of 60 controlled human feeding trials found that replacing carbohydrate calories with monounsaturated or polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL cholesterol, while saturated fat raises it. The effect looked largest for polyunsaturated fat, which is why choosing oils and nuts over refined starch and sugar helps the lipid profile.
