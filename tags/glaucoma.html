Vitamin B3 (nicotinamide) shows early promise for protecting retinal cells in glaucoma

Nov 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 5 Supplements & vitamins

A shared summary says replenishing NAD+ with vitamin B3 may help retinal ganglion cells keep their energy supply and function longer in glaucoma. A 2020 randomized clinical trial of 57 people with glaucoma is cited as human evidence. Evidence is early and not specific to heart health.

Vitamin B3 users with high eye pressure were about two-thirds less likely to get glaucoma

Jul 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares an observational study where patients with high eye pressure taking nicotinamide were about two-thirds less likely to be diagnosed with open-angle glaucoma. He cites a small 2020 RCT and a larger ongoing trial, with an NAD mechanism.

Nicotinic acid (vitamin B3) may prevent or treat glaucoma, possibly via NAD

Dec 10, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 2 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes growing evidence that nicotinic acid, the niacin form used to lower cholesterol, may prevent or treat glaucoma, a disease with no effective treatment that causes irreversible blindness. The mechanism likely involves NAD. Most studies used nicotinic acid, so it is unclear how nicotinamide, NR or NMN compare. This is an eye-health tangent, supported by a video.

Vitamin B3 forms and NAD: strong evidence in skin cancer, only preliminary in glioblastoma

Jun 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 ↩ 2 Cancer

Louis notes randomized trials show nicotinamide can reduce certain skin cancers, and early niacin trials are testing glioblastoma, with both likely acting through NAD. The shared analysis stresses the brain-cancer result comes from 15 patients with no control group, while the skin-cancer effect is real but modest.

Nicotinamide plus pyruvate improved visual-field function in glaucoma in a phase 2 trial

Oct 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Supplements & vitamins

A randomized Columbia University trial tested high-dose nicotinamide (vitamin B3) with pyruvate in patients being treated for open-angle glaucoma. After about two months the supplement group had significantly more improving visual-field locations than placebo, and Louis notes the mechanism likely involves NAD.

Glaucoma neurorecovery review: stressed retinal neurons may recover; vitamin B3 candidate

Oct 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Supplements & vitamins

A shared post describes a review by Jonathan Crowston's team proposing 'neurorecovery', the idea that retinal nerve cells in glaucoma may stop working before they die and some may bounce back. Louis ties this to vitamin B3 (nicotinamide) as a possible treatment.

Statin use linked to 15% lower risk of open-angle glaucoma at 5 years

Oct 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Statins

A Medscape report says statin use was associated with a 15% lower risk of developing open-angle glaucoma at 5 years and a substantially reduced need for laser or surgery in patients who already had it. Associations were somewhat weaker at 10 years and seen with atorvastatin and rosuvastatin. Observational data.

Statin users had 15% lower glaucoma risk and needed fewer eye procedures

Nov 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Statins

Louis shares a report from the British Journal of Ophthalmology: among over 200,000 statin users and matched controls, statin users were 15% less likely to develop open-angle glaucoma over 5 years and 10% less over a decade. Among nearly 280,000 glaucoma patients, statin users needed fewer laser treatments and surgeries. The mechanism is unclear and the study is observational.

Statin use linked to lower open-angle glaucoma risk in a large UC San Diego database study

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Statins

A retrospective TriNetX cohort study reported in the British Journal of Ophthalmology found statin use was associated with lower open-angle glaucoma incidence and less need for treatment escalation. It is observational, so it shows association and not proof of protection.
