Low LDL in cancer patients reflects reverse causality: tumours scavenge LDL

May 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 12 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that rapidly dividing tumour cells take up circulating LDL, so LDL falls in people with cancer and observational data show low LDL with higher cancer mortality. The shared 2026 Science Advances study found aggressive breast cancers with TP53 mutations depend on cholesterol, and blocking PI5P4K enzymes trapped cholesterol and disrupted mTOR signalling.

Statins after high-risk prostate cancer diagnosis linked to 42% lower cancer death

Dec 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 9 Statins

A shared summary of a cohort study of high-risk prostate cancer patients: post-diagnosis statin use (atorvastatin, pravastatin, rosuvastatin, not lovastatin) lowered prostate cancer mortality 42%, statins plus metformin 54%, and metformin alone showed no benefit.

Statin plus sildenafil may block cancer metastasis in preclinical models

Jul 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 4 Cancer

A 2026 Cancer Research study found sildenafil (a PDE5 inhibitor) caused cholesterol to accumulate in lysosomes of mouse and human cancer models, limiting cholesterol available to cancer cells and impairing their migration and spread. Combining it with a statin, which blocks cholesterol production, gave stronger anti-metastatic effects than either alone. Louis notes the research is preclinical but promising.

Isoleucine and valine fuel prostate cancer by boosting tumor cholesterol

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 9 Cancer

Louis notes that isoleucine, valine and methionine shorten mouse lifespan, and a 2026 Nature Metabolism study links isoleucine and valine to prostate cancer progression. Their breakdown produces propionyl-CoA, which enhances the cancer cells' cholesterol synthesis, transport and storage, supplying lipids for growth.

Statins did not improve cancer outcomes in trials but did not worsen them either

Aug 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 4 Statins

Strong preclinical evidence led to dozens of trials of statins as cancer treatment; they mostly failed to improve outcomes but did not harm. Cohort studies and Mendelian randomization show neutral to protective cancer associations. Louis added an AI fact check.

Statin use linked to lower liver cancer and decompensation in chronic liver disease

May 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 ↩ 3 Statins

JAMA Internal Medicine: statin use associated with lower hepatocellular carcinoma and hepatic decompensation and slower fibrosis progression.

Ezetimibe-type absorption inhibitors linked to 31% lower liver cancer odds; statins 35%

Aug 01, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 3 ↩ 2 Statins

A genetic/drug-target analysis reported by Medical Xpress found cholesterol absorption inhibitors associated with 31% lower odds of liver cancer and statins with 35% lower odds. Fibrates, omega-3 and niacin showed no association, while bile acid sequestrants showed inconsistent results needing replication.

Statins after early breast cancer diagnosis linked to 10% lower breast cancer death

Nov 06, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 3 Cancer

A Medscape-reported Danish registry study found that starting statins within 3 years of early-stage breast cancer diagnosis was associated with a 10% lower risk of breast cancer death and 8% lower all-cause mortality.

Low LDL's link to cancer is reverse causality: cancer lowers cholesterol, not vice versa

Dec 21, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains why observational studies linking low LDL to cancer are not causal. Citing a Circulation review on reverse causality, he notes cholesterol declines before cancer diagnosis (likely from cancer-related inflammation), statin RCTs show no increase in cancer, and Mendelian randomization with an average of 52 years of lifetime exposure shows no higher cancer rates in people with genetically lower cholesterol.

Statin use linked to 35% lower odds of liver cancer in UK database study

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Statins

A UK Clinical Practice Research Datalink case-control study of 3,719 liver cancer cases and 14,876 controls found statin users had 35% lower odds of liver cancer, and cholesterol absorption inhibitors (ezetimibe class) 31% lower odds. This is an association, not proof, and non-statin findings need replication.

Mendelian randomization shows genetically lower LDL does not raise cancer risk

May 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that old observational links between low LDL and cancer are largely reverse causality. MR studies find genetically lowered LDL does not increase overall cancer risk, and genetically higher LDL is linked to more breast and prostate cancer; statin proxies lower colorectal cancer risk.

Statins may help overcome cancer resistance to immunotherapy by blocking PD-L1 release

Mar 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Statins

Reports a newly described mechanism by which cancers shed immunosuppressive PD-L1 on extracellular vesicles, which statins may interfere with. Because statins are cheap and widely used, the finding could translate to the clinic quickly, though this is early mechanistic work.
