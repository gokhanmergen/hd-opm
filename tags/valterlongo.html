Can fasting reverse plaque? Autophagy makes it plausible but evidence is indirect

Jun 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 14 Fasting & meal timing

Louis argues there is biological plausibility that fasting could clear damaged arterial tissue via autophagy, linking earlier posts. He cites the 2010 DIRECT-Carotid trial showing large weight loss can regress carotid atherosclerosis, and a shared post on a Nature Metabolism 7-day fast study in 12 people with a day-3 shift in about 1,000 proteins. Direct evidence for plaque reversal by fasting is anecdotal.

Valter Longo says higher whole-food plant protein likely protects against heart disease

May 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Protein

Louis notes that Longo, in a podcast, accepts the epidemiology showing whole-food plant protein protects the heart. The rationale is getting plant protein from legumes, nuts, seeds and whole grains without the excess of isoleucine, methionine and BCAAs found mainly in animal protein, which shorten lifespan in animal models.

People with Laron syndrome (low IGF1) appear to have low cardiovascular risk

Jun 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis notes that people with genetically low IGF1 (Laron syndrome) have very low rates of cancer, diabetes and Alzheimer's, and a recent study suggests lower cardiovascular risk too. The linked Nature news piece describes lower blood sugar and insulin resistance in these individuals, hinting at new treatment strategies.
