Plaque-stopping supplements rest on small, low-quality studies; gotu kola most plausible

Dec 17, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 29 · 💬 36 ↩ 8 Supplements & vitamins

Louis comments on a video about 7 supplements said to stop artery plaque. He says the studies are small and low quality but promising, and personally finds gotu kola (Centella asiatica) the most plausible for CVD based on mechanism, explained in the comments.

Gotu kola extract reportedly stabilized high-risk plaque in small studies

Nov 17, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 16 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis flags, as needing larger trials, Life Extension's summary that 60 mg of Centella asiatica extract three times daily hardened soft carotid plaque by 30% over 12 months, with fewer cardiovascular events in the randomized phase, and hardened femoral plaque by 63% with no size increase versus 23% in controls. He says it may target endothelial health. Evidence comes from small studies summarized by a supplement-company magazine.

Mendelian randomization: longer telomeres linked to less coronary calcification

Feb 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a January 2026 MR study suggesting longer telomere length causally protects against coronary artery calcification. He ties this to replicative senescence of artery wall cells in atherosclerosis.
