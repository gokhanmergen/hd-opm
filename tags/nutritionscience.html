Study of 48 hours of oatmeal cutting LDL attracts wide attention

Feb 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 16 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis notes the University of Bonn oatmeal trial keeps attracting attention. The shared summary: adults with metabolic syndrome eating about 300 g/day of oatmeal for two days saw about 10% lower LDL than a calorie-restricted control.

RCT: 60 g of pistachios daily lowered HbA1c 0.2% and LDL 10 mg/dL in prediabetes

Mar 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a 12-week RCT in 120 people with prediabetes testing 60 g of pistachios daily (30 g before breakfast and dinner). Pistachios lowered HbA1c, post-meal glucose spikes, LDL, triglycerides and waist circumference, while blood pressure, fasting glucose and insulin did not change.

Chronically high insulin drives aging biology, and calorie restriction lowers it

Aug 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Luigi Fontana explains that insulin is a nutrient-signaling hormone and that persistent activation of the insulin/IGF-1-AKT-mTOR network is linked to cell growth, aging biology and cancer-related pathways. Calorie restriction and weight loss are among the strongest ways to improve insulin sensitivity.

High protein intake linked to MASLD in NHANES; amino acids fuel liver fat synthesis

Dec 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 7 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares a Cell Metabolism study suggesting high-quality protein may drive fatty liver. In NHANES (4,693 participants), carbohydrate and total fat were not associated with MASLD, while medium/high protein intake raised MASLD risk about 1.45-fold and high protein doubled MASH risk; low carbohydrate intake was associated with about 2-fold higher MASH progression. Amino acids serve as carbon sources for liver fatty acid synthesis, and mTOR may play a role.

Choline deficiency can cause fat to accumulate in the liver

Sep 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A shared post explains that choline is needed for phosphatidylcholine, which packages and exports liver fat in lipoproteins. Severely inadequate intake can cause liver fat to accumulate in controlled feeding studies and is reversible with adequate choline. Good sources include eggs, liver, meat, fish and soybeans.

Swapping to a Western diet for weeks raised inflammation in a Tanzanian multiomics trial

Jun 04, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A Nature Medicine study of 77 healthy Tanzanian men tested a switch between a traditional Kilimanjaro diet and a Western diet, plus a Western diet with the fermented banana drink mbege. The Western diet quickly increased weight and inflammatory and cardiometabolic proteins, while the heritage diet and mbege reduced inflammatory markers.

Meta-analysis of 87 trials: whole grains improved LDL, glucose, blood pressure, weight

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A European Heart Journal meta-analysis of 87 randomized trials found greater whole-grain intake improved LDL and total cholesterol, triglycerides, fasting glucose and systolic blood pressure, and reduced body weight, waist and IL-6. Effects were estimated per 48 g/day and were generally largest around 60-100 g/day, though certainty of evidence varied.
