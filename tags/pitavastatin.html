Admin's experience adding ezetimibe, pitavastatin and Repatha after statin intolerance

Personal story: atorvastatin felt bad at any dose; rosuvastatin was uneventful and dropped LDL from ~155 to ~75 mg/dL; ezetimibe added in 2025; the past week he switched to pitavastatin 1 mg plus Repatha and feels good. Japan-ACS: pitavastatin and atorvastatin both regressed coronary plaque by about 17-18%

Sep 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 23 ↩ 21 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summary of the Japan-ACS trial, in which over 300 acute coronary syndrome patients were randomized to pitavastatin or atorvastatin and imaged with IVUS. Both statins produced similar, substantial regression of non-culprit plaque volume as LDL fell into the 80s mg/dL, which is one reason statins remain standard care after ACS.

Admin asks cardiologist about rosuvastatin raising insulin, and pitavastatin plus PCSK9i

Dec 30, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 39 Statins

Personal message to his cardiologist: on rosuvastatin 5 mg plus ezetimibe 10 mg his LDL was 90 then 63 mg/dL, not very different from low-dose rosuvastatin alone. He is concerned that fasting insulin rose roughly 50% on any rosuvastatin dose and wants to explore pitavastatin and a PCSK9 inhibitor. Anecdotal, n-of-1.

Japan-ACS: modest statin doses cut non-culprit plaque volume by about 17-18%

Sep 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 5 Statins

The Japan-ACS randomized trial enrolled over 300 patients with acute coronary syndrome at 33 centers and compared 4 mg pitavastatin with 20 mg atorvastatin. Both produced similar major plaque regression over 8 to 12 months; Louis uses it to rebut claims that statins do nothing.

Switching from rosuvastatin to pitavastatin removed a statin-related A1C rise in one case

Jun 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 19 Statins

Louis comments on a video of a doctor whose A1C rose slightly on rosuvastatin and normalized on pitavastatin. He notes this is a reasonable option for lower-risk people sensitive to glucose effects, but rosuvastatin has stronger large-RCT evidence in high-risk patients, so switching should not be taken lightly.

Which statin to choose: atorvastatin, rosuvastatin and other options by patient type

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Statins

A shared infographic summarizes how statins work and how the choice varies by patient: atorvastatin after MI/ACS and usable in CKD, rosuvastatin as the most potent with few interactions. General reference without study data.

JAPAN-ACS sub-study: statins lowered AGEs, which were linked to plaque progression

Sep 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Statins

A sub-study of the JAPAN-ACS trial found high baseline advanced glycation end products (AGEs) were independently associated with plaque progression, and statin therapy significantly lowered AGE levels. Louis notes the trial produced the largest plaque regression, about -17%, in a drug trial.
