Turmeric liver case report criticised: 2.25 g is a normal intake, no contamination test

Jul 11, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 30 · 💬 19 Supplements & vitamins

Louis criticises a media-hyped case report of liver damage in a woman taking turmeric capsules, noting average daily turmeric intake in India is about 2.5 g and she took 2.25 g. He suggests contamination is the likelier cause and argues anecdotes are not clinical evidence.

Louis notes acetaminophen and ibuprofen misuse can cause serious liver and kidney damage and argues this is a bigger concern than fear-mongering about turmeric or green tea. Shared text is a social media warning anecdote from a hospital setting.
