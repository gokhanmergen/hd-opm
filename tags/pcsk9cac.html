PCSK9 inhibitors regress soft plaque without the calcification surge seen with statins

Jul 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis highlights Gokhan Can's article on imaging evidence that PCSK9 inhibitors shrink plaque without the calcific increase often seen with statins. He cautions that no randomized outcome trials test PCSK9 inhibitors as monotherapy, though adding one to a statin attenuates statin-induced calcification and lowers events more than statins alone.
