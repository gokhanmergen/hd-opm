DISCO-CT: DASH diet plus statins reversed ~40 mm3 of soft plaque in a year on CT

Aug 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 38 · 💬 21 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

In the DISCO-CT randomized trial, a DASH-style diet with statins reduced non-calcified plaque volume by 39.5 mm3 (adjusted to one year from 66 weeks), the largest dietary regression on CT angiography to date. An at-risk person typically grows about 5 mm3 per year, so this equals roughly 8 years of progression. DASH fat was ~30% of calories, mostly unsaturated.

Ornish trial's 'plaque reversal' may be x-ray angiography measurement error

Dec 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 12 ↩ 9 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains criticisms of the small 1986-1992 Ornish trial: it used x-ray angiography, which outlines the blood-flow lumen rather than measuring plaque, so the small reported reversal may largely be measurement error. He contrasts this with modern CT angiography and IVUS, and notes the trial's tiny size as another major weakness.
