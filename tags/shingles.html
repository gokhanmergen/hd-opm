Shingles vaccine linked to 28% fewer heart attack and 42.5% fewer stroke hospitalizations

Aug 29, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 28 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A Kaiser Permanente Southern California cohort matched 102,766 Shingrix recipients to 411,064 unvaccinated patients. Vaccinated people had lower shingles complications and fewer hospitalizations for heart attack and stroke. Gokhan notes the effect sizes are striking and that flu vaccine has similar heart-protective effects.

Shingles vaccination tied to less dementia and fewer deaths in people with dementia

Dec 05, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 22 Infections & vaccines

Short post sharing a Cell paper suggesting shingles vaccination may be a strong tool to prevent or slow dementia: associated with less mild cognitive impairment and dementia in healthy people and fewer deaths in those who already have dementia.

Shingles vaccine tied to slower biological aging and less inflammation in 3,800 elders

Feb 28, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 22 · 💬 11 Infections & vaccines

Reports a large national study of more than 3,800 Americans aged 70+, in which shingles-vaccinated people showed slower biological aging, lower chronic inflammation and slower aging-related changes in gene activity. This suggests the vaccine may calm 'inflammaging', the low-grade inflammation tied to heart disease, frailty and cognitive decline.

Shingles vaccine linked to 46% fewer major cardiac events in heart disease (ACC.26)

Aug 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 6 Infections & vaccines

In 246,822 US adults over 50 with ASCVD, vaccinated patients had 46% fewer major cardiac events and 66% lower mortality at one year. It is observational, not yet peer reviewed, and may reflect healthy-user bias.

BMJ: evidence mounts that COVID-19 disrupts immunity, challenging 'immunity debt' idea

Aug 20, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A BMJ feature reports that the immunity debt theory is increasingly challenged and that SARS-CoV-2 may cause lasting immune disruption. Evidence includes more infections after covid than after flu or RSV, viral reactivation, T-cell exhaustion and persistent inflammation.

Recombinant shingles vaccine linked to fewer cardiovascular events in Nature Medicine

Aug 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 3 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a 2026 Nature Medicine study comparing recombinant (Shingrix) and live attenuated shingles vaccines as a natural experiment. The recombinant vaccine was associated with lower incidence of cardiovascular events, adding to possible Alzheimer's protection.

Flu raised heart attack risk 4-fold and COVID 3-fold in following weeks (155-study review)

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares an AHA review of 155 studies showing influenza and COVID raise heart attack and stroke risk three- to five-fold in the weeks after infection, with lingering viruses also implicated. He suggests people with CVD ask their doctor about temporary baby aspirin during illness, and notes inflammation and clotting as the mechanisms.

Hypothesis: shingles vaccine may protect the brain by suppressing virus reactivation

Aug 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Infections & vaccines

Shared video explains that dormant varicella-zoster virus can reactivate silently with age, causing low-grade inflammation of brain immune cells, and that the vaccine may suppress this. Described as a published hypothesis not yet proven in humans.

Shingles vaccination linked to slower biological aging in over 3,800 older adults

Oct 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 Infections & vaccines

A US Health and Retirement Study cohort analysis of over 3,800 adults aged 70+ found those vaccinated against shingles between 2008 and 2018 had lower inflammation scores, slower genetic aging and lower overall biological aging scores. The poster links this with evidence the vaccine may lower dementia risk, with reduced inflammaging a possible mechanism.

Shingrix linked to 24% lower dementia risk in over 500,000 Medicare recipients

Aug 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares another study linking the shingles vaccine to lower dementia risk, notable because it examined the newer Shingrix rather than Zostavax. Brown University researchers analyzed records of more than 500,000 Medicare recipients aged 66+ and reported 24% lower dementia risk over four years among vaccinated people.

Shingles vaccine linked to 25-47% lower Alzheimer's risk in large observational studies

Aug 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Infections & vaccines

After Wales and Australia signals, a Korean study of 2.5 million adults found ~25% lower Alzheimer's risk and a meta-analysis of 21 studies and 104 million people found 47% lower risk, with caution that these are observational.

BCG vaccine may remodel brain immunity and shift Alzheimer's markers

Jul 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Infections & vaccines

Mass General Brigham research suggests BCG vaccine remodels the brain's immune environment over 12 months, offering an explanation for observed links between vaccines and lower Alzheimer's risk.

Welsh birthday rule natural experiment suggests shingles vaccine protects against dementia

Aug 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Infections & vaccines

Eligibility by birthday produced uptake of 0.01% vs 47.2% between otherwise similar groups, making this close to a randomized comparison. Louis calls it some of the strongest evidence yet for a causal protective effect.
