High LDL does not protect the brain: 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL as top dementia risk

Dec 15, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 38 Brain health & dementia

Louis counters the claim that high blood LDL protects against dementia. The 2024 Lancet Commission lists LDL cholesterol as the biggest modifiable dementia risk factor, accounting for 7% of cases among 14 factors that together account for 45%. He adds circulating cholesterol does not cross the blood-brain barrier; the brain makes its own.

Mendelian randomization in 1 million people: statin-like genetics cut dementia risk 76-82%

Nov 06, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 17 Brain health & dementia

Summarises Dr Stanfield's video on a Mendelian randomization study of over a million people. Short RCTs show no cognitive harm or benefit and observational studies suggest protection; genetic variants mimicking lifelong statin and ezetimibe action were linked to 76-82% relative dementia risk reduction, implying long-term benefit missed by short trials. The video also notes treating hearing loss, vision loss and midlife depression.

CHOICE trial: hearing aids gave nearly six-fold cognitive improvement in MCI

Sep 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

A secondary outcome of the CHOICE randomized trial found cognitive improvement in 15.34% of older adults with mild cognitive impairment given hearing aids versus 2.36% of controls. The primary outcome was null and the authors say the surprisingly large effect needs replication.

Hearing aids before age 70 linked to a 61% lower dementia risk

Aug 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis recommends treating hearing loss with a hearing aid before age 70, citing news of a study of nearly 3,000 participants over up to two decades. Those with hearing loss who used hearing aids had 61% lower dementia risk among people younger than 70. He says multiple studies now show this.

Case report: atorvastatin caused fully reversible hearing loss in a 45-year-old woman

Aug 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 4 Statins

Louis shares a Journal of Medical Case Reports case of atorvastatin-associated sensorineural hearing loss that resolved completely after stopping the drug. She was then switched to rosuvastatin without problems. It is a single rare case and not evidence of common risk.
