What CRP and hs-CRP actually measure, and why a single reading is unreliable

Oct 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 5 Inflammation & CRP

Louis clarifies that CRP and hs-CRP are the same marker, with hs-CRP simply more sensitive for the low levels relevant to heart disease. Both reflect inflammation anywhere in the body, spike temporarily with infections, vaccination, injury, surgery or intense exercise, and fluctuate day to day, so the aim is to capture the quiet background level and not rely on one test.
