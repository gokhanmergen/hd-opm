What cardiologists say about statins: dementia, muscle pain, diabetes, primary prevention

Jan 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 18 Statins

A long point-by-point rebuttal of anti-statin claims. It cites a meta-analysis linking statins to lower dementia risk, the SAMSON trial on nocebo muscle pain, JUPITER's 44% reduction in major events, and a pooled analysis showing about 5 cardiac events prevented per extra diabetes case.

Shingles vaccine linked to 28% fewer heart attack and 42.5% fewer stroke hospitalizations

Aug 29, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 28 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A Kaiser Permanente Southern California cohort matched 102,766 Shingrix recipients to 411,064 unvaccinated patients. Vaccinated people had lower shingles complications and fewer hospitalizations for heart attack and stroke. Gokhan notes the effect sizes are striking and that flu vaccine has similar heart-protective effects.

Pomegranate juice and heart health: CIMT, blood pressure and ACE evidence reviewed

Jan 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 16 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A review of pomegranate juice and cardiovascular disease credits its polyphenols (ellagitannins, punicalagins, anthocyanins). It cites a study where juice for up to a year reduced carotid intima-media thickness by up to 35% in carotid stenosis patients while controls rose 10%, and a meta-analysis of 8 RCTs showing lower blood pressure.

YELLOW-III 'melting butter' sign: lipid core shrinks and fibrous cap thickens in 6 months

Mar 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 11 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares Evan Levine's post and explains that YELLOW-III, using near-infrared imaging, showed a major drop in plaque volume driven by the soft lipid core and a major rise in fibrous cap thickness within 6 months of statin plus PCSK9 inhibitor therapy.

Berberine modestly lowers LDL, triglycerides and ApoB: meta-analysis of 18 RCTs

Sep 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials in adults with dyslipidemia found berberine produced small reductions in LDL cholesterol, triglycerides and apoB, with possible sex-specific effects on HDL. The conclusion calls for larger trials assessing clinical outcomes.

Pelacarsen and ziltivekimab lowered their targets but not events, and why that matters

Nov 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 6 Lp(a)

Louis shares a physician's commentary on the Lp(a) and inflammation trials presented at AHA: one drug lowered Lp(a) nearly 80% and another sharply cut inflammation, yet event rates matched placebo. The lesson is that measuring and changing a marker does not guarantee benefit, and that the underlying risk factors still matter.

Anabolic steroid use linked to 3x heart attack and 9x cardiomyopathy risk

Feb 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 3 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a long-term cohort of 1,189 anabolic androgenic steroid users versus 59,450 matched controls, showing sharply higher rates of cardiovascular events even in recreational gym-goers. He warns against pushing androgen levels excessively high.

Sitosterolemia may be more common than doctors realize, WSJ essay says

Nov 24, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis shares a Wall Street Journal essay about a patient with clogged arteries despite good health, found to have sitosterolemia. People with this condition over-absorb plant sterols from the gut, and high blood sterol levels then cause plaque. The article suggests it may be underdiagnosed.

Louis notes grip strength predicts heart attack risk comparably to blood pressure, but argues this is reverse causality: aging weakens grip, and training grip will not lower heart disease risk.
