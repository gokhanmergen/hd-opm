Low vitamin C reduced fat burning during exercise, and 500 mg fixed it in a small trial

Dec 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 3 Supplements & vitamins

A 2006 trial found young adults with marginal vitamin C status oxidized less fat and felt more fatigue; supplementation with 500 mg resolved it.

Higher adipose linoleic acid linked to lower CVD mortality, countering seed-oil claims

Oct 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A shared critique argues that influencers dismiss research showing higher adipose-tissue linoleic acid (omega-6) is associated with lower cardiovascular and all-cause mortality. It says the contradicting evidence was lower quality and excluded from the meta-analysis for not meeting inclusion criteria.

GLP-1 drugs improved testosterone and sperm quality, probably via weight loss

Jul 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 0 · 💬 1 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Menno Henselmans explains that studies show GLP-1 drugs improve testosterone and sperm measures, but the effect is likely due to fat loss, since fat cells convert testosterone to estrogen via aromatase.
