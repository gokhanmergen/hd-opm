SCOT-HEART: CT angiography cut 5-year coronary death or MI risk 41% in chest pain patients

Jan 03, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 18 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summarises SCOT-HEART (4,146 patients with stable chest pain, standard care vs standard care plus coronary CTA). CTA guidance lowered coronary death or nonfatal MI to 2.3% from 3.9%, mainly by directing aspirin and statin use to patients with plaque. Total procedures were similar by year 5; results apply to chest-pain patients only.

Same CT plaque, lower MI risk in women than men in a 10,003-person Danish cohort

Aug 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

A Danish population study of 10,003 asymptomatic adults over 40 found subclinical coronary plaque less frequent in women at every age, for example 6% vs 20% at age 40-44. After 6 years, obstructive disease carried 4.1-fold higher MI risk in women versus 12-fold in men, though the sex interaction was not statistically significant.

CT angiography plaque burden often predicts events better than stenosis severity

Jun 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis predicts coronary CT angiography will become standard in preventive cardiology as cost and radiation fall. The shared review argues that total plaque volume and non-calcified plaque often outperform stenosis in large cohorts, supporting disease-based rather than risk-factor-based prevention.

A normal BMI does not guarantee health: discordant cardiometabolic profiles

Feb 21, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a Nature Medicine paper on discordant health profiles. Many people with normal BMI have poor lipids, blood pressure or glucose, especially with visceral fat, and metabolic markers such as insulin, glucose, cholesterol, hsCRP and blood pressure predict risk better than BMI, with differences by ethnicity.

Kidney function (eGFR) now an input in the AHA risk calculator for heart attack and stroke

Aug 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Kidney health

Louis notes that kidney disease is a major risk factor for CVD and dementia and that the latest AHA risk calculator now includes eGFR. A shared review describes the kidney's crosstalk with the heart, brain, gut, liver and lungs.

Heart failure in 2026: SGLT2 inhibitors foundational, HFpEF now treatable, early GDMT

Mar 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A Cardiophilia summary of the latest heart failure evidence update lists several shifts: SGLT2 inhibitors are foundational across the ejection fraction spectrum, HFpEF now has options (finerenone, GLP-1 and incretin therapies), guideline therapy starts in-hospital, decongestion becomes guided by urine sodium, and devices are integrated into care.
