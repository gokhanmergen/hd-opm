Waist minus thigh circumference estimates visceral fat better than a DEXA scan

Aug 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 11 ↩ 2 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis explains the video's method: waist circumference reflects abdominal fat and thigh circumference reflects subcutaneous fat, so waist minus thigh is a better visceral fat estimate than a DEXA number, avoiding radiation.

Tape-measure calculator estimates visceral fat, said to rival DEXA

Feb 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis shares the Luxembourg Institute of Health visceral fat calculator, which uses waist and thigh/hip tape measurements. He says it gives a good estimate and a study found it more accurate than a DEXA scan number.

GLP-1 drugs may improve muscle quality; the '40% muscle loss' figure reflects DXA limits

Jul 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 6 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares an analysis arguing the 40% figure from STEP-1 is DXA 'lean mass', which includes water, organs and liver. In SEMALEAN, 12 months of high-dose semaglutide reduced lean mass only about 5%, improved grip strength 4.5 kg, and cut sarcopenic obesity from 49% to 33%.
