Master@Heart: higher wearable-measured training load linked to more coronary plaque

Mar 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 27 ↩ 3 Exercise & fitness

Louis argues intense exercise, especially high intensity plus high volume, carries risks as well as benefits, citing a Circulation 2026 Master@Heart analysis of wearable training load and coronary atherosclerosis in middle-aged and older athletes versus active controls. He notes the net benefit of exercise remains large but the plaque signal can no longer be ignored.

Sauna works the heart like light cardio and lowered blood pressure after one session

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 4 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a summary of sauna physiology. Heat opens skin vessels, so the heart works harder to hold blood pressure, matching light to moderate exercise. One session lowered arterial stiffness and systolic pressure from 137 to 130 in 102 adults with risk factors, and a Finnish cohort of 2,315 men followed about 20 years showed lower mortality with more frequent sauna use.

Extreme endurance exercise may accelerate plaque, but effect on events is uncertain

Jun 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 6 Exercise & fitness

Louis notes that while exercise is clearly beneficial, lifelong high-volume endurance athletes showed more plaque, including non-calcified and mixed plaque, than later starters and lightly active controls. A follow-up suggests plaque tracks with exercise duration more than intensity, and overall mortality still appears lower even at extreme levels.

Mendelian randomization: high VO2max is a health proxy, not a causal driver of longevity

May 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares a 2-sample Mendelian randomization of about 70,000 people: genetic predisposition to high VO2max did not predict longevity, but genetic predisposition to low body fat, high muscle mass and high activity did. The same logic applies to grip strength.

Glycine may be geroprotective and protective against CVD, per a narrative review

Dec 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis quotes a narrative review saying glycine may mitigate risks in acute MI, aortic conditions and endothelial dysfunction. Glycine modestly extends rodent lifespan.
