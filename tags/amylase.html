Swapping refined starch for resistant starch halved liver fat in 4 months in an RCT

Jan 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 24 ↩ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis highlights a randomized trial in which one-for-one replacement of refined starch with resistant starch cut liver fat in half in 4 months. The proposed mechanism is microbiome changes and more short-chain fatty acids.

Acarbose cut liver fat in a 12-week RCT in women with PCOS, unlike metformin or diet

Jan 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A small RCT in women with PCOS found acarbose significantly decreased liver fat, triglycerides and free fatty acids. Metformin and a lower-calorie diet did not. Louis links it to a trial in which swapping refined starch for resistant starch also cut liver fat.
