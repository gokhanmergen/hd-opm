Vegetable intake correlates with slower epigenetic aging, as smoking does with faster

Jun 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Aging & longevity

Louis relays Steve Horvath's point that in postmenopausal women of the Women's Health Initiative, blood carotenoids (a marker of fruit and vegetable intake) correlated with lower GrimAge more strongly than exercise. Horvath changed his own diet to include more vegetables. The finding is a correlation with an aging clock.

Semaglutide-driven visceral fat loss slowed epigenetic aging clocks in a 32-week trial

Jun 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis highlights a FoundMyFitness note that in a 32-week semaglutide trial in adults with excess visceral fat, multiple epigenetic aging clocks showed improvement. The proposed mechanism is less adipose-driven inflammatory and metabolic signaling.
