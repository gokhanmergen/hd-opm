Q-SYMBIO: CoQ10 cut cardiovascular death in heart failure patients

Apr 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 36 · 💬 15 Heart rhythm & heart failure

A shared summary of the Q-SYMBIO RCT (JACC: Heart Failure, 2014; 420 patients with moderate to severe heart failure given 100 mg ubiquinone three times daily for two years vs placebo). Louis states it cut cardiovascular death roughly in half and calls CoQ10 the best-supported supplement for this purpose in heart failure.

Q-SYMBIO: CoQ10 cut major cardiovascular events 15% vs 26% in heart failure

Dec 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 36 · 💬 6 Heart rhythm & heart failure

In the randomized Q-SYMBIO trial of 420 patients with moderate-to-severe heart failure, CoQ10 100 mg three times daily reduced major adverse cardiovascular events over 2 years, though short-term endpoints did not change.
