Strategies for lowering TMAO: diet, supplements and repurposed drugs

Aug 29, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 22 · 💬 18 ↩ 1 Gut microbiome

A written review of how TMAO forms from choline and carnitine via gut bacteria and the liver, and how to lower it. It points to less red meat and egg yolk, a fiber-rich Mediterranean-style diet, polyphenols and allicin, and statins and metformin. Gokhan says TMAO is not his top concern but worth knowing; he mentions a small human berberine trial that lowered TMAO and plaque.

Stuart Phillips: protein past 1.6 g/kg adds little, and longevity gadgets are unproven

Sep 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 16 Protein

Phillips says a bit above the RDA helps, more so if you lift or are older, but above 1.6 g/kg per day extra protein does next to nothing. He adds that after 33 years of human research, wellness products sold for longevity have not been shown to extend life, giving examples such as weighted vests and growth hormone.

Nattokinase looks promising but is unproven and is no substitute for statins

Louis summarizes nattokinase as promising but unproven, quoting a KevinMD article that no one should stop a statin because of it. Large independent RCTs showing fewer heart attacks, strokes and deaths are needed.

Stuart Phillips preprint: muscle benefit of protein still caps at 1.6 g/kg

Oct 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 1 Protein

Phillips posted a new, not peer-reviewed preprint reviewing the evidence and concluding that the muscle-related benefit of protein still tops out at about 1.6 g/kg per day, and only in people doing resistance training. He argues that most trials report lean mass, about half of which is not muscle, and that women's needs are not different from men's.

Growth hormone is not a fountain of youth: lower GH linked to longer life

A shared post citing a new review warns that GH boosting via peptides and supplements is marketed as anti-aging although low-GH states are associated with longer life and slower aging, while acromegaly brings many long-term harms. Louis added only hashtags; the post includes a course advertisement.

Men and women lose weight similarly with calorie restriction and GLP-1 drugs

May 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Stuart Phillips debunks claims that women need special weight loss strategies. A meta-analysis of 13 long-term RCTs found no sex difference, low-carb reviews similar, and GLP-1 trials showed women losing about 1 kg more.

Taurine is not a reliable aging biomarker, says new multi-species study

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A shared summary of a large longitudinal study across humans (26-100 years), monkeys and mice finds taurine levels do not consistently fall with age, often rise (especially in women), and vary more between individuals than with age. The post notes that taurine, like NAD+ boosters and resveratrol, shows promise in animals but human evidence is limited.
