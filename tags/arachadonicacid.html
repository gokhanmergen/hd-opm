PREVENT-E4: 2 g/day DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 365 at-risk older adults

Jun 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis reports the PREVENT-E4 randomized trial found no cognitive or hippocampal-volume difference between DHA and placebo over two years, though it showed 2 g DHA reached spinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. The trial was small and designed mainly to test brain penetration.

Bill Harris: omega-6/omega-3 ratio does not predict heart disease or diabetes

May 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 6 ↩ 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a clip where fatty acid scientist Bill Harris explains the omega-6 to omega-3 ratio does not predict cardiovascular disease or diabetes risk. He notes Harris co-authored two very large pooled biomarker studies (~70,000 and ~40,000 people) showing higher blood omega-6 linked to lower heart disease and diabetes.

30 RCTs: omega-6 does not raise inflammation; higher linoleic acid tracks with less CVD

May 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes a 2017 meta-analysis of 30 RCTs (1,377 participants) finding no effect of higher linoleic acid on inflammatory markers, and a 2019 pooled analysis of 30 cohorts linking higher LA levels to lower CVD risk.

High-dose DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 2-year APOE4 trial, but reached the brain

Jun 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Brain health & dementia

Louis comments on a Medscape report of a trial of high-dose omega-3 in older adults at high dementia risk. It found no improvement in memory, cognition or hippocampal volume, although DHA reached the cerebrospinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. He notes the trial was short at two years, and APOE4 carriers with the highest DHA to arachidonic acid ratio declined less.

Over 30 human studies find omega-6 fats do not raise inflammation markers

May 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis adds another study to over 30 showing omega-6 does not increase inflammatory markers or oxidized LDL in humans, and may reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity. The shared Layne Norton post cites a human trial giving about 30 g/day soybean oil with no increase in inflammation or oxLDL.
