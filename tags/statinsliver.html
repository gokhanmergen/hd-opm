Serious liver problems from statins are rare and routine liver monitoring is unnecessary

Feb 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 17 ↩ 2 Statins

Louis quotes Harvard Health: among statin users about 1 in 1,000 has an abnormal liver test attributable to the drug, and abnormal results are about as common on placebo. A single baseline test is recommended and routine monitoring is not useful for most people.

Statins may benefit chronic liver disease rather than harm it, per a review

Jul 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 12 ↩ 3 Statins

A review of statin use in chronic liver disease and cirrhosis says statins, once feared as liver-harming, may beneficially affect cirrhosis, portal hypertension and liver cancer prevention. The authors stress randomized trials are still needed to confirm this.

Statin use linked to lower liver cancer and decompensation in chronic liver disease

May 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 4 ↩ 3 Statins

JAMA Internal Medicine: statin use associated with lower hepatocellular carcinoma and hepatic decompensation and slower fibrosis progression.

Ezetimibe-type absorption inhibitors linked to 31% lower liver cancer odds; statins 35%

Aug 01, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 3 ↩ 2 Statins

A genetic/drug-target analysis reported by Medical Xpress found cholesterol absorption inhibitors associated with 31% lower odds of liver cancer and statins with 35% lower odds. Fibrates, omega-3 and niacin showed no association, while bile acid sequestrants showed inconsistent results needing replication.

Statin-related liver enzyme elevations are rare (about 1 in 1,000) and usually transient

Apr 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 Statins

Louis explains that lasting liver enzyme elevation from statins affects about 1 in 1,000, short-term rises are usually transient, and newer research suggests statins may protect the liver long term. Quotes Harvard Health that abnormal tests were about as common on placebo.

Statin use linked to lower liver decompensation risk in primary biliary cholangitis

Feb 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Statins

Medscape reports new research that statins were linked to lower hepatic decompensation and major adverse liver outcomes in PBC, adding to evidence of liver benefits. Louis links earlier posts.

Statin use linked to 35% lower odds of liver cancer in UK database study

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Statins

A UK Clinical Practice Research Datalink case-control study of 3,719 liver cancer cases and 14,876 controls found statin users had 35% lower odds of liver cancer, and cholesterol absorption inhibitors (ezetimibe class) 31% lower odds. This is an association, not proof, and non-statin findings need replication.
