Three repurposed drugs for Alzheimer's: shingles vaccine, sildenafil and riluzole

Feb 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 5 Brain health & dementia

A Delphi consensus led by the University of Exeter reviewed 80 existing medicines and ranked a shingles vaccine, sildenafil and riluzole as top candidates, with good safety and mechanistic plausibility.

Statin plus sildenafil may block cancer metastasis in preclinical models

Jul 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 4 Cancer

A 2026 Cancer Research study found sildenafil (a PDE5 inhibitor) caused cholesterol to accumulate in lysosomes of mouse and human cancer models, limiting cholesterol available to cancer cells and impairing their migration and spread. Combining it with a statin, which blocks cholesterol production, gave stronger anti-metastatic effects than either alone. Louis notes the research is preclinical but promising.

Sildenafil users had lower Alzheimer's risk in real-world data and neuron experiments

Mar 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 3 Brain health & dementia

Observational insurance-claims analysis plus patient iPSC-derived neuron experiments suggest sildenafil (a PDE5 inhibitor) is a candidate repurposed drug for Alzheimer's. The authors say randomized trials are needed to confirm causal effects.
