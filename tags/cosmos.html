Cocoa flavanol extract cut CVD death 27% in the 21,000-person COSMOS trial

May 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 53 ↩ 4 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis summarizes the COSMOS randomized trial of 500 mg/day cocoa flavanols (and multivitamin) in over 21,000 adults for 3.6 years. Total cardiovascular events fell 10%, which was not statistically significant, but CVD death fell a significant 27% and major CV events were also significantly reduced. Adherence-adjusted analyses showed 15% fewer total events and 39% fewer CVD deaths; authors urge cautious interpretation.

Daily multivitamin slowed cognitive aging by about two years in a COSMOS meta-analysis

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a summary of COSMOS cognition results: in the main analysis of 573 adults 60+, a daily multivitamin modestly improved overall cognition and episodic memory over two years. Pooling with two earlier COSMOS cognition studies (more than 5,000 older adults) gave clear benefits, estimated at about two years of slower cognitive aging. Effects were modest, it is unclear which nutrient matters, dementia prevention was not tested, and the manufacturer donated tablets. He adds that this was an RDA-level multivitamin (Centrum Silver).

Cocoa flavanols lower blood pressure and cut cardiovascular deaths 27% in COSMOS

Nov 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 3 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a summary of cocoa flavanol evidence. An updated meta-analysis of 25 RCTs showed modest drops in blood pressure and improved flow-mediated dilation.
