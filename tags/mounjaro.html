Tirzepatide beat testosterone therapy for raising testosterone in obese men (pilot, n=83)

Jul 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 1 ↩ 2 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis argues that low testosterone in middle-aged men is mainly driven by visceral fat and should be treated by fat loss rather than TRT. The shared pilot study in 83 men with obesity found tirzepatide plus lifestyle gave the greatest weight and fat loss and raised testosterone, LH and FSH more than lifestyle alone or TRT.

FDA approves tirzepatide for CV risk reduction in type 2 diabetes after SURPASS-CVOT

Aug 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 3 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares that the FDA approved tirzepatide to lower heart attack and stroke risk in type 2 diabetes, based on SURPASS-CVOT. In 13,299 adults with established heart disease, tirzepatide was noninferior to dulaglutide for MACE (12.2% vs 13.1%, HR 0.92), without formally reaching superiority.

Tirzepatide cut major kidney events 23% vs dulaglutide in SURPASS-CVOT post-hoc analysis

May 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 · 💬 2 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares a summary of a post-hoc kidney analysis of SURPASS-CVOT in people with type 2 diabetes and heart disease. Tirzepatide gave greater kidney protection than dulaglutide, supporting the kidney-protective role of GLP-1-based therapies.
