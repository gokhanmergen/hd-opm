Paricalcitol plus chemotherapy raised partial response to 42% vs 9% in pancreatic cancer

May 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 3 ↩ 2 Cancer

Louis summarizes a small Dana-Farber randomized trial (Nature Cancer) of a synthetic vitamin D analog added to gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel in 36 patients. Tumour vitamin D receptor levels tracked with longest survival. Small and early, so not definitive.

Paricalcitol plus chemo safe in 36 pancreatic cancer patients; reduced tumor fibroblasts

Jun 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 2 Cancer

Louis points to a Nature Cancer study from Dana-Farber in which the vitamin D analog paricalcitol was combined with chemotherapy in 36 patients with untreated metastatic pancreatic cancer. Biopsies showed reduced fibroblast activation and more T-cell infiltration; the trial was not designed to prove survival benefit and it is not about vitamin D supplements.

A shared news item on a 36-patient Dana-Farber trial where paricalcitol plus chemotherapy opened the protective barrier around metastatic pancreatic tumours. Small trial, so results are preliminary.

Vitamin D receptor agonist plus chemotherapy proved safe in pancreatic cancer trial
