Neutrophil vitamin C saturates near 200 mg twice daily; infection raises the need

May 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 Supplements & vitamins

William Wallace's shared explanation: neutrophils use vitamin C as an internal antioxidant pool to survive the respiratory burst they use to kill microbes. In healthy people neutrophil levels saturate at around 200 mg twice per day, and the dose needed to saturate them rises during infections.

CORDIOPREV: Mediterranean diet lowered neutrophil counts, a marker tied to carotid plaque

Feb 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 6 Inflammation & CRP

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a CORDIOPREV analysis in the European Heart Journal. Higher neutrophil counts and ratios were linked to thicker carotid walls and plaques, and the Mediterranean diet lowered neutrophils over 5-7 years, especially in those whose carotid IMT regressed.

Calprotectin predicts ASCVD independent of traditional risk factors (JAMA Cardiology)

Aug 04, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Inflammation & CRP

Louis shares a JAMA Cardiology finding that calprotectin may be a mechanistically informed biomarker of atherosclerotic disease. It rises with arterial inflammation and adds risk information beyond standard factors.

How to interpret a complete blood count (CBC): a stepwise clinical approach

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Risk assessment & lab tests

A shared infographic explains how to read a CBC in order: hemoglobin, MCV, MCH/MCHC, RDW, white cells with differential, then platelets, using the 'RWP' mnemonic. It covers what low or high values may point to, such as anemia, infection or platelet abnormalities. It is a general clinical reference rather than heart-specific content.
