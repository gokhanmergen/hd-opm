FDA approves tirzepatide for CV risk reduction in type 2 diabetes after SURPASS-CVOT

Aug 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 3 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares that the FDA approved tirzepatide to lower heart attack and stroke risk in type 2 diabetes, based on SURPASS-CVOT. In 13,299 adults with established heart disease, tirzepatide was noninferior to dulaglutide for MACE (12.2% vs 13.1%, HR 0.92), without formally reaching superiority.

GLP-1 drugs raised testosterone; normal levels rose from 67% to 86% of men with obesity

May 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Hormones, women's & men's health

Karl Nadolsky shares a real-world study where incretin-based drugs in men with obesity produced modest weight loss, better glycemic control and higher total and free testosterone, most in those who lost the most weight. Louis argues GLP-1s treat the root cause (visceral fat) of low testosterone, unlike TRT.
