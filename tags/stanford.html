Stanford finds BHB-amino acids, a new keto metabolite family that suppresses appetite

Nov 25, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 13 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Stanford researchers led by Jonathan Long discovered a third ketosis pathway in which enzymes attach beta-hydroxybutyrate to amino acids, forming BHB-amino acids that suppress appetite and promote weight loss. It may explain reduced hunger during fasting or keto diets; heart relevance is indirect.
