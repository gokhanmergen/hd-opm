Evidence is building that GLP-1 drugs may slow aging in mammals

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 7 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis comments that evidence is accumulating that GLP-1 agonists slow aging in mammals, in line with calorie restriction extending lifespan in animals. The shared investigation covers the semaglutide 3.1-year biological age claim and a 2026 Nature study of longer-lived old female mice.

Semaglutide extended lifespan 12% in elderly female mice, hinting GLP-1 slows aging

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 3 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

A Nature study found elderly female mice given semaglutide lived 12 percent longer than controls, regained curiosity and memory, and improved across hallmarks of aging. Louis notes the main mechanism is almost certainly calorie restriction. Researchers suggest slowed aging could explain GLP-1's benefits across heart, kidney, liver and brain conditions.

Semaglutide's 3.1-year biological age reversal rests on one DNA clock

Nov 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares a podcast summary examining whether semaglutide slows aging. The 2025 claim of a 3.1-year biological age reversal came from a single DNA aging clock over 32 weeks scaled up. LEADER, SELECT and FLOW showed real reductions in heart events and deaths, evoke trials showed no Alzheimer's slowing, and old female mice lived about 12% longer.
