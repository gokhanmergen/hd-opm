Menopausal hormone therapy is fairly safe for low-risk women, risky with established CVD

Apr 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 11 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares Cleveland Clinic guidance on hormone therapy and heart disease. HRT can be safe in healthy women under 60 or within 10 years of menopause, but risk rises when started over 10 years after menopause, and women with established CVD (10-year ASCVD risk over 10%) or breast cancer should avoid it. Low risk applies to recent menopause, normal weight and BP, active, and 10-year ASCVD risk under 5%.

Israeli HRT study's 749% higher cancer risk at ages 50-65 is implausible, says oncologist

Mar 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 7 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares Dr. Brian Lawenda's critique of an observational Israeli study of over 83,000 women. Reported risk increases were larger in women starting HRT at 50-65 than after 65, which contradicts physiology and prior evidence, so he calls it a red flag and warns against overriding better evidence.

Hormone therapy started after 65 linked to higher cancer and stroke risk in Israeli cohort

Mar 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 6 Hormones, women's & men's health

Medscape reports an Israeli study of over 80,000 women (2000-2022) where hormone therapy was linked to several cancers, with some cardiovascular benefit when started at 50-65 but higher stroke and cancer risk, and significantly increased cancer and cerebrovascular risk when started at 65 or older. It is observational.

Plasma p-tau217 predicts dementia: 3x per SD, 7x top vs bottom quartile in 2,766 women

Apr 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 6 Brain health & dementia

A cohort of 2,766 women followed up to 25 years found each SD rise in p-tau217 tripled dementia risk and the top quartile had 7 times the risk of the bottom, with stronger effects in women over 70 and APOE4/4 carriers.

Pre- and postmenopausal women gain strength equally from resistance training

Apr 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 2 Exercise & fitness

A 2026 JSAMS meta-analysis of randomized trials found women gain strength at similar rates across the lifespan, suggesting estrogen loss at menopause does not blunt response to resistance training. Louis adds that HRT alone does not add muscle.

Hormone therapy and dementia: benefit mostly vanished in naturally menopausal women

Sep 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Hormones, women's & men's health

A shared Barbell Medicine critique of a study of about 183,000 women says hormone therapy users had around 10% lower dementia diagnosis, but the effect was mostly absent in women with natural menopause and only held in those with surgical ovary removal. A meta-analysis of ten studies in over a million women found no reliable effect either way.

HRT is not recommended for women with a history of breast cancer, even bioidentical

Aug 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis says HRT can be reasonably safe for many women but, based on RCTs and cohorts, not for those with breast cancer history. A 2021 review found HRT significantly increased recurrence, especially in hormone receptor-positive disease.

Hormone therapy has no proven dementia benefit and may add risk in APOE4 carriers with MCI

Mar 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares a post by Hussein Yassine on hormone therapy, brain health and APOE4. There is no strong randomized evidence that HRT reduces dementia risk; benefits are best supported for bone protection and menopausal symptoms, and APOE4 carriers who already have MCI or dementia may face specific risks.
