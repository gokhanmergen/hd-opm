Meta-analysis of 155 studies: flu, shingles and COVID raise heart attack and stroke risk

Oct 29, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 8 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a report on a meta-analysis of 155 studies finding that common viral infections raise heart attack and stroke risk, both soon after infection and long term. Flu carried up to six times higher heart attack risk the month after infection, and COVID-19 nearly doubled risk.

Flu and respiratory infections raise heart attack risk; ask your doctor about baby aspirin

Sep 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 8 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares MedPage Today coverage of another study confirming that infections like flu temporarily raise heart attack and stroke risk. He suggests that people with CVD ask their doctor about daily baby aspirin during illness and for a period afterward. The proposed mechanism is increased metabolic demand plus inflammatory and coagulation effects that destabilize plaque.

High-dose flu vaccine linked to lower Alzheimer's risk than standard dose

Apr 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 6 Infections & vaccines

A UTHealth Houston study in Neurology found older adults who received the high-dose influenza vaccine had a lower risk of Alzheimer's dementia than those given standard dose, adding to evidence that some vaccines such as shingles lower dementia risk.

Flu raised heart attack risk 4-fold and COVID 3-fold in following weeks (155-study review)

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares an AHA review of 155 studies showing influenza and COVID raise heart attack and stroke risk three- to five-fold in the weeks after infection, with lingering viruses also implicated. He suggests people with CVD ask their doctor about temporary baby aspirin during illness, and notes inflammation and clotting as the mechanisms.

Infections temporarily raise heart attack and stroke risk; vaccines may do so far less

Oct 14, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Infections & vaccines

Louis explains that infections such as flu raise short-term heart attack and stroke risk through inflammation and coagulation effects that destabilize plaque, and that the effect fades in a week or two. Vaccines can in theory do this to a much smaller degree. He suggests asking a doctor about a baby aspirin during and shortly after infection, and avoiding hs-CRP tests during that window.

Flu vaccine halves the heart attack and stroke spike after influenza, even if infected

Apr 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Infections & vaccines

A study reports influenza raises stroke risk about threefold and heart attack about fivefold, but vaccination cut that increase roughly in half even in people who still got infected.

BCG vaccine may remodel brain immunity and shift Alzheimer's markers

Jul 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Infections & vaccines

Mass General Brigham research suggests BCG vaccine remodels the brain's immune environment over 12 months, offering an explanation for observed links between vaccines and lower Alzheimer's risk.

COVID, flu and RSV infections can destabilize people with heart conditions

Dec 06, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a short cath-lab note that respiratory viruses can provoke inflammation and raise the chance of acute coronary syndromes or decompensated heart failure in people with heart disease, recommending vaccination, early symptom recognition and treatment adherence.
