Ezetimibe beyond statins: fewer events, no mortality gain in trials, registry benefits

Nov 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Evidence digest on ezetimibe. A meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (123,785 patients) found add-on lipid lowering cut cardiovascular events 19% versus statins alone but did not improve all-cause mortality. Other data show moderate statin plus ezetimibe matches high-intensity statins, and registry studies hint at lower mortality, with strong LDL-target attainment.

2025 ACC statement: inflammation is actionable in CVD, with universal hsCRP screening

It contrasts this with a more cautious 2002 CDC/AHA workshop.

Bempedoic acid cut CV events and may also dampen age-related inflammation

Louis shares Gokhan Can's summary of bempedoic acid in 4,206 statin-intolerant primary prevention patients: the primary composite fell 30%, MI 39%, and all-cause mortality 27%. It lowers LDL by 21.3% and hsCRP by 21.5%, and inhibits ACLY, which drives senescence inflammation in aged mice.

JACC review: rapid plaque progression precedes rupture and LDL below 70 mg/dL halts it

Jan 02, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 19 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Summarises a 2019 JACC review arguing plaque progression is a necessary, modifiable step between subclinical atherosclerosis and MI, and that preventive strategy should shift toward imaging and aggressively treating subclinical disease. It reports data from PROSPECT, PARADIGM, GLAGOV and PESA showing progression stops at LDL <70 mg/dL and regression needs lower.

High Lp(a) raises CVD death risk 16% per 50 mg/dL, independent of other risk factors

May 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 22 Lp(a)

Louis argues Lp(a) alone raises heart disease mortality even in people with no other risk factors. Citing a Current Opinion in Lipidology review, he notes each 50 mg/dL increase corresponds to a hazard ratio of 1.16 for CVD mortality, and works an example: Lp(a) of 250 mg/dL would imply roughly an 80% higher risk, taking a 20% lifetime risk to about 36%. The linear extrapolation is his own illustration.

Cumulative LDL-C thresholds: endothelial damage above 4000, plaque from 6000 mg-year/dL

A synthesis of three studies showing cumulative LDL exposure predicts atherosclerosis better than one reading. Statins work as well in women as men: 27-trial CTT meta-analysis of 174,000 participants

Dec 19, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 11 Statins

Louis rebuts the claim that statins are less effective in women using the Cholesterol Treatment Trialists' meta-analysis of 27 randomized trials. Per 1 mmol/L LDL reduction, proportional reductions in major vascular events and all-cause mortality were similar in women and men, with no adverse effect on cancer or non-CV mortality. Trials averaged 4.9 years, so longer-term benefit is likely larger.

PCSK9 inhibitor plus statin nearly halted coronary calcium progression versus statin alone

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 4 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summary of alirocumab 75 mg biweekly added to moderate statins in 61 CAD patients over 18 months: median CAC +2.9% vs +10.6%, with CAC not rising significantly from baseline. A retrospective study (Ikegami, n=120) found about half the annual calcium progression. Mechanism is thought to be stronger LDL and Lp(a) lowering.

MeMeMe trial: metformin cut diabetes 80-92% in metabolic syndrome; Med diet did not

In 1,442 people with metabolic syndrome followed about 3 years, 1,700 mg/day metformin prevented type 2 diabetes, which fully explained the difference in major noncommunicable diseases. The Mediterranean diet intervention did not. Gokhan notes no heart disease or cancer prevention from metformin either.

VESALIUS-CV: Repatha cut first heart attack or stroke 25% in high-risk primary prevention

One zoledronate infusion cut vertebral fractures about 44% over 10 years in women 50-60

REACT: silent atherosclerosis found in 57% of 16,808 adults, 7-9% of 18-29-year-olds

Sep 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

The NEJM REACT study used carotid and femoral ultrasound plus coronary CT angiography in 16,808 adults aged 18-70 without known disease. Plaque was present in 57% overall, appeared in young adults, and a zero calcium score often missed early non-calcified plaque. Ten-year risk scores detected very little silent disease, so keeping risk factors favorable throughout life is the priority.
