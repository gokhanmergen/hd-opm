NEJM 2020 dairy review: a mixed bag, best for fermented forms, weak for fractures

Jan 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 21 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis highlights the widely cited 2020 NEJM review on dairy and disease risk. It concludes that high dairy intake is not supported for preventing fractures, is not clearly linked to weight, diabetes or CVD, and may raise prostate and endometrial cancer risk while lowering colorectal cancer risk. Effects depend on the comparison food: dairy fares better than processed red meat or sugary drinks but worse than nuts.

Regular coffee drinkers have about 18% lower cancer risk, video says

Oct 06, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares a video on coffee and cancer, stating that regular coffee drinkers have around 18% lower risk of any cancer and that heavy coffee drinkers have around 50% lower risk of liver or endometrial cancer.

Early-onset cancer incidence doubled since 1992 but mortality is stable: overdiagnosis

Nov 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Cancer

Louis shares a JAMA Internal Medicine analysis finding that rising cancer rates in people under 50 largely reflect more diagnostic scrutiny, because deaths have not risen. Colon and endometrial cancers are exceptions, with small genuine increases in death rates of under 1 per 100,000.
