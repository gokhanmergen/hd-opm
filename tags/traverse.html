Testosterone raised soft plaque growth by 41 mm3 in a 1-year RCT of older men

Nov 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 8 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis covers a 2017 randomized trial by Matthew Budoff using CT angiography. Testosterone users had much more noncalcified plaque growth than placebo, compared with a typical ~5 mm3/year; TRT is beneficial only in true hypogonadism.

Testosterone therapy accelerated soft plaque growth by 41 mm3 in older men in a 2017 RCT

Aug 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 13 ↩ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis summarizes a 2017 RCT by Matthew Budoff in which older men on testosterone had about 41 mm3 greater soft plaque (NCPV) growth in a year versus placebo, equal to roughly 8 years of typical progression. He notes TRAVERSE (2023) found no increase in heart attacks or strokes with treated hypogonadism over a few years.
