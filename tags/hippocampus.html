PREVENT-E4: 2 g/day DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 365 at-risk older adults

Jun 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis reports the PREVENT-E4 randomized trial found no cognitive or hippocampal-volume difference between DHA and placebo over two years, though it showed 2 g DHA reached spinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. The trial was small and designed mainly to test brain penetration.

High-dose DHA did not slow cognitive decline in 2-year APOE4 trial, but reached the brain

Jun 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Brain health & dementia

Louis comments on a Medscape report of a trial of high-dose omega-3 in older adults at high dementia risk. It found no improvement in memory, cognition or hippocampal volume, although DHA reached the cerebrospinal fluid equally in APOE4 carriers and non-carriers. He notes the trial was short at two years, and APOE4 carriers with the highest DHA to arachidonic acid ratio declined less.

Daily peanuts for 16 weeks improved brain insulin responsiveness in older adults

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A Maastricht University trial in healthy adults aged 60 to 75 gave 60 g of unsalted skin-roasted peanuts daily for 16 weeks. Functional brain imaging showed enhanced insulin responsiveness in memory regions including the hippocampus and parahippocampal gyrus. The shared post says more research is needed.

Green Mediterranean diet with green tea and mankai slowed brain atrophy in people over 50

Oct 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

In an 18-month randomized trial of nearly 300 participants, a standard healthy diet, a Mediterranean diet, and a Green Mediterranean diet with green tea and mankai (duckweed) were compared on hippocampal occupancy, a marker of brain aging. Louis highlights that Mediterranean diets slowed brain aging in people over 50. Linked paper is the DIRECT-PLUS Green-MED brain atrophy analysis.
