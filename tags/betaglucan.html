Oat beta-glucan (4 g) cut breakfast glucose peaks 28% and lunch response 24% in small RCT

May 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 30 · 💬 50 ↩ 2 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis says slow-cooked steel-cut oats are among the heart-healthiest foods and rejects influencer claims that oats are 'the same as eating sugar'. The shared post summarizes a small RCT in 19 healthy adults where oat beta-glucan blunted glucose and insulin responses and carried over to the next meal. About 3/4 to 1 cup of oats supplies ~4 g beta-glucan.

Oats raise blood sugar only about 34 mg/dL on average in a Levels analysis

Feb 21, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 28 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares a post countering fear of oats, noting a Levels glucose-monitor analysis found a mild-to-moderate spike after a bowl of oats and that beta-glucan fiber stabilizes blood sugar. He adds the spike is even lower with slow-cook steel-cut oats.

Oat beta-glucan may help clear PFAS and lowers LDL about 5-7% via bile acid binding

May 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 2 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

FoundMyFitness shares that beta-glucan from oats and mushrooms reduces bile acid reabsorption, which in animal studies increases fecal PFAS excretion and also underlies LDL lowering of about 5-7% at roughly 3 g/day of beta-glucan. PFAS clearance in humans is a proposed mechanism.

Only gel-forming fibers such as psyllium and beta-glucan lower LDL

Nov 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 3 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares a video explaining that fibers lower LDL by forming a gel that traps bile acids, forcing the liver to pull LDL from blood. Psyllium, oat and barley beta-glucan and guar gum gel, while inulin, FOS and wheat bran do not. Psyllium lowered LDL about 13 mg/dL across 28 trials and about 9 mg/dL in a 2025 analysis of 41 trials.

Soluble vs insoluble fiber is an oversimplification

Jul 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 1 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared explainer says fiber varies independently in solubility, viscosity, fermentability and structure. Cellulose is insoluble, non-viscous, barely fermented; beta-glucan is soluble and highly viscous.
