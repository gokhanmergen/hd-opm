ACE inhibitors cut all-cause mortality 10% in RCT meta-analysis; ARBs showed none

Apr 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 28 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

A summary of the evidence on ACE inhibitors versus ARBs for all-cause mortality. RCT meta-analyses favor ACE inhibitors, while observational and head-to-head data are mixed or neutral, so the question is not settled. ARBs are better tolerated (less cough and angioedema).

ARBs, thiazides and DHP calcium blockers tied to fewer dementia markers than ACE drugs

Feb 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 3 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

An autopsy cohort found blood pressure drugs that stimulate angiotensin II signaling were linked to lower Alzheimer's biomarkers and less brain small-vessel disease than those that inhibit it.

Network meta-analysis of 716 trials: ARB-based regimens had fewest BP drug dropouts

Jun 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares the JAMA network meta-analysis of 716 short-term randomized trials comparing discontinuation due to adverse events across blood pressure drug classes. ARB-containing regimens, especially ARB plus calcium channel blocker, had fewer discontinuations than placebo, while CCB monotherapy had more.

Losartan may stimulate brown fat, preclinical Rockefeller study suggests

Oct 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Rockefeller researchers report that the blood pressure drug losartan, an angiotensin receptor blocker, may stimulate brown fat. Brown fat is linked to better insulin sensitivity and lower diabetes and cardiovascular risk. The link between ARBs and brown fat was first found in an analysis of over 100,000 patients, and this study is preclinical.

ARBs, alone or with a calcium channel blocker, are best-tolerated BP drugs in 700+ trials

Jun 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 1 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis summarizes the JAMA network meta-analysis showing ARBs have fewer treatment discontinuations from side effects than even placebo. ARB plus CCB was the best-tolerated regimen, ARBs appeared in four of the top five, and CCB monotherapy was worse, suggesting ARB-based regimens may improve long-term adherence.
