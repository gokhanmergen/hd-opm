PCSK9 gene-editing therapy may not be safe yet; patient deaths occurred in a trial

Mar 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 24 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis warns that experimental PCSK9 gene therapies for lowering LDL may be unsafe because people have died in trials, and notes that safe, effective drugs already exist, including PCSK9 inhibitors. He clarifies that the deaths were specific to the gene therapy in that trial, with unclear cause, and were not seen in long PCSK9 inhibitor drug trials.

FGF21 gene therapy protects multiple organs in aged mice

Aug 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Aging & longevity

Louis links this mouse gene therapy study to FGF21, the gene Dudley Lamming ties to benefits of isoleucine restriction, which extended male mouse lifespan 33%. The company is developing it for fatty liver disease.
