Hunter-gatherers have LDL of 50-70 mg/dL and no atherosclerosis, per a review

Aug 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 20 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

The post counters carnivore/keto claims by citing a review stating that native hunter-gatherers, healthy neonates, free-living primates and wild mammals have LDL of 50-70 mg/dL and do not develop atherosclerosis. Per the review, RCTs show events minimized when LDL is below 70 mg/dL with no major safety concerns in that range.

The Hadza eat about 70% plants and have LDL around 62 mg/dL, not a carnivore diet

Feb 24, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 8 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis rebuts carnivore influencers' claims about the Hadza hunter-gatherers. Their diets are well studied: about 70% of calories from plants (tubers, berries, baobab) plus honey and lean game meat, making them low fat, moderate protein and high in natural sugar and starch. Their LDL is very low (62 mg/dL), heart disease risk is low, and their gut microbiome reflects a high-fiber plant diet.
