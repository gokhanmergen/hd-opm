Statins activate AMPK via mild mitochondrial stress, which Louis argues is beneficial

Nov 23, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 12 ↩ 5 Statins

Louis argues statins do not harmfully impair mitochondria; instead, like metformin, they mildly stress mitochondria and activate the longevity-linked AMPK pathway (mitohormesis), possibly contributing to cardiovascular benefit. He cites a 2006 Circulation study showing statins activate AMPK in vitro and in vivo, leading to endothelial NO synthase activation. The extension to lifespan and mitohormesis is his interpretation.

Caffeine activates AMPK in yeast cells, which may explain coffee's longevity links

Jun 26, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 4 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A Queen Mary University of London study in Microbial Cell found that caffeine acts via AMPK, the cellular fuel gauge, influencing growth, DNA repair and stress response. It was done in fission yeast, so human relevance is unproven.
