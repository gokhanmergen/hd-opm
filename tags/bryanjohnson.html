A zero CAC score does not rule out blockages: 54% of 23,759 symptomatic patients had CAC 0

Jan 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 37 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis challenges the myth that a CAC of zero means no risk. In the Western Denmark Heart Registry of 23,759 symptomatic patients undergoing CT angiography, 54% had CAC zero, and half of people with obstructive blockages have zero CAC. Soft plaque can grow without calcification, so CAC shows late-stage disease and is poor for early detection.

Commercial blood microplastics tests mostly measure contamination, says Louis

Dec 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 20 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

Louis argues that the microplastics blood test sold by Bryan Johnson measures plastic contamination from blood draw, transport and processing. Single-digit particle counts in a 100 uL sample require a cleanroom and plastic-minimized lab, which no commercial lab offers.

Chronic growth hormone elevation is dangerous, as Bryan Johnson's CJC-1295 test showed

May 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis explains that the effects Bryan Johnson reported after the peptide CJC-1295 are textbook growth hormone responses: GH counter-regulates insulin, raising blood glucose and causing insulin resistance while shifting the body to fat burning.

Blood microplastics reflect recent diet and inhalation, so a blood test is not useful

Dec 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

Louis cites the group that first measured microplastics in human blood (2022): the main entry routes are diet/gut and inhalation, plus surgery. Even a contamination-free measurement would only reflect what was recently eaten, drunk or inhaled, so he calls the Bryan Johnson test a scam.
