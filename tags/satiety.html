Oat beta-glucan (4 g) cut breakfast glucose peaks 28% and lunch response 24% in small RCT

May 14, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 30 · 💬 50 ↩ 2 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis says slow-cooked steel-cut oats are among the heart-healthiest foods and rejects influencer claims that oats are 'the same as eating sugar'. The shared post summarizes a small RCT in 19 healthy adults where oat beta-glucan blunted glucose and insulin responses and carried over to the next meal. About 3/4 to 1 cup of oats supplies ~4 g beta-glucan.

Whey shake did not curb buffet intake versus a calorie-free drink, challenging satiety

Nov 01, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Protein

Louis, sharing Menno Henselmans, argues protein per se is not more satiating than fat or carbs. A study by Oberoi et al. found a 30 g whey shake did not reduce buffet intake more than a calorie-free drink, and a meta-analysis in older adults found extra protein increased energy intake. Louis says meat satiates because of muscle fiber structure and fat.

Gut microbes turn fiber and polyphenols into GLP-1 and other satiety hormones

Jan 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 Gut microbiome

Louis quotes Scientific American: the body makes its own incretin hormones such as GLP-1 and PYY, and gut bacteria ferment indigestible fiber and polyphenols into molecules that stimulate them. These hormones help regulate blood sugar, appetite and the speed of food moving through the gut (the 'colonic brake'). This suggests whole plant foods support the same pathways targeted by Ozempic and Wegovy.
