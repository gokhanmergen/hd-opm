Evidence that plaque can shrink: CENIT, PRECISE-IVUS, ZEUS, EVAPORATE and GLAGOV

Jun 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 72 · 💬 42 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Post by group member Daniel Chong summarising trials showing plaque change: supervised HIIT in CENIT, ezetimibe in PRECISE-IVUS, ezetimibe added to statin in ZEUS, icosapent ethyl in EVAPORATE and evolocumab in GLAGOV. It notes some research suggests a 1% reduction in plaque volume may equate to a 20% reduction in event risk.

The longer the lower the better: lifelong LDL lowering and an estimated 60% risk reduction

Nov 20, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 21 ↩ 2 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Argues that the benefit of LDL lowering depends on both how much and how long LDL is reduced. The author, one of the group's admins, is betting on very low-dose statins to hold LDL at 70-100 mg/dL, and estimates about 60% lifetime risk reduction from a 70-point lowering sustained for 30 years. The post also cites Charles Grashow as a case of CAC regression.

Natto: spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase in one food, with mortality and plaque data

Dec 09, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 25 · 💬 12 ↩ 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Summarizes natto as a food rich in spermidine, vitamin K2 and nattokinase. It cites a Frontiers clinical study where high-dose nattokinase slowed carotid plaque progression, an association with lower all-cause mortality, and mouse lifespan data for spermidine.

Nattokinase and PAI-1: how it may work and why PAI-1 matters

Dec 03, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 16 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Notes that nattokinase appears to act by inhibiting PAI-1, a marker linking thrombosis, inflammation, metabolic disorder and aging. People who genetically make less PAI-1 live about 7 years longer, and PAI-1 inhibition helped fatty liver and dyslipidemia in preclinical work.

Nattokinase looks promising but is unproven and is no substitute for statins

Aug 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 9 Supplements & vitamins

Louis summarizes nattokinase as promising but unproven, quoting a KevinMD article that no one should stop a statin because of it. Large independent RCTs showing fewer heart attacks, strokes and deaths are needed.

Charles Grashow's protocol to regress coronary calcium over two years

Dec 02, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 7 ↩ 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Describes how Charles Grashow responded to worsening coronary calcium in 2020-2021 with an intensive regimen of lifestyle, high-dose nattokinase, LDL-lowering and anti-inflammatory supplements and off-label drugs. His later scans reportedly showed that the trajectory of his coronary disease had reversed. It is a single-patient story, not a trial.

Nattokinase 10,800 FU/day improved carotid plaque and lipids; 3,600 FU/day did not

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 7 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Quotes a clinical study of 1,062 participants in which nattokinase at 10,800 FU/day managed atherosclerosis and hyperlipidemia, shrinking carotid intima-media thickness and plaque size. The lower dose of 3,600 FU/day was ineffective.

Shared caution from Joel Kahn that the hype around nattokinase is undeserved and patients adding it to other blood thinners risk bleeding.
