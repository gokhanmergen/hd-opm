LDL particle size does not predict CVD risk once particle number or ApoB is accounted for

Jan 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 18 ↩ 3 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that LDL particle size is not associated with CVD risk after adjusting for total LDL particle number. A large particle size will not protect someone with high LDL or ApoB. Particle size can still reflect insulin sensitivity, but more accurate direct tests exist.

NMR LDL particle size adds no risk information once LDL-P or ApoB is known

Apr 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 5 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains NMR test reports themselves state small LDL size is associated with CVD risk but not after LDL-P is taken into account, so most cardiologists no longer order size testing.

NMR reports state LDL particle size adds no risk information once LDL-P is accounted for

Jul 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 ↩ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

The post points out that NMR lipid reports themselves say small LDL-P and LDL size are associated with CVD risk but not after total LDL particle number is taken into account. ApoB is an equivalent measure of particle number, so large particles do not protect against high LDL-P or ApoB.

NMR lipid reports state LDL size adds no risk information once LDL-P is known

Aug 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 6 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis points out that NMR cholesterol reports themselves say small LDL-P and LDL size are associated with CVD risk but not after total LDL particle number is accounted for, and that ApoB is an equivalent particle count. He calls people claiming large size excuses high ApoB charlatans.
