Super-oxidized fish oil fed for 7 weeks caused no harm in human trial

Sep 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

The poster shares a Bill Harris clip arguing oxidized fish oil is likely not harmful. In the trial described, researchers deliberately super-oxidized fish oil and gave it to participants for 7 weeks, finding no difference in oxidation, peroxidation, inflammation, glucose, lipids or liver enzymes versus non-oxidized fish oil.

How LDL causes atherosclerosis: oxidation, inflammation, foam cells and genetics

May 19, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 9 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Explains five lines of evidence for a causal role of LDL in atherosclerosis: oxidized LDL in the artery wall, endothelial dysfunction and inflammation, macrophage foam-cell formation, Mendelian randomization and familial hypercholesterolemia, and the benefit of LDL-lowering therapies. Useful as a mechanism overview for readers who doubt that LDL is a cause rather than a bystander.

Human trials: omega-6 and oxidized fish oil do not raise oxidation or inflammation markers

Sep 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 4 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

The post states that human randomized trials have repeatedly shown omega-6 fats do not cause oxidation or lipid peroxidation, and that oxidized fish oil does not raise inflammation, oxidation or peroxidation markers or worsen cardiometabolic or liver health. It shares a saturated fat versus omega-6 breakdown by Dr Alan Flanagan.
