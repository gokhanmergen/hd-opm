Nov 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 11 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a ScienceAlert report on Baylor research showing metformin acts on the brain (VMH, Rap1). He adds that Rap1 is a known longevity pathway that protects telomeres.

Metformin acts in the brain's hypothalamus via Rap1 to lower blood sugar in mice

Nov 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 7 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Baylor researchers found metformin activates SF1 neurons in the ventromedial hypothalamus by turning off Rap1. Mice lacking Rap1 did not get the glucose benefit, indicating a brain pathway in addition to liver and gut effects.

Metformin's glucose lowering is regulated by Rap1 in brain neurons, mouse study finds

Aug 03, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 15 · 💬 1 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A Baylor College of Medicine study in Science Advances found metformin acts largely through the protein Rap1 in SF1 neurons of the hypothalamus. Mice lacking Rap1 in the brain did not respond, and a tiny dose given into the brain lowered glucose. This is preclinical mouse research.
