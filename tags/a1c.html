Jul 31, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 38 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis argues a lower A1C on low-carb or keto does not prove diabetes is cured, because liver and pancreas fat may persist. Only passing an OGTT is definitive, and he advises scepticism about unverified cure claims.

CGM glucose estimates ran 0.6% above lab A1C in people without diabetes

May 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 50 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis highlights a study finding that the CGM-derived glucose management indicator (GMI) overestimates lab HbA1c in people without diabetes. A 0.6% gap is large enough to turn a normal A1C of 5.1% into an apparent prediabetic 5.7%, so CGM-based A1C estimates should not replace lab tests in healthy people.

U-shaped mortality curves for cholesterol, BMI, A1C and BP reflect reverse causality

Jun 12, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 24 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

A post explaining that U-shaped hazard curves in observational data appear for cholesterol, BMI, A1C and blood pressure alike, and are explained by reverse causality rather than benefit of high values. Illness lowers LDL, BMI, A1C or blood pressure, creating false appearances of protection.

CGMs are a poor guide because liver fat drives insulin resistance

May 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 21 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis says liver fat is the true cause of insulin resistance and that CGM-guided avoidance of whole grains and fruit is misleading. He reports his A1C of 4.7 with liver fat under 1%.

U-shaped mortality curves for cholesterol, BMI, A1C and BP reflect reverse causality

May 11, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 12 How to read research

Louis explains why observational studies show higher mortality at both low and high LDL or total cholesterol: the same U-curves appear for BMI, A1C and blood pressure, and illnesses like cancer lower these values. RCTs and Mendelian randomization show lower LDL causes lower mortality, so high LDL does not protect.

Twice-weekly resistance training reversed prediabetes in a third of adults in 12 weeks

Jun 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 6 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares the 2017 Resist Diabetes randomized trial in sedentary adults aged 50-69 with prediabetes. Two days a week of machine-based, low-to-moderate-weight resistance training returned over a third of participants to normal blood sugar within 12 weeks.

Very low-carb diets rarely push A1C below 5 because of physiological insulin resistance

May 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis argues that in people who already have low A1C, severe carb restriction makes muscles temporarily insulin resistant to spare glucose for heart and brain, so blood sugar can rise rather than fall. This 'adaptive glucose sparing' or 'pseudo-diabetes' also explains why people on long-term keto often fail glucose tolerance tests. Personal observation and physiology reasoning rather than a cited trial.

Insulin resistance precedes abnormal A1C by years; fasting insulin and OGTT catch it first
