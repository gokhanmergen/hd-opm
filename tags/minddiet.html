APOE4 carriers eating unprocessed meat or fish had lower dementia risk in Swedish study

Mar 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 22 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis summarizes a widely shared observational study: APOE 3/4 and 4/4 carriers eating unprocessed meat or fish had lower dementia risk and possibly slower progression, with fish and poultry equal to red meat. No benefit was seen in non-APOE4 carriers, and processed meat raised dementia risk in everyone.

Healthy lifestyle tied to less dementia despite amyloid and tau in the brain (n=586)

Louis shares an observational study of 586 people whose brains were examined at autopsy and linked to up to 24 years of lifestyle data. A healthy lifestyle (not smoking, 150+ min/week exercise, light to moderate alcohol, mental stimulation and MIND diet) was associated with protection from cognitive decline even when amyloid or tau pathology was present. MIND diet: 20% less grey matter loss per 3-point score rise in Framingham MRI study

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 Brain health & dementia

In 1,647 Framingham Offspring participants followed about 12 years with repeated MRI scans, closer adherence to the MIND diet (Mediterranean-DASH hybrid) was linked to slower grey matter loss. Each 3-point rise in MIND score matched 20% less age-related grey matter decline, equal to about 2.5 years of slower brain aging. This is an observational association.
