ESC scientific statement: diet is a primary factor in CVD prevention and treatment

Dec 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 23 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares the European Society of Cardiology/EAPC scientific statement on diet and cardiovascular disease. It concludes that diet is a primary factor, that plant-based whole-food patterns lower risk, and that ultraprocessed food, processed and red meat, salt, sugar and saturated fat raise it.

Adventists: pesco-vegetarians had 18% lower mortality, vegans only 3%

Oct 28, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 12 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares Adventist Health Study results showing pesco-vegetarian diets had the lowest mortality: 18% lower versus non-vegetarians, 15% for lacto-ovo-vegetarians and 3% for vegans. He notes similar patterns in the Harvard cohorts.

Adventist Health Study-2: pesco-vegetarians had lowest mortality; vegans only 3% lower

Sep 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 17 ↩ 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes the Adventist Health Study-2 cohort, where all participants eat a relatively healthy diet, allowing head-to-head comparison of healthy patterns. Pesco-vegetarians had 18% lower all-cause mortality than non-vegetarians, beating lacto-ovo vegetarians and vegans.

Defending a lean, nut-and-olive-oil vegetarian diet against 'junk food' labels

Jul 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 12 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis argues a diet heavy in nuts, avocado, unsaturated oils, some cheese and seafood, and low in refined starch, sugar and saturated fat is healthy. He says unsaturated fat improves insulin sensitivity and lowers LDL, and that his example's good lipids reflect leanness, whereas a person avoiding all fat but staying overweight needs to lose visceral fat first.

Adventist study: vegans 85+ had higher neurological death risk than non-vegetarians

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 8 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Among AHS-2 participants (about 88,000, over 12,000 deaths), vegans aged 85 and older had higher risk of death from stroke (17%), dementia (13%) and Parkinson's (37%) than healthy non-vegetarians, while pesco-vegetarians had the lowest risk. The comparison was against very healthy non-vegetarians, not the standard American diet.
