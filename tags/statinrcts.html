Statin absolute risk reduction is small over years but can exceed 10% over a lifetime

Nov 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 25 ↩ 2 Statins

Louis explains that short statin trials show only 1-2% absolute risk reduction because baseline risk over a few years is only 2-5%, the same as diet and exercise trials. Over a lifetime, with a 20% baseline risk of heart attack, cohort and Mendelian randomization data suggest cumulative reductions of 10% or more.

Each ~40 mg/dL LDL drop cuts heart attack and stroke risk about 20% (relative)

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains the rule of thumb from dozens of randomized trials that every roughly 40 mg/dL LDL reduction lowers relative risk of heart attack and stroke by about 20%. He shows how to convert to absolute risk: a 20% 10-year risk becomes 16% after a 40 mg/dL drop. People with prior events get more absolute benefit, hence LDL goals under 55 or even 40.
