90-120 min/week of strength training linked to 13% lower mortality, 19% fewer CVD deaths

Jul 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 3 Exercise & fitness

Louis summarizes new results from the Harvard Nurses' Health Studies and Health Professionals Follow-up Study, following about 147,000 people for 30 years. Roughly 90-120 minutes a week of strength training was tied to lower all-cause, cardiovascular and neurological mortality, with the largest gains when combined with plenty of aerobic exercise.

Combining aerobic and resistance training improves blood lipids more than either alone

Aug 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares a systematic review summary showing that combined aerobic plus resistance exercise improves total cholesterol, LDL and triglycerides more than either type of training alone.
