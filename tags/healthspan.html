Bempedoic acid cut CV events and may also dampen age-related inflammation

Jul 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 14 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis shares Gokhan Can's summary of bempedoic acid in 4,206 statin-intolerant primary prevention patients: the primary composite fell 30%, MI 39%, and all-cause mortality 27%. It lowers LDL by 21.3% and hsCRP by 21.5%, and inhibits ACLY, which drives senescence inflammation in aged mice.

Alpha-ketoglutarate (AKG): longevity data in worms and mice, plus anti-thrombotic effects

Mar 02, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 13 · 💬 13 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes the case for the supplement AKG, which extended lifespan in worms by about 50% and healthspan in aged mice, apparently by mildly suppressing mitochondrial function and mimicking calorie restriction. The linked EBioMedicine paper suggests AKG inhibits thrombosis and inflammation. The human evidence for cardiovascular benefit is limited and the author notes he personally uses it.

Mendelian randomization: higher ApoB shortens healthspan and may raise Alzheimer's risk

Feb 25, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 25 · 💬 4 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Summarizes Martin et al. 2024, a Mendelian randomization study finding that genetically higher ApoB shortens healthspan (time to first chronic disease), with a direct effect independent of LDL. ApoB, but not LDL, also appeared to raise Alzheimer's risk, though the replication attenuated in a larger sample.

Late-life folate restriction reduced biosynthesis without harming healthspan in mice

Aug 08, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 16 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Mice switched at 52 weeks to a folate- and choline-free diet held their weight, avoided anemia, and showed reduced protein synthesis pathways over 68 weeks. Gokhan stresses the work is in yeast, worms and mice, not humans.

Spermidine linked to lower mortality and cognitive decline, and longer lifespan in animals

Mar 16, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 4 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes studies on spermidine, an autophagy-inducing polyamine: higher dietary intake is associated with lower all-cause mortality and cognitive decline in humans, and it extends lifespan in animal models. Natto, wheat germ, mushrooms, soy products and aged cheese are rich sources, and supplementation appears well tolerated in older adults.

No risk factors at 50 meant up to 13.3 more CVD-free years in a 2M-person NEJM analysis

Apr 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 1 Cardiovascular disease: the big picture

Luigi Fontana's summary of a NEJM pooled analysis of over 2 million people in 39 countries on blood pressure, non-HDL cholesterol, BMI, diabetes and smoking. At age 50, no risk factors versus all five meant up to 13.3 more CVD-free years and lower lifetime CVD risk; modifying risk factors at 55-60 still helped. Only about half of CVD events are attributable to the five classic factors.

Review: calorie restriction has downsides, incl. slower wound healing and bone loss

Apr 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Aging & longevity

Louis shares a Nature Reviews Endocrinology review of potential adverse effects of calorie restriction, which extends lifespan in many animals but has uncertain effects in humans. It summarizes effects on wound healing, hunger, cold sensitivity, bone health, brain size, cognition, reproduction and infection, mostly from rodent data with some human data.

LDL lowering is essential but cardiometabolic health is bigger than LDL, says Fontana

Mar 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 1 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Luigi Fontana, reacting to a 2026 JAMA editorial on lipid lowering, argues that statins, ezetimibe and PCSK9 inhibitors now drive LDL very low, but many patients still have visceral fat, insulin resistance, fatty liver and inflammation. Louis agrees; lowering apoB remains essential but does not fix the underlying metabolic disease.

Reducing B-cell insulin signaling extended mouse lifespan about 36%

Feb 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis cites a Science Immunology paper (Khan et al. 2026) showing that with aging B cells become insulin-responsive and drive chronic T cell overstimulation. He argues avoiding insulin resistance and high insulin is key to lowering risk for age-related diseases like CVD. Mouse data.
