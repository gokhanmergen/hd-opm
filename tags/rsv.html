BCG vaccine may remodel brain immunity and shift Alzheimer's markers

Jul 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Infections & vaccines

Mass General Brigham research suggests BCG vaccine remodels the brain's immune environment over 12 months, offering an explanation for observed links between vaccines and lower Alzheimer's risk.

RSV vaccine Arexvy associated with 25-30% lower dementia risk in observational data

Apr 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Infections & vaccines

A shared summary says Arexvy vaccination was associated with roughly 25-30% lower dementia risk within about 18 months and Shingrix with about 17% lower, with the shared AS01 adjuvant hypothesized as the reason. Observational data only.

COVID, flu and RSV infections can destabilize people with heart conditions

Dec 06, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a short cath-lab note that respiratory viruses can provoke inflammation and raise the chance of acute coronary syndromes or decompensated heart failure in people with heart disease, recommending vaccination, early symptom recognition and treatment adherence. No specific study is cited.
