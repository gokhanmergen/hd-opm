Swapping ghee for rapeseed (canola) oil cut liver fat and insulin resistance in an RCT

Sep 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 21 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis highlights a 12-week randomized trial in 110 people with NAFLD who replaced ghee with the same amount of rapeseed (canola) oil. Liver steatosis, liver enzymes, lipids, glucose and insulin resistance improved versus controls, countering the idea that canola oil causes metabolic disease.

Swapping ghee for rapeseed (canola) oil cut liver fat in an RCT of 110 NAFLD patients

Aug 12, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 14 ↩ 5 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A 12-week randomized trial in 110 patients with NAFLD found that replacing ghee with rapeseed oil significantly reduced liver steatosis versus control, and improved liver enzymes, lipids, insulin resistance and weight. The post uses it against claims that canola oil is toxic.

Swapping saturated for polyunsaturated 'seed oil' fats lowered liver fat in an RCT

Dec 18, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 13 ↩ 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis highlights a secondary analysis of a 12-week RCT in NAFLD. Higher dietary and plasma polyunsaturated fat, especially n-6, was associated with larger liver fat loss, while higher saturated fat intake or plasma saturated fat was associated with less reduction in liver stiffness and liver fat. He says multiple well-done studies show this.

Swapping ghee for rapeseed oil cleared liver fat in NAFLD without cutting calories

Apr 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A March 2024 RCT replaced ghee/butter with an equal number of calories of canola (rapeseed) oil in NAFLD patients; liver fat fell and nearly all glycemic and insulin resistance measures improved. Louis notes over 100 RCTs show replacing saturated with unsaturated fat improves glucose, A1C and HOMA-IR.

Scoping review of about 500 studies finds seed oil concerns lack scientific foundation

May 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a 2026 review in Critical Reviews in Food Science and Nutrition covering RCTs, cohorts, biomarker and Mendelian randomization data on seed oils and linoleic acid. It found no inflammatory effect of linoleic acid and supports replacing saturated with polyunsaturated fat for CVD.
