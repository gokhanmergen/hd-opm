High-dose omega-3 raises AFib risk in high-CVD-risk people, not at low doses (2026)

Sep 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 12 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis warns that the atrial fibrillation risk from high-dose omega-3 is real. A 2021 meta-analysis of 7 RCTs (81,210 people) found higher AF risk, and a 2026 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs (114,592 people) confirmed it at higher doses in people at high CVD risk, but not in those without high risk, and not at low doses in either group.

Exercise lowers cardiac risk, though extreme endurance exercise ties to AFib

Jun 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 18 Exercise & fitness

A cardiologist quote shared from a sports medicine institute: exercise generally lowers cardiac event risk, while extreme long-duration exercise such as marathons or triathlons is associated with atrial fibrillation. Even so, exercise still benefits blood pressure, cholesterol and diabetes risk and lowers overall death risk.

Rapid heartbeat is most often AFib, but coronary blockage is another common cause

Mar 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 13 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Cleveland Clinic researchers analyzed blood samples from over 5,000 people undergoing heart procedures and found TMAO, made by gut bacteria from nutrients in red meat and other animal products, was linked to higher AFib risk.
