Q-SYMBIO: CoQ10 cut cardiovascular death in heart failure patients

Apr 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 36 · 💬 15 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Selenium plus CoQ10 trial: over 50% lower CV death, benefit persisted 10-12 years

Dec 18, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 12 Supplements & vitamins

Louis reshares a post quoting Life Extension on a trial where older people took selenium plus coenzyme Q10 for four years and had more than half the cardiovascular mortality. Follow-up showed 49% lower CV death at 10 years and 41% at 12 years, including in subgroups with heart disease, diabetes and hypertension. He suggests adding about 200 ug selenium to CoQ10. The source is a supplement-company magazine and the trial is not named.

Pooled blinded trials: statins cause only about 1 in 15 reported muscle aches

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 12 Statins

A pooled analysis of 19 blinded trials with 123,940 people found that over about four years, 27.1 of 100 on a statin reported muscle pain or weakness versus 26.6 of 100 on placebo. This implies that the vast majority of muscle complaints on statins are not caused by the drug, consistent with a nocebo effect.

CoQ10 curbed Alzheimer's-like damage spread in mice; human CoQ10 trials disappointed

Nov 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 3 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a mouse study (Nature Aging) in which mitochondria-derived vesicles from Alzheimer's-model brains impaired memory in healthy mice and daily CoQ10 injections cut their release. Human CoQ10 trials in Alzheimer's were mostly disappointing, but it might still work as early prevention. Louis stresses mouse studies reveal mechanisms, not proof of benefit in humans.

CoQ10 rejuvenated aging platelet-producing cells in mice via COPS3 and autophagy

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 Supplements & vitamins

A mouse study in Aging Cell found dietary CoQ10 restored function of aged megakaryocytes, the bone marrow cells that make platelets, through a COPS3-autophagy pathway. Aging makes platelets stickier and more clot-prone, raising heart attack and stroke risk. The poster calls it promising but it is animal-only.
