Arterial and breast-implant calcification share an inflammatory damage response

Jan 12, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 10 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis uses breast-implant calcification as an analogy for arterial calcification. In both, an inflammatory damage response to foreign, dying or aging tissue ends with calcium deposition that walls off the area.

Pelacarsen and ziltivekimab lowered their targets but not events, and why that matters

Nov 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 6 Lp(a)

Louis shares a physician's commentary on the Lp(a) and inflammation trials presented at AHA: one drug lowered Lp(a) nearly 80% and another sharply cut inflammation, yet event rates matched placebo. The lesson is that measuring and changing a marker does not guarantee benefit, and that the underlying risk factors still matter.

Whole-blood NAD+ does not change with age or lifestyle, so it is a poor aging marker

May 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Aging & longevity

Louis, echoing Charles Brenner, shares a Nature Metabolism paper reporting that human whole-blood NAD+ is stable across age and not altered by exercise or lifestyle interventions. Nicotinamide riboside raised NAD+, but that does not prove clinical or longevity benefit; tissue NAD matters.

Blood tests and AI scan analysis are improving early detection of pancreatic cancer

Aug 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 Cancer

Shared video describes two approaches to earlier pancreatic cancer detection: AI analysis of CT and MRI scans, and a blood test combining two novel proteins with CA19-9 that is said to detect 90% of cases. A group member reported having had the blood test because of family history.

Honest scientists use words like 'may' and 'could': uncertainty is part of science

Sep 15, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 · 💬 1 How to read research

A short shared video clip notes that credible scientists such as Walter Willett rarely sound 100% certain, and that hedged language reflects testing and revising. It promotes a podcast episode and carries little concrete content.
