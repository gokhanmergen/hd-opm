Replacing butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality in 221,054 US adults

Mar 06, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 68 ↩ 6 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes a Harvard/Brigham cohort analysis of the Nurses' Health Study, HPFS and NHS II covering over 30 years. Replacing 10 g/day of butter with plant oils linked to 17% lower mortality

Oct 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 19 · 💬 13 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares Harvard research on 221,054 people from the Nurses' Health Studies I and II and the Health Professionals Follow-Up Study, published in JAMA Internal Medicine. Higher plant-oil intake (especially soybean, canola, olive) was linked to lower total, cancer and cardiovascular mortality, while butter was linked to higher total and cancer mortality; a modeled swap of 10 g butter for plant oils lowered mortality by 17%.

Higher blood linoleic acid predicts lower CVD risk in 68,000-person pooled analysis

Jul 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 7 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Johns Hopkins piece citing the 2019 Marklund pooled analysis of over 68,000 participants in 30 cohorts across 13 countries. People with the highest blood linoleic acid had lower CVD risk, especially for CV mortality and stroke; controlled trials also show LA lowers LDL and may improve glucose metabolism. The association is observational, which the author notes.

Olive oil has the strongest trial evidence, but canola and soybean oil also have support

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon). Lyon also tested canola oil with olive oil and saw the largest total mortality reduction in a cardiology prevention trial, and the Indo-Mediterranean study (about 1,000 people) tested soybean oil. Harvard cohorts link canola or soybean oil in place of butter to lower cancer, CV and total mortality.
