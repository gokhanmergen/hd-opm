Pooled blinded trials: statins cause only about 1 in 15 reported muscle aches

Oct 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 12 Statins

A pooled analysis of 19 blinded trials with 123,940 people found that over about four years, 27.1 of 100 on a statin reported muscle pain or weakness versus 26.6 of 100 on placebo. This implies that the vast majority of muscle complaints on statins are not caused by the drug, consistent with a nocebo effect.

No risk factors at 50 meant up to 13.3 more CVD-free years in a 2M-person NEJM analysis

Apr 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 1 Cardiovascular disease: the big picture

Luigi Fontana's summary of a NEJM pooled analysis of over 2 million people in 39 countries on blood pressure, non-HDL cholesterol, BMI, diabetes and smoking. At age 50, no risk factors versus all five meant up to 13.3 more CVD-free years and lower lifetime CVD risk; modifying risk factors at 55-60 still helped. Only about half of CVD events are attributable to the five classic factors.

Nuts, seeds and corn did not raise diverticulitis risk in nearly 30,000 women

May 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a large prospective cohort study in women showing that eating nuts, seeds, corn or seeded fruit did not increase diverticulitis risk, while healthy diet patterns (DASH, Mediterranean-style) were linked to lower risk. He notes his mother with diverticulosis was told to avoid nuts for years. Peripheral to heart health but relevant to nut-eating advice.

Low-insulinemic and Planetary Health diets tied to less weight gain at menopause

Jun 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares results from the Harvard Nurses' Health Study published in JAMA Network Open. Among over 38,000 women followed 12 years around menopause, a low-insulinemic dietary pattern and the Planetary Health Diet were most associated with less weight gain and lower obesity risk.

Cardiovascular and cognitive health share drivers: inflammation, insulin resistance, more

Jun 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a JACC piece arguing that cognitive impairment and CVD share the same biological drivers: chronic inflammation, insulin resistance, hypertension, dyslipidemia, oxidative stress, endothelial dysfunction and atherosclerosis. It suggests lifestyle strategies such as diet, exercise, stress reduction and sleep can address both.

JACC PREVENT analysis: nearly 3 in 10 US adults have CVD or elevated 10-year risk

Jun 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Risk assessment & lab tests

A shared summary of a JACC study applying the AHA PREVENT equations to US adults aged 30 to 79, showing high burden of existing or predicted cardiovascular disease, higher risk in men, Black and Hispanic adults, and an elevated 30-year risk in about two-thirds of middle-aged adults without CVD.
