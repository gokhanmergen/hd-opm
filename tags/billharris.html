Harris and Patrick: omega-6 to omega-3 ratio does not predict heart disease or diabetes

Dec 05, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 11 ↩ 6 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a FoundMyFitness clip with Bill Harris and Rhonda Patrick explaining that the omega-6:omega-3 ratio is not useful for predicting heart disease or diabetes. Harris co-authored the two large biomarker studies (about 70,000 and 40,000 people) showing higher blood omega-6 linked to lower heart disease and diabetes. They agree that avoiding nuts over unfounded ratio claims makes no sense.

Super-oxidized fish oil fed for 7 weeks caused no harm in human trial

Sep 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 16 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

The poster shares a Bill Harris clip arguing oxidized fish oil is likely not harmful. In the trial described, researchers deliberately super-oxidized fish oil and gave it to participants for 7 weeks, finding no difference in oxidation, peroxidation, inflammation, glucose, lipids or liver enzymes versus non-oxidized fish oil.
