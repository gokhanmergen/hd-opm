Excess calories shut down autophagy, one reason calorie restriction extends lifespan

Aug 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Fasting & meal timing

Louis states that overeating shuts down autophagy (cellular self-recycling), a main reason calorie restriction extends lifespan in animals including mammals. A shared item describes autophagy as triggered by fasting or restriction.

Duke researchers report pancreatic alpha cells make GLP-1, the hormone mimicked by Ozempic

Sep 26, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 5 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

A shared news item says Duke University scientists found pancreatic alpha cells, long thought to produce only glucagon, also generate substantial amounts of GLP-1. The finding suggests the body makes its own version of the hormone targeted by semaglutide drugs.
