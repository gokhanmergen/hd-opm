Nick Norwitz's eight statements on ApoB, keto, LMHR and the J-shaped curve

Jul 23, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 5 ↩ 8 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

The poster paraphrases the final hour of a Nick Norwitz interview: ApoB is causal and necessary for atherosclerosis but not alone sufficient; lowering it lowers risk; the J-shaped LDL mortality curve disappears after correcting for confounding and reverse causality. In LMHR, a distinct physiology may change absolute risk and the benefit of lowering ApoB is still unknown.

Keto influencer with LDL near 500 mg/dL discovers isoleucine restriction (Lamming work)

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 6 ↩ 3 Protein

Louis comments on a Nick Norwitz video in which a ketovore diet, whose LDL reached 500 mg/dL, is paired with isoleucine restriction. He explains Dudley Lamming's research showing isoleucine restriction extends HET3 mouse lifespan 33% and promotes weight loss, noting animal protein is high in isoleucine and whole plant protein is naturally low. The attachment mentions human data on protein restriction raising energy expenditure via FGF21 (Nature Metabolism).

Protein leverage hypothesis: eating more on low protein does not mean weight gain

Mar 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 · 💬 1 Protein

Louis argues that even if low protein makes people eat more, human and mouse data suggest it does not cause weight gain. Protein-restricted mice ate more yet were leaner and lived longer, and a small human feeding RCT in metabolic syndrome showed similar benefits.
