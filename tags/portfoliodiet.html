Canadian studies: Portfolio Diet linked to lower CVD risk and mortality in diverse groups

Jun 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 · 💬 6 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares news of two Canadian studies suggesting the plant-based Portfolio Diet, built from cholesterol-lowering foods such as nuts, seeds and plant oils, reduces CVD and mortality in more diverse populations than earlier work with mostly older, White participants. No numbers are given.
