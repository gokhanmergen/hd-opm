Erythritol and xylitol heart risk is unclear; the Cleveland Clinic studies were limited

Oct 27, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 5 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis notes it is unclear whether sugar alcohols raise CVD risk. A shared post explains that the group linking higher blood erythritol and xylitol to more heart attacks and strokes also showed that 30 g doses made platelets more reactive, but both studies had serious problems, including that the association study did not measure intake.

Xylitol linked to 57% higher heart attack and stroke risk in cohorts presented at ESC

Aug 31, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A Fox News story reports that people with the highest blood xylitol levels had a 57% higher risk of cardiovascular events across 17,700 participants in two cohorts, with xylitol also possibly enhancing platelet clotting. Louis added only a hashtag; this duplicates an earlier xylitol post.

High blood xylitol linked to 57% higher MACE risk in observational cohorts (ESC 2026)

Sep 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A study presented at ESC Congress 2026 analyzed 17,710 participants from CLSA and EPIC-Norfolk. Highest-quartile xylitol levels were associated with 57% higher MACE risk over six years in CLSA and 18% higher over 30 years in EPIC-Norfolk, with a dose-response pattern. It is observational and cannot show xylitol causes events.
