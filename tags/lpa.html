Lp(a) makes clots form more easily and resist breakdown via OxPL and apo(a)

Nov 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Lp(a)

Louis shares a summary of an ATVB commentary by Rafael Zubiran explaining why high Lp(a) may be worse than LDL.
