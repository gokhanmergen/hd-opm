Louis shares Layne Norton's summary of a 2026 Lipids in Health and Disease study in people with hyperlipidemia. Higher fiber intake was linked to lower all-cause and cardiovascular mortality with a dose-response, consistent with a broader evidence base across over 17 million subjects.

People with Laron syndrome (low IGF1) appear to have low cardiovascular risk

Jun 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis notes that people with genetically low IGF1 (Laron syndrome) have very low rates of cancer, diabetes and Alzheimer's, and a recent study suggests lower cardiovascular risk too. The linked Nature news piece describes lower blood sugar and insulin resistance in these individuals, hinting at new treatment strategies.

Mediterranean diet linked to higher mitochondrial microproteins humanin and SHMOOSE

Mar 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A USC study reported by Medical Xpress found that people closely following a Mediterranean diet had higher levels of the mitochondrial microproteins humanin and SHMOOSE, both linked to protection against cardiovascular disease and neurodegeneration. Foods like olive oil, fish and legumes may influence aging at a cellular level.

JACC review: guidelines favor whole-food plant-based diets for CVD and cancer prevention

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis quotes a September 2025 JACC: CardioOncology state-of-the-art review noting that AHA, ACC, ACS, NCCN and WCRF/AICR guidelines all recommend a predominantly whole-food plant-based diet, as does the EAT-Lancet consensus. Patterns like Mediterranean and DASH are included, emphasizing fruits, vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, seeds and olive oil while minimizing red meat.

Nearly 1 in 4 young adults has high blood pressure, raising later dementia risk

Louis quotes a clip (Rhonda Patrick) noting that nearly 1 in 4 young adults has hypertension and that earlier, cumulative exposure to high blood pressure impairs cerebral blood flow and speeds brain atrophy. He links exercise as a protective factor for both heart and brain.

A shared post notes that SGLT2 inhibitors protect heart and kidneys beyond glucose control, and that emerging research suggests influences on inflammation, mitochondrial function, autophagy, AMPK and mTOR. It is largely promotional for a blog, and Louis added only hashtags.

Hormone therapy has no proven dementia benefit and may add risk in APOE4 carriers with MCI

Mar 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares a post by Hussein Yassine on hormone therapy, brain health and APOE4. There is no strong randomized evidence that HRT reduces dementia risk; benefits are best supported for bone protection and menopausal symptoms, and APOE4 carriers who already have MCI or dementia may face specific risks.

Clonal hematopoiesis: mutant bone-marrow cells as a heart disease risk factor

Oct 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Inflammation & CRP

The poster explains that clonal hematopoiesis, in which mutated blood stem cell clones expand in bone marrow, is a heart disease risk factor that becomes likely with advancing age, appears earlier in some people through genetics or chance, and a minority avoid. A shared video describes how these cells can fuel atherosclerosis.

Vitamin D trials in heart disease mostly disappointing despite mechanistic promise

Mar 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 0 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a 2026 review describing a 'vitamin D paradox': promising cell, animal and observational data, but supplementation has not consistently reduced cardiovascular events in unselected populations unless people are truly deficient. Aside from bone health, he says the best RCT evidence is for autoimmune disease.
