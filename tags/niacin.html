Niacin can cut Lp(a) (one member saw 40% with 1500 mg) but has no outcome proof

Jan 19, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 37 Lp(a)

Louis explains niacin can be effective at lowering Lp(a) and is still used by some cardiologists, with one group member reporting a 40% Lp(a) drop on 1500 mg. He gives tips for reducing the harmless flush and explains sustained-release formulations. He warns niacin has not been shown in large RCTs to reduce heart attacks, strokes or mortality, and two large trials found no added benefit on top of high-dose statins but more side effects.

Blood NAD levels do not fall with age, so commercial NAD blood tests are useless

Oct 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 5 Aging & longevity

A new Nature Metabolism study found whole-blood NAD+ levels stay stable with age across seven independent human cohorts. Louis explains blood NAD mainly reflects recent vitamin B3 intake, so commercial NAD blood tests are not meaningful, while tissue NAD levels may matter but cannot be commercially measured. There is still no convincing clinical evidence NR or NMN supplements slow human aging.

HDL and ApoA-I raising therapies repeatedly failed, so HDL is probably not directly causal

Mar 11, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 9 ↩ 22 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis reviews why HDL or ApoA-I is probably not causal for heart disease. CETP inhibitor trials failed (ILLUMINATE with torcetrapib was stopped for harm; dal-OUTCOMES with dalcetrapib and ACCELERATE with evacetrapib showed no benefit), as did fibrate and niacin trials and CSL112 infusions, and Mendelian randomization agrees. He proposes HDL and ApoA-I mainly act as a proxy for insulin sensitivity, which is the real causal factor.

Aug 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 4 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis explains that not every LDL-lowering therapy reduces heart attacks and strokes. Niacin added to high-dose statins lowered LDL more but two large RCTs showed no extra MACE benefit, and the CETP inhibitors torcetrapib and dalcetrapib lowered LDL but failed in outcome trials. Niacin breakdown products 2PY and 4PY linked to higher cardiovascular risk (Ferrell 2024)

Feb 19, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 6 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes Ferrell et al. 2024: untargeted metabolomics found that the niacin end-products 2PY and 4PY predict major adverse cardiac events, with 4PY promoting vascular inflammation.
