Your body makes DGLA from linoleic acid, linking seed oils to a possible senolytic effect

Nov 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis points out that the body makes DGLA from the omega-6 linoleic acid in seed oils and that this senolytic mechanism might explain some effects of GLA supplements. The shared Bryan Johnson post summarizes research in which DGLA killed zombie (senescent) cells in mice through ferroptosis. Low-linoleic-acid diets reduce DGLA production.

DGLA, an omega-6 fat made from linoleic acid, may selectively kill senescent cells

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Cell study summary showing senescent cells keep the omega-6 fat DGLA unusually low because it triggers ferroptosis, making DGLA a possible natural senolytic. The body makes DGLA from GLA and linoleic acid, found in nuts, seeds and their oils, and Louis notes that unlike other senolytics it is made endogenously.

Omega-6 DGLA selectively killed senescent cells through ferroptosis in a Cell study

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a report on a Cell study in which the omega-6 fat DGLA, made from linoleic acid, selectively killed senescent cells via ferroptosis while sparing healthy cells. Oral DGLA reduced senescent cells in aged mice and improved inflammation and organ function markers. Senescent cells contribute to age-related diseases including cardiovascular disease.
