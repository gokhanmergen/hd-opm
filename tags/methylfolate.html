Methylfolate is not 'natural', and higher blood levels may promote cancer

Mar 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 33 Supplements & vitamins

Louis explains food folate is polyglutamate-bound and less bioavailable than folic acid, which is less bioavailable than 5-MTHF supplements. Raising methylfolate too high may fuel precancerous cells and shortened lifespan in worms and mice.

High-dose methylfolate shortens lifespan in animals; stick to the RDA of folate

Sep 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 8 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis warns that too much folate, especially the methylated form (5-MTHF), shortened lifespan and promoted cancer growth in mice and worms. He recommends about the RDA from food-grade folate or folic acid and suggests safer ways to lower homocysteine.

Too much folate may shorten lifespan in animals and promote cancer in humans

Mar 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis warns against folate megadosing: excess folate shortened lifespan in animal models, and some human studies hint at cancer promotion, true for any folate form. Gokhan Can's post describes a mouse study of late-life folate restriction.

Harvard Nutrition Source review of folate: a little is good, too much may be bad

Aug 08, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 9 ↩ 4 Supplements & vitamins

The post shares Harvard's Nutrition Source summary of human research on folate and folic acid, concluding moderate intake helps and excess may harm. Quoted material covers cancer: observational links to lower colon and breast cancer, but a high-dose (1,000 mcg) trial in people with prior polyps found no protection and more advanced polyps.
