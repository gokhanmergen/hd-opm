A normal angiogram can hide large plaque burden because of Glagov remodeling

Dec 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 28 · 💬 6 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Louis explains that angiography is a 'luminogram' that misses plaque inside the artery wall because early plaque causes outward (Glagov) remodeling that preserves the lumen. Modern plaque-progression studies use IVUS or CT angiography instead, and he cites this as a flaw of Dean Ornish's small 1990s trial.

COVID, flu and RSV infections can destabilize people with heart conditions

Dec 06, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a short cath-lab note that respiratory viruses can provoke inflammation and raise the chance of acute coronary syndromes or decompensated heart failure in people with heart disease, recommending vaccination, early symptom recognition and treatment adherence. No specific study is cited.
