Daily peanuts raised brain blood flow about 4% in older adults, possibly via arginine

Dec 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 27 · 💬 17 ↩ 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares a Clinical Nutrition study in which a daily 60 g serving of skin-roasted peanuts improved global brain blood flow by about 4% in older adults, roughly the decline seen over 10 years of aging. He suggests arginine-driven nitric oxide as the mechanism, and nuts as slow-release arginine that may also help heart health.

Johns Hopkins arginine RCT stopped early: 6 deaths vs 0 in heart-attack survivors

Nov 29, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 25 ↩ 3 Supplements & vitamins

Louis warns that L-arginine supplements, taken to raise nitric oxide, can be dangerous after a heart attack. A Johns Hopkins randomized trial of 153 heart attack survivors given 3 g arginine three times daily was stopped after six months when six deaths occurred in the arginine group and none on placebo. Citrulline, which the body converts to arginine, might carry a similar concern, which is his speculation.

Daily peanuts improved brain blood flow, memory and BP in older adults in a crossover RCT

Jul 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A Maastricht randomized crossover trial of 31 adults aged 60-75 found 60 g of peanuts daily for 16 weeks raised cerebral blood flow 3.6%, verbal memory 5.8%, and lowered systolic BP 5 mmHg.

Plaque reduction: statins, PCSK9 inhibitors and ezetimibe have the strongest evidence

May 18, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 32 · 💬 5 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Ranks interventions for regressing or stabilizing atherosclerotic plaque. Statins, PCSK9 inhibitors and ezetimibe have the strongest trial evidence, with high-dose EPA and pioglitazone fairly good; colchicine, aged garlic, Centella, pine bark, nattokinase and plant-based diets are promising but need rigorous imaging RCTs, and evidence for exercise, L-arginine and vitamin K2 is limited.

Dietary nitrate from leafy greens and beets boosts nitric oxide and vessel relaxation

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Louis shares an explainer on endothelial health and nitric oxide. Nitrate-rich vegetables such as beetroot, spinach, arugula and lettuce are converted to nitrite and then nitric oxide, supporting vasodilation. Research on beetroot nitrate shows improved vascular function and in some studies modest blood pressure reductions.
