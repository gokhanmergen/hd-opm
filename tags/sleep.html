Magnesium L-threonate for 6 weeks improved cognitive age ~7.5 years (RCT, n=100)

Jun 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 29 · 💬 22 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 100 healthy adults aged 18-45 who were unhappy with their sleep. Magnesium L-threonate improved overall cognition, cognitive age, working memory and reaction time, and some subjective but not objective sleep measures. (Louis's post says six months, but the trial and article describe six weeks.)

'Grounding' during sleep is unnatural because primates evolved sleeping off the ground

Mar 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 36 Myths, misinformation & controversies

Louis argues that sleeping grounded (connected to the earth) is not natural, because primates slept in trees for tens of millions of years and wood is an insulator, so a raised bed mirrors natural primate behavior. This is an evolutionary argument and cites no study.

All magnesium forms, even oxide, are absorbed; form-specific marketing is hype

Apr 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 7 Supplements & vitamins

A shared piece argues that every magnesium salt dissociates in stomach acid and Mg2+ is what is absorbed, so threonate-for-brain or taurate-for-heart claims lack human data. Louis says differences between forms are fairly minor.

CTT meta-analysis: of 66 label-listed statin side effects, 62 showed no causal link

Feb 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Statins

Shared summary of the Cholesterol Treatment Trialists meta-analysis in The Lancet using individual data from 23 double-blind RCTs. Most side effects on statin labels were not caused by the drug, while muscle symptoms, new-onset diabetes, mild liver enzyme rises and a few minor effects were confirmed.

Higher fiber intake linked to 53% lower mortality in 3,894 US adults with diabetes

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared summary of a cohort of 3,894 US adults with diabetes found the third quartile of fiber intake had a 53% lower relative risk of death than the lowest (HR 0.47), with about 13% mediated by lower systemic immune inflammation index. It is observational and does not prove fiber extends life by 53%.
