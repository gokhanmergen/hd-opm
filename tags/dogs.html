High insulin/IGF-1 tied to cancer, diabetes; Laron syndrome and low-IGF-1 mice live longer

May 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 13 Aging & longevity

Louis praises a video on height, insulin/IGF-1 and longevity and lays out his argument: diet (refined starch/sugar and excess animal protein) is the main driver of IGF-1, and low IGF-1 is linked to lower cancer and diabetes risk. He cites Laron syndrome, with no documented cancer deaths, little diabetes and less heart disease and Alzheimer's, and a long-lived low-IGF-1 mouse. Claims come from the post and videos, not named papers.

Processed meat has no safe intake level in Nature Medicine burden-of-proof review

Apr 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 11 Meat, eggs & dairy

A shared summary of the IHME Burden of Proof analysis of 70+ studies finding a dose-response between processed meat and health risks with no apparent safe threshold. Louis clarifies that processed meat means chemically transformed by salting, curing, smoking or fermenting, including 'uncured' celery-powder products.

Nature Medicine: even small amounts of processed meat raise diabetes, heart, cancer risk

Oct 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 2 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis shares coverage of a burden-of-proof meta-analysis in Nature Medicine reviewing over 60 studies. Habitual intake of even small amounts of processed meat, sugary drinks and trans fats was linked to type 2 diabetes, ischemic heart disease and colorectal cancer.

Experts: processed meats are worse than unprocessed, and processing itself is the problem

Louis shares (via a paywall bypass) a 2022 NYT piece quoting Harvard's Frank Hu and the American Cancer Society on processed meat.
