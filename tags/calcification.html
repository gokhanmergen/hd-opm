PCSK9 inhibitors regress plaque without the calcification surge seen with statins

Jan 01, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 44 · 💬 35 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Explains the 'statin paradox' (statins reduce events and plaque but raise coronary calcium as plaque densifies) and argues PCSK9 inhibitors regress plaque without a calcific surge. Cites GLAGOV (evolocumab, LDL 36.6 mg/dL, PAV -0.95% vs +0.05%), PACMAN-AMI (alirocumab triple regression) and Gao 2022 (CAC +2.9% vs +10.6%).

Why a coronary calcium score can rise as plaque stabilizes on statins

Dec 03, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 28 · 💬 47 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Explains that the Agatston score is not true calcium volume: it multiplies area by a stepwise density factor (1-4), so slightly denser plaque can jump a category and raise the score. Statins turn soft lipid-rich plaque into stable calcified plaque, so a rising CAC on treatment can reflect stabilization rather than failure.

Statins do not slow coronary calcium; severe-calcification odds hit 4.48 after 10+ years

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 41 Statins

Evidence review concluding statins reduce events and soft plaque but do not slow Agatston CAC progression, and that long-term cohorts show calcification accelerating without plateau, interpreted as plaque repair into stable scar. Trials: St. Francis (CAC unchanged, events down 33%), SALTIRE (progression 26% vs 18%) and BELLES (similar progression, ~11-14%).

New vitamin K2 trial slowed arterial calcification but did not reduce plaque, video argues

Aug 31, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 23 · 💬 38 Supplements & vitamins

Summarises a Gil Carvalho video on a randomized trial of vitamin K2: calcification progressed more slowly, but total plaque did not fall, the plaque was just less calcified. Carvalho argues calcium stabilises plaque, so slowing calcification without reducing plaque could even be harmful, and no evidence shows K2 reduces heart attacks or strokes, so he advises relying on proven therapies, diet and exercise.

Vitamin K2 slows coronary calcium mainly in severe calcification; event benefit unproven

Dec 02, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 16 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Review of vitamin K2 (with or without vitamin D) and CAC. A 2023 meta-analysis of 14 RCTs (n=1,533) found a -17.3 Agatston units/year pooled difference. The largest RCT (389 men, 720 ug MK-7 + 1000 IU D vs placebo for 2 years) missed significance overall but slowed CAC at baseline CAC >=400; some CKD trials were null. Not yet a standard therapy.

Louis explains that soft tissue calcification is an actively regulated process that can sometimes be reversed, for example in calcific tendonitis, through macrophage-driven dissolving. Whether artery calcification can be reversed is poorly understood.

Charles Grashow's protocol to regress coronary calcium over two years

PCSK9 inhibitor plus statin nearly halted coronary calcium progression versus statin alone

Dec 01, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 14 · 💬 4 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summary of alirocumab 75 mg biweekly added to moderate statins in 61 CAD patients over 18 months: median CAC +2.9% vs +10.6%, with CAC not rising significantly from baseline. A retrospective study (Ikegami, n=120) found about half the annual calcium progression. Mechanism is thought to be stronger LDL and Lp(a) lowering.

The four stages of coronary artery disease, from soft plaque to ischemia

Jul 27, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 17 · 💬 2 Plaque, CAC & CT angiography

Explainer on how coronary artery disease develops over decades: soft plaque forms from childhood or early adulthood, plaques harden and calcify, arteries narrow causing ischemia with angina or silent ischemia, and later events. Many people stay asymptomatic through the early stages.

Small dense HDL tied to coronary calcium in insulin resistance; drugs may offset 40%

Louis highlights a paper in the International Journal of Cardiology reporting that small HDL particles are associated with prediabetes, diabetes and subclinical coronary atherosclerosis. Alirocumab plus statin slowed coronary calcium progression: +2.9% vs +10.6% at 18 months

Jan 01, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 16 · 💬 1 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Summarises a 2022 RCT in CAD patients on moderate statins: alirocumab 75 mg every 2 weeks for 18 months limited CAC progression, with Lp(a) change correlating with CAC change. A retrospective study (Ikegami) found about half the annual CAC progression with PCSK9 inhibitor plus statin; studies are small and larger trials are needed.

Vitamin K2 slowed coronary calcium only when started early; two late-stage trials failed

Louis shares a summary of three RCTs of vitamin K2 (MK-7) on arterial calcification. K2 lowered the inactive MGP marker in all three, but calcification slowed only in the most recent trial, which enrolled people with early coronary disease; two trials in established aortic valve or dialysis calcification showed no change.

Centella asiatica and pine bark extract: small trials on arterial plaque stability

Nov 17, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes small trials of Centella asiatica and French maritime pine bark for arterial plaque, including a 1-year study of 60 patients where soft plaque did not grow compared with a 23% increase on placebo. A 3-year study of over 300 patients reported plaque progression in 5.3% with the combination versus over 20% in controls.
