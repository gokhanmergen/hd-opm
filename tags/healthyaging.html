Whey protein raised IGF-1 but not muscle or strength in 24-week RCT of older adults

Jul 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 17 Protein

A 24-week placebo-controlled trial raised protein to 1.5 g/kg/day with whey in healthy older adults. It did not improve muscle power, strength, performance or mass, though IGF-1 rose; potassium bicarbonate was also ineffective. Louis argues this means the harms of high IGF-1 without benefit.

Osteoarthritis is partly metabolic, which is why GLP-1 drugs show promise

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's view that osteoarthritis is not simple wear and tear but reflects whole-body metabolic state, and that the semaglutide headlines risk missing this message.

Late-life folate restriction reduced biosynthesis without harming healthspan in mice

Aug 08, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 9 · 💬 16 ↩ 2 Supplements & vitamins

Mice switched at 52 weeks to a folate- and choline-free diet held their weight, avoided anemia, and showed reduced protein synthesis pathways over 68 weeks. Gokhan stresses the work is in yeast, worms and mice, not humans.

Get protein from whole plant foods to lower intake of isoleucine, methionine and valine

Aug 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Protein

Louis suggests replacing red and processed meat with legumes, nuts, seeds and whole grains, which lowers the three amino acids that accelerate aging in animal models, whatever the human relevance.

Wall squat isometric holds may lower systolic blood pressure about 9 points in weeks

Nov 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 8 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a post saying a large review found isometric exercises, especially wall squats, lowered resting blood pressure more than cardio, intervals or weights. In one trial wall squats cut systolic pressure by about 9.4 points. The routine is four 2-minute holds with 2-minute rests, three times weekly, without holding the breath.

Mediterranean and DASH diets slow biological aging most, per npj Aging perspective

Sep 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 3 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares an npj Aging review proposing that diet acts as a molecular signal modulating aging through inflammation, the microbiome and resilience, and says biological-age metrics show the traditional Mediterranean and DASH diets slow aging the most so far.

Chronically high insulin drives aging biology, and calorie restriction lowers it

Aug 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 5 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Luigi Fontana explains that insulin is a nutrient-signaling hormone and that persistent activation of the insulin/IGF-1-AKT-mTOR network is linked to cell growth, aging biology and cancer-related pathways. Calorie restriction and weight loss are among the strongest ways to improve insulin sensitivity.

Low-insulinemic and Planetary Health diets tied to less weight gain at menopause

Jun 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 · 💬 2 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares results from the Harvard Nurses' Health Study published in JAMA Network Open. Among over 38,000 women followed 12 years around menopause, a low-insulinemic dietary pattern and the Planetary Health Diet were most associated with less weight gain and lower obesity risk.

Whole-blood NAD+ does not change with age or lifestyle, so it is a poor aging marker

May 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Aging & longevity

Louis, echoing Charles Brenner, shares a Nature Metabolism paper reporting that human whole-blood NAD+ is stable across age and not altered by exercise or lifestyle interventions. Nicotinamide riboside raised NAD+, but that does not prove clinical or longevity benefit; tissue NAD matters.

PNAS: brain network destabilization begins in midlife, tied to insulin resistance and fat

Jun 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a summary of a PNAS study finding that brain network destabilization, a precursor to cognitive decline, begins in midlife and is associated with insulin resistance and elevated blood pressure. Cardiovascular and cognitive health share drivers: inflammation, insulin resistance, more

Jun 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a JACC piece arguing that cognitive impairment and CVD share the same biological drivers: chronic inflammation, insulin resistance, hypertension, dyslipidemia, oxidative stress, endothelial dysfunction and atherosclerosis. It suggests lifestyle strategies such as diet, exercise, stress reduction and sleep can address both.

Senescent cells may drive atherosclerosis, making senolytics a possible target

Nov 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Aging & longevity

Louis suggests that senescent cells, which accumulate with age, are a causal factor in atherosclerosis, especially in the endothelium, and that drugs targeting them might help. The shared AFAR post notes mouse studies show senescent cells can be removed.

Metformin eased knee osteoarthritis pain more than placebo in a 6-month Australian RCT

Apr 25, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 ↩ 2 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Luigi Fontana's summary of a JAMA RCT in people with knee osteoarthritis and overweight/obesity: metformin reduced pain more than placebo. He frames it as aging biology (AMPK, mTOR, inflammation) as a treatment target, and notes lifestyle approaches like caloric restriction act on the same pathways. Little direct heart relevance.

Taurine is not a reliable aging biomarker, says new multi-species study

Jun 07, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A shared summary of a large longitudinal study across humans (26-100 years), monkeys and mice finds taurine levels do not consistently fall with age, often rise (especially in women), and vary more between individuals than with age. The post notes that taurine, like NAD+ boosters and resveratrol, shows promise in animals but human evidence is limited.
