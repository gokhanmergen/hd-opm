Atorvastatin improved kidney function dose-dependently across 6 RCTs with 30,621 patients

Oct 13, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis cites a JAHA post hoc analysis of six double-blind outcome trials in patients at risk of or with cardiovascular disease. Atorvastatin improved kidney function over time in a dose-dependent way, and improvement in kidney function was associated with lower cardiovascular risk. The authors suggest kidney function could serve as a surrogate end point.

PLANET trial: atorvastatin preferred over rosuvastatin in CKD (with July 2026 correction)

Dec 06, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 7 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis shares a teaching card that atorvastatin is the preferred statin in chronic kidney disease, citing the PLANET trial comparing it with rosuvastatin. His later edit says the original text had a typo: the correct statement is that atorvastatin improved proteinuria but had no effect on eGFR.

Rosuvastatin can stress kidneys at high dose; atorvastatin appears kidney-friendly

Jul 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 4 Statins

Louis explains rare dose-related proteinuria, hematuria or AKI with rosuvastatin, especially 40 mg or with CKD. PLANET I and II found rosuvastatin 40 mg lowered eGFR whereas atorvastatin 80 mg reduced proteinuria and preserved function.
