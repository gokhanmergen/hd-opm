White potatoes: short-term endothelial decline (MODERATE) and more hypertension in cohorts

Apr 19, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 7 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Summarizes the MODERATE trial, where participants who ate boiled, baked or mashed potatoes in the previous 24 hours had 1.7% lower flow-mediated dilation, roughly the 1.2%-per-decade aging decline for 14 years, as a short-term effect. It is linked to cohort data (Nurses' Health Studies and Health Professionals) tying baked, boiled or mashed potatoes to hypertension.

Paleolithic humans were omnivores who relied heavily on cooked starchy tubers

May 09, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 13 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Louis rebuts the idea that paleolithic humans ate keto or carnivore diets, pointing to evidence of cooked starch use and Richard Wrangham's 'Catching Fire' hypothesis that cooked tubers and roots, a reliable glucose source, helped drive brain growth. Starchy underground foods were available even when meat and fish were not.

Paleo diet is a myth: our ancestors also ate wild grains, legumes and starchy tubers

Sep 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 4 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

A video interview with Harvard palaeoanthropologist Daniel Lieberman explains why the paleo diet is not what Stone Age ancestors ate. The poster highlights that they also ate wild grains, legumes and starchy tubers.

Cut refined sugar and starch, ~40% of calories in the standard American diet

Jan 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 2 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Louis shares a Nutrition Made Simple video with Ford Brewer on whether to cut carbs, noting that the one point of consensus is removing refined sugar and refined starch, about 40% of calories in the standard American diet.
