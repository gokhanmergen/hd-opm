Olive oil may reduce clotting by raising apoA-IV, which limits platelet aggregation

Jul 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 10 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A Life Extension article describes mechanistic studies in which apoA-IV, a protective HDL component, reduces platelet aggregation and thrombosis, and monounsaturated fats such as extra virgin olive oil raise apoA-IV, especially after meals. This is mechanistic and partly animal evidence.

Platelet reactivity score (PRESS) predicts cardiovascular events in PAD patients

Aug 20, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 19 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

NYU researchers developed PRESS, combining platelet aggregation responses and RNA sequencing, to detect platelet hyperreactivity. In PAD patients, hyperreactive platelets predicted more major adverse cardiac or limb events within 30 days of revascularization, and PRESS above the median carried a 90% higher risk of major events over 18 months. It might help target aspirin.

The author describes the metabolite supplements he uses (AKG, L-arginine, beta-alanine, taurine, L-carnitine) with brief rationales, mostly from animal data, and links to his blog notes. It is a personal experiment rather than evidence of cardiovascular benefit.

Aging stem cells open a direct platelet pathway that raises thrombosis risk (Cell 2024)

Jun 09, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 12 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

Shares the abstract of a Cell paper showing that with age, hematopoietic stem cells increasingly make platelets through a direct route that bypasses the usual progenitor cascade. The platelets from this pathway are more prone to thrombosis and cause higher platelet counts. It offers a possible cellular explanation for why clotting-related cardiovascular disease rises so steeply in older people.

Intermittent fasting may prevent dangerous clots by raising gut-derived IPA

Sep 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 Fasting & meal timing

Louis shares a summary of a Life Metabolism study in 160 coronary artery disease participants plus human blood and mice. Intermittent fasting appeared to inhibit platelet activation and clot formation through increased gut microbial production of indole-3-propionic acid.

CoQ10 rejuvenated aging platelet-producing cells in mice via COPS3 and autophagy

Oct 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 Supplements & vitamins

A mouse study in Aging Cell found dietary CoQ10 restored function of aged megakaryocytes, the bone marrow cells that make platelets, through a COPS3-autophagy pathway. Aging makes platelets stickier and more clot-prone, raising heart attack and stroke risk. The poster calls it promising but it is animal-only.

Review: functional foods as safer alternatives to antiplatelet drugs

Nov 20, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

Review of diet-based ways to curb platelet hyperactivity, a driver of thrombosis, CVD, diabetes and cancer. It notes aspirin resistance in 25-30% of people and bleeding risks, and highlights omega-3s, polyphenols, tomato extract (Fruitflow) and kiwifruit as promising natural antiplatelet agents.

How to interpret a complete blood count (CBC): a stepwise clinical approach

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Risk assessment & lab tests

A shared infographic explains how to read a CBC in order: hemoglobin, MCV, MCH/MCHC, RDW, white cells with differential, then platelets, using the 'RWP' mnemonic. It covers what low or high values may point to, such as anemia, infection or platelet abnormalities. It is a general clinical reference rather than heart-specific content.

ASPREE sub-analysis: aspirin tied to ~70% lower dementia in a platelet-genetics subgroup

Oct 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

A genetic analysis of 13,541 ASPREE participants found aspirin did not prevent dementia overall, but in the top 20% of a platelet-count genetic score dementia occurred in about 1% on aspirin versus 3.3% on placebo. Major bleeding was more than twice as common in this subgroup, so the post cautions against starting aspirin for dementia prevention.
