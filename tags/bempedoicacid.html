Only lipophilic statins like atorvastatin enter the brain; PCSK9 drugs and ezetimibe don't

Oct 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 10 Brain health & dementia

Louis explains the blood-brain barrier and cholesterol: the brain makes its own cholesterol separately from blood, so non-statin LDL drugs and hydrophilic statins like rosuvastatin have little brain effect. Lipophilic statins do enter the brain, with promising observational data against Alzheimer's, and a 20,000-person atorvastatin trial is under way. Two prior trials showed statins do not worsen existing Alzheimer's, and midlife high LDL is a leading modifiable dementia risk (2024 Lancet Commission, about 7% of US cases per Louis).

Ezetimibe beyond statins: fewer events, no mortality gain in trials, registry benefits

Nov 07, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 11 · 💬 22 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Evidence digest on ezetimibe. A meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (123,785 patients) found add-on lipid lowering cut cardiovascular events 19% versus statins alone but did not improve all-cause mortality. Other data show moderate statin plus ezetimibe matches high-intensity statins, and registry studies hint at lower mortality, with strong LDL-target attainment.

Bempedoic acid cut CV events and may also dampen age-related inflammation

Jul 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 14 PCSK9, ezetimibe & newer drugs

Louis shares Gokhan Can's summary of bempedoic acid in 4,206 statin-intolerant primary prevention patients: the primary composite fell 30%, MI 39%, and all-cause mortality 27%. It lowers LDL by 21.3% and hsCRP by 21.5%, and inhibits ACLY, which drives senescence inflammation in aged mice.

Evolving LDL-C management (JAHA 2023): risk categories, thresholds and treatment goals

Nov 25, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 · 💬 11 ↩ 2 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Summarizes a 2023 JAHA review of personalized LDL-C management, giving risk categories from low to very high and the LDL goals for each. High-risk patients should reduce LDL at least 50% to below 70 mg/dL, while very-high-risk patients should aim below 55 mg/dL, or below 40 after recurrent events on statins.

Genetics suggest statins' small diabetes risk is tied to LDL lowering (Gemini-assisted)

Sep 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 11 Statins

Louis shares a post, written with Gemini AI, arguing that the small rise in new-onset type 2 diabetes with intensive lipid lowering reflects target-mediated effects rather than an off-target drug side effect. Genetic variants mimicking HMGCR (statin) and NPC1L1 (ezetimibe) inhibition show modestly higher glucose and diabetes risk.

2025 ESC/EAS update: where ezetimibe, bempedoic acid, PCSK9 drugs and inclisiran fit
