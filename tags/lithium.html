Low-dose lithium carbonate failed to slow cognitive decline in mild cognitive impairment

Mar 02, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 2 Brain health & dementia

A JAMA Neurology RCT found low-dose lithium did not significantly delay cognitive decline or change imaging and plasma biomarkers over 2 years in older adults with MCI, though it was safe. Louis notes the trial used lithium carbonate, whereas Yankner's Harvard work suggests lithium orotate is the active form in Alzheimer's models.

Low-dose lithium shows neuroprotective signals and lower dementia risk in a review

Jun 11, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 8 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares a JAMA Psychiatry narrative review concluding lithium has neuroprotective effects across cellular, imaging and epidemiologic studies. Low-dose lithium below psychiatric doses preserves neurons and gray matter, cumulative exposure is associated with lower dementia risk, and early trials in mild cognitive impairment look favorable.
