Peter Attia: early-life sugar restriction study is too weak for recommendations

Dec 08, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 8 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

Gokhan shares Peter Attia's critique of a study claiming sugar restriction in the first 1000 days of life protects against later diabetes and hypertension. The critique calls it an interesting historical analysis that raises questions, but not reliable enough to justify sugar restriction advice.

Nuts, seeds and corn did not raise diverticulitis risk in nearly 30,000 women

May 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a large prospective cohort study in women showing that eating nuts, seeds, corn or seeded fruit did not increase diverticulitis risk, while healthy diet patterns (DASH, Mediterranean-style) were linked to lower risk. He notes his mother with diverticulosis was told to avoid nuts for years. Peripheral to heart health but relevant to nut-eating advice.

Meta-analysis of 119 trials: nuts did not cause weight gain and modestly lowered LDL

Oct 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 2 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized trials (154 articles, 119 unique RCTs) found adding about 120 kcal/day of nuts did not significantly change weight, BMI or waist; body fat fell slightly and lean mass rose. Nuts were associated with small reductions in total cholesterol, LDL and triglycerides, with no significant effect on HDL.
