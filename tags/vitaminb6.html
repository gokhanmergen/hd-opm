Gly-Low glycation-lowering cocktail extended lifespan in mice

Nov 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 20 · 💬 12 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

A Buck Institute study in Cell Reports tested a five-ingredient mix (nicotinamide, thiamine, pyridoxamine, alpha lipoic acid, piperine) that lowers glycation. In mice it extended lifespan, reduced appetite and body fat and improved insulin sensitivity.

100 mg vitamin B6 reduced depression symptoms in a new randomized trial; B12 failed

Aug 25, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 11 ↩ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes a new RCT reports that 100 mg vitamin B6 significantly reduced depression symptoms while B12 did not. The same authors found in 2022 that B6 reduces anxiety. He notes depression and anxiety are CVD risk factors and warns of nerve toxicity with prolonged high doses.

High homocysteine can reflect low B12, B6 or folate and raises clot and CVD risk

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 13 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a short Cleveland Clinic explainer that homocysteine is an amino acid broken down by vitamins B12, B6 and folate, and that high levels may indicate deficiency and increase clot, heart disease and stroke risk. It is basic background without trial evidence.

Peter Attia's current supplement stack

Nov 23, 2023 · Gokhan Can ♥ 8 · 💬 14 Supplements & vitamins

Summarizes the supplements Peter Attia says he takes: B6, high-EPA fish oil aimed at a red-cell EPA+DHA level near 12%, vitamin D 5,000 IU, several forms of magnesium, methylfolate and B12 to keep homocysteine below nine, and baby aspirin. This is a personal regimen, not evidence.

Glycation and AGEs in cardiovascular aging: Kapahi on methylglyoxal and the GLYLO suppleme

Dec 18, 2023 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 9 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis says he underestimated glycation and AGEs in aging after Pankaj Kapahi's work at the Buck Institute. Kapahi's post explains methylglyoxal as a reactive glycolysis byproduct that drives AGEs and aging, and describes GLYLO, a combination of lipoic acid, nicotinamide, piperine, pyridoxine and thiamine that lowered methylglyoxal and AGEs and extended mouse lifespan even when started late. Louis says Kapahi plans a paper showing it reduces arterial stiffening. Evidence is preclinical and the product is commercial.

VITACOG: B vitamins cut brain atrophy 30% in mild cognitive impairment

Jun 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 5 Brain health & dementia

Louis reshares his summary of the Oxford VITACOG randomized trial of folate, B6 and B12 in elderly people with MCI. B vitamins slowed brain shrinkage, most in those with high baseline homocysteine, and later analyses suggested benefit depended on high omega-3 status.

High-dose vitamin B6 can cause sensory neuropathy despite benefits for mood

Sep 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 2 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes that recent randomized trials suggest B6 helps anxiety and depression but warns that too much can be dangerous. The shared text says high-dose pyridoxine can cause sensory neuropathy, clearest at 1-3 g/day, while the minimum toxic dose is uncertain and regulatory limits vary from about 10 to 100 mg/day.

GLYLO's mouse lifespan benefit may stem from calorie restriction, per a critical analysis

Dec 03, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 2 · 💬 5 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a Substack critique of the glycation-focused supplement GLYLO, which questions the mouse lifespan data, the role of reduced food intake and differences between the research and consumer formulas. His comment is that even if benefits come from inadvertent calorie restriction, that is still significant, as with GLP-1 drugs.

Glycation-lowering supplement combination curbed hunger and extended mouse lifespan

Oct 29, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 1 Aging & longevity

Louis shares a Cell Reports study in mice where a combination of nicotinamide, alpha-lipoic acid, thiamine, pyridoxamine and piperine (Gly-Low) lowered AGEs, curbed hunger, improved insulin resistance and extended lifespan. It is preclinical.

B12 lowers homocysteine but has not cut heart attacks; B vitamins may lower stroke risk

Nov 08, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Supplements & vitamins

Louis shares a review summary showing B12 supplements reliably lower homocysteine, but large randomized trials did not consistently reduce heart attacks or cardiac deaths. The strongest benefit was for stroke, mainly where foods are not folic-acid fortified, but most trials combined B12 with folic acid and B6. Routine B12 solely for CVD prevention is not supported, though it is essential if deficient.
