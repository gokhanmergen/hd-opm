Louis summarizes the Lyon Heart Study (1988-1992), which randomized over 600 heart attack survivors to a Mediterranean-style diet or an AHA diet. Louis explains that olive oil is the only fat tested in multiple large randomized outcome trials (CORDIOPREV, PREDIMED, Lyon).
