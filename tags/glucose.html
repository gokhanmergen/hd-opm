100+ RCTs and 20 cohorts: omega-6 improves glucose control, 43% lower diabetes risk

Oct 21, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis summarizes evidence that omega-6 polyunsaturated fat lowers fasting glucose and A1C and improves insulin resistance and secretion across more than 100 randomized trials. A pooled analysis of about 40,000 people from 20 prospective cohorts with biomarker-measured omega-6 found the top fifth had 43% lower diabetes risk than the bottom fifth. He lists the supporting meta-analyses.

Louis quotes a claim that high fiber intake can cut colon cancer risk by up to 60%, with each 10 g/day lowering risk by about 10%. He ties this to why a fiber-rich Mediterranean diet outperforms others for cancer prevention.

Iron deficiency can falsely raise HbA1c even before anemia develops

Nov 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares a physician's explanation that low iron can inflate HbA1c, possibly by slowing red blood cell turnover so older cells with more glycation remain in circulation. If A1c seems high on a healthy diet, check iron status.

JAPAN-ACS sub-study: statins lowered AGEs, which were linked to plaque progression

Sep 13, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 1 Statins

A sub-study of the JAPAN-ACS trial found high baseline advanced glycation end products (AGEs) were independently associated with plaque progression, and statin therapy significantly lowered AGE levels. Louis notes the trial produced the largest plaque regression, about -17%, in a drug trial.
