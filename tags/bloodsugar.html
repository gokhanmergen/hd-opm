Low-carb diets lose their diabetes advantage by 12 months, trials and meta-analyses show

Nov 25, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 8 Low-carb, keto & carnivore

Argues low-carb gives better remission and HbA1c at 6 months, but the edge fades. A 2021 BMJ meta-analysis (23 RCTs) found benefit at 6 months diminishing by 12; Silverii 2020 (37 RCTs, 3,301 patients) found HbA1c differences vanishing and reversing by 24 months; a 2-year calorie-matched RCT showed identical weight loss.
