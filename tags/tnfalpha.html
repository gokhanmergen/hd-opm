Taurine 3 g/day lowered insulin, HOMA-IR and inflammation in type 2 diabetes RCT

Jul 04, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 15 Insulin resistance & diabetes

A 120-person RCT gave 3 g taurine daily with a low-calorie diet for 8 weeks. Insulin, HOMA-IR, TNF, hs-CRP and MDA fell, but lipids, HbA1c and fasting glucose did not improve.

Inflammaging may begin around age 26 in men and 35 in women, ATTICA analysis finds

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 Inflammation & CRP

Louis shares a summary of an ATTICA analysis of 1,825 Greek adults showing a combined IL-6, TNF-alpha and fibrinogen immune activation score crosses into the activated range by about age 26 in men and 35 in women. IL-6 and fibrinogen rose steadily with age while CRP barely changed. Earlier studies link these markers to heart disease, diabetes, dementia and death.
