Muscle contraction after meals clears glucose by a second, insulin-independent pathway

Mar 22, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 6 Exercise & fitness

William Wallace explains that sitting after eating relies on insulin signaling, while moving activates GLUT4 transporters through muscle contraction, so glucose enters muscle faster with lower peaks and less pancreatic demand. Louis shares it as a rationale for post-meal walking.

Vigorous exercise is 4-10x more potent than moderate, and lactate may be beneficial

Aug 01, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 6 Exercise & fitness

A Nature Communications study reported 1 minute of vigorous activity equals 4 minutes for all-cause mortality, 7.8 for CVD and 9.4 for diabetes. Louis argues physiological lactate is beneficial, disputing claims that metformin's lactate rise is harmful.

Exercise activates AS160 and GLUT4 for muscle glucose uptake; Zierath wins 2024 EASD prize

Aug 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares news that Prof. Juleen Zierath won the 2024 EASD Diabetes Prize for Excellence for 30 years of work on exercise and muscle insulin signalling. Her group found exercise activates AS160, which boosts GLUT4 to move glucose into muscle and fat cells, a pathway still working when insulin's effect is impaired in type 2 diabetes. This supports exercise for lowering blood sugar.
