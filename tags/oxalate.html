Foods high in oxalate may worsen gadolinium retention after MRI contrast

Nov 17, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 10 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

Louis shares University of New Mexico research that oxalic acid in foods like chocolate, spinach and vitamin C can form gadolinium nanoparticles in tissues, and suggests avoiding such foods before contrast MRI.

Vitamin C 1-2 g/day raises urinary oxalate, a concern for calcium oxalate stone-formers

Aug 29, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 10 Supplements & vitamins

Louis warns that vitamin C supplementation increases oxalate production, with 1-2 g daily producing a detectable rise in urinary oxalate excretion. Quoting a Reviews in Urology paper, he notes it does not change urinary pH and might promote stone activity in calcium oxalate stone-formers.

Preventing calcium oxalate kidney stones: AUA guideline recommendations

Aug 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 5 Kidney health

Louis outlines American Urological Association guidance on preventing calcium oxalate stones, which make up about 80% of kidney stones, including fluid intake targets, with evidence strength noted for each recommendation.
