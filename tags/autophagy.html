Can fasting reverse plaque? Autophagy makes it plausible but evidence is indirect

Jun 29, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 23 · 💬 14 Fasting & meal timing

Louis argues there is biological plausibility that fasting could clear damaged arterial tissue via autophagy, linking earlier posts. He cites the 2010 DIRECT-Carotid trial showing large weight loss can regress carotid atherosclerosis, and a shared post on a Nature Metabolism 7-day fast study in 12 people with a day-3 shift in about 1,000 proteins. Direct evidence for plaque reversal by fasting is anecdotal.

Omega-6 fats activate autophagy and extend roundworm lifespan 20-25%

Louis cites Harvard/MGH work (senior author Gary Ruvkun) showing omega-6 fatty acids inhibit mTOR and activate autophagy, extending C. elegans lifespan, with similar responses in human cells. It is mechanistic and animal evidence, not human outcome data.

Sabatini on diet and mTOR: limit amino acids and insulin; ~18 h fasting may inhibit mTORC1

Dec 09, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 11 ↩ 3 Aging & longevity

Louis shares notes on a David Sabatini interview about how diet affects mTOR and autophagy. Sabatini suggests low amino acid or low carbohydrate diets lower mTOR, favors a higher-carbohydrate diet of low-glycemic carbs, and thinks mTORC1 senses total daily insulin. He guesses about 18 hours of fasting inhibits mTORC1, while stressing nobody has all the answers.

Excess calories shut down autophagy, one reason calorie restriction extends lifespan

Aug 18, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Fasting & meal timing

Louis states that overeating shuts down autophagy (cellular self-recycling), a main reason calorie restriction extends lifespan in animals including mammals. A shared item describes autophagy as triggered by fasting or restriction.

Halving dietary protein for 4 weeks did not increase autophagy in human immune cells

Sep 17, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 4 Protein

In a randomized crossover trial of healthy adults, reducing protein from 20% to 10% of calories for four weeks did not significantly change autophagic flux in blood immune cells. Louis concludes that moderate protein restriction alone, without fasting or calorie restriction, does not appear to raise basal autophagy. Caveats include measurement only in circulating immune cells and a short duration; this is a preprint.

Muscle aging involves falling chaperone-mediated autophagy; fasting and exercise raise it

Dec 08, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 7 ↩ 1 Aging & longevity

Louis shares two Nature Metabolism papers from Ana Maria Cuervo's group showing chaperone-mediated autophagy declines with age in muscle and that restoring it improved strength and regeneration in aging mice. He notes fasting and resistance training increase CMA, rapamycin activates it and excess high-quality protein inhibits it, and ties it to heart muscle.

GLP-1 drugs may improve muscle quality; the '40% muscle loss' figure reflects DXA limits

Jul 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 6 GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide, tirzepatide)

Louis shares an analysis arguing the 40% figure from STEP-1 is DXA 'lean mass', which includes water, organs and liver. In SEMALEAN, 12 months of high-dose semaglutide reduced lean mass only about 5%, improved grip strength 4.5 kg, and cut sarcopenic obesity from 49% to 33%.
