French cohort of 22.7 million: mRNA COVID shots cut COVID death 74%, all-cause death 25%

Dec 05, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 27 · 💬 47 Infections & vaccines

Reports a French cohort of 22.7 million vaccinated and 5.9 million unvaccinated adults followed nearly four years: 74% lower COVID death risk and 25% lower risk of death overall, with no turbocancer signal. The admin adds that vaccination protects against heart disease deaths in high-risk groups and that he prefers flu vaccine to COVID mRNA.

Oct 04, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 16 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a post about a Lancet eClinicalMedicine study in which 36 healthy unvaccinated young adults were deliberately inoculated with SARS-CoV-2 and 18 became infected, almost all with mild symptoms. Baby aspirin during and after Covid may be worth discussing if you have heart disease

Jan 06, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 12 Infections & vaccines

Louis suggests people with heart disease who get Covid ask their doctor about baby aspirin during infection and for a few weeks after. Infections temporarily raise heart attack and stroke risk through inflammation, and Covid raises clot risk more than other infections. A linked 2022 meta-analysis found aspirin use associated with lower Covid mortality without more bleeding.

Platelet reactivity score (PRESS) predicts cardiovascular events in PAD patients

Aug 20, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 6 · 💬 19 Aspirin, antiplatelets & blood thinners

NYU researchers developed PRESS, combining platelet aggregation responses and RNA sequencing, to detect platelet hyperreactivity. In PAD patients, hyperreactive platelets predicted more major adverse cardiac or limb events within 30 days of revascularization, and PRESS above the median carried a 90% higher risk of major events over 18 months. It might help target aspirin.

Meta-analysis of 155 studies: flu, shingles and COVID raise heart attack and stroke risk

Oct 29, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 8 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a report on a meta-analysis of 155 studies finding that common viral infections raise heart attack and stroke risk, both soon after infection and long term. Flu carried up to six times higher heart attack risk the month after infection, and COVID-19 nearly doubled risk.

Flu and respiratory infections raise heart attack risk; ask your doctor about baby aspirin

BMJ: evidence mounts that COVID-19 disrupts immunity, challenging 'immunity debt' idea

Aug 20, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 · 💬 10 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A BMJ feature reports that the immunity debt theory is increasingly challenged and that SARS-CoV-2 may cause lasting immune disruption. Evidence includes more infections after covid than after flu or RSV, viral reactivation, T-cell exhaustion and persistent inflammation.

Quoting a Clinical Infectious Diseases analysis of the COVID-OUT trial, the post says metformin suppresses viral protein translation via host mTOR and reduced viral load versus placebo. Metformin also lowered hospitalization/death and long Covid odds.

COVID-OUT: early metformin cut long Covid risk by 42% in a large randomized trial

Aug 22, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 4 ↩ 2 Infections & vaccines

The COVID-OUT phase 3 trial (>1000 mostly non-diabetic outpatients) found metformin lowered long Covid incidence by 42% relative to placebo, and by 63% when started within 4 days of symptoms. Ivermectin and fluvoxamine did not prevent long Covid.

Infections temporarily raise heart attack and stroke risk; vaccines may do so far less

Oct 14, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 Infections & vaccines

Louis explains that infections such as flu raise short-term heart attack and stroke risk through inflammation and coagulation effects that destabilize plaque, and that the effect fades in a week or two. Vaccines can in theory do this to a much smaller degree. He suggests asking a doctor about a baby aspirin during and shortly after infection, and avoiding hs-CRP tests during that window.
