High added sugar linked to over double the risk of heart disease death in a JAMA IM study

Aug 22, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 35 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

A shared summary describes a 15-year JAMA Internal Medicine study in which people getting 25% or more of calories from added sugar had over twice the CVD death risk of those under 10%, regardless of weight or other factors. Louis clarifies that the comparison is with dietary cholesterol, not blood cholesterol.

Cane-sugar Coke is no healthier: sucrose splits into glucose and fructose just like HFCS

Jul 16, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 18 Carbs, fiber, sugar & whole grains

In response to news that Coca-Cola would use cane sugar in US cola, Louis explains that sucrose is exactly 50% glucose and 50% fructose and is split by sucrase almost immediately, much like HFCS. He warns members not to think it makes soda healthier.

Unhealthy plant-based diets raise CHD risk, healthy ones lower it, in Harvard cohorts

Dec 26, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 12 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis stresses that not all plant-based diets are healthy. In the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study, a plant-based index rich in healthy plant foods was linked to substantially lower CHD risk while one emphasizing sugary drinks, refined grains, potatoes and sweets was linked to higher risk, worse than the standard diet baseline.

Most ultraprocessed food CVD risk in Harvard cohorts came from soda and processed meat

Louis shares a NY Times report on Harvard Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals data. Cane sugar and high-fructose corn syrup are both roughly 50% glucose and 50% fructose
