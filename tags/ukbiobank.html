APOE4 carriers eating unprocessed meat or fish had lower dementia risk in Swedish study

Mar 23, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 22 ↩ 1 Brain health & dementia

Louis summarizes a widely shared observational study: APOE 3/4 and 4/4 carriers eating unprocessed meat or fish had lower dementia risk and possibly slower progression, with fish and poultry equal to red meat. No benefit was seen in non-APOE4 carriers, and processed meat raised dementia risk in everyone.

Most omnivores already eat enough fish or poultry for the APOE4 benefit in a Swedish study

Mar 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 5 Brain health & dementia

Louis comments that participants with an APOE4 gene variant saw the maximum benefit at about 2.5 oz per day of unprocessed meat or fish, which people eating Mediterranean or MIND-style diets typically already reach. He notes the study is observational, benefit was seen only in APOE 3/4 and 4/4, and processed meat raised dementia risk in everyone.
