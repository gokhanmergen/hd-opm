ESC scientific statement: diet is a primary factor in CVD prevention and treatment

Dec 01, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 22 · 💬 23 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis shares the European Society of Cardiology/EAPC scientific statement on diet and cardiovascular disease. It concludes that diet is a primary factor, that plant-based whole-food patterns lower risk, and that ultraprocessed food, processed and red meat, salt, sugar and saturated fat raise it.

ACC/ESC consensus statement on abnormal cardiovascular findings in masters athletes

Apr 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 14 ↩ 1 Exercise & fitness

Louis highlights a new joint EAPC/ESC and ACC clinical consensus statement offering guidance for long-term high-volume exercisers over 35-40, covering atrial fibrillation, arrhythmias, coronary atherosclerosis, aortic dilatation, myocardial fibrosis and exercise-induced arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy. Notes that very high exercise volumes over years may have cardiovascular downsides.

2026 ESC guidelines: manage CVD and CKD together as one cardiorenal syndrome

Aug 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 Kidney health

Louis shares a summary of the 2026 ESC guideline on cardiovascular disease and CKD. Key messages include managing CVD and CKD together, testing both eGFR and urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio, treating albuminuria as a cardiovascular vital sign, and staging CKD with both measures.
