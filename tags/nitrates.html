Jun 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 3 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Louis recounts that Louis Ignarro won the 1998 Nobel Prize for showing nitric oxide is a signaling molecule that relaxes blood vessels. A shared Ignarro post explains oral bacteria convert vegetable nitrate to nitrite en route to nitric oxide, and that strong antiseptic mouthwash may disrupt this.

'Uncured' and 'nitrate-free' processed meats still get nitrates from celery and sea salt

Jan 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis warns that natural-labeled bacon, sausage, ham, jerky and deli meats use celery-derived nitrates that chemically cure the meat just like sodium nitrite. He says the result is nitrosamines, which the WHO links to colon cancer, and that they are also atherogenic and diabetagenic.

Dietary nitrate from leafy greens and beets boosts nitric oxide and vessel relaxation

Nov 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 1 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Louis shares an explainer on endothelial health and nitric oxide. Nitrate-rich vegetables such as beetroot, spinach, arugula and lettuce are converted to nitrite and then nitric oxide, supporting vasodilation. Research on beetroot nitrate shows improved vascular function and in some studies modest blood pressure reductions.

Gokhan shares a video comparing nitrate sources. Processed meats can form harmful N-nitrosamines during processing and cooking, whereas vegetable nitrates come with antioxidants that may offset any downside.

Vegetable nitrates tied to lower dementia risk; processed-meat nitrates to higher

Jun 28, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Brain health & dementia

Louis adds that 'natural' or 'uncured' processed meats also form N-nitrosamines. The shared Dale Bredesen post describes a recent study in which the source of nitrates mattered more than the amount: vegetable nitrates were tied to lower dementia risk, while processed meat or drinking-water nitrates were tied to higher risk.
