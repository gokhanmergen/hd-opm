CoQ10 may reduce gum bleeding and inflammation in periodontitis (11-trial meta-analysis)

Aug 24, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 35 · 💬 8 Supplements & vitamins

Louis notes the CoQ10 taken for heart health might also reduce gum disease. A 2022 meta-analysis of 11 clinical trials found CoQ10, topical or oral, lowered gum bleeding and gingival inflammation alongside standard periodontal treatment.

Treating gum disease slows carotid artery thickening in a clinical trial

Aug 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 8 ↩ 2 Blood vessels, circulation & endothelium

Explains how periodontitis lets oral bacteria and inflammatory products reach the bloodstream and vessel wall, and cites a clinical trial in which intensive gum treatment reduced carotid artery thickening. Gokhan's advice is to brush and floss diligently.

Omega-3 plus low-dose aspirin treated severe gum disease about as well as antibiotics

Aug 09, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 25 · 💬 4 Inflammation & CRP

A one-year, multicenter, placebo-controlled randomized trial found omega-3 with low-dose aspirin achieved the clinical goal in 57.7% of severe periodontitis patients vs about 58% with amoxicillin and metronidazole.

Metformin prevented periodontal disease in non-diabetics in a small randomized trial

Aug 22, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 1 Aging & longevity

A small human RCT with a supporting mouse study found metformin helped prevent periodontal disease in non-diabetic people, which the authors call oral-systemic preventive medicine. The post argues periodontal disease is aging-driven and speculates rapamycin plus metformin might be additive.

Gum disease care may reduce inflammation and plaque (reshare of Gokhan's own post)

Aug 28, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 10 ↩ 1 Inflammation & CRP

Gokhan reshares his earlier post advising regular cleanings, brushing and flossing. The shared content discusses how untreated gum disease contributes to atherosclerosis and a trial of intensive gum treatment slowing carotid thickening.
