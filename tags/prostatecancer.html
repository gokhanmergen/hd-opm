Statin RCT meta-analyses show no increase or decrease in cancer risk

Dec 31, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 13 Statins

Louis compiles meta-analyses of randomized trials showing statins neither raise nor lower cancer incidence or mortality, plus cohort studies suggesting neutral or lower cancer risk, e.g. prostate cancer. He adds that RCTs and prospective cohorts sit at the top of the evidence hierarchy.

NEJM 2020 dairy review: a mixed bag, best for fermented forms, weak for fractures

Jan 23, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 21 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis highlights the widely cited 2020 NEJM review on dairy and disease risk. It concludes that high dairy intake is not supported for preventing fractures, is not clearly linked to weight, diabetes or CVD, and may raise prostate and endometrial cancer risk while lowering colorectal cancer risk. Effects depend on the comparison food: dairy fares better than processed red meat or sugary drinks but worse than nuts.

Statins after high-risk prostate cancer diagnosis linked to 42% lower cancer death

Dec 05, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 9 Statins

A shared summary of a cohort study of high-risk prostate cancer patients: post-diagnosis statin use (atorvastatin, pravastatin, rosuvastatin, not lovastatin) lowered prostate cancer mortality 42%, statins plus metformin 54%, and metformin alone showed no benefit.

Isoleucine and valine fuel prostate cancer by boosting tumor cholesterol

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 9 Cancer

Louis notes that isoleucine, valine and methionine shorten mouse lifespan, and a 2026 Nature Metabolism study links isoleucine and valine to prostate cancer progression. Their breakdown produces propionyl-CoA, which enhances the cancer cells' cholesterol synthesis, transport and storage, supplying lipids for growth.

Metformin failed in large trials as a treatment for prostate and breast cancer

Dec 28, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 14 Cancer

Louis shares a Knowable Magazine article reporting that early population and animal data suggested metformin could treat cancer, but large clinical trials in prostate and breast cancer did not confirm it.

Only 12% of men prescribed testosterone had recommended diagnostic testing

Jun 21, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 7 · 💬 7 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis shares a Medscape report of a University of Michigan single-center study finding that most men prescribed testosterone did not get recommended diagnostic testing, and many had contraindications. Prescriptions have quadrupled in three decades despite stable hypogonadism diagnosis rates. Louis's remark about 'incompetence and greed' is opinion.

Video: large cohorts show no link between seed oils and prostate or colon cancer

Louis shares a Nutrition Made Simple video analyzing two headline-making studies tying seed oils to cancer. Large prospective cohorts show no association with prostate cancer, and with colon cancer once ultra-processed food intake is corrected for. Harvard: unsaturated fats protect, trans fats harm; PREDIMED backs olive oil and nuts

Sep 10, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 3 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes Harvard's Nutrition Source page correcting myths about dietary fat. It says polyunsaturated fats from vegetable oils, nuts and seeds help ward off type 2 diabetes, trans fats do the opposite, and the PREDIMED Mediterranean diet with olive oil or nuts reduced cardiovascular events.

SGLT2 inhibitors linked to delayed progression in prostate cancer on hormone therapy

Louis shares a JAMA Oncology finding that men with prostate cancer on hormone therapy using SGLT2 inhibitors had lower risk of treatment failure and delayed progression.

Swapping 10% of carb calories for vegetable fats cut lethal prostate cancer 29%

Dec 20, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 · 💬 2 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes a UCSF-led study of nearly 4,600 men with non-metastatic prostate cancer. Substituting healthy vegetable fats (olive and canola oil, nuts, seeds, avocado) for carbohydrates or animal fats was associated with lower lethal prostate cancer and all-cause mortality. The study adjusted for age, treatment, BMI, smoking, exercise and other factors.

Harvard review: fat not clearly linked to cancer; red and processed meat raise colon risk

Dec 20, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 2 ↩ 1 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis quotes the Harvard Nutrition Source summary on dietary fat and cancer. Total fat percentage and fat types show no clear association with cancer, and the Women's Health Initiative low-fat trial did not reduce breast or colon cancer. Red and processed meat do increase colon cancer risk, and animal fat in young women was linked to higher breast cancer risk in Nurses' Health Study II. He notes a later Harvard study updates this.

ERSPC 23-year follow-up: PSA screening cut prostate cancer deaths 13% with overdiagnosis

Nov 15, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 4 Cancer

Summarises the 23-year European Randomized Study of Screening for Prostate Cancer follow-up: 13% lower prostate cancer mortality (22 fewer deaths per 10,000 men), one death prevented per 456 men invited (628 at 16 years), with higher diagnosis rate (14% vs 12%) reflecting overdiagnosis. Authors call for risk-based screening.

Statins associated with lower advanced and fatal prostate cancer and longer survival

Aug 26, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis cites a 2022 review of the epidemiology of statins and prostate cancer. Registry and cohort studies find statin use linked to lower advanced and fatal prostate cancer risk and longer survival in men already diagnosed. These are observational associations.
