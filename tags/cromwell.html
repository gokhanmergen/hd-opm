Interview: insulin resistance and LDL/ApoB are both independent causes of heart disease

Feb 23, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 23 ↩ 15 Insulin resistance & diabetes

Louis shares an interview with William Cromwell, an expert in both metabolic disorders and lipidology, who walks through evidence that insulin resistance and LDL (ApoB) are both independent causative risk factors for heart disease. Louis's takeaway is that neither is the 'true cause' and both need addressing.

LDL particle size probably matters little once confounding and particle number are counted

Jun 16, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 16 ↩ 5 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

The poster explains that older studies suggested small, dense LDL was riskier, but newer, better-adjusted studies show large particles can enter the arterial wall too and carry more cholesterol, which compensates. Lipidologist William Cromwell's explanation is shown in images.
