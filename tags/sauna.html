Sauna works the heart like light cardio and lowered blood pressure after one session

Nov 07, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 4 Blood pressure, salt & potassium

Louis shares a summary of sauna physiology. Heat opens skin vessels, so the heart works harder to hold blood pressure, matching light to moderate exercise. One session lowered arterial stiffness and systolic pressure from 137 to 130 in 102 adults with risk factors, and a Finnish cohort of 2,315 men followed about 20 years showed lower mortality with more frequent sauna use.

Hot baths and saunas may lower heart attack and stroke risk, per Harvard Health

Jul 19, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 5 Environment (microplastics, pollution, heat)

The poster shares a Harvard Health article saying regular baths or saunas may lower risk of heart attack and stroke, noting there is real science behind it. The article cautions older people with low blood pressure because heat lowers blood pressure.

Frequent sauna use linked to 50% lower CVD mortality, but evidence is mostly observational

Sep 30, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 2 Exercise & fitness

A shared summary reports that sauna use 4 to 7 times weekly is associated with about half the cardiovascular mortality and 40% lower all-cause mortality, possibly because heat mimics moderate aerobic exercise. Louis cautions that the evidence is mostly observational, though the potential benefit could be large if causal.

Finnish men who used a sauna 4-7 times weekly had 65% lower Alzheimer's risk over 20 years

Quoted post summarizing Finnish cohort data on frequent sauna use and Alzheimer's disease. Observational, with no further detail in the post.

Heat-shock proteins may explain one man's escape from inherited early Alzheimer's

May 05, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 2 Brain health & dementia

Louis discusses a Presenilin 2 mutation carrier who avoided Alzheimer's into his late 70s, possibly due to long heat exposure in ship engine rooms and high heat shock proteins in his spinal fluid. He notes heat is hormetic and that frequent sauna use is associated with lower CVD risk, but human evidence is limited.

Man with Alzheimer's gene stayed healthy; Louis speculates heat exposure may help

Feb 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 Brain health & dementia

Louis shares an NPR story about a man genetically destined for early Alzheimer's whose brain remained healthy at 75, speculating that decades of heat in navy engine rooms raised protective heat shock proteins. The researchers are unsure of the cause and this is a single case.
