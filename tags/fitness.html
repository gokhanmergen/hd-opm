Norwegian 4x4 intervals: Rhonda Patrick on reversing heart aging and raising VO2 max

Oct 04, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 27 · 💬 29 Exercise & fitness

Summarises a Rhonda Patrick video: moderate exercise is ineffective for many, so vigorous HIIT such as the Norwegian 4x4 (four 4-minute efforts at about 80-90% max heart rate with 4-minute recovery) improves VO2 max. She cites a two-year study that reversed about 20 years of cardiac aging in sedentary 50-year-olds, and exercise reducing glycation-driven heart stiffness (Asif et al., PNAS 2000).

A 39-year-old marathon runner's heart attack: symptoms and delayed care

May 27, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 29 · 💬 21 Heart attack, stroke & procedures

An AHA story about Jason Shaw, 39, who ran seven marathons and kept exercising through chest tightness before a heart attack. It illustrates that exercise does not rule out heart attack and symptoms should not be ignored.

High-dose omega-3 raises AFib risk in high-CVD-risk people, not at low doses (2026)

Sep 16, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 12 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Louis warns that the atrial fibrillation risk from high-dose omega-3 is real. A 2021 meta-analysis of 7 RCTs (81,210 people) found higher AF risk, and a 2026 meta-analysis of 35 RCTs (114,592 people) confirmed it at higher doses in people at high CVD risk, but not in those without high risk, and not at low doses in either group.

Mendelian randomization: high VO2max is a health proxy, not a causal driver of longevity

May 14, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 11 · 💬 2 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares a 2-sample Mendelian randomization of about 70,000 people: genetic predisposition to high VO2max did not predict longevity, but genetic predisposition to low body fat, high muscle mass and high activity did. The same logic applies to grip strength.

Visceral fat acts like an endocrine organ driving inflammation, insulin resistance and BP

Aug 24, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 6 Weight, visceral & liver fat

A shared explainer describes visceral fat as biologically active tissue that releases inflammatory cytokines, reduces insulin sensitivity and produces an angiotensin precursor that can raise blood pressure.

Anabolic steroid users had 3x heart attack and 8.9x cardiomyopathy risk (Circulation)

Mar 15, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 5 Hormones, women's & men's health

Louis notes that excess exogenous testosterone is also an anabolic steroid and shares Luigi Fontana's summary of a Circulation study comparing 1,189 anabolic androgenic steroid users with 59,450 controls over 11 years. Users had several-fold higher risks of heart attack, revascularization, clots, arrhythmias, cardiomyopathy and heart failure.

A shared post citing a new review warns that GH boosting via peptides and supplements is marketed as anti-aging although low-GH states are associated with longer life and slower aging, while acromegaly brings many long-term harms. Each 1-MET fitness gain linked to 11-17% lower all-cause mortality (20.9M observations)

Feb 12, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Exercise & fitness

Louis shares a meta-analysis (PMID 38599681) showing higher cardiorespiratory fitness tracks with lower mortality in a dose-dependent way, while noting reverse causality is possible. A 1-MET increase was associated with large reductions in heart failure and cardiovascular mortality.
