Olive oil may reduce clotting by raising apoA-IV, which limits platelet aggregation

Jul 02, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 17 · 💬 10 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A Life Extension article describes mechanistic studies in which apoA-IV, a protective HDL component, reduces platelet aggregation and thrombosis, and monounsaturated fats such as extra virgin olive oil raise apoA-IV, especially after meals. This is mechanistic and partly animal evidence.

Baby aspirin during and after Covid may be worth discussing if you have heart disease

Jan 06, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 12 Infections & vaccines

Louis suggests people with heart disease who get Covid ask their doctor about baby aspirin during infection and for a few weeks after. Infections temporarily raise heart attack and stroke risk through inflammation, and Covid raises clot risk more than other infections. A linked 2022 meta-analysis found aspirin use associated with lower Covid mortality without more bleeding.

Dormant streptococcal biofilms in plaque can be woken by infection and rupture the plaque

Sep 13, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A JAHA study reports that oral-type bacteria can form protective biofilms inside atherosclerotic plaques, shielded from the immune system. A trigger such as a viral infection may awaken them, causing inflammation that ruptures the fibrous cap and causes a clot. This challenges the view that only oxidized LDL initiates coronary disease.

Intermittent fasting may prevent dangerous clots by raising gut-derived IPA

Sep 05, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 Fasting & meal timing

Louis shares a summary of a Life Metabolism study in 160 coronary artery disease participants plus human blood and mice. Intermittent fasting appeared to inhibit platelet activation and clot formation through increased gut microbial production of indole-3-propionic acid.

Long COVID linked to higher risk of venous and arterial blood clots in meta-analysis

Jun 30, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Infections & vaccines

Louis shares a 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis finding that people with post-COVID syndrome have an increased risk of thrombosis. This raises risk of heart attack, stroke, DVT and pulmonary embolism.

News: mouth bacteria biofilms in artery plaque may trigger some heart attacks

Sep 10, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 2 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A news report on the same study: oral bacteria can form dormant biofilms within arterial plaque and be woken by stressors such as viral infection, causing inflammation and rupture. The takeaway is that good oral hygiene may matter for heart health.
