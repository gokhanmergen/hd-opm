Engineered regulatory T cells targeting oxidized LDL cut plaque 70% in mice

Nov 22, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 35 · 💬 7 Inflammation & CRP

Summarises a University of Pennsylvania preclinical study adapting CAR T therapy: Tregs engineered to recognise oxidized LDL dampened arterial inflammation and reduced plaque 70% in cholesterol-prone mice. Unlike cancer CAR-T, the cells suppress rather than destroy; it is a possible add-on for patients at risk despite cholesterol drugs.

Native, non-oxidized LDL alone pushed endothelial cells into an oxidative state

Jun 18, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 15 · 💬 13 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis shares a post describing a classic in-vitro study (PMID 7543827) where native LDL made endothelial cells release about four times more superoxide, partly from the enzyme that normally makes nitric oxide, forming peroxynitrite. It is cited to counter claims that LDL is harmless unless oxidized.

Circulating oxidized LDL tests do not predict CVD risk once ApoB is accounted for

Sep 12, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 12 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Louis explains that blood oxidized LDL tests do not predict cardiovascular disease after adjusting for total ApoB particles, because LDL oxidation that matters occurs within the artery wall, which no commercial test measures. He adds that interventions preventing oxidation of circulating LDL have failed in randomized trials.

How LDL causes atherosclerosis: oxidation, inflammation, foam cells and genetics

May 19, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 12 · 💬 9 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Explains five lines of evidence for a causal role of LDL in atherosclerosis: oxidized LDL in the artery wall, endothelial dysfunction and inflammation, macrophage foam-cell formation, Mendelian randomization and familial hypercholesterolemia, and the benefit of LDL-lowering therapies. Useful as a mechanism overview for readers who doubt that LDL is a cause rather than a bystander.

Circulating oxidized LDL is a poor test; measure ApoB instead, says Dayspring

Feb 04, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 9 · 💬 12 LDL, ApoB & lipid panel

Tom Dayspring argues that oxidized LDL measured in the bloodstream is a 'useless' biomarker and that ApoB should be measured instead. Louis adds that this refers to circulating oxidized LDL, not oxidized LDL inside plaques, which still matters but cannot be measured by blood test. Newer studies that adjust for ApoB reportedly show circulating oxidized LDL is not related to CVD risk.

Dormant streptococcal biofilms in plaque can be woken by infection and rupture the plaque

Sep 13, 2025 · Gokhan Can ♥ 5 · 💬 4 ↩ 1 Infections & vaccines

A JAHA study reports that oral-type bacteria can form protective biofilms inside atherosclerotic plaques, shielded from the immune system. A trigger such as a viral infection may awaken them, causing inflammation that ruptures the fibrous cap and causes a clot. This challenges the view that only oxidized LDL initiates coronary disease.

Layne Norton rebuts seed-oil claims with human evidence on inflammation, LDL and oxidation

Nov 03, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 3 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

A shared post argues that human trial evidence does not support the claim that seed oils are harmful. Systematic reviews of randomized trials find that raising linoleic acid does not consistently increase inflammatory markers, replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat lowers LDL and cuts coronary events, and trials show no significant increase in lipid peroxidation. It adds that LDL oxidation mostly occurs after retention in the artery wall, so LDL concentration still matters.

Over 30 human studies find omega-6 fats do not raise inflammation markers

May 06, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 6 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis adds another study to over 30 showing omega-6 does not increase inflammatory markers or oxidized LDL in humans, and may reduce liver fat and improve insulin sensitivity. The shared Layne Norton post cites a human trial giving about 30 g/day soybean oil with no increase in inflammation or oxLDL.
