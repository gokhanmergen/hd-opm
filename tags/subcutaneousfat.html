Sumo wrestlers stay metabolically healthy because heavy exercise shifts fat under the skin

Mar 09, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 21 · 💬 9 Weight, visceral & liver fat

Louis explains why sumo wrestlers, despite huge calorie and refined-carbohydrate intake, store little fat in the liver and pancreas and little visceral fat. He attributes this to up to 7 hours of daily exercise, which releases adiponectin and steers fat storage to subcutaneous tissue, a reason exercise protects against metabolic syndrome and diabetes.

Higher omega-6 in subcutaneous fat linked to lower death rates in older men

Aug 20, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 5 Fats & oils (saturated fat, seed oils, omega-6, olive oil)

Louis shares a Harvard Health item on a JAMA Cardiology study finding that high omega-6 levels in subcutaneous fat, mostly reflecting dietary linoleic acid from plant oils, correlated with lower death rates in older men.
