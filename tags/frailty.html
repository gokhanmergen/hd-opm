Nurses' Health Study: plant protein, not animal protein, linked to lower frailty risk

Nov 02, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 12 · 💬 19 Protein

Louis cites a 2022 Nurses' Health Study analysis in which higher plant protein intake was associated with lower frailty risk, while animal and dairy protein were not. He adds that protein alone does not build muscle without resistance exercise.

One tablespoon of cocoa daily improved muscle mass and grip strength in older adults

Feb 13, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 24 · 💬 11 Nuts, fish, coffee, tea, cocoa & other foods

NutritionFacts reports older adults taking a tablespoon of unprocessed cocoa daily for 12 weeks improved muscle mass index, grip strength and four physical function tests.

Shingles vaccine tied to slower biological aging and less inflammation in 3,800 elders

Feb 28, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 22 · 💬 11 Infections & vaccines

Reports a large national study of more than 3,800 Americans aged 70+, in which shingles-vaccinated people showed slower biological aging, lower chronic inflammation and slower aging-related changes in gene activity. This suggests the vaccine may calm 'inflammaging', the low-grade inflammation tied to heart disease, frailty and cognitive decline.

Frailty in older adults is not a protein-deficiency disease, per Luigi Fontana

Dec 19, 2025 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 14 · 💬 9 Protein

Louis shares Luigi Fontana's argument that frailty and sarcopenia in Western older adults reflect decades of overnutrition and inactivity, not low protein. The post cites randomized trials in which protein supplements gave small or no functional gains, while resistance exercise produced the functional benefit.

Single dose of donor stem cells raised walking endurance about 20% in frail older adults

Feb 25, 2026 · Gokhan Can ♥ 18 · 💬 9 Aging & longevity

Describes a trial of donor bone-marrow mesenchymal stem cells in 148 walking adults aged 70-85 with frailty (118 on varying doses, 30 on placebo). The cells lack immune-triggering surface proteins so no immunosuppressants were needed; at nine months the highest dose group walked about 60 m farther in a six-minute walk test than at baseline.

Nurses' Health Study: plant but not animal protein linked to lower frailty in older women

Aug 31, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 10 · 💬 10 ↩ 3 Protein

Louis cites a 2022 Nurses' Health Study analysis in older women: higher plant protein intake was associated with lower frailty risk, but animal and dairy protein were not. Substituting 5% of energy from plant protein for animal, dairy or non-dairy animal protein was linked to 38%, 32% and 42% lower frailty risk respectively.

Red meat tied to lower mortality only in frail underweight over-80s (Chinese case-control)

Mar 20, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 4 · 💬 18 Meat, eggs & dairy

Louis comments on an AJCN 2026 nested case-control study of Chinese adults 80+: non-meat eaters were less likely to reach 100, but only if underweight and frail. He cautions that case-control studies are low on the evidence hierarchy and may not be causal, and suggests protein and IGF-1 may matter in frail old age.

The US eats more high-quality protein than anywhere, yet heart disease and frailty persist

Oct 30, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 18 · 💬 9 ↩ 2 Protein

Louis argues the standard American diet supplies the most total and animal protein of any major region, yet the US has high rates of heart disease, cancer, sarcopenia and frailty. He concludes there is no protein emergency in the US. This is an ecological argument without cited studies.

Top Mediterranean diet adherence linked to 39% lower frailty in Nurses' Health Study

May 25, 2024 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Mediterranean, DASH, MIND & plant-forward diets

Louis summarizes a Nurses' Health Study analysis in women over 60 followed 1990 to 2010. Those with the highest adherence to a traditional Mediterranean-style pattern had a 39% lower risk of frailty than average adherers. Components independently linked to lower frailty included less red and processed meat, lower sodium, higher monounsaturated-to-saturated fat ratio, vegetables and moderate alcohol.

Older veterans starting statins were less likely to develop frailty

Jun 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 13 · 💬 3 ↩ 1 Statins

Louis shares a large observational study in the European Heart Journal where older US veterans who initiated statins had significantly lower frailty risk over time. Authors suggest anti-inflammatory effects may slow functional decline but emphasize randomized trials are needed.

New statin use linked to less frailty in older veterans; STAREE will test it

Jun 10, 2026 · Louis Nervegna ♥ 5 · 💬 1 Statins

Louis shares the observational VA/Mass General Brigham study (JACC) reporting that older US veterans starting statins developed less frailty over time. He notes two large randomized trials, PREVENTABLE and STAREE, are testing statins in the elderly and tracking frailty, with STAREE expected to report within months.

Plasma proteomics: 18 proteins shared between heart failure and frailty in ARIC

Jul 29, 2024 · Gokhan Can ♥ 0 · 💬 2 Heart rhythm & heart failure

Using proteomics in over 10,000 ARIC participants, researchers found 18 proteins independently associated with both incident heart failure and frailty, mainly in fibrosis, inflammation and cell-death pathways, with stronger links to HFpEF than HFrEF.
